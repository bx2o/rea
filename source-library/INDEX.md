- **Volume:** 953 unique sources across a 231-file corpus — deep, but the tail is long

  wiki). Where Grokipedia is the *only* citation, claims fail the >=2-independent-source rule
  (`DATA-MODEL-CONTRACTS.md` §6) and must be downgraded to `partial`.
- **Filings vs. narrative:** primary investor PDFs (integrated/annual reports, SENS,
  `reporting.standardbank.com`) are present for Capitec, Standard Bank, Absa, Nedbank and the global
  peers, but several bank financials lean on market-data aggregators rather than the audited PDF.
- **App-store evidence is shallow:** store/analytics domains present, but ratings are
  point-in-time and not version-pinned, so re-verification will drift.
- **Competitor depth is asymmetric:** the six `competitors/` entities (Fanatics, FanDuel, Gate.io,
  Polymarket, Socios/Chiliz, Sorare) carry 8 flat files each but no `customer`, `people` or
  `partnerships` coverage (see `COVERAGE-MATRIX.md`).

  leadership) are historical anchors and should be read as baseline, not current state.
