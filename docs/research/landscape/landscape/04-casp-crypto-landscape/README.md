## Scope

In scope: CASPs, crypto exchanges, wallets, and digital-asset trading/investment platforms operating in or benchmarked against the South African market, including FSCA-registered CSPs where applicable.

Explicitly out of scope: traditional banking entities (covered in `01-sa-banking-landscape`) and web3 fan-engagement competitors (covered in `05-global-web3-landscape`).
