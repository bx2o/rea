# Resource Allocation Framework

**Purpose**: Allocate personnel, budget, time across competing priorities  
**Use When**: Planning quarterly work, managing team capacity, deciding on investments  
**Owner**: The Engineer + The Strategist  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Resource Categories

### Personnel

- Engineers
- Product managers
- Designers
- Sales/Marketing
- Operations

### Budget

- Salaries
- Infrastructure
- Tools/services
- Marketing spend
- R&D

### Time/Capacity

- Team bandwidth
- Leadership focus
- Planning cycles
- Review cycles

---

## Allocation Approach

### Portfolio View

```
Team Size: 10 people

Allocation:
- 60% Current product
- 20% Improvements/tech debt
- 10% Innovation/experiments
- 10% Operations/processes

Translation: 6 people, 2 people, 1 person, 1 person
```

### By Priority

```
Template:
- P0 (Critical): 50%
- P1 (Important): 30%
- P2 (Nice to have): 15%
- P3 (Nice to have later): 5%
```

---

## Rebalancing

**Quarterly Reviews**:

- Are we on track?
- Should we shift allocation?
- New priorities emerged?
- Resource constraints?

**Triggers for Change**:

- Major initiative needs work
- Performance improvement needed
- Customer issue critical
- Technical debt unsustainable
- Team burnout risk

---

## Constraints

### Hard Limits

- FTE count (people available)
- Budget approved (money available)
- Time to completion (must ship when?)

### Soft Constraints

- Skill mix needed
- Cross-team dependencies
- Knowledge silos to avoid
- Burnout prevention

---

## Recommendation Template

```
Initiative A: 3 engineers, $200k, 12 weeks
Initiative B: 2 engineers, $100k, 8 weeks
Maintenance: 5 engineers (existing team)

Total: 10 engineers, $300k, ongoing
Cost: Within budget
Risk: Medium (dependent on hiring)
```
