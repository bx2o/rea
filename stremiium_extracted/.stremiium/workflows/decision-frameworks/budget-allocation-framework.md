# Budget Allocation Decision Framework

**Purpose**: Allocate budget across departments and initiatives strategically  
**Use When**: Annual planning, quarterly reforecasting, major spending decisions  
**Owner**: The Capitalist + Executive Team  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Budget Categories

### Fixed Costs

- Salaries (60-70%)
- Office/infrastructure (10-15%)
- Tools/subscriptions (5-10%)

### Variable Costs

- Cloud infrastructure (5-15%)
- Vendor services (2-5%)
- Contingency (5-10%)

### Investment

- R&D (5-10%)
- Training (1-2%)
- Growth initiatives (10-20%)

---

## Allocation Approach

### Top-Down

1. Total company revenue target
2. Spend as % of revenue
3. Allocate to departments
4. Managers break down further

### Bottom-Up

1. Each team estimates needs
2. Prioritize across teams
3. Aggregate to total
4. Compare to target

### Hybrid

- Fixed costs: Top-down (known)
- Variable: Bottom-up (team knows)
- Investment: Strategic allocation

---

## Decision Framework

```
Step 1: Determine total budget
- % of revenue?
- Growth targets?
- Profitability goals?

Step 2: Allocate to departments
- What drives growth?
- What's underinvested?
- What should shrink?

Step 3: Evaluate initiatives
- ROI potential?
- Strategic alignment?
- Risk/reward?

Step 4: Build budget
- Department totals
- Initiative allocation
- Contingency reserve
```

## Common Allocations

**SaaS Company**:

- Engineering: 30%
- Sales/Marketing: 40%
- Operations: 15%
- Admin/Other: 15%

**High-Growth Startup**:

- Engineering: 25%
- Sales/Marketing: 50%
- Operations: 15%
- Admin/Other: 10%

**Mature Company**:

- Engineering: 30%
- Sales/Marketing: 35%
- Operations/Support: 20%
- Admin/Other: 15%

---

## Principles

- **Strategic**: Aligned with goals
- **Flexible**: Can adjust quarterly
- **Transparent**: Clear reasoning
- **Balanced**: Avoid single-bets
- **Reserve**: 10% contingency
