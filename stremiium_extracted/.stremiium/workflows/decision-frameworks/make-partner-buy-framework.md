# Make/Partner/Buy Decision Framework

**Purpose**: Decide whether to build, partner, or acquire capability  
**Use When**: Addressing capability gap or market opportunity  
**Owner**: The Strategist + The Architect  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Options Summary

### Make (Build in-house)

- **Time**: 6-18 months
- **Cost**: High ($500k-$5M)
- **Control**: 100%
- **Capability**: Builds team strength
- **Risk**: Execution, team scaling

### Partner (Integrate with another)

- **Time**: 2-6 months
- **Cost**: Medium ($50k-$500k)
- **Control**: Shared
- **Capability**: Extends reach
- **Risk**: Partner stability, alignment

### Buy (Acquire company/asset)

- **Time**: 2-4 months
- **Cost**: High ($1M-$100M+)
- **Control**: 100%
- **Capability**: Immediate + team
- **Risk**: Integration, retention

---

## Decision Matrix

| Factor              | Make   | Partner    | Buy       |
| ------------------- | ------ | ---------- | --------- |
| **Speed**           | Slow   | Fast       | Fast      |
| **Cost**            | High   | Low-Medium | Very High |
| **Control**         | Full   | Partial    | Full      |
| **Team Growth**     | Yes    | No         | Yes       |
| **Core Competency** | Yes    | Maybe      | Yes       |
| **Tech Debt**       | Risk   | None       | Inherit   |
| **Scaling Effort**  | Medium | Low        | High      |

---

## Decision Tree

```
Start: Need a capability

1. Is it core to business?
   NO → PARTNER or BUY
   YES → Continue

2. Do we have team/time?
   NO → BUY or PARTNER
   YES → MAKE

3. Is there a great partner?
   YES → PARTNER
   NO → MAKE or BUY

4. Can we acquire competitor?
   YES → BUY (if strategic)
   NO → MAKE or PARTNER
```

- **Partner**: Aligned incentives, integration plan
- **Buy**: Cultural fit, retention, integration
