# The Strategist - Business & Operations Workflows

**Agent**: The Strategist 🎯  
**Domain**: Business strategy, operational planning, market analysis, optimization  
**Purpose**: Strategic planning, business analysis, operational improvements, cross-team coordination

---

## Workflow 1: Strategic Planning Workflow

### Trigger

- Annual/quarterly planning cycle
- New business initiative
- Market opportunity identified
- Strategic direction needed

### Process

#### Step 1: Assess Current State

**Goal**: Understand where we are  
**Actions**:

- Review last period's performance
- Assess key metrics and KPIs
- Analyze competitive landscape
- Identify market trends
- Gather stakeholder input
- Document challenges and opportunities

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Current state clearly understood
- ✅ Strengths and weaknesses identified
- ✅ Market context clear

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 2: Define Strategic Vision

**Goal**: Envision desired future state  
**Actions**:

- Articulate vision for period (1-3 years)
- Define strategic goals
- Identify key success factors
- Clarify competitive advantages
- Get stakeholder alignment

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Vision inspiring and clear
- ✅ Goals specific and measurable
- ✅ Stakeholder alignment achieved

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 3: Develop Strategies

**Goal**: Define how to achieve vision  
**Actions**:

- Identify strategic initiatives
- Analyze implementation approaches
- Define resource requirements
- Assess risks and mitigations
- Create scenario analysis

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Strategies well-thought-out
- ✅ Initiatives clearly defined
- ✅ Risks understood

**Duration**: 3-4 hours

---

#### Step 4: Create Implementation Plan

**Goal**: Define concrete execution steps  
**Actions**:

- Break strategies into projects
- Define project objectives
- Create timeline/milestones
- Allocate resources
- Define dependencies
- Create governance structure

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Projects clearly defined
- ✅ Timeline realistic
- ✅ Resources allocated
- ✅ Governance clear

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 5: Communicate & Get Buy-in

**Goal**: Align organization on strategy  
**Actions**:

- Create strategy presentation
- Present to executive team
- Gather feedback and adjust
- Communicate to organization
- Address concerns and questions
- Get commitment

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Organization understands strategy
- ✅ Buy-in obtained
- ✅ Enthusiasm generated

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 6: Monitor & Adjust

**Goal**: Track execution and optimize  
**Actions**:

- Establish KPI tracking
- Create monthly review cadence
- Track progress vs. plan
- Identify obstacles early
- Adjust tactics as needed
- Keep communication flowing

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Progress tracked systematically
- ✅ Issues identified early
- ✅ Strategy adapted appropriately

**Ongoing**

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Clear strategic plan created
- ✅ Organization aligned
- ✅ Implementation projects defined
- ✅ Resources allocated
- ✅ Monitoring process established

## Workflow 2: Business Analysis Workflow

### Trigger

- Business decision needed
- Investment analysis required
- Cost/benefit evaluation needed
- Market opportunity assessment

### Process

#### Step 1: Frame the Question

**Goal**: Clearly define what we need to analyze  
**Actions**:

- Define decision to be made
- Identify stakeholders
- Clarify decision timeline
- Define success criteria
- Outline key unknowns

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Question clearly stated
- ✅ Decision makers identified
- ✅ Timeline understood

**Duration**: 30-60 minutes

---

#### Step 2: Gather Information

**Goal**: Collect relevant data  
**Actions**:

- Conduct research
- Interview stakeholders
- Analyze historical data
- Benchmark against competitors
- Gather market data
- Document assumptions

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Comprehensive data gathered
- ✅ Assumptions documented
- ✅ Data quality verified

**Duration**: 2-4 hours

---

#### Step 3: Analyze Alternatives

**Goal**: Evaluate options  
**Actions**:

- Identify alternative approaches
- Define evaluation criteria
- Model each alternative
- Perform financial analysis (if relevant)
- Assess risks
- Score alternatives

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Alternatives clearly defined
- ✅ Analysis rigorous
- ✅ Comparison apples-to-apples

**Duration**: 3-5 hours

---

#### Step 4: Make Recommendation

**Goal**: Propose best course of action  
**Actions**:

- Analyze trade-offs
- Make recommendation
- Document reasoning
- Clarify assumptions
- Acknowledge uncertainties
- Define success metrics

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Recommendation clear
- ✅ Reasoning sound
- ✅ Risks acknowledged

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

#### Step 5: Present Findings

**Goal**: Get decision made  
**Actions**:

- Create presentation deck
- Present analysis to decision makers
- Address questions and concerns
- Adjust recommendations if needed
- Get decision/approval
- Document decision rationale

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Decision made
- ✅ Reasoning understood
- ✅ Organization aligned

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Thorough business analysis completed
- ✅ Recommendations provided
- ✅ Decision made and documented
- ✅ Implementation can proceed

---

## Workflow 3: Process Optimization Workflow

### Trigger

- Process improvement opportunity identified
- Performance metrics declining
- Bottleneck spotted
- Efficiency improvement needed

### Process

#### Step 1: Understand Current Process

**Goal**: Document how it works now  
**Actions**:

- Map current process step-by-step
- Identify participants and dependencies
- Measure current performance
- Identify pain points
- Gather feedback from users
- Document process metrics

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Process fully mapped
- ✅ Pain points identified
- ✅ Baseline metrics established

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

#### Step 2: Analyze Problems

**Goal**: Understand root causes  
**Actions**:

- Why is process inefficient?
- What are the bottlenecks?
- What causes delays?
- Where is quality impacted?
- What causes errors?
- Use root cause analysis

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Root causes identified
- ✅ Problem severity understood
- ✅ Opportunity quantified

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

#### Step 3: Design Improvements

**Goal**: Plan how to improve  
**Actions**:

- Generate improvement ideas
- Evaluate feasibility
- Estimate impact
- Design improved process
- Identify required changes
- Plan pilot/rollout

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Improvement clear and viable
- ✅ Expected benefits quantified
- ✅ Implementation approach defined

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 4: Implement Changes

**Goal**: Execute the improvements  
**Actions**:

- Run pilot (if appropriate)
- Measure pilot results
- Train team on new process
- Gradually roll out
- Monitor for issues
- Support team through transition

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Rollout smooth
- ✅ Team comfortable with new process
- ✅ No critical issues

**Duration**: 1-4 weeks (depends on change)

---

#### Step 5: Measure Results

**Goal**: Verify improvements achieved  
**Actions**:

- Measure key metrics post-implementation
- Compare to baseline
- Document improvements
- Gather user feedback
- Identify any unintended consequences
- Celebrate success

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Improvements measured and confirmed
- ✅ Benefits realized
- ✅ Process sustainable

**Duration**: 1-2 weeks post-implementation

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Process improved and optimized
- ✅ Efficiency gains achieved
- ✅ Team trained on new approach
- ✅ Results measured and documented

---

## Workflow 4: Cross-Team Coordination Workflow

### Trigger

- Multi-team project initiated
- Cross-functional initiative needed
- Stakeholder alignment required
- Team conflict resolution needed

### Process

#### Step 1: Define Scope & Stakeholders

**Goal**: Understand the initiative and who's involved  
**Actions**:

- Define project objectives
- Identify all teams involved
- Map dependencies
- Clarify success criteria
- Identify conflicts/tensions

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Scope clear
- ✅ All stakeholders identified
- ✅ Potential issues surface

**Duration**: 1 hour

---

#### Step 2: Create Coordination Plan

**Goal**: Define how teams will work together  
**Actions**:

- Define communication cadence
- Create collaboration structure
- Define decision-making process
- Establish escalation path
- Create shared roadmap
- Allocate resources

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Coordination plan clear
- ✅ Everyone understands their role
- ✅ Decision process defined

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

#### Step 3: Facilitate Alignment

**Goal**: Get all teams on same page  
**Actions**:

- Hold alignment meeting
- Review coordination plan
- Surface concerns/questions
- Resolve conflicts
- Get commitment from teams
- Document decisions

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ All teams aligned
- ✅ Concerns addressed
- ✅ Commitment obtained

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

#### Step 4: Monitor & Facilitate

**Goal**: Keep teams coordinated  
**Actions**:

- Hold regular coordination meetings
- Track progress across teams
- Identify integration issues early
- Facilitate problem-solving
- Escalate conflicts
- Keep communication flowing

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Teams coordinated effectively
- ✅ Issues resolved quickly
- ✅ Progress on track

**Ongoing**

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Cross-team coordination effective
- ✅ Integration issues resolved
- ✅ Project delivering on target
- ✅ Team satisfaction high

## Decision Points

### When to Escalate to Sparkling Curious

- [ ] Strategy conflicts with other initiatives
- [ ] Major resource reallocation needed
- [ ] Org structure change required
- [ ] Significant risk identified

### When to Delegate to Specialists

- [ ] Financial analysis → The Capitalist
- [ ] Technical feasibility → The Architect
- [ ] Hiring needs → The Guardian
- [ ] Market research → The Amplifier
