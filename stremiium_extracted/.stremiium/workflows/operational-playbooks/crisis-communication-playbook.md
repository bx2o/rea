# Crisis Communication Playbook

**Purpose**: Structured communication during organizational crisis  
**Trigger**: Major incident, outage, security breach, leadership change, regulatory issue  
**Owner**: CEO + Communications Lead  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Crisis Types & Response Times

### Critical Crisis (1-2 hour response)

- Service outage > 1 hour
- Data breach
- Security incident
- Customer data loss
- Regulatory violation

### Significant Crisis (4-8 hour response)

- Major product bug
- Customer lawsuit
- Leadership departure
- Competitive threat
- Financial issue

### Standard Crisis (24 hour response)

- Process issue
- Customer complaint
- Minor bug
- Team change
- Routine update

---

## Internal Communication (Employee-Facing)

### Phase 1: Notification (First 30 min)

```
Channel: Slack all-hands, email

Content:
- What happened?
- When will we know more?
- What should employees do?
- Where to get updates?

Example:
"We've experienced a production issue affecting 2% of
users. Our engineering team is actively investigating.
Updates every 30 minutes in #incident channel."
```

### Phase 2: Status Updates

```
Every 30 min for critical crisis
Every 2 hours for significant
Daily for standard

Include:
- Current status
- What we know/don't know
- What we're doing
- Next update time
```

### Phase 3: Resolution

```
"Issue resolved. Root cause: X.
Impact: Y. We're implementing: Z
Post-mortem scheduled for Thursday."
```

---

## External Communication (Customer-Facing)

### Status Page

```
Update status.example.com real-time
- Clear status (Operational, Degraded, Outage)
- What's affected?
- ETA on resolution
- Recent updates
```

### Email to Affected Customers

```
Subject: [Action Required / Update] - [Service] Issue

Content:
- Impact summary
- When it started
- Current status
- What we're doing
- ETA
- Compensation (if applicable)
- Resources (help.example.com)
```

### Public Announcement

```
For major crises:
- Blog post (detailed explanation)
- Tweet (factual, brief)
- Press statement (if needed)
```

---

## Message Principles

### Clarity

- Simple language
- Avoid jargon
- Clear what's happening

### Transparency

- What we know
- What we don't know
- What we're doing

### Timeliness

- Regular updates
- Don't leave people hanging
- Even "no update" is an update

### Empathy

- Acknowledge impact
- Apologize for issues
- Commitment to prevent

---

## Chain of Command

During crisis:

- CEOifies on strategy/message approval
- Communications: Drafts and distributes
- Engineering: Technical accuracy
- Legal: Review for risk/liability

---

## Post-Crisis

- [ ] Send thank-you email (24 hours)
- [ ] Compensation decisions (48 hours)
- [ ] Public postmortem (72 hours)
- [ ] Internal postmortem (1 week)
- [ ] Process improvements (2 weeks)
