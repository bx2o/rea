## KNOWLEDGE BASE: CEO FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Strategic Planning

**Q: How do we balance short-term execution with long-term vision?**

- Allocate 70% resources to strategic priorities, 20% to operational excellence, 10% to innovation
- Maintain quarterly planning cycles tied to annual strategy
- Review and adjust strategy quarterly based on market conditions

**Q: What's the right way to handle conflicting priorities between squads?**

- Use weighted decision criteria tied to organizational strategy
- Involve affected squad captains in discussion
- Make decision transparent with clear rationale
- Document decision for future reference and learning

**Q: How do we scale to 200+ agents while maintaining cohesion?**

- Create more specialized squads rather than growing existing ones
- Establish clear governance and decision frameworks
- Invest in knowledge management and communication systems
- Build culture of collaboration and shared values

### Organizational Health

**Q: How do we maintain agent satisfaction and prevent burnout?**

- Regular 1-on-1s with squad captains about team health
- Quarterly agent satisfaction surveys
- Investment in learning and development
- Recognition and celebration of wins
- Clear career paths and growth opportunities

**Q: What's the early warning system for organizational problems?**

- Weekly metrics dashboards from all squads
- Monthly risk and issue reports from advisors
- Real-time incident escalation protocols
- Quarterly culture and engagement surveys
- Open-door policy for agent concerns

**Q: How do we handle major failures or crises?**

- Rapid response team assembly within hours
- Root cause analysis and documentation
- Clear accountability assignment
- Corrective action plans with timeline
- Communication and transparency to all stakeholders
- Post-incident review and learning capture

### Financial Management

**Q: What's the budgeting process for squads?**

- Squad captains submit annual budgets in Q4
- CFO advisor reviews for efficiency and viability
- CEO approves with strategic alignment check
- Monthly tracking against budget
- Quarterly re-forecasting and adjustments

**Q: How do we prioritize spending when resources are limited?**

- Tier initiatives by strategic impact and ROI
- Consider opportunity cost and alternatives
- Seek ways to deliver value with existing resources
- Make trade-offs transparent to affected parties
- Document decisions for accountability
