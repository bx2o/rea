## 3.2 People Management Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### SOP 1: Hiring Process

**Scope:** Full-cycle recruiting process
**Trigger:** Approved requisition received

**Steps:**

1. **Intake** (Day 1-2)
   - Hiring manager meeting
   - Job description finalization
   - Interview panel identification
   - Compensation band confirmation

2. **Sourcing** (Week 1-3)
   - Job posting to boards (LinkedIn, Web3 jobs, AngelList)
   - Sourcing on LinkedIn Recruiter
   - Employee referral activation
   - Talent pipeline outreach

3. **Screening** (Week 2-4)
   - Resume review (48-hour SLA)
   - Recruiter phone screen
   - Technical assessment (if applicable)
   - Top 3-5 candidates shortlisted

4. **Interviewing** (Week 3-6)
   - Round 1: Hiring manager (culture, experience)

# SECTION 4: COMPREHENSIVE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## 4.1 Stremiium People FAQ

### Hiring & Recruiting

**Q1: How do I request to hire someone?**
A: Submit a hiring requisition through the HRC-T system. Include role requirements, budget approval, and justification. HR-Culture Squad will review and initiate recruiting.

**Q2: What is the typical hiring timeline?**
A: Target 30 days from requisition to offer. Complex roles may take 45-60 days. Urgent roles can be expedited.

**Q3: How do we source Web3 talent?**
A: Multiple channels: LinkedIn, specialized Web3 job boards (CryptoJobs, Web3.career), AngelList, employee referrals, community outreach, conference networking.

**Q4: What is our interview process?**
A: Typically: Recruiter screen (30 min) → Hiring manager interview (45 min) → Technical/skills assessment (60 min) → Panel interview (45 min) → Executive interview for senior roles (30 min).

**Q5: How do we evaluate culture fit?**
A: Values-based interview questions, behavioral scenarios, reference checks, and team interaction opportunities. Look for culture add, not just fit.

### Compensation & Tokens

**Q6: How is compensation structured?**
A: Hybrid model: Competitive base salary + STM token incentives + performance bonuses + benefits.

**Q7: How do token grants work?**
A: New hires receive token grants with 4-year vesting and 1-year cliff. Annual refresh grants for retention. Tokens earned as they vest.

**Q8: When are salary reviews conducted?**
A: Annual compensation review typically in Q4, effective January 1. Market adjustments and promotions can happen off-cycle when needed.

**Q9: What benefits do we offer?**
A: Remote-first benefits: Home office stipend, co-working allowance, health benefits (region-dependent), learning budget, wellness stipend, generous PTO.

### Performance & Development

**Q10: How do performance reviews work?**
A: OKRs set quarterly. Weekly 1:1s with managers. Quarterly check-ins. Annual comprehensive review with 360 feedback. Continuous feedback encouraged.

**Q11: What is the promotion process?**
A: Promotions considered during annual review and when role significantly changes. Manager recommendation, calibration review, approval, and communication.

**Q12: How do I request training or development?**
A: Discuss with manager in 1:1. Submit request through People-Developer. Learning budget available per person annually.

**Q13: Are there career paths defined?**
A: Yes, career paths exist for all functions: Individual contributor tracks and management tracks. Skills matrices define progression.

### Culture & Engagement

**Q14: What are Stremiium's core values?**
A: Innovation First, User Obsession, Radical Transparency, Bias for Action, Growth Mindset, Ownership Mentality, Collaborative Spirit, Integrity Always.

**Q15: How do we maintain culture while remote?**
A: Intentional culture rituals, virtual events, async communication, in-person retreats, culture champions, values recognition, all-hands meetings.

**Q16: How do I recognize a colleague?**
A: Use the peer recognition platform, shout-outs in all-hands, nominate for values awards, or simply say thank you in team channels.

**Q17: What team events do we have?**
A: Monthly all-hands, quarterly virtual socials, annual team retreat, interest-based slack channels, virtual coffee chats, game nights.

### Policies & Support

**Q18: Where can I find company policies?**
A: Employee handbook in Notion/Confluence. Key policies: Remote work, PTO, expense reimbursement, code of conduct, anti-harassment.

**Q19: Who do I talk to about a workplace concern?**
A: Start with your manager. Can also reach Employee-Advocate directly. Anonymous reporting available. No retaliation for good-faith reports.

**Q20: How do I request time off?**
A: Submit in HR system with appropriate notice. Discuss with manager for planning. Unlimited PTO policy with reasonable use.
