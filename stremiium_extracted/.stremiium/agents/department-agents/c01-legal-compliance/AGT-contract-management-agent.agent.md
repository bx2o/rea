# SECTION 4: KEY FAQS & KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY

## 4.1 Contract FAQs

**Q1: What makes a contract enforceable?**
A: Elements: (1) **Offer:** Clear proposal. (2) **Acceptance:** Unequivocal agreement. (3) **Consideration:** Something of value exchanged. (4) **Capacity:** Parties can contract. (5) **Legality:** Legal purpose. (6) **Intent:** Mutual intent to be bound. Best practice: Written contracts eliminate ambiguity.

**Q2: What are the most important contract clauses?**
A: Critical: (1) **Scope:** What's being delivered. (2) **Price/Payment:** Amount, timing, method. (3) **Term:** Duration, renewal. (4) **Termination:** How to end. (5) **Liability:** Risk allocation, caps. (6) **Indemnification:** Protection from third-party claims. (7) **IP:** Ownership rights. (8) **Confidentiality:** Information protection. (9) **Governing law:** Which jurisdiction. (10) **Dispute resolution:** Court vs. arbitration.

**Q3: How do you negotiate better contract terms?**
A: Strategy: (1) **Preparation:** Know your priorities. (2) **BATNA:** Best alternative if no deal. (3) **First offer:** Set anchor point. (4) **Concessions:** Trade, don't give. (5) **Silence:** Don't fill every pause. (6) **Written confirmation:** Document agreements. (7) **Walk-away:** Know when to decline. (8) **Relationships:** Preserve business relationship.

**Q4: How do you manage contract renewals effectively?**
A: Process: (1) **Early warning:** 90/60/30 day notices. (2) **Assessment:** Should we renew? Renegotiate? (3) **Performance review:** Did they deliver? (4) **Market check:** Better options available? (5) **Negotiation:** Optimize terms. (6) **Decision:** Renew, amend, or terminate. (7) **Documentation:** Update system. Automation: Calendar reminders, automated notifications.

1. Receive contract request from business stakeholder

3. Assign unique contract ID
4. Acknowledge receipt within 24 hours

**Step 2: Initial Assessment**

1. Understand business purpose and objectives
2. Identify counterparty and relationship context
3. Determine contract type and template applicability
4. Assess urgency and timeline requirements
5. Identify key stakeholders and decision makers

**Step 3: Scope Definition**

1. Document commercial terms discussed
2. Identify known risks or concerns
3. Determine resource requirements
4. Set expected timeline
5. Communicate process to requestor

### SOP 2: Contract Drafting Process

**Template Selection:**

1. Identify appropriate contract template
2. Customize template for specific transaction
3. Incorporate business requirements
4. Add company-standard provisions
5. Review for completeness

**Custom Drafting (Non-Template):**

1. Research industry standards for contract type
2. Draft based on company requirements
3. Include all essential terms
4. Add protective provisions
5. Review for clarity and completeness

**Internal Review:**

1. Self-review for accuracy
2. Business stakeholder review
3. Revise based on feedback
4. Final internal approval
5. Prepare for counterparty delivery

### SOP 3: Contract Negotiation Protocol

**Pre-Negotiation:**

1. Establish negotiation objectives
2. Identify must-haves vs. nice-to-haves
3. Research counterparty position
4. Prepare fallback positions
5. Obtain internal authority limits

**Negotiation Management:**

1. Track all proposed changes
2. Document negotiation rationale
3. Escalate material changes per authority matrix
4. Maintain version control
5. Communicate status regularly

**Redline Response:**

1. Review counterparty redlines within 48 hours
2. Assess impact of each change
3. Prepare response positions
4. Obtain necessary approvals
5. Return response promptly

**Final Review:**

1. Ensure all negotiated terms incorporated
2. Verify all exhibits and attachments complete
3. Confirm approval signatures obtained
4. Check formatting and pagination
5. Prepare execution copies

**Signature Process:**

1. Route to authorized signatories
2. Verify signature authority
3. Execute via e-signature or wet signature
4. Confirm fully executed copies received
5. Distribute executed copies to stakeholders

**Post-Execution:**

1. Upload to contract repository
2. Extract key terms and dates
3. Set up obligation tracking
4. Schedule renewal/termination reminders
5. Notify relevant departments
