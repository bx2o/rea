## 0.3 POPIA (South Africa) Compliance Framework

**Legal Basis for Processing:**

- **Consent:** Explicit consent for marketing, cookies, optional profiling
- **Contract:** Processing necessary for service provision (account operation)
- **Legal Obligation:** FICA compliance, tax reporting, regulatory requirements
- **Legitimate Interests:** Fraud prevention, network security, service improvement
- **Public Interest:** Law enforcement cooperation, financial crime prevention

**Data Subject Rights:**

- **Access (Section 23):** Right to request personal information held, 30-day response
- **Correction (Section 24):** Right to request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion (Section 25):** Right to request destruction/ deidentification (with exceptions)
- **Objection (Section 11):** Right to object to processing for direct marketing
- **Complaint (Section 74):** Right to lodge complaints with Information Regulator

**Accountability Requirements:**

- **Information Officer:** Mandatory appointment, registration with Regulator, oversight duties
- **PAIA Manual:** Publication of access procedures, data categories, retention periods
- **Processing Records:** Documentation of processing activities (Section 17 records)
- **Impact Assessments:** Prior assessment for high-risk processing (DPIAs)
- **Security Safeguards:** Appropriate technical and organizational measures (Section 19)

**Breach Notification:**

- **72-Hour Rule:** Notification to Regulator within 72 hours of awareness
- **Data Subject Notification:** Required if high risk to rights and freedoms
- **Breach Register:** Documentation of all breaches, regardless of notification requirement
- **Remediation:** Steps taken to mitigate harm, prevent recurrence

## 0.4 Blockchain & Privacy Tensions

**Immutability vs. Data Subject Rights:**

- **Right to Deletion:** Technical impossibility of deleting blockchain data vs. legal requirement
- **Mitigation Strategies:** Off-chain storage of PII, on-chain only pseudonymous identifiers
- **Encryption:** Encrypting sensitive data before chain storage, key management for "deletion"
- **Zero-Knowledge Proofs:** Proving facts without revealing underlying personal data
- **Regulatory Position:** Engaging with regulators on technical constraints, seeking guidance

**Pseudonymization Challenges:**

- **Wallet Addresses:** Pseudonymous but potentially re-identifiable through transaction analysis
- **Chain Analysis:** Third-party blockchain analytics linking wallets to identities
- **Re-identification Risks:** Combining on-chain data with off-chain KYC information
- **Privacy Coins:** Monero, Zcash integration considerations for enhanced privacy
- **Mixers & Tornado Cash:** Regulatory scrutiny of privacy-enhancing technologies

**Smart Contract Data:**

- **Automated Processing:** Smart contracts as automated decision-making under GDPR/POPIA
- **Transparency:** Explaining automated logic to users, algorithmic accountability
- **Controller/Processor:** Determining roles in decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols
- **Governance Data:** DAO voting records, governance token holder information

**NFT Privacy Issues:**

- **Creator Identity:** Pseudonymous creators vs. verified identity requirements
- **Ownership Transparency:** Public ownership records vs. privacy expectations
- **Phygital Links:** Connecting physical identity to digital ownership
- **Royalty Payments:** Payment trail linking to creator identities

## SOP 1: Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA)

**Duration:** 1-2 weeks | **Frequency:** Per new product/feature

1. **Scope:** Identify data processing activities
2. **Data mapping:** What data, where stored, who accesses
3. **Risk assessment:** Identify privacy risks
4. **Mitigation:** Design controls to address risks
5. **Documentation:** Complete PIA report
6. **Review:** Legal and security sign-off
7. **Approval:** Privacy officer approval
8. **Monitoring:** Ongoing compliance checks

## SOP 2: Data Subject Request (DSR) Handling

**Duration:** 1-30 days | **Frequency:** Per request

1. **Intake:** Receive and log request
2. **Verification:** Confirm requestor identity
3. **Scope:** Clarify request specifics
4. **Gather:** Collect relevant data
5. **Review:** Legal/HR review if needed
6. **Respond:** Provide data or action
7. **Document:** Record response
8. **Close:** Complete and file

## SOP 3: Breach Response Protocol

**Duration:** Immediate | **Frequency:** As needed

1. **Detect:** Identify potential breach
2. **Assess:** Severity and scope
3. **Contain:** Stop ongoing exposure
4. **Notify:** Internal stakeholders
5. **Evaluate:** Notification obligations
6. **Report:** To regulators if required
7. **Remediate:** Fix vulnerability
8. **Document:** Full incident record
