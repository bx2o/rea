# SECTION 4: FIFTY+ FAQs

**Q1: How do you estimate TAM (Total Addressable Market)?**
A: Approaches: (1) **Top-down:** Start with large market (e.g., "software market" = $500B). Drill down by segment (CRM = $50B). Estimate company's addressable market within segment. (2) **Bottom-up:** Count target customers. Estimate revenue per customer. Multiply customers × ACV = TAM. (3) **Analogy:** Similar market in different geography/stage. Example: USA CRM market $30B; Europe likely $15-20B. Methods: Combine approaches for validation. Sanity check: Does TAM make sense given company growth ambitions? Example: $1M company in $500B TAM is totally reasonable (lots of room to grow).

**Q2: How do you analyze competitors?**
A: Framework: (1) **Product features:** What do they offer vs. us? (2) **Pricing:** How much do they charge? (3) **Target customer:** Who do they sell to? (4) **Go-to-market:** How do they acquire customers? (5) **Partnerships:** Who do they partner with? (6) **Financial:** Funding, revenue trajectory (public data). (7) **Positioning:** What's their value proposition? (8) **Weaknesses:** Where are they weak? Tools: Product trial, customer interviews, LinkedIn, press releases, industry analyst reports. Frequency: Quarterly update.

**Q3: What's competitive positioning and how do you develop it?**
A: Positioning = how you want customers to perceive you vs. competitors. Process: (1) **Map market:** Plot competitors on 2 dimensions (price vs. features, easy vs. powerful, etc.). (2) **Identify gap:** Where's white space? (3) **Choose position:** "We're the simplest" or "We're the most powerful" or "We're cheapest." (4) **Message:** Develop messaging supporting position. (5) **Validate:** Test with customers (does it resonate?). (6) **Differentiate:** Make sure your positioning is defensible (hard for competitors to copy). Example: Competitors = complex software. Opportunity = position as "simple CRM." Messaging supports this differentiation.

**Q4: How do you do customer research/interviews?**
A: Process: (1) **Choose participants:** Current customers, lost customers, non-customers. Mix across segments. (2) **Prepare:** Script of open-ended questions. (3) **Interview:** 30-60 min conversations. Let them talk; don't lead. (4) **Document:** Take notes/record. (5) **Analyze:** Look for patterns in responses. (6) **Share:** Report findings + recommendations. Ideal: 10-15 customer interviews for good signal. Cadence: Continuous (always learning from customers). Tools: Google Meet, Dovetail (customer research platform).

**Q5: How often should you update market analysis?**
A: Cadence: (1) **Competitive monitoring:** Monthly (track competitor moves). (2) **Market trends:** Quarterly (identify emerging trends). (3) **Customer insights:** Continuous (always interviewing customers). (4) **TAM/opportunity:** Annually (large markets don't change fast). (5) **Strategic positioning:** Annually (validate position still resonates). Annual strategic review: Comprehensive market analysis + competitive landscape + customer needs + positioning validation.
