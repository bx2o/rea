# SECTION 4: FIFTY+ FAQS, SKILLS & KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY

## 4.1 Fifty+ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### **Category 1: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE (14 FAQs)**

**Q1: What's competitive intelligence and how do you gather it?**
A: Intelligence: Information about competitors (products, pricing, strategy, hiring, partnerships). Gathering: (1) **Public sources:** News, press releases, websites, social media. (2) **Customer feedback:** What do customers say about competitors? (3) **Job postings:** What's competitor hiring for? (4) **Analyst reports:** Industry analysts track competitors. (5) **Sales conversations:** What do prospects say about competitive options? (6) **Trade shows:** Attend events, watch competitor presence. (7) **Patent filings:** What's competitor innovating on? (8) **Earnings calls:** Public companies share strategy in earnings calls. Legal: Stick to public information. Never illegal surveillance.

**Q2: What's a competitive battle card and when do you use one?**
A: Card: 1-2 page guide for sales team when facing specific competitor. Contents: (1) **Competitor overview:** Who are they? (2) **Strengths:** What's their competitive advantage? (3) **Weaknesses:** Where vulnerable? (4) **Positioning:** How do they position vs. us? (5) **Our advantages:** Why we're better. (6) **Messaging:** How to position when competing? (7) **Customer references:** Case studies/references vs. competitor. (8) **Pricing comparison:** How do prices compare? Use: Sales team uses when competing head-to-head. Updates: Quarterly as competitive landscape changes. Tools: Usually battle card template in sales wiki.

**Q3: How do you assess competitive threats (which competitors matter most)?**
A: Approach: (1) **Direct competitors:** Same product, same market. (2) **Adjacent competitors:** Similar product, adjacent market. (3) **Potential competitors:** Could enter market. (4) **Threat assessment:** Which most dangerous? Why? (5) **Impact:** If threat materializes, impact to us? (6) **Probability:** How likely? (7) **Timeline:** How soon? (8) **Response:** What should we do? Framework: Plot by threat level (impact × probability). Focus: Highest threat competitors. Monitor: Continuously as market evolves.

**Q4: What's win/loss analysis and how do you conduct it?**
A: Analysis: When you win/lose deal to competitor, analyze why. Process: (1) **Win:** Customer chose us. Why? (2) **Loss:** Customer chose competitor. Why? (3) **Interview:** Talk to customer about decision. (4) **Themes:** Aggregate findings (what themes emerge?). (5) **Insights:** What are we learning? (6) **Response:** How should we change based on learnings? Frequency: Analyze all major deals. Timeline: Interview within 30 days of deal close (while fresh). Value: Win/loss analysis is gold for strategy/positioning.

**Q5: How do you monitor social media for competitive intelligence?**
A: Approach: (1) **Set up monitoring:** Tools like Brandwatch, Mention track mentions. (2) **Keywords:** Monitor company name, product names, competitors. (3) **Sentiment:** Track positive/negative mentions. (4) **Trends:** Are mentions increasing/decreasing? Why? (5) **Sources:** Blog posts, news, social media, forums. (6) **Alerts:** Set alerts for competitive moves, brand mentions. (7) **Analysis:** Weekly/monthly review of findings. Tools: Brandwatch, Mention, Google Alerts, Twitter Advanced Search. Cost: $500-5K/month depending on tool. Value: Spot trends early, understand customer sentiment.
