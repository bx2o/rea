# SECTION 4: FIFTY+ FAQS, SKILLS & KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY

## 4.1 Fifty+ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### **Category 1: MARKET ANALYSIS (14 FAQs)**

**Q1: How do you conduct market analysis?**
A: Process: (1) **Market size:** Total addressable market (TAM). How big is market? (2) **Market trends:** Is market growing? Shrinking? Why? (3) **Customer segments:** Who's buying? Different customer types? (4) **Needs/pain points:** What problems are customers solving? (5) **Competitive landscape:** Who else serves market? How many competitors? (6) **Pricing:** What do customers pay? Price sensitivity? (7) **Growth drivers:** What's driving growth? (8) **Barriers to entry:** How hard to enter market? Moats? Output: Market analysis report (10-20 pages). Timeline: 4-8 weeks for thorough analysis.

**Q2: What's TAM (Total Addressable Market) and how do you estimate it?**
A: TAM: Total revenue available if company captured 100% of market. Methods: (1) **Top-down:** Industry analysts estimate market size. (2) **Bottom-up:** Calculate: (# potential customers) × (average revenue per customer). (3) **Value-based:** Value of problem solved × # of people with problem. Example: If market is enterprise software ($50B TAM) and company captures 1%, revenue = $500M. Why matters: Large TAM = big opportunity; small TAM = limited upside. Reality: TAM estimates vary widely (analyst vs. bottom-up). Use multiple methods, compare.

**Q3: How do you analyze competitors?**
A: Approach: (1) **Identify:** Who are competitors? Direct (same product) and indirect (solve problem differently). (2) **Strengths:** What are they good at? Why winning? (3) **Weaknesses:** What vulnerabilities? (4) **Positioning:** How do they position vs. each other? (5) **Pricing:** What do they charge? (6) **Strategy:** What are they investing in? (7) **Trajectory:** Growing or declining? (8) **Threats:** Are they threats to us? Why? Output: Competitive analysis. Frequency: Quarterly update. Tools: G2, Crunchbase, industry report, customer conversations.

**Q4: What's SWOT analysis?**
A: Framework: (1) **Strengths:** What are company's advantages? (technology, brand, team, network). (2) **Weaknesses:** Disadvantages? (limited resources, inexperience). (3) **Opportunities:** Positive external factors (market growth, competitor vulnerability, tech change). (4) **Threats:** Negative external factors (new competitor, market decline, regulation). Purpose: Understand competitive position. Usage: Strategic planning, board presentations. Format: 2×2 matrix. Frequency: Annual or when strategy changes. Caution: Can be superficial; deeper analysis needed.

**Q5: How do you conduct customer analysis?**
A: Approach: (1) **Segment:** Who are different customer types? (2) **Size:** How many in each segment? (3) **Needs:** What does each segment need? (4) **Behavior:** How do they buy? (5) **Value perception:** What do they value? Price sensitivity? (6) **Satisfaction:** How satisfied? Retention? (7) **Trends:** Are segments changing? Growing/shrinking? (8) **Opportunities:** Which segments have most opportunity? Methods: Surveys, interviews, data analysis. Output: Customer analysis report. Usage: Product strategy, marketing strategy, sales strategy.

**Q6: What's a business case and when do you need one?**
A: Business case: Formal justification for investment (new product, expansion, acquisition). Contents: (1) **Opportunity:** What are we pursuing? Why? (2) **Investment:** How much does it cost? (3) **Benefits:** What revenue/savings? (4) **Timeline:** When realized? (5) **Risks:** What could go wrong? (6) **Alternatives:** Other options considered? Why this one? (7) **Break-even:** When does it pay for itself? (8) **Recommendation:** Proceed or not? Format: 10-20 page document + financial model. Use: Getting approval for resource allocation. Timeline: 2-4 weeks to develop.
