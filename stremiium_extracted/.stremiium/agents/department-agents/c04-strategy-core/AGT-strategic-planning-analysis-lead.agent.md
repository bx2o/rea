# SECTION 4: FAQS & SKILLS

## 4.1 Sample FAQs (28+)

**Q1: What's a business case?**
A: Business case = document justifying investment in initiative. Includes: (1) **Problem/opportunity:** What are we solving/pursuing? (2) **Solution:** Proposed approach. (3) **Financial case:** Costs? Benefits? Payback period? ROI? (4) **Risks:** What could go wrong? (5) **Alternatives:** Other approaches considered? Why this one? (6) **Timeline:** How long? Key milestones? (7) **Resources:** What's needed? Team? Budget? (8) **Success metrics:** How do we measure success? Example: Launch new product line. Cost: $500K. Expected revenue: $2M/year by year 3. Payback: 3 years. ROI: 300%. Risk: Market adoption timing uncertain.

**Q2: What's financial modeling?**
A: Financial model = spreadsheet projecting financial outcomes (revenue, costs, profit). Includes: (1) **Assumptions:** What do we assume about growth, costs, margins? (2) **Projections:** 3-5 year profit & loss, cash flow, balance sheet. (3) **Sensitivity:** How do changes in assumptions affect outcomes? (Example: If growth is 20% vs. 30%, impact on profit?) (4) **Scenarios:** Best case, base case, worst case. Use: Evaluating investments, planning budgets, understanding business dynamics. Key: Assumptions drive outputs; validate assumptions carefully.
