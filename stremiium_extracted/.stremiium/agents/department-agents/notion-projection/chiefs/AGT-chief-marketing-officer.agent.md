# SECTION 4: KEY FAQS & KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY

## 4.1 Fifty+ Frequently Asked Questions

### Category 1: BRAND & POSITIONING (10 FAQs)

**Q1: How do you define a strong brand position?**
A: Strong brand position requires: (1) **Differentiation:** Clear unique value vs. competitors. (2) **Relevance:** Addresses customer pain points. (3) **Credibility:** Believable claims backed by proof. (4) **Sustainability:** Defensible over time. (5) **Clarity:** Easy to understand and remember. Framework: Who we are → What we do → How we're different → Why it matters.

**Q2: What makes a messaging framework effective?**
A: Effective messaging: (1) **Audience-centric:** Addresses customer needs, not product features. (2) **Consistent:** Same message across all channels. (3) **Hierarchical:** Company → Solution → Product level. (4) **Proof-based:** Backed by evidence. (5) **Flexible:** Adaptable for different contexts. Structure: Core message → Supporting points → Proof points → Differentiators.

**Q3: How do you maintain brand consistency across teams?**
A: Consistency requires: (1) **Brand guidelines:** Documented standards for all elements. (2) **Templates:** Pre-approved formats for common materials. (3) **Training:** Regular brand education for all teams. (4) **Review process:** Brand approval for external materials. (5) **Tools:** Brand management platforms. (6) **Governance:** Brand council or owner.

**Q4: When should you rebrand vs. refresh?**
A: **Refresh** (evolution): Brand is recognized but feels dated, minor positioning shifts, visual modernization. **Rebrand** (revolution): Merger/acquisition, major strategic pivot, brand damage, entering new markets, significant audience change. Refresh: Lower risk, faster, less cost. Rebrand: Higher risk, longer, more investment.

**Q5: How do you measure brand health?**
A: Metrics: (1) **Awareness:** Unaided and aided recall. (2) **Perception:** Brand attributes and positioning. (3) **Sentiment:** Positive/negative mentions. (4) **Engagement:** Brand interaction metrics. (5) **Preference:** Choice vs. competitors. (6) **Loyalty:** NPS, repeat purchase. (7) **Equity:** Financial brand value. Tools: Brand tracking surveys, social listening, web analytics.

**Q6: How do you position against a dominant competitor?**
A: Strategies: (1) **Differentiation:** Own a different attribute. (2) **Niche:** Focus on underserved segment. (3) **Innovation:** Lead on new dimension. (4) ** challenger:** Attack weakness. (5) **Reframe:** Change evaluation criteria. Never compete head-to-head on their strength. Find your unique value wedge.

**Q7: What's the difference between brand and reputation?**
A: **Brand:** Intentional positioning - who we say we are. **Reputation:** Perceived reality - who stakeholders think we are. Brand is built through marketing. Reputation is earned through actions. Ideal: Brand promise = Reputation reality. Gap = brand risk. Manage both: deliver on brand promise.

**Q8: How do you build brand authenticity?**
A: Authenticity comes from: (1) **Truth:** Claims backed by reality. (2) **Consistency:** Actions match words. (3) **Transparency:** Open about imperfections. (4) **Values:** Stand for something real. (5) **Voice:** Human, not corporate-speak. (6) **Purpose:** Beyond profit motivation. Test: Would employees and customers endorse the brand?

**Q9: How do you adapt brand for different markets?**
A: Global brand strategy: (1) **Core elements:** Consistent globally (positioning, values, visual identity). (2) **Local adaptation:** Tailored locally (language, cultural references, channels, messaging emphasis). (3) **Balance:** Think global, act local. (4) **Governance:** Central brand team with local input. (5) **Flexibility:** Allow local market needs within brand framework.

**Q10: What role does storytelling play in branding?**
A: Storytelling transforms features into meaning: (1) **Emotional connection:** Stories engage emotions, not just logic. (2) **Memorability:** Stories stick better than facts. (3) **Differentiation:** Unique story vs. commodity features. (4) **Purpose:** Why we exist beyond what we sell. (5) **Community:** Shared narrative creates belonging. Structure: Hero (customer) → Challenge (problem) → Guide (brand) → Solution (product) → Success (outcome).

### Category 2: DEMAND GENERATION (10 FAQs)

**Q11: What's the difference between lead generation and demand generation?**
A: **Lead generation:** Capture existing demand (people already looking). Tactics: SEO, SEM, content downloads. **Demand generation:** Create new demand (people not yet looking). Tactics: Thought leadership, education, brand awareness. Both needed: Create demand → Capture demand → Convert demand. Funnel: Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Intent → Purchase.

**Q12: How do you build an effective lead scoring model?**
A: Lead scoring components: (1) **Demographic fit:** Title, company size, industry. (2) **Behavioral engagement:** Website visits, content downloads, email opens. (3) **Buying stage:** Educational vs. decision content. (4) **Intent signals:** Pricing page visits, demo requests. (5) **Recency:** Recent activity weighted higher. Model: Point-based (0-100) or matrix (fit × engagement). Review: Quarterly optimization.

**Q13: What makes a landing page convert effectively?**
A: High-converting landing pages: (1) **Clear value prop:** Above-fold headline stating benefit. (2) **Relevant imagery:** Visual proof or context. (3) **Single CTA:** One clear action. (4) **Form optimization:** Minimum fields, progressive profiling. (5) **Social proof:** Testimonials, logos, stats. (6) **Trust elements:** Security badges, privacy assurances. (7) **Mobile optimized:** Responsive design. (8) **A/B testing:** Continuous optimization.

**Q14: How do you align marketing and sales on lead quality?**
A: Alignment requires: (1) **Shared definitions:** MQL, SQL, opportunity criteria. (2) **Service level agreement:** Response time, lead handling. (3) **Regular meetings:** Weekly pipeline review, feedback loop. (4) **Closed-loop reporting:** Which leads convert to revenue. (5) **Joint KPIs:** Shared pipeline contribution goals. (6) **Lead feedback:** Sales marks lead quality, marketing adjusts.

**Q15: What's the optimal marketing channel mix?**
A: Channel mix depends on: (1) **Audience:** Where do they spend time? (2) **Buying stage:** Awareness vs. decision channels. (3) **Resources:** Budget, team capacity. (4) **Attribution:** Which channels drive conversion? Typical B2B mix: Content/SEO (30%), Paid digital (25%), Events (20%), Email (15%), PR/Partners (10%). Test and optimize continuously.

**Q16: How do you measure marketing attribution?**
A: Attribution models: (1) **First-touch:** Credits first interaction. (2) **Last-touch:** Credits last interaction. (3) **Linear:** Equal credit to all touchpoints. (4) **Time-decay:** More credit to recent touchpoints. (5) **Position-based:** Credit to first and last, some to middle. (6) **Data-driven:** Algorithmic based on actual data. Best: Multi-touch attribution for complex B2B journeys.

**Q17: What are the key metrics for demand generation?**
A: Funnel metrics: (1) **Top:** Impressions, reach, engagement rate. (2) **Middle:** MQLs, cost per MQL, MQL quality score. (3) **Bottom:** SQLs, opportunities, pipeline, revenue. (4) **Efficiency:** CAC, LTV:CAC ratio, marketing ROI. (5) **Velocity:** Time from lead to opportunity to close. Dashboard: Weekly funnel review.

**Q18: How do you optimize email marketing performance?**
A: Email optimization: (1) **List hygiene:** Clean inactive subscribers. (2) **Segmentation:** Relevant content to relevant people. (3) **Personalization:** Beyond first name (behavior, preferences). (4) **Subject lines:** Test curiosity, urgency, benefit. (5) **Send time:** Test optimal timing by segment. (6) **Mobile:** 60%+ opened on mobile. (7) **CTA:** Single, clear action. Benchmarks: 20%+ open, 3%+ click, <0.5% unsubscribe.

**Q19: What's the role of account-based marketing (ABM)?**
A: ABM is strategic approach: (1) **Targeted:** Focus on high-value accounts, not broad market. (2) **Personalized:** Tailored messaging per account. (3) **Coordinated:** Marketing and sales aligned. (4) **Multi-channel:** Orchestrated touchpoints. (5) **Measured:** Account-level metrics. When to use: Enterprise deals, concentrated market, complex sales cycle.

**Q20: How do you use marketing automation effectively?**
A: Marketing automation best practices: (1) **Strategy first:** Automation amplifies strategy, doesn't replace it. (2) **Segmentation:** Different paths for different personas. (3) **Personalization:** Dynamic content based on behavior/data. (4) **Lead nurturing:** Timely, relevant follow-up sequences. (5) **Scoring:** Automated lead qualification. (6) **Sales alerts:** Notify on high-value actions. (7) **Testing:** A/B test everything. Tools: HubSpot, Marketo, Pardot.

### Category 3: PUBLIC RELATIONS & COMMUNICATIONS (10 FAQs)

**Q21: How do you build relationships with journalists?**
A: Media relations: (1) **Research:** Know their beat and recent articles. (2) **Relevance:** Pitch stories that fit their audience. (3) **Timing:** Respect deadlines and lead times. (4) **Value:** Offer exclusive data, expert access, customer stories. (5) **Relationships:** Build before you need them. (6) **Responsiveness:** Quick replies, available sources. (7) **Follow-up:** Thank you, share coverage, maintain contact.

**Q22: What makes a press release effective?**
A: Effective press releases: (1) **Newsworthy:** Actually news, not promotion. (2) **Lead paragraph:** Who, what, when, where, why. (3) **Quotes:** Credible spokespeople with meaningful quotes. (4) **Data:** Supporting statistics or proof points. (5) **Boilerplate:** Brief company description. (6) **Contact:** Media contact with phone/email. (7) **Multimedia:** Images, video if relevant. (8) **SEO:** Keywords in headline and body.

**Q23: How do you handle a PR crisis?**
A: Crisis response: (1) **Speed:** Respond quickly (first 24 hours critical). (2) **Facts:** Gather truth before responding. (3) **Empathy:** Acknowledge impact on stakeholders. (4) **Action:** State what you're doing. (5) **Transparency:** Be honest about what you know/don't know. (6) **Spokesperson:** Trained, authorized communicator. (7) **Monitoring:** Track coverage and sentiment. (8) **Follow-up:** Update as situation evolves. Never: Hide, blame, speculate, or delay.

**Q24: How do you establish thought leadership?**
A: Thought leadership building: (1) **Expertise:** Deep knowledge in specific domain. (2) **Point of view:** Unique perspective, not generic. (3) **Content:** Publish insights regularly (blog, whitepapers, research). (4) **Speaking:** Industry conferences and events. (5) **Media:** Contributed articles, interviews. (6) **Social:** Active LinkedIn/Twitter presence. (7) **Community:** Engage in industry discussions. Authenticity: Share real expertise, not promotional content.

**Q25: What's the difference between PR and marketing?**
A: **Marketing:** Paid, controlled, promotional, drives demand. **PR:** Earned, third-party validated, credibility-building, shapes perception. Integration: PR creates awareness and credibility → Marketing captures and nurtures → Sales converts. PR is long-term reputation; marketing is shorter-term demand.

**Q26: How do you measure PR effectiveness?**
A: PR metrics: (1) **Outputs:** Press releases, pitches, events. (2) **Outtakes:** Coverage volume, reach, sentiment. (3) **Outcomes:** Share of voice, message pull-through, audience perception. (4) **Business impact:** Website traffic from PR, lead generation, pipeline influence. (5) ** AVE alternatives:** Multiplier method, target audience reach, quality scoring. Best: Tie PR to business outcomes, not just coverage volume.

**Q27: How do you prepare executives for media interviews?**
A: Media training: (1) **Key messages:** 3-5 core points to communicate. (2) **Bridging:** Techniques to redirect to key messages. (3) **Proof points:** Data, examples, stories. (4) **Difficult questions:** Practice tough Q&A. (5) **Body language:** Confidence, eye contact, posture. (6) **Sound bites:** Memorable, quotable phrases. (7) **Mock interviews:** Practice with realistic scenarios. (8) **Briefing:** Reporter background, angle, recent work.

**Q28: How do you leverage speaking opportunities?**
A: Speaking strategy: (1) **Target selection:** Right audience, right stage. (2) **Proposal:** Compelling topic, unique angle. (3) **Content:** Educational, not promotional. (4) **Preparation:** Rehearse, know your material. (5) **Engagement:** Interactive, stories, visuals. (6) **Follow-up:** Share slides, connect with attendees. (7) **Amplification:** Record, blog, social. ROI: Leads, awareness, positioning.

**Q29: How do you manage internal communications?**
A: Internal communications: (1) **Strategy:** Align with business goals. (2) **Channels:** All-hands, email, intranet, Slack. (3) **Cadence:** Regular rhythm (weekly, monthly, quarterly). (4) **Content:** News, strategy, recognition, culture. (5) **Two-way:** Feedback mechanisms, Q&A. (6) **Crisis:** Rapid, clear internal-first communication. (7) **Culture:** Reinforce values and behaviors. (8) **Measurement:** Engagement, sentiment, understanding.

**Q30: How do you handle negative reviews or comments?**
A: Negative response strategy: (1) **Monitor:** Track mentions and reviews. (2) **Speed:** Respond quickly (24 hours). (3) **Empathy:** Acknowledge their experience. (4) **Apologize:** If wrong, say so sincerely. (5) **Action:** State what you'll do. (6) **Offline:** Move detailed resolution private. (7) **Follow-up:** Ensure resolution. (8) **Learn:** Fix systemic issues. Never: Delete (unless abusive), argue, ignore, or be defensive.

### Category 4: DIGITAL MARKETING (10 FAQs)

**Q31: What's the difference between SEO and SEM?**
A: **SEO (Search Engine Optimization):** Organic ranking improvement. Free traffic, long-term, content-focused, technical optimization. **SEM (Search Engine Marketing):** Paid search advertising. Immediate results, pay-per-click, keyword bidding, budget controlled. Together: SEO for sustainable growth, SEM for immediate visibility. Both target searchers with intent.

**Q32: How do you create an effective content strategy?**
A: Content strategy components: (1) **Objectives:** Awareness, consideration, conversion. (2) **Audience:** Personas and their needs. (3) **Topics:** Themes aligned to buyer journey. (4) **Formats:** Blog, video, podcast, interactive. (5) **Channels:** Where to distribute. (6) **Calendar:** Publishing schedule. (7) **Resources:** Who creates, reviews, publishes. (8) **Measurement:** KPIs and optimization. Pillars: Pillar content → Cluster content → Distribution.

**Q33: How do you optimize for search engines (SEO)?**
A: SEO optimization: (1) **Technical:** Site speed, mobile, crawlability, indexation. (2) **On-page:** Keywords in title, headers, content, meta. (3) **Content:** Quality, depth, freshness, relevance. (4) **Off-page:** Backlinks from authoritative sites. (5) **Local:** Google Business Profile, local citations. (6) **User signals:** CTR, dwell time, bounce rate. (7) **Schema markup:** Structured data. Timeframe: 6-12 months for significant results.

**Q34: What's effective social media marketing?**
A: Social media strategy: (1) **Platform selection:** Where your audience is active. (2) **Content mix:** Educational, entertaining, promotional (80/20 rule). (3) **Consistency:** Regular posting schedule. (4) **Engagement:** Respond to comments and messages. (5) **Visuals:** High-quality images and video. (6) **Hashtags:** Relevant, researched. (7) **Paid:** Boost high-performing organic content. (8) **Analytics:** Track and optimize. B2B focus: LinkedIn, Twitter. B2C: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook.

**Q35: How do you use paid advertising effectively?**
A: Paid advertising best practices: (1) **Objectives:** Brand awareness, consideration, conversion. (2) **Targeting:** Demographics, interests, behavior, lookalikes. (3) **Creative:** Compelling visuals and copy. (4) **Landing pages:** Relevant, optimized conversion. (5) **Testing:** A/B test creative, targeting, bidding. (6) **Budget:** Start small, scale winners. (7) **Frequency:** Cap to avoid fatigue. (8) **Measurement:** ROAS, CPA, conversion rate. Platform selection based on audience and objectives.

**Q36: How do you measure content marketing ROI?**
A: Content ROI measurement: (1) **Consumption:** Views, downloads, time on page. (2) **Engagement:** Shares, comments, likes. (3) **Leads:** Form fills, subscriptions. (4) **Pipeline:** Opportunities influenced by content. (5) **Revenue:** Deals closed with content touch. (6) **Cost:** Production and distribution costs. Formula: (Revenue attributed - Cost) / Cost. Attribution models: First-touch, last-touch, multi-touch.

**Q37: What makes video marketing effective?**
A: Video best practices: (1) **Hook:** Capture attention in first 3 seconds. (2) **Length:** Match platform (15s TikTok, 2min LinkedIn, 10min YouTube). (3) **Subtitles:** 80% watch without sound. (4) **Story:** Narrative arc, not just features. (5) **Quality:** Good lighting and audio essential. (6) **CTA:** Clear next step. (7) **Thumbnail:** Compelling preview image. (8) **SEO:** Title, description, tags. Types: Explainer, testimonial, demo, behind-the-scenes, thought leadership.

**Q38: How do you optimize website conversion?**
A: CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization): (1) **Data:** Analytics, heatmaps, session recordings. (2) **Funnel analysis:** Where do visitors drop off? (3) **Hypothesis:** Why aren't they converting? (4) **Testing:** A/B test changes. (5) **Priority:** Impact vs. effort matrix. (6) **Iterate:** Continuous improvement. Elements to test: Headlines, CTAs, forms, images, layout, social proof, urgency. Best practices: Clear value prop, single CTA, remove friction, build trust.

**Q39: How do you use influencer marketing?**
A: Influencer strategy: (1) **Selection:** Relevance over reach, authentic alignment. (2) **Tiers:** Nano (1K-10K), Micro (10K-100K), Macro (100K-1M), Mega (1M+). (3) **Compensation:** Cash, product, commission, equity. (4) **Brief:** Clear guidelines but creative freedom. (5) **Disclosure:** FTC compliance required. (6) **Content rights:** Usage rights in contract. (7) **Measurement:** Engagement, reach, conversions. (8) **Long-term:** Build relationships, not one-off transactions.

**Q40: How do you leverage marketing analytics?**
A: Marketing analytics framework: (1) **Data collection:** Website, CRM, email, ads, social. (2) **Integration:** Single source of truth (data warehouse). (3) **Attribution:** Understand touchpoint value. (4) **Dashboards:** Real-time visibility for stakeholders. (5) **Insights:** Analysis beyond reporting. (6) **Action:** Data-driven decisions. (7) **Testing:** Experiment and learn. (8) **Privacy:** Compliant data practices. Tools: Google Analytics, HubSpot, Tableau, Looker.

### Category 5: MARKETING OPERATIONS & STRATEGY (10 FAQs)

**Q41: How do you build a marketing budget?**
A: Budget building approaches: (1) **Percentage of revenue:** 5-15% typical for B2B. (2) **Objective-based:** Cost to achieve goals. (3) **Competitive parity:** Match competitor spend. (4) **Zero-based:** Justify every dollar. Categories: Personnel (50-60%), Programs (30-40%), Technology (10%), Contingency (5%). Allocation by funnel stage: Awareness (40%), Consideration (30%), Conversion (30%). Review: Quarterly reallocation based on performance.

**Q42: What's the role of marketing operations?**
A: Marketing operations: (1) **Technology:** Martech stack management. (2) **Data:** Database management, hygiene, segmentation. (3) **Process:** Workflow optimization. (4) **Analytics:** Measurement and reporting. (5) **Governance:** Compliance, standards. (6) **Enablement:** Support for marketing teams. (7) **Planning:** Budget, forecasting. Value: Efficiency, scale, measurement, alignment.

**Q43: How do you select marketing technology?**
A: Martech selection criteria: (1) **Requirements:** Must-have vs. nice-to-have. (2) **Integration:** Compatibility with existing stack. (3) **Usability:** Adoption by team. (4) **Scalability:** Growth accommodation. (5) **Vendor:** Stability, support, roadmap. (6) **TCO:** Total cost including implementation. (7) **ROI:** Value vs. cost. (8) **Security:** Data protection. Categories: CRM, MAP, CMS, Analytics, Social, SEO, ABM, BI.

**Q44: How do you manage marketing compliance?**
A: Compliance areas: (1) **GDPR/CCPA:** Consent, data rights, privacy. (2) **CAN-SPAM:** Email unsubscribe, identification. (3) **Advertising:** Truth in advertising, disclosures. (4) **Accessibility:** ADA compliance for digital. (5) **Intellectual property:** Trademark, copyright. (6) **Industry-specific:** Healthcare (HIPAA), finance (FINRA). Process: Legal review, training, monitoring, documentation.

**Q45: How do you structure a marketing team?**
A: Team structure models: (1) **Functional:** SEO, content, demand gen, product marketing. (2) **Segment:** Enterprise, SMB, verticals. (3) **Product:** Per product line. (4) **Geographic:** Regional teams. (5) **Hybrid:** Combination. Typical B2B: Brand/content (2-3), Demand gen (2-3), Product marketing (1-2), Ops (1-2), Design (1-2). Scale: Startup (3-5), Growth (10-20), Enterprise (50+).

**Q46: How do you forecast marketing performance?**
A: Forecasting methods: (1) **Historical:** Past performance adjusted for changes. (2) **Pipeline:** Current funnel × conversion rates. (3) **Campaign:** Sum of planned initiative impacts. (4) **Driver-based:** Key metrics that drive outcomes. (5) **Scenario:** Best case, expected, worst case. Accuracy: Improve over time with data. Review: Weekly actuals vs. forecast, adjust tactics.

**Q47: How do you align marketing with company strategy?**
A: Strategic alignment: (1) **OKRs:** Marketing objectives linked to company goals. (2) **Prioritization:** Focus on strategic initiatives. (3) **Communication:** Regular updates to leadership. (4) **Metrics:** Report on business impact, not just activity. (5) **Planning:** Annual marketing plan aligned to company plan. (6) **Feedback:** Input into company strategy. (7) **Agility:** Adapt as strategy evolves.

**Q48: How do you build marketing culture?**
A: Marketing culture elements: (1) **Customer-centric:** Obsess over customer needs. (2) **Data-driven:** Decisions based on evidence. (3) **Experimentation:** Test and learn mindset. (4) **Collaboration:** Cross-functional partnership. (5) **Innovation:** Embrace new channels and tactics. (6) **Accountability:** Own results. (7) **Learning:** Continuous skill development. (8) **Fun:** Enjoy the work.

**Q49: How do you handle marketing during economic downturn?**
A: Recession marketing strategy: (1) **Maintain presence:** Brands that maintain or increase spend recover faster. (2) **Shift focus:** Efficiency, ROI, value messaging. (3) **Optimize:** Cut underperforming channels. (4) **Innovate:** New channels often cheaper. (5) **Customer retention:** Keep existing customers. (6) **Share of voice:** Capture competitors' lost presence. (7) **Automation:** Do more with less. (8) **Measurement:** Prove every dollar's impact.

**Q50: How do you evolve marketing as company scales?**
A: Marketing evolution by stage: (1) **Startup (1-10 employees):** Founder-led, organic, PR. (2) **Early (10-50):** First marketers, basic automation, content. (3) **Growth (50-200):** Specialized roles, paid acquisition, events. (4) **Scale (200-1000):** Full functions, international, enterprise marketing. (5) **Enterprise (1000+):** Global teams, ABM, brand, sophisticated ops. Adapt: Structure, process, technology, skills.

# SECTION 5: SOPs & STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

## SOP 1: Campaign Planning & Execution

**Duration:** 4-8 weeks | **Frequency:** Quarterly or per initiative | **Owner:** CMO

1. **Strategy:** Define objectives, audience, messaging
2. **Planning:** Budget, timeline, resources, channels
3. **Creative:** Develop assets, copy, design
4. **Setup:** Configure marketing automation, landing pages
5. **Launch:** Execute campaign across channels
6. **Monitor:** Track performance daily/weekly
7. **Optimize:** A/B test, adjust, improve
8. **Report:** Analyze results, document learnings
9. **Iterate:** Apply insights to next campaign

**Deliverables:**

- Campaign brief
- Creative assets
- Landing page
- Measurement plan
- Performance report

## SOP 2: Content Production Workflow

**Duration:** 1-4 weeks per piece | **Frequency:** Continuous | **Owner:** Content Lead

1. **Ideation:** Brainstorm topics aligned to strategy
2. **Prioritization:** Score by impact, effort, fit
3. **Brief:** Outline key points, angle, SEO keywords
4. **Draft:** Create first version
5. **Review:** Subject matter expert review
6. **Edit:** Professional editing for quality
7. **Design:** Visual assets, formatting
8. **Approve:** Final sign-off
9. **Publish:** Release on scheduled date
10. **Distribute:** Amplify across channels
11. **Measure:** Track engagement and conversion

**Templates:**

- Content brief template
- Editorial calendar
- Style guide
- SEO checklist

## SOP 3: PR & Media Relations

**Duration:** Ongoing | **Frequency:** Daily/weekly | **Owner:** PR Lead

1. **Monitor:** Track industry news, journalist activity
2. **Identify:** News hooks and story opportunities
3. **Pitch:** Develop targeted pitches for relevant journalists
4. **Respond:** Answer media inquiries promptly
5. **Prepare:** Brief spokespeople, provide materials
6. **Track:** Monitor coverage, share internally
7. **Amplify:** Share coverage on social, website
8. **Measure:** Coverage volume, sentiment, impact
9. **Follow-up:** Thank journalists, maintain relationships

**Materials:**

- Press kit
- Spokesperson bios
- Fact sheet
- Image library
