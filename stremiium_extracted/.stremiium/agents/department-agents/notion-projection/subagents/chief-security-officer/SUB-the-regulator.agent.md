# SECTION 4: FAQs (20+)

**Q1: What is compliance vs. security?**
A: Compliance = meeting requirements. Security = protecting assets. Compliance is baseline; security goes beyond.

**Q2: What is the difference between policy, standard, and procedure?**
A: Policy = what and why. Standard = specific requirement. Procedure = how to do it. Hierarchy of detail.

**Q3: How do you keep up with regulatory changes?**
A: Regulatory feeds, industry groups, legal updates, conferences, consultants, government websites, horizon scanning.

**Q4: What is a compliance risk assessment?**
A: Identify regulatory obligations, assess current state, identify gaps, prioritize, develop roadmap.

**Q5: What is the role of internal audit vs. compliance?**
A: Compliance = designs/builds program. Internal audit = independent assessment. Separation important.

**Q6: How do you evidence compliance?**
A: Documentation, logs, screenshots, attestations, test results, meeting minutes, training records, audit reports.

**Q7: What is a SOX 404 assessment?**
A: Management assessment of internal controls over financial reporting. External auditor attestation.

**Q8: How do you handle a regulatory examination?**
A: Preparation, document organization, point of contact, honest responses, prompt follow-up, relationship management.

**Q9: What is a control deficiency, significant deficiency, material weakness?**
A: Increasing severity. Deficiency = needs improvement. Significant = more important. Material = could cause material error.

**Q10: What is SOC 2 and who needs it?**
A: Service Organization Control. Trust Services Criteria: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, Privacy. For SaaS.

**Q11: How do you build a compliance culture?**
A: Tone at top, training, accountability, reporting mechanisms, recognition, integration into operations.

**Q12: What is continuous compliance monitoring?**
A: Automated testing of controls throughout year vs. point-in-time. More real-time assurance.

**Q13: How do you prioritize compliance activities?**
A: Risk-based approach. Regulatory deadline, enforcement priority, business impact, resource availability.

**Q14: What is the difference between ISO 27001 and SOC 2?**
A: ISO = international standard, certification. SOC 2 = AICPA standard, attestation. Different but complementary.

**Q15: How do you handle compliance in cloud environments?**
A: Shared responsibility model, CSP compliance documentation, customer obligations, contractual clarity.

**Q16: What is a compliance exception/waiver?**
A: Temporary deviation from policy with approval. Documented, time-bound, risk-accepted, monitored.

**Q17: How do you measure compliance program effectiveness?**
A: Metrics: Completion rates, audit findings, violations, training scores, incident rates, maturity assessments.

**Q18: What is regulatory engagement strategy?**
A: Proactive relationship, transparency, responsiveness, industry participation, consistent messaging.

**Q19: How do you manage multiple jurisdictions?**
A: Highest common denominator approach, local requirements map, centralized policy with local addendums.

**Q20: What is the role of the board in compliance?**
A: Oversight, tone at top, resource allocation, risk appetite, regular reporting, accountability.
