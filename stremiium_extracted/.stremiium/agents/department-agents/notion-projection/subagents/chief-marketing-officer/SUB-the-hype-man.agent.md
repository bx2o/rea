# SECTION 4: FAQs (20+)

**Q1: What makes a good press release?**
A: Newsworthy angle, clear headline, inverted pyramid structure, quotes, data, boilerplate, contact info.

**Q2: How do you build media relationships?**
A: Research their beat, provide value first, respect deadlines, be responsive, maintain contact regularly.

**Q3: When should you use embargoes?**
A: For major news giving journalists time to prepare. Risk: Leaks. Trust-building with key outlets.

**Q4: How do you handle negative coverage?**
A: Assess accuracy, respond factually, offer correction if wrong, escalate if defamatory, learn from feedback.

**Q5: What's the difference between PR and marketing?**
A: PR = earned media, credibility, reputation. Marketing = paid, controlled, demand generation. Complementary.

**Q6: How do you measure PR ROI?**
A: AVE alternatives: Reach, impressions, sentiment, message pull-through, web traffic, lead attribution, share of voice.

**Q7: What is newsjacking?**
A: Injecting brand into breaking news. Fast, relevant, authentic. Risk: Tone-deaf responses.

**Q8: How do you prepare executives for media interviews?**
A: Research interviewer, prepare key messages, anticipate tough questions, practice soundbites, media training.

**Q9: What is a media kit?**
A: Press releases, executive bios, fact sheets, high-res images, logo files, recent coverage.

**Q10: How do you manage PR crises?**
A: Speed, facts, empathy, action plan, single spokesperson, monitor, learn.

**Q11: What's the role of social media in PR?**
A: Amplification, real-time engagement, monitoring, influencer relations, direct communication.

**Q12: How do you pitch journalists effectively?**
A: Personalize, relevant to their beat, compelling subject line, concise, exclusive angle if possible.

**Q13: What is an exclusive?**
A: Giving one outlet first rights. Builds strong relationship, ensures prominent coverage. Timing critical.

**Q14: How do you handle PR for product launches?**
A: Pre-brief key journalists, coordinated timing, review units, press release, launch event, follow-up.

**Q15: What is thought leadership?**
A: Sharing expertise to build authority. Not promotional. Educational, insightful, forward-looking.

**Q16: How do you build a speaking program?**
A: Identify target conferences, submit proposals early, prepare compelling topics, speaker training, leverage content.

**Q17: What is brand journalism?**
A: Creating journalistic content (not promotional). Storytelling, expert insights, industry trends.

**Q18: How do you handle fake news/misinformation?**
A: Monitor, document, factual correction, legal if defamatory, don't amplify false claims.

**Q19: What's the difference between PR agency and in-house?**
A: Agency = relationships, bandwidth, expertise. In-house = deep knowledge, culture, always-on.

**Q20: How do you stay current with media landscape?**
A: Read widely, follow journalists, attend industry events, media monitoring tools, continuous learning.

# SECTION 5: SOPs

## SOP 1: Press Release Process

1. Identify news angle
2. Draft release
3. Internal review
4. Executive approval
5. Prepare media list
6. Distribute (embargo if needed)
7. Follow up with key outlets
8. Track coverage
9. Report results
10. Amplify on social

## SOP 2: Media Pitch

1. Research journalist and outlet
2. Personalize approach
3. Craft compelling subject
4. Keep email brief (3 paragraphs)
5. Include why relevant
6. Offer exclusive if major
7. Follow up once
8. Track responses

## SOP 3: Crisis Response

1. Alert crisis team
2. Gather facts
3. Assess severity
4. Draft holding statement
5. Approve with leadership
6. Distribute to media
7. Monitor coverage
8. Update as needed
9. Post-crisis analysis
