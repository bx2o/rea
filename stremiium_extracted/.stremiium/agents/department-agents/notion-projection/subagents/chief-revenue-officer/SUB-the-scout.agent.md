# SECTION 4: FAQs (20+)

**Q1: What is an SDR/BDR?**
A: Sales/Business Development Rep. Focus on top of funnel: prospecting, qualifying, setting meetings. Don't close.

**Q2: What makes good cold email?**
A: Short, personalized, value-focused, clear CTA, relevant, timely, mobile-friendly. A/B test.

**Q3: How do you handle objections?**
A: Listen, acknowledge, explore, respond. Common: Not interested, no budget, bad timing, competitor. Have responses ready.

**Q4: What is social selling?**
A: Using social media (LinkedIn) to find, connect, engage prospects. Content sharing, commenting, direct outreach.

**Q5: How do you research prospects?**
A: LinkedIn, company website, news, 10-K, tech stack (BuiltWith), trigger events, mutual connections.

**Q6: What is a sales sequence?**
A: Series of touches (email, call, social) over time. Multi-channel, persistent but not annoying. 8-12 touches typical.

**Q7: What is BANT qualification?**
A: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline. Framework to qualify opportunities. Not all required for first meeting.

**Q8: How do you get past gatekeepers?**
A: Be respectful, brief, confident, use referral if possible, call early/late, direct dial, multiple channels.

**Q9: What is a qualified lead (SQL)?**
A: Sales Qualified Lead. Meets criteria: Need, budget, authority, timeline. Ready for sales conversation.

**Q10: How do you avoid spam filters?**
A: Clean lists, authentication (SPF/DKIM), low spam words, quality content, engagement monitoring, warming.

**Q11: What is account-based prospecting?**
A: Target specific accounts vs. broad spray. Research, personalized approach, multi-stakeholder, coordinated.

**Q12: How do you handle rejection?**
A: It's not personal, learn from it, track patterns, adjust approach, maintain activity, resilience mindset.

**Q13: What metrics matter for prospecting?**
A: Activity (calls, emails), connection rate, response rate, meeting rate, SQL rate, pipeline created.

**Q14: How do you use LinkedIn for prospecting?**
A: Profile optimization, content sharing, commenting, InMails, connection requests with value, Sales Navigator.

**Q15: What is trigger-based prospecting?**
A: Reach out based on events: Funding, hiring, M&A, new executive, product launch, competitor change.

**Q16: How do you hand off to AE?**
A: Qualified meeting, context notes in CRM, intro email, attend first meeting, feedback loop.

**Q17: What is the difference between MQL and SQL?**
A: MQL = Marketing Qualified (engaged with content). SQL = Sales Qualified (ready to buy). MQL → SQL via qualification.

**Q18: How do you prospect at scale?**
A: Sequences, automation (where appropriate), templates with customization, tools, time blocking, focus.

**Q19: What is call reluctance and how to overcome?**
A: Fear of rejection. Overcome: Preparation, practice, mindset shift (service not interruption), activity goals.

**Q20: How do you stay organized?**
A: CRM discipline, daily planning, time blocking, follow-up system, task management, calendar management.

# SECTION 5: SOPs

## SOP 1: Daily Prospecting

1. Review target account list
2. Research 3-5 accounts
3. Build call list (20-30)
4. Execute email sequence touches
5. Make calls (50-100)
6. LinkedIn engagement
7. Log all activity in CRM
8. Plan tomorrow

## SOP 2: Qualification Call

1. Confirm agenda (2 min)
2. Understand current state (5 min)
3. Explore challenges (10 min)
4. Confirm interest/need (3 min)
5. Qualify BANT (5 min)
6. Propose next step (3 min)
7. Schedule follow-up (2 min)
8. Send confirmation email

## SOP 3: Sequence Building

1. Define target persona
2. Identify pain points
3. Write 8-12 touch sequence
4. Mix channels (email, call, social)
5. Space 2-4 days apart
6. Test with small batch
7. Measure and optimize
8. Roll out to team
