# SECTION 4: FAQs (20+)

**Q1: What makes a meeting effective?**
A: Clear purpose, right people, good prep, active facilitation, clear decisions, documented actions, follow-through.

**Q2: When should a meeting NOT be held?**
A: Info-only (send email), status update (use dashboard), no decision needed, wrong people, no prep done.

**Q3: What is the role of a facilitator?**
A: Neutral guide, process owner, not content expert. Ensure participation, manage time, drive to outcomes.

**Q4: How do you handle dominant participants?**
A: Acknowledge, redirect, "Let's hear from others," parking lot, private conversation if persistent.

**Q5: What should meeting minutes include?**
A: Date, attendees, purpose, key discussion points, decisions made, action items (who, what, when), next meeting.

**Q6: How do you keep meetings on time?**
A: Pre-work, tight agenda, timekeeper, parking lot, pre-decisions, right duration, discipline.

**Q7: What is a parking lot?**
A: Place to capture off-topic but valid points for later. Keeps focus while respecting contributions.

**Q8: How do you drive to a decision?**
A: Clear decision criteria, options presented, pros/cons, convergence technique, explicit decision, confirmation.

**Q9: What is pre-work and why important?**
A: Materials reviewed before meeting. Ensures informed discussion, reduces meeting time, better outcomes.

**Q10: How do you facilitate virtual meetings effectively?**
A: Extra engagement, clear protocols, tools mastery, shorter segments, breaks, recording, chat use.

**Q11: What's the difference between minutes and notes?**
A: Minutes = formal record of decisions/actions. Notes = broader capture of discussion. Context matters.

**Q12: How do you handle conflicts in meetings?**
A: Acknowledge, understand perspectives, find common ground, escalate if needed, focus on issue not person.

**Q13: What meeting cadences are typical?**
A: Daily standup (15 min), weekly team (60 min), monthly business review (2-3 hrs), quarterly strategic (4-6 hrs).

**Q14: How do you ensure action items get done?**
A: Clear ownership, realistic dates, tracking system, reminders, blocker escalation, completion recognition.

**Q15: What is the 2-pizza rule?**
A: Amazon concept: Meeting size = two pizzas can feed. ~8 people max for effective decision-making.

**Q16: How do you facilitate brainstorming?**
A: Separate ideation from evaluation, all ideas welcome, timebox, build on ideas, capture everything, converge later.

**Q17: What makes a good agenda?**
A: Purpose statement, time allocations, topics with owners, pre-work noted, desired outcomes, realistic scope.

**Q18: How do you handle late arrivals or no-shows?**
A: Start on time, catch up briefly if needed, address pattern privately if chronic, adjust future invites.

**Q19: What is a standing meeting and when useful?**
A: Regular recurring meeting. Useful for: Team sync, reviews, decisions needing ongoing attention.

**Q20: How do you improve meeting culture?**
A: Model good practices, train, feedback loops, accountability, celebrate improvements, eliminate bad meetings.

# SECTION 5: SOPs

## SOP 1: Strategic Meeting Facilitation

1. Pre-meeting: Confirm agenda, pre-work sent
2. Opening: Purpose, agenda, ground rules (5 min)
3. Review: Pre-work, context setting (10 min)
4. Discussion: Facilitated dialogue, all voices (varies)
5. Decision: Converge, explicit decision (10 min)
6. Actions: Capture, owners, dates (5 min)
7. Closing: Summary, next steps, feedback (5 min)
8. Post: Minutes within 24h, action tracking

## SOP 2: Board Meeting Support

1. Pre: Agenda coordination, materials compilation
2. During: Attendance, minutes, decision capture
3. Post: Draft minutes, approval process, distribution
4. Action: Track, remind, report completion
5. Archive: Proper storage, compliance

## SOP 3: Action Item Management

1. Capture during meeting
2. Confirm owner and date
3. Enter in tracking system
4. Send reminder at 50% time
5. Check-in at due date
6. Verify completion
7. Report status
8. Escalate blockers
