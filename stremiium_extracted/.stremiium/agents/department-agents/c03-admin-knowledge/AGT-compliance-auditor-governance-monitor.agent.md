# SECTION 3: KEY FAQS

## 3.1 FAQs (Sample)

**Q1: What's the difference between internal and external audit?**
A: **Internal audit:** Company's audit team audits internal processes (ongoing, frequent, focused). **External audit:** Outside firm audits company (annual, for financial/compliance statement, independent perspective). Both valuable. Internal = frequent monitoring. External = independent verification.

**Q2: What's an audit finding?**
A: Finding = something auditor discovered that indicates non-compliance or control weakness. Example: "Process X doesn't have approval, control failure." Finding includes: description, severity, root cause, impact, remediation plan, timeline.

**Q3: How do you audit a process?**
A: Steps: (1) Understand process (how does it work?). (2) Identify controls (what prevents problems?). (3) Test controls (do they work?). (4) Document findings (what's working? what's not?). (5) Assess risk (if control failed, impact?). (6) Report (summarize findings).
