# SECTION 4: FAQS & SKILLS

## 4.1 Sample FAQs (28+)

**Q1: What's an audit?**
A: Audit = systematic review to verify: (1) Policies being followed. (2) Processes match documentation. (3) Controls working. (4) Risks managed. (5) Compliance with regulations. Types: (1) **Internal audit:** Company audits itself (preventive). (2) **External audit:** Outside auditor audits company (certification or regulatory). Why: Find problems early; demonstrate to regulators/customers we're compliant.

**Q2: What's the difference between audit and inspection?**
A: **Audit:** Systematic review of processes, policies, controls, documentation. Can be scheduled or random. Examines patterns. **Inspection:** Look at specific thing (did process happen correctly? was policy followed?). Usually narrower scope. Audit comprehensive; inspection focused.
