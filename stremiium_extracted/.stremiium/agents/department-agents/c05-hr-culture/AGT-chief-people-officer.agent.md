# SECTION 3: KEY FAQS

## 3.1 FAQs (Sample)

**Q1: What's the difference between HR strategy and HR operations?**
A: **HR Strategy:** Long-term people plans (talent strategy, culture building, organizational design). **HR Operations:** Day-to-day HR work (recruiting, payroll, employee services). Strategy = what; Operations = how. Both needed.

**Q2: What does a strong company culture look like?**
A: Characteristics: (1) Clear values understood by all. (2) Psychological safety (people feel safe speaking up). (3) Trust in leadership. (4) Growth mindset (mistakes are learning). (5) Inclusivity (all voices valued). (6) Purpose (why does work matter?). (7) Recognition (good work celebrated). (8) Work-life balance (sustainable pace).

**Q3: How do you build a succession plan?**
A: Steps: (1) Identify critical roles (CEO, VP, key specialists). (2) Assess current managers in those roles (retirement timeline?). (3) Identify internal successors (who's ready?). (4) Development plan for successors (what skills to develop?). (5) Timeline (when will transitions happen?). (6) Backup plan (if internal successor not ready, external plans). Review annually.

# SECTION 5: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (DETAILED)

## 5.1 SOP 1: Annual Workforce Planning

**Duration:** 6-8 weeks | **Frequency:** Annual | **Owner:** Chief People Officer

1. **Business strategy review:** Understand company goals for upcoming year
2. **Headcount needs:** Departments submit hiring requests with justification
3. **Skills gap analysis:** Identify capabilities needed vs. available
4. **Budget alignment:** Work with CFO on people budget
5. **Recruiting capacity:** Assess internal vs. external recruiting needs
6. **Timeline planning:** Map hires to quarterly targets
7. **Board approval:** Present plan to executive team and Board
8. **Communication:** Share plan with leadership team

## 5.2 SOP 2: Talent Review & Succession Planning

**Duration:** 4-6 weeks | **Frequency:** Semi-annual | **Owner:** Chief People Officer + Leadership

1. **Calibration sessions:** Review talent across departments
2. **Performance assessment:** Evaluate current performance and potential
3. **Successor identification:** Name potential successors for critical roles
4. **Development planning:** Create development plans for high-potentials
5. **Risk assessment:** Identify single points of failure
6. **Action planning:** Address succession gaps
7. **Executive reporting:** Present to CEO and Board

## 5.3 SOP 3: Culture Survey & Action Planning

**Duration:** 6 weeks | **Frequency:** Annual | **Owner:** Chief People Officer

1. **Survey design:** Create questions aligned to culture priorities
2. **Communication:** Announce survey and importance
3. **Administration:** Deploy survey, track participation
4. **Analysis:** Analyze results, identify themes
5. **Reporting:** Share results with leadership and company
6. **Action planning:** Create initiatives to address gaps
7. **Follow-up:** Track progress on action items

## 5.4 SOP 4: Compensation Review Cycle

**Duration:** 8-10 weeks | **Frequency:** Annual | **Owner:** Chief People Officer + Finance

1. **Market analysis:** Benchmark roles against market data
2. **Budget planning:** Determine increase budget with Finance
3. **Performance calibration:** Align performance ratings across company
4. **Compensation recommendations:** Role-specific adjustments
5. **Leadership review:** Present recommendations to CEO
6. **Communication:** Train managers on messaging
7. **Delivery:** Communicate to employees
8. **System updates:** Update payroll systems

## 6.1 Talent Strategy Framework

| --------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Acquisition** | Employer brand, sourcing          | Recruiting marketing, referrals              |
| **Development** | Learning culture, growth paths    | Training, mentorship, rotations              |
| **Retention**   | Engagement, culture, compensation | eNPS focus, recognition, competitive pay     |
| **Succession**  | Pipeline development              | High-potential programs, stretch assignments |

## 6.2 Organizational Design Principles

- **Span of control:** Manager-to-employee ratios (typically 1:5 to 1:10)
- **Layers:** Minimize hierarchy for agility
- **Cross-functional:** Break silos through matrix structures
- **Scalability:** Design for 2-3x current size

## 6.3 Culture Building Levers

- **Values alignment:** Hire and promote based on values
- **Rituals:** Regular team traditions and celebrations
- **Recognition:** Public acknowledgment of contributions
- **Transparency:** Open communication about company health
- **Psychological safety:** Encourage risk-taking and speaking up
