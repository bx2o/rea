# Market Research & Competitive Analysis - Skill Guide

**Skill Category**: Business & Strategy  
**Level**: Intermediate  
**Time to Proficiency**: 6-8 weeks  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Overview

Understanding your market and competitors is essential. This skill covers conducting research and analysis.

---

## Market Sizing

### TAM (Total Addressable Market)

- Global opportunity
- Industry size estimate
- "If you captured 10% of cloud market..."

### SAM (Serviceable Available Market)

- What you can realistically target
- Your geography/segment
- More realistic than TAM

### SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)

- What you'll actually capture (3-5 years)
- Your realistic growth plan

---

## Competitive Analysis

### Direct Competitors

- Same product type
- Example: Slack, Teams, Discord

### Indirect Competitors

- Different product, same need
- Example: Email, IRC

---

## SWOT Analysis

```
Strengths:
- First-mover advantage
- Strong team
- Unique technology

Weaknesses:
- Limited resources
- Smaller brand

Opportunities:
- Growing market
- New use cases
- Partnerships

Threats:
- Big tech entering market
- Economic downturn
- Regulatory changes
```

## Primary Research

- Surveys
- Interviews
- User testing
- Focus groups
