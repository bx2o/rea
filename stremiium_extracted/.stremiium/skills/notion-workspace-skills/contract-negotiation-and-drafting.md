# Contract Negotiation & Drafting - Skill Guide

**Skill Category**: Legal  
**Level**: Intermediate  
**Time to Proficiency**: 8-10 weeks  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Overview

Contract negotiation and drafting is central to business relationships. This skill covers understanding contracts, negotiating terms, and protecting interests.

---

## Core Contract Elements

### 1. Parties

- Who's involved?
- Legal entities
- Authorized signatories

### 2. Statement of Work (SoW)

- What's being delivered?
- Timeline
- Deliverables
- Acceptance criteria

### 3. Terms & Conditions

- Payment terms
- Confidentiality
- Indemnification
- Liability limits
- Termination clauses

### 4. Pricing

- Cost structure
- Payment schedule
- Expenses
- Reimbursement

---

## Negotiation Strategy

### Preparation

- Research comparable deals
- Identify your priorities
- Set walk-away points
- Understand their position

### Negotiation

- Start with high/low anchors
- Ask questions to understand interests
- Look for win-win opportunities
- Don't concede without getting something

### Common Terms to Negotiate

| Term            | Typical     | Negotiation       |
| --------------- | ----------- | ----------------- |
| Liability Cap   | 1-2x fees   | Push down         |
| Payment Terms   | Net 30      | Push for Net 60   |
| IP Ownership    | Vendor owns | Negotiate joint   |
| Termination     | After year  | Push for shorter  |
| Confidentiality | 3+ years    | Reasonable period |
