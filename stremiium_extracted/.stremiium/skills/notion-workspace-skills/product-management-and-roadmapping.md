# Product Management & Roadmapping - Skill Guide

**Skill Category**: Business Strategy  
**Level**: Intermediate to Advanced  
**Time to Proficiency**: 8-10 weeks  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Overview

Product management guides development from idea to market. This skill covers strategy, roadmapping, and user-focused product decisions.

---

## Product Strategy

### 1. Vision

- What's the end state?
- Why does it matter?
- How is it different?

### 2. Market

- Who are customers?
- What problems do they have?
- Market size and growth

### 3. Positioning

- What's unique?
- What's the value proposition?
- How to communicate?

---

## Roadmapping

### Roadmap Structure

```
Q1 2025
├─ Feature A: Description (0-3 months)
├─ Feature B: Description
└─ Bug fixes & technical debt

Q2 2025
├─ Feature C: Description
└─ Integration D
```

### Roadmap Elements

- **Themes**: Problems to solve
- **Features**: Solutions
- **Timeline**: When
- **Success metrics**: How we measure

---

## Decision Framework

### Use Data

- User research
- Analytics
- Feedback

### Consider Impact

- User value
- Business value
- Implementation effort

### Prioritize

- High value, low effort: Do first
- High value, high effort: Plan
- Low value: Postpone
