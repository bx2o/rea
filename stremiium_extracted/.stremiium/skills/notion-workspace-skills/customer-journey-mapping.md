# Customer Journey Mapping - Skill Guide

**Skill Category**: Business & Strategy  
**Level**: Intermediate  
**Time to Proficiency**: 6-8 weeks  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Overview

Customer journey mapping visualizes how customers interact with your product. This skill covers identifying opportunities across the journey.

---

## Journey Stages

```
Awareness → Consideration → Decision → Retention → Advocacy

Touchpoints:
- Ads/SEO (Awareness)
- Website/Demo (Consideration)
- Sales/Pricing (Decision)
- Product/Support (Retention)
- Referrals/Reviews (Advocacy)
```

---

## Mapping Process

### 1. Define Persona

- Name: "Tech Sarah"
- Role: Engineering Manager
- Goal: Find tools for team productivity

### 2. Map Stages

```
Stage: Awareness
Touchpoint: LinkedIn ad
Pain: Overwhelming options
Emotion: Curious but skeptical

Stage: Consideration
Touchpoint: Product demo
Pain: How will team adopt it?
Emotion: Hopeful but cautious

Stage: Decision
Touchpoint: Pricing page
Pain: Budget constraints
Emotion: Excited but hesitant
```

### 3. Identify Opportunities

- Reduce friction points
- Speed up decision process
- Improve onboarding
- Increase retention

---

## Visualization

Create visual maps showing:

- Sequential steps
- Emotions at each stage
- Pain points
- Opportunities
- Key metrics

---

**Proficiency Verified**: Can map and optimize customer journeys.
