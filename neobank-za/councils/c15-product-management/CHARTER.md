## 1. Council ID & name

**C15 — Product-Management.** The product council. Holds the **product special authority** and owns the roadmap, feature prioritisation and MVP scope.

## 2. Mandate

Decide **what we build and in what order**: product vision, MVP scope (D-005), roadmap, requirements and success metrics — the "visionary" of SAOS §8.3.

## 3. Captain role

**Product-Management Captain** (Tier-2). Owns: **product authority** (binding on roadmap), MVP scope, requirements, product success metrics. Escalates to SC.

## 5. Scope & authority

- **In scope:** product vision, roadmap, MVP scope, requirements, metrics.
- **Authority:** **product special authority** (binding on roadmap/prioritisation); proposes D-005; owns G-6 acceptance.
- **Out of scope:** engineering execution (C11), go-to-market (C09/C07).

## 10. South-Africa-specific mandate notes

- **MVP localisation:** transactional account (sponsor-held), **PayShap**/instant payments, card, **FICA Tier-1** KYC — lending/crypto deferred (D-005).
- **Regulatory-constrained product:** every feature mapped to the NPS/POPIA/FICA/FAIS perimeter with C01/C16.
- **Segment fit:** prioritise features that serve under-served/township segments at low cost-to-serve.
- **COFI readiness:** map the product suite to the expected activity-based conduct regime.
