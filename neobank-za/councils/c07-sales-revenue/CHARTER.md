## 1. Council ID & name

**C07 — Sales-Revenue.** The revenue, partnerships and business-development council. Owns distribution partnerships, sponsorship/commercial agreements, and revenue growth.

## 2. Mandate

Create **revenue and distribution**: partnerships (retailers, telcos, employers, stokvels/community groups), B2B2C channels, sponsor/commercial agreements, and revenue models beyond the app.

## 3. Captain role

**Rainmaker-Captain** (Tier-2). Owns: partnership pipeline, commercial deals, distribution strategy, revenue modelling (with C10). Holds sales-strategy authority (escalating via C04/SC). Escalates to SC.

## 10. South-Africa-specific mandate notes

- **Distribution realities:** reach township and informal-economy customers via **retail chains, telcos (MoMo), spaza/township POS (Lesaka/Kazang-style networks), employers and stokvels**.
- **Stokvel and community finance:** design partnership-compatible models that respect group-saving customs.
- **Sponsor/commercial agreements:** if D-001 (sponsor-first) proceeds, partner terms are critical-path (R-002).
- **Cost-to-serve:** prioritise low-cost acquisition channels reflecting SA ARPU realities.
