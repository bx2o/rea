## 1. The competitive set

Two distinct battlefields:

- **The Big Five (incumbents):** Standard Bank, FirstRand/FNB, Absa, Nedbank, Capitec.
- **The digital challengers:** TymeBank, Discovery Bank, Bank Zero, OM Bank, African Bank, Access Bank SA — plus **non-bank** threats (Shoprite Money Market, MTN MoMo, Lesaka/Kazang township POS).

We are a **new entrant**, so we position against **both** the scale of incumbents and the speed of challengers.

## 3. Positioning vs the Big Five

| Axis | Incumbents (Big Five) | Our position `[DESIGN]` |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust & scale** | Deep; decades of brand | We cannot win this early — we borrow trust via **sponsor bank** + regulatory credibility |
| **Cost-to-serve** | Legacy cores; branch/ATM overhead | Modern core + STP + cloud elasticity → structurally lower |
| **Product breadth** | Full universal banking | We are **narrow at launch** (account + payments + card) and expand deliberately |
| **Pricing** | Bundled, often punitive for low balances | Transparent, low/no-fee basics; earn on rails/interchange later |
| **Speed of change** | Slow release cadence | Modern delivery (C11/C06) → fast, reversible change |
| **Under-served segment** | Served expensively or not at all | **Primary target** |
| **Ecosystem** | eBucks/Vitality-class rewards | Not attempted at launch; partnership-based rewards later |

**→ Attack surface (inference):** incumbent fee structures and legacy core constraints on the low-balance segment; the "expensive to serve" tail the Big Five price away from.

## 5. Where we must NOT compete

- **Affluent rewards battle** (eBucks/Vitality) — needs an ecosystem we don't have.
- **App-only mass acquisition** — distribution wins the mass market ([`strategic-plan.md`](./strategic-plan.md) §2).
- **Crypto/investment** at launch — deferred (D-005; R-029).
- **Price war to zero** — commoditised; erodes unit economics.

---

## 6. Strategic implications

1. **Partner for distribution** (retail/agent/telco) rather than build physical reach.
2. **Win on cost-to-serve + conduct**, not on features alone.
3. **Design for consolidation optionality** — the mature market consolidates (Nedbank→iKhokha; Lesaka→Bank Zero).
4. **Ride the rails shift** — PayShap/non-bank entry changes who can compete; be rail-agnostic.
5. **Keep the licence as optionality** — sponsor-first buys time while preserving the full-licence upside.
