## 1. Council ID & name

**C04 — Strategy-Growth.** The direction-setting council: market strategy, competitive intelligence, business modelling, growth design and program roadmap ownership.

## 2. Mandate

Define **where the NeoBank competes and how it wins** in South Africa: segment focus, positioning against incumbents and challengers, the sponsor-vs-licence strategy (D-001), and the phased roadmap.

## 3. Captain role

**Chief-Strategist** (Tier-2). Owns: market strategy, competitive intelligence, roadmap and implementation-plan stewardship, business-case modelling, coordination of strategic decisions to SC.

## 10. South-Africa-specific mandate notes

- **Competitive set:** TymeBank, Discovery Bank, Bank Zero, OM Bank, African Bank, Access Bank SA (licensed); plus **non-bank** threats (Shoprite Money Market, MTN MoMo, Lesaka/Kazang township POS) — see [`../../research/neobank-competitors/`](../../research/neobank-competitors/README.md).
- **Segment strategy:** under-served/township/informal-economy focus to differentiate from incumbent scale.
- **Sponsor-first rationale (D-001):** weigh against the non-statutory PA timeline for de novo banks.
- **Macro sensitivity:** model against SA consumer strain and unemployment (see R-030).
