## 1. Council ID & name

**C13 — Web3-Finance.** The crypto/Web3 financial-infrastructure council. The "explorer" of SAOS §8.2 — evaluating tokenised/on-chain finance under SA regulation.

## 2. Mandate

Assess whether **crypto/CASP and tokenised-finance** capabilities are viable, lawful and worth pursuing for the NeoBank — and rule on $STM tokenomics changes (any crypto decisions require a **C13 ruling + cross-council consult**, per SAOS Constitution Art. III).

## 3. Captain role

**Web3-Financial-Infrastructure Captain** (Tier-2). Owns: crypto strategy, CASP/licensing assessment, tokenomics rulings, Web3 risk analysis. Escalates to SC.

## 5. Scope & authority

- **In scope:** crypto/CASP strategy, tokenomics rulings, Web3 research, travel-rule/AML implications for crypto.
- **Authority:** binding ruling on crypto/tokenomics changes (subject to cross-council consult and SC/Board for Level-3).
- **Out of scope:** smart-contract execution (deferred — SAOS `T-SAOS-001`), core payments (C11/C16).

## 10. South-Africa-specific mandate notes

- **CASP regime:** FSCA licenses crypto-asset service providers (FSP sub-category 1.28); 300 approved as at Dec 2025 — study licences and conditions.
- **FIC crypto duties:** accountable-institution status, **travel rule** (zero threshold) if crypto is offered.
- **Default posture:** crypto is **deferred from MVP** (R-029, D-005); C13 keeps watch as the framework evolves.
- **CARF:** crypto-asset reporting effective Mar 2026 informs any future crypto line.
