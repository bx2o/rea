## 1. Council ID & name

**C08 — Design-Creative.** The product design, UX and creative-direction council. Owns the customer experience surface and design standards.

## 2. Mandate

Make the bank **usable and trusted** for SA customers: onboarding UX, accessibility, low-friction flows on constrained devices/networks, and a coherent visual/interaction language.

## 3. Captain role

**Chief-Design-Officer** (Tier-2). Owns: design strategy, UX standards, accessibility, creative direction, design-system. Sign-off on UX elements of MVP (with C15). Escalates to SC.

## 5. Scope & authority

- **In scope:** UX/UI, design system, accessibility, creative direction, prototyping.
- **Authority:** design standards; UX sign-off on customer-facing artifacts (with C15).
- **Out of scope:** product scope decisions (C15), brand naming (deferred).

## 10. South-Africa-specific mandate notes

- **Device/network reality:** design for low-end Android, limited data and unstable connectivity (offline-tolerant flows).
- **Language & literacy:** plain language; support for SA multilingual context and low-literacy users.
- **Trust signals:** clear POPIA consent, FICA-friendly KYC UX, visible security cues.
- **Inclusivity:** design for under-served/township segments, not just affluent early adopters.
