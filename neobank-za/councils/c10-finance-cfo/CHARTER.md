## 1. Council ID & name

**C10 — Finance-CFO.** The finance, capital and investor-relations council. Holds the **budget special authority** and owns capital adequacy, financial modelling and reporting.

## 2. Mandate

Ensure the program is **funded, solvent and financially controlled**: capital strategy to the regulatory threshold, financial modelling, treasury, tax, reporting (BA returns), investor relations and budget governance.

## 3. Captain role

**Chief-Financial-Officer** (Tier-2). Owns: budget authority (binding), capital & funding strategy, financial control, treasury, tax, investor relations, BA-return production. Escalates to SC/Board.

## 5. Scope & authority

- **In scope:** budgeting, capital adequacy (R-003), funding (R-004), treasury, tax/AEOI, financial reporting, investor relations.
- **Authority:** **budget special authority** (binding on allocations); jointly approves resource allocation with C02.
- **Out of scope:** product scope (C15), compliance interpretation (C01).

## 10. South-Africa-specific mandate notes

- **Capital floor:** R250m minimum for a full bank vs R10m for a mutual (see [D-004](../../program/governance/decision-log.md)); the PA may require more.
- **Reporting:** **BA 100 / BA 200–210 / BA 700** production; **Basel III/IV** capital, leverage, LCR/NSFR.
- **Tax & AEOI:** VAT, income tax, **STT**, and **FATCA/CRS/CARF** (CARF effective Mar 2026).
- **Funding environment:** SA funding-cost and macro sensitivity (R-016, R-030) inform pricing and ALM.
