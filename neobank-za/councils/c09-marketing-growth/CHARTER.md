## 1. Council ID & name

**C09 — Marketing-Growth.** The marketing, communications and growth council. Owns brand-adjacent positioning, acquisition, communications and crisis comms.

## 2. Mandate

Drive **awareness, acquisition and trust**: positioning, campaigns, content, PR, growth loops and crisis communications for a brand-new bank in a saturated market.

## 3. Captain role

**Chief-Marketing-Officer** (Tier-2). Owns: marketing strategy, brand-adjacent positioning, acquisition funnels, communications, crisis comms (with SC). Marketing-strategy authority (escalating via C04/SC). Escalates to SC.

## 10. South-Africa-specific mandate notes

- **Market saturation:** differentiate against incumbent scale (Capitec/FNB/Standard Bank) and challenger brands (TymeBank/Discovery/Bank Zero/OM Bank).
- **Channel mix:** mobile-first, WhatsApp/community, township media, employer channels; consider multilingual messaging.
- **Conduct compliance:** all financial-product claims must pass **C01** (COFI/FAIS/Code of Banking Practice discipline).
- **Trust:** a new bank's reputation is its launch asset — align with crisis comms and POPIA transparency.
