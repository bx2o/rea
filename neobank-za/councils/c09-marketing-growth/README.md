## 1. Purpose of the marketing function

Marketing & Growth owns **demand, brand and trust** for a bank nobody has heard of yet. For a new deposit-taker, marketing is 50% acquisition and 50% **trust-building** — the research is emphatic that **distribution beats product** in SA retail banking (`../../research/neobank-competitors/competitive-matrix.md` §F2). The function owns:

- **positioning & brand narrative** ([`brand-positioning.md`](./brand-positioning.md));
- the **go-to-market strategy** and phased launch ([`go-to-market-strategy.md`](./go-to-market-strategy.md));
- the **acquisition channel mix** and funnel ([`acquisition-channels.md`](./acquisition-channels.md));
- **growth loops, referrals and retention** ([`growth-loops-and-referrals.md`](./growth-loops-and-referrals.md)).

It is measured on **funded, activated customers** — not downloads ([`../c15-product-management/product-metrics.md`](../c15-product-management/product-metrics.md) §6: "anti-metrics"). It shares CAC/LTV targets with C07 ([`../c07-sales-revenue/unit-economics.md`](../c07-sales-revenue/unit-economics.md)).

## 3. Marketing principles

1. **Trust first.** Every claim must be provable; every fee disclosed. New-bank scepticism is the #1 conversion barrier (see [`brand-positioning.md`](./brand-positioning.md) §4).
2. **Distribution-led, not ad-led.** Prioritise partner and referral channels (lower CAC, higher trust) over pure paid media.
3. **Funded > registered.** Success = activated, funded customers, not installs.
4. **Inclusive by default.** Marketing must reach feature-phone, low-data, multi-language audiences — media that only reaches urban smartphone users is misaligned.
5. **Data-honest.** All campaign numbers are measured or labelled; we never present assumptions as results.
6. **Compliant comms.** POPIA-compliant consent and direct-marketing (opt-in) at every touch ([`../../research/regulatory/privacy-popia.md`](../../research/regulatory/privacy-popia.md)); CPA-compliant advertising claims.
