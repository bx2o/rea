## 1. Positioning summary

> **Umoya is a licensed, phone-first South African bank for the people the market under-serves** — the cash-reliant, the debt-careful, the self-employed — delivering **honest pricing, statutory deposit safety, and full usefulness at the till and the spaza**, with a real path from everyday banking into responsible credit.

Full narrative and competitive map: [`brand-positioning.md`](./brand-positioning.md). Product vision: [`../c15-product-management/product-vision-and-principles.md`](../c15-product-management/product-vision-and-principles.md).

**Three proof points marketing must land** (see [`brand-positioning.md`](./brand-positioning.md) §4):
1. **Safe** — licensed bank; deposits covered by **CODI up to R100,000 per qualifying depositor per bank** **[FACT]**.
2. **Honest** — fees shown before they're incurred; no hidden charges.
3. **Useful** — pay, get paid (PayShap), save, cash in/out nearby, and borrow fairly.

## 2. Launch-market strategy

**Principle:** we cannot boil the ocean. Distribution depth (agents/partners/employers) determines where we launch, not geography alone. The research shows **distribution is king** and that app-only struggles while kiosk/retail/township-POS reach wins the mass market (`../../research/neobank-competitors/competitive-matrix.md` §F2).

**Launch-market selection criteria (ASSUMPTION weighting):**
1. **Partner/distribution density** — where we already have cash-in/out and acquisition partners.
2. **Employer/gig density** — concentration of salary/gig payrolls we can onboard B2B2C.
3. **SME density** — spaza/township merchant concentration for embedded SME.
4. **Digital+connectivity mix** — enough smartphone and PayShap-ready customers to seed the loop, plus feature-phone reach via USSD.
5. **Competitive whitespace** — avoid head-on Capitec/FNB strongholds early.

> **ASSUMPTION:** launch as a **"beachhead metro + adjacent township corridor"** model (e.g. one major metro plus its surrounding townships and commuter belt), then expand. **The specific metro is a decision for C04 with partner input — not fixed here.**

GTM phases align to the **product roadmap** ([`../c15-product-management/product-roadmap.md`](../c15-product-management/product-roadmap.md)) and the **licence roadmap** (`../../research/regulatory/licensing-roadmap.md`).

| GTM phase | Aligns to | Objective | Primary motion | Success metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G0 — Pre-launch / seed** | R0–R1 (build) | Build trust, waitlist, partner recruitment, brand groundwork | Employer/partner LOIs, community seeding, PR, waitlist | Waitlist + signed partners (ASSUMPTION) |
| **G1 — Controlled launch (MVP)** | R2 | Prove activation & funding in beachhead | Invite + partner-led + referral; controlled paid tests | **Activation rate**, **first-deposit rate**, CAC (ASSUMPTION) |

## 4. Sponsor / partnership-led acquisition

Because **distribution beats product**, partnerships are the **primary** GTM lever, not a tactic. Detailed partner strategy lives in [`../c07-sales-revenue/partnerships-and-bd.md`](../c07-sales-revenue/partnerships-and-bd.md); the **marketing** dimension:

| Partner type | GTM role | Why (research anchor) |
|---|---|---|
| **Employers & payroll** | Onboard whole workforces; salary-credit primacy | Payroll-driven primacy is the strongest activation trigger (ASSUMPTION); mirrors how banks win primary accounts. |
| **Retail / spaza / township agents** | Cash-in/out + assisted acquisition | TymeBank kiosks (PnP/Boxer), Shoprite (2,800+ stores), Lesaka/Kazang (90k+ merchants) prove retail/township reach **[FACT]**. |
| **Telcos / wallets** | Reach prepaid/USSD users; co-marketing | MTN MoMo, Absa ChatWallet show telco/WhatsApp reach **[FACT]**. |
| **Gig platforms / e-hailing / taxi associations** | Self-employed onboarding | Segment C is a differentiator; gig payroll + working capital. |
| **SME networks / associations / payment enablers** | Embed business accounts & merchant acceptance | The Yoco/iKhokha/Payfast embedded-finance model **[FACT]**. |
| **NGOs / financial-inclusion programmes** | Inclusion credibility + community trust | Supports trust narrative for a new bank (ASSUMPTION). |

**Sponsorship/co-marketing model (ASSUMPTION):** co-branded campaigns where the partner is a **trust sponsor** (their brand vouches for ours); revenue-share or referral payment per activated customer; joint in-language content.

## 6. Trust as GTM core

For a new bank, **trust is the conversion bottleneck**. Every GTM element must reduce perceived risk:

- **Regulatory proof** — licence status, CODI protection, NFO membership, Code of Banking Practice adoption displayed plainly.
- **Social proof** — endorsed by partners the customer already trusts (employer, retailer, telco, community).
- **Radical fee transparency** — pricing on the landing page, not buried in T&Cs.
- **Privacy confidence** — clear POPIA notice; "we never sell your data" if true (must be true).
- **Local presence** — assisted onboarding and cash access where the customer lives.

See [`brand-positioning.md`](./brand-positioning.md) §4.

## 7. Budget & measurement (all ASSUMPTION)

- **Budget split (ASSUMPTION):** ~50% partner/co-marketing, ~25% referral/community, ~15% paid performance tests, ~10% brand/content at launch — shifting toward lower-cost owned/earned channels as the referral loop matures.
- **Measurement:** blended and channel CAC, activation rate, first-deposit rate, MAFC, LTV/CAC and payback ([`../c07-sales-revenue/unit-economics.md`](../c07-sales-revenue/unit-economics.md)). Attribution to funded customers, not clicks.
- **Experimentation:** controlled paid tests in G1 before scaling any channel.

## 8. GTM risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| New-bank trust deficit | Partner/trust-sponsor strategy; regulatory proofs surfaced |
| Licence/launch delay | Phase-relative planning; seed demand while building |
| CAC inflates vs low ARPU | Distribution-led channels; referral loops; measure to funded |
| Regulatory comms risk (POPIA/CPA) | Consent-first; compliant claims; C01 review |
| Partner dependency | Multi-partner mix; avoid single-channel reliance |
