## 1. Brand narrative

**The story we tell:**

> *South Africans are asked to pay to hold their own money. Fees for breathing, fees for moving your own cash, fees for being told your balance. And the "cheap" banks still leave the people who need banking most — the cash-reliant, the self-employed, the ones who watch every rand — at the back of the queue.*
>
> *We started a bank for them. A licensed, real bank — where a rand is a rand. Where you can see every fee before you pay it. Where your savings are protected by law. Where you can get paid, pay, save, and — when the timing is right — borrow, without being tricked into it.*
>
> *Banking should feel like it's on your side. That's the whole idea.*

**Brand promise (one line):**
> **"Your money, taken seriously."**

**Emotional territory:** dignity, honesty, being seen, relief, control — **not** aspiration/luxury, **not** fear.

| Pillar | Meaning | Proof we must be able to show |

## 3. Tone of voice

- **Plain, warm, direct.** Short sentences. No banker jargon ("facility", "mandate", "leverage").
- **Respectful, never patronising.** Segment A is not "the poor"; they are "people whose money matters."
- **Confident but not boastful.** We don't claim to be the biggest — we promise to be honest and useful.
- **Multilingual.** Voice translates to South Africa's **11 official languages** where feasible — not English-first with translated leftovers (see [`../c08-design-creative/design-principles.md`](../c08-design-creative/design-principles.md)).
- **Serious about money, light about banking.** A little wit is fine; never about people's hardship.

| Do say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "See the fee before you pay it." | "Zero hidden costs.*" (*in 6-pt font) |
| "Your money, taken seriously." | "Bank smarter, live better." |
| "Made for how South Africa really banks." | "The bank of the future." |
| "We're a licensed bank. Your deposits are protected." | "Totally secure." (unprovable) |

## 4. Trust & safety messaging (critical for a new bank)

A new, unfamiliar bank's **#1 barrier is trust**. Messaging must pre-empt scepticism.

**Trust building blocks (must be verifiable or removed):**

1. **Regulatory proof**
   - "Licensed by the South African Reserve Bank's Prudential Authority." *(only once true)*
   - "Registered with the Financial Intelligence Centre (FICA)." *(only once true)*
   - "Your deposits are covered by CODI up to **R100,000 per depositor, per bank**." **[FACT]** — `../../research/sa-banking-landscape/README.md` §4
2. **Friction-free recourse** — "If we ever get it wrong, you can escalate free to the **National Financial Ombud**." (`../../research/regulatory/conduct-fais-nca.md` §6)
3. **Privacy confidence** — "We only use your data to run your account, and you're in control." Link the **POPIA** notice; if we claim "we don't sell your data", it must be **true** (`../../research/regulatory/privacy-popia.md`).
4. **Fee transparency as a feature** — show the full price list, not a calculator after signup.
5. **Real people** — named leadership, visible support channels, physical cash access via partners.
6. **Anti-fraud education** — proactive content on common scams ("we will never ask for your PIN/OTP").

**Language discipline for safety messages:**
- Use "licensed", "regulated", "protected by law" — **precisely and only where true**.
- Never imply government guarantee beyond CODI's actual scope.
- Never use fear-of-loss as a marketing tactic.

## 5. Competitive positioning map (text)

Two axes: **Affordability/Inclusivity (low-cost & mass-market ← → premium & affluent)** vs **Service depth/breadth (digital-only narrow ← → full-service/ecosystem)**.

```
FULL-SERVICE / ECOSYSTEM  ▲
                          │   Standard Bank, FNB, Absa, Nedbank
                          │      (universal, complex, higher fees)
                          │                ● Capitec
                          │          (mass, low-fee, credit-led, trusted)
                          │        ○ Discovery Bank (affluent, Vitality)
                          │   ○ OM Bank (insurer-backed, mid-market)
        ○ African Bank    │
       (inclusion,        │        ★ UMOYA (target)
        omni, lending)    │     licensed · phone-first · honest · inclusive
                          │        ○ TymeBank (kiosk, low ARPU)
                          │     ○ Bank Zero (zero-fee, sub-scale)
LOCAL MASS / LOW-COST ◀───┼────────────────────────────────────▶ PREMIUM / AFFLUENT
                          │   ○ Shoprite Money Market (R5)  [non-bank]
                          │   ○ MTN MoMo / wallets [non-bank]
DIGITAL-ONLY / NARROW     ▼
```

**Reading the map:**
- **Capitec** occupies the toughest spot (mass + full-ish service + trust). We **do not attack on scale**; we attack on **experience simplicity + genuine inclusion + gig/SME tools**.
- **Discovery/OM** are premium/ecosystem — **not our fight**.
- **TymeBank/Bank Zero** are the closest digital analogues — we differentiate on **ARPU depth (credit/SME) + app-first experience + honest positioning**.
- **Shoprite/MTN/wallets** beat us on **cost of cash/reach** — we **partner** rather than compete head-on.
- **Whitespace we claim (★):** the **under-served mass + informal/gig/SME** segment, served by a **full licensed bank** with **radical transparency** and **distribution partnerships**.

## 6. Brand architecture (working, ASSUMPTION)

- **Master brand:** one consumer brand (trust compounds in a single name).
- **Product descriptors:** plain names ("Everyday Account", "Goals", "Pockets", "Buffer") — no invented product-brand clutter.
- **SME sub-brand:** a clear "Business" descriptor under the master brand (not a separate brand at launch).
- **Partner co-branding:** co-branded campaigns where the partner is a **trust sponsor**; keep Umoya visually distinct.

---

## 7. Brand measurement (ASSUMPTION)

- **Aided/unaided brand awareness** in beachhead.
- **Trust score** ("I would trust this bank with my money") — the key brand KPI for a new bank.
- **Message recall** on the three proof points (honest/safe/useful).
- **Consideration → onboarding** conversion.

All values are **ASSUMPTION** until measured; brand tracking to be commissioned.
