## 1. Purpose

A bank is a **regulated, people-intensive trust business**. Two constraints shape everything C05 does:

1. **Regulatory fit-and-proper.** The Prudential Authority and FSCA assess the competence and integrity of directors and Key Individuals (`../../research/regulatory/banking-licensing.md` §4; `../../research/regulatory/conduct-fais-nca.md` §2.2). People decisions are therefore *regulatory* decisions, not just HR decisions.
2. **Scarce specialist skills.** Compliance, risk, AML, secure engineering and data skills are constrained in the South African market `[ASSUMPTION]`; the hiring plan must sequence around the licence critical path, not around volume.

C05 bridges to **C03** (knowledge & skills academy), **C01** (compliance fit-and-proper), **C10** (cost/headcount budget), and **SC** (org design authority).
