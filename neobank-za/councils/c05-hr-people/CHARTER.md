## 1. Council ID & name

**C05 — HR-People.** The people, talent and culture council. Owns the people plan, fit-and-proper bench, culture, development and key-person resilience.

## 2. Mandate

Build and sustain the **people capabilities** the launch demands — fit-and-proper directors and Key Individuals (with C01), scarce-skill acquisition, culture, and reduction of key-person dependency.

## 3. Captain role

**Chief-People-Officer** (Tier-2). Owns: people strategy, hiring, development, culture, competency frameworks, key-person risk (with C03), and people-related escalation.

## 10. South-Africa-specific mandate notes

- **Fit-and-proper:** meet **Banks Act** competence & integrity requirements for directors/Key Individuals (with C01).
- **Scarce skills:** SA payments, core-banking, cybersecurity and data skills are constrained (R-008); design for mentoring and partner leverage.
- **Employment law:** compliance with the **BCEA, LRA, EE Act/B-BBEE** considerations in hiring and reward.
- **Culture:** support an async-first, distributed workforce across SA time zones with load-shedding-aware practices.
