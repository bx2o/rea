## 1. Council ID & name

**C16 — Banking-Fintech.** The banking-domain and payments council. Holds the **banking-compliance special authority** and owns the licence/sponsor and payment-rail strategy.

## 2. Mandate

Provide **banking-domain expertise**: licensing path, sponsor-bank arrangements, payment rails, prudential interfaces and fintech-capability fit — the "domain expert" of SAOS §8.3.

## 3. Captain role

**Banking-Fintech Captain** (Tier-2). Owns: **banking-compliance authority** (binding), licence/sponsor strategy (D-001/D-004), payment-rail access, prudential liaison. Escalates to SC (with C01 for disputes).

## 10. South-Africa-specific mandate notes

- **Licensing:** Banks Act s12 authorisation + s16 registration; alternatives: Mutual Banks Act / Co-operative Banks Act / sponsor model / non-bank PSP (see [D-004](../../program/governance/decision-log.md)).
- **Payments:** NPS Act participation, **PayShap**, **ISO 20022**, clearing/settlement access, and the **PSMB→SARB** transition (Aug 2026).
- **Standards:** Basel III/IV capital & liquidity; BA-return interfaces; PA supervisory engagement.
- **Sponsor dependency:** if D-001 (sponsor-first) proceeds, manage counterparty and pricing risk (R-002) with a documented exit plan.
- **FATF context:** maintain AML/CFT rigour post grey-list exit (with C01/C12).
