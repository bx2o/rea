## 1. Council ID & name

**C14 — Customer-Success.** The customer advocate council. Owns onboarding success, support, retention and the customer voice — the "advocate" of SAOS §8.3.

## 2. Mandate

Ensure customers **succeed with the product**: smooth onboarding, effective support, healthy retention and measurable satisfaction (NPS), and represent the customer in every decision.

## 3. Captain role

**VP-Customer-Success** (Tier-2). Owns: customer-success strategy, pilot cohort, support operations, retention, customer communications, the customer voice into product.

- **In scope:** onboarding, support, retention, complaints, customer comms, pilot cohort management.
- **Authority:** customer-communication sign-off; owns pilot operations (Phase 3).

## 10. South-Africa-specific mandate notes

- **Support realities:** design for mobile-first, WhatsApp/community support and low-data channels.
- **NFO:** prepare for **National Financial Ombud Scheme** membership and complaint handling (with C01).
- **Trust:** visible handling of fraud/POPIA concerns; empathetic support for first-time bank customers.
- **Inclusion:** support under-served/township segments with plain-language and low-friction journeys.
