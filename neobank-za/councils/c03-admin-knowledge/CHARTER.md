## 1. Council ID & name

**C03 — Admin-Knowledge.** The knowledge-management and program-administration council. Steward of the governance tree, decision log, artifact standards, records and institutional memory. **This is the governance team that authored the program governance and council files.**

## 2. Mandate

Preserve and structure the program's **institutional memory** and ensure every artifact is discoverable, versioned, compliant with the vocabulary law, and traceable. C03 turns work into durable knowledge — "if it isn't written down, it didn't happen".

## 3. Captain role

**Chief-Librarian** (Tier-2). Owns: knowledge architecture, artifact standards, decision-log stewardship, records/audit, indexing and cross-linking, program administration. Escalates to SC. Largest agent pool on the program (knowledge at scale).

## 5. Scope & authority

- **In scope:** governance tree, decision log, artifact standards, vocabulary-law enforcement, records/retention, indexing, knowledge synthesis, program administration.
- **Authority:** binding on **artifact standards** (format, IDs, links, vocabulary) and on the **decision log** (an unlogged Level-2/3 decision is not a decision). Steward (assured) role on governance artifacts.
- **Out of scope:** the substantive content of domain artifacts (owned by domain councils).

## 10. South-Africa-specific mandate notes

- **POPIA records:** support Information Officer with Records of Processing and PAIA Manual maintenance (structured, auditable).

- **King IV records:** maintain committee/decision records consistent with King IV governance expectations.
- **Languages:** maintain artifacts in English; retain SA-context terms (e.g., "stokvel", "township economy") accurately.
