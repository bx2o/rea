## Evidence-usage notes

- **Regulator/primary filings (S1–S6, S7–S34, S36–S44, S47–S52, S55–S56, S60, S62–S72, S77, S80, S82–S89, S94–S96):** safe to cite as observed fact.
- **Secondary/aggregator sources (S35, S40, S41, S45, S46, S51, S53, S54, S57–S59, S61, S73–S76, S78–S79, S81, S90–S93, S95):** figures must be treated as [EST] until corroborated by a primary source. In particular: Bidvest scale, Albaraka/HBZ assets and branch counts, Nedbank SA-only splits, Standard Bank/Absa SA branch-ATM counts, FirstRand shareholder percentages, and any currency-converted comparison.
- **Temporal caveat:** regulator registers (S1, S2, S3) lag dated announcements (e.g. Sasfin licence cancellation 30 Jun 2026; Bank Zero→Lesaka; Ubank curatorship). Where they conflict, the dated filing is authoritative for *status* and the register is authoritative for *licence class*.
