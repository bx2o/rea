# Licensing & Launch Roadmap (0–24 Months)

**As-of (access) date:** 2026-10-16 · See [`README.md`](./README.md) for the evidence legend and [`sources.md`](./sources.md) for `[S#]`.

> **How to use this.** This is a **sequencing plan**, not a legal deadline list. Statutory deadlines are marked **[hard]**; everything else is a recommended workstream. Dependencies matter: you cannot file a banking application without a public company, a draft RMCP, a named Information Officer, and a coherent capital plan `[S1][S25][S33]`. Costs, headcount and vendor selections are out of scope here.

## Assumptions
- Target: a **de novo full banking licence** (R250m minimum capital `[S1]`) with a consumer-credit and payments proposition, potentially crypto-enabled.
- The DRAFT non-bank payments framework may or may not be final by the time certain steps are reached `[S10]`.
- Regulators act on complete submissions; the 6–9 month PA figure is a **best case** `[S1]`.

## Phase 0 — Foundations (Months 0–3)

| # | Workstream | Key actions | Refs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | **Entity & structure** | Incorporate a **public company** (required for a bank) and register at CIPC; agree shareholding and controlling-company structure (s37/s42 constraints) | `[S1]` |
| 0.2 | **Board & KI bench** | Recruit fit-and-proper directors/KIs; document competence & integrity | `[S1][S23]` |
| 0.3 | **Governance design** | King IV-aligned board charter; plan **audit committee (s94)** and **social & ethics committee (s72)** | `[S70]` |
| 0.4 | **Privacy kick-off** | Appoint and **register the Information Officer** (POPIA s55); start PAIA Manual and record of processing | `[S25][S32]` |
| 0.5 | **AML kick-off** | Draft **RMCP**; scope CDD/EDD, sanctions/TFS screening, reporting | `[S33][S37]` |
| 0.6 | **Capital strategy** | Design equity raise to ≥R250m (or R10m if mutual `[S1]`); model CAR/LCR/NSFR | `[S1][S5]` |
| 0.7 | **PA pre-application (optional)** | Meet the PA to align on business plan and application format | `[S1]` |

**Gate to Phase 1:** public company registered; board seated; IO registered; draft RMCP; credible capital plan.

## Phase 1 — Applications & authorisation (Months 3–9)

| # | Workstream | Key actions | Refs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | **Banking authorisation** | File **Banks Act s12** application (business plan, governance, risk, IT/accounting systems, capital & funding plan, fit-and-proper) | `[S1]` |
| 1.2 | **FSCA engagement** | PA consults the **FSCA for concurrence**; prepare the conduct case | `[S1]` |
| 1.3 | **Conduct licence** | If advice/intermediary (incl. crypto): file **FSP application** for the right Category (I–IV) with fit-and-proper | `[S23]` |
| 1.4 | **Credit licence** | If lending: register as a **credit provider** with the **NCR** | `[S41][S42]` |
| 1.5 | **FIC registration** | Register as an **accountable institution** on **goAML**; item-12 registration if crypto | `[S33][S72]` |
| 1.6 | **Forex (if needed)** | Apply to **FinSurv** for **AD/ADLA** status (banks = AD; limited-scope = ADLA) | `[S51][S52]` |
| 1.7 | **Sandbox (optional)** | If features are novel, apply to the **IFWG Regulatory Sandbox** for supervised testing | `[S62]` |

**Hard milestone:** PA **authorisation** for establishment. **Gate:** proceed to build only after authorisation (or a documented PA path).

## Phase 2 — Build & condition-satisfaction (Months 6–18)

| # | Workstream | Key actions | Refs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **Capital & Treasury** | Close the raise; place HQLA; stand up CAR (≥11.5%), leverage (≥4%), **LCR/NSFR**, stress testing, funding diversification | `[S1][S5][S6]` |
| 2.2 | **Risk & credit** | Credit policy; **affordability assessment**; large-exposure framework (D14/2025) and board credit committee | `[S43][S6]` |
| 2.3 | **AML systems** | Deploy CDD/EDD, UBO tracing, **TFS screening**, CTR/STR/TPR pipelines; crypto **travel-rule** (zero threshold) if applicable | `[S33][S40][S72]` |
| 2.4 | **Privacy programme** | Privacy notices, consent, cross-border transfer mechanisms (BCRs/agreements/SCCs), breach runbook; assess **prior authorisation** needs | `[S25][S27]` |
| 2.5 | **Payments build** | **ISO 20022** messaging; PayShap readiness; clearing-house rulebook compliance; decide **sponsorship vs direct** participation | `[S9][S11][S14]` |
| 2.6 | **Tax & AEOI** | Register income tax/VAT; build **apportionment**; onboard **FATCA/CRS**; build **CARF** reporting if crypto | `[S55][S57][S59]` |
| 2.7 | **Reporting (BA returns)** | Build **BA 100 / BA 200–210 / BA 700** production and sign-off controls | `[S68][S69]` |
| 2.8 | **Conduct** | Complaints handling; join **NFO**; adopt **Code of Banking Practice**; CPA/T&Cs review | `[S46][S47][S50]` |

**Ongoing:** regulator engagement, remediation of PA queries.

## Critical-path summary

```
Month 0  ✓ Public company + IO registered + draft RMCP + capital plan
Month 3  → File s12 banking authorisation + FSP + NCR + FIC (+ ADLA if fx)
Month 6  → PA authorisation expected (6–9 mo guidance) [best case]
Month 6–18 → Build capital/AML/privacy/payments/BA-return capability
Month 12–24 → s16 registration → settlement/clearing → LAUNCH
[hard] s16 registration must be applied for within 12 months of authorisation [S1]
```

## Standing obligations after launch (non-exhaustive)
- **FIC:** CTR (R49,999.99 / 3 days), STR (15 days), TPR `[S33][S34]`.
- **POPIA:** breach notification; PAIA annual report (1 Apr–30 Jun) `[S25][S32]`.
- **AEOI:** annual CRS/FATCA (+ CARF if crypto) `[S55][S57]`.
- **Prudential:** periodic BA returns; LCR/NSFR; annual Pillar III disclosure `[S68][S69]`.
- **NFO:** complaint handling and binding determinations `[S47]`.

## Highest-risk roadmap assumptions (verify before committing dates)
1. **PA timeline** — 6–9 months is guidance, not statutory `[S1]`.
2. **Non-bank payments framework** may not be final in time `[S10]`.
3. **COFI** may be enacted before/at launch, changing conduct obligations `[S22]`.
4. **R250m** is a *minimum*; the PA may impose a higher CAR and larger buffer for new banks `[S1]`.
5. **Open Finance** could impose API/consent duties mid-life `[S61]`.
