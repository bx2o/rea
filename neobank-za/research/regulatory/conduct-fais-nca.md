## 1. Twin Peaks — who regulates conduct

The **Financial Sector Regulation Act 9 of 2017 (FSRA)** created two "peaks": `[S64][S65]`
- **Prudential Authority (PA)** inside the SARB — safety & soundness of banks, insurers, co-operatives.
- **Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA)** — **market conduct** regulator (product-provider, advice/intermediary and consumer-protection conduct), established as a national public entity `[S64][S65]`.

The FSCA supervises a broad population including **banks, insurers, retirement funds, FSPs, CIS, CASPs and market infrastructures** `[S64]`. The **Conduct of Financial Institutions (COFI) Bill** is the second leg of Twin Peaks, intended to consolidate market-conduct law into a single activity-based regime — **still a Bill as at early 2026**, with promulgation anticipated and a multi-year transition `[S22][S24]`. **[CONSULT]**

## 2. FAIS — licensing to give advice / intermediate

**Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act 37 of 2002 (FAIS)** governs the provision of **advice** and **intermediary services** in respect of **financial products** `[S19][S20]`.

### 2.1 FSP licence categories

| Category | What it is | Ref |
|---|---|---|
| **Cat I** | Advice and/or intermediary services, **non-discretionary** (broadest product scope) | `[S23]` |
| **Cat II** | **Discretionary** FSP (manages client portfolios under mandate) | `[S23]` |
| **Cat IIA** | **Hedge fund** FSP | `[S23]` |
| **Cat III** | **Administrative** FSP (bulking / on instruction) | `[S23]` |
| **Cat IV** | **Assistance business** FSP | `[S23]` |

### 2.2 Fit-and-proper requirements
Set out in the **Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements for Financial Services Providers, 2017** (Board Notice 194 of 2017) `[S23]`. Components include `[S23]`:
- **Honesty and integrity** (no disqualifying criminal convictions etc.).
- **Competence** — including **regulatory examinations**: RE1 + RE3 for **Key Individuals**; RE5 for **representatives** (Cat I/IV); Cat II adds RE1/RE3 for KIs.
- **Operational ability / financial soundness** — e.g. Cat II liquidity of 8/52 of annual expenditure (Table B).
- **Continuous professional development**.

A **Key Individual (KI)** — a natural person responsible for managing/overseeing the FSP's services to clients — must meet fit-and-proper standards `[S23]`.

### 2.3 CASPs (crypto)
- **19 Oct 2022:** the FSCA declared **crypto assets a "financial product"** under FAIS (Government Notice 1350, Gazette 47334) `[S20]`.
- CASP licensing window opened **1 June 2023** — every CASP must hold an **FSP licence** with crypto-asset authorisation. Miners, node operators and NFT-only providers were excluded from the declaration `[S20][S21]`.
- By **mid-December 2025:** ~**512 applications**, ~**300 approved**, with refusals/withdrawals and enforcement against unlicensed operators `[S21]`. (A Dec-2024 FSCA update showed 248 approvals of 420 applications — the regime has been progressively licensing `[S21]`.)
- **Regulatory exam deadline:** all licensed CASPs and KIs had to meet RE requirements **by 30 June 2025** — no further extensions `[S21 — secondary]`.
- CASPs are also **FICA accountable institutions** (item 12) with a **second FIC registration** and **travel-rule** obligations `[S72]`.

> **Consequence for a crypto-enabled neobank:** you need **two** gates — FSCA FSP authorisation for the crypto product *and* FIC/FICA compliance. See [`aml-fica.md`](./aml-fica.md).

### 2.4 The statutory Ombud for FSPs
The Ombud for Financial Services Providers is regulated under the FSRA; determinations are subject to reconsideration by the **Financial Services Tribunal** `[S50 — secondary]`.

## 3. National Credit Act (NCA) — consumer credit

**National Credit Act 34 of 2005** governs consumer credit. **[STATUTE]** `[S41][S42]`

### 3.1 Registration as a credit provider (s40, s41, reg 4)
Registration with the **National Credit Regulator (NCR)** is mandatory if a person, alone or with an associated person `[S41][S42]`:
- is the credit provider under **at least 100** credit agreements (other than incidental credit); **or**
- has total principal debt owed under outstanding credit agreements **exceeding the prescribed threshold** (per s42(1)) — commonly cited as **R500,000** `[S44 — secondary; confirm current threshold]`.

Registration is required **before** entering into credit agreements; **no exemptions** for informal/small operators `[S41]`.

### 3.2 Affordability assessment (regulations, effective 2016)
Before extending credit, a provider must conduct an **affordability assessment**, taking practical steps to assess the consumer's discretionary income — typically **three months' payslips/income proof or bank statements** (adjusted for self-employed/informally employed consumers). Objectives include the consumer's understanding of risks/costs, rights/obligations, repayment history and existing financial means `[S43]`.

### 3.3 Reckless credit & debt review
- NCA **s81** makes pre-agreement assessment mandatory; **reckless credit** provisions give courts remedies (setting aside / suspending agreements). **[STATUTE]** `[S45 — secondary]`
- **Debt review:** over-indebted consumers may be assisted by registered **debt counsellors** to restructure repayments; the NCA also aims to prevent over-indebtedness and encourage responsible lending `[S45]`.

### 3.4 Credit-provider categories (fees & reporting)
Secondary sources describe categories by loan-book size — **Cat A (>R500m), B (R100m–R500m), C (R10m–R100m), D (<R10m)** — determining registration fees/reporting `[S45 — secondary; needs authoritative confirmation against the current NCR fee schedule]`.

## 4. Consumer Protection Act (CPA)

**Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008** creates consumer rights enforced through the **National Consumer Commission (NCC)**, the **National Consumer Tribunal (NCT)**, accredited **industry ombuds** and provincial authorities `[S49]`. Non-compliance sanctions include fines and imprisonment `[S50]`.

**Banking interface:** many financial-services suppliers are also answerable to the NCC/NCT `[S50 — secondary]`. Certain CPA provisions (e.g. **s14 fixed-term agreements**) have historically been **exempted for banks**, but exemptions are time-limited and must be re-confirmed `[S49 — secondary; verify current exemptions]`. The CPA and NCA have **different thresholds** for juristic-person coverage `[S49]`.

Practical takeaway: even where FAIS/FSCA conduct rules apply, the CPA can still bite on consumer-facing T&Cs, marketing and complaint handling — design defensively.

## 5. Code of Banking Practice (voluntary)

- The **Code of Banking Practice** is a **voluntary industry code** setting minimum service/conduct standards, applying to **personal and small-business customers** `[S46]`.
- It requires banks to manage complaints fairly and to inform customers of the relevant ombud `[S46]`.
- A **2025 edition** (effective 1 July 2025) reaffirms that signatories **accept the jurisdiction of the National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFO)** and may be bound by its determinations `[S46]`.
- **All banks that are members of the Banking Association South Africa (BASA) are automatically subject to the Ombud's jurisdiction** `[S48]`.

---

## 6. Ombud schemes — National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFO)

- The **NFO** (National Financial Ombud Scheme South Africa NPC) was formed by amalgamating four legacy schemes: the **Ombudsman for Banking Services (OBS)**, the **Credit Ombud (CO)**, the **Ombudsman for Long-Term Insurance (OLTI)** and the **Ombudsman for Short-Term Insurance (OSTI)** `[S47]`.
- It provides **free, independent** dispute resolution; an Ombud within jurisdiction can mediate and make **binding determinations** based on the Code, the law and/or equity `[S47][S46]`.
- A neobank should **join the NFO** and align internal complaints handling with its terms of reference `[S47]`.

## 7. Neobank conduct checklist

- [ ] Determine FAIS need; if advice/intermediary (incl. crypto) applies, obtain the correct **FSP category** (`[S23]`) and meet **fit-and-proper** including RE exams `[S23]`.
- [ ] If extending credit: register with the **NCR** and build **affordability assessment** `[S41][S43]`.
- [ ] Implement **reckless-credit** guardrails and debt-review handling `[S45]`.
- [ ] Adopt CPA-compliant T&Cs and complaint processes `[S49][S50]`.
- [ ] Adopt the **Code of Banking Practice** and join the **NFO** `[S46][S47]`.
- [ ] Prepare for **COFI** consolidation `[S22][S24]`.
