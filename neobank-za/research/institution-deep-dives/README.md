# Institution Deep-Dives — UnConsolidated Research & Analysis

**Deliverable owner:** C04 Strategy / Market-Intelligence (with C02 Legal, C03
Finance, C07 Technology, C09 People, C10 Risk, C12 Customer).
**Program:** NeoBank ZA — South Africa retail/digital banking entry.
**Access date context:** 2026-10-16.
**Method:** Every institution gets its own folder and the standard 10-file set
defined in [`_TEMPLATE/FILE-SET.md`](_TEMPLATE/FILE-SET.md). All claims are
evidence-graded `[FACT]/[EST]/[UNKNOWN]/[INFER]` and sourced `[S#]`.

> **UnConsolidated by design.** These are raw departmental work-products.
> Nothing here is merged into a single "official" narrative; conflicting
> sources are preserved side-by-side and flagged.

Start at each institution's `README.md`, then read the numbered files in
order. `09-sources.md` is the evidence backbone. Cross-institution insights
are consolidated in [`../competitive-analysis/`](../competitive-analysis/).

## 4. Constraints & honesty notes

- **Private data is not public.** Exact internal salary tables, unredacted
  customer contracts, and internal operational runbooks are almost never
  public. Where such data is sought, the file records `[UNKNOWN]` and names
  the document that would contain it (e.g. "PAIA manual", "remuneration
  report", "supplier MSA"). Nothing is fabricated.
- **Figures drift.** FY2025/FY2026 numbers are reported as at their source
  date and re-verified where possible; a verification caveat is carried where
  a figure could not be re-confirmed from a primary filing.
