# Payments & Fintech Challengers (non-bank)

Entities here generally do **not** hold a South African banking licence. They hold customer funds via **partner banks**, trust/escrow structures, or are designated/registered payment participants. Most are registered for AML (FIC) and some are FSPs; lenders require NCR registration. This is the "embedded finance / merchant acquiring / remittance / investing" layer.

## Cross-cutting notes (payments fintech)

- **Fund custody:** none are banks; all rely on partner banks/clearing. This is a **structural difference from the licensed digital banks** and means they need a bank partner (e.g. Mukuru→Bank Zero; Payfast→TymeBank funding) or a licence to hold float at scale.
- **Consolidation wave (2025–26):** Nedbank→iKhokha (R1.65bn); Lesaka→Adumo and →Bank Zero; Yoco→Dyner.AI. Banks are buying distribution; fintechs are buying capability.
- **PayShap disruption:** free/cheap interbank real-time rails (839m+ transactions, R774bn since Mar 2023 [S77]) pressure Instant-EFT economics (Ozow/Payfast/Peach/Stitch) and open the door for non-bank wallets [S75][S76][S77].
- **Embedded finance is the growth axis:** TymeBank↔Payfast SME funding; Mama Money↔Capitec; EasyEquities↔Capitec; Mukuru↔Bank Zero.
