## F. Strategic implications for a new neobank

1. **Profitability is achievable but capital-intensive** — the winners (Tyme, Discovery) took years and either $250m (Tyme) or a listed parent (Discovery). Bank Zero shows under-capitalisation stalls break-even [S18][S19].
2. **Distribution beats product** — kiosks (Tyme), stores (Shoprite/PEP/PnP) and township POS (Lesaka) reach the mass market; app-only struggles [S1][S83][S85].
3. **Zero-fee is commoditised** — differentiate on credit, ecosystem or B2B rails, not fees.
4. **Consolidation is the exit path** — banks are buying distribution (Nedbank→iKhokha) and fintechs are buying licences (Lesaka→Bank Zero). A new entrant should plan for partnership/M&A.
5. **PayShap reshapes economics** — free/cheap real-time rails erode Instant-EFT margins and let non-banks (MTN MoMo) enter [S75][S76][S79].
6. **Licence = optionality** — only a bank licence allows deposits + lending margin at scale; non-banks must rent a licence (Mukuru→Bank Zero).
