# 00 — Keyword & Search-Term Taxonomy

> Reusable search-term bank for the NeoBank research programme. Terms are an
> **analyst-constructed taxonomy** (`[INF]` derived), grounded where a term is validated by a cited
> source (`[OBS]`). Use with WebSearch/WebFetch; pair each banking term with a year (e.g. "2025"/"2026")
> and "South Africa" where the South-African scope matters.
> Tags: `[OBS]` observed, `[INF]` inference, `[EST]` estimate, `[UNK]` unknown.

## Build method `[INF]`
`[INF]` This taxonomy was assembled from the entities, rails, legislation, programmes and metrics
encountered during the research passes for this workspace (e.g. SARB licensing classes `[OBS]`, the
SARB Digital Payments Roadmap's list of retail payment methods `[OBS]`, FICA accountable-institution
concepts `[OBS]`, and brand/usability indices such as the oCX report `[OBS]`). It is intended to be
expanded iteratively as each department research pass surfaces new terms.
