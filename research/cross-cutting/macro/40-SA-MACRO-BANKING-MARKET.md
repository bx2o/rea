# 40 — South African Macro & Banking-Market Context

> Public information only. Tags: `[OBS]`, `[DER]`, `[INF]`, `[EST]`, `[UNK]`. Confidence is **medium**
> because several market-size and inclusion figures come from vendors/secondary sources with divergent
> methodologies — these are flagged, not reconciled.

## 1. Repo rate (SARB MPC decisions)

`[OBS]` Recent policy-rate path (each figure sourced):

| MPC date | Repo rate | Change | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| September 2025 | **7.00%** | hold | [SARB MPC Sept 2025](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/statements/monetary-policy-statements/2025/september) |
| January 2026 | **6.75%** | hold | [SARB MPC Jan 2026](https://resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/statements/monetary-policy-statements/2026/january) |
| May 2026 | **7.00%** | +25 bps (eff. 29 May) | [SARB MPC May 2026](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/statements/monetary-policy-statements/2026/may) |
| July 2026 | **7.00%** (prime 10.50%) | hold | [SAnews — Reserve Bank keeps rate unchanged](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/reserve-bank-keeps-interest-rate-unchanged) |
| September 2026 | **7.25%** | +25 bps (eff. 25 Sept) | [SARB MPC Sept 2026](https://www.sarb.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/statements/monetary-policy-statements/2026/september) |

`[OBS]` The SARB's Quarterly Projection Model interprets the stance as **moderately restrictive**, with
rates reaching neutral levels during 2027 `[OBS]` (Jan 2026 MPC). `[INF]` The **May and September 2026
hikes** reflect renewed upside inflation risks (fuel-price shock, global rates), a reversal of earlier
easing expectations.

## 2. Inflation

- `[OBS]` In **September 2025** the SARB expected headline inflation to average **3.4% in 2025, 3.6% in
  2026, then revert to 3% in 2027**, with risks assessed as balanced
  ([SARB MPC Sept 2025](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/statements/monetary-policy-statements/2025/september)).
- `[OBS]` By **September 2026** the SARB reported inflation at **4.4%** with upside risks
  ([SAnews — MPC raises repo rate to 7.25%](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/mpc-raises-repo-rate-725)).
- `[OBS]` The SARB is targeting a **~3% point target** as "the new normal"
  ([SARB MPC Jan 2026](https://resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/statements/monetary-policy-statements/2026/january)).

## 3. GDP growth

- `[OBS]` **National Treasury (2026 Budget Review):** the economy is expected to grow **1.6% in 2026**,
  up from an estimated **1.4% in 2025**
  ([Treasury — 2026 Budget Review, Chapter 2, PDF](https://www.treasury.gov.za/documents/National%20Budget/2026/review/Chapter%202.pdf)).
- `[OBS]` **SARB (March 2026 forecast):** GDP growth **0.5% (2024), 1.1% (2025), 1.4% (2026), 1.9%
  (2027), 1.9% (2028)**, steady state 2.5%
  ([SARB — MPC forecast report, PDF](https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/publications/statements/monetary-policy-statements/2026/march/forecast.pdf)).
- `[OBS]` The economy expanded for **four consecutive quarters** by January 2026, driven by household
  consumption ([SARB MPC Jan 2026](https://resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/statements/monetary-policy-statements/2026/january)).

## 4. Banked / unbanked

- `[OBS]` **~98% of adults are banked** (FinScope 2024) — **37.3 million banked adults**, only ~2%
  unbanked; banking-account penetration forecast at 96.62% by 2029
  ([PaymentBrief — South Africa Payments](https://paymentbrief.com/markets/south-africa/)).
- `[OBS]` Contradicting the ~2% figure, a FinMark Trust / Global Findex-derived report estimates
  **~7.3 million South African adults remain unbanked**, with nearly half keeping savings in cash
  ([African News Agency — Cash at home](https://africannewsagency.com/cash-at-home-why-many-south-africans-still-avoid-formal-savings/)).
- `[OBS]` Historical trajectory: **81% banked** (age 16+) in 2019 per FinScope, up from **63% in 2010**
  ([World Bank FSAP — South Africa Financial Inclusion technical note, PDF](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099125002222218689/pdf/P1707070caa09c03a08feb09ebf52824b7a.pdf)).
- `[INF]` The "2% unbanked" vs "7.3m unbanked" gap reflects different definitions (account
  ownership vs *active* usage / *formally served*), not a factual conflict; for segmentation, the
  **~7 million adults without a bank account** is the conservative planning number. Tag `[EST]`.

## 6. Credit extension

- `[OBS]` Growth in total loans and advances to the domestic private sector accelerated from a recent low
  of **3.9% in February 2025 to 6.5% in July 2025**, driven by corporate credit; **household credit
  growth remained subdued**
  ([SARB Quarterly Bulletin — September 2025](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/quarterly-bulletins/quarterly-bulletin-publications/2025/september)).
- `[OBS]` By **October 2025** credit extension growth had reached **7.2% y/y** (again corporate-led),
  while broadly defined money supply **M3 accelerated to 7.5%**
  ([SARB Quarterly Bulletin — December 2025](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/quarterly-bulletins/quarterly-bulletin-publications/2025/december)).
- `[OBS]` **Mortgage advances** reached ~**R1.95 trillion** in November 2025, up **3.8% y/y**
  ([Global Property Guide — South Africa residential market 2026](https://www.globalpropertyguide.com/africa/south-africa/price-history)).
- `[OBS]` TransUnion Q1 2026: **non-bank personal loans grew rapidly** — account volumes +27.6% y/y,
  originations +19.0%, but average balances −16.4%, indicating smaller, more frequent (short-term)
  borrowing ([TransUnion SA Consumer Credit Trends Q1 2026, PDF](https://www.transunion.co.za/content/dam/transunion/za/business/iir/documents/2976951-sa-q1-26-iir-rpr.pdf)).

## 7. Banking-sector size and concentration

- `[OBS]` **Total sector assets ≈ R8.5 trillion (June 2025)**, with the **big four banks holding
  ~90%** of assets ([IdeaToola — Banking Market Share Dashboard](https://ideatoola.com/insights/banking-market-share-dashboard);
  [Who Owns Whom — Banking Industry 2025](https://www.whoownswhom.co.za/store/banking-industry-including-stokvels-south-africa/)).
- `[OBS]` **PwC Major Banks Analysis (March 2026):** the SA operations of the major banks comprised
  **93% of total banking-sector assets** (June 2025 BA 900 data); FY25 combined balance sheet reached a
  record, with lending **+5.8%** and deposits **+8.4%** y/y
  ([PwC — Major Banks Analysis, PDF](https://www.pwc.co.za/en/assets/pdf/sa-major-banks-analysis-march-2026.pdf)).
- `[OBS]` Big-four combined headline earnings **R135bn+ (+9% y/y)** (BusinessDay / PwC, March 2026)
  ([IdeaToola](https://ideatoola.com/insights/banking-market-share-dashboard)).
- `[OBS]` Digital banks are **growing faster than the big banks in number of customers**
  ([Who Owns Whom](https://www.whoownswhom.co.za/store/banking-industry-including-stokvels-south-africa/)).
