# Gate.io / Gate — Overview

## What it is
**Gate** (formerly **Gate.io**) is one of the oldest global **cryptocurrency exchanges**, offering spot, derivatives, margin, copy trading, ETFs, stocks and Web3 services. [OBS]

## History
- Founded **April 2013** in China by **Dr. Lin Han** as **Bter.com**. [OBS] ([CMC](https://coinmarketcap.com/exchanges/gate/))
- Relocated offshore after China's **Sept 2017** crypto restrictions; **rebranded Gate.io in late 2017**; HQ now **George Town, Cayman Islands**. [OBS] ([wikipedia](https://pt.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gate.io))
- **2025:** major brand upgrade to simply **"Gate"**. [OBS] ([exchangereviewlab](https://exchangereviewlab.com/is-gate-io-safe-or-a-scam/))
