# Gate.io / Gate — Funding & Valuation

## Ownership / funding
- Gate is a **private company** (Gate Technology Inc., Cayman Islands); it has **no disclosed VC equity rounds** and is **not listed**. [OBS]/[DER] ([zipmex](https://zipmex.com/blog/what-country-is-gate-io-from/))
- Founded and led by **Dr. Lin Han** (PhD Optoelectronics); no outside institutional investors publicly disclosed. [OBS] ([CMC](https://coinmarketcap.com/exchanges/gate/))
- Growth has been **self-funded from trading-fee revenue**. [INF]

## "Valuation" proxies
- The closest public proxy is the **GT token market cap** and the exchange's **reserves** ($8.215B, Aug 2026). [OBS]

## Reading [INF]
- Gate is the **anti-pattern** to Sorare/Polymarket: a **bootstrapped, profitable exchange** grown on fees, not venture capital. For the NeoBank, this underscores that **fee-based financial platforms can scale without external equity** if unit economics are strong.
