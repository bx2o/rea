# Gate.io / Gate — Tokenomics

## GateToken (GT)
- **GT** is the exchange/platform token. [OBS] ([Gate GT page](https://www.gate.com/zh/gt))
- **Deflationary burn model**: Gate conducts **quarterly on-chain burns** funded from business/revenue. [OBS]
- **Cumulative burns:**
  - By **Q3 2026**: **191,934,541 GT** burned, worth **>$1.504B**; that quarter burned **1,987,321 GT** (~$22.35M). [OBS] ([Gate blog](https://www.gate.com/blog/gatetoken-gt-burns-nearly-2-million-tokens-in-q3-2026-further-strengthening-its-long-term-value-foundation))
  - By **Oct 2025**: **182,655,526 GT** burned — a **60.88% reduction** from the original **300,000,000** supply; cumulative burn value ~**$2.955B**. [OBS] ([CoinStats](https://coinstats.app/news/6f7c930e9c65b85275ad0910c51b75efb310383bd339e915d00293298c28a24b_Gate-Releases-October-Transparency-Report:-Steady-Growth-and-Ecosystem-Prosperity-Lead-the-Way-for-High-Quality-Industry-Development/))
- **Utility**: fee discounts (paying fees with GT reduces spot fees), VIP benefits, ecosystem access. [OBS]

## Supply trajectory
- Original supply **300M GT**; ~**60–64% already burned** → supply is materially deflationary over time. [OBS]/[DER]
- Total supply and current circulating figures are not fully consolidated publicly: [UNK].

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- GT is a **revenue-linked deflationary token** (burns funded by fees) — similar in spirit to Chiliz's revenue-funded buyback, and a cleaner model than emission-based loyalty tokens. If a NeoBank ever issued a rewards token, a **fee-funded burn** is the most defensible value mechanism.
