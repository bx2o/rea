# Gate.io / Gate — Products & Mechanics

## Trading products
- **Spot** — 4,400–4,800+ crypto assets. [OBS]
- **Futures / derivatives** — leverage up to **100x**; 12.2% derivatives market share (Mar 2026). [OBS]
- **Margin trading**, **copy trading**, **ETFs**. [OBS]
- **P2P / fiat on-ramps**. [INF]

## Web3 / wealth
- **Web3 wallet**, NFT marketplace, **Gate Layer** (own L2; >100M addresses, Jan 2026). [OBS] ([Gate Jan 2026 report](https://bi.live/announcements/article/49713))
- **Wealth management** — interest-earning on account assets, structured products. [OBS]

## TradFi expansion ("Gate TradFi")
- **Zero-fee US stocks & ETFs**, **gStocks**, **stock copy trading**, **IPO Access** (e.g., Jersey Mike's Phase 2). [OBS] ([Gate July 2026 report](https://www.gate.com/announcements/article/101129))
- Multi-asset: crypto + stocks, ETFs, forex, metals, indices, commodities (12,500+ traditional assets/ETFs). [OBS] ([Gate Q2 2026](https://www.gate.ac/pt-br/blog/gate-releases-q2-2026-report-user-base-surpasses-58-million-as-spot-trading-volume-ranks-top-3-globally))

## Transparency mechanics
- **Proof of Reserves** using **zk-SNARK zero-knowledge proofs + Merkle tree verification**; quarterly reserve reports. [OBS] ([CoinStats Oct report](https://coinstats.app/news/6f7c930e9c65b85275ad0910c51b75efb310383bd339e915d00293298c28a24b_Gate-Releases-October-Transparency-Report:-Steady-Growth-and-Ecosystem-Prosperity-Lead-the-Way-for-High-Quality-Industry-Development/))
- Reserve ratio **117% (Jul 2026) → 125% (Jan 2026) → 127% ($8.215B, Aug 2026)**. [OBS]
