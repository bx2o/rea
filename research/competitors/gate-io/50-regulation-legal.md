# Gate.io / Gate — Regulation & Legal

## Licensing & compliance
- Gate holds licences/registrations across multiple jurisdictions: **Malta (MiCA), Dubai (VARA), Japan (FSA), Gibraltar (GFSC), Cyprus (CySEC), Australia (AUSTRAC)** — **9 licences** per one source. [OBS] ([Gate licenses guide](https://www.gate.com/learn/articles/gatecom-licenses-global-regulatory-guide), [cryptonomist](https://ar.cryptonomist.ch/2026/09/21/))
- **Gate US** holds multiple **Money Transmitter Licenses (MTLs)** — **36 US state-level MTLs** as of mid-2026 (NMLS ID **2272810**). [OBS]
- Main platform **blocks US, UK and Japan** residents; US users are directed to Gate US. [OBS]

## Compliance measures
- **Proof of Reserves** via zk-SNARK + Merkle trees; **reserve ratio 117–127%**. [OBS]
- AML/KYC as a CASP/VASP under applicable regimes. [INF]

## South Africa applicability [INF]
- In SA, crypto **exchanges must register as Crypto Asset Service Providers (CASPs)** with the **FSCA** (crypto assets = financial products under FAIS since Oct 2022) and comply with **FICA** as accountable institutions (since 19 Dec 2022). [OBS] ([CMS](https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/cms-expert-guide-to-crypto-regulation/south-africa), [BitcoinChaser](https://bitcoinchaser.com/country/south-africa/))
- Gate does **not** publicly hold an SA FSP/CASP licence: **[UNK]/[INF]** — SA users would access via the offshore platform.
- **Net read-across:** any SA NeoBank offering crypto/trading must obtain FSCA CASP authorisation and FICA compliance; using an offshore exchange's rails for SA consumers carries **licensing risk**. [INF]
