# Gate.io / Gate — Regulation & Legal

## Licensing & compliance
- Gate holds licences/registrations across multiple jurisdictions: **Malta (MiCA), Dubai (VARA), Japan (FSA), Gibraltar (GFSC), Cyprus (CySEC), Australia (AUSTRAC)** — **9 licences** per one source. [OBS] ([Gate licenses guide](https://www.gate.com/learn/articles/gatecom-licenses-global-regulatory-guide), [cryptonomist](https://ar.cryptonomist.ch/2026/09/21/))
- **Gate US** holds multiple **Money Transmitter Licenses (MTLs)** — **36 US state-level MTLs** as of mid-2026 (NMLS ID **2272810**). [OBS]
- Main platform **blocks US, UK and Japan** residents; US users are directed to Gate US. [OBS]

## Compliance measures
- **Proof of Reserves** via zk-SNARK + Merkle trees; **reserve ratio 117–127%**. [OBS]
- AML/KYC as a CASP/VASP under applicable regimes. [INF]

## South Africa applicability [INF]
- In SA, crypto **exchanges must register as Crypto Asset Service Providers (CASPs)** with the **FSCA** (crypto assets = financial products under FAIS since Oct 2022) and comply with **FICA** as accountable institutions (since 19 Dec 2022). [OBS] ([CMS](https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/cms-expert-guide-to-crypto-regulation/south-africa), [BitcoinChaser](https://bitcoinchaser.com/country/south-africa/))
- Gate does **not** publicly hold an SA FSP/CASP licence: **[UNK]/[INF]** — SA users would access via the offshore platform.
- **Net read-across:** any SA NeoBank offering crypto/trading must obtain FSCA CASP authorisation and FICA compliance; using an offshore exchange's rails for SA consumers carries **licensing risk**. [INF]

## Corrections / Additions
- **Addition — SA crypto is not money (2026).** **Joint Communication 1 of 2026 (28 May 2026)**, issued jointly
  by **SARB, FSCA, the Prudential Authority and the FIC**, confirmed that crypto assets **including stablecoins
  are not money or legal tender** and fall **outside the National Payment System Act** for domestic payment
  purposes. Any SA NeoBank crypto/stablecoin payment feature must account for this. [OBS]
  ([SARB joint communication](https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/regulation-oversight-and-supervision/designation-notices/280526%20Joint%20Communication_Crypto%20assets%20for%20domestic%20payment%20purposes.pdf), [Payments.gi](https://payments.gi/jurisdictions/south-africa/))
- **Addition — cross-border crypto authorisation.** National Treasury + SARB's **draft Capital Flow Management
  Regulations, 2026** and **draft Crypto Asset Manual for Cross-Border Activities** would require CASPs
  facilitating **international** crypto transfers to obtain **separate FinSurv (SARB) authorisation** on top of
  FSP/FICA requirements, with transaction reporting. A threshold restricts buying/selling/loaning crypto outside
  authorised CASPs. [OBS] ([National Treasury draft](https://www.treasury.gov.za/public%20comments/CapFlow/Draft%20Capital%20Flow%20Management%20Regulations%202026%20April.pdf), [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/08/south-africa-proposed-framework-for-cross-border-crypto-asset-transactions), [ENSafrica](https://www.ensafrica.com/news/detail/12226/crypto-crosses-the-border-sarb-maps-the-next-))
- **Addition — CASP licensing anchor.** The "FSCA CASP licence" is legally an **FSP authorisation under FAIS**
  (crypto subcategory **1.28**) following the **FSCA General Notice 1350 of 2022** (19 Oct 2022) declaration;
  CASPs are also **FICA accountable institutions** (from 19 Dec 2022). [OBS]
  ([Cliffe Dekker Hofmeyr](https://www.cliffedekkerhofmeyr.com/export/sites/cdh/news/publications/2022/Practice/Finance/Downloads/Finance-Banking-Alert-20-October-2022.pdf), [Licentium](https://www.licentium.io/post/south-africa-casp-licensing))
- **Addition — SARB mandate.** SARB "does not currently oversee, supervise or regulate crypto assets" and its
  position remains as per the **2014 Position Paper on Virtual Currencies**; crypto is not legal tender. [OBS]
  ([SARB FinSurv FAQ](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/financial-surveillance/FinSurvFAQ))
