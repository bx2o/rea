# Gate.io / Gate — Business Model

## Revenue streams
1. **Trading fees** — spot and derivatives. Base spot **0.1% maker/taker** (0.09% paying with GT); futures base **0.02% maker / 0.05% taker**; VIP tiers lower further. Some sources cite spot as high as **~0.2%**. [OBS] ([gatereview](https://skrumble.com/it/crypto-exchanges/gate-io-review/), [gate fees](https://www.gate.com/learn/articles/gatecom-fees-explained))
2. **Withdrawal fees** (dynamic, network-congestion based); free crypto deposits. [OBS]
3. **Futures funding**, NFT, Web3 operations. [OBS]
4. **Wealth management / interest-earning on account assets.** [OBS]
5. **TradFi expansion**: **zero-fee US stock/ETF trading**, **gStocks**, stock copy trading, IPO access subscriptions. [OBS] ([Gate July 2026 report](https://www.gate.com/announcements/article/101129))
6. **Token deflation**: quarterly **GT burns** funded from business revenue (see tokenomics). [OBS]

## Model characteristics
- **Multi-asset venue**: crypto + **stocks/ETFs, forex, metals, indices, commodities** (12,500+ traditional assets + ETFs). [OBS] ([Gate Q2 2026](https://www.gate.ac/pt-br/blog/gate-releases-q2-2026-report-user-base-surpasses-58-million-as-spot-trading-volume-ranks-top-3-globally))
- **High-frequency / high-leverage**: futures with **leverage up to 100x**. [OBS]
- **Liquidity & scale**: **top-3 global spot trading volume**; derivatives share **12.2%** (March 2026, all-time high). [OBS] ([Gate March 2026 report](https://apim.gateapi.io/id/post/topic/GateMarchTransparencyReport))

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- Gate monetises **transaction velocity** (spot + derivatives) with **no lending book** — the opposite of a bank's NII model. Its **cross-asset expansion (crypto → equities)** signals that "trading + investing" is becoming a **single consumer super-app category**, relevant to a Neobank's investment feature.
