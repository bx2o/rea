# Gate.io / Gate — Marketing & Sponsorships

## Marketing approach
- Gate markets heavily through **transparency reports** and **user-metrics PR**: monthly "Transparency Report" releases emphasising **derivatives share, reserve ratio, users, liquidity**. [OBS] ([Gate March 2026](https://apim.gateapi.io/id/post/topic/GateMarchTransparencyReport))
- Positions itself as **"top-3 global"** by spot volume and a leader in **industry transparency**. [OBS]

## Sponsorship footprint
- Gate has used **sports/sponsorship branding** as a global-growth tactic (typical of exchanges), but specific current deals are not clearly documented in the sources gathered: **[UNK]**. [OBS]
- Earlier industry pattern: crypto exchanges sponsor sports (e.g., F1/football); Gate's specific deals: [UNK].

## Metrics used as marketing
- **>58M users (Q2 2026)**; earlier **>51M (Mar 2026)**. [OBS]
- **Total reserves $8.215B, ratio 127% (Aug 2026)**. [OBS] ([Gate PoR](https://www.gateioservicefront.com/blog/gate-releases-latest-proof-of-reserves-report-total-reserve-ratio-reaches-127-total-reserves-exceed-8.215-billion))
- **Derivatives market share 12.2%** (Mar 2026, all-time high). [OBS]

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- "**Transparency-as-marketing**" (proof of reserves, ratios, user counts) is a trust-building tactic a NeoBank could adopt for **deposit-safety and solvency communication**.
