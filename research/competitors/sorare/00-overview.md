# Sorare — Overview

## What it is
Sorare is a Paris-based **blockchain fantasy-sports platform** built around officially licensed digital player cards issued as NFTs. [OBS] Founded **1 October 2018** in Paris by **Nicolas Julia (CEO)** and **Adrien Montfort (CTO)**, with the game launching in 2019. [OBS] ([fr.wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorare))

## Positioning
- Fantasy football first, expanded to **NBA** and **MLB**; licensed via **FIFPRO** and club/league deals. [OBS] ([bitbase](https://www.bitbase.com/blog/sorare-vs-fifa-collect))
- Peaked during the 2021 NFT boom; is now in a **restructuring / return-to-profitability** phase. [OBS] ([blockchaingamer](https://www.blockchaingamer.biz/news/42931/sorare-raises-10-million-rebuilds-profitability/))

## Why it matters for the SA NeoBank
Sorare is the reference case for **licensed digital-collectible + fantasy-gamification** mechanics — directly analogous to a NeoBank loyalty/gamification stack. Its regulatory collision (UK Gambling Commission prosecution, frozen Premier League sponsorship money) is the single most instructive cautionary tale for tokenised fan rewards in a strict licensing environment such as South Africa's National Gambling Act.
