# Sorare — Business Model

## Revenue model
1. **Primary market (card issuance)** — Sorare mints a limited supply of player-card NFTs per season and sells/auctions them directly; this captures full initial proceeds. [OBS] ([grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Sorare))
2. **Secondary-market commission — 5%** on manager-to-manager sales (per seller guides). [OBS] ([ludo9](https://www.ludo9.com/sorare/cartes-sorare/vendre-cartes-sorare/prix-de-vente-cartes-sorare/))
3. **Free-to-play / premium currency** — freemium model: Common cards are free at sign-up; **Arcade** free-to-play mode monetises via pack purchases and premium currency. [OBS] ([fr.wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorare))
4. **Licensing cost side** — pays clubs/leagues for official rights (e.g., a four-year Premier League deal reported ~$140M). [OBS]

## Mechanics that drive monetisation
- **Scarcity ladder** creates perceived value: Limited (1,000/season), Rare (100), Super Rare (10), Unique (1). [OBS] ([sorare help](https://sorare.com/pt/help/a/4406429217053/understanding-sorare-cards-pro-set-and-scarcity-levels))
- Auctions run 2–4 days; demand concentrated in final minutes. [OBS]
- Bought-with-market-credit cards locked 14 days before resale. [OBS] ([ludo9](https://www.ludo9.com/sorare/cartes-sorare/vendre-cartes-sorare/prix-de-vente-cartes-sorare/))

## Unit economics / trajectory [OBS]
- Revenue **fell €59M (2023) → €43M (2024)** as NFT demand cooled. [OBS]
- CEO reported **net revenue +85% YoY in H1 2026**; **Arcade** targeted at **€15M–€20M net revenue in 2026**. [OBS] ([blockchaingamer](https://www.blockchaingamer.biz/news/42931/sorare-raises-10-million-rebuilds-profitability/))
- The pivot from high-cost licensed scarcity cards to **free-to-play + reduced licensing costs** is the core strategic shift. [OBS]/[INF]

## Read-across for the NeoBank [INF]
- A "mint scarce, sell primary, take a % on resale" loop is a **marketplace take-rate model** — margin comes from issuance + 5% secondary fee, not from holding inventory.
- Licensing cost is the dominant variable cost; a NeoBank building fan mechanics must decide whether to **license IP** (expensive, regulated) or build **own-brand collectibles** (cheaper, weaker pull).
