## Why it matters [INF]
- Sorare shows the **VC-to-token-marketplace** pattern: raise a large equity round on NFT-boom multiples, then retrench when secondary demand normalises. For the NeoBank this argues against **capitalising a loyalty programme on speculative asset appreciation**.
