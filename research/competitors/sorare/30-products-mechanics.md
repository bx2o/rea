# Sorare — Products & Mechanics

## Core product: digital player cards (NFTs)
- Cards are **officially licensed** digital collectibles issued as NFTs; each represents a specific player for a specific season. [OBS] ([zugestates](https://zugestates.com/platforms/sorare-review-sports-card-nft-platform-2026/))
- Two-layer design: **(1) ownership layer** (wallet-held, transferable NFT) and **(2) game layer** (cards compose fantasy line-ups whose rewards depend on real-world performance). [OBS]

## Scarcity tiers
Sorare documentation cites per-player-per-season mints (note: sources differ, likely by sport/season): [OBS]
- **Unique** — 1 per player per season.
- **Super Rare** — 10–100 per player per season.
- **Rare** — 100–1,000 per player per season.
- **Limited** — 1,000–5,000 per player per season.
- **Common** — free, not resellable.

([sorare help](https://sorare.com/tr/help/a/4402780446097/what-is-a-sorare-card), [sorare help scarcity](https://sorare.com/pt/help/a/4406429217053/understanding-sorare-cards-pro-set-and-scarcity-levels))

## Gameplay mechanics
- **Auctions**: new cards daily; highest bid wins; 2–4 day windows. [OBS] ([sorare-guide](https://sorare-guide.net/en/sorare-beginners-guide/))
- **Fantasy contests**: assemble line-ups; scoring driven by real match stats; rewards in cards/packs. [OBS]
- **Arcade (free-to-play)**: collect cards, build teams, progress through challenges; earn packs via play or buy premium currency. [OBS] ([blockchaingamer](https://www.blockchaingamer.biz/news/42931/sorare-raises-10-million-rebuilds-profitability/))

## Mechanics worth copying for the NeoBank [INF]
- **Scarcity tiers** → natural loyalty tiers (bronze/silver/gold/platinum rewards).
- **Auction/last-minute bidding** → drives engagement spikes.
- **Free-to-play entry + paid upgrade** → low-CAC funnel that converts a minority to payers.

## Risks
- **NFT illiquidity** and price collapse (see tokenomics / regulation files) create reputational risk if framed as "investment." [INF]
