# Sorare — Regulation & Legal

## UK Gambling Commission prosecution
- **Sept 2024:** The UK Gambling Commission charged Sorare with **three counts of providing facilities for gambling without an operating licence**, contrary to the Gambling Act 2005 (ss. 33 & 36), covering conduct from 2021. [OBS] ([bitbase](https://www.bitbase.com/blog/sorare-vs-fifa-collect))
- **4 Oct 2024:** Sorare entered **not-guilty pleas** at Birmingham Magistrates' Court on all three counts. [OBS]
- **Trial listed for June 2027** (reported 7 June 2027 / 20 June 2027 depending on source). [OBS] ([fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/01/crypto-fantasy-platform-premier-league-13-million-uk-criminal-probe/))
- It is described as the **regulator's first action against a blockchain-based platform**, and the UKGC "rarely prosecutes at all." [OBS] ([lbk.pub](https://www.lbk.pub/news/uks-national-crime-agency-freezes-13-6m-of-premier-league-money-in-sorare-probe-report))

## National Crime Agency (NCA) freeze
- **Aug/Sept 2026:** The **NCA froze £10,024,041.33 (~$13.5M)** held in a **Premier League** bank account under the **Proceeds of Crime Act**. [OBS] ([fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/01/crypto-fantasy-platform-premier-league-13-million-uk-criminal-probe/))
- The money was the **opening instalment of Sorare's four-year Premier League sponsorship deal**. The freeze stems from a **civil investigation into suspected third-party criminality**; the NCA did not specify the crimes. [OBS]
- Runs alongside the UKGC prosecution and an **FCA warning to clubs over unauthorised crypto sponsors**. [OBS] ([cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/crypto-sports-uk-freezes-sorare-payment/))

## France
- France's regulator focused on **Web3-based gaming** rather than banning it, with Sorare explicitly cited as the driver; France regulated advertising to minors / provisions around NFTs. [OBS] ([IMGL](https://www.imgl.org/publications/imgl-magazine-volume-3-no-1/regulating-gambling-in-france-in-the-digital-age/))
