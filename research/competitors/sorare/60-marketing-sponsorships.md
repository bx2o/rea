# Sorare — Marketing & Sponsorships

## Premier League partnership (flagship)
- **2023:** Sorare signed a **four-year Premier League deal reported at ~$140M**, granting rights to release licensed digital player cards. [OBS] ([fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/01/crypto-fantasy-platform-premier-league-13-million-uk-criminal-probe/))
- The **opening instalment (~£10M)** was later frozen by the NCA (see regulation file). [OBS]
- Deal narrative: cards give fans "a new way to feel closer to the Premier League." [OBS]

## Club / league footprint
- Licensing via **FIFPRO** plus individual clubs; one source cites **245+ football clubs** including Real Madrid, Liverpool, PSG. [OBS] ([coinmarketcap](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/what-is-starkware-the-ultimate-guide-to-the-starkware-starknet-and-starkex-ecosystem))
- Expanded to **NBA** and **MLB**. [OBS] ([bitbase](https://www.bitbase.com/blog/sorare-vs-fifa-collect))

## Athlete/celebrity alignment
- Athlete investors (e.g., **Kylian Mbappé**) act as marketing amplifiers. [OBS]

## Strategic reading [INF]
- Sorare's sponsorship strategy is **IP-licensing-as-marketing**: pay leagues for official rights that both enable the product and buy brand legitimacy. The 2026 NCA freeze shows this strategy carries **counterparty/regulatory risk** — the "crypto sports sponsorship boom" is under UK scrutiny. [OBS]/[INF]
