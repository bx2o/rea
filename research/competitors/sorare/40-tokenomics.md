# Sorare — Tokenomics

## Does Sorare have a token?
- **Sorare does not issue a fungible platform token.** Its "token" layer is the **NFT player cards** (ERC-standard NFTs) plus in-app credits/currency. [OBS]/[DER] ([zugestates](https://zugestates.com/platforms/sorare-review-sports-card-nft-platform-2026/))
- This distinguishes Sorare from **Socios/Chiliz** (fungible CHZ + Fan Tokens). [INF]

## NFT supply model
- Supply is **fixed per player per season** by rarity tier (Unique=1, Super Rare≤100, Rare≤1000, Limited≤5000), creating artificial scarcity. [OBS] ([sorare help](https://sorare.com/tr/help/a/4402780446097/what-is-a-sorare-card))
- Cards are transferable, wallet-held, and traded on the Sorare marketplace with a **5% platform commission**. [OBS]

## Value mechanics
- Card prices vary with **player form, rarity, and fantasy utility**; swept from a few euros (Limited) to six figures (Unique). [OBS]
- Secondary trading is the primary "tokenomics" — value is **demand/speculation-driven**, not staking- or yield-based. [DER]

## Economic risk
- No native token means no protocol-level inflation/deflation levers; instead Sorare controls value via **minting supply**, which aligns supply with licensing seasons. [INF]
