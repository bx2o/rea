# Fanatics — Products & Mechanics

## Product lines
1. **Fanatics Commerce** — licensed sports merchandise; **Lids** retail; official on-site retail for NFL events and **FIFA World Cup 2026** (104 matches). [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/fanatics/))
2. **Fanatics Collectibles (Topps)** — trading cards & memorabilia; exclusive rights NFL, NBA, MLB, WWE, Disney, Premier League, FIFA (2031). Topps' card revenue grew **$368M (2020) → ~$1.6B (2024)**. [OBS] ([cardcapsule](https://cardcapsule.com/blogs/blog/fanatics-acquisition-of-topps))
3. **Fanatics Betting & Gaming** — **sportsbook (24 states)**, **Fanatics Casino** (MI/NJ/PA/WV), **Fanatics Markets** (prediction markets, 24 states). [OBS]
4. **Fanatics Advertising** — FAN + Sports Video Network. [OBS]
5. **Fanatics Live** — live shopping (acquired **Voggt**, 500k+ members). [OBS]
6. **Fanatics Studios** — content JV with OBB Media. [OBS]

## Key mechanics
- **Collectibles commerce**: live "card breaks," marketplace (**Fanatics Collect**, **PWCC**), **Fanatics Fest** (200k attendees). [OBS] ([cardcapsule](https://cardcapsule.com/blogs/blog/fanatics-acquisition-of-topps))
- **Fanatics ONE loyalty** — 5–10% FanCash back, redeemable across apparel, collectibles, tickets, experiences; ~**10M converted FanCash users** at launch. [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/fanatics/))
- **FanCash**: 1–5% of stake back as site credit; "the only US sportsbook where loyalty cashback buys jerseys/jackets/cards." [OBS] ([tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/sports-betting/platforms/fanatics-sportsbook-review/))
- **Branded credit card** integrating betting, merchandise, ticketing. [OBS]

## Notable
- **Topps acquisition** (4 Jan 2022) for ~**$500M**; NFL/NFLPA 20-year exclusive; first Topps Chrome Football set April 2026. [OBS]
- Competitive threat to Panini/Upper Deck (locked out of key leagues). [OBS]

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- **FanCash** is a **non-crypto loyalty currency** redeemable across a real product ecosystem — the safest, most proven engagement mechanic. Fanatics shows **collectibles + live commerce + loyalty** can drive engagement without any token. [INF]
