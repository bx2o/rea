# Fanatics — Marketing & Sponsorships

## Partnership & licensing engine
- **Exclusive long-term league deals** (NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, FIFA, WWE, Disney, Premier League) with **equity alignment** — leagues own ~10% of Fanatics. [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/fanatics/))
- **Nike three-way partnership** to manufacture/distribute Nike-branded NFL apparel. [OBS] ([fanatics history](https://www.fanaticsinc.com/our-history))
- Officially the **NFL's on-site retail partner** for the Draft, Super Bowl, Kickoff, International Games, Pro Bowl; **FIFA World Cup 2026** exclusive on-site retail licensee. [OBS]

## Events & brand
- **Fanatics Fest** — the largest sports festival; **200,000 fans** expected in NYC; celebrity participation (LeBron James, Tom Brady, etc.). [OBS] ([fanatics](https://www.fanaticsinc.com/michael-rubin/))
- **Fanatics Live** — live shopping (Voggt acquisition for Europe). [OBS]
- **Advertising**: **Fanatics Advertising Network (FAN)** + **Sports Video Network (SVN)** reach **100M+ sports fans**; led by ex-Netflix/Snap/Amazon exec **Jeremi Gorman**. [OBS]
- **Betting marketing**: Fanatics plans to spend up to **$1B on betting ads** (2026). [OBS] ([bookmakersreview](https://www.bookmakersreview.com/industry/fanatics-to-drop-1-billion-on-betting-ads/))

## People
- **Michael Rubin** — Founder & CEO; **22,000+ employees**, 80 offices. [OBS]
- **Tom Brady** joined as an investor/partner. [OBS] ([tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/sports-betting/platforms/fanatics-sportsbook-review/))

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- Fanatics' marketing is **IP-anchored and event-led** (league rights, festivals, live commerce). A NeoBank's sports-adjacency strategy could mirror this by **owning a fan event/experience** rather than buying ad inventory.
