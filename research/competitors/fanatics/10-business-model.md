# Fanatics — Business Model

## Revenue segments (2025 est.) [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/fanatics/))
| Segment | Revenue (approx.) | Share | Margin note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commerce | ~$7B | ~54% | apparel/merchandise |
| Collectibles | ~$5B | ~38% | **highest margin (>20% EBITDA)** |
| Betting & Gaming | ~$1.6B | ~12% | pre-profitability |
| Advertising | [UNK] | — | audience monetisation |

- ~**75% of sales are direct-to-consumer**. NFL and MLB each ~$3B; soccer ~$1.3B; international 10–15%. [OBS]
- Processes **>40M e-commerce transactions/year** and 25M via **Lids** stores. [OBS]

## Core model: vertical integration + exclusive licensing
- **Exclusive, long-term (15–20 yr) licensing** with NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, FIFA, WWE, Disney, Premier League. [OBS]
- **Equity-for-alignment**: leagues/players/owners own ~**10% of Fanatics**; partners get **6–8% revenue share** on retail sales. [OBS]
- **"V-commerce"**: just-in-time manufacturing of hot-market items → **40%+ gross margins** vs 15–20% for traditional retail. [OBS]

## Monetisation beyond retail
- **Betting & Gaming**: sportsbook (24 states), **Fanatics Casino** (MI/NJ/PA/WV), **Fanatics Markets** (prediction markets, 24 states). [OBS]
- **Loyalty**: **Fanatics ONE** — 5% FanCash back in-app, up to 10% on sportsbook bets, redeemable for apparel/collectibles/tickets/experiences. [OBS]
- **Branded credit card** (announced Jan 2026, spring 2026 launch) integrating betting, merchandise and ticketing — a **consumer-finance monetisation layer**. [OBS]
- **Fanatics Collectibles**: exclusive trading card rights (NFL, NBA, MLB, WWE, Disney, Premier League; FIFA from 2031). [OBS]

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- Fanatics is the **canonical "own the fan, monetise everywhere" model**: acquisition via commerce, margin via collectibles, engagement via loyalty, expansion via betting + credit. For a NeoBank, the analogue is **acquire via core account, monetise via cross-sell + loyalty + credit**. The **branded credit card integrated with rewards** is the single most transferable element.
