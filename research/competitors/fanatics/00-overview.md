# Fanatics — Overview

## What it is
Fanatics is a **vertically integrated global digital sports platform** spanning **commerce (merchandise), collectibles, betting & gaming, and advertising**. [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/fanatics/))

## History
- **1995:** Michael Rubin launched **GSI Commerce** (ecommerce-storefront-as-a-service for brands and leagues); sold to **eBay for $2.4B in 2011**, bought back the sports division, and rolled up licensed sports retailers — adopting the "Fanatics" name. [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/fanatics/))
- **Aug 2021:** reimagined as a **digital sports platform** with three businesses — merchandise, collectibles, sports betting; Rubin became CEO of the platform. [OBS] ([fanatics history](https://www.fanaticsinc.com/our-history))

## Why it matters for the SA NeoBank
Fanatics is the **loyalty + commerce + collectibles + betting super-platform** — arguably the closest thing to a full "fan engagement NeoBank." Its **Fanatics ONE** loyalty programme (cash-back → merchandise/collectibles/bets) and **branded credit card** are directly analogous to a NeoBank loyalty/payments stack. [INF]
