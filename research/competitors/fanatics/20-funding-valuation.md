# Fanatics — Funding & Valuation

## Funding & valuation history
| Date | Round | Valuation | Notes | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jun 2013 | Funding | ~$3B | led by **Temasek** & **Alibaba** ($170M) | [OBS] |
| Mar 2022 | Series H | **$27B** | NFL, MLB, MLBPA, NHL, QIA, BlackRock, Fidelity | [OBS] |
| **Dec 2022** | **Series I** | **$31B** | **Clearlake Capital**; ~$700M raised | [OBS] |

## Key points
- **Total raised ≈ $4.8–4.9B.** [OBS] ([revenuememo](https://www.revenuememo.com/p/who-owns-fanatics))
- Key investors: **SoftBank, Silver Lake, Fidelity, Tiger Global, DST Global, Clearlake**, plus strategic league/player stakes. [OBS]
- **Leagues/players/owners own ~10%** of Fanatics (equity alignment). [OBS]
- **IPO**: anticipated but **not filed**; Rubin says "absolutely no rush." ~$1B cash. [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/fanatics/))

## Reading [INF]
- Fanatics shows a **roll-up → platform** valuation journey ($3B → $31B → ~$33.5B mark). The **equity-for-alignment** with leagues is both a moat and a governance feature. For the NeoBank, strategic equity with sports/retail partners could similarly align incentives.
