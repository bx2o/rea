# Fanatics — Tech Stack

## Platform architecture
- **E-commerce at scale**: operates e-commerce sites for **900+ teams, leagues, colleges**; **"v-commerce"** just-in-time manufacturing and logistics; **>40M e-commerce transactions/year**. [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/fanatics/))
- **Vertically integrated**: controls manufacturing, distribution and retail (online + Lids physical stores). [OBS]
- **Betting stack**: sportsbook + casino + **Fanatics Markets** built with **Crypto.com Derivatives North America** and **Paragon Global Markets** (CFTC-registered IB). [OBS]
- **Live commerce**: **Fanatics Live** (acquired **Voggt**). [OBS]
- **Loyalty/payments**: **Fanatics ONE** + planned branded **credit card** — implies a modern payments/loyalty platform. [OBS]

## Organisational footprint
- **22,000+ employees, 80 offices globally.** [OBS] ([fanatics](https://www.fanaticsinc.com/michael-rubin/))
- Content: **Fanatics Studios** JV with OBB Media. [OBS]

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- Fanatics is not a crypto company — its edge is **supply-chain + licensing + data (100M+ fans)**. For a NeoBank, the analogous "tech moat" is **proprietary customer data + payments rails**, not blockchain.
