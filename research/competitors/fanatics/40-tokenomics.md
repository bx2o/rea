# Fanatics — Tokenomics

## Token model
- **Fanatics issues no crypto token** and has no on-chain tokenomics. [OBS]/[DER] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/fanatics/))
- Its loyalty "token" is **FanCash** — a **centralised, non-transferable rewards currency** in the **Fanatics ONE** programme. [OBS]
- Physical/digital **collectibles (Topps)** are the "asset" layer, but they are **traditional collectibles**, not NFTs (though card NFTs exist across the hobby). [INF]

## Comparison
- Contrast with Socios (fungible Fan Tokens) and Sorare (NFT player cards): Fanatics deliberately keeps value in **real merchandise, physical cards and cash-back**, avoiding securities/asset-classification risk. [INF]

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- FanCash is the **regulatory-safe template**: a **closed-loop rewards currency** redeemable for goods, not a tradeable speculative asset. This is likely the most appropriate model for a South African NeoBank fan-loyalty programme, given the NGA/FSCA environment.
