# Socios.com / Chiliz — Tokenomics

## CHZ (platform token)
- **Initial supply: 8,888,888,888 CHZ**, minted entirely in 2018; initial sale via **private placement** (no public ICO). [OBS] ([chiliz whitepaper](https://www.chiliz.com/CHZ_whitepaper_v1.1.pdf))
- Supply is now **inflationary/indefinite** under the protocol's consensus mechanism ("Dragon 8" upgrade); expected circulating supply ~**15,050,414,348 CHZ** by end of year 14. [OBS]
- **Current circulating supply ~10.03B (whitepaper 2025) → ~10.5B (Oct 2026 per KuCoin)**. [OBS] ([ainvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/chiliz-chz-3-5-24-monthly-gains-sportfi-catalysts-build-regulatory-overhang-remains-2610/))
- Inflation split: **65% validators/delegators, 10% Community Vault, 25% ecosystem/operations**. [OBS] ([blockworks](https://blockworks.com/token-transparency/filing/chiliz))
- An early source cites a **fixed 8.888B cap**; this **conflicts** with the whitepaper's inflationary model → treat fixed-cap claim as [UNVERIFIED]. ([coinstats](https://coinstats.app/ai/a/fundamental-analysis-chiliz))

## Deflation lever
- **10% of revenue → CHZ buybacks/burns**, permanently removing CHZ from circulation, tying fan-token sales to CHZ scarcity. [OBS]

## Fan Tokens (club tokens)
- Issued per club on Chiliz Chain; **40M BAR at €2** (Barça) is the canonical example. [OBS]
- Extreme price decay: **BAR down ~99% from ATH**. [OBS] ([bitbase](https://www.bitbase.com/blog/what-fan-tokens-actually-vote-on))
- Live BAR: ~**$0.276, market cap ~$7.9M, rank ~#1071**. [OBS] ([coinmarketcap](https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/fc-barcelona-fan-token/))

## Reading [INF]
- CHZ mixes **inflationary validator rewards** with **revenue-funded buybacks** — a hybrid, and one of the first "real-revenue-linked" crypto token models in sport. For the NeoBank this is the most directly replicable **revenue-linked reward currency** idea, if any token is contemplated at all. [INF]
