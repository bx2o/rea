# Socios.com / Chiliz — Overview

## What it is
- **Chiliz** is the sports-focused blockchain (and CHZ token) company founded by **Alexandre Dreyfus** in **2018**; **Socios.com** is its fan-engagement platform, also founded **18 June 2018**. [OBS] ([grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Socios.com))
- Socios pioneered the **"Fan Token"** category: club-branded fungible tokens that grant voting rights, rewards and exclusive experiences. [OBS] ([socios](https://www.socios.com/socios-securitize-partner-tokenized-sports-equity-offerings/))

## Positioning
- Market leader in **sports fan tokens**, pairing blockchain infrastructure (Chiliz Chain) with club IP. [OBS]
- Monetises via **fan token offerings** and **transaction commissions**, sharing revenue with clubs. [OBS]

## Why it matters for the SA NeoBank
Socios is the template for **club-branded loyalty tokens with voting utility** — and its history (ASA rulings, SEC scrutiny, token price collapse ~99%) is the key evidence base for **why speculative fan tokens are risky and increasingly regulated**. [INF]
