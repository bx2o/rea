- Chiliz **did not hold a public ICO** and **accepted no US investors** in the 2018 private placement. [OBS] ([Chiliz/Binance proposal](https://www.chiliz.com/docs/CHZ_binance_proposal.pdf))
- Total raised ≈ **$65–66M** via private placement. [OBS] ([blockworks](https://blockworks.com/token-transparency/filing/chiliz))
- Key early investors: **Binance Labs, OK Blockchain Capital, FBG Capital**. [OBS] ([grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/chiliz))

## Valuation
- Chiliz is a **listed token company** (CHZ trades publicly); it does not have a conventional VC equity valuation. [DER]
- CHZ **market cap ≈ $246M (SGD/market-cap snapshot)**; CHZ peaked far higher in 2021. [OBS] ([coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/en-sg/price/chiliz))
- In the **2026 sports-token rankings**, CHZ showed a **~$4.98B** figure in one CMC sports-token view — treat with caution (likely includes FDV or stale data). [UNVERIFIED] ([coinmarketcap sports](https://coinmarketcap.com/zh-tw/view/sports/))

## Reading [INF]
