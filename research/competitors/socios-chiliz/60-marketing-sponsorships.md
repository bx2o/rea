# Socios.com / Chiliz — Marketing & Sponsorships

## Partnership model
- Socios signs **direct club/league partnerships** that include a **Fan Token launch** (a paid IP + revenue-share arrangement). [OBS] ([Palermo](https://www.socios.com/socios-com-partners-with-palermo-fc/))
- Socios states it has issued Fan Tokens with **more than 70 leading global sports organisations**, and separately that it connects **80+ sports clubs**. [OBS] ([socios](https://www.socios.com/socios-securitize-partner-tokenized-sports-equity-offerings/))

## Named partners (selection)
- **Clubs:** Arsenal, AS Roma, Manchester City, Paris Saint-Germain, FC Barcelona, Atlético de Madrid, Inter Milan, AC Milan, Juventus, Aston Villa, Tottenham Hotspur, Flamengo, SSC Napoli, Palermo, Olympique de Marseille, Valencia CF, AS Monaco. [OBS]
- **National teams:** Argentina, Portugal, Spain, Italy, Belgium. [OBS]
- **Other sports:** UFC. [OBS]
- **Colleges (US):** first-ever US college Fan Tokens with five major university programmes. [OBS] ([socios](https://www.socios.com/socios-launches-first-us-college-fan-tokens/))

## Growth narrative
- Chiliz markets the network as a growing "**60+ team**" then "**70+ organisation**" network, with football leagues (e.g., **K League**) and validators including OKX, Animoca Brands, Ankr, SBI Holdings, EDF Group. [OBS] ([chiliz](https://www.chiliz.com/), [cryptowisser](https://www.cryptowisser.com/news/olympique-de-marseille-joins-chilizs-60-team-fan-token-network/))

## Reading [INF]
- Socios's moat is **club IP breadth**, not technology. Its marketing leans on **club-brand trust** — precisely what regulators (ASA) found risky when consumers don't understand they're buying crypto.
