# Socios.com / Chiliz — Business Model

## Revenue streams
1. **Percentage of Fan Token Offering (FTO) proceeds** — Chiliz keeps a pre-agreed share of each club's token sale. [OBS] ([Chiliz/Binance proposal](https://www.chiliz.com/docs/CHZ_binance_proposal.pdf))
2. **Commissions on Fan Token ⇄ CHZ transactions** — acting as marketplace/exchange provider. [OBS]
3. **Revenue shared with partner clubs**, with the "vast majority" generated in **CHZ** (the exclusive on-platform currency). [OBS]
4. **Planned adjacent services** (on-site payments etc.). [OBS]
5. **Buyback policy**: a strategic **10% of revenue allocated to CHZ buybacks**, linking fan-token commercial success to CHZ deflation. [OBS] ([coinstats](https://coinstats.app/news/293c31e73e16afd6668ee89bd52af3f60f20553bee55138bb397cd9e0fce7e55_Chiliz-CHZ-Buyback-Strategic-10-Revenue-Allocation-Ignites-Tokenomics-Revolution/))

## Mechanics
- Fans buy **CHZ**, then exchange it for club **Fan Tokens**; tokens grant **voting rights in club polls** and access to **VIP rewards/experiences**. [OBS] ([ironwallet](https://ironwallet.io/coins/chiliz/))
- Chiliz takes a **% of initial sales + trading fees**; clubs share in proceeds. [OBS]

## Claimed scale
- Socios reports having **generated over $700M for the sports industry since 2018**. [OBS] ([socios](https://www.socios.com/socios-securitize-partner-tokenized-sports-equity-offerings/))

## Business-model reading [INF]
- Socios is effectively a **two-sided IP marketplace**: it monetises club relationships (issuance) and user trading (commission), while the **CHZ currency captures the ecosystem**.
- The **buyback-funded-from-revenue** loop is the closest thing to "tokenomics tied to real revenue" in the fan-token space — but it depends on continued fan-token sales volume, which collapsed post-2021. [INF]
