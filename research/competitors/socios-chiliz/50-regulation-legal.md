# Socios.com / Chiliz — Regulation & Legal

## UK ASA ruling (Arsenal / Socios)
- The **Advertising Standards Authority** upheld a ruling against **Arsenal FC** over adverts promoting **Socios Fan Tokens**, finding them **"misleading" and "irresponsible"**; they failed to make clear the tokens had to be bought with cryptocurrency, took advantage of fans' inexperience, and didn't warn of trading risk. [OBS] ([ASA](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/arsenal-football-club-plc-a21-1121873-arsenal-football-club-plc-1.html), [Onside Law](https://www.onsidelaw.co.uk/news-update/asa-confirms-ruling-that-arsenals-adverts-for-fan-token-partner-socios-breached-advertising-rules/))
- Ruling was **upheld on independent review (Aug 2022)**; the FCA later took over regulation of "qualifying cryptoasset" ads (8 Oct 2023). [OBS]
- Academic work classifies fan tokens as having **gambling-like features**, raising consumer-protection concerns. [OBS] ([Springer, J. Gambling Studies](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10899-023-10215-0.pdf))

## US SEC scrutiny
- The **SEC has scrutinised CHZ** (in exchange lawsuits) as a **potential unregistered security** under the Howey test; Chiliz engaged with the SEC Crypto Task Force in 2025 to clarify Fan Token non-security status. [OBS] ([grokipedia Chiliz](https://grokipedia.com/page/chiliz))
- A secondary claim that the SEC **fined Chiliz $5M** is **single-source and low-quality [UNVERIFIED]** — do not rely on it. ([fujitac](https://www.fujitac.com/flash/4b3987c8144cd992.html))

## EU MiCA compliance
- **Socios Europe Services Limited became the first sports-focused platform authorised under MiCA**, offering regulated crypto-asset services across all 27 EU countries; CHZ white paper made MiCA-compliant. [OBS] ([socios](https://www.socios.com/the-chiliz-group-celebrates-dual-mica-milestone-european-licence-for-socios-europe-services-and-mica-compliant-chz-white-paper/))

## Class action
- A class action alleged **delayed Fan Token deliveries**; Chiliz/Socios denied the claims, attributing delays to the project's early stage (CHZ not yet listed) and stating outstanding payments were settled. [OBS] ([grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Socios.com))

## South Africa applicability [INF]
- Fan tokens are **not explicitly regulated** in SA; SA treats crypto assets as **financial products** (FSCA/FAIS since Oct 2022) and NFTs likely fall under the broad "crypto asset" definition (CMS view). [OBS] ([cms](https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/cms-expert-guide-to-crypto-regulation/south-africa))
- Because fan tokens are **tradeable at fluctuating prices** and marketed to fans, SA consumer-protection (CPA) and advertising risk mirrors the UK ASA outcome. [INF]
