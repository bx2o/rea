# Socios.com / Chiliz — Products & Mechanics

## Products
1. **Fan Tokens** — club-branded fungible tokens on the Chiliz Chain/Socios platform. [OBS]
2. **Socios.com app** — hub for voting, rewards, polls, VIP experiences, and (per latest whitepaper) fantasy. [OBS]
3. **Socios Equity Tokens** (new) — tokenised equity offerings for sports teams, developed with **Securitize** (separate from Fan Tokens). [OBS] ([socios](https://www.socios.com/socios-securitize-partner-tokenized-sports-equity-offerings/))

## Fan Token mechanics
- Fans buy **CHZ**, swap for a club Fan Token, and use it to **vote in bindings/loyalty polls** (e.g., jersey design, songs), access **rewards and VIP experiences**, and compete in fantasy leagues. [OBS] ([ironwallet](https://ironwallet.io/coins/chiliz/))
- Tokens are **ERC-20-style** contracts with club/platform multisig mint control. [OBS]/[UNVERIFIED] ([fujitac](https://www.fujitac.com/flash/4b3987c8144cd992.html))

## FC Barcelona Fan Token ($BAR) — worked example
- Announced **Feb 2020**; launched **June 2020**; **40 million BAR offered at €2** initial price. [OBS] ([fcbarcelona](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/news/1608032/barca-and-blockchain-platform-chiliz-join-forces-for-new-global-alliance-to-increase-interaction-with-fans))
- All-time high **$72.55 (21 Apr 2021)**; ~**$0.50 (May 2026)** — a fall of **~99%**. [OBS] ([bitbase](https://www.bitbase.com/blog/what-fan-tokens-actually-vote-on))
- Live CMC price **~$0.276 (24h vol ~$3.16M; rank ~#1071)** at time of writing. [OBS] ([coinmarketcap BAR](https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/fc-barcelona-fan-token/))

## Voting reality check
- Fans most often vote on **low-stakes decisions** (kit, songs); analysis argues tokens are mostly **speculative**, not decision-making tools. [OBS]/[INF] ([bitbase](https://www.bitbase.com/blog/what-fan-tokens-actually-vote-on))

## Read-across for the NeoBank [INF]
- "Voting utility" is a **weak engagement hook** on its own; the token's attraction is **speculation + VIP access**. For a NeoBank, **access/rewards** (not investability) is the safer, legally-defensible mechanic.
