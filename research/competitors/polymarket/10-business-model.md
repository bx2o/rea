# Polymarket — Business Model

## Core model
- **On-chain marketplace for trading event outcomes**: users buy/sell binary contracts in USDC that settle to **$1 (event occurs)** or **$0 (does not)**. [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/polymarket/))
- **All trading, clearing, settlement happen on Polygon**; liquidity is **entirely user-provided** — Polymarket takes **no proprietary positions or risk**. [OBS]
- Non-custodial: **UMA's decentralised oracle** writes resolution on-chain; Polymarket "never touches user funds or settlement." [OBS]

## Monetisation (pivoted to fees in 2026)
- Historically **$0 fees** (2025 revenue = $0). [OBS]
- **Taker-fee model rolled out in stages through 2026**: [OBS]
  - Jan 2026 — high-frequency crypto markets.
  - Feb 2026 — select sports markets.
  - Mar 30 2026 — **Fee Structure V2**: taker fees — crypto **0.07**, sports **0.03**, finance/politics/mentions/tech **0.04**, economics/culture/weather/other **0.05**; **geopolitics/world events fee-free**; **makers pay $0** and receive rebates funded by taker fees. [OBS]
  - US exchange (Apr 3 2026) — uniform taker **0.05**, maker rebate **-0.0125**, capped at **$1.25 per 100 contracts at 50¢**. [OBS]
- **Data monetisation**: "Polymarket Signals & Sentiment" distributed via **ICE** terminals (institutional data product). [OBS]

## Revenue
- **~$1B annualised by June 2026** (Sacra estimate), up from $0 in 2025. [EST] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/polymarket/))
- Revenue "driven primarily by taker fees on trading volume." [OBS]

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- Polymarket shows a **capital-light, no-house-risk marketplace**: it monetises **flow (taker fees)** and **data**, not book risk. This is a fundamentally different economics from FanDuel's vig. The **data-as-product** angle (sentiment signals) is the under-appreciated monetisation layer.
