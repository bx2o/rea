# Polymarket — Products & Mechanics

## Core product
- **Binary outcome contracts** on real-world events; each market is a **pair of outcome tokens** priced $0–$1 reflecting implied probability. [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/polymarket/))
- Settlement: winning position → **$1**, losing → **$0**; denominated in **USDC**, settled on **Polygon**. [OBS]
- Resolution via **UMA's decentralised oracle** written on-chain. [OBS]

## Market categories
- Politics, crypto, sports, finance, tech, economics, culture, weather; **geopolitics fee-free**. [OBS]
- **Private-company markets** (from May 2026): valuation milestones, IPO timing, secondary activity — resolution data sourced from **Nasdaq Private Market**. [OBS]
- 2025 top categories: politics, crypto, sports. [OBS] ([spark.money](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-prediction-market-comparison))

## UX / onboarding (key differentiator)
- **60-second wallet-less signup** via MoonPay/Stripe fiat on-ramps and social-login hot wallets. [OBS]
- **Meta-transactions eliminate visible gas costs** — solving the friction that killed Augur/Gnosis. [OBS]
- Acquired **Brahma** (crypto/DeFi infra) to scale operations. [OBS]

## Liquidity & incentives
- **Maker incentives**: performance-based rewards to accounts posting resting limit orders and keeping spreads tight. [OBS]
- Algorithmic matching; every trade executes **user-to-user**, never against the house. [OBS]

## Distribution integrations
- **X (Twitter)** official prediction-market partner (embedded prices). [OBS]
- **Google Finance** and **Yahoo Finance** hubs featuring Polymarket data. [OBS]
- **ICE** distributes "Signals & Sentiment" institutional data. [OBS]

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- The **wallet-less, gas-less, social-login UX** is the single most transferable lesson: mainstream users will not manage seed phrases. Any NeoBank loyalty/collectible offering must be **invisible-crypto**.
