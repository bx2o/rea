## Key points
- **ICE (NYSE parent) became the strategic anchor**: acquired ~17% of outstanding shares (11% fully diluted) via Series D; total carrying value ~$2.0B (~23% outstanding). [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/polymarket/))
- **Total disclosed primary capital ≈ $2.764B.** [OBS]
- Valuation rose ~**15–20x in under 12 months** ($1B → $15B → ~$21B). [OBS]
- Early backers included crypto hedge fund **Polychain Capital** and celebrity investor **Alan Keating**. [OBS] ([forbes.pe](https://forbes.pe/activos-digitales/2024-07-31/conoce-al-joven-de-26-anos-que-construye-un-mercado-de-predicciones-de-1-000-millones-de-dolares))

## Reading [INF]
- Polymarket's ascent is driven by **strategic/financial-institution capital (ICE)** betting that event contracts become a **new asset class**. For the NeoBank, the signal is that prediction-market infrastructure is being **institutionalised**, not fringe.
