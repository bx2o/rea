# Polymarket — Tokenomics

## Does Polymarket have a token?
- **Polymarket does not currently have a public native token.** Its markets are denominated in **USDC** and settled on **Polygon**. [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/polymarket/))
- Reports indicate Polymarket is **weighing a native token** (alongside a possible IPO) as part of expansion. [OBS]/[UNVERIFIED] ([Europesays](https://www.europesays.com/3311972/))

## Implicit "tokenomics"
- Value accrual today is to **equity** (ICE/VC shareholders), not a token. [DER]
- **Outcome share tokens** are the only on-chain tradable instruments: priced 0–1, redeemable at $1/$0. [OBS]
- Fee economics function like a **take-rate on flow**, not a token-emission model. [DER]

## Contrast with the fan-token model [INF]
- Polymarket deliberately **avoided a speculative token** to preserve regulatory clearance (CFTC). This is a relevant design lesson: **token issuance is a regulatory liability**, and prediction markets chose not to carry it. [INF]
