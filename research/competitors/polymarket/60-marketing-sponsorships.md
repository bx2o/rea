# Polymarket — Marketing & Sponsorships

## Distribution-led growth
- Growth is driven by **viral, Twitter-native link previews showing real-time prices** — organic distribution formalised into platform integrations. [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/polymarket/))
- **X (Twitter)** named Polymarket its **official prediction-market partner**, embedding its data. [OBS]
- **Google Finance** and **Yahoo Finance** integrated Polymarket data / launched prediction-market hubs. [OBS]

## Sports & league deals
- **MLB**: signed as **exclusive Official Prediction Market Exchange Partner (March 2026)**, deal **worth up to $300M**, granting MLB marks/logos, official data via **Sportradar**, and an integrity framework with MLB/CFTC cooperation. [OBS] ([crypto.news](https://crypto.news/polymarket-hit-1-billion-in-revenue-and-now-20-states-want-to-shut-it-down/))
- **ATP Tour**: official prediction-market provider **with streaming rights to 20,000 matches**. [OBS]
- Expanded **Sportradar** relationship. [OBS]

## Institutional marketing
- **ICE** distributes "Polymarket Signals & Sentiment" as an institutional data product (launched Feb 2026) — positioning Polymarket as **information infrastructure**, not just a betting site. [OBS]

## Reading [INF]
- Polymarket's marketing is **embedded data distribution** (X/Google/Yahoo) plus **league legitimacy deals** — a playbook a NeoBank could imitate by embedding "fan sentiment" data in partner apps rather than running ads.
