# Polymarket — Overview

## What it is
Polymarket is a **decentralised prediction-market platform** where users trade **binary outcome contracts** (priced $0.01–$0.99) on real-world events, settled in **USDC on the Polygon blockchain**. [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/polymarket/))

## History
- Founded **2020** by **Shayne Coplan** (solo founder, NYU dropout), launched June 2020 from his NYC apartment. First markets: NYC reopening and a COVID vaccine. [OBS] ([polymart](https://polymart.app/blog/polymarket-founder))
- HQ New York. [OBS]
- Forced offshore 2022 by a CFTC order; returned to the US in 2025 after acquiring a CFTC-licensed exchange. [OBS]

## Why it matters for the SA NeoBank
Polymarket is the **fastest-scaling event-contract platform** and the clearest example of prediction markets invading sports betting. It is also the sharpest **US regulatory case study** (CFTC fine → FBI raid → legalisation). In **South Africa, prediction markets sit in a legal gap** — see the regulation file. [INF]
