# Polymarket — Regulation & Legal

## CFTC enforcement (2022)
- **3 Jan 2022:** CFTC issued a **cease-and-desist order** against **Blockratize, Inc. d/b/a Polymarket** for offering **off-exchange event-based binary options** and operating an **unregistered swap facility**, violating CEA §5h(a)(1)/§4c(b) and CFTC Reg. 37.3(a). [OBS] ([marketswiki](https://www.marketswiki.com/wiki/Polymarket_US))
- Polymarket **settled for a $1.4M civil penalty** and had to **block US users** (roughly 3+ years). [OBS] ([britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Polymarket))

## FBI raid & closure (2024–2025)
- **Nov 2024:** FBI raided founder **Shayne Coplan's** apartment; the probe examined whether Polymarket adequately enforced US user restrictions. [OBS] ([revenuememo](https://www.revenuememo.com/p/who-owns-polymarket))
- **July 2025:** the **CFTC and DOJ closed the investigation without charges**, clearing the return to the US. [OBS]

## Return to US market (2025)
- Acquired CFTC-licensed exchange **QCX LLC d/b/a Polymarket US**. [OBS]
- **CFTC contract market designation (effective July 2025)**; **no-action letter** for fully-collateralised binary options clearing via Polymarket Clearing (**Sept 2025**). [OBS]
- **CFTC Amended Order of Designation (Nov 2025)** enabled **intermediated access** via brokerages/FCMs. [OBS]
- A source dates **CFTC approval to Nov 2025**. [OBS] ([predscope](https://predscope.com/guide/polymarket-review))

## State-level opposition (2026)
- **~20 states** moved to shut Polymarket/Kalshi down as unlicensed gambling; **Nevada warned operators** they could jeopardise gambling licences; the **American Gaming Association** condemned league prediction-market deals as "backdoor gambling." [OBS] ([crypto.news](https://crypto.news/polymarket-hit-1-billion-in-revenue-and-now-20-states-want-to-shut-it-down/), [sacra](https://sacra.com/c/polymarket/))

## South Africa applicability [INF]
- **Prediction markets sit in a legal gap in SA**: ENSafrica places them **between crypto, exchange-control and gambling law**; SABA asked regulators to treat them as **exchange betting**, but **no law classifies them either way**. [OBS] ([nextpredict](https://nextpredict.io/legal/africa/))
- SA's **National Gambling Act 2004 (s.11)** prohibits unlicensed gambling; **online casino-style games are illegal**, only provincially-licensed online sports/horse betting is legal. A prediction market is **not clearly licensed**. [OBS] ([bestbitcoincasino](https://www.bestbitcoincasino.com/crypto-casinos-south-africa/))
