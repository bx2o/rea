# FanDuel — Marketing & Sponsorships

## Brand strategy
- **"Huddle Up" / $50B playbook** framing: FanDuel markets itself as the **leading US operator**, leveraging **product superiority (pricing)** and **disciplined customer acquisition**. [OBS] ([flutter blog](https://flutter.com/news-media/blogs/huddle-up-recap-inside-fanduel-s-50-billion-playbook/))
- Marketing emphasis: **"loyalty, product innovation and disciplined generosity"** (from Flutter's 2026 strategy). [OBS] ([flutter factsheet](https://flutter.com/media/k2lju3sf/q2-factsheet-2026.pdf))

## Sponsorships & partnerships
- **League/team sponsorships** across US sports (official betting partner deals), plus media/broadcast integrations. [OBS]/[INF]
- **CME Group** partnership for prediction-market products (FanDuel Predicts). [OBS]
- Named official **sportsbook partners** of major franchises; heavy **TV/media** presence. [INF]

## Metrics
- **14.3M average monthly players (Q2 2026)** across Flutter's US business; #1 sportsbook (39–44% GGR share). [OBS] ([flutter factsheet](https://flutter.com/media/k2lju3sf/q2-factsheet-2026.pdf), [sportbotai](https://www.sportbotai.com/stats/sportsbook-market-share))

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- FanDuel proves that **product quality + loyalty** beats bonus-chasing for sustained share — relevant to a NeoBank trying to avoid a costly cash-back war. Sponsorship of leagues/teams builds **legitimacy and trust**, which also matters for a bank brand.
