# FanDuel — Tech Stack

## Platform
- **Consumer-facing:** iOS/Android sportsbook + casino apps, web platform. [OBS]
- **Backend:** Flutter operates a large-scale, high-availability betting platform (odds engine, risk/wallet, in-play feeds). Specific cloud/vendor stack is not publicly disclosed: [UNK]. [INF]
- **Pricing/odds**: "best-in-class sports pricing" implies a proprietary **trading/odds model** and risk engine. [OBS] ([flutter factsheet](https://flutter.com/media/k2lju3sf/q2-factsheet-2026.pdf))
- **Prediction markets** built in partnership with **CME Group**. [OBS]

## Organisation & leadership
- **CEO:** **Christian Genetski** (President, took over May 2026 after **Amy Howe** left; Howe had been CEO since Feb 2021). [OBS] ([flutter press release](https://flutter.com/news-media/press-releases/flutter-announces-leadership-transition-at-fanduel/))
- **Parent:** Flutter Entertainment (NYSE: FLUT; delisted from LSE 3 Aug 2026). [OBS]
- Founded 2009 in Edinburgh; originally the DFS platform **Hubdub**. [OBS] ([grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/FanDuel))

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- FanDuel is a **regulated, high-scale transactional platform** — the engineering bar (99.99% uptime, real-time odds, fraud/AML) is comparable to a bank's. A NeoBank partnership/integration would be significant.
