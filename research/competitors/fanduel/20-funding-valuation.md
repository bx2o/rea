## Notes
- **2018 merger:** FanDuel's board valued its stake at **$465M** in the merger; Flutter's initial 57.8% stake valued at **$158M plus contributed US assets**. [OBS] ([empor.top](https://empor.top/us/FLUT))
- No cash went to FanDuel common holders in the 2018 Paddy Power deal. [OBS] ([revenuememo](https://www.revenuememo.com/p/who-owns-fanduel))
- FanDuel itself has **not raised venture rounds** since; it is funded inside Flutter (a listed company). [DER]
- Flutter (parent): FY2025 group revenue and US adj EBITDA **$922M**; delisted from LSE **3 Aug 2026**, NYSE-only. [OBS] ([flutter](https://flutter.com/media/hdshhrmb/q4-2025-earnings-release.pdf))

## Reading [INF]
- FanDuel is a **corporate-owned asset**, not a venture bet — its "valuation" is embedded in Flutter's market cap. The 2018–2020 markup (from ~$0.5B stake to $11.2B) is one of the greatest capital-appreciation stories in sports tech, driven by the PASPA repeal.
