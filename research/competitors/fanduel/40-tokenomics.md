# FanDuel — Tokenomics

## Token model
- **FanDuel has no crypto token and no blockchain tokenomics.** It is a **fiat, regulated sportsbook/iGaming** business. [OBS]/[DER]
- Its "currency" layer is **promotional credits / bonus bets / loyalty rewards** within the FanDuel/Flutter ecosystem, not a tradeable token. [OBS] ([grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/FanDuel_Sportsbook))

## Why include it in a "web3 competitor" set
- FanDuel is the **centralised incumbent** that web3 fan-engagement players (Socios, Polymarket) are disrupting — and it is now **entering prediction markets** via FanDuel Predicts. [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/polymarket/))
- Its **loyalty mechanics without a token** are the "control group" for whether a NeoBank needs tokenomics at all. [INF]

## Reading [INF]
- FanDuel demonstrates you can build **mass-market fan engagement and a ~70% profit share without any token**. This is a strong argument that token issuance is **optional, and a liability in regulated markets**. [INF]
