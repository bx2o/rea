# FanDuel — Business Model

## Revenue streams
1. **Sportsbook (B2C)** — earns the **house edge (vig)** built into odds, profitable regardless of outcome. [OBS] ([grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/FanDuel_Sportsbook))
2. **Daily Fantasy Sports (DFS)** — takes **5–15% commission** on contest entry fees. [OBS]
3. **iGaming (casino)** — house-edge casino products (the growth engine in 2025–26). [OBS]
4. **Prediction markets (FanDuel Predicts)** — new offering via a **CME Group partnership** (financial contracts like oil prices/S&P 500). [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/polymarket/))
5. **Ancillary** — promotional odds boosts, ancillary services. [OBS]

## Model characteristics
- **Disciplined generosity**: best-in-class pricing + focused promotions rather than blanket bonuses. [OBS] ([flutter factsheet](https://flutter.com/media/k2lju3sf/q2-factsheet-2026.pdf))
- **Cross-sell flywheel** — sportsbook → iGaming → prediction markets within one account. [OBS]

## Financial scale [OBS] ([flutter](https://flutter.com/media/hdshhrmb/q4-2025-earnings-release.pdf))
- **US (2025):** revenue **+20%**, adjusted EBITDA **+82% to $922M**.
- **Q4 2025:** US revenue +33%, adj EBITDA +90%; **41% sportsbook GGR share**, 28% iGaming.
- **Q1 2026:** US revenue **$1,763M (+6%)** — sportsbook +1%, iGaming +19%; **39% sportsbook / 27% iGaming** share.
- **Q2 2026:** group revenue **$4.3B (+3%)**; **AMPs 14.3M (–11%)**.
- **Market EBITDA share est. ~70%.** [OBS]

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- FanDuel's economics = **high-margin house-edge + cross-sell**. The NeoBank analogue is **interchange + cross-sold product margin**, with loyalty as the retention glue. The lesson: **be the leader, take ~70% of the category profit pool** through pricing superiority.
