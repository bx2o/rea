# FanDuel — Regulation & Legal

## US licensing model
- FanDuel operates under **state-by-state licences** (sportsbook launched in NJ/WV in 2018, expanded nationally post-PASPA). [OBS]
- **State gambling commissions** require compliance; operators must hold licences per state; sports betting is generally **21+**. [OBS]
- Flutter/FanDuel are **publicly listed and audited** (NYSE: FLUT) — a high governance bar. [OBS]

## Prediction-market tension (2026)
- FanDuel entered prediction markets via **FanDuel Predicts / CME Group partnership**, but **state regulators warned operators** that offering prediction markets could **jeopardise their gambling licences** (e.g., **Nevada**). [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/polymarket/))
- Structural question: prediction markets face **lighter federal (CFTC) oversight** and can operate in non-gambling states, undercutting state-licensed sportsbooks' **4–5% house edge** with **0–1% take rates** — a direct competitive threat to FanDuel. [OBS] ([sccgmanagement](https://sccgmanagement.com/sccg-articles/2026/02/13/three-structural-scenarios-for-the-future-of-u-s-prediction-markets-and-sportsbooks/))
