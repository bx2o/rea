# FanDuel — Products & Mechanics

## Product lines
1. **Sportsbook** — pre-game and in-play betting across major leagues; house-edge pricing. [OBS]
2. **Daily Fantasy Sports (DFS)** — short-term real-money contests; **5–15% commission** on entry fees. [OBS] ([grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/FanDuel_Sportsbook))
3. **iGaming / Casino** — online slots and table games (house-edge). [OBS]
4. **FanDuel Predicts** — **prediction markets** offering (built on a **CME Group** partnership for financial contracts). [OBS] ([sacra](https://sacra.com/c/polymarket/))
5. **Racing, Poker, free-to-play** others. [INF]

## Mechanics
- **Odds/pricing superiority** is the core competitive weapon — "best-in-class sports pricing." [OBS] ([flutter factsheet](https://flutter.com/media/k2lju3sf/q2-factsheet-2026.pdf))
- **Loyalty & generosity**: retention via loyalty (see marketing file) rather than pure bonus-chasing. [OBS]
- **Single-account cross-sell** across sportsbook, casino, DFS, prediction markets. [OBS]

## Competitive position
- **#1 US sportsbook** (≈39–44% GGR share, 2026); #1 iGaming (~27–28%); ~**70% of market EBITDA**. DraftKings is #2 (~34–36%). [OBS] ([sportbotai](https://www.sportbotai.com/stats/sportsbook-market-share))

## Reading for the NeoBank [INF]
- The **single-account cross-sell** and **pricing-superiority** mechanics are the key transferable ideas. For loyalty specifically, FanDuel relies on **product quality** (better odds) more than gamified currency — a contrast to Fanatics' FanCash model.
