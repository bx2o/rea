# FanDuel — Overview

## What it is
FanDuel is the **#1 US online sportsbook and iGaming operator**, owned by **Flutter Entertainment** (NYSE: FLUT). [OBS] ([flutter factsheet](https://flutter.com/media/k2lju3sf/q2-factsheet-2026.pdf))

## History
- Founded **2009 in Edinburgh, Scotland** by **Nigel Eccles, Lesley Eccles, Tom Griffiths, Rob Jones, Chris Stafford** — originally **Hubdub**, pivoted to daily fantasy sports (DFS), launched at SXSW. [OBS] ([grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/FanDuel))
- **May–July 2018:** after PASPA repeal, **merged with Paddy Power Betfair's US operations** to form FanDuel Group (PPB took 58%). [OBS] ([revenuememo](https://www.revenuememo.com/p/who-owns-fanduel))
- **2020:** Flutter raised its stake to **95% at an $11.2B valuation**; **July 2025:** reached **100%** (bought Boyd Gaming's final 5%). [OBS]

## Why it matters for the SA NeoBank
FanDuel is the **gold standard of a US market-leading, loyalty-driven, cross-sell platform** (sportsbook + iGaming + DFS + now prediction markets via FanDuel Predicts). Its **"FanDuel ONE"-style loyalty** and **superior pricing** playbook is directly relevant to NeoBank engagement mechanics. [INF]
