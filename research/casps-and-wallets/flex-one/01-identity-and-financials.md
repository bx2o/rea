## Key take-aways for Stremiium

1. The **card-spends-stablecoin-with-BaaS-partner** pattern is directly relevant to Stremiium's card design → **ADOPT/ADAPT**.
2. Flex's **business/HNW** orientation is a **different market** from Stremiium's SA consumer NeoBank → treat as a *mechanic donor*, not a direct competitor.
3. **Identity ambiguity is a real risk**: Stremiium must confirm the intended entity before modelling — do not assume a Mastercard consumer card exists.
