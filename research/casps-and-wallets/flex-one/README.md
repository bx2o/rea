## Executive summary

**Flex** (branded **flex.one**) is a **US-origin fintech** that markets itself as an *"AI-native private bank for HNW business owners,"* combining business spend management, institutional USD accounts, multi-currency accounts, private credit, and a **Visa "Global Card" that is connected to a user's stablecoin (USDC/USDT) balance**. Verifiable public sources describe it as a **business/private-banking platform**, not (as an anchor framing suggests) a purely crypto-native consumer card. It has raised a reported **~$105m equity plus ~$300m in debt facilities**, with a **$70m Series B1 led by Halo Fund**, and names **Visa, Lead Bank and Thread Bank** as platform partners.

> **⚠️ IDENTITY CAVEAT (important thin-data finding):** the program's entity anchor characterises "Flex.one" as a "crypto-native payment card + digital wallet" with "virtual + physical Mastercard/Visa issuance via BaaS partners" serving consumers globally. The **verifiable** public entity named `flex.one` is an **AI-native business banking / spend-management** platform for **business owners**, whose Global Card spends **stablecoin balances** and which partners with **Visa** (not Mastercard) and US BaaS banks. The two descriptions **overlap only partially**. This dossier documents the *verifiable* entity and flags every anchor-derived claim it cannot confirm. **Stremiium MUST resolve which "Flex.one" the anchor intends before acting.**
