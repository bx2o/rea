## Key take-aways for Stremiium

1. VALR's moat is **liquidity depth (ZAR) + institutional plumbing + low-fee model** — not consumer banking.
2. VALR's **licence stack is expanding deliberately** (CASP → derivatives ODP → EU reach): a roadmap Stremiium should study for sequencing.
3. VALR is **retail-trader/institution-centric**; it has **no deposit, card, credit or loyalty bundle** — the NeoBank wedge.
