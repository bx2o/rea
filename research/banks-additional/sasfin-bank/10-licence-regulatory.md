## 2. Compliance obligations (Banks Act s60A)

- Section 60A of the Banks Act requires banks to maintain an **independent compliance function** headed by a
  compliance officer, with duties including reporting non-compliance and establishing a compliance culture —
  cited in the SARS v Sasfin litigation ([Semantis law report](https://semantis.ai/law-reports/judgment/56498/)). [OBS]

## 3. SARS litigation

- SARS issued summons for **R5.3bn** against Sasfin Bank Limited; on **3 November 2025** the court confirmed
  SARS has a **statutory right of action under section 278 of the Financial Sector Regulation Act (FSRA)**
  for losses from contraventions of financial-sector laws
  ([SARS media release](https://www.sars.gov.za/latest-news/media-release-sars-welcomes-court-ruling-in-landmark-case-against-sasfin-bank-limited/)). [OBS]

## 4. Securitisation / receivables

- Sasfin Bank acts as **seller and servicer** of pooled receivables (SASP securitisation), disclosed in an
  investor report to 31 December 2025 ([Sasfin SASP S1 investor report, PDF](https://www.sasfin.com/media/deqahnfi/sasp-s1-investor-report-31-december-2025.pdf)). [OBS]
