# Sasfin Bank — Financials

Sasfin has **exited banking** (licence cancelled 30 June 2026), so normal bank financial reporting has
ceased. The group is repositioning as an investment-holding company. All figures [UNK] unless noted.

## 1. Known financial facts

| Item | Value | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Historical asset size | Under R100bn | [OBS] |
| Rental Finance funding raised (2025) | **R2bn** (growth + replacement funding) | [OBS] |
| SARS claim | **R5.3bn** summons; s278 FSRA right of action confirmed | [OBS] |
| Capitec Rental Finance acquisition by Sasfin Capital | **R201m** cash (agreed July 2026) | [OBS] |
| Sold to African Bank | Capital Equipment Finance (CEF) + Commercial Property Finance (CPF) divisions (2024–2025) | [OBS] |

## 2. New structure

  with their own brands under long-term capital ([Sasfin](https://www.sasfin.com/)). [OBS]
