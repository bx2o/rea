## 2. What Sasfin was, and what it is becoming

A **specialist business/corporate bank** serving mid-market businesses in trade finance and specialised
lending ([Grokipedia — List of banks in SA](https://grokipedia.com/page/List_of_banks_in_South_Africa)). [OBS]

It is now **exiting banking** to become an **investment-holding company** with two cornerstone
investments — **Wealth** and **Rental Finance** ([Sasfin](https://www.sasfin.com/)). [OBS]

## 3. Exit from banking (2024–2026)

- **30 June 2026**: Sasfin voluntarily relinquished its banking licence; the SARB cancelled both Sasfin
  Bank's registration and the Sasfin Group's banking-group registration under **Section 48** of the Banks
  Act ([Sasfin](https://www.sasfin.com/),
  [Megazone/Reuters-style report](https://app.megazone.fm/news/c/0/i/97378718/reserve-bank-cancels-sasfin-s-banking-licence-lender-exits-banking-sector)). [OBS]
- The licence cancellation was **gazetted 17 July 2026**; Sasfin Bank Limited will convert from a public to
  a private company ([Sasfin](https://www.sasfin.com/)). [OBS]
- This concluded a **two-year process** of withdrawal from banking ([Megazone](https://app.megazone.fm/news/c/0/i/97378718/reserve-bank-cancels-sasfin-s-banking-licence-lender-exits-banking-sector)). [OBS]
- **2025**: the Rental Finance business raised **R2bn** of growth/replacement funding ([Sasfin](https://www.sasfin.com/)). [OBS]

## 4. Division disposals & acquisitions

- Sold its **Capital Equipment Finance (CEF)** and **Commercial Property Finance (CPF)** divisions to
  **African Bank** (2024–2025) ([Daily Investor](https://dailyinvestor.com/banking/140282/end-of-an-era-for-african-bank/)). [OBS]
- In **July 2026** Capitec agreed to sell **Capitec Rental Finance** to **Sasfin Capital** (wholly-owned
  Sasfin subsidiary) for **R201m** cash ([Billionaires.Africa](https://www.billionaires.africa/2026/10/13/south-african-billionaire-michiel-le-rouxs-bank-capitec-sheds-its-rental-finance-arm/)). [OBS]

## 5. Key findings (for the NeoBank programme)

1. **A licence can be surrendered** — Sasfin shows that a banking licence is not an end in itself; focused
   non-bank niches (rental finance, wealth) can be more attractive. [INF]
2. **Regulatory/litigation overhang** — SARS sued Sasfin for **R5.3bn**; a court confirmed (3 Nov 2025) SARS
   may claim under s278 of the FSRA (see `10-licence-regulatory.md`). [OBS]
