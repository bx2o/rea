# Sasfin Bank — Technology & Differentiators

## 1. Differentiators

1. **Specialist mid-market focus** — tailored lending and trade finance for businesses, with niche expertise
   in aviation and property — not a mass-retail proposition
   ([Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/List_of_banks_in_South_Africa)). [OBS]
2. **Capital-light pivot** — moving to an investment-holding company model with two focused businesses
   (Wealth, Rental Finance) rather than a full banking stack ([Sasfin](https://www.sasfin.com/)). [OBS]
3. **Securitisation capability** — active receivables securitisation programme (SASP), indicating structured
   funding expertise ([Sasfin SASP S1 investor report](https://www.sasfin.com/media/deqahnfi/sasp-s1-investor-report-31-december-2025.pdf)). [OBS]
