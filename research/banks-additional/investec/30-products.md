# Investec — Products

Premium / specialist proposition. All [OBS] unless noted.

## 1. Private Bank Account (individuals)

| Feature | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly fee | **R705** (R355 for Young Professionals under 30) — single transparent fee | [OBS] |
| Card | Visa Platinum credit card; up to 45 interest-free days; no annual card/credit-facility fees | [OBS] |
| Credit rate | Competitive debit rate of up to prime | [OBS] |
| FX / multicurrency | Multicurrency pockets; offshore structuring via Channel Islands booking centre | [OBS] |
| Extras | Specialist bankers, family accounts, complimentary travel insurance, unlimited free ATM withdrawals (local & international) | [OBS] |
| Integration | Integrated with Investec Wealth & Investment | [OBS] |

Sources: [Investec Private Bank Account](https://www.investec.com/en_za/banking/private-bank-account.html),
[Private Banking for the individual (PDF)](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/south-africa/myself/private-banking/factsheets/private_banking_for_individuals_factsheet.pdf),
[The Citizenship Desk](https://www.thecitizenshipdesk.com/banking/investec).

## 2. Private Business Account

- Combines transactional banking with an **Investec Visa Business credit card** in one account; multiple
  user access with different permission levels and a full audit trail; differentiated payment-plan facility
  ([Private Business Account fact sheet, PDF](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/south-africa/myself/private-banking/factsheets/private_banking_for_you_and_your_business_factsheet.pdf)). [OBS]

## 3. Wealth, investment & specialist lending

- **Wealth & Investment management** and **Corporate & Investment Banking** (specialist advisory, capital
  markets, structured/asset-backed lending) ([dcf-model history](https://dcf-model.com/blogs/history/invpl)). [OBS]
- **Cash / savings**: term deposits from 7 days to 5 years via the Corporate Cash Manager (CCM) online
  platform, with a minimum deposit threshold (e.g. R5,000 tier) ([Investec cash products, PDF](https://irp.cdn-website.com/eb648acb/files/uploaded/Investec%20Products.pdf)). [OBS]

## 4. Notes & unknowns

- US persons are **not accepted** for the SA or UK private bank (FATCA) ([The Citizenship Desk](https://www.thecitizenshipdesk.com/banking/investec)). [OBS]
- Full fee schedule and the complete product set (insurance, offshore trusts, structured products) → [UNK]
  in this pass.
