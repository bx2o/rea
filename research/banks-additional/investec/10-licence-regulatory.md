## 2. Prudential framework

- Governed in SA by the **Banks Act 1990** and supervised by the **SARB Prudential Authority**; the UK
  banking arm (Investec Bank plc) is authorised/supervised under the UK regime. [INF]
- Group discloses **Basel Pillar 3** information and capital ratios (CET1 13.6% at FY2026 — see
  `20-financials.md`) ([listcorp FY2026 results](https://www.listcorp.com/jse/inp/investec-plc/news/unaudited-condensed-combined-consolidated-financial-results-for-the-year-ended-31-march-2026-and-cash-dividend-3355716.html)). [OBS]
