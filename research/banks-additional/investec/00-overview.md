## 2. Founding & journey

- Investec was founded **in Johannesburg in 1974** by Larry Nestadt, Errol Grolman and Ian Kantor as a small leasing and financing company; the name blended "investment" and "technology"
  ([Investec Group fact sheet](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/investor-relations/downloads/fact-sheets/2018/Investec-Group-Q&A-Fact-sheet-Nov-2018.pdf),
  [Porter's Five Force — owners](https://portersfiveforce.com/blogs/owners/investec)). [OBS]
- It **acquired a banking licence in 1980** and **listed on the JSE in 1986** (via a merger with Metboard)
  ([Investec Group fact sheet](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/investor-relations/downloads/fact-sheets/2018/Investec-Group-Q&A-Fact-sheet-Nov-2018.pdf),
  [dcf-model history](https://dcf-model.com/blogs/history/invpl)). [OBS]
- In **July 2002** it implemented a **dual-listed companies (DLC) structure** with linked companies listed in London and Johannesburg ([Investec Group fact sheet](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/investor-relations/downloads/fact-sheets/2018/Investec-Group-Q&A-Fact-sheet-Nov-2018.pdf)). [OBS]

## 3. What Investec is

An **Anglo-South African specialist bank and wealth manager** — private banking, wealth & investment
management, specialist lending and corporate & investment banking — rather than a mass-market retail bank
([dcf-model history](https://dcf-model.com/blogs/history/invpl),
[creditdeals — Investec](https://creditdeals.io/za/savings-accounts/investec)). [OBS]

## 4. Key findings (for the NeoBank programme)

1. **Specialist, not volume**: Investec competes on relationships and tailored credit/wealth, explicitly
   avoiding mass retail — the opposite pole to a neobank's low-touch, high-volume model. [INF]
2. **Dual-listed, cross-border**: A genuine SA + UK franchise (FATCA note: US persons not accepted in
   either jurisdiction) — relevant to any SA bank targeting cross-border high-net-worth clients. [INF]
3. **Premium pricing**: The Private Bank Account carries a single transparent monthly fee of **R705**
   (R355 for Young Professionals under 30) — see `30-products.md`. [OBS]
