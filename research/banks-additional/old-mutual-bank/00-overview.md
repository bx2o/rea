## 2. Founding & journey

- Old Mutual received **Section 17 approval** to establish a bank, transitioning from "bank build mode" into
  industry testing with bank partners before integrating into the National Payments System
  ([Old Mutual media release, PDF](https://www.oldmutual.com/om-docs/bltfcf2f5ab659bb081/Old_Mutual_has_received_section_17_approval_to_establish_a_bank.pdf)). [OBS]
- The licence (Section 16, Banks Act) was **approved April 2024**; the bank **launched publicly in
  September 2025**, notifying existing Money Account clients to migrate to the new OM Bank app
  ([CreditDeals FAQ](https://creditdeals.io/za/debit-cards/ombank/faq),
  [Daily Investor](https://dailyinvestor.com/banking/99582/south-africas-new-bank-is-here/)). [OBS]
- OM Bank Limited is a **member bank** of the SARB deposit-insurance scheme (CODI) register
  ([SARB — member banks](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/Deposit-insurance/member-banks0)). [OBS]

## 3. What OM Bank is

Old Mutual's **digital-first retail bank**, migrating its large existing insurance/investment client base
(the Money Account) into a licensed transactional bank — a distribution-led neobank strategy exploiting an
incumbent's customer base. [INF]

## 4. Key findings (for the NeoBank programme)

1. **Incumbent-powered neobank**: leverages Old Mutual's ~existing customer base rather than cold acquisition — a "brownfield" entry ([CreditDeals](https://creditdeals.io/za/debit-cards/ombank)). [OBS]
2. **Aggressive entry pricing**: R4.95/month account, R0 savings fee, up to 7.23% p.a. savings interest —
   directly competitive with TymeBank/Bank Zero positioning. [OBS]
3. **No ATM estate**: relies on other banks' ATMs and digitisation to keep costs low. [OBS]
