## 2. Licensing pathway (why two sections)

- **Section 17** of the Banks Act permits an applicant to *proceed to establish* a bank after the Registrar
  is satisfied, prior to full commencement of business; the bank then integrates into the **National Payments
  System** after error-free testing ([Old Mutual media release](https://www.oldmutual.com/om-docs/bltfcf2f5ab659bb081/Old_Mutual_has_received_section_17_approval_to_establish_a_bank.pdf)). [OBS]
- The **Section 16** licence is the authorisation to conduct the business of a bank; OM Bank obtained this in
  **April 2024** and launched publicly in **September 2025**
  ([CreditDeals FAQ](https://creditdeals.io/za/debit-cards/ombank/faq)). [OBS]

## 3. Prudential framework

- As a licensed commercial bank, OM Bank is subject to the full **capital, liquidity and governance**
  requirements of the Banks Act 94 of 1990 ([CreditDeals](https://creditdeals.io/za/debit-cards/ombank/faq)). [OBS]
