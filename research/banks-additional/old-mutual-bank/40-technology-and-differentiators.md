# Old Mutual Bank (OM Bank) — Technology & Differentiators

## 1. Differentiators

1. **Incumbent distribution** — an established insurer/asset manager monetising an existing client base via a
   licensed app-only bank, rather than acquiring customers cold. [INF]
2. **Low entry price + savings yield** — R4.95/month account and up to 7.23% p.a. savings interest, competing
   head-on with SA's zero/low-fee digital banks. [OBS]
3. **Integrated ecosystem ambition** — potential to bundle banking with Old Mutual insurance/investment. [INF]

## 3. Analyst read [INF]

OM Bank is a **fast-follower** neobank: it adopts the proven low-fee/digital template and layers on an
incumbent's balance sheet and brand. Its key open question is whether the app + ecosystem integration is
sufficiently differentiated versus TymeBank/Bank Zero/Discovery. [INF]
