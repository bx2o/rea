# Al Baraka Bank — Technology & Differentiators

## 1. Differentiators

1. **Only fully-fledged Islamic bank in SA** — a structural product/regulatory niche; competitors use
   "Islamic windows" inside conventional banks ([Sharia Banking](https://shariabanking.com/south-africa/)). [OBS]
2. **Shari'ah-by-design** — all products (home, business, savings) are riba-free and structured for
   compliance, not bolted on. [OBS]
3. **Group scale + Sukuk capability** — Bahraini parent (>US$31bn assets) and demonstrated ability to issue
   retail Sukuk (tier-2 capital) ([Al Baraka Group news](https://albaraka.com/en/media-center/news),
   [Albaraka Sukuk](https://www.albaraka.com.pk/page/al-baraka-bank-south-africa-has-successfully-raised-r200m-from-retail-investors-via-a-shariah-compliant-instrument-called-a-sukuk/)). [OBS]
