# Al Baraka Bank — Products

All products are Shari'ah-compliant (riba-free). All [OBS] unless noted.

## 1. Home / property finance

| Product | Structure | Terms | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential finance | **Musharaka Purchase of Equity** (diminishing musharaka) | Up to 90% of property value; up to 20–30 years; fixed rate for 12 months with annual review; no monthly admin fees; no early-settlement penalties | [OBS] |
| Home refinance / bond switch | Musharaka | Up to 90% of value | [OBS] |

Sources: [Al Baraka — Residential finance](https://www.albaraka.co.za/products/home-finance),
[Sharia Banking — home finance](https://shariabanking.com/south-africa-islamic-home-finance.html).

## 2. Business & trade finance

- Business partnership financing (musharaka/mudaraba-based), corporate and trade finance for the
  SA/Africa corridor ([Sharia Banking — South Africa](https://shariabanking.com/south-africa/)). [OBS]
