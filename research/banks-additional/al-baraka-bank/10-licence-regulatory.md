## 2. Shari'ah governance

- As a fully-fledged Islamic bank, products are structured to be **Shari'ah-compliant** and governed by a
  Shari'ah supervisory function (all products follow Shari'ah principles)
  ([CodeCash](https://codecash.co.za/islamic-banking/),
  [Islamic Finance Calculator — SA guide](https://islamicfinancecalculator.com/country/south-africa-islamic-finance)). [OBS]

## 3. Secular regulation

- SARB regulates Islamic banking within the conventional prudential framework
  ([Islamic Finance Calculator](https://islamicfinancecalculator.com/country/south-africa-islamic-finance)). [OBS]
- **Tier-2 capital** instrument: a **R200m retail Sukuk** issued in 2018, counting toward the bank's tier-two
  capital and representing an undivided share in the bank's advances book
  ([Albaraka — Sukuk](https://www.albaraka.com.pk/page/al-baraka-bank-south-africa-has-successfully-raised-r200m-from-retail-investors-via-a-shariah-compliant-instrument-called-a-sukuk/)). [OBS]
