## 2. What Al Baraka Bank is

**South Africa's only fully-fledged Islamic (Shari'ah-compliant) commercial bank**, operating entirely on
non-interest (riba-free) principles since 1989, offering retail, corporate and trade finance
([Sharia Banking SA](https://shariabanking.com/south-africa/),
[CodeCash — Islamic banking](https://codecash.co.za/islamic-banking/)). [OBS]

It is the local arm of the Bahrain-headquartered **Al Baraka Group**, whose total assets exceeded **US$31bn**
in 2025 ([Grokipedia — Al Baraka Group](https://grokipedia.com/page/al_baraka_group),
[Al Baraka news](https://albaraka.com/en/media-center/news)). [OBS]

## 3. Key findings (for the NeoBank programme)

1. **A distinct regulatory/product niche** — Islamic banking is a small but real SA segment; Al Baraka is the
   only standalone Shari'ah bank (other big banks run "Islamic windows"). [OBS]
2. **Profit-sharing, not interest** — participation accounts pay profit-share returns (e.g. ~2.01%–5.53% for
   August 2026) rather than conventional interest — see `30-products.md`. [OBS]
3. **Group-backed capital** — Tier-2 capital raised via a **R200m retail Sukuk in 2018**, the first of its
   kind in SA ([Albaraka news](https://www.albaraka.com.pk/page/al-baraka-bank-south-africa-has-successfully-raised-r200m-from-retail-investors-via-a-shariah-compliant-instrument-called-a-sukuk/)). [OBS]
