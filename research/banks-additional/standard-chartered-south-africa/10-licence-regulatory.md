## 2. Regulatory framework (branch)

- Branches of foreign banks are separately regulated in SA (Banks Act) and are distinct from locally
  incorporated or locally controlled banks. SC Johannesburg Branch files **quarterly Basel Pillar 3**
  disclosures under the BCBS revised framework
  ([SC Pillar 3 Q4 2025, PDF](https://www.sc.com/za/uploads/sites/36/content/docs/Pillar-3-Disclosure-Q4-25-CMT-V2.pdf)). [OBS]

## 3. Group regulatory posture

- Globally, Standard Chartered PLC Group + Standard Chartered Bank are regulated by the UK FCA and PRA
  ([SC PLC Pillar 3 2025, PDF](https://www.sc.com/en/uploads/sites/66/content/docs/standard-chartered-plc-full-year-2025-pillar3-disclosure.pdf)). [OBS]
