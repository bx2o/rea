## 2. Group context (Africa retrenchment)

- Across Africa, Standard Chartered's **weighted market-size index fell to 4.5% in 2025** (from 5.4%),
  demoting it below "large-bank" status in Kenya's CBK peer grouping
  ([Kenya Insights](https://kenyainsights.com/stancharts-slow-goodbye-reading-kenyas-cbk-demotion-against-a-continental-exit-pattern)). [OBS]
- It sold WRB businesses in Botswana, Uganda and Zambia; Absa bought the Ugandan assets ([Mail & Guardian](https://mg.co.za/business/2026-01-15-major-shift-in-african-banking/)). [OBS]
