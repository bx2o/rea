# Standard Chartered South Africa — Technology & Differentiators

## 1. Differentiators

1. **Global CIB network** — SA clients plug into a global corporate/institutional network, trade corridors and
   cross-border FX ([SC SA contact](https://www.sc.com/za/contact/)). [OBS]
2. **Light-touch branch structure** — operates as a foreign-bank branch rather than a full local subsidiary,
   minimising local footprint/commitment. [INF]
3. **Corridor specialisation** — an Africa/Asia/Middle East trade-banking angle. [INF]

## 3. Analyst read [INF]

Standard Chartered is the archetype of a **global bank narrowing to corporate/institutional** in SA. For a
neobank, it is a competitor for corporate/treasury business only, not retail. Its tech is largely group-level
and not a public SA-specific differentiator. [INF]
