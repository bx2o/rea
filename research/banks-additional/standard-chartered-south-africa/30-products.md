# Standard Chartered South Africa — Products

Standard Chartered's SA offering is **corporate & institutional**, not retail. All [OBS] unless noted.

## 1. Product scope (CIB)

| Area | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate & Investment Banking | Corporate account/CIB relationship management (dedicated SA client managers) | [OBS] |
| Transaction banking | Cash management, trade finance, FX/global markets (group capability) | [INF] |
| Wealth & Retail Banking | Being divested/retrenched across Africa (WRB hived off) | [OBS] |
| Retail (SA) | Not offered as a domestic retail bank | [OBS] |

Sources: [SC SA contact](https://www.sc.com/za/contact/),
[Mail & Guardian](https://mg.co.za/business/2026-01-15-major-shift-in-african-banking/).
