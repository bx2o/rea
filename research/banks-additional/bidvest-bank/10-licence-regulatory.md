## 2. Ownership change (regulatory conditionality)

- The attempted sale to **Access Bank Plc** (announced 12 Dec 2024) was **conditional on regulatory
  approvals** in multiple jurisdictions; the long-stop date of **26 Jan 2026** passed with conditions unmet
  and the transaction was **terminated** in February 2026
  ([Access Bank statement](https://www.accessbankplc.com/media/press-releases/re-access-bank-plc-signs-agreement-to-acquire-100-equity-stake-in-south-african-based-bidvest-bank),
  [Ecofin Agency](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/1002-52732-access-bank-bidvest-bank-deal-falls-through-in-south-africa)). [OBS]
- This illustrates the **multi-jurisdiction regulatory complexity** of SA bank M&A (SARB PA + Minister of
  Finance section 37/54 approvals are typically required). [INF]

## 3. Disclosure & reporting

- Bidvest Bank is treated as a **discontinued operation** in Bidvest Group's reporting following the failed
