# Bidvest Bank — Technology & Differentiators

## 1. Differentiators

1. **Fleet + FX niche, widened into business banking** — a distinctive "asset-and-mobility" anchor rather
   than pure deposits ([Bidvest Financial Services](https://bidvest.com/bidvest-industrial-financial-services)). [OBS]
2. **Group synergy** — sits inside a diversified industrial/services group (Bidvest), enabling cross-sell
   into a large corporate client base. [INF]
3. **"Bank born out of business"** positioning — targets SMEs and corporates with integrated mobility and
   payment solutions ([Bidvest Bank](https://www.bidvestbank.co.za/fleet-services)). [OBS]
