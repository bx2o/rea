## 2. What Bidvest Bank is

A **second-tier South African bank** within Bidvest Group's financial services division. Historically
strong in **foreign exchange and fleet management**, with a strategy to diversify into corporate, business
and personal banking ([Bidvest Financial Services](https://bidvest.com/bidvest-industrial-financial-services)). [OBS]

## 3. Sale saga (2024–2026)

- **12 Dec 2024**: Access Bank Plc (Nigeria) signed a binding agreement to acquire **100%** of Bidvest Bank
  Holdings for **~R2.8bn (~US$159m)** ([Business AM Live](https://businessamlive.com/access-bank-plc-signs-agreement-to-acquire-100-equity-stake-in-bidvest-bank/),
  [Access Bank statement](https://www.accessbankplc.com/media/press-releases/re-access-bank-plc-signs-agreement-to-acquire-100-equity-stake-in-south-african-based-bidvest-bank)). [OBS]
- The **long-stop date expired 26 January 2026** without all (including regulatory) conditions being met
  ([Access Bank statement](https://www.accessbankplc.com/media/press-releases/re-access-bank-plc-signs-agreement-to-acquire-100-equity-stake-in-south-african-based-bidvest-bank)). [OBS]
- **9 February 2026**: Bidvest announced the **deal had fallen through**; the sale process was **relaunched**
  ([Ecofin Agency](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/1002-52732-access-bank-bidvest-bank-deal-falls-through-in-south-africa),
  [African Insider](https://www.africaninsider.com/business/major-south-african-bank-back-on-the-market-after-r2-8bn-deal-fails/)). [OBS]

## 4. Key findings (for the NeoBank programme)

1. **Niche-to-broad pivot in motion** — from FX + fleet into business/personal banking, an unusual
