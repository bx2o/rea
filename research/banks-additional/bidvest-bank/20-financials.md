# Bidvest Bank — Financials

Reporting year of parent Bidvest Group: **1 July – 30 June**. Bidvest Bank is reported within Bidvest's
**financial services** segment and, since the failed disposal, as a **discontinued operation**. Standalone
bank financials are not separately disclosed in public summaries.
