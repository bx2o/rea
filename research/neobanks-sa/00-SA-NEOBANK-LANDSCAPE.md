# South African Neobank Landscape — Comparison

> Comparative synthesis of the four SA digital-only banks researched by council C16.
> All four are covered by dedicated entity folders under `research/neobanks-sa/`.

## 1. Snapshot comparison

| Dimension | **TymeBank → GoTyme Bank** | **Discovery Bank** | **Bank Zero** | **Bettr** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Launch (public) | Feb 2019 | Mar 2019 | 2021 (reg. 2018) | Never fully launched |
| Status (2026) | Operating, rebranding global | Operating, first annual profit | Operating, first break-even month | **Out of business** |
| Licence | Commercial bank, Banks Act 94/1990 | Commercial bank, Banks Act 94/1990 | **Mutual bank** (MB0001, Mutual Banks Act) | **None** (sponsor bank: Access Bank) |

## 2. Founding & journey (contrast)

### TymeBank / GoTyme Bank
- Born from **Tyme** (2015); ARC bought the business from Commonwealth Bank of Australia (Nov 2018); live Feb 2019 as SA's first licensed digital bank and first majority black-owned retail bank [OBS] — [Tyme newsroom](https://www.tyme.com/news-room/tymebank-south-africas-first-digital-bank-goes-live)
- Fastest to profitability (Dec 2023, <5 yrs) of the cohort; unicorn at $1.5bn (Dec 2024) [OBS] — [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/press-releases/tymebank-south-africa-hits-profitability-in-under-5-years)
- **2026:** rebrand to **GoTyme Bank**; kiosk-to-mall-hub pivot [OBS] — [MyBroadband](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/banking/640299-tymebank-changes-its-name.html)
- Exported model to **GoTyme Bank Philippines** (BSP digital licence 2022; JV with Gokongwei Group) [OBS] — [GoTyme 2022 Annual Report](https://www.gotyme.com.ph/static-assets/files/pdf/gotyme-bank-2022-annual-report.pdf)

### Discovery Bank
- Launched Mar 2019 on Discovery's **Vitality shared-value / behavioural** model [OBS] — [JEF Journal](https://jefjournal.org.za/index.php/jef/article/view/602/1163)
- Took ~7 years and an estimated ~R15bn group investment to reach first annual profit (FY2026, R370m) [OBS] — [Daily Investor](https://dailyinvestor.com/banking/152648/discovery-bank-enters-a-new-era-with-600-profit-jump/)
- Vertically integrated with Discovery's health/insurance/investment ecosystem [OBS] — [Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/business/the-bank-is-now-firmly-at-the-centre-of-everything-discovery-does/)

### Bank Zero
- Founded 2018 by Michael Jordaan (ex-FNB CEO) + Yatin Narsai + 5 others; SA's first app-driven **mutual bank** [OBS] — [Grokipedia/Bank Zero](https://grokipedia.com/page/Bank_Zero)
- Took ~4 years 10 months from public opening to first break-even month (Aug 2026) — slower to profit than Tyme but without external equity profit leakage (mutual) [OBS] — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/09/22/bank-zero-break-even-alliance-partnerships/)
- Pivoted growth to **alliance banking** (Mukuru, Jan/Aug 2026); being acquired by Lesaka for R1.1bn (deadline extended to Jan 2027) [OBS] — [Lesaka](https://lesakatech.com/newsroom-all/bank-zero-snapped-up-for-r1-1-billion)

### Bettr
- Founded 2015 (Cape Town) aimed at **creators / younger consumers**; never obtained a banking licence, relied on Access Bank as sponsor [OBS] — [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/challenger-banks/challenger-banks-in-south-africa-who-s-who-and-what-s-their-tech)
- Reported **out of business**; the licensing gap (no sponsor-free route at the time) is cited as a core obstacle [OBS] — [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/264925-45)

## 3. Licence / regulatory comparison
- **Commercial banks (Banks Act 94/1990), SARB/PA regulated:** TymeBank/GoTyme Bank; Discovery Bank [OBS] — [neobanks.guide](https://neobanks.guide/best-neobank-south-africa/)
- **Mutual bank (Mutual Banks Act 124/1993), MB0001:** Bank Zero [OBS] — [CreditDeals](https://creditdeals.io/za/debit-cards/bankzero)
- **No licence (sponsor-bank distribution channel):** Bettr [OBS] — [MyBroadband](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/banking/489889-south-africas-neobanks.html)
- **Sector shift:** SARB's **activity-based authorisation** (draft exemption notices 2025–2026) would let non-banks issue e-money/participate directly — closing the exact gap Bettr's founders flagged [OBS] — [Bowmans](https://bowmanslaw.com/insights/the-south-african-reserve-bank-has-recently-published-a-draft-activity-based-authorisation-framework-for-participants-banks-and-non-banks-in-the-national-payment-system/)

## 4. Financial comparison
- **Profitability order:** TymeBank (Dec 2023 monthly) → Discovery Bank (FY2025 first profitable period; FY2026 first annual) → Bank Zero (Aug 2026 break-even month) → Bettr (never profitable) [OBS]
- **Capital-intensity contrast:** TymeBank raised ~US$500m+ and reached profit relatively cheaply via kiosks; Discovery required ~R15bn group investment; Bank Zero reached break-even on a small balance sheet (~R860m deposits) [OBS] — [Embedded Finance Index](https://www.embeddedfinanceindex.com/companies/tymebank), [Daily Investor](https://dailyinvestor.com/banking/152648/discovery-bank-enters-a-new-era-with-600-profit-jump/), [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)

## 5. Products & pricing comparison
- **Lowest-cost positioning:** TymeBank (R0) and Bank Zero (R0 everything) vs **Discovery Bank's tiered fee/premium rewards** model [OBS] — [NextRand](https://nextrand.co.za/banks), [MoneyToday](https://moneytoday.co.za/banking/best-digital-banks)
- **Rewards/behavioural:** Discovery (Vitality Money, Discovery Mileage) unique in the cohort [OBS] — [Brand Finance](https://brandfinance.com/insights/discovery)

## 8. Strategic takeaways [INF]
1. **Two viable paths to scale:** (a) mass-market, low-cost, retail-distribution (TymeBank) and (b) premium, behaviour-linked, ecosystem-integrated (Discovery Bank). Bank Zero occupies a third: narrow, fee-free, member-owned.
2. **Distribution is destiny:** TymeBank's kiosk/retail model drove the lowest CAC (~US$3–4); its 2026 pivot to mall hubs signals a maturity shift.
3. **Licence = capability:** Bettr's failure and Bank Zero's sponsor/alliance reliance show that *not* holding a full banking licence constrains deposit-taking and product breadth; SARB's activity-based reform is the key structural wildcard.
4. **Profitability timelines are long:** even the best-in-class took 3–7 years; Discovery's ~R15bn build cost vs TymeBank's capital-light kiosk model is the sharpest contrast in the cohort.
