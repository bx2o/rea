# Bank Zero — Partnerships

## 1. Mukuru (alliance banking / card issuing)
- **~500,000 Mukuru customers** migrated onto Bank Zero's platform in August 2026 [OBS] — [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)
- The **Mukuru Card** had previously been backed by **Standard Bank**; the migration moves a large card base away from a big-four bank [OBS] — same source
- Rationale: sponsor banks raised fintech prices sharply (100%+ in some cases); Bank Zero offered lower pricing [OBS] — same source

## 2. Paymentology (processing)
- Partnership with **Paymentology** (April 2026) to prepare for the alliance-banking model [OBS] — [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)

## 3. Alliance banking (channel)
- **Alliance banking** proposition developed over >18 months; went live **January 2026**; now the primary growth engine [OBS] — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/09/22/bank-zero-break-even-alliance-partnerships/)

## 4. Lesaka Technologies (acquirer/capital partner)
- **Lesaka** agreed June 2025 to buy 100% of Bank Zero for R1.1bn; to provide capital for the planned lending push [OBS] — [Lesaka](https://lesakatech.com/newsroom-all/bank-zero-snapped-up-for-r1-1-billion), [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)

## 5. Technology partners
