# Bank Zero — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

## 1. Brand positioning
- Core message: **"powerful banking at zero fees"**; zero monthly + zero transaction fees for individuals and businesses [OBS] — [Bank Zero press release](https://www.bankzero.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/PR007-Bank-Zero-opens-fully-and-publishes-2022-Zero-pricing-guide-2.pdf)
- Differentiation: value + control + security (patented card) rather than cash-back/reward programmes [OBS] — [CreditDeals](https://creditdeals.io/za/debit-cards/bankzero)

## 2. Launch & PR narrative
- Publicity leaned on the "**first bank in the world launched by a fully WFH team**" story and an "unconstrained" full-open launch [OBS] — [Bank Zero press release](https://www.bankzero.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/PR007-Bank-Zero-opens-fully-and-publishes-2022-Zero-pricing-guide-2.pdf)
- Founder halo: **Michael Jordaan** (ex-FNB CEO) as high-profile chairman [OBS] — [TechMoonshot](https://techmoonshot.com/2026/06/19/lesaka-pushes-bank-zero-acquisition-deadline-to-2027-as-regulators-stall/)

## 3. Pricing-guide marketing
