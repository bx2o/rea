# Bank Zero — Customer Service & Complaints

## 1. App ratings
- On-site reviews: **4.6** overall rating [OBS] — [Bank Zero](https://www.bankzero.co.za/)
- Aggregator rating: **4.2** [OBS] — [NextRand](https://nextrand.co.za/banks)
- One review site notes a "4.0 Rating" for the app [OBS] — [Bank Zero](https://www.bankzero.co.za/)
- Google Play/App Store official averages: **[UNK]** (not captured in reviewed sources).

## 2. Customer sentiment themes
- Positive: low/zero fees, easy to use, responsive support [OBS] — [Bank Zero](https://www.bankzero.co.za/)
- Negative: interface described as dated (e.g., iconography) [OBS] — [Bank Zero](https://www.bankzero.co.za/)
- Structural limitation: no branches/cash-deposit tellers; limited international wires [OBS] — [VaultCue](https://vaultcue.com/accounts/bank-zero/review/)

## 3. Service model
- App-only; **no call centres/branch network** — savings opened and managed in-app [OBS] — [VaultCue](https://vaultcue.com/accounts/bank-zero/review/)
- Earlier public access was waitlist-constrained while digital support processes were stress-tested [OBS] — [Bank Zero press release](https://www.bankzero.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/PR007-Bank-Zero-opens-fully-and-publishes-2022-Zero-pricing-guide-2.pdf)

## 4. Ombudsman context (sector-wide)
- Complaints fall under the **National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFO)**; 15,412 banking cases opened 2024 (~79% outcomes favour banks) [OBS] — [Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/when-banks-get-it-wrong-ombud-cases-reveal-costly-mistakes/)
