# Bank Zero — Entity Overview

## 1. Identity
- **Bank Zero** — South Africa's first **app-driven mutual bank** [OBS] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_Zero), [Grokipedia/Bank Zero](https://grokipedia.com/page/Bank_Zero)
- **HQ:** Johannesburg, South Africa; operations confined to South Africa [OBS] — [Grokipedia/Bank Zero](https://grokipedia.com/page/Bank_Zero)
- **Model:** positive — zero monthly and zero transaction fees; app-only, no branches; profits returned to members (mutual bank) [OBS] — [Bank Zero pricing guide 2026](https://www.bankzero.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Bank-Zero-pricing-guide-2026.pdf)

## 2. Founding & journey
- **16 January 2018:** founded by **7 co-founders**, incl. **Michael Jordaan** (chairman; ex-CEO of FNB) and **Yatin Narsai** (CEO) [OBS] — [Grokipedia/Bank Zero](https://grokipedia.com/page/Bank_Zero), [Embedded Finance Index](https://www.embeddedfinanceindex.com/companies/bank-zero)
- **17 August 2018:** officially registered as a **mutual bank** by SARB (licence **MB0001**) [OBS] — [Grokipedia/Bank Zero](https://grokipedia.com/page/Bank_Zero), [CreditDeals](https://creditdeals.io/za/debit-cards/bankzero)
- **2021:** opened to the public (waitlist first, then "fully open" — announced Oct 2021 / public Aug 2021) [OBS] — [Bank Zero press release](https://www.bankzero.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/PR007-Bank-Zero-opens-fully-and-publishes-2022-Zero-pricing-guide-2.pdf), [Grokipedia/Bank Zero](https://grokipedia.com/page/Bank_Zero)
- **Historic claim:** **first bank in the world to be launched by a fully WFH team** [OBS] — [Bank Zero press release](https://www.bankzero.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/PR007-Bank-Zero-opens-fully-and-publishes-2022-Zero-pricing-guide-2.pdf)
- **September 2026:** announced first **break-even month (August 2026)**, beating its own Dec 2026 break-even projection [OBS] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_Zero), [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/09/22/bank-zero-break-even-alliance-partnerships/)

## 3. Ownership
- **45% black-owned, 20% women-owned** (transformation credentials) [OBS] — [TechMoonshot](https://techmoonshot.com/2026/06/19/lesaka-pushes-bank-zero-acquisition-deadline-to-2027-as-regulators-stall/)

## 4. Acquisition (in progress)
- **June 2025:** **Lesaka Technologies** agreed to buy **100% of Bank Zero for R1.1bn** (~R1.09bn total, incl. small cash component up to R91m) [OBS] — [Lesaka](https://lesakatech.com/newsroom-all/bank-zero-snapped-up-for-r1-1-billion), [ainvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/bank-profit-turnaround-story-side-effect-incomplete-deal-2609/)
- Competition Commission recommended approval; SARB Prudential Authority review delayed the deal; **deadline extended to 31 January 2027** [OBS] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_Zero), [TechMoonshot](https://techmoonshot.com/2026/06/19/lesaka-pushes-bank-zero-acquisition-deadline-to-2027-as-regulators-stall/)

## 5. Scale snapshot
- Customers: designed to reach profitability with ~100,000 customers originally [OBS] — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/09/22/bank-zero-break-even-alliance-partnerships/)
- Deposits: **>R400m** (end April 2025) → **~R860m** (2026) [OBS] — [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/), [ainvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/bank-profit-turnaround-story-side-effect-incomplete-deal-2609/)
