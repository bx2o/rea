# Bank Zero — Banking Rails

## 1. NPS participation
- As a **registered mutual bank**, Bank Zero participates in the **South African National Payment System (NPS)**; it integrated with SARB national payment systems [OBS] — [CreditDeals](https://creditdeals.io/za/debit-cards/bankzero)
- Key SA rails: **RTC**, **PayShap** (instant, launched 13 Mar 2023), **SAMOS** (settlement) [OBS] — [PRIFinance](https://prifinance.com/en/payments/south-africa-payment-license/)
- Card: **Mastercard** debit card (issued/processed with Paymentology support) [OBS] — [CreditDeals](https://creditdeals.io/za/debit-cards/bankzero), [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)

## 2. Card issuance model / alliance banking
- Bank Zero now issues cards under **alliance-banking** arrangements for third parties (e.g., Mukuru), moving a card base from sponsor-bank model to Bank Zero [OBS] — [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)

## 3. Cash & access
- No branch/cash-deposit teller services; app-only; limited international wires [OBS] — [VaultCue](https://vaultcue.com/accounts/bank-zero/review/)
- Savings/notice cash-investment accounts at tiered rates effective 25 Sep 2026 [OBS] — [Bank Zero rates & pricing](https://www.bankzero.co.za/rates-and-pricing/)

## 4. Pending rails expansion
- Applied to SARB/PA to add **lending** and **cross-border FX** services [OBS] — [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)

## 5. Regulatory rails context
- SA moving toward activity-based authorisation for non-bank payment providers (SARB draft exemption notices) — affects who can access the NPS [OBS] — [Bowmans](https://bowmanslaw.com/insights/the-south-african-reserve-bank-has-recently-published-a-draft-activity-based-authorisation-framework-for-participants-banks-and-non-banks-in-the-national-payment-system/)
