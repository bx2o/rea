# Bank Zero — Licence & Regulatory

## 1. Licence type
- Bank Zero holds a **mutual bank licence** — registered with the **South African Reserve Bank (SARB)** as a **mutual bank effective 17 August 2018** [OBS] — [Grokipedia/Bank Zero](https://grokipedia.com/page/Bank_Zero)
- **Licence number MB0001** [OBS] — [CreditDeals](https://creditdeals.io/za/debit-cards/bankzero), [Embedded Finance Index](https://www.embeddedfinanceindex.com/companies/bank-zero)
- SA's **first app-driven mutual bank** [OBS] — [Grokipedia/Bank Zero](https://grokipedia.com/page/Bank_Zero)
- Regulated under the **Mutual Banks Act 124 of 1993** and SARB supervision (distinct licensing class from the Banks Act commercial banks) [INF] — [SARB Mutual Banks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_Zero)

## 2. Mutual-bank model
- **Profits returned to members** rather than distributed to external shareholders — distinguishes it from shareholder-driven banks [OBS] — [Grokipedia/Bank Zero](https://grokipedia.com/page/Bank_Zero)
- This member-focus model is emphasised prior to any ownership change (relevant to the pending Lesaka acquisition) [OBS] — same source

## 3. Transformation credentials
- **45% black-owned, 20% women-owned** [OBS] — [TechMoonshot](https://techmoonshot.com/2026/06/19/lesaka-pushes-bank-zero-acquisition-deadline-to-2027-as-regulators-stall/)
- Regulators (Prudential Authority) are reported to be scrutinising these ownership credentials as part of the Lesaka approval [INF] — same source

## 4. Pending ownership change / regulatory approvals
- **Lesaka Technologies** agreed (June 2025) to acquire 100% of Bank Zero for R1.1bn [OBS] — [Lesaka](https://lesakatech.com/newsroom-all/bank-zero-snapped-up-for-r1-1-billion)
- **Competition Commission** recommended approval; the **SARB Prudential Authority** extended its review; closing deadline **31 January 2027** [OBS] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_Zero), [TechMoonshot](https://techmoonshot.com/2026/06/19/lesaka-pushes-bank-zero-acquisition-deadline-to-2027-as-regulators-stall/)
- Bank Zero has applied to **SARB/PA to offer loans** and to add **cross-border FX services** (approvals pending) [OBS] — [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)

## 5. Conduct
