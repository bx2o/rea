# Bank Zero — Financials

## 1. Profitability milestone
- **August 2026:** first **break-even month** — revenue covered operating costs for the month, **4 years and 10 months after opening to the public** [OBS] — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/09/22/bank-zero-break-even-alliance-partnerships/), [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)
- This **beat the bank's own break-even projection of December 2026** [OBS] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_Zero)
- Break-even ≠ consistent profitability; earnings expected to stay **uneven in the short term**, with **stronger revenue growth and healthy profit forecast for 2027** [OBS] — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/09/22/bank-zero-break-even-alliance-partnerships/)
- Bloomberg headline: "South Africa's Bank Zero Turns Profitable, Plans Lending Push" (22 Sep 2026) [OBS] — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/south-africa-s-bank-zero-turns-profitable-plans-lending-push)

## 2. Deposits
- **>R400m** at end April 2025 [OBS] — [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)
- **~R860m** (2026) — to fund planned lending [OBS] — [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)

## 3. Customer scale & the alliance-banking pivot
- Originally engineered to reach profitability with only **~100,000 customers** [OBS] — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/09/22/bank-zero-break-even-alliance-partnerships/)
- Growth now driven by **alliance banking** (spent >18 months developing; went live **January 2026**) [OBS] — same source
- **~500,000 Mukuru customers** began migrating onto the platform in August 2026 [OBS] — [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)

## 4. Acquisition & capital
- **Lesaka Technologies** agreed (June 2025) to acquire 100% of Bank Zero for **R1.1bn** (up to R91m cash component) [OBS] — [Lesaka](https://lesakatech.com/newsroom-all/bank-zero-snapped-up-for-r1-1-billion)
- Planned lending to be funded from the ~R860m deposit base plus capital from Lesaka [OBS] — [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)

## 5. Cost context
- Sponsor banks have raised prices for fintechs sharply (in some cases 100%+); Bank Zero's lower costs attracted Mukuru's card migration away from a big-four bank [OBS] — [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/)
