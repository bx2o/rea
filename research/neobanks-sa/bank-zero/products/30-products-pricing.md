# Bank Zero — Products & Pricing

## 1. Zero-fee transactional banking (individuals & businesses)
- **R0 monthly account fees** on personal and business accounts (any income/balance) [OBS] — [Bank Zero pricing guide 2026](https://www.bankzero.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Bank-Zero-pricing-guide-2026.pdf)
- **R0 transaction fees:** debit orders, EFTs, card online, card in-store, phone tap, payments to other Bank Zero customers [OBS] — [Bank Zero rates & pricing](https://www.bankzero.co.za/rates-and-pricing/)
- First SA bank to charge **zero for both EFTs and debit orders** [OBS] — [Bank Zero pricing FAQ](https://www.bankzero.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Pricing-FAQ.pdf)

## 2. Savings / cash investments
- **Savings and Notice accounts** (cash investments) free to open [OBS] — [Bank Zero rates & pricing](https://www.bankzero.co.za/rates-and-pricing/)
- Savings rate **5.00%** (<R1k) rising with balance (**6.25%** at ≥R1k; tiers up to higher rates at ≥R25k, ≥R50k, ≥R100k, ≥R500k, ≥R1m) — effective 25 Sep 2026 [OBS] — [Bank Zero rates & pricing](https://www.bankzero.co.za/rates-and-pricing/)
- Headline rate reported as **5%–6.5%** variable [OBS] — [VaultCue](https://vaultcue.com/accounts/bank-zero/review/)

## 3. Card security
- **Patented debit card security architecture** — card data changes to limit fraud during transactions [OBS] — [VaultCue](https://vaultcue.com/accounts/bank-zero/review/), [CreditDeals](https://creditdeals.io/za/debit-cards/bankzero)
- Mastercard debit card (physical + digital) [OBS] — [CreditDeals](https://creditdeals.io/za/debit-cards/bankzero)

## 4. Business banking
- **Multi-user business authorisations** and automated invoicing tools without separate software subscriptions [OBS] — [VaultCue](https://vaultcue.com/accounts/bank-zero/review/)
- Business account for sole traders to large companies, even NPOs, at R0 [OBS] — [Bank Zero pricing guide 2026](https://www.bankzero.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Bank-Zero-pricing-guide-2026.pdf)

## 5. Lending (planned)
- Applied to SARB/PA to offer **loans**; plans a lending push funded by deposits + Lesaka capital (pending approval) [OBS] — [itnewsafrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/bank-zero-profit/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/south-africa-s-bank-zero-turns-profitable-plans-lending-push)

## 6. Limitations
- No physical branches or cash-deposit teller services; international wire services limited vs corporate clearing banks [OBS] — [VaultCue](https://vaultcue.com/accounts/bank-zero/review/)
