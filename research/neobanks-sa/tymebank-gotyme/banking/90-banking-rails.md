# TymeBank / GoTyme Bank — Banking Rails

## 1. Payment rails & participation
- As a fully licensed bank, TymeBank participates directly in the **South African National Payment System (NPS)** [INF] — being a Banks Act-licensed bank is the prerequisite for direct participation [OBS] — [PRIFinance](https://prifinance.com/en/payments/south-africa-payment-license/)
- **RTC (Real-Time Clearing)**, **PayShap** (instant rail launched 13 Mar 2023) and **SAMOS** (settlement system) are the key SA rails; banks settle on SAMOS [OBS] — [PRIFinance](https://prifinance.com/en/payments/south-africa-payment-license/)
- Card scheme: **Mastercard** debit card (issued via BPC SmartVista) [OBS] — [BPC](https://www.bpcbt.com/en/press/from-zero-to-12-million-customers.-a-decade-long-partnership-of-gotyme-bank-and-bpc-drives-south-african-digital-adoption)

## 2. Cash access network
- Cash deposits/withdrawals at **Pick n Pay and Boxer tills**; cardless **TymeCode** withdrawals [OBS] — [MoneyToday](https://moneytoday.co.za/banking/tymebank-internet-banking)
- Kiosk-based biometric onboarding (<5 min) [OBS] — [Grokipedia/TymeBank](https://grokipedia.com/page/TymeBank)

## 3. Deposit protection
- Customer deposits covered by the **Corporation for Deposit Insurance (CODI)** under the same banking licence, unchanged through the rebrand [OBS] — [MoneyToday](https://moneytoday.co.za/banking/tymebank-internet-banking)

## 4. Interchange / revenue mix
- FY2025 revenue R3.1bn supported by growing **net interest income and diversified fee streams** [OBS] — [AFS 2025](https://gotyme.co.za/static-assets/Disclosures/tymebank-limited-afs-2025-signed.pdf)

## 5. Regulatory rails evolution
