# TymeBank / GoTyme Bank — Products & Pricing

## 1. Core transactional & savings
- **EveryDay transactional account:** no monthly fees; account opening at kiosk/online [OBS] — [Forbes Africa](https://www.forbesafrica.com/wealth/2019/06/04/the-big-bank-theory-south-africas-banks-of-the-future)
- **GoalSave:** goal-based savings tool; tiered interest. Reported base ~**6%** with bonus tier up to **~10%** APY (2026) [OBS] — [MoneyToday best digital banks](https://moneytoday.co.za/banking/best-digital-banks)
- **Fixed-deposit products:** reported up to **9.75%** [OBS] — [MoneyToday](https://moneytoday.co.za/banking/best-digital-banks)
- **MoneyTransfer:** peer transfers [OBS] — [Forbes Africa](https://www.forbesafrica.com/wealth/2019/06/04/the-big-bank-theory-south-africas-banks-of-the-future)
- Product suite at launch also included a **TymeCoach** app [OBS] — same source

## 2. Lending
- Consumer and **MSME/SME lending**; merchant cash advance offered via Tyme's product set [OBS] — [Tyme newsroom](https://www.tyme.com/news-room/singapores-tyme-group-delivers-the-worlds-fastest-profitable-standalone)
- Net advances R2.25bn (FY2025) [OBS] — [AFS 2025](https://gotyme.co.za/static-assets/Disclosures/tymebank-limited-afs-2025-signed.pdf)

## 3. Cards & access
- **Mastercard**/scheme debit card issued via BPC SmartVista [OBS] — [BPC](https://www.bpcbt.com/en/press/from-zero-to-12-million-customers.-a-decade-long-partnership-of-gotyme-bank-and-bpc-drives-south-african-digital-adoption)
- Cardless cash via **TymeCode** at Pick n Pay/Boxer tills [OBS] — [MoneyToday](https://moneytoday.co.za/banking/tymebank-internet-banking)

## 4. Distribution (part of product experience)
- Kiosk onboarding in retail stores (<5 min account opening, biometric verification) [OBS] — [Grokipedia/TymeBank](https://grokipedia.com/page/TymeBank)
- **Mar 2026 pivot:** kiosks removed from Pick n Pay; moved to mall-based **Customer Hubs**, plus kiosks in Boxer and TFG stores [OBS] — [MoneyToday](https://moneytoday.co.za/banking/tymebank-internet-banking), [Pick n Pay FAQ](https://www.pnp.co.za/smart-shopper/smart-shopper-partners/tymebank-faq)
