# TymeBank / GoTyme Bank — Customer Service & Complaints

## 1. Ombudsman context (sector-wide)
- South Africa's banking complaints now fall under the **National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFO)**, which consolidated four former ombud offices [OBS] — [Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/fraud-floods-complaints-as-banking-ombud-speeds-up-resolutions/)
- NFO Banking Division opened a record **15,412 cases in 2024** (up from 8,521 in 2023); ~**79% of formal outcomes favoured the banks**, 21% the complainant [OBS] — [Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/when-banks-get-it-wrong-ombud-cases-reveal-costly-mistakes/)
- NFO returned **R328.5m** to consumers in year 1 (2024) and **~R443m** in year 2 (2025); 50,065 cases opened / 34,277 closed in 2025 [OBS] — [Headtopics](https://be.headtopics.com/news/national-financial-ombud-scheme-returns-r443-million-to-84276381)
- >R60m returned to bank clients in 2025, bulk fraud-related [OBS] — [Primedia Plus](https://www.primediaplus.com/2025/12/17/financial-ombud-makes-banks-return-r60-million-to-clients-bulk-around-cases-of-fraud/)
- Bank-specific complaint volumes (TymeBank): **[UNK]** — NFO reports do not publicly disaggregate per neobank in the sources reviewed.

## 2. Documented complaint themes
- **Fraud/scam grievances**, notably a **July 2025 wave of scams** affecting dozens of accounts; TymeBank denied a data breach and emphasised user responsibility for OTP sharing [UNVERIFIED] — [Grokipedia/TymeBank](https://grokipedia.com/page/TymeBank)
- **Failed/erroneous transfers** and refusal to reverse funds [OBS] — [HelloPeter](https://www.hellopeter.com/tymebank/reviews/tymebank-refuses-to-reverse-my-money-5683736)
- Card replacement fee handling complaints [OBS] — [HelloPeter](https://www.hellopeter.com/tymebank/reviews/r70-expiring-card-replacement-fee-to-be-refunded-5964796)
