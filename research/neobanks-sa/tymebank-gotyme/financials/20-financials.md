# TymeBank / GoTyme Bank — Financials

## 1. Headline results (Tyme Bank Limited / Group, year to 30 June)

### FY2025 (year ended 30 June 2025) [OBS]
- Customer base **+21% to 11.6m** [OBS] — [AFS 2025](https://www.tymebank.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TymeBank-Limited-AFS-2025-Signed.pdf)
- Deposits **+22% to R7.7bn** [OBS] — [AFS 2025](https://www.tymebank.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TymeBank-Limited-AFS-2025-Signed.pdf)
- Net advances **+17% to R2.25bn** [OBS] — [AFS 2025](https://gotyme.co.za/static-assets/Disclosures/tymebank-limited-afs-2025-signed.pdf)
- Revenue **+29% to R3.1bn**; **loss before tax narrowed 32% to R226m** [OBS] — [AFS 2025](https://gotyme.co.za/static-assets/Disclosures/tymebank-limited-afs-2025-signed.pdf)

### FY2024 (year ended 30 June 2024) [OBS]
- Customers **+29% to 9.75m** [OBS]
- Deposits **+55% to R6.29bn** [OBS]
- Customer advances **+25% to R1.77bn** [OBS]
- Revenue reported at R1.92bn [OBS]
- Source: [TymeBank AFS 2024](https://www.tymebank.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/TymeBank-Limited-Annual-Report-2024-26-signed.pdf)

### Customer-growth trajectory (SA) [OBS]
- 50,000 customers within 2 months of launch [OBS] — [billionaires.africa](https://www.billionaires.africa/2026/05/18/billionaire-patrice-motsepes-gotyme-bank-eyes-15-billion-listing-as-customer-count-surpasses-21-million/)
- 1m in year 1; 3.5m by year 2; 6m+ by year 3 [OBS] — same source
- 2.3m in first 18 months (world's fastest growing at the time) [OBS] — [FAFW case study](https://financialallianceforwomen.org/wp-content/uploads/download-manager-files/FAFW%20CaseStudy-TymeGroup%20%281%29.pdf)
- 10.7m (Dec 2024) → 11.6m (FY2025, Jun 2025) [OBS] — [Africa Signal](https://africasignal.com/tymebank-the-digital-disruptor-democratizing-banking-in-south-africa/amp/)

## 2. Profitability milestone
- **First profitable month: December 2023**, in under 5 years since Feb 2019 launch — the first digital bank in South Africa and Africa to break even [OBS] — [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/press-releases/tymebank-south-africa-hits-profitability-in-under-5-years)
- Prior accumulated losses of **US$347m (≈R6.6bn)** before break-even; annual revenue run-rate ~US$95m (≈R1.8bn) by late 2023 [OBS] — [GhanaHype](https://ghanahype.com/tymebank-reaches-profitability-and-unicorn-valuation-in-2025/)
- Sustained profitability targeted by June 2024 [OBS] — [Investing.com ZA](https://za.investing.com/news/technology-news/tyme-group-expands-its-customer-base-to-124-million-across-sa-and-the-philippines-3185779)
- GoTyme Bank Philippines forecast to break even in Q4 2025 [OBS] — same source

## 3. Capital raises & valuation
- **Dec 2021:** US$180m raise (Tencent, CDC Group) [OBS] — [Tyme media statement](https://www.tymebank.co.za/content/uploads/2021/12/Capital-Raise_Media-Statement_Final-071221.pdf)
- **Dec 2024 (Series D):** US$250m at **US$1.5bn valuation** — Nubank US$150m (≈10% stake), M&G Catalyst US$50m, existing shareholders US$50m [OBS] — [Tyme newsroom](https://www.tyme.com/news-room/latin-americas-nubank-leads-newly-minted-unicorn-tymes-capital-raise)
- Total disclosed funding ~US$500m+ [OBS] — [Embedded Finance Index](https://www.embeddedfinanceindex.com/companies/tymebank)
- 2026: reported ambition of a **US$15bn listing / IPO** [UNVERIFIED] — [billionaires.africa](https://www.billionaires.africa/2026/05/18/billionaire-patrice-motsepes-gotyme-bank-eyes-15-billion-listing-as-customer-count-surpasses-21-million/)
