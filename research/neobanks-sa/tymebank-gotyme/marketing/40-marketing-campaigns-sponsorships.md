# TymeBank / GoTyme Bank — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

## 1. Brand positioning
- Launch tagline: **"It's Tyme to bank on your terms"**; promise of low-cost, convenient, accessible banking [OBS] — [TymeBank media release 2019](https://www.tymebank.co.za/content/uploads/2019/02/TymeBank-Consumer-Education-Story.pdf)
- First-mover marketing: "South Africa's first digital bank" and "first majority black-owned bank" [OBS] — [Tyme newsroom](https://www.tyme.com/news-room/tymebank-south-africas-first-digital-bank-goes-live)
- 2026 rebrand campaign: GoTyme Bank — "From South Africa to the world. A bank born in South Africa, built for everyone everywhere." [OBS] — [AppBrain](https://developers.appbrain.com/app/gotyme-bank-%7C-south-africa/za.co.gotyme)

## 2. Distribution as marketing (retail / kiosk-led growth)
- Growth driven by **retail kiosk presence** rather than heavy traditional ad spend; ~150,000 new SA customers/month [OBS] — [Grokipedia/TymeBank](https://grokipedia.com/page/TymeBank)
- Low customer-acquisition cost cited (~US$3–4 per kiosk customer; ~US$0.60 web) [OBS] — [CGAP presentation](https://www.cgap.org/sites/default/files/event_documents/2020_11_02-Webinar-Presentation-Inclusive-Digital-Banking.pdf)

## 3. Rebrand & app launch (2026)
