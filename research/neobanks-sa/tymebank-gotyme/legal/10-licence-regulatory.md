# TymeBank / GoTyme Bank — Licence & Regulatory

## 1. Banking licence
- **Tyme Bank Limited** holds a full **commercial banking licence under the Banks Act 94 of 1990**, granted/regulated by the **South African Reserve Bank (SARB)** and the **Prudential Authority (PA)**, effective 2018 [OBS] — [neobanks.guide](https://neobanks.guide/best-neobank-south-africa/), [TrustFinance](https://www.trustfinance.com/companies/tyme-bank-1/overview)
- It was the **first digital-only bank granted a commercial banking licence by SARB** [OBS] — [TrustFinance](https://www.trustfinance.com/companies/tyme-bank-1/overview)
- **Registered entity identifiers:** Tyme Bank Limited Reg no. 2015/231510/06; FSP 49140 (authorised Financial Services Provider); NCRCP 10774 (registered credit provider) [OBS] — [bank.tymedigital.co.za](https://bank.tymedigital.co.za/)
- Regulator class per neobanks.guide: **Commercial Bank · Banks Act 1990**; **CODI** (Corporation for Deposit Insurance) eligible [OBS] — [neobanks.guide](https://neobanks.guide/best-neobank-south-africa/)

## 2. Name change / regulator notice
- SARB **Financial Surveillance Department** gave notice in the Government Gazette (Monday **13 April 2026**) that the name of **Tyme Bank Limited** changed to **GoTyme Bank** [OBS] — [MyBroadband](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/banking/640299-tymebank-changes-its-name.html)
- The **banking licence, account numbers, cards and login details carried over**; deposit protection (CODI) unchanged [OBS] — [MoneyToday](https://moneytoday.co.za/banking/tymebank-internet-banking)
- Note: SARB notice text reported as "the name of Tyme Bank Limited has..." [OBS] — [MyBroadband](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/banking/640299-tymebank-changes-its-name.html)

## 3. Ownership & prudential structure
- **ARC Financial Services Holdings** is the majority shareholder of Tyme Bank Holdings Limited (the registered controlling company of Tyme Bank Limited); historical stakes: 76.95% (Jun 2019) → 59.96% (Sep 2021) → 50.03% (FY2023) [OBS] — [Basel Pillar III 2019](https://www.tymebank.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/Tyme-Bank-Pillar-III-Report_June-2019.pdf), [Basel Pillar III 2021](https://www.tymebank.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/basel-pillar-iii-disclosure-as-at-30-september-2021.pdf.pdf), [Tyme Bank Holdings AFS 2023](https://www.tymebank.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TymeBank-Holdings_AFS.pdf)
- ARC is a 50.1% subsidiary within the **Ubuntu-Botho Investments** group [OBS] — [Basel Pillar III 2021](https://www.tymebank.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/basel-pillar-iii-disclosure-as-at-30-september-2021.pdf.pdf)
- **Basel Pillar III disclosures** are published for Tyme Bank Limited and Tyme Bank Holdings Limited [OBS] — [Basel Pillar III 2020](https://gotyme.co.za/static-assets/Disclosures/basel-pillar-3-disclosure-30-june-2020.pdf)
- Majority **black-owned** and controlled; described as SA's first majority black-owned retail bank [OBS] — [Tyme Group newsroom](https://www.tyme.com/news-room/tymebank-south-africas-first-digital-bank-goes-live)

## 4. Adjacent regulated licences
- **Philippines:** GoTyme Bank obtained a **digital banking licence from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP)** on 29 July 2022 [OBS] — [GoTyme Bank 2022 Annual Report](https://www.gotyme.com.ph/static-assets/files/pdf/gotyme-bank-2022-annual-report.pdf)
- **FS licence & credit provider registration** held in SA (FSP 49140; NCRCP 10774) [OBS] — [bank.tymedigital.co.za](https://bank.tymedigital.co.za/)

## 5. Regulatory context (SA neobank sector)
- Non-bank fintechs generally could only access the National Payment System through a **sponsoring bank**; SARB's **Vision 2025** and draft exemption notices (Banks Act s.1(1)(cc) / NPS Act) propose an **activity-based authorisation framework** for non-banks [OBS] — [Bowmans](https://bowmanslaw.com/insights/the-south-african-reserve-bank-has-recently-published-a-draft-activity-based-authorisation-framework-for-participants-banks-and-non-banks-in-the-national-payment-system/), [FacePhi](https://facephi.com/observatory/en/national-payment-system-south-africa-psp-licences/)
