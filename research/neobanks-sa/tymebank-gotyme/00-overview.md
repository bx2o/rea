# TymeBank / GoTyme Bank — Entity Overview

## 1. Identity
- **Legal name (bank):** Tyme Bank Limited (Reg no. 2015/231510/06) [OBS] — [bank.tymedigital.co.za](https://bank.tymedigital.co.za/)
- **Parent:** Tyme Bank Holdings Limited (Reg no. 2015/228007/06); controlling company is Tyme Group (Tyme Limited), headquartered in Singapore [OBS] — [Tyme Group AFS 2025](https://gotyme.co.za/static-assets/Disclosures/tymebank-holdings-limited-afs-2025-signed.pdf), [Embedded Finance Index](https://www.embeddedfinanceindex.com/companies/tymebank)
- **Brand:** launched as **TymeBank** (Feb 2019); rebranded to **GoTyme Bank** (2026) to align with GoTyme Bank Philippines [OBS] — [MyBroadband](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/banking/640299-tymebank-changes-its-name.html)
- **HQ:** Johannesburg, South Africa (30 Jellicoe Avenue, Gauteng) [OBS] — [LeadIQ](https://www.leadiq.com/c/gotyme-bank/5a1d989d2300005a008746d0/employee-directory)

## 2. Founding & journey
- **2015:** Tyme (Tyme Limited) established; Tyme Bank Limited registered [OBS] — [neobanks.guide](https://neobanks.guide/best-neobank-south-africa/)
- **5 Nov 2018:** Patrice Motsepe's **African Rainbow Capital (ARC) Financial Services Holdings** obtains Prudential Authority approval to acquire the business (~90% stake) from the **Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA)** (acquisition concluded 29 Oct 2018) [OBS] — [Basil Pillar III 2020](https://gotyme.co.za/static-assets/Disclosures/basel-pillar-3-disclosure-30-june-2020.pdf), [Tyme Group newsroom](https://www.tyme.com/news-room/tymebank-south-africas-first-digital-bank-goes-live)
- **Nov 2018:** soft launch (limited kiosks + online account opening) [OBS] — [MonoKromatik](https://www.monokromatik.com/intelligence/case-studies/tymebank-south-africa-digital-bank)
- **Feb 2019:** full public launch as **South Africa's first fully licensed, fully digital retail bank** and first majority black-owned retail bank [OBS] — [Tyme Group newsroom](https://www.tyme.com/news-room/tymebank-south-africas-first-digital-bank-goes-live)
- **Dec 2023:** first profitable month — first digital bank in South Africa and Africa to break even, in under 5 years [OBS] — [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/press-releases/tymebank-south-africa-hits-profitability-in-under-5-years)
- **Dec 2024:** Series D raise of US$250m at **US$1.5bn valuation** (unicorn), led by Nubank [OBS] — [Tyme newsroom](https://www.tyme.com/news-room/latin-americas-nubank-leads-newly-minted-unicorn-tymes-capital-raise)
- **21 Oct 2025 → early 2026:** rebrand to **GoTyme Bank**; SARB confirms name change in Government Gazette (13 Apr 2026) [OBS] — [MyBroadband](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/banking/640299-tymebank-changes-its-name.html), [Grokipedia/TymeBank](https://grokipedia.com/page/TymeBank)
- **Mar 2026:** kiosks removed from Pick n Pay stores; pivot to mall-based "consumer hubs" [OBS] — [Pick n Pay FAQ](https://www.pnp.co.za/smart-shopper/smart-shopper-partners/tymebank-faq)

## 3. Scale snapshot (latest verified)
- Customers: **11.6m in SA (FY2025, +21% YoY)**; Tyme Group **~21–22m across SA + Philippines** (2026) [OBS] — [Tyme Bank Limited AFS 2025](https://www.tymebank.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TymeBank-Limited-AFS-2025-Signed.pdf), [Tyme Group](https://www.tyme.com/)
- Deposits: **R7.7bn (FY2025, +22%)**; net advances R2.25bn [OBS] — [Tyme Bank Limited AFS 2025](https://www.tymebank.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TymeBank-Limited-AFS-2025-Signed.pdf)
- Revenue FY2025: **R3.1bn (+29%)**; loss before tax **R226m (narrowed 32%)** [OBS] — [Tyme Bank Limited AFS 2025](https://gotyme.co.za/static-assets/Disclosures/tymebank-limited-afs-2025-signed.pdf)
- Group: Tyme Group 2,000+ employees worldwide; ~500k new customers/month across SA+PH [OBS] — [Tyme Group who-we-are](https://www.tyme.com/who-we-are)

## 4. Positioning
- Low-cost, no-branch, digital-first bank with retail **kiosk** distribution; "banking on your terms"; undercuts incumbent fees [OBS] — [TymeBank Fintech Story](https://www.tymebank.co.za/content/uploads/2019/02/TymeBank-Fintech-Story.pdf)
- Global strategy: "embed banking in people's everyday lives" via retail ecosystems (Pick n Pay/Boxer in SA; Gokongwei/Robinsons in PH) [OBS] — [Tyme newsroom](https://www.tyme.com/news-room/tyme-secures-new-international-investors-in-latest-capital-raise-with-more)
