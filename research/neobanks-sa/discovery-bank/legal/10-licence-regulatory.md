# Discovery Bank — Licence & Regulatory

## 1. Banking licence
- **Discovery Bank Limited** is a **registered bank** under the **Banks Act 94 of 1990**, regulated by **SARB / Prudential Authority** [OBS] — [neobanks.guide](https://neobanks.guide/best-neobank-south-africa/)
- Regulator class: **Commercial Bank · Banks Act 1990** [OBS] — [neobanks.guide](https://neobanks.guide/best-neobank-south-africa/)
- **Registered identifiers:** Discovery Bank Limited, Reg no. 2015/408745/06; **FSP 48657** (authorised financial services provider); **NCRCP 9997** (registered credit provider); VAT reg. 45902 [OBS] — [online.discovery.bank](https://online.discovery.bank/), [Discovery Bank launch doc](https://cdn.discovery.co.za/assets/cdn-content/public/documents/faz/bank-discoverer-sept-2024.pdf)

## 2. Group & prudential
- Wholly part of **Discovery Limited**, a JSE-listed group; the bank is presented as central to the group's "shared-value" ecosystem [OBS] — [Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/business/the-bank-is-now-firmly-at-the-centre-of-everything-discovery-does/)
- Covered by **CODI** deposit-insurance framework as a registered bank [INF] — applies to all SA registered banks: [neobanks.guide](https://neobanks.guide/best-neobank-south-africa/)

## 3. Conduct / disclosure
- Discovery Bank publishes **benefit guides, rewards guides and product T&Cs** (e.g., Vitality Money dynamic discounts/rewards tables) [OBS] — [Discovery rewards guide](https://cdn.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/bank/marketing-material/vitality-money/understanding-your-dynamic-discounts-rewards-percentages-discovery-purple-full-banking-suite.pdf)
- Compliance with **NCA** (credit provider registration NCRCP 9997) and **FAIS** (FSP 48657) [OBS] — [online.discovery.bank](https://online.discovery.bank/)

## 4. Regulatory context
- Part of SA's licensed-digital-bank cohort alongside TymeBank and African Bank (all Banks Act-licensed); Bank Zero holds a separate **mutual bank** licence [OBS] — [neobanks.guide](https://neobanks.guide/best-neobank-south-africa/)
