# Discovery Bank — Products & Pricing

## 1. Vitality Money (behavioural engine)
- Core behavioural programme; clients earn **dynamic discounts and rewards percentages** based on **Vitality Money status** (Blue → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Diamond) and money-management behaviour [OBS] — [Discovery rewards guides](https://cdn.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/bank/marketing-material/vitality-money/understanding-your-dynamic-discounts-rewards-percentages-discovery-purple-full-banking-suite.pdf)
- Higher status → greater value via a personalised "stack of shared-value rewards" [OBS] — [Brand Finance](https://brandfinance.com/insights/discovery)

## 2. Banking suites (transaction + credit card)
- Four **Discovery Bank Suites**: **Gold, Platinum, Black, Purple** — each with a transaction account + credit card account, best-in-class interest rates and rewards [OBS] — [Discovery transactional products](https://www.discovery.co.za/bank/info-and-tips-discovery-bank-products)
- Standalone **credit cards** also offered, with lifestyle benefits [OBS] — same source
- Example pricing: Employee benefit guide cites **Gold Suite card from R74/month** (with free Gold Transaction Account) [OBS] — [Discovery benefit guide](https://www.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/bank/benefit-guides/db-benefit-guide-consolidated.pdf)
- **Purple Suite** rewards: 5–6% dynamic discounts at higher spend bands (R3,150+) [OBS] — [Purple rewards overview](https://cdn.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/bank/marketing-material/vitality-money/understanding-your-dynamic-discounts-rewards-percentages-discovery-purple-full-banking-suite.pdf)

## 3. Other products
- **Savings and investment** accounts (free, unlimited savings in employee benefit) [OBS] — [Discovery benefit guide](https://www.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/bank/benefit-guides/db-benefit-guide-consolidated.pdf)
- **Lending:** home loans, vehicle finance, personal loans added to boost profitability (2023 board proposal) [OBS] — [HBS case](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?num=66477)
- **Discovery Mileage:** the group's rewards currency, linked to the Vitality/shared-value ecosystem [OBS] — [Brand Finance](https://brandfinance.com/insights/discovery)
- Insurance/medical-aid/investment products viewable in-app (ecosystem integration) [OBS] — [Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/business/the-bank-is-now-firmly-at-the-centre-of-everything-discovery-does/)

## 4. Digital features
- **Discovery Pay**, **Live Assist**, **Smart Vault**; shared design across app and online banking [OBS] — [Discovery digital banking](https://www.discovery.co.za/bank/digital-banking)
- In-app authorisation (app-based approval rather than OTP) [OBS] — [HelloPeter reviews](https://www.hellopeter.com/discovery-bank/reviews/not-user-friendly-bae08e2bb2cd61528ae7ceb2839546989e4db289-5467156)

## 5. Pricing model
- **Tiered monthly pricing** (unlike the R0 model of TymeBank/Bank Zero); fee depends on suite/account chosen [OBS] — [NextRand](https://nextrand.co.za/banks), [MoneyToday](https://moneytoday.co.za/banking/best-digital-banks)
