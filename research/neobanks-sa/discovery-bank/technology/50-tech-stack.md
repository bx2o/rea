# Discovery Bank — Technology Stack

## 1. Digital platform
- Fully digital bank with an **app + online banking platform** sharing the same design language (no traditional branch network) [OBS] — [Discovery digital banking](https://www.discovery.co.za/bank/digital-banking)
- Built-in features: **Discovery Pay**, **Live Assist** (in-app assistance/calling), **Smart Vault** [OBS] — [Discovery digital banking](https://www.discovery.co.za/bank/digital-banking)

## 2. Ecosystem / platform integration
- 2023 strategy: integrate Discovery's suite of apps with **the banking app as the central platform** for all group products [OBS] — [HBS case](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?num=66477)
- In-app real-time connection to specialists via in-app calling; cross-product visibility (medical aid claims, insurance, investments) [OBS] — [Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/business/the-bank-is-now-firmly-at-the-centre-of-everything-discovery-does/)

## 3. AI & data
- FY2026 results cite improving efficiency/client experience **"increasingly through the deployment of AI"** [OBS] — [Discovery FY2026 results](https://www.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/corporate/investor-relations/2026/annual-results.pdf)
- Behavioural/risk analytics underpins the shared-value model (data-driven incentives) [OBS] — [Brand Finance](https://brandfinance.com/insights/discovery)

## 4. Security model
- **App-based transaction authorisation** (in-app approval); some clients report OTP unavailability [OBS] — [HelloPeter](https://www.hellopeter.com/discovery-bank/reviews/not-user-friendly-bae08e2bb2cd61528ae7ceb2839546989e4db289-5467156)
