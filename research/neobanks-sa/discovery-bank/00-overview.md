# Discovery Bank — Entity Overview

## 1. Identity
- **Legal name:** Discovery Bank Limited (Reg no. 2015/408745/06); FSP 48657; NCRCP 9997 [OBS] — [online.discovery.bank](https://online.discovery.bank/), [Discovery Bank benefit guide](https://www.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/bank/benefit-guides/db-benefit-guide-consolidated.pdf)
- **Parent:** **Discovery Limited** (JSE-listed), founder & Group CEO **Adrian Gore** [OBS] — [Discovery leadership](https://www.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/corporate/investor-relations/discovery-leadership.pdf)
- **HQ:** Johannesburg / Sandton, South Africa [OBS] — [Discovery Bank](https://online.discovery.bank/)
- **Model:** world's first **"shared-value" / behavioural bank** [OBS] — [Brand Finance](https://brandfinance.com/insights/discovery)

## 2. Founding & journey
- **March 2019:** Discovery Bank launched (with core product **Vitality Money**) [OBS] — [JEF Journal](https://jefjournal.org.za/index.php/jef/article/view/602/1163)
- Applies Discovery's **Vitality shared-value insurance model** (introduced 1997) to financial behaviour — rewarding good money management with better rates/rewards [OBS] — [Brand Finance](https://brandfinance.com/insights/discovery), [Grokipedia/Adrian Gore](https://grokipedia.com/page/Adrian_Gore)
- **2023:** CEO **Hylton Kallner** presented proposals to integrate Discovery's suite of apps with banking as central platform, and to add lending products [OBS] — [HBS case](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?num=66477)
- **FY2025 (to 30 Jun 2025):** reached monthly break-even at end of H1; first profitable period in H2, ahead of plan [OBS] — [Discovery FY2025 results](https://mso.discoveryholdings.com/wcm/discoverycoza/assets/investor-relations/2025/results-booklet-fy-2025.pdf)
- **FY2026 (to 30 Jun 2026):** delivered **first annual profit of R370m** (vs R68m loss FY25) [OBS] — [Discovery FY2026 annual results](https://www.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/corporate/investor-relations/2026/annual-results.pdf)

## 3. Scale snapshot (latest verified)
- **1.57m clients (+26%)** and **3.77m accounts (+26%)** at FY2026 [OBS] — [Discovery FY2026 results](https://www.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/corporate/investor-relations/2026/annual-results.pdf)
- ~**1,500 new customers daily** by mid-FY2026 [OBS] — [The Banking Brief](https://the-banking-brief.beehiiv.com/p/discovery-adding-1500-customers-daily)
- Estimated cumulative group investment to build the bank ~**R15bn** [EST] — [Daily Investor](https://dailyinvestor.com/banking/152648/discovery-bank-enters-a-new-era-with-600-profit-jump/)

## 4. Positioning
- "The world's first shared-value bank" combining technology, risk understanding, behaviour change and incentives [OBS] — [Brand Finance](https://brandfinance.com/insights/discovery)
- Financial health as a measurable, improvable behaviour — better credit book than one that merely prices existing risk [OBS] — [Kurums](https://kurums.com/discovery-limited-business-story/)
