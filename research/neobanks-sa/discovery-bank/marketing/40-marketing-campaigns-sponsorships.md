# Discovery Bank — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

## 1. Brand positioning
- "World's first **shared-value bank**"; "The Future of Banking. Now." [OBS] — [Discovery digital banking](https://www.discovery.co.za/bank/digital-banking), [Brand Finance](https://brandfinance.com/insights/discovery)
- Core message: improving financial behaviour earns greater value (better rates + rewards) [OBS] — [Brand Finance](https://brandfinance.com/insights/discovery)

## 2. Ecosystem marketing (Vitality flywheel)
- Leverages the wider **Discovery Vitality** ecosystem (health, insurance, investments) to acquire and retain bank clients; banking app positioned as central platform for all Discovery products [OBS] — [Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/business/the-bank-is-now-firmly-at-the-centre-of-everything-discovery-does/)
- Vitality programme expanded to 40+ countries/markets globally [OBS] — [Grokipedia/Adrian Gore](https://grokipedia.com/page/Adrian_Gore)

## 3. Growth & engagement
- Growing ~**1,500 customers/day** (H1 FY2026); ~1,400 daily sales June 2025 [OBS] — [The Banking Brief](https://the-banking-brief.beehiiv.com/p/discovery-adding-1500-customers-daily), [Discovery FY2025](https://mso.discoveryholdings.com/wcm/discoverycoza/assets/investor-relations/2025/results-booklet-fy-2025.pdf)
- Increasing use of **AI** in client experience/engagement (FY2026) [OBS] — [Discovery FY2026 results](https://www.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/corporate/investor-relations/2026/annual-results.pdf)

## 4. Sponsorships
- Discovery's sponsorship footprint is primarily via the **Vitality** brand (wellness/behaviour change) and its own ecosystem, rather than a jersey/team naming deal [INF].
