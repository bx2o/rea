## 2. Trust index / complaints themes
- **Hellopeter TrustIndex 3.3/10** based on 917 reviews (trailing 12 months); 7,548 total reviews tracked; replies to 95% of negative reviews (typical ~5h 8m) [OBS] — [HelloPeter](https://www.hellopeter.com/discovery-bank)
- Documented themes:
  - **Accounts blocked / app-login problems** [OBS] — [HelloPeter](https://www.hellopeter.com/discovery-bank/reviews/unresolved-discovery-bank-app-login-problem-6124031)
  - **OTP/app-only authorisation** makes access hard if phone is lost/forgotten [OBS] — [HelloPeter](https://www.hellopeter.com/discovery-bank/reviews/not-user-friendly-bae08e2bb2cd61528ae7ceb2839546989e4db289-5467156)

  - **Home-loan/refinance declines and escalation failures** [OBS] — [HelloPeter](https://www.hellopeter.com/discovery-bank/reviews/writing-discovery-bank-has-handled-my-refinance-application-and-complaint-escalation-extremely-poorly-6376646)
