# Discovery Bank — Financials

## 1. FY2026 (year ended 30 June 2026) [OBS]
- **First annual profit: R370m** (normalised profit from operations), vs a **loss of R68m in FY25** [OBS] — [Discovery FY2026 annual results](https://www.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/corporate/investor-relations/2026/annual-results.pdf)
- Profit jump reported as **~600%** [OBS] — [Daily Investor](https://dailyinvestor.com/banking/152648/discovery-bank-enters-a-new-era-with-600-profit-jump/)
- **Client base +26% to 1.57m**; **total accounts +26% to 3.77m** [OBS] — [Discovery FY2026 results](https://www.discovery.co.za/assets/discoverycoza/corporate/investor-relations/2026/annual-results.pdf)
- Bank "no longer requires investment from the group" [OBS] — [Daily Investor](https://dailyinvestor.com/banking/152648/discovery-bank-enters-a-new-era-with-600-profit-jump/)

## 2. FY2025 (year ended 30 June 2025) [OBS]
- Operating loss before new business acquisition costs improved by **R420m**; overall loss **R386m better** than prior year [OBS] — [Discovery FY2025 results](https://mso.discoveryholdings.com/wcm/discoverycoza/assets/investor-relations/2025/results-booklet-fy-2025.pdf)
- **Monthly break-even at end of H1**; **first profitable period in H2**, ahead of plan [OBS] — same source
- Client base **+30% to June 2025**; reached **~1,400 daily sales** in June 2025 [OBS] — same source

## 3. Interim / H1 FY2026 [OBS]
- Swung to profit in H1 FY2026, posting **R210–230m** vs a **R145m loss** a year earlier [OBS] — [The Banking Brief](https://the-banking-brief.beehiiv.com/p/discovery-adding-1500-customers-daily)
- Averaging **~1,500 new customers daily** by period-end; low credit-loss ratios; non-interest revenue and NII both up [OBS] — same source

## 4. Group context (FY2026) [OBS]
- Discovery Limited Group: normalised headline earnings **R11,795m (+21%)**; normalised operating profit **R17,750m (+29%)** [OBS] — [Discovery investor relations](https://www.discovery.co.za/corporate/investor-relations)

## 5. Investment recovery
- Estimated cumulative group investment to build the bank **≈R15bn** [EST] — [Daily Investor](https://dailyinvestor.com/banking/152648/discovery-bank-enters-a-new-era-with-600-profit-jump/)
