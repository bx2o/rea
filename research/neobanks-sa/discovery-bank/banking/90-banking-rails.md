# Discovery Bank — Banking Rails

## 1. NPS participation
- As a **Banks Act-licensed bank**, Discovery Bank participates directly in the **South African National Payment System (NPS)**, settling via **SAMOS** and using **RTC** and **PayShap** instant rails [INF] — licensed-bank prerequisite: [PRIFinance](https://prifinance.com/en/payments/south-africa-payment-license/), [neobanks.guide](https://neobanks.guide/best-neobank-south-africa/)
- Card scheme: Visa/Mastercard (product-level) [OBS] — [Discovery products](https://www.discovery.co.za/bank/info-and-tips-discovery-bank-products)

## 2. Proprietary rails / mechanics
- **Discovery Pay** — in-app payment capability [OBS] — [Discovery digital banking](https://www.discovery.co.za/bank/digital-banking)
- **Smart Vault** — secure storage feature [OBS] — same source
- **Discovery Mileage** — rewards currency tied to the Vitality shared-value ecosystem [OBS] — [Brand Finance](https://brandfinance.com/insights/discovery)

## 3. Deposit protection
- Deposit-taking under a registered banking licence → covered by **CODI** (Corporation for Deposit Insurance) [INF] — [neobanks.guide](https://neobanks.guide/best-neobank-south-africa/)

## 4. Lending rails
- Home loans, vehicle finance, personal loans (added to improve profitability) [OBS] — [HBS case](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?num=66477)
