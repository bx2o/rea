# Bettr — Licence & Regulatory

## 1. Licensing status
- **Bettr was NOT a licensed bank.** It relied on a **sponsoring bank** to access payment/rails [OBS] — [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/challenger-banks/challenger-banks-in-south-africa-who-s-who-and-what-s-their-tech)
- It operated as an **authorised distribution channel of Access Bank South Africa Limited** and was an authorised **Financial Services Provider** (FSP 5865) [OBS] — [Bettr showcase](https://bettr-showcase.webflow.io/)
- Cards provided by **Visa**; banking services via **Access Bank** [OBS] — [MyBroadband](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/banking/489889-south-africas-neobanks.html)

## 2. Structural constraint (founder commentary)
- Co-founders described the difficulty of building a "full-stack bank" **without holding a full banking licence** (Tier 1/Tier 2), accessing the payments book only via a sponsoring arrangement [OBS] — [TechCentral interview via Bettr showcase](https://bettr-showcase.webflow.io/post/tech-central-south-african-digital-bank-bettr-nears-launch-an-interview-with-the-founders)
- At the time there was **no "fintech payments-only licence"** to receive deposits and manage payments directly; the SARB was working on innovative fintech initiatives [OBS] — same source

## 3. Regulatory context
- Non-bank fintechs generally could access the NPS only through a **sponsoring bank**; SARB's **Vision 2025** and later **activity-based authorisation** drafts (2025–2026) aim to open the NPS to non-banks [OBS] — [Bowmans](https://bowmanslaw.com/insights/the-south-african-reserve-bank-has-recently-published-a-draft-activity-based-authorisation-framework-for-participants-banks-and-non-banks-in-the-national-payment-system/), [FacePhi](https://facephi.com/observatory/en/national-payment-system-south-africa-psp-licences/)

## 4. Outcome
- Bettr is reported **out of business** without having secured a banking licence [OBS] — [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/264925-45)
