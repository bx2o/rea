# Bettr — Entity Overview

## 1. Identity
- **Bettr** (Bettr Finance) — South African **digital banking app** targeting creators / younger consumers; HQ Cape Town [OBS] — [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/264925-45), [The Fintech Times](https://thefintechtimes.com/top-african-challenger-banks-helping-the-unbanked-through-mobile-services/)
- Positioning: "the digital banking app for creators"; "a new culture of money"; alternative to "the dominant world of finance" [OBS] — [Bettr showcase](https://bettr-showcase.webflow.io/)

## 2. Founding & journey
- **Founded 2015** (some sources note project creation date 09 May 2019) by **Tobie van Zyl (CEO)**, **Andrzej Stempowski (CTO, ex-Westpac)** and **Angus Brown (ex-COO)**; **Ryan Brüssow** later Chief Digital Officer [OBS] — [The Fintech Times](https://thefintechtimes.com/top-african-challenger-banks-helping-the-unbanked-through-mobile-services/), [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/challenger-banks/challenger-banks-in-south-africa-who-s-who-and-what-s-their-tech)
- Angus Brown **left in March 2018** [OBS] — [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/challenger-banks/challenger-banks-in-south-africa-who-s-who-and-what-s-their-tech)
- Target market: younger consumers (reported **16–35**) [OBS] — [The Fintech Times](https://thefintechtimes.com/top-african-challenger-banks-helping-the-unbanked-through-mobile-services/)
- Stated launch timeline slipped to 2022 ("targeting March 2022", alpha/beta) [OBS] — [Bettr showcase](https://bettr-showcase.webflow.io/)
- **Status: out of business** [OBS] — [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/264925-45), [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/bank-zero-vs-bettr)

## 3. Business model
- Zero monthly fees; no paperwork registration; QR-code payments; digital + physical Visa cards; transaction and savings accounts [OBS] — [MyBroadband](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/banking/489889-south-africas-neobanks.html)
- Relied on **Visa** and **Access Bank South Africa** to provide services (not a licensed bank itself) [OBS] — [MyBroadband](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/banking/489889-south-africas-neobanks.html)

## 4. Note on name confusion
- A separate, unrelated company also uses the **"Bettr"** name — **Bettr Finance** (bettr.com), an **Ant International** embedded-finance service for e-commerce SMEs (e.g., Brazil), announced March 2025 [OBS] — [bettr.com newsroom](https://www.bettr.com/zh/newsroom/ant-international-launches-embedded-finance-service-for-e-commerce-smes-in-brazil/). This is **not** the South African consumer neobank and must not be conflated [INF].

## 5. Scale snapshot
- WhatsApp-based sign-up waitlist reached ~**6,500 users** [OBS] — [The Fintech Times](https://thefintechtimes.com/top-african-challenger-banks-helping-the-unbanked-through-mobile-services/)
- Employees: **~16** [OBS] — [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/264925-45)
