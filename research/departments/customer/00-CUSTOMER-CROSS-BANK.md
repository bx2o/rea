# 00 — Customer Success: Cross-Bank Profile (SA)

> Public information only. Tags: `[OBS]`, `[DER]`, `[INF]`, `[EST]`, `[UNK]`.

## 1. The ombudsman landscape

- `[OBS]` The **Ombudsman for Banking Services** now sits within the consolidated **National Financial
  Ombud Scheme (NFO)**, which has divisions for Banking, Life Insurance, Non-life Insurance and Credit.
  Lead Ombud for the Banking Division is **Nerosha Maseti**
  ([Primedia Plus](https://www.primediaplus.com/2025/12/17/financial-ombud-makes-banks-return-r60-million-to-clients-bulk-around-cases-of-fraud)).

## 2. NFO 2025 annual report — headline findings

- `[OBS]` The NFO **opened 50,065 cases in 2025** (+16.36% vs 2024) and **closed 34,277** (+2.01%);
  average monthly case openings rose from **3,585 (2024)** to a higher 2025 rate
  ([Moonstone — NFO recovers R442.99m](https://www.moonstone.co.za/nfo-recovers-r442-99m-for-consumers-as-complaints-climb/);
  [AllAfrica — Financial Ombud claws back R443m](https://allafrica.com/stories/202606090101.html)).
- `[OBS]` Total recovery for consumers was **R442.99 million**, split: Life insurance **R299.6m**,
  Non-life **R82.9m**, **Banking R53m**, Credit **R7.47m**
  ([AllAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202606090101.html)). The Credit figure was **more than
  triple** the R2.36m recovered in 2024
  ([George Herald — Credit crisis](https://www.georgeherald.com/News/Article/Business/credit-crisis-ombud-exposes-exploitation-202606230245)).
- `[OBS]` Only **19% of closed cases were decided in favour of the consumer**
  ([Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/nfo-recovers-r442-99m-for-consumers-as-complaints-climb/)).

## 3. Banking Division complaint volumes & themes

- `[OBS]` **Banking was the largest driver of case volumes.** Formal banking cases rose **25.4%**, from
  an average of **593 to 742.83 cases per month**; premature cases rose ~11%, from 948.5 to 1,051.17
  per month ([Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/nfo-recovers-r442-99m-for-consumers-as-complaints-climb/)).
- `[OBS]` The Banking Division returned **more than R60 million** to consumers in 2025, mostly via
  rulings/settlements related to **banking fraud and service failures**; **~60%** of the R53m awarded
  related to fraud refunds
  ([African News Agency](https://africannewsagency.com/financial-ombuds-banking-division-returns-over-r60m-to-consumers-as-fraud-complaints-surge/);
  [HeadTopics](https://be.headtopics.com/news/national-financial-ombud-scheme-returns-r443-million-to-84276381)).
- `[OBS]` **Fraud remained the highest category of banking complaints**, driven by **unauthorised
  transactions, account hijacking and social-engineering scams**; cases involved disclosure failures
  more than pure liability disputes
  ([HeadTopics](https://be.headtopics.com/news/national-financial-ombud-scheme-returns-r443-million-to-84276381);
  [Moonstone — six banking disputes](https://www.moonstone.co.za/what-six-banking-disputes-reveal-about-how-the-ombud-thinks/)).
- `[OBS]` The NFO warned that consumers who are **dishonest when taking out credit** cannot later claim
  "reckless lending" ([AllAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202512160303.html)).

## 5. Satisfaction / sentiment / NPS-equivalent signals

- `[OBS]` **Discovery Bank** recorded the highest **Net Sentiment of 61%** (+14 pts y/y), 15 pts ahead of
  the second-ranked bank and 34 pts above the industry average; ranked top in both Operational and
  Reputational Sentiment ([Mizizi Yangu](https://sandbox.miziziyangu.com/2025/12/19/discovery-bank-named-south-africas-most-loved-bank-with-record-61-net-sentiment/)).
- `[OBS]` **Sagaci Research (end-2024 to end-2025):** Capitec consolidated a strong satisfaction lead,
  while Standard Bank, FNB and Absa declined; Discovery Bank and Nedbank gained on perceived service /
  digital performance ([Sagaci Research — Top Banks in SA](https://sagaciresearch.com/top-banks-in-south-africa/)).
- `[INF]` No bank publishes a formal **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** in its public reports; the closest
  proxies are third-party sentiment/usability indices above. Treat any quoted bank NPS as `[UNK]`/
  `[UNVERIFIED]` unless sourced to a named survey.

## 6. Notable case themes (from NFO case studies)

- `[OBS]` Case studies spanned a **cancelled vehicle sale, a repossession gone wrong, an investment scam
  and a mortgage-rate disagreement**, illustrating that disputes often turn on **disclosure** rather
  than whether money is owed ([Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/what-six-banking-disputes-reveal-about-how-the-ombud-thinks/)).
- `[OBS]` In a high-profile **Standard Bank** disputed-transactions review, the NFO found **no evidence
  of wrongdoing or system failure**; the transactions used the customer's registered device and valid
  credentials ([African News Agency — Inside Standard Bank's fraud crisis](https://africannewsagency.com/millions-lost-silence-demanded-inside-standard-banks-fraud-crisis/)).
- `[OBS]` In **non-life insurance**, motor and homeowners' cover generated the largest share of disputes,
  and only 19% of closed cases were decided for the consumer
  ([Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/nfo-recovers-r442-99m-for-consumers-as-complaints-climb/)).
