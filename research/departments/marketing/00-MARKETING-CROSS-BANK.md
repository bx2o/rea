## 2. Notes per bank

### ABSA
- `[OBS]` Sponsorship portfolio includes the **Betway SA20 cricket league (2024–25)** and the **MEST
  Africa Challenge (2025)** `[OBS]`
  ([PESTEL Analysis — Absa Marketing Mix](https://pestel-analysis.com/products/absa-marketing-mix)).
- `[OBS]` Higher-profile sports sponsorships include the **Absa Cape Epic** and rugby, delivering
  estimated ~18m TV impressions in 2025 `[EST]`
  ([Canvas Business Model — Absa Marketing Mix](https://canvasbusinessmodel.com/products/absa-marketing-mix)).
- `[OBS]` **Ronwen Williams** (Bafana Bafana captain) unveiled as ambassador in partnership with Visa,
  tied to AFCON Morocco 2025 ([Vantage Daily](https://vantagedaily.wordpress.com/2025/11/11/celebrating-stories-of-progress-with-ronwen-williams-and-absa/)).

### Nedbank
- `[OBS]` Inaugural **brand sponsor of Effie College** launching with the **YouthX Challenge** (UCT, UJ,
  NWU, AAA School) `[OBS]` ([Effie SA](https://effie.org/he/partners/south-africa/latesteffiesanews/)).
- `[OBS]` Sponsored **Nedbank Business Ignite 2025** with 702 and CapeTalk, championing entrepreneurs
  ([Som2ny Network](https://som2nynetwork.com/business/marketing/702-and-capetalk-announce-nedbank-business-ignite-2025/)).
- `[OBS]` Student Loans campaign exploits the "better marks, better interest rate" insight
  ([Best Ads on TV](https://www.bestadsontv.com/news/index.php?reg=16)).

### FNB
- `[OBS]` In **February 2025 FNB announced the expansion of eBucks with Pick n Pay**, effective
  **1 April 2025**, for all FNB customers, **replacing the previous Checkers partnership**; earn rates are
  "aggressive" ([Tuzo — Pick n Pay Smart Shopper](https://tuzo.africa/breakdowns/pick-n-pay-smart-shopper.html)).
- `[OBS]` The partnership offers **up to 30% back in eBucks on PnP asap!** and **up to 20% in store**;
  **Burger Friday** reached **6.2 million burgers redeemed**; the **99c Bread** initiative targets
  entry-level customers ([African Retail](https://www.africanretail.com/fnb-and-pick-n-pay-a-dynamic-partnership-redefining-real-value-for-south-africans/);
  [Daily Investor](https://dailyinvestor.com/retail/131970/6-million-burgers-and-r600-million-in-one-year/)).
- `[OBS]` FNB's stated mandate is to "become an indispensable digital platform", monetising data via SME
  and insurance growth and turning security into a trust pillar `[INF]`
  ([SWOTAnalysis.com — FNB Marketing](https://www.swotanalysis.com/fnb/marketing)).

### Standard Bank
- `[OBS]` Launched its **"Keep Growing"** brand campaign in the SA market (July 2025), affirming the
  purpose "Africa is our home, we drive her growth"; **Africa's largest bank by assets**
  ([Standard Bank Newsroom](https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/news-and-media/newsroom/standard-bank-keep-growing-campaign-a-promise-to-embrace-the-journey-of-growth-with-customers)).
- `[OBS]` Became the **first financial institution in Africa to launch TikTok Search Ads**, working with
  dentsu ([Standard Bank — TikTok Search Ads](https://www.stanbicibtcinsurance.sbgzr.com/southafrica/news-and-media/newsroom/standard-bank-becomes-first-african-bank-to-pioneer-tiktok-search-ads)).
- `[OBS]` Sponsorships focus on community upliftment, the arts and sport
  ([Standard Bank — Sponsorships](https://www.standardbank.com/sbg/standard-bank-group/our-impact/sponsorships)).

### Capitec
- `[OBS]` Launched the **"Bank on better"** brand-positioning campaign with creative agency **Promise**,
  featuring real clients in interview-style commercials
  ([Capitec — new brand campaign](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/blog/news/2025/capitec-launches-new-brand-campaign/)).
- `[OBS]` Ran a nationwide **fees-simplicity campaign** from 14 April 2025, with a jingle performed by
  **employees' children** ([Capitec — new fees 2025](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/blog/news/2025/new-fees-2025/)).
- `[OBS]` **Live Better** rewards reached **~22 million clients** enrolled `[OBS]`
  ([Let's Talk Loyalty](https://letstalkloyalty.com/a-disruptive-mindset-creates-60-market-share-and-92-loyalty-member-penetration-for-retail-banking-in-south-africa-capitec-live-better-rewards-633)).

### Discovery Bank
- `[OBS]` Marketing centres on a **shared-value framework** using gamification and behavioural economics,
  digital-first activation via the Discovery/Vitality apps and data-driven micro-segmentation `[OBS]`
  ([GrowthShareMatrix — Discovery marketing](https://growthsharematrix.com/blogs/marketing-strategy/discovery)).
- `[OBS]` Named **South Africa's "most loved bank" with a record 61% net sentiment** in 2025, up 14 pts
  y/y and 15 pts ahead of the second-ranked bank; top in both Operational and Reputational Sentiment
  ([Mizizi Yangu](https://sandbox.miziziyangu.com/2025/12/19/discovery-bank-named-south-africas-most-loved-bank-with-record-61-net-sentiment/)).

### TymeBank / GoTyme
- `[OBS]` Positions on a **"high tech–high touch"** model blending digital banking with physical kiosks;
  **Rachel Freeman** is Chief Growth Officer
  ([Tech in Africa](https://www.techinafrica.com/tymebank-reaches-profitability-unicorn-valuation-2025/)).
- `[OBS]` Tyme Group reports **22M+ customers** across SA and the Philippines and ~500K new customers
  per month ([Tyme Group](https://www.tyme.com/)).

## 3. Observable brand-performance signals

- `[OBS]` **oXC (everyday-app) scores (2025):** Absa **74**, FNB **60**, Standard Bank **51**, Bank Zero
  **49**, TymeBank **45** ([Altemacx — SA Everyday Apps oCX Report](https://alternacx.com/south-africa-everyday-apps-ocx-report/)).
- `[OBS]` **Sagaci Research (2025):** Capitec consolidates a strong customer-satisfaction lead while
  Standard Bank, FNB and Absa declined across 2024–2025; Discovery Bank and Nedbank gained
  ([Sagaci Research — Top Banks in SA](https://sagaciresearch.com/top-banks-in-south-africa/)).
- `[OBS]` **MMA Smarties SA 2026** finalists/winners include Standard Bank ("Money Reels", "Digital
  Security") and Discovery Insure (#DriveNowTextLater)
  ([MMA Global — Smarties SA 2026](https://mmaglobal.com/smarties-2026/finalists/winners/region:7)).
- `[UNK]` Per-bank **customer acquisition cost (CAC)** and **total marketing spend** are largely
  **not publicly disclosed**; the only CAC data point found relates to TymeBank's channel economics
  (see `../partnerships/00-PARTNERSHIPS-CROSS-BANK.md`).
