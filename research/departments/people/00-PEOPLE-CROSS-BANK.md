## 3. Remuneration disclosures (executive)

- `[OBS]` **FirstRand CEO Mary Vilakazi** received total compensation of **R78.2 million (~US$4.8m)** in
  2025, a **92.85% increase** from R40.5m the prior year
  ([Shore Africa](https://shore.africa/2026/06/04/firstrand-ceo-mary-vilakazi-4-8-million-pay-2025/)).
- `[OBS]` **FNB's Harry Kellan** received **R50.18 million (~US$3.04m)** in 2025
  ([Shore Africa](https://shore.africa/2026/06/11/harry-kellan-bags-3-04m-pay-at-firstrand-in-2025/)).
- `[OBS]` **Absa Group CEO Kenny Fihla** earned **~US$7.6m in his first year** as CEO
  ([Billionaires.Africa](https://www.billionaires.africa/2026/04/30/south-african-banking-giant-absa-groups-kenny-fihla-earns-7-6-million-in-first-year-as-ceo/)).
- `[OBS]` **Standard Bank** staff costs increased **5%** in FY2025, driven by annual salary adjustments,
  higher performance-related incentives and continued investment in specialist skills
  ([SBG 2025 Annual Results booklet, PDF](https://reporting.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/Documents/Financial-results/Annual-Results/SBG-2025-Annual-Results-Booklet-(spreads).pdf)).
- `[OBS]` Capitec salary incentives grew **28%** y/y in the Feb 2025 year
  ([Capitec Feb 2025 results](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/1a436-2025-04-23-06-24-32.pdf)).
- `[UNK]` Detailed per-role **salary bands** across banks are not publicly disclosed; only
  aggregate staff-cost and executive-remuneration data are available.

## 4. Transformation / B-BBEE

- `[OBS]` **Standard Bank retained Level 1 B-BBEE status for the eighth consecutive year**
  ([SBSA Annual Report 2025, PDF](https://www.standardbank.com/static_file/StandardBankGroup/filedownloads/RTS/2025/SBSA_AnnualReport2025.pdf)).
- `[OBS]` **Investec (Limited & subsidiaries)** is a **Level 1** B-BBEE contributor
  ([Investec BEE Report, PDF](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/investor-relations/dti-rating/2025/Investec-Limited-and-Subsidiaries-BEE-Report.pdf)).
- `[OBS]` The **Banking Association South Africa (BASA) 2025 Transformation Report** states banks
  **remain above targets for ownership in all categories** — voting rights 48% above target, women's
  voting rights 70% above target, economic interest 16% above target, women's economic interest 30%
  above target, and economic interest of designated black groups 100% above target
  ([BASA Transformation Report 2025, PDF](https://banking.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/basa-2025-transformation-report-final.pdf)).
- `[OBS]` **Absa's** current ownership score aggregates the "once empowered always empowered" principle,
  indirect ownership and an equity-equivalent arising from surplus; its 2004 Batho Bonke 10% black stake
  was fully unwound by 2012 ([Absa — corporate information](https://www.absa.co.za/about-us/absa-bank/corporate-information/)).
