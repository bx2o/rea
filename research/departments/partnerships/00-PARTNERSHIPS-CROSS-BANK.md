# 00 — Partnerships, Sponsorships & Affinity Programmes: Cross-Bank (SA)

> Public information only. Tags: `[OBS]`, `[DER]`, `[INF]`, `[EST]`, `[UNK]`.

## 1. Retail / kiosk distribution networks

- `[OBS]` **TymeBank** built its distribution on **~1,000 kiosks located in Pick n Pay, Boxer and TFG
  stores** rather than traditional branches, slashing operational cost and improving accessibility
  ([Tech in Africa](https://www.techinafrica.com/tymebank-reaches-profitability-unicorn-valuation-2025/)).
- `[OBS]` In **January 2023 TymeBank partnered with The Foschini Group (TFG)**, deploying **600
  additional kiosks** across TFG outlets with co-branded debit cards; this attracted **1 million customers
  in its first year** ([Grokipedia — TymeBank](https://grokipedia.com/page/TymeBank)).
- `[EST]` `[OBS]` The retail channel **lowered customer-acquisition cost to ~R120 per account in FY2025
  vs ~ZAR 850 for traditional branches**; by March 2026 the network added 120 SME service points
  ([Canvas Business Model — TymeBank](https://canvasbusinessmodel.com/products/tymebank-business-model-canvas)).
  (Vendor estimate — tag `[EST]`.)
- `[OBS]` By 2025 TymeBank's terminal footprint reached **~1,450 terminals** across Pick n Pay/Boxer as
  part of the **Home Affairs** service rollout ([Sohu — SA Home Affairs & eight banks](https://m.sohu.com/a/952935794_121124422/)).

## 2. Retail loyalty / rewards partnerships

### FNB eBucks
- `[OBS]` FNB's **eBucks** rewards partners include **Pick n Pay** (from 1 April 2025), **Clicks** and
  **Engen**; the Pick n Pay tie-up replaced the prior **Checkers** partnership
  ([Tuzo — Pick n Pay Smart Shopper](https://tuzo.africa/breakdowns/pick-n-pay-smart-shopper.html);
  [Financial Mail — The cash back clash](https://financial-mail-prod-383389740755.newsteam.io/2026-03-24-the-cash-back-clash-which-banks-really-pay/)).
- `[OBS]` On an FNB Aspire account customers can earn **up to R1.20/litre back at Engen**, with richer
  account types advertising higher fuel rewards
  ([Financial Mail](https://financial-mail-prod-383389740755.newsteam.io/2026-03-24-the-cash-back-clash-which-banks-really-pay/)).

### Nedbank Greenbacks
- `[OBS]` **Greenbacks** partners include **BP** (25c/litre fuel cashback), **Nu Metro Cinemas**,
  **Avo SuperShop**, **Greenbacks Travel**, **Apple** and **Samsung**
  ([Digital Banker Africa — Nedbank](https://digitalbankerafrica.com/nedbank-and-the-digital-revolution-in-africa/);
  [Nedbank Greenbacks at bp](https://www.nedbank.co.za/content/nedbank/desktop/gt/en/aboutus/greenbacks-bp/greenbacks-bp.html)).
- `[OBS]` Greenbacks perks include up to 30% off flights, 50% off Nu Metro movie tickets, and up to 10%
  off Apple/Samsung devices ([Nedbank Greenbacks brochure, PDF](https://business.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/nedbank/pdfs/greenbacks-rewards-programme-brochure.pdf)).

### Discovery Vitality
- `[OBS]` **Vitality** bundles health, lifestyle and leisure benefits and links to **Vitality Money** and
  **Vitality Drive**; travel discounts range from **10% to 75%** off local flights
  ([Discovery Vitality 2026 brochure, PDF](https://www.discovery.co.za/wcm/discoverycoza/assets/faz/marketing-support/vitality/vitality-sales-brochure-2026.pdf);
  [Discovery — Vitality travel benefits](https://www.discovery.co.za/bank/info-and-tips-travel-benefits-and-rewards)).
- `[OBS]` Vitality-related partnerships cited with **Checkers and Woolworths** in the Discovery Bank
  rewards ecosystem ([Mizizi Yangu](https://sandbox.miziziyangu.com/2025/12/19/discovery-bank-named-south-africas-most-loved-bank-with-record-61-net-sentiment/)).
- `[OBS]` Internationally, Vitality licenses its platform to **Ping An and AIA** (asset-light growth)
  ([MatrixBCG — Discovery competitive landscape](https://matrixbcg.com/blogs/competitors/discovery)).

### Other
- `[OBS]` **Pick n Pay Smart Shopper** has **10 million members**; the FNB partnership is described as the
  biggest structural change to the programme in years
  ([Tuzo — Pick n Pay Smart Shopper](https://tuzo.africa/breakdowns/pick-n-pay-smart-shopper.html)).
- `[OBS]` **Standard Bank** UCount and **Absa Rewards** exist, but specific partner lists were not
  confirmed in this pass `[UNK]`.

## 3. Sports & event sponsorships (affinity)

- `[OBS]` **Absa:** Betway SA20 cricket (2024–25), MEST Africa Challenge (2025), Absa Cape Epic, plus the
  Ronwen Williams ambassadorship (with Visa)
  ([PESTEL Analysis — Absa](https://pestel-analysis.com/products/absa-marketing-mix);
  [Vantage Daily](https://vantagedaily.wordpress.com/2025/11/11/celebrating-stories-of-progress-with-ronwen-williams-and-absa/)).
- `[OBS]` **Standard Bank:** focuses sponsorships on community upliftment, the arts and sport
  ([Standard Bank — Sponsorships](https://www.standardbank.com/sbg/standard-bank-group/our-impact/sponsorships)).
- `[OBS]` **Nedbank:** Effie College / YouthX Challenge brand sponsorship (2025)
  ([Effie SA](https://effie.org/he/partners/south-africa/latesteffiesanews/)).

## 4. Public-sector & merchant partnerships

- `[OBS]` In **April 2025** South Africa's **Department of Home Affairs** partnered with **eight banks**
  to deliver smart ID and passport services at **1,000+ bank branches**, with **TymeBank** signing last,
  via **1,450 terminals** in Pick n Pay/Boxer stores, tested in Johannesburg first
  ([Sohu](https://m.sohu.com/a/952935794_121124422/)).
- `[OBS]` TymeBank deploys **merchant POS devices** as a distribution channel and cites **Pick n Pay,
  Boxer Superstores, TFG, Zion Christian Church (ZCC)** and the **Gokongwei Group (Philippines)** among
  key partners ([SWOTAnalysis.com — Tyme](https://www.swotanalysis.com/tyme)).

## 5. API / open-banking partnerships

- `[OBS]` Nedbank operates an **API Marketplace** supporting open-banking initiatives (authentication,
  account transactions, payments, rewards, wallet services), alongside its Greenbacks loyalty partners
  ([Digital Banker Africa](https://digitalbankerafrica.com/nedbank-and-the-digital-revolution-in-africa/)).
