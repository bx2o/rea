# 00 — Fraud & AML: Cross-Bank Profile (SA)

> Public information only. Tags: `[OBS]`, `[DER]`, `[INF]`, `[EST]`, `[UNK]`.
> **Note:** SABRIC reports by calendar period; different secondary sources cite different reporting
> windows, so figures are kept separate and tagged.

## 1. SABRIC 2025 Annual Banking Crime Statistics Report — headline figures

`[OBS]` Digital banking crime in 2025:

| Metric | 2025 | vs 2024 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reported client claims (digital banking crime) | **R2.41 billion** | **+29.2%** (from ~R1.86bn) | [ITWeb Africa](https://itweb.africa/article/how-to-tell-emergencies-and-scams-apart-in-the-age-of-ai/RgeVDvPR3XoMKJN3) |
| Reported incidents | **110,074** | **+12.8%** | [EDORM / Arcadia](https://www.arcadiafinance.co.za/news/banking-app-fraud-targets-south-africans/) |
| Average loss per incident | **R21,865** | — | [Arcadia Finance](https://www.arcadiafinance.co.za/news/banking-app-fraud-targets-south-africans/) |
| Share via banking apps | **~89% of cases; 70.5% of claim value** | — | [Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/how-scammers-turn-your-trust-against-you/) |

- `[OBS]` **Social engineering** (criminals persuade victims to approve payments or hand over access) is
  the driver of most losses ([ITWeb Africa](https://itweb.africa/article/how-to-tell-emergencies-and-scams-apart-in-the-age-of-ai/RgeVDvPR3XoMKJN3)).
- `[OBS]` **Home and mortgage loan fraudulent applications** accounted for **R595.9 million** in reported
  losses in 2025 — roughly a **quarter of the R2.4bn** lost to digital banking crime
  ([Knysna-Plett Herald](https://www.knysnaplettherald.com/News/Article/Property/a-property-deposit-is-one-of-the-riskiest-online-payments-south-africans-make-202610130817)).
- `[OBS]` South Africa was ranked **first among the African countries analysed** for suspected digital
  fraud in 2025 ([Knysna-Plett Herald, citing TransUnion](https://www.knysnaplettherald.com/News/Article/Property/a-property-deposit-is-one-of-the-riskiest-online-payments-south-africans-make-202610130817)).
- `[OBS]` Forbes Africa, citing SABRIC, reports digital banking as the biggest threat with losses rising
  to **R2.4 billion (~US$148m) in 2025** from ~R1.9 billion (~US$117m) in 2024
  ([Forbes Africa](https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/09/02/south-africa-records-high-number-of-occupational-fraud-cases-in-sub-saharan-africa)).

## 2. Reporting-window variant (Aug–Sep 2025)

- `[OBS]` A financial-services trend report cites SABRIC (Aug–Sep 2025) showing digital-banking fraud
  **up 86% to ~98k cases with losses up 74% to R1.89bn**, driven by **AI-phishing, deepfakes, synthetic
  IDs** and heavy app targeting (~65%)
  ([Zensar — SA Financial Services Industry Trends, PDF](https://www.zensar.com/assets/files/3pnveaYNIaI4DTTRjUBQnW/south-africas-financial-services-industry-trends.pdf)).
  `[INF]` This is a **different reporting window** from the calendar-2025 figures above and is therefore
  not contradictory; it is tagged separately rather than merged.

## 3. Major incidents and threat vectors

- `[OBS]` Commercial banking is among the **top three industries attacked** in South Africa, with
  **6,800+ DDoS attacks recorded in six months** ([Zensar, PDF](https://www.zensar.com/assets/files/3pnveaYNIaI4DTTRjUBQnW/south-africas-financial-services-industry-trends.pdf)).
- `[OBS]` Escalating **WhatsApp impersonation/account-hijacking scams** drove the rise in reported losses
  ([African Insider](https://www.africaninsider.com/business/south-africans-warned-as-whatsapp-scams-become-more-sophisticated/)).
- `[OBS]` High-profile disputed-transaction cases (e.g. the **Standard Bank** matter) have been reviewed
  by the National Financial Ombud, which in one case found **no bank wrongdoing** where the customer's
  registered device and valid credentials were used
  ([African News Agency — Inside Standard Bank's fraud crisis](https://africannewsagency.com/millions-lost-silence-demanded-inside-standard-banks-fraud-crisis/)).
- `[OBS]` South Africa records a **high number of occupational fraud cases in Sub-Saharan Africa**
  ([Forbes Africa](https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/09/02/south-africa-records-high-number-of-occupational-fraud-cases-in-sub-saharan-africa)).

## 4. AML context

- `[OBS]` AML/CFT obligations flow from **FICA** — accountable institutions register with the FIC,
  perform CDD/KYC, file STRs/UTRs and retain records for five years (see
  `../../cross-cutting/regulation/20-FICA-POPIA-NCA-FSCA.md`)
  ([FIC — FAQ](https://www.fic.gov.za/faq/)).
- `[OBS]` **SABRIC** is the South African Banking Risk Information Centre, the industry body that
  publishes the Annual Banking Crime Statistics Report and coordinates bank-to-bank crime intelligence
  ([ITWeb Africa](https://itweb.africa/article/how-to-tell-emergencies-and-scams-apart-in-the-age-of-ai/RgeVDvPR3XoMKJN3)).
- `[INF]` `[EST]` **Deepfakes, synthetic identity and AI-phishing** are the named emerging threat vectors,
  implying that biometric/KYC liveness and behavioural-fraud analytics are key defensive investments for
  a new entrant ([Zensar, PDF](https://www.zensar.com/assets/files/3pnveaYNIaI4DTTRjUBQnW/south-africas-financial-services-industry-trends.pdf)).

## 5. Security-posture observations

- `[OBS]` SABRIC attributes most 2025 losses to **social engineering**, i.e. authorised-push-payment
  style fraud rather than system breaches — the report calls for **stronger authentication**
  ([EDORM](https://edorm.unaux.com/2026/10/12/scammernomics-hitting-scammers-where-it-hurts-the-most/?i=1)).
- `[OBS]` The NFO's banking-fraud awards (~60% of R53m) confirm that **refund/liability allocation**
  remains a live consumer-protection battleground
  ([HeadTopics](https://be.headtopics.com/news/national-financial-ombud-scheme-returns-r443-million-to-84276381)).
- `[UNK]` Bank-specific breach counts and per-bank fraud-loss disclosures are **not publicly available**
  at this granularity.
