# FanDuel / DraftKings — 08 Stremiium Implications (ADOPT / ADAPT / AVOID / BEAT)

Applied to the Stremiium **DFS / real-money sports** line (Brief §7 filter).

## ADOPT

| # | Finding | Stremiium action |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Geo-gating before action** (GeoComply-style) | Adopt: province-aware geolocation gate before any wagering/real-money action in SA. |
| A2 | **Tiered loyalty program** (status + perks) | Adopt the tier/perk structure, funded by margin, surfaced via `$STM`/FanCash. |
| A3 | **Responsible-gaming controls** (limits, self-exclusion, reality checks) | Adopt as table stakes — non-negotiable for licensing. |
| A4 | **Anti-collusion monitoring** | Adopt for DFS integrity (shared-account/lineup analysis). |
| A5 | **Real-time data feeds** | Adopt: source official sports data from established providers. |

## ADAPT

| # | Finding | Stremiium change |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | US state-by-state licensing | Adapt to **provincial** licensing (9 SA provinces) + NGB-aligned posture; online gambling licences pending under the Amendment Bill. |
| D2 | Volume-weighted reward tiers | Adapt: weigh **engagement/learning** and cap wagering-linked rewards to stay responsible-gaming-safe. |
| D3 | Promo-credit currency | Adapt to `$STM`/FanCash with clear, POPIA-compliant, non-misleading terms. |
| D4 | Card/ACH payment rails | Adapt to **PayShap/RTC** rails and reuse NeoBank FICA KYC across verticals. |

## AVOID

| # | Finding | Why Stremiium refuses it |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | Unlicensed or grey-market real-money operation | Licensing is existential (gambling enforcement). |
| V2 | Gamification that ignores harm | Volume-maximising promos conflict with responsible-gaming duties and reputation. |
| V3 | Opaque promo terms | Consumer-protection trap (CPA). |
| V4 | Vendor lock-in on odds/KYC without exit | Concentrates operational risk. |

## BEAT

| # | Competitor weakness | How Stremiium beats it |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Rewards tied to money wagered | Reward *engagement and skill*; a fairer, safer loop. |
| B2 | Separate sportsbook app / separate KYC | One NeoBank identity + one KYC across banking, collectibles, DFS. |
| B3 | Cross-border data handling | POPIA-native, `af-south-1` data residency. |
| B4 | No crypto/collectible integration | `$STM` + collectibles tie sports engagement to the wider programme. |

## Net strategic verdict

FanDuel is the reference for **regulated, real-money engagement at scale** — the
operational and compliance baseline. Stremiium should adopt its geofencing, responsible-
gaming, tiered loyalty and anti-collusion mechanics; adapt licensing to SA provinces; avoid
unlicensed or harm-indifferent design; and beat it by unifying KYC, identity and rewards
inside a POPIA-native NeoBank.
