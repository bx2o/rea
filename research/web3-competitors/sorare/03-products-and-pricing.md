## 2. Scarcity tiers (the core pricing axis)

Cards are minted in **scarcity tiers**; the tier sets both the mint count and the
competitive value. Commonly referenced tier ladder (names as publicly used):

| Tier | Relative scarcity | Relative price | Class |
|---|---|---|---|
| Limited | Most supply | Lowest | SEEN-as-reported |
| Rare | Mid | Mid | SEEN-as-reported |
| Super Rare | Scarce | Higher | SEEN-as-reported |
| Unique | 1-of-1 (per player/season) | Highest | SEEN-as-reported |

> Exact mint caps per tier are **not asserted here** (UNKNOWN without in-app/Docs capture).
