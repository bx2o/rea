# Sorare — 08 Stremiium Implications (ADOPT / ADAPT / AVOID / BEAT)

Applies the Brief §7 strategic filter to the collectibles/fantasy line of the Stremiium
NeoBank. Each finding is classified and given the Stremiium action.

## ADOPT

| # | Finding | Stremiium action |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Skill-first reward loop** (earn before you can spend) | Reproduce in the collectibles layer: free-to-play skill contests seed the roster; paid depth comes later. |
| A2 | **Scarcity tiers** as an engineered pricing axis | Adopt an explicit, published tier ladder (mirroring Rare/Super Rare/Unique) for `$STM`-earned collectibles with transparent mint caps. |
| A3 | **Gasless UX via abstraction** | Adopt the *outcome* (users never pay gas / touch seeds) via the MPC Wallet-as-a-Service + sponsored transactions in Brief §8. |
| A4 | **Dual take-rate** (primary margin + secondary fee) | Adopt both revenue lines; the secondary fee is the annuity that funds the rewards engine. |

## ADAPT

| # | Finding | Stremiium change |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | StarkEx Validium scaling | Adapt to the Brief's mandate: **Polygon PoS / ZK-EVM** with The Graph indexing — same user benefit, different chain, our own custody posture. |
| D2 | 3-currency surface (fiat/ETH/cards) | Adapt to a **single loyalty currency (`$STM`) + FanCash-style credit** to unlock cross-sell across banking, collectibles and betting — something Sorare structurally cannot do. |
| D3 | Regional geoblock patchwork | Adapt to **licence-aware-from-day-one** market gating rather than retrofitting. |

## AVOID

| # | Finding | Why Stremiium refuses it |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | Gaming-classification ambiguity on paid prize contests | Do not launch paid-entry prize formats until provincial-board treatment is pre-cleared; ambiguity is a licence-killer. |
| V2 | Opacity of mint caps / prize tables | Never ship under-disclosed mechanics; it invites consumer-trust and regulatory attack. |
| V3 | Heavy dependency on single licensors | Avoid a spine that dies when one league licence lapses; diversify to SA-relevant rights (e.g., local leagues, cricket, rugby) and non-IP collectibles. |
| V4 | Pro-cyclical NFT revenue model | Do not let the balance sheet depend on card-market froth. |

## BEAT

| # | Competitor weakness | How Stremiium beats it |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | No cross-vertical loyalty currency | `$STM` + FanCash-style credit lets collectible earnings pay down banking fees — a loop Sorare cannot offer. |
| B2 | POPIA/data-residency gap (EU/US platforms) | South-African-first data-residency + POPIA-native design is a trust differentiator. |
| B3 | Skill-opacity | Publish scoring rules and prize tables → trust as a moat. |
| B4 | No fiat-account integration | Collectibles sit inside a real NeoBank account (spend, save, custody) — Sorare users must bolt on a bank elsewhere. |

## Net strategic verdict

Sorare is the **mechanics** blueprint (skill loop, scarcity, dual take-rate, gasless UX)
but the **wrong ownership model** for Stremiium. Adopt its loop, adapt its currency and
chain, avoid its opacity and licensing fragility, and beat it on cross-vertical loyalty
inside a regulated NeoBank.
