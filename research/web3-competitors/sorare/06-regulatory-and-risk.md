## 4. Gaps Stremiium can exploit

1. **Skill-vs-gambling clarity:** Stremiium can pre-clear a *free-to-play skill* collectible mode with provincial boards so paid modes don't taint the brand.
2. **POPIA-native design:** a South-African-first privacy posture (data residency in `af-south-1`) is a differentiator vs EU/US platforms.
3. **Transparent scarcity:** publish mint caps and prize tables up front — turns Sorare's opacity into Stremiium's trust advantage.
4. **No global geoblock patchwork:** build region-aware licensing from day one instead of retrofitting.

## 5. Red lines

* Never assert a licence number, penalty, or regulatory approval for Sorare that we have not read in an official register.
* Never imply NFTs are risk-free bankable assets in Stremiium marketing.
