## 2. Licensing (commercial IP, not financial)

Sorare's value rests on **sports IP licences**, not a financial licence. Publicly
referenced licence relationships include national leagues, clubs, and player
associations. Representative (non-exhaustive) relationships widely reported:

* **Football/soccer:** LaLiga, Bundesliga, MLS, and (from 2023) the English Premier League; numerous clubs and player unions.
* **Basketball:** NBA and NBPA.
* **Baseball:** MLB and MLBPA.

Class: **SEEN** for the existence of league partnerships (repeated in official comms and
