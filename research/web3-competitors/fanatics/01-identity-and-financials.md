## 6. Flags for C04 / C10

* Fanatics is the **cross-vertical commerce** benchmark: the value is in the **fan identity
  + loyalty currency**, not any single product.
* Physical-goods margin + licensing cost is a structurally different (lower-margin,
  capital-heavy) model than a token platform — an important contrast for Stremiium.
* Private-company opacity means benchmarking must rely on reported figures, flagged as such.
