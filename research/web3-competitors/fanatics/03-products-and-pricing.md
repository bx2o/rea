## 3. The cross-vertical value loop

```
Buy merch ──► earn FanCash ──► spend on collectibles ──► earn FanCash ──► bet (where licensed)
      ▲                                                                        │
      └──────────────────── FanCash / identity makes the loop sticky ◄─────────┘
```

* **Value driver:** one currency + one identity raises cross-category attach and LTV.
* Class: **INFERRED** (from the existence of a shared currency + unified ID).

## 5. Economics Stremiium cares about

| Lever | Fanatics behaviour | Stremiium read |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-sell | One currency across categories | Adopt via `$STM`/FanCash |
| Margin | Physical goods = lower margin, capital-heavy | Stremiium should keep digital-first |
| Licensing cost | League royalties | Major cost line for collectibles |
| CAC | Identity reuse lowers repeat CAC | Key lever |

## 6. Warnings

* Do not equate "FanCash" with a tradable token — it is a **closed loyalty currency** (a key distinction from `$STM`).
* Physical-collectibles economics (inventory, fulfilment, returns) are outside a digital NeoBank's natural scope; treat as a partner/adapt play, not a build.
* No precise revenue/earn-rate figures may be quoted without a named source.
