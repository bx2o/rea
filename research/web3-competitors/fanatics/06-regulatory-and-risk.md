| Regulator / regime | Relationship | Class |

## 2. Structural risk register

| Risk | Description | Severity |

## 3. South Africa comparison

| SA regime | Relevance to a Fanatics-like product | Class |
|---|---|---|
| **FSCA** (FAIS; crypto assets = financial products 2022; CASP licensing) | If loyalty currency becomes a tradable token, CASP/FSP licensing bites; a closed FanCash-style currency generally does not. Stremiium's `$STM` must stay utility-framed. | SEEN (regime) / INFERRED |
| **NGB / provincial gambling boards** (National Gambling Act 7 of 2004) | The betting pillar requires provincial licensing; online gambling licensing is evolving under the Amendment Bill. | SEEN (statute) / INFERRED |
| **FICA** (Act 38 of 2001; Directive 9 Travel Rule) | Betting-side KYC + crypto flows (Travel Rule). | SEEN |
| **POPIA** (Act 4 of 2013) | Cross-vertical customer profiling (commerce + betting) = personal information; lawful basis, purpose limitation, security. | SEEN |
| **Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008** | Loyalty-currency terms (expiry, redemption limits) and merch marketing must be fair/transparent. | SEEN (regime) |
| **SARB / FinSurv / Exchange Control** | Cross-border merch/royalty flows + crypto settlement. | SEEN (regime) |
| **B-BBEE** | Local licensing/enterprise considerations for retail/partner networks. | SEEN (regime context) |

## 4. Gaps Stremiium can exploit

1. **One identity, POPIA-governed:** unify commerce/collectibles/betting under explicit POPIA
   purpose limitation — Fanatics' cross-vertical data model invites scrutiny; Stremiium can
   design it legally from day one.
2. **Digital-first collectibles:** avoid physical inventory/fulfilment drag while keeping the
   collectible *experience* via phygital + token.
3. **Utility-token clarity:** keep `$STM` non-tradable-speculation framing (fee discount +
   rewards) to sit outside the securities/CASP trap that a tradable FanCash-like currency
   would risk.
4. **Licensing strategy:** global IP is expensive; SA-relevant rights (local leagues) are
   cheaper and defensible.

## 5. Red lines

* Never assert a licence number, penalty, or revenue without a register/filing read.
* Never combine commerce and betting data without a POPIA lawful basis and purpose
  limitation.
* Never let loyalty-currency terms mislead (CPA).
