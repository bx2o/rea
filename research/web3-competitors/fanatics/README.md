## Executive summary (one paragraph)

Fanatics is a US sports-commerce giant (licensed merchandise, jerseys, memorabilia) that has
expanded aggressively into **trading cards (Topps)**, **collectibles**, **live-commerce**,
**sportsbook**, and **live-event merchandise**. Its strategic move is not a token — it is a
**unified customer identity** spanning e-commerce, collectibles and betting, glued together
by a cross-vertical loyalty currency, **"FanCash"**. For Stremiium, Fanatics is the closest
analogue to the *phygital collectibles + cross-vertical loyalty* ambition in the Program
Brief: it demonstrates how one identity and one currency can monetise fans across
categories — while its reliance on physical goods and league IP, and its licence-heavy
expansion, show the cost of that strategy. All app-internal specifics carry the REA caveat:
nothing was decompiled here.
