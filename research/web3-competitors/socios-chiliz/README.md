## Executive summary (one paragraph)

Chiliz is a Malta-rooted blockchain company that builds **fan tokens** — ERC-20 tokens
issued to sports clubs (FC Barcelona `$BAR`, Paris Saint-Germain `$PSG`, Manchester City
`$CITY`, UFC `$UFC`, and others) — and distributes them through its consumer app
**Socios.com**. Fans acquire tokens via **Fan Token Offerings (FTO)** priced in `$CHZ`,
the platform's native token, then use them for club polls, rewards ("locker room"
experiences) and trading on exchanges. Infrastructure has moved to **Chiliz Chain**, an
EVM-compatible L1, from earlier Ethereum-based deployment. The model is the closest global
analogue to Stremiium's planned **fan/BLT loyalty token** line, and its MiCA-exposed
European footing is a live regulatory case study. All chain-level and contract specifics
carry the REA caveat: nothing was decompiled here.
