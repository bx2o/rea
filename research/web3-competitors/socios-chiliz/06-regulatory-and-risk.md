# Socios.com / Chiliz — 06 Regulatory & Risk

## 1. Home-market posture (EU / Malta)

| Regulator / regime | Relationship | Class |
|---|---|---|
| **MiCA** (EU Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation) | `$CHZ` and fan tokens fall in scope as crypto-assets; issuer/CASP obligations apply where classified as non-ART/EMT utility tokens. Malta is an EU member → MiCA directly relevant. | SEEN (regime) / INFERRED (specific classification) |
| **Malta MFSA** (VFA/other regimes) | Historically a permissive DLT jurisdiction; Chiliz's Maltese footing interacts with MFSA frameworks | SEEN-as-reported |
| **Consumer/utility-token character** | Fan tokens sold as utility/engagement, not securities — a classification Chiliz relies on | INFERRED |
| **MiFID II** | Risk if a fan token were treated as a financial instrument | INFERRED |
| **AML/CFT** | Exchange/custody flows require VASP AML compliance | SEEN (regime) |

> No licence number is asserted. Any downstream citation of a Maltese licence or MiCA
> authorisation must be read from the MFSA/ESMA register first.

## 3. South Africa comparison

| SA regime | Relevance to a Socios/Chiliz-like product | Class |
|---|---|---|
| **FSCA** (FAIS; crypto assets = financial products since 2022; CASP licensing) | A fan/BLT token that is *traded* and marketed as an investment likely triggers **CASP/FSP** licensing; a pure rewards token may not. Stremiium's `$STM` positioning must be drafted to stay on the utility side. | SEEN (regime) / INFERRED (application) |
| **NGB / provincial boards** (National Gambling Act 7 of 2004) | Token-gated prize draws / prediction elements can touch gambling law; keep rewards non-wagering. | SEEN (statute) / INFERRED |
| **FICA** (Act 38 of 2001; Directive 9 Travel Rule) | Token purchases/redemptions and cross-border crypto flows trigger KYC + Travel Rule. | SEEN |
| **POPIA** (Act 4 of 2013; Information Regulator) | Fan profiling + token activity = personal information; lawful basis, consent, security required. | SEEN |
| **SARB / FinSurv / Exchange Control** | `$CHZ`/`$STM` in/out flows and any cross-border royalty require FinSurv attention. | SEEN (regime) |
| **Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008** | Utility-token marketing must not mislead about value/refunds. | SEEN (regime) |

## 4. Gaps Stremiium can exploit

1. **Utility-token clarity:** define `$STM` strictly as a fee-discount/rewards token (Brief §8: 25% fee discount, 20%-of-fees buy-back/burn) — sidesteps the securities/MiCA classification Chiliz wrestles with.
2. **Single-token simplicity:** avoid the two-token flywheel's liquidity drag and regulatory surface.
3. **POPIA-native + FSCA-aligned from day one:** a licensed CASP posture beats an offshore fan-token issuer on trust.
4. **No speculative promises:** Stremiium marketing must never imply investment returns — a direct weakness of the fan-token model.

## 5. Red lines

* Never assert a Chiliz/Socios licence, MiCA authorisation, or penalty without a register read.
* Never let Stremiium `$STM` collateral imply a security or a promised return.
