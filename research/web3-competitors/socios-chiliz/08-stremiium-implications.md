# Socios.com / Chiliz — 08 Stremiium Implications (ADOPT / ADAPT / AVOID / BEAT)

Applied to the Stremiium **fan-token / BLT loyalty** line (Brief §7 filter).

## ADOPT

| # | Finding | Stremiium action |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Club/community token as identity** | Adopt the identity hook: a Stremiium BLT/fan token that fans *belong to*, not just hold. |
| A2 | **Token-gated experiences** | Adopt: gate real experiences/perks behind holding + behaviour, not pure balance. |
| A3 | **EVM-compatible chain toolchain** | Adopt: club/fan contracts port cleanly to the Brief's Polygon PoS / ZK-EVM target. |
| A4 | **Voting/engagement layer** | Adopt a governance/engagement surface (polls, community votes) funded by the rewards engine. |

## ADAPT

| # | Finding | Stremiium change |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Two-token flywheel (`$CHZ` + club tokens) | Adapt to **one `$STM`** + optional BLT tier tokens — removes liquidity fragmentation and correlation risk. |
| D2 | Rewarding **holding** a speculative asset | Adapt to rewarding **behaviour**: "action ➔ currency ➔ tier ➔ redemption" driven by banking/engagement activity, not by how much you park. |
| D3 | Issuance-for-cash (FTO) model | Adapt **away** from selling tokens for cash; mint `$STM` via rewards and buy-back/burn from fee flows (Brief §8). |
| D4 | EU/MiCA footing | Adapt to **FSCA/CASP-aligned SA posture** with explicit utility-token framing. |

## AVOID

| # | Finding | Why Stremiium refuses it |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | Selling tokens to retail as a funding mechanism | Securities/MiCA/consumer-trust trap; conflicts with CASP + POPIA posture. |
| V2 | Pro-cyclical token revenue dependence | Never let the P&L hinge on token price. |
| V3 | Long-tail illiquid club tokens | Avoid token death-spirals; keep utility and liquidity unified under `$STM`. |
| V4 | Implying investment returns | Fan-token marketing that hints at profit is the exact trap Stremiium must avoid. |

## BEAT

| # | Competitor weakness | How Stremiium beats it |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Token model = speculation, no banking utility | `$STM` pays real NeoBank fees (25% discount) and sits inside an FSCA-facing CASP — utility > speculation. |
| B2 | Fragmented multi-token ecosystem | One token + a clean rewards engine = simpler UX and cross-sell. |
| B3 | MiCA/Malta classification overhang | SA-first CASP + utility-token clarity is a cleaner regulatory story. |
| B4 | Rewards tied to holding, not doing | Stremiium rewards *actions* (spend, save, engage) → deeper, more defensible retention. |

## Net strategic verdict

Chiliz is the **closest structural analogue** to Stremiium's fan/BLT token ambition, so it
is the most important competitor to study — but Stremiium should adopt its *engagement and
identity* mechanics, radically adapt its *token model* (single utility token, behaviour-
funded), avoid its *issuance/speculation* framing, and beat it by embedding the token
inside a regulated NeoBank with real fee utility.
