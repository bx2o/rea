## 2. Licensing

Chiliz/Socios is **not a bank** and, in Europe, fan tokens have generally been treated as
**utility/consumer tokens** rather than financial instruments — but this is exactly what
**MiCA** now pressures. There is no single "licence number" to cite; the relevant posture is
(a) MiCA classification of `$CHZ` and fan tokens, and (b) any exchange/VASP registrations
for the exchange side. Both: **INFERRED/UNKNOWN** without regulator-register inspection.

## 6. Flags for C01 / C10

* No audited financials → peer-benchmarking must rely on token-ecosystem data, not P&L.
* **Token-model risk**: an FTO-funded model has no deposit base and no NIM; its "revenue"
  is issuance + trading, which is cyclical and regulatory-sensitive.
* Stremiium's fan-token line must not copy the **issuance-for-cash** framing; the Brief
  positions `$STM` as a *utility/rewards* token with programmatic buy-back/burn — a
  materially different, safer story.
