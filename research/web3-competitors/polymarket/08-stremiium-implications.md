# Polymarket — 08 Stremiium Implications (ADOPT / ADAPT / AVOID / BEAT)

Applied to the Stremiium **prediction-market** line (Brief §7 filter).

## ADOPT

| # | Finding | Stremiium action |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Hybrid CLOB** (off-chain match, on-chain settle) | Adopt: cheap, fast trading with self-custody settlement — portable to the Polygon target. |
| A2 | **Gnosis CTF outcome tokens** | Adopt the ERC-1155 conditional-token pattern for outcome shares. |
| A3 | **Negative-risk conversion** for multi-outcome markets | Adopt for capital efficiency in "who wins" markets. |
| A4 | **Event-driven virality** | Adopt the event-calendar acquisition mechanic (sporting + local events). |

## ADAPT

| # | Finding | Stremiium change |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | USDC collateral | Adapt to **ZAR-first + `$STM`/stablecoin-agnostic** collateral to dodge depeg & exchange-control friction. |
| D2 | UMA oracle dependency | Adapt: evaluate **UMA vs in-house resolution + market-maker panel**; make resolution transparent and disclosed. |
| D3 | Zero-fee trading | Adapt to an explicit monetisation (spread, subscription, or rewards-tied fee discount) — zero-fee is subsidy, not a business. |
| D4 | PnL-only reward loop | Adapt: wrap participation in the `$STM`/FanCash behavioural engine so retention isn't purely PnL-driven. |

## AVOID

| # | Finding | Why Stremiium refuses it |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | Operating outside a licensed perimeter (the CFTC failure) | **Never** launch prediction markets unlicensed; the enforcement is existential. |
| V2 | Relying on geoblocks as a compliance strategy | Users route around them; it is not a defence. |
| V3 | Marketing implied probabilities as predictions/advice | Regulatory + consumer-trust trap. |
| V4 | Stablecoin-collateral concentration | Single-asset peg risk. |

## BEAT

| # | Competitor weakness | How Stremiium beats it |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | No loyalty/tier currency | `$STM`/FanCash overlay gives retention Polymarket lacks. |
| B2 | Regulatory overhang / geoblock history | Licensed-by-design in SA beats offshore-then-block. |
| B3 | Zero-fee = opaque economics | Transparent, sustainable monetisation builds trust. |
| B4 | Event-concentration churn | Cross-sell into banking/collectibles stabilises engagement between events. |

## Net strategic verdict

Polymarket is the **mechanics** gold standard (hybrid CLOB, CTF, oracle resolution) and the
**compliance** cautionary tale (CFTC). Stremiium should adopt its architecture faithfully,
adapt its collateral/monetisation/rewards, avoid any unlicensed or geoblock-dependent
launch, and beat it by folding prediction participation into a licensed NeoBank's rewards
engine.
