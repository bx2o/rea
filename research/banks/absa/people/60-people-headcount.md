## 2. Headcount
- **Total employees: 37,033** (FY2025). [OBS] ([Results booklet FY25](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Results-booklet-for-the-period-ended-31-December-2025-double-page.pdf))
  - South Africa: **26,565**; Africa Regions: **10,258**; international: 210. [OBS] (same)
- Reported mid-region figures: 37,272 (H1 2025). [OBS] ([Absa interim FY25](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Results-booklet-for-the-period-ended-30-June-2025-single-page.pdf))
- Sustainability data sheet headcount series (year-end): 37,033 / 36,779 / 37,107 / 35,451 / 35,267. [OBS] ([Sustainability Data Sheets 2025](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Absa-Group-Limited-Sustainability-Data-Sheets.pdf))
- **Technology employees: 3,904**; ~39.67% women (in one metric); **7.37% turnover**; 95.5% retention of high performers. [OBS] ([Integrated Report 2025](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Absa-Group-Limited-Integrated-Report.pdf))

## 3. Cost of people
- Staff costs **rose 8% to R36,373m** in FY2025 — **58% of total operating expenses**, driven by ~6% salary growth. [OBS] ([Results booklet FY25](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Results-booklet-for-the-period-ended-31-December-2025-double-page.pdf))
- R36.4bn paid to employees in salaries, incentives and benefits. [OBS] ([Integrated Report 2025](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Absa-Group-Limited-Integrated-Report.pdf))

## 4. Restructuring & transformation
- `[OBS]` Sector context: "Over the past five years, **Nedbank has cut its workforce by over 5,000 employees**, with **Absa and Standard Bank shedding 1,200** [each]." ([TITC](https://titc.io/fintech-jobs-already-being-cut/))
- `[OBS]` Absa Group reports a refreshed strategy under Fihla requiring "adjustment at every level" during transition (2025). ([Integrated Report 2025](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Absa-Group-Limited-Integrated-Report.pdf))
- `[OBS]` Absa Bank Kenya ran a voluntary-severance programme (82 employees) and automated ~71% of processes after a ~US$31m tech spend (2026). ([EdgeX](https://pro.edgex.exchange/ja-JP/news/article/absa-bank-kenya-kes-4b-tech-spend-automation)) — `[INF]` illustrative of automation-driven headcount shaping in Africa Regions.

## 5. Employment equity / B-BBEE (people dimension)
- **Level 1 B-BBEE rating** (verified by Empowerdex). [OBS] ([Integrated Report 2025 / B-BBEE Report](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Absa-Group-Limited-Integrated-Report.pdf))
- Absa pursued a **B-BBEE equity transaction** (CSI Scheme + SA Staff Scheme) to meet Financial Sector Code ownership targets (≥25% Black ownership for SA subsidiaries). [OBS] ([Absa SENS — Proposed B-BBEE transaction, 2023](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/SENS-Absa-Group-Proposed-BEE-Transaction-31-March-2023-Final.pdf))
