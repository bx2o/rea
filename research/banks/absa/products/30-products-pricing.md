# Absa — Product Catalogue & Pricing

## 1. Everyday transactional accounts
| Product | Monthly fee (ZAR) | Key features | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Transact Account** (basic) | **R6.50** | Free card swipes, free app/online/USSD banking, R5,000 life cover, up to R810/month cashback for "banking smartly" | [OBS] ([2025 pricing, products on sale](https://www.absa.co.za/content/dam/south-africa/absa/pdf/2025/2025-benefits-and-pricing-everyday-banking-products-on-sale.pdf)) |
| **Premium Banking Package** | **R240** | Premium current account + no monthly credit card fee + free Absa Rewards; Bidvest airport lounge access; travel insurance | [OBS] ([Absa Premium package](https://www.absa.co.za/personal/bank/an-account/premium-banking-package/)) |
| Everyday banking (other products bundle) | Service fee **R105.00** | Unlimited local debit card purchases, internal transfers, interbank payments, CashSend, PayShap | [OBS] ([2025 pricing — other products](https://www.absa.co.za/content/dam/south-africa/absa/pdf/pricing-brochure/2025/benefits-and-pricing-everyday-banking-other-products.pdf)) |

> All 2025 Absa pricing effective 1 January 2025. [OBS] ([2025 pricing guide](https://www.absa.co.za/content/dam/south-africa/absa/pdf/2025/2025-benefits-and-pricing-everyday-banking-products-on-sale.pdf))

## 2. Credit cards
- Range includes a **Gold Credit Card**, **Premium Credit Card** (previously Platinum), and an electronic Visa card. [OBS] ([Absa credit cards](https://www.absa.co.za/personal/bank/a-credit-card/explore/))
- Premium Banking Credit Card benefits: Bidvest lounge access (domestic & international), basic travel insurance, free secondary card (once-off fee), rewards cashback worldwide. [OBS] ([Premium package](https://www.absa.co.za/personal/bank/an-account/premium-banking-package/))
- Ultimate / Ultimate Plus accounts can link a Gold/Premium Credit Card at **zero additional monthly fee**. [OBS] ([Absa credit cards](https://www.absa.co.za/personal/bank/a-credit-card/explore/))

## 3. Loans & credit
- **Home loans:** up to 100% finance; fixed or variable rate; manageable online. [OBS] ([Private Banking 2025 pricing](https://www.absa.co.za/content/dam/south-africa/absa/pdf/pricing-brochure/2025/benefits-and-pricing-private-banking-2025.pdf))
- **Vehicle & asset finance:** instalment sale or financial leases on movable assets; commercial vehicles, manufacturing/agricultural equipment, aviation, marine, renewable energy. [OBS] ([Absa commercial VAF](https://www.absa.co.za/business/funding-my-business/vehicle-and-asset-finance/explore/))
- **Islamic Vehicle Finance:** Shari'ah-compliant (Ijaarah), 100% financing. [OBS] ([Absa Islamic VAF](https://www.absa.co.za/personal/loans/for-a-car/explore-solutions/islamic-vehicle-finance/))
- **Overdrafts & personal loans:** preferential rates for Premium Banking clients. [OBS] ([JustMoney](https://www.justmoney.co.za/products/absa-premium-credit-card/))

## 4. Rewards
- **Absa Rewards** — free programme, tiered by products/behaviour; up to **30% cashback** at Rewards partners:
  - Groceries: Woolworths, Pick n Pay, Shoprite, Checkers, Food Lover's Market.
  - Fuel: Sasol, Shell.
  - Travel: upfront discounts booked on the app. [OBS] ([Absa Rewards](https://www.absa.co.za/personal/bank/absa-rewards/explore/))
- Rewards tier assigned monthly based on the previous month's qualifying products, banking behaviour and "Engagements". [OBS] ([Rewards T&Cs, eff. 15 Jan 2025](https://www.absa.co.za/content/dam/south-africa/absa/pdf/Offers/terms-and-conditions/rewards-programme-terms-and-conditions-ex.pdf))
- Absa Rewards was formerly **"Absa Rewards"** only (no separate brand); there is no "eBucks" equivalent. [OBS]

## 5. Insurance & value-added (via Absa Financial Advisors)
- Funeral cover, life cover, fixed deposits, travel insurance, short-term insurance. [OBS] ([Absa Abby](https://www.absa.co.za/self-service/absa-abby/))

## 6. Pricing notes & gaps
- `[UNK]` Exhaustive fee table (per-transaction ATM, cash deposit, debit-order fees) beyond headline monthly fees is not fully captured here; the official [2025 Benefits & Pricing PDF](https://www.absa.co.za/content/dam/south-africa/absa/pdf/2025/2025-benefits-and-pricing-everyday-banking-products-on-sale.pdf) holds the detail.
