# Absa Group — Overview (Identity, Journey, Scale)

## 1. Identity
- **Legal names:** Absa Group Limited (holding company, Reg No 1986/003934/06) and Absa Bank Limited (Reg No 1986/004794/06). [OBS] ([Absa SENS FY25](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Full-year-results-Group-short-form-SENS-announcement.pdf), [Absa personal client agreement](https://www.absa.co.za/content/dam/south-africa/absa/pdf/legal/personal-client-agreement-2021.pdf))
- **Tickers / listings:** Primary listing on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (**JSE: ABG**); secondary listing on **A2X**. [OBS] ([Absa SENS FY25](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Full-year-results-Group-short-form-SENS-announcement.pdf))
- **Ticker (US OTC):** AGRPY. [OBS] ([FinanceCharts](https://www.financecharts.com/stocks/AGRPY/profile))
- **Headquarters:** Sandton, Johannesburg, South Africa. [INF] (group is South Africa–domiciled; SA is largest market per [Integrated Report 2025](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Absa-Group-Limited-Integrated-Report.pdf))
- **Self-description:** "a purpose-driven, pan-African financial services group." [OBS] ([SENS FY25](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Full-year-results-Group-short-form-SENS-announcement.pdf))

## 2. Founding & Journey (timeline)
| Year | Event | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1888 | Earliest lineage traced to founding of the Bank of Africa in Pretoria. [OBS] ([Veri-Global](https://veri-global.com/blog/absa-group-south-africa-investment-in-focus-mpnqaxgd)) | [OBS] |
| 1986 | **Amalgamated Banks of South Africa (Absa) Limited incorporated** through merger of UBS Holdings (United), Allied Bank, Volkskas Group and certain Sage Group interests. [OBS] ([Barclays archive](https://home.barclays/archive-barclays/founding-banks/absa-group/)) | [OBS] |
| 1992 | Absa acquired the entire shareholding of the Bankorp Group (incl. TrustBank, Senbank, Bankfin). [OBS] ([Barclays archive](https://home.barclays/archive-barclays/founding-banks/absa-group/)) | [OBS] |
| 1997 | Holding company renamed **Absa Group Ltd**. [OBS] ([Barclays archive](https://home.barclays/archive-barclays/founding-banks/absa-group/)) | [OBS] |
| 2005 | Barclays PLC acquired a 55.5% stake in Absa. [OBS] ([Absa CIB brochure](https://cib.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Absa-CIB-International-Banking-Brochure.pdf)) | [OBS] |
| 2013 | Majority of Barclays' African businesses combined with Absa → **Barclays Africa Group Ltd**. [OBS] ([Absa CIB brochure](https://cib.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Absa-CIB-International-Banking-Brochure.pdf)) | [OBS] |
| 2017–2018 | Barclays reduced its stake to ~16.4%; business rebranded as **Absa Group Ltd** (Africa rebrand completed 2020). [OBS] ([Barclays archive](https://home.barclays/archive-barclays/founding-banks/absa-group/)) | [OBS] |
| Aug 2022 | Barclays sold its remaining 7.4% stake for ~US$620m — Absa becomes fully independent of Barclays. [OBS] ([Wikipedia mirror](https://browse.library.kiwix.org/content/wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Absa_Group)) | [OBS] |
| Jun 2025 | Kenny Fihla appointed permanent Group CEO (effective 17 June 2025). [OBS] ([Absa board](https://www.absa.africa/about-us/board-and-management/)) | [OBS] |

> **Note on listing year:** sources conflict — some cite a JSE listing in **1986** (as Absa) and others **1992** (FinanceCharts). Treated as `[UNVERIFIED]` nuance; business-lineage date of 1986 incorporation is well corroborated.

## 3. Scale (FY2025 and latest)
- **Active customers:** 13.1 million (FY2025; 2024: 12.7 million); **5.4 million digitally active** (2024: 4.6m). [OBS] ([Integrated Report 2025](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Absa-Group-Limited-Integrated-Report.pdf))
- Customers rose to **13.4 million** by 1H2026. [OBS] ([Absa media statement, Aug 2026](https://www.absa.africa/media-statements/2026/absa-group-delivered-an-8-increase-in-headline-earnings-to-r12-8-billion-supported-by-revenue-growth-of-4-to-r58-8-billion/))
- **Employees:** 37,033 (FY2025); 26,565 in South Africa, 10,258 in Africa Regions. [OBS] ([Results booklet FY25](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Results-booklet-for-the-period-ended-31-December-2025-double-page.pdf))
- **Footprint:** 16 countries (H1 2026: 17 incl. UAE representative presence); banks in Botswana, Ghana, Kenya, Mauritius, Mozambique, Seychelles, South Africa, Tanzania, Uganda and Zambia; representative offices in Namibia & Nigeria; securities entities in UK & USA; technology office in Czech Republic. [OBS] ([SENS FY25](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Full-year-results-Group-short-form-SENS-announcement.pdf), [Absa H1 2026 booklet](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Results-booklet-for-the-period-ended-30-June-2026-single-page.pdf))
- **Network (FY2025):** 1,035 outlets and 6,240 ATMs (group). [OBS] ([Results booklet FY25](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Results-booklet-for-the-period-ended-31-December-2025-double-page.pdf)) — BusinessTech reports 566 branches / 5,016 ATMs using a narrower definition. [OBS] ([BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/banking/876479/battle-of-the-banks-2026-capitec-vs-fnb-vs-standard-bank-vs-absa-vs-nedbank/))
- **Market position:** 3rd-largest SA bank by headline earnings/total income behind Standard Bank and FirstRand; **cheapest** of the Big Five on a P/E basis (7.89 as at Sep 2026). [OBS]/[DER] ([BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/banking/876479/battle-of-the-banks-2026-capitec-vs-fnb-vs-standard-bank-vs-absa-vs-nedbank/))
- **Market cap:** ~R197.4bn (Sep 2026). [OBS] ([BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/banking/876479/battle-of-the-banks-2026-capitec-vs-fnb-vs-standard-bank-vs-absa-vs-nedbank/))

## 4. Ownership
- **No controlling shareholder** following Barclays' full exit (2022). [OBS] ([Barclays archive](https://home.barclays/archive-barclays/founding-banks/absa-group/), [Wikipedia mirror](https://browse.library.kiwix.org/content/wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Absa_Group))
- Broad free-float institutional ownership; primary JSE, secondary A2X. [OBS] ([SENS FY25](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Full-year-results-Group-short-form-SENS-announcement.pdf))

## 5. Strategy positioning
- Ambition: "to be a leading pan-African bank that creates value by enabling inclusive growth, building financial resilience, and driving sustainable development." [OBS] ([SENS FY25](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Full-year-results-Group-short-form-SENS-announcement.pdf))
- 2025 brought a "refreshed strategy" under CEO Kenny Fihla focused on winning with customers, execution efficiency, and digital-first relevance. [OBS] ([Integrated Report 2025](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Absa-Group-Limited-Integrated-Report.pdf))
