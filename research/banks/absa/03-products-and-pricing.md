## Pricing findings summary

1. Absa's breadth (Islamic, SME) is real differentiation Stremiium should note for niche parity. SEEN/medium
2. Rewards are cashback-centric — less behavioural than FNB eBucks. SEEN/medium
3. Absa's pricing is conventional incumbent pricing; transparency remains a seam to attack. INFERRED/low
