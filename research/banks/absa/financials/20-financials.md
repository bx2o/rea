## 5. Analyst observations
- `[DER]` Absa's total income is ~2.7× Nedbank's, yet its market cap (R197.4bn) is only ~1.4× — reflecting a lower P/E (7.89 vs 15.53) and structurally lower ROE (15.0% vs 15.4% but similar; heavier Africa Regions drag). [DER]
- `[INF]` The gap between total income and market cap suggests the market applies a "SA-bank discount" and/or concerns about Africa Regions execution (Africa Regions earnings −10% in 1H2026).
