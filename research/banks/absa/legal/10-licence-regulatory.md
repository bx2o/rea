# Absa Bank — Licence & Regulatory Posture

## 1. Licence class & supervisors
- **Bank licence:** Absa Bank Limited is licensed to conduct the business of a bank under the **Banks Act 94 of 1990**. [OBS] ([Grokipedia — Absa Bank](https://grokipedia.com/page/Absa_Bank))
- **Prudential supervisor:** the **Prudential Authority (PA)** within the **South African Reserve Bank (SARB)** — the PA is mandated under section 12 of the Banks Act to authorise and supervise banks. [OBS] ([SARB — Functions of the Prudential Authority](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/Prudentialregulation/functions-of-the-prudential-authority))
- **Market-conduct supervisor:** the **Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA)** — "Authorised Financial Services Provider". [OBS] ([Absa Personal Client Agreement](https://www.absa.co.za/content/dam/south-africa/absa/pdf/legal/personal-client-agreement-2021.pdf))
- **Credit provider:** registered with the **National Credit Regulator (NCR)** — Absa Bank **Reg No NCRCP7**. [OBS] ([Absa Personal Client Agreement](https://www.absa.co.za/content/dam/south-africa/absa/pdf/legal/personal-client-agreement-2021.pdf), [CodeCash](https://codecash.co.za/absa-vehicle-finance/))

## 4. Regulatory context
- The PA has fined several SA banks for FIC Act non-compliance (incl. Sasfin, Standard Bank, Capitec, Absa) — indicating active enforcement across the sector. [OBS] ([Eritv News](https://eritvnews.com/prudential-authority-fines-absa-r10-million-for-fica-non-compliance/))
- Sanctions are tiered: cautions, reprimands, directives, and financial penalties proportionate to the non-compliance. [OBS] ([FIC — Supervision and enforcement](https://www.fic.gov.za/compliance/supervision-and-enforcement/))

## 5. Compliance posture assessment
- `[INF]` Absa maintains the full standard SA bank licence stack (Banks Act + FSCA + NCR + FIC). The 2025 R10m FIC penalty is a remediation signal rather than a licence risk, and it is modest relative to the R24.8bn FY25 headline earnings. Data protection (`[UNK]`) is the least-documented area publicly.
