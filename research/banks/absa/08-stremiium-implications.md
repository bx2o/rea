# 08 — Stremiium Implications: ABSA Group

Strategic filter for the Stremiium NeoBank against ABSA Group.

| # | Finding | Decision | Improvement Stremiium makes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dual-core estate (SAP + Finacle) = duplication, cost drag, slow delivery | **AVOID** | Single cloud-native core (Mambu) — no duplicated processing estate |
| 2 | AWS-led cloud migration direction | **ADOPT** | Cloud-first on AWS `af-south-1` from day one (no migration debt) |
| 3 | WhatsApp ChatBanking (low-friction channel) | **ADOPT** | Ship messaging-based onboarding/servicing for mass-market reach |
| 4 | Cashback rewards (clear value, weak gamification) | **ADAPT** | Hybrid: cashback clarity + `$STM` gamified token rewards |
| 5 | Sasol-style partner-funded fuel rebates | **ADOPT** | Replicate partner-funded fuel/grocery rebates **and** add protocol rewards |
| 6 | Islamic Banking / SME working capital breadth | **ADAPT** | Offer Shari'ah-compliant and SME products as parity features once core is live |
| 7 | Separation-driven leadership/execution instability | **BEAT** | Stable, transparent roadmap signals reliability to customers/partners |
| 8 | No crypto/banking convergence at Absa | **BEAT** | FSCA CASP + NeoBank in one app; non-custodial MPC wallet |
| 9 | Opaque rebate tables | **BEAT** | Auditable, simulatable reward rules |
| 10 | Conventional incumbent fee ladder | **BEAT** | Transparent, programmable fees with `$STM` discounts |

## Net position

- **Adopt:** cloud-first; messaging banking channel; partner-funded rebates.
- **Adapt:** reward model (cashback + token); product breadth (Islamic/SME as parity).
- **Avoid:** heterogeneous/duplicated core; migration-debt-driven architecture.
- **Beat:** reward transparency/gamification; crypto/banking convergence; execution stability.
