## 2. Merchant acquiring & cards
- `[OBS]` Absa is described as the **largest merchant acquirer in Africa (2024 Nilson Report)** and a leading card processor for SA retailers. ([Absa merchant solutions brochure](https://www.absa.co.za/content/dam/south-africa/absa/pdf/business/make-and-receive/merchant-solutions-brochure.pdf))
- `[OBS]` Absa offers in-store, mobile and contactless payment acceptance, POS systems, and online payment gateways for businesses. (same)

## 3. Sponsorship partnerships (affinity/brand)
- **Absa Cape Epic** (title) — extended to **2032**; ~20-year relationship; UCI hors catégorie. [OBS] ([TREAD Media](https://www.treadmtb.co.za/absa-extends-cape-epic-sponsorship-to-2032/))
- **Absa SuperSport Shootout** — co-title with SuperSport. [OBS] ([Absa Sponsorships](https://www.absa.africa/absa-sponsorships/))
- **Absa Run Your City**, **Absa Champagne in Africa Festival**, **Johannesburg Film Festival**, **L'Atelier**, **Qhubekha**, **#SheUntamed**. [OBS] ([Absa Sponsorships](https://www.absa.africa/absa-sponsorships/))
- Absa runs a **formal sponsorship intake process** for rights-holders (sponsorships@absa.co.za). [OBS] ([Absa Sponsorships](https://www.absa.africa/absa-sponsorships/))

## 4. Pan-African structural partnerships
- `[OBS]` Absa operates banks in 10 African countries plus representative offices (Namibia, Nigeria), securities entities (UK, USA), a non-banking advisory subsidiary (China), a technology office (Czech Republic), and from April 2026 a **Dubai International Financial Centre branch (UAE)**. ([Absa H1 2026 booklet](https://www.absa.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Results-booklet-for-the-period-ended-30-June-2026-single-page.pdf))
- `[INF]` Some African subsidiaries are not wholly owned (majority stakes); Absa's JV/associate footprint is a structural "partnership by equity" model.
