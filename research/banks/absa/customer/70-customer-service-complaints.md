## 2. Complaints & Ombudsman data
- **National Financial Ombud (NFO) — Banking Division:** a record **15,412 complaints in 2024** (11,535 closed); **79% of formal outcomes favoured the banks**, 21% complainants. [OBS] ([Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/when-banks-get-it-wrong-ombud-cases-reveal-costly-mistakes/))
- Top-five banks by formal cases opened (one reporting year): Capitec 1,203 (20%), FNB 1,017 (17%) lead. [OBS] ([Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/fraud-floods-complaints-as-banking-ombud-speeds-up-resolutions/))
- Prior-year trend: **Absa complaints fell 8%**, while Standard Bank fell 5.3%, FNB rose 15.1%, Capitec +12.4%, and Nedbank (2nd-highest total) +3.6%. Indicator: the Banking Ombudsman returned **R25m** to consumers. [OBS] ([Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/business/personal-finance/banking-ombudsman-r25-million-consumers/))
- **2025:** NFO returned **R60m+** to bank clients; **mobile/internet banking fraud = 39% of all cases** received — the dominant complaint category sector-wide. [OBS] ([Primedia Plus](https://www.primediaplus.com/2025/12/17/financial-ombud-makes-banks-return-r60-million-to-clients-bulk-around-cases-of-fraud))
- `[INF]` Absa's declining (improving) complaint trend (−8%) and its non-leadership in case counts suggest a comparatively better-than-peers complaints position among the Big Five, though absolute Absa-specific case numbers are not stated in these sources.

## 3. Fraud & security (customer-facing)
- Absa offers a **free Digital Fraud Warranty** in the banking app; lets users cancel unauthorised debit orders in-app. [OBS] ([Absa App Stores](https://www.absa.co.za/app-stores/), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.barclays.absa.banking))
- `[OBS]` Industry-wide, fraud (esp. mobile/internet banking fraud) drives the majority of NFO complaints — a key service risk area for Absa too. ([Primedia Plus](https://www.primediaplus.com/2025/12/17/financial-ombud-makes-banks-return-r60-million-to-clients-bulk-around-cases-of-fraud))
