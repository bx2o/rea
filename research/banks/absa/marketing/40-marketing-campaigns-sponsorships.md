## 2. Historical sponsorships (the "journey")
- **Bafana Bafana / SAFA:** Absa sponsored SAFA and the national team but **announced its resignation in Oct 2013**, ending the contract in **December 2013**. [OBS] ([Wikipedia mirror — SA national soccer team](https://iiab.live/kiwix/content/wikipedia_en_top1m_maxi_2024-05/A/South_Africa_national_soccer_team))
- **Absa Premiership (PSL):** Absa sponsored SA football for 16 years, **13 as Absa Premiership sponsor from 2007**, ending when the contract expired at the end of the 2019/20 season. [OBS] ([Absa Group news](https://www.absa.africa/page/36/))
- **Absa Currie Cup (rugby):** Absa was the long-standing title sponsor of the Currie Cup; the competition was rebranded **Carling Currie Cup** (Carling Black Label) by 2021. [OBS]/[INF] ([SA Rugby — Carling Currie Cup 2021](https://springboks.rugby/news-features/articles/2021/06/17/the-carling-currie-cup-bulletin-1), [FR Wikipedia — Currie Cup](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Currie_Cup_(rugby)))
- **Absa SuperSport Shootout** (golf) — Absa remains active in golf via this property. [OBS] ([Absa Sponsorships](https://www.absa.africa/absa-sponsorships/))

> **Correction to brief assumption:** the brief associated "Cape Epic" with Nedbank. Evidence shows **Cape Epic is an ABSA title property** (Absa Cape Epic), while Nedbank's flagship golf property is the **Nedbank Golf Challenge**. See `nedbank/marketing/`.

## 3. Brand & app marketing
- **"Data is on us!"** — free in-app data usage for the Absa Banking App; free Digital Fraud Warranty; "one of the top apps in South Africa". [OBS] ([Absa App Stores](https://www.absa.co.za/app-stores/))
- App is self-rated **4.8/5 on both App Store and Google Play** (Absa claim). [OBS] ([Absa App Stores](https://www.absa.co.za/app-stores/))

## 4. Economic-impact narrative (sponsorship ROI)
- `[OBS]` The Absa Cape Epic has contributed **close to R11 billion to the Western Cape economy** over two decades (tourism, jobs, SMMEs). ([Modern Opulent Gazette](https://www.modernopulentgazette.com/post/what-20-years-of-absa-cape-epic-means-for-south-africa-s-economy))
- `[OBS]` Absa frames title sponsorship as "driving economic investment and impact." ([Absa 2025 Cape Epic](https://www.absa.africa/2025-absa-cape-epic/))
