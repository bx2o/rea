# JPMorgan Chase — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

## 1. Major Sponsorships

| Property | Relationship | Since |
|---|---|---|
| **US Open** (tennis) | Official sponsor | [OBS] **45 years** as of 2026 ([Chase US Open](https://www.chase.com/personal/events/us-open)) |
| **Chase Center** (Golden State Warriors) | Naming-rights partner | [OBS] **20-year agreement signed 2016** ([Chase Center partnership](https://www.lauriegarrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/ChaseCenter_Sept23_2019.pdf)) |
| **Madison Square Garden** | Partner | [OBS] ([JPMorganChase Athlete Council](https://www.chase.com/personal/jpmc-athletes-council)) |
| **Home Nations football** (FA, IFA, SFA, FAW) | Chase UK Official Banking Partner | [OBS] **Feb 28, 2024**, multi-year ([Chase UK press](https://www.chase.co.uk/gb/en/media/chase-named-the-official-banking-partner-of-the-home-nations-football-teams/)) |
| **Transport for London (TfL)** | Chase UK "Official Payment Partner" | [OBS] **Nov 24, 2025**, multi-year; Chase branding on every contactless Oyster reader ([Chase UK press](https://www.chase.co.uk/gb/en/media/chase-tfl/)) |

- [OBS] Chase invests in women's sports (KPMG Women's PGA Championship; founding partner of the
  Golden State Valkyries and League One Volleyball)
  ([Athlete Council](https://www.chase.com/personal/jpmc-athletes-council)).
- [OBS] US Open activation includes the **Chase Lounge**, giveaways and customer benefits
  ([Chase US Open](https://www.chase.com/personal/events/us-open)).

## 2. Brand & Product Campaigns

- [OBS] **Chase Freedom®** runs rotating quarterly 5% cash-back categories as an always-on
  acquisition mechanic (e.g. Q3 2026: gas stations/EV charging, public transit, live entertainment)
  ([Chase press announcements](https://media.chase.com/news)).
- [OBS] Chase UK campaigns: 2024 football-partnership activation provided fully funded coaching
  qualifications and learning tools for low-income communities
  ([Chase UK press](https://www.chase.co.uk/gb/en/media/chase-named-the-official-banking-partner-of-the-home-nations-football-teams/)).
- [OBS] Marketing is described as data-driven/personalised, leveraging customer data and a
  significant share of the ~$17B tech budget for marketing platforms
  ([matrixbcg](https://matrixbcg.com/blogs/marketing-strategy/jpmorganchase)).

## 3. Concierge / Affluent Marketing

- [OBS] J.P. Morgan Private Client marketing emphasises **concierge services**, preferred savings
  rates and complimentary wealth planning ([Chase/Media release](https://media.chase.com/news/jpmorganchase-opens-flagship-financial-centers-for-affluent-clients)).
