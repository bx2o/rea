# JPMorgan Chase — Kinexys & JPM Coin (Digital Assets)

## 1. Kinexys (formerly Onyx)

- [OBS] **Kinexys by J.P. Morgan** is the firm's blockchain business unit, **rebranded from Onyx
  in November 2024** (announced at Singapore Fintech Festival by Umar Farooq, co-head of J.P.
  Morgan Payments) ([Introducing Kinexys](https://www.jpm.com/insights/payments/payment-trends/introducing-kinexys)).
- [OBS] Onyx was the name given in **2020** to the business unit housing the firm's blockchain and
  digital-asset work ([DiPprofit](https://www.dipprofit.com/what-happened-to-onyx-crypto-created-by-jpmorgan-in-2026/)).
- [OBS] Kinexys comprises three product lines: **Kinexys Digital Payments** (formerly the JPM Coin
  System), **Kinexys Digital Assets** (formerly Onyx Digital Assets), and **Kinexys Labs**
  ([Solana Compass / Kinexys product overview](https://solanacompass.com/projects/jpmorgan-chase)).

## 2. Scale (as reported)

- [OBS] Launched **2019**; has processed **over $1.5 trillion** cumulative (earlier disclosure)
  with **>$2B average daily volume** and **10× YoY** payments-volume growth
  ([Fintech News MENA citing JPMorgan](https://fintechnews.media/eight-major-mena-banks-adopt-jpmorgans-kinexys-blockchain-platform-for-cross-border-payments)).
- [OBS] More recent disclosure: **over $4 trillion cumulative**, **~$7B average daily volume**,
  clients across **five continents** and settlement in multiple currencies (8 currencies as of
  mid-2026) ([HBS case study newsroom](https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/newsroom/harvard-business-school-case-study-kinexys);
  [KB Kookmin Bank newsroom](https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/newsroom/kb-kookmin-bank-kinexys-bda)).
- [OBS] Kinexys enables **24/7 near-real-time cross-border settlement** and **programmable payments**
  ([KB Kookmin Bank newsroom](https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/newsroom/kb-kookmin-bank-kinexys-bda)).
- [OBS] Technology milestones: executed some of the **largest repo transactions on any blockchain**;
  tokenized money-market funds (MONY) and tokenized commercial paper settlement
  ([Introducing Kinexys](https://www.jpm.com/insights/payments/payment-trends/introducing-kinexys);
  [HBS case study](https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/newsroom/harvard-business-school-case-study-kinexys)).

## 3. JPM Coin

- [OBS] **JPM Coin** was first announced/piloted in **February 2019** as a wholesale deposit token
  ([Grokipedia — JPM Coin](https://grokipedia.com/page/JPM_Coin)).
- [OBS] In **June 2025** JPM Coin was piloted on a public blockchain; in **November 2025** it
  launched as a **live offering** for institutional clients — the **first time commercial-bank
  money was extended beyond private blockchain infrastructure into public blockchain environments**
  ([Kinexys/JPM Coin recognition](https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/newsroom/kinexys-jpm-coin-industry-recognition-2026)).
- [OBS] Institutional clients can use JPM Coin for **near-real-time peer-to-peer transfers between
  EVM-compatible wallets**; J.P. Morgan became the **first bank to issue a USD deposit token on a
  public blockchain** ([J.P. Morgan payments newsroom](https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/newsroom/jpm-coin-usd-deposit-token-institutional-clients)).
- [OBS] The JPM Coin **Deposit Token launched on the Base blockchain** (2025)
  ([Kinexys About](https://www.jpmorgan.com/kinexys/about)).

## 4. Product Roadmap Milestones (Kinexys About timeline)

- [OBS] 2024: introduction of Kinexys; Kinexys Digital Payments announces first Treasury features.
- [OBS] 2025: JPM Coin Deposit Token on Base; first tokenized private equity fund supported;
  **MONY** becomes the first tokenized money-market fund offering backed by J.P. Morgan.
  ([Kinexys About](https://www.jpmorgan.com/kinexys/about))

## 5. Relevance Flags

- [INF] Kinexys/JPM Coin is the incumbent-bank benchmark for **tokenized deposits and
  programmable cross-border money**, directly comparable to the SA Neobank launch's own
  payments/settlement ambitions and to stablecoin rails competitors.
