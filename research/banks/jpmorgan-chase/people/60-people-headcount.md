## 2. Headcount

| Year-end | Employees |
|---|---|
| 2023 | 309,926 |
| 2024 | 317,233 |
| 2025 | 318,512 |

- [OBS] Employees: **309,926** (2023) → **317,233** (2024) → **318,512** (2025)
  ([2025 Annual Report](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/annualreport-2025.pdf)).
- [OBS] Compensation expense grew to **$54,487M** (2025) driven by front-office headcount growth
  and higher revenue-related compensation, predominantly in CIB and AWM
  ([2025 MD&A](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/managements-discussion-analysis-2025.pdf)).
- [OBS] Third-party tracker reports ~**319,550–323,028** employees (2025/2026) consistent with
  filings ([Revelio Labs](https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/jpmorgan-chase/employees/)).

## 3. Talent Focus

- [OBS] Headcount growth skewed to **front-office** roles; the firm also invests heavily in
  technology/AI talent (see `../technology/50-tech-stack.md`).
- [OBS] New global HQ 270 Park Avenue will house **10,000 employees** by year-end 2025
  ([press release](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/newsroom/press-releases/2025/grand-opening-270-park)).
