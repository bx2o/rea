## 2. Affluent Tiers (Chase → J.P. Morgan)

- [OBS] **Chase Private Client** — for clients with **$150,000+** in qualifying deposit & investment
  balances; available in all Chase branches
  ([Chase/Media release](https://media.chase.com/news/jpmorganchase-expands-private-client-offering-53-branches)).
- [OBS] **J.P. Morgan Private Client** — for clients with **$750,000+** in qualifying balances;
  concierge services, preferred savings rates, complimentary wealth planning
  ([Chase/Media release](https://media.chase.com/news/jpmorganchase-opens-flagship-financial-centers-for-affluent-clients)).
- [OBS] **J.P. Morgan Private Bank** — for clients with **more than $5M** in investable assets
  (see `../jpmorgan-private-bank/`).
  ([Chase/Media release](https://media.chase.com/news/jpmorganchase-opens-flagship-financial-centers-for-affluent-clients)).
