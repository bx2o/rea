# JPMorgan Chase — Licence & Regulatory

## 1. Corporate Structure & Charter

- [OBS] JPMorgan Chase & Co. is a **bank holding company** and financial holding company.
  ([SEC filing](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/node/210081/html))
- [OBS] National bank subsidiaries **JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A.** and **Chase Bank USA, N.A.**
  are subject to supervision by the **Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC)** and,
  for certain matters, by the **Federal Reserve** and the **FDIC**.
  ([SEC filing](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/node/525601/html))
- [OBS] JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. details per its 2025 Call Report: FDIC Certificate No. 628,
  **OCC Charter No. 8**, headquarters Columbus, OH (FFIEC 031).
  ([Call Report FY2025](https://www.jpmorgan.com/content/dam/jpm/global/disclosures/AU/disclosure-statements/banks-call-report-fy-2025.pdf))
- [INF] The OCC is the primary federal chartering/supervisory agency for national banks; the Fed
  and FDIC supervise at the holding-company and deposit-insurance levels respectively.
  ([OCC explainer](https://policyrisk.com/wiki/occ-national-bank-regulation))

## 2. Prudential Regulation (G-SIB / Stress Testing)

- [OBS] JPMorgan Chase is a **Global Systemically Important Bank (G-SIB)** — subject to enhanced
  capital surcharges, stricter liquidity standards (LCR/NSFR), resolution planning and the Fed's
  annual **CCAR / DFAST** supervisory stress tests.
  ([Federal Reserve explainer](https://www.financialregulationcourses.com/dictionary/f/federal-reserve))
- [OBS] The firm must run semi-annual company-run stress tests and submit an annual capital plan
  to the Federal Reserve under the CCAR framework.
  ([SEC filing](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/node/210081/html))
- [OBS] JPMorgan conducts and publishes an **annual Dodd-Frank Act Stress Test (DFAST)** results
  disclosure. ([2026 DFAST disclosure](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/events/2026/2026-annual-dodd-frank-act-stress-test-results-disclosure/2026-results-methodology-disclosure-dfast-2026-20260624.pdf))
- [OBS] Capital ratios (4Q25 actual → 1Q28 projected minimum under stress): Common equity tier 1
  **14.6%** → 12.4%; Tier 1 risk-based 15.5% → 13.3%; Total risk-based 17.4% → 15.3%.
  ([8-K iXBRL viewer](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/node/902791/ixbrl-viewer))

## 3. Resolution & Deposit Insurance

- [OBS] JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. is a **covered insured depository institution (CIDI)** that
  files periodic resolution plans with the FDIC under the FDI Act.
  ([FDIC JPMCB IDI Resolution Plan 2023](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/resolutions/resolution-authority/resplans/plans/jpmchase-idi-2312.pdf))
- [OBS] Deposits in Chase national bank subsidiaries are **FDIC-insured**.

## 4. Securities & Other Regulators

- [OBS] JPMorgan Chase's U.S. **broker-dealers** are supervised by the **SEC** (and FINRA), and
  certain subsidiaries are regulated by additional authorities based on their activities/licences.
  ([SEC filing](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/node/525601/html))
