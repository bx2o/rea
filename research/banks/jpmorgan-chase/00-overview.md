# JPMorgan Chase & Co. — Overview

> Council C10 (Finance & IR) · Global reference bank · Reverse-engineering profile.
> Scope: founding/history/listing, financials, products, technology, marketing, people, regulation.
> Public information only. Every non-obvious claim is tagged and cited.

## 1. Identity & Listing

- [OBS] JPMorgan Chase & Co. is an American multinational banking and financial services
  company formed through the **December 2000 merger of J.P. Morgan & Co. and The Chase
  Manhattan Corporation** ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/JPMorgan-Chase-and-Co)).
- [OBS] The firm traces its roots to **1799** in New York City (the Manhattan Company) and is
  built on the foundation of **more than 1,200 predecessor institutions** over ~225 years
  ([History of Our Firm](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/about/our-history)).
- [OBS] Listed on the **New York Stock Exchange under the ticker "JPM"**
  ([Investing.com profile](https://www.investing.com/equities/jp-morgan-chase-company-profile)).
- [OBS] Headquarters: New York City. The new global headquarters at **270 Park Avenue** opened
  **October 21, 2025** — designed by Foster + Partners, 60 floors, ~423 m; home to ~10,000
  employees by year-end ([JPMorganChase press release](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/newsroom/press-releases/2025/grand-opening-270-park)).

## 2. What It Is (Business Model)

- [OBS] Operates through four reportable segments: **Consumer & Community Banking (CCB)**,
  **Commercial & Investment Bank (CIB)**, **Asset & Wealth Management (AWM)** and
  **Corporate** ([JPMorgan Chase 10-K filings](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/node/210081/html)).
- [OBS] CCB = Chase retail/consumer banking, cards, auto; CIB = merged Commercial Banking +
  Corporate & Investment Bank (integration announced 2024); AWM = asset & wealth management
  including J.P. Morgan Private Bank and J.P. Morgan Asset Management.
- [INF] The firm is the largest US bank by assets and, as of FY2024, reported the highest annual
  net income of any bank globally ([visuwire summary](https://visuwire.com/companies/jpmorgan/)).

## 3. Key Financial Anchors (see `financials/20-financials.md`)

- [OBS] **FY2024:** managed revenue **$180.6B**, net income **$58.5B**, ROTCE **20%**
  ([CEO letter, 2024 Annual Report](https://www.jpmorgan.com/about-us/corporate-news/2025/2024-annual-report)).

## 4. Digital / Blockchain (see `blockchain/70-kinexys-jpmcoin.md`)

- [OBS] **Kinexys by J.P. Morgan** — rebranded from **Onyx** in **November 2024** — is the
  firm's blockchain business unit ([Introducing Kinexys](https://www.jpm.com/insights/payments/payment-trends/introducing-kinexys)).
- [OBS] **JPM Coin** launched as a live USD deposit token offering on a public blockchain in
  **November 2025** — the first commercial-bank money extended to public blockchain rails
  ([J.P. Morgan newsroom](https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/newsroom/kinexys-jpm-coin-industry-recognition-2026)).

## 5. Regulation Snapshot (see `legal/10-licence-regulatory.md`)

- [OBS] Bank holding company under **Federal Reserve** consolidated supervision; national bank
  subsidiaries (JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A.; Chase Bank USA, N.A.) regulated by the **OCC**,
  **Fed** and **FDIC** ([SEC filing](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/node/210081/html)).
- [OBS] Designated a **Global Systemically Important Bank (G-SIB)** subject to enhanced capital
  surcharges and annual supervisory stress tests (DFAST/CCAR)
  ([2026 DFAST disclosure](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/events/2026/2026-annual-dodd-frank-act-stress-test-results-disclosure/2026-results-methodology-disclosure-dfast-2026-20260624.pdf)).
