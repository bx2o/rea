# JPMorgan Chase — Technology Stack

## 1. Technology Budget

- [OBS] **2025 firmwide technology expense outlook ~$18B**; 2025 technology *investments* ~$8.0B
  ($5.3B products/platforms/features + $2.7B structural/volumes), split by line of business
  (CCB ~$3.2B) ([2025 Investor Day presentation](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/events/2025/jpmc-2025-investor-day/full-presentation.pdf)).
- [OBS] A prior-year budget of **~$17B (2024)** is widely reported, with a growing allocation to
  AI ([matrixbcg analysis](https://matrixbcg.com/blogs/marketing-strategy/jpmorganchase)).
- [EST] Industry analysts estimate **~$2B in 2024** was allocated to AI-related initiatives
  ([tanok-tech](https://www.tanok-tech.com/en/blog/jpmorgan-2-billion-ai-investment-core-infrastructure)) — [EST], third-party.

## 2. Cloud & Data Platform

- [OBS] Multi-cloud strategy across **AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure**; consolidating data
  centres to ~17 highly-automated AI-optimised sites.
  ([CSDN / JPM AI strategy decode](https://blog.csdn.net/keylink_con/article/details/149356835))
- [OBS] Internal data & AI platform uses **AWS SageMaker**, with **5,000+ employees** using the
  tool monthly ([Business Insider Africa](https://africa.businessinsider.com/news/heres-how-aws-is-helping-financial-giants-like-jpmorgan-and-bridgewater-with-their-ai/fehdr39)).
- [OBS] The firm standardised data formats across business lines and built internal data
  governance for model risk management and privacy
  ([The Kaash Journal](https://www.thekaashjournal.com/blog/jpmorgan-ai-banking-strategy-case-study)).

## 3. AI at Scale

- [OBS] $18B platform supports **450+ AI use cases** in production serving **200,000 daily users**;
  data pipelines process 30+ years of history ([anikethfocus](https://anikethfocus.org/how-jpmorgans-18b-ai-strategy-reveals-the-five-technologies-every-professional-should-master/)).
  - [INF] These figures come from an aggregator blog, not a primary JPM organ disclosure; treat as
    **partial** / single-source.
- [OBS] Payments processing scale described as handling ~$10 trillion per day in core banking/fx
  flows ([CSDN](https://blog.csdn.net/keylink_con/article/details/149356835)).

## 4. Blockchain Infrastructure

- [OBS] See `../blockchain/70-kinexys-jpmcoin.md` — Kinexys (formerly Onyx) blockchain platform,
  JPM Coin deposit token, Tokenized Collateral Network.
