# JPMorgan Chase — Financials

## 1. Firm-Level Results

| Metric | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Managed/reported net revenue | $158.1B (reported) | $180.6B managed / $177.6B reported | $185.6B managed / $182.4B reported |
| Net income | — | $58.5B | $57.0B |
| Diluted EPS | — | — | $20.02 |
| ROTCE | — | 20% | 20% |
| ROE | — | — | 17% |

- [OBS] FY2024: revenue **$180.6B**, net income **$58.5B**, ROTCE **20%**
  ([2024 Annual Report CEO letter](https://www.jpmorgan.com/about-us/corporate-news/2025/2024-annual-report)).
- [OBS] FY2025: revenue **$185.6B**, net income **$57.0B**, diluted EPS **$20.02**, ROTCE
  **20%**, ROE **17%** — record revenue for the **8th consecutive year**
  ([2025 CEO letter](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/ir/annual-report/2025/ar-ceo-letters);
  [Q4 2025 press release](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/quarterly-earnings/2025/4th-quarter/d868c7ef-1670-465d-ba75-c2b36ddbcc6b.pdf)).
- [OBS] FY2025 reported total net revenue **$182,447M**; net interest income **$95.9B** vs
  **$93.1B** in FY2024; noninterest revenue **$89.7B**; total noninterest expense **$95.6B**
  ([8-K / FY2025 summary](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/node/838396/html); [2025 Annual Report](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/annualreport-2025.pdf)).
- [OBS] FY2024 net interest income **$92.6B** — the single largest revenue component
  ([visuwire](https://visuwire.com/companies/jpmorgan/)).
- [OBS] Compensation expense: **$54,487M** (2025), **$51,357M** (2024), **$46,465M** (2023)
  ([2025 MD&A](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/managements-discussion-analysis-2025.pdf)).
- [OBS] 2026 guidance: net interest income projected **~$103B**
  ([Investing.com Q4 2025 summary](https://uk.investing.com/news/company-news/jpmorgan-q4-2025-presentation-reveals-earnings-beat-projects-103b-nii-for-2026-93CH-4451751)).

## 2. Segments (reportable)

- [OBS] Four reportable segments: **CCB**, **CIB**, **AWM**, **Corporate**
  ([10-K](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/node/210081/html)).
- [OBS] Segment net income (aggregator, year ended Dec 31, 2024 — *treat as approximate*):
  CCB $17.60B; Corporate $10.60B; AWM $1.52B; Commercial Banking $1.87B; CIB $4.75B
  ([stockanalysis earnings-by-segment](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jpm/metrics/earnings-by-segment/)).
  - [INF] The aggregator row mixes the pre-2024 "Commercial Banking" and "CIB" split with a
    large "Corporate" figure and does **not** reconcile cleanly to the $58.5B firm total; treat
    exact segment net income as **[UNK]** pending the audited 10-K segment note.
- [OBS] FY2025 revenue growth was broad-based; CIB and AWM drove higher revenue-related
  compensation ([2025 MD&A](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/managements-discussion-analysis-2025.pdf)).
