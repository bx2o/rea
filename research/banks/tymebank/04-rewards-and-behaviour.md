# 04 — Rewards & Behaviour · TymeBank / GoTymeBank

## Headline finding: TymeBank competes on *cost and access*, not on a loyalty currency

Unlike Discovery Bank (closed-loop **Miles**) or African Bank (**Audacious Rewards**), TymeBank's SA proposition has **no dominant transferable loyalty currency**. Its "behavioural" levers are **price and access mechanics**:

```
BEHAVIOUR                       MECHANISM                        OUTCOME
------------------------------- -------------------------------- ------------------------------
Save regularly / stay loyal  -> GoalSave tiered interest rate -> visible higher savings yield
Receive salary into TymeBank -> TymeAdvance eligibility        -> earned-wage access liquidity
Open account at kiosk        -> instant card, zero fee account -> immediate usability
Spend on card                -> (interchange to bank)          -> not returned as points
Buy airtime/electricity      -> VAS margin to bank             -> not returned as points
```

## Retention / velocity effect

* TymeBank's retention is driven by **switching cost avoidance** (salary into TymeBank → free advances; savings → tiered GoalSave rate) rather than by a fungible reward balance.
* **Gap/risk:** without a reward currency, TymeBank has **no cross-ecosystem loyalty lock**; a competitor offering a transferable reward can peel off engaged clients.
