## 2. Prudential framework

- South African banking is governed primarily by the **Banks Act, 1990** and its regulations; no person
  may conduct "the business of a bank" unless registered as a public company bank (or authorised foreign
  branch). The **SARB Prudential Authority (PA)** is the prudential supervisor
  ([Mondaq — SA Banking Regulation](https://www.mondaq.com/guides/results/15/1196/all/south-africa-banking-regulation)). [OBS]
- As a **controlling company registered under section 44 of the Banks Act**, Capitec Bank Holdings'
  name change to Capitec Limited was gazetted by the PA on 2 October 2026
  ([Newsday](https://newsday.co.za/business/28443/south-africas-largest-bank-changes-its-official-name/),
  [IT News Africa](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/capitec-officially-becomes-capitec-limited/)). [OBS]

## 4. Conduct & payments regulation

- **PayShap** — Capitec participates in South Africa's real-time, low-value payment scheme mandated by
  the SARB and operated by BankservAfrica/PayInc since 13 March 2023
  ([SARB press release](https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/publications/media-releases/2023/payshap-/Press%20release%20on%20the%20launch%20of%20Payshap%20-%20a%20digital%20payment%20service.pdf)). [OBS]
- **Complaints escalation** — unresolved complaints may be referred to the **National Financial Ombud
  Scheme (NFO)**, free of charge ([Capitec — Complaints](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/contact-us/get-in-touch/)). [OBS]
- **Insurance** — the group holds a **life insurance licence** and an insurance cell captive
  ([Integrated annual report FY2026](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/globalassets/pages/investor-relations/financial-results/2026/annual-report/integrated_annual_report_2026.pdf)). [OBS]
