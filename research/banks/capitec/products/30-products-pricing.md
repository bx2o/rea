# Capitec — Products & Pricing

Pricing effective 2026 (VAT-inclusive where stated). Sources: Capitec's own 2026 personal-banking fees
schedule ([PDF](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/globalassets/pages/documents-library/transact/personal-banking-fees-2026.pdf)),
Capitec Rewards pages, and the FY2026 results commentary. All [OBS] unless noted.

|---|---|---|

| Monthly administration fee | **R7.50** | [OBS] |

Sources: [African Insider — Capitec 2026 bank fees](https://www.africaninsider.com/business/here-is-what-youll-pay-capitec-reveals-2026-bank-fees/),

## 2. Savings

- **Live Better savings** — linked savings with cash back and Interest Sweep; cash back is paid into
  Live Better savings, earns interest and **does not expire**
  ([Capitec — Live Better savings](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/rewards/lb-savings/)). [OBS]
- **Savings tools** — **Round-Up** (rounds up card spend into savings) and **Interest Sweep** (sweeps
  interest from the main account into Live Better savings)
  ([Capitec — Savings tools](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/rewards/save-better/)). [OBS]
- Tax-free savings accounts offered ([Capitec — Tax-free savings](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/personal/save/tax-free-savings/)). [OBS]

## 4. Business banking

- **Business Banking** grew gross loans 30% to R30,389m; CLR 2.4% ([CPIFY26 summary PDF](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/globalassets/pages/investor-relations/financial-results/2026/audited-results/cpify26_summary.pdf)). [OBS]
- **Merchant / card machines** — reduced commission and discounts saved business clients R213m FY2026. [OBS]
- **Entrepreneur Account** — launched Dec 2025; **zero monthly fee**, up to 4 accounts, cash-flow-based
  credit assessment for sole proprietors/informal enterprises ([CPIFY26 commentary](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/globalassets/pages/investor-relations/financial-results/2026/audited-results/cpify26_summary.pdf)). [OBS]

## 5. Insurance & value-added services

- **Life insurance licence** held in-group; Cell captive for other lines. Credit Life + Funeral & Life
  Cover contributed R5,224m net insurance result FY2026 (+38%) ([CPIFY26 summary PDF](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/globalassets/pages/investor-relations/financial-results/2026/audited-results/cpify26_summary.pdf)). [OBS]
- **VAS** (airtime, data, electricity, bill payments etc.) revenue R5,650m (+34%). [OBS]

## 6. Connectivity — Capitec Connect (MVNO)

- MVNO launched **September 2022** on the **Cell C** network; prepaid voice/data/SMS bundles that do
  **not expire**; 1.1m active clients by end-Aug 2025; ~1.5m 3-month-active subscribers FY2026
  ([Scrolla](https://scrolla.africa/capitec-rewards-connect-customers-with-a-year-of-free-data/),
  [Avante](https://avantebss.com/en/insights/blog/why-banks-launch-mvnos)). [OBS]
- FY2026 Capitec Connect net income **R442m** (+>100%) ([CPIFY26 summary PDF](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/globalassets/pages/investor-relations/financial-results/2026/audited-results/cpify26_summary.pdf)). [OBS]
- Sells Samsung devices via the Capitec app ([Scrolla Lite](https://lite.scrolla.africa/news/capitec-connect-now-sells-samsung-phones-through-its-app.html)). [OBS]
