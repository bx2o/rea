# Capitec — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

## 1. Positioning & message

Capitec's marketing is **fee-and-simplicity led**: transparent, low-cost banking, benefit-led messaging and a
consistent friendly tone aimed at broadening trust and recall
([Umbrex — Capitec strategy profile](https://umbrex.com/resources/company-profiles/capitec-ltd/),
[PortersFiveForce — Capitec marketing mix](https://portersfiveforce.com/products/capitecbank-marketing-mix)). [OBS]/[INF]

## 2. Sponsorship strategy — evidence check

> The brief hypothesised a "no-sponsorship strategy." **Public evidence contradicts a blanket
> no-sponsorship position.** Capitec does not historically run the large sport-property/stadium-naming
> sponsorships of the Big Four incumbents [INF], but it **does** sponsor selected cultural, community and
> educational properties. Findings:

| Property | Nature | Source | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Comic Con Africa / Comic Con Cape Town** | Three-year **headline sponsor** | [Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/comic-con-africa-names-capitec-as-new-headline-sponsor-285617a) | [OBS] |
| **Kota Festival Tour** | Official **banking sponsor** (2026) | [Capitec blog](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/blog/events/2026/kasi-culture-is-coming-to-you/) | [OBS] |
| **My Design Rules** | Sponsor of interior-design show | [Capitec blog](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/blog/articles/experiences/my-design-rules-sponsorship/) | [OBS] |
| **GRAD / GRADnext** | Sponsor (with StudyTrust, Van Schaik) | [Capitec](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/live-better/money-up/learning/grad/) | [OBS] |

**Conclusion [INF]**: Capitec's differentiation is built on low fees and the Live Better rewards
programme rather than on premium sport sponsorships; its sponsorships skew to inclusive, mass-market and
community properties rather than elite sport. This is a *positioning* choice, not an absolute ban on
sponsorship.

## 3. Owned campaigns & offerings driving acquisition

- **Live Better** rewards ecosystem — cash back and partner discounts (Cashbuild, Dis-Chem, Tiger Wheel &
  Tyre, Shell); presented as a loyalty/engagement engine rather than points
  ([Capitec partner rewards](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/live-better/spend-better/)). [OBS]
- **Live Better Day** — periodic partner reward events ([Capitec Rewards](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/rewards/)). [OBS]
- **Capitec Connect** — data-price-reduction messaging; gave clients R108m in rewards/data in FY2026 and
  saved R330m via data price reductions ([CPIFY26 summary PDF](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/globalassets/pages/investor-relations/financial-results/2026/audited-results/cpify26_summary.pdf)). [OBS]

## 4. Value-return narrative (FY2026)

Capitec returns value to clients framed as "lower fees, reduced pricing, cash back, rewards":
R1bn returned to clients in FY2026, incl. R228m fee savings, R107m credit-card cash back, R61m forex
savings, R213m merchant savings ([CPIFY26 commentary PDF](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/globalassets/pages/investor-relations/financial-results/2026/audited-results/cpify26_summary.pdf)). [OBS]
