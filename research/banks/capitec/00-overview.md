## 2. Founding & journey

- PSG acquired several micro-lending businesses (incl. Smartfin, Finai) from 1997; these seeded
  Capitec, which registered as a bank and received its licence on **1 March 2001**
  ([Our journey](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/about-us/our-journey/),
  [African Insider](https://www.africaninsider.com/business/missed-opportunity-what-r1000-invested-in-capitec-24-years-ago-is-worth-today/)). [OBS]
- Initial shareholding was structured **PSG 58% / management 22% / independent investors 20%**, meeting
  the then R250m minimum capital floor during the 1999–2002 "small banks crisis"
  ([Grokipedia — Capitec](https://grokipedia.com/page/Capitec_Bank)). [UNVERIFIED] (single secondary source)
- **Global One** launched 2003 (South Africa's first account with 4 free personalised savings pockets);
  biometric authentication introduced 2004; workplace banking 2006–2010
  ([Our journey](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/about-us/our-journey/)). [OBS]
- **Listed on the JSE on 18 February 2002**, nine days before the Saambou collapse
  ([Capitec: a company that thought differently](https://mahapa.substack.com/p/capitec-a-company-that-thought-differently),
  [African Insider](https://www.africaninsider.com/business/missed-opportunity-what-r1000-invested-in-capitec-24-years-ago-is-worth-today/)). [OBS]
- PSG **unbundled its Capitec stake entirely in November 2003** — a decision Jannie Mouton later
  described as a costly strategic error
  ([Billionaires.Africa](https://www.billionaires.africa/2026/09/01/south-african-billionaire-jannie-mouton-admits-the-capitec-mistake-that-cost-psg-1-4-billion/)). [OBS]
- Over five years to FY2026 the group diversified into **Business banking, VAS, Capitec Connect (MVNO),
  insurance (own life licence) and online consumer lending (AvaFin)** ([CPIFY26 summary](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/globalassets/pages/investor-relations/financial-results/2026/audited-results/cpify26_summary.pdf)). [OBS]
- The holding company **changed its name from Capitec Bank Holdings Limited to Capitec Limited**,
  effective 1 August 2026 and gazetted by the Prudential Authority on 2 October 2026
  ([Newsday](https://newsday.co.za/business/28443/south-africas-largest-bank-changes-its-official-name/),
  [IT News Africa](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/fintech_news/capitec-officially-becomes-capitec-limited/)). [OBS]

## 3. What Capitec is

Capitec positions itself as a **simplified, low-fee, high-volume retail bank** that has expanded into a
diversified financial-services group. Per FY2026, Personal Banking contributed ~41% of Group headline
earnings, with the balance from Business Banking, insurance and value-added services
([Capitec annual results blog](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/blog/news/2026/annual-results/)). [OBS]

## 4. Key findings (for the NeoBank programme)

1. **Scale**: Capitec's 25.2m+ active clients make it South Africa's largest retail bank by customer
   numbers ([Capitec annual results blog](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/blog/news/2026/annual-results/)). [OBS]
2. **Fee-led differentiation**: administration fee R7.50/month (2026), free or near-free transactions,
   zero forex margin on the card ([CPIFY26 summary](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/globalassets/pages/investor-relations/financial-results/2026/audited-results/cpify26_summary.pdf)). [OBS]
3. **Ecosystem, not just a bank**: MVNO (Capitec Connect on Cell C), insurance, VAS and lending broaden
   non-interest income to R28.3bn ([CPIFY26 summary](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/globalassets/pages/investor-relations/financial-results/2026/audited-results/cpify26_summary.pdf)). [OBS]
4. **Cloud-native engineering**: heavy AWS adoption (contact centre, app, Capitec Pay) — see
   `technology/50-tech-stack.md`. [OBS]
5. **Complaint profile improving but material**: industry complaints rising; Capitec's *share* of formal
   complaints fell from 21% to 16% YoY in 2025 Ombud data — see `customer/70-customer-service-complaints.md`. [OBS]
