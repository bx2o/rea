# 00 — Global Digital-Bank Peers (Revolut · Monzo · Nubank · Chime · N26 · Starling)

> Council C10 (Finance & IR) · Combined reference file (UnConsolidated; self-contained).
> Brief profiles of six leading digital banks / neobanks, focused on founding, financials,
> products, technology, marketing, people and regulation. Public information only.

---

## 1. Revolut (UK / global)

- **Founding/listing:** [OBS] Founded **2015 in London** by **Nikolay Storonsky & Vlad Yatsenko**;
  private (secondary-market valuation); received its **full UK banking licence from the PRA on 11
  March 2026** after a three-year process
  ([European Business Magazine](https://europeanbusinessmagazine.com/finance-revolut-uk-banking-licence-ipo-valuation-2026/)).
- **Financials:** [OBS] FY2024 revenue **$4.0B** (£3.1B, +72%); FY2025 revenue **$6.0B** (£4.5B,
  +46%), **profit before tax $2.3B** (£1.7B, PBT margin 38%), net profit £1.3B; **68.3M
  customers** (2025) ([Revolut 2025 results](https://www.revolut.com/news/revolut_reports_record_profit_of_2_3bn_for_2025_as_revenue_surges_to_6bn/);
  [binaryx](https://app.binaryx.com/equity/revolut)).
- **Products:** [OBS] Super-app — multi-currency accounts (30+ currencies), card, FX, crypto,
  stocks, insurance; full EU banking licence with IBAN ([freenance](https://freenance.io/rankings/best-neobanks-2026/)).
- **Technology:** [INF] Cloud-native super-app; multiple product lines.
- **Marketing:** [OBS] Customer-referral-led growth; no physical branches.
- **People:** [OBS] CEO/founder Nikolay Storonsky.
- **Regulation:** [OBS] UK banking licence (PRA, Mar 2026); EU banking licence for European IBANs;
  deposit protection per jurisdiction.

## 2. Monzo (UK)

- **Founding/listing:** [OBS] Founded **Feb 2015** (as "Mondo") in London by **Tom Blomfield,
  Jonas Huckestein, Jason Bates, Paul Rippon, Gary Dolman**; private; full UK banking licence
  ([Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Monzo)).
- **Financials:** [OBS] FY2025 revenue **£1.2B** (+48%), **adjusted PBT £113.9m** (8×), **12M+
  customers**, **£16.6bn deposits**, NPS **+70** ([Monzo Annual Report 2025](https://monzo.com/annual-report/2025)).
- **Products:** [OBS] Personal current accounts, savings pots, overdrafts, loans, business banking;
  FSCS-protected ([Money To The Masses](https://moneytothemasses.com/banking/revolut-vs-monzo-which-is-best)).
- **Technology:** [INF] Mobile-first, real-time notifications, budgeting; crowdfunded £1M in 96s (2016).
- **Marketing:** [OBS] Community/crowdfunding-led growth.
- **People:** [OBS] Founder Tom Blomfield; **TS Anil** was CEO for five years, stepped down late 2024
  ([Litmus](https://litmus.lapaas.com/learn/monzo-business-model)).
- **Regulation:** [OBS] Full UK banking licence; FCA + PRA; FSCS £85,000.

## 3. Nubank / Nu Holdings (Brazil → LatAm)

- **Founding/listing:** [OBS] Founded **2013 in São Paulo** by **David Vélez, Cristina Junqueira,
  Edward Wible**; **IPO Dec 2021 on NYSE ("NU")** at ~$45B valuation
  ([Nu About](https://international.nubank.com.br/about)).
- **Financials:** [OBS] FY2024 revenue **$11.5B** (+43.7%), net income **$1.9B** (+91.8%), **114M
  customers**; FY2025 revenue **$16.3B** (+45%) ([Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/nubank-statistics/);
  [Nu 4Q25 results](https://international.nubank.com.br/pt-br/companhia/nu-holdings-ltd-divulga-resultados-financeiros-do-quarto-trimestre-e-do-ano-de-2025/)).
- **Products:** [OBS] No-fee credit card (initial), accounts, lending, investing, insurance.
- **Technology:** [INF] Data/ML-driven, branchless.
- **Marketing:** [OBS] Brand-led, viral customer acquisition; Berkshire Hathaway invested ~$1B.
- **People:** [OBS] CEO David Vélez.
- **Regulation:** [OBS] Regulated financial institution in Brazil since 2016; **Nu Mexico bank
  licence approved Apr 2025** (CNBV), operations as a bank **July 2026**; Nu Colombia financing
  company approved Jan 2024; **applied for a US national bank charter** (Sept 2025)
  ([Nu Mexico](https://international.nubank.com.br/company/nu-mexico-receives-authorization-to-begin-operations-as-a-bank/);
  [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/dashboard-post/bank-regulation/2025/nubank-seeks-banking-license-brazil-comply-with-new-rule-branding/)).

## 4. Chime (US)

- **Founding/listing:** [OBS] US fintech founded 2012 (San Francisco); **IPO June 12, 2025 on
  Nasdaq ("CHYM")** at **$27.00/share**, raising **$864M**, valuing it at **~$11.6B** on a
  fully-diluted basis ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/chime/); [prospectus](http://pdf.secdatabase.com/234/0001628280-25-030855.pdf)).
- **Financials:** [OBS] FY2025 revenue **$2.2B** (+31%); **9.5M active members** (end 2025);
  **$127M adjusted EBITDA** ([Chime FY2025 results](https://chime.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/chime-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-financial)).
- **Products:** [OBS] Fee-free checking, spot-me overdraft, credit builder, high-yield savings.
- **Technology:** [INF] Mobile-first; revenue model = interchange + platform (OIT) revenue.
- **Marketing:** [OBS] Mass-market, "fee-free" positioning.
- **People:** [UNK] Founders Chris Britt & Ryan King (widely reported; not re-verified here).
- **Regulation:** [OBS] **Not a bank** — banking services via **The Bancorp Bank, N.A.** and
  **Stride Bank, N.A.** (Members FDIC) ([Chime disclosures](https://www.chime.com/disclosures/)).

## 5. N26 (Germany / EU)

- **Founding/listing:** [OBS] Founded **2013 in Berlin** by **Valentin Stalf & Maximilian
  Tayenthal**; private; **full German banking licence since July 2016** (BaFin)
  ([Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/N26); [N26](https://www.number26.eu/en-fr)).
- **Financials:** [OBS] 2025 revenue **>$500M** (€/$567M, +18%); **first full-year net profit
  (€1.6M)**; cumulative losses 2015–2024 **€1.025B** ([TabInsights](https://riskandregulation.tabinsights.com/article/-n26-s-first-full-year-profit-ends-its-11-year-start-up-phase);
  [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/n26/)).
- **Products:** [OBS] Mobile bank account, cards, savings; 24 European markets; ~8M+ customers
  ([N26](https://www.number26.eu/en-fr)).
- **Technology:** [INF] Minimalist mobile-first app; premium tiering.
- **Marketing:** [OBS] Premium everyday-banking positioning; Standard €0 plan
  ([freenance](https://freenance.io/fintech/neobanks-europe-comparison-2026/)).
- **People:** [OBS] Stalf stepped down as CEO Aug 2025; **Mike Dargan** became CEO April 2026
  ([Litmus](https://litmus.lapaas.com/learn/n26-business-model)).
- **Regulation:** [OBS] BaFin + Deutsche Bundesbank; deposit protection up to **€100,000**.

## 6. Starling Bank (UK)

- **Founding/listing:** [OBS] Founded **2014 by Anne Boden** (former Allied Irish Banks COO);
  private; **full UK banking licence since July 2016** ([Barcoder](https://barcoder.lumiverse.hr/docs/wallets/starling)).
- **Financials:** [OBS] FY2025 (to Mar 2025) revenue **£714M** (+4.7%), underlying **PBT £280.6M**
  (−7%), **4.6M accounts**, **£12.1bn deposits** ([Starling Annual Report 2025](https://www.starlingbank.com/docs/annual-reports/Starling-Bank-Annual-Report-2025.pdf)).
- **Products:** [OBS] Personal/business current accounts, lending; **Engine by Starling** BaaS SaaS
  platform ([Starling news](https://www.starlingbank.com/news/starling-reports-strong-revenue-and-invests-for-growth/)).
- **Technology:** [OBS] Cloud-native core; **Engine** licensed to other banks — **Salt Bank
  (Romania)**, **AMP Bank (Australia)**, **Cora Bank (Canada)**
  ([Starling news](https://www.starlingbank.com/news/starling-reports-strong-revenue-and-invests-for-growth/)).
- **Marketing:** [OBS] Disciplined/profitable challenger positioning.
- **People:** [OBS] Founder **Anne Boden**.
- **Regulation:** [OBS] FCA + PRA; FSCS **£85,000**.

## Key Findings (peer-level)

- [OBS] **Nubank** is the clear scale leader among pure neobanks (revenue $16.3B FY25; 114M+
  customers).
- [OBS] **Revolut** is the growth/profitability standout (FY25 PBT $2.3B, 68.3M customers).
- [OBS] **Starling** is the model for **B2B BaaS** (Engine) as a second revenue engine.
- [OBS] **Chime** and **Nubank** show the two US/LatAm structures: **BaaS partner model vs. own
  charter**; Nubank is now pursuing a **US national bank charter**.
- [OBS] **Monzo/N26/Starling** demonstrate the **UK/EU full-banking-licence** road and the long
  path to first profit (N26: €1.025B cumulative losses to first profit).
