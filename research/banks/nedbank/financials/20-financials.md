## 4. Analyst observations
- `[DER]` Nedbank has the **highest HEPS** of the non-Capitec Big Four (3,706c) but the **lowest total income** among the Big Four — i.e., it punches above its weight on per-share earnings while being sub-scale vs Standard Bank/Absa/FirstRand.
- `[INF]` The market prices Nedbank at a **premium P/E (15.53)** vs Absa (7.89) despite lower absolute income — suggesting investors value its higher ROE stability and cleaner SA-centric franchise, or discount Absa's Africa-region risk.
- `[OBS]` PwC noted industry combined headline earnings grew **9.3%** in H1 2026 YoY. ([BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/banking/876479/battle-of-the-banks-2026-capitec-vs-fnb-vs-standard-bank-vs-absa-vs-nedbank/))
