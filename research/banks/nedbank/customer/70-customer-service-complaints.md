## 2. Complaints & Ombudsman data
- **National Financial Ombud (NFO) — Banking Division:** record **15,412 complaints in 2024** (11,535 closed); **79%** of formal outcomes favoured banks. [OBS] ([Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/when-banks-get-it-wrong-ombud-cases-reveal-costly-mistakes/))
- Prior-year trend (Banking Ombudsman): **Nedbank had the second-highest number of complaints** but only a **+3.6%** increase; FNB +15.1%; Capitec +12.4%; Absa −8%; Standard Bank −5.3%. R25m returned to consumers. [OBS] ([Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/business/personal-finance/banking-ombudsman-r25-million-consumers/))
- **2025:** NFO returned **R60m+**; **mobile/internet banking fraud = 39%** of all cases (dominant category). [OBS] ([Primedia Plus](https://www.primediaplus.com/2025/12/17/financial-ombud-makes-banks-return-r60-million-to-clients-bulk-around-cases-of-fraud))
- `[INF]` Nedbank's high absolute complaint count (2nd of the Big Five) but modest growth rate (−/+3.6%) indicates a large customer-service workload that is neither rapidly improving nor deteriorating. Combined with the low 3.9/5 app rating, customer experience is likely a **relative weakness** vs Absa/FNB.

## 3. Self-service & complaints handling
- `[OBS]` Nedbank positions **Enbi** (chatbot) as an efficiency tool that "streamlines digital queries and complaints," distinguishing queries from formal complaints. ([Nedbank — Enbi](https://personal.nedbank.co.za/learn/blog/enbi-query-vs-complaint.html))
- `[OBS]` Nedbank claims "client satisfaction metrics remained at the top end of the peer group." ([Nedbank FY25 highlights](https://personal.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-highlights-nedbank.pdf)) — `[INF]` this claim sits in tension with the high ombud case volume and 3.9/5 app rating.
