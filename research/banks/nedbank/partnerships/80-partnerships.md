# Nedbank — Partnerships, M&A & Alliances

## 1. M&A — iKhokha (2025)
- `[OBS]` Nedbank entered a binding agreement to acquire **100% of fintech iKhokha** in an all-cash deal of **~R1.65bn** (announced 13 August 2025), to accelerate SME/merchant-payments growth. ([Nedbank press release](https://group.nedbank.co.za/news-and-insights/press/2025/nedbank-acquires-ikhokha.html), [Mergr](https://mergr.com/company/ikhokha/ownership))
- `[OBS]` South Africa's **Competition Commission recommended unconditional approval** (24 Oct 2025); deal valued ~US$93.3m. ([IntelliNews](https://www.intellinews.com/nedbank-gets-green-light-for-93mn-acquisition-of-south-african-fintech-ikhokha-408228/))
- `[OBS]` Investors Apis Partners, the IFC and Crossfin Holdings exited. ([Dealmakers SA](https://www.dealmakerssouthafrica.com/_files/ugd/cf215e_99d2f1cf1b674eec946f4cb79a6af6bc.pdf))
- `[OBS]` iKhokha adds **+351 employees** to the group. ([Nedbank FY25 analyst presentation](https://group.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-analyst-presentation-nedbank.pdf))

## 2. Geographic M&A reset
- `[OBS]` **Disposal of 21.2% stake in Ecobank Transnational Incorporated (ETI)** for US$100m (~R1.6bn) — completed **17 December 2025**; removes associate income from H2 2025 onwards. ([Nedbank FY25 transcript](https://group.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-presentation-transcript-nedbank.pdf), [S&P ratings](https://personal.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/11_credit-ratings/s-p/snp-nedbank-limited-ratings-direct-1-sep-2025.pdf)) — buyer reported as Bosquet Investments Limited. [UNVERIFIED]
- `[OBS]` **Offer to acquire ~66% of NCBA Group** (one of East Africa's leading financial services groups) announced January 2026 — offer of 80% in Nedbank shares (at R250) & 20% cash, R13.9bn total; subject to regulatory approvals. ([Nedbank FY25 transcript](https://group.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-presentation-transcript-nedbank.pdf), [Equity Axis](https://equityaxis.net/index.php/post/18842/2026/3/nedbank-hits-historic-8-million-client-milestone-in-2025-eyes-66-stake-in-ncba-group))
- `[OBS]` Strategic reset: focus on **SADC and East Africa** in "businesses we own and control." ([Nedbank interim 2025 booklet](https://group.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/interim-results/2025-interim-results-booklet-single-page-nedbank.pdf.pdf))
