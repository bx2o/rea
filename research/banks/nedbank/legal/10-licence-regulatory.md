# Nedbank — Licence & Regulatory Posture

## 1. Licence class & supervisors
- **Bank licence:** Nedbank Limited is a licensed financial services provider under the **Banks Act 94 of 1990**. [OBS] ([Nedbank PAIA manual](https://nedbank.co.za/content/dam/nedbank/site-assets/AboutUs/About%20Nedbank%20Group/Corporate%20Governance/Governance%20and%20Ethics/Access%20to%20information.pdf))
- **Prudential supervisor:** the **Prudential Authority (SARB)** under the Banks Act. [OBS] ([SARB PA functions](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/Prudentialregulation/functions-of-the-prudential-authority))
- **Market-conduct supervisor:** **FSCA** — Nedbank Limited is an authorised financial services provider, **FSP 9363**. [OBS] ([Law Insider — Nedbank Limited](https://www.lawinsider.com/dictionary/nedbank-limited))
- **Credit provider:** registered with the **NCR** — **NCRCP16**. [OBS] ([Nedbank PAIA manual](https://nedbank.co.za/content/dam/nedbank/site-assets/AboutUs/About%20Nedbank%20Group/Corporate%20Governance/Governance%20and%20Ethics/Access%20to%20information.pdf))

## 4. Anti-money-laundering context
- `[OBS]` South Africa was on the **FATF grey list** (dirty-money monitoring); outgoing Nedbank CEO Mike Brown expressed scepticism about a 2025 exit. This framed sector-wide AML remediation pressure. ([BizNews](https://www.biznews.com/tag/nedbank))
- `[OBS]` The SARB/PA has imposed administrative sanctions on multiple banks (Nedbank R35m; others incl. Sasfin, Standard Bank, Capitec, Absa) — an active enforcement cycle. ([SARB sanctions](https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/publications/media-releases/2022/pa-nedbank-sanctions/South%20African%20Reserve%20Bank%20imposes%20administrative%20sanctions%20on%20Nedbank%20Limited.pdf))

## 5. Compliance posture assessment
- `[INF]` Nedbank holds the full SA bank licence stack. Its **R35m FIC penalty (2022)** was materially larger than Absa's R10m (2025), reflecting the seriousness of AML data-quality findings, but it pre-dates the current CEO's tenure and the group reports no licence-level risk. POPIA exposure remains `[UNK]` publicly.
