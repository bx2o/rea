# Nedbank Group — Overview (Identity, Journey, Scale)

## 1. Identity
- **Legal names:** Nedbank Group Limited (holding company, Reg No **1966/010630/06**) and Nedbank Limited (Reg No **1951/000009/06**). [OBS] ([Nedbank PAIA](https://stg-apim.nedbank.africa/content/nedbank/za/en/personal/home/the-promotion-of-access-to-information-act.html), [Nedbank PAIA manual](https://nedbank.co.za/content/dam/nedbank/site-assets/AboutUs/About%20Nedbank%20Group/Corporate%20Governance/Governance%20and%20Ethics/Access%20to%20information.pdf))
- **Tickers / listings:** Primary listing on the **JSE (NED)**; **secondary listing on A2X Markets from 1 April 2022**. [OBS] ([Nedbank heritage](https://group.nedbank.co.za/home/heritage.html))
- **Headquarters:** 135 Rivonia Road, Sandton, Johannesburg, South Africa. [OBS] ([Nedbank PAIA](https://stg-apim.nedbank.africa/content/nedbank/za/en/personal/home/the-promotion-of-access-to-information-act.html), [Grokipedia — Nedbank](https://grokipedia.com/page/Nedbank))
- **Clusters (post-2025 reorganisation):** Nedbank Corporate & Investment Banking (CIB); Nedbank Personal & Private Banking (PPB); Nedbank Business & Commercial Banking (BCB); Nedbank Africa Regions (NAR); plus Nedbank Wealth. [OBS] ([Nedbank Group home](https://group.nedbank.co.za/home.html), [Nedbank FY25 results](https://group.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-booklet-double-page-nedbank.pdf))

## 2. Founding & Journey (timeline)
| Year | Event | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1831 | Heritage collective traces to the **Cape of Good Hope Savings Bank Society** — second-oldest financial institution in southern Africa. [OBS] ([Nedbank heritage](https://group.nedbank.co.za/home/heritage.html)) | [OBS] |
| 1888 | **Nederlandsche Bank en Credietvereeniging** founded in Amsterdam (6 April); first SA branch in Pretoria (1 Aug) with £50,000 capital. [OBS] ([Nedbank heritage](https://group.nedbank.co.za/home/heritage.html)) | [OBS] |
| 1969 | Syfrets SA listed on the JSE (heritage milestone). [OBS] ([Nedbank heritage](https://group.nedbank.co.za/home/heritage.html)) | [OBS] |
| 1971 | Renamed **Nedbank**. [OBS] ([MatrixBCG](https://matrixbcg.com/blogs/brief-history/nedbank)) | [OBS] |
| 1986 | **Old Mutual Plc became the major shareholder (53%)** of what was then Nedcor Limited. [OBS] ([Old Mutual](https://www.oldmutual.com/investor-relations/old-mutual-plc/nedbank-unbundling)) | [OBS] |
| 1988 | Nedbank merged with Perm → NedPerm Bank. [OBS] ([Heritage Portal](https://www.theheritageportal.co.za/article/nedbank-historical-collective-exploring-oldest-collective-financial-institution-south-africa)) | [OBS] |
| 1992 | Nedbank, Perm and Nedfin amalgamated into **Nedcor**. [OBS] ([Heritage Portal](https://www.theheritageportal.co.za/article/nedbank-historical-collective-exploring-oldest-collective-financial-institution-south-africa)) | [OBS] |
| 2003–2005 | Nedcor merged with BoE Bank, NIB, Cape of Good Hope Bank; holding company renamed **Nedbank Group Limited** (2005). [OBS] ([MatrixBCG](https://matrixbcg.com/blogs/brief-history/nedbank)) | [OBS] |
| 15 Oct 2018 | **Managed Separation:** Old Mutual unbundled its 52% stake (R43.2bn distribution), retaining **19.9%**. [OBS] ([Old Mutual plc](https://www.oldmutual.com/investor-relations/old-mutual-plc/)) | [OBS] |
| 2021 | Old Mutual reduced holding to ~**7%**. [OBS] ([MatrixBCG — owners](https://matrixbcg.com/blogs/owners/nedbank)) | [OBS] |
| 31 May 2024 | **Jason Quinn** became CEO, succeeding Mike Brown. [OBS] ([Grokipedia — Jason Quinn](https://grokipedia.com/page/jason_quinn_accountant)) | [OBS] |
| Dec 2025 / Jan 2026 | Disposed of 21.2% ETI stake ($100m); announced offer for ~66% of NCBA Group (East Africa). [OBS] ([Nedbank FY25 transcript](https://group.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-presentation-transcript-nedbank.pdf)) | [OBS] |

## 3. Scale (FY2025)
- **Total clients: 8.0 million** (2024: 7.5m) — first time in the group's ~140-year history. [OBS] ([Nedbank FY25 highlights](https://personal.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-highlights-nedbank.pdf))
- **Permanent employees: 25,795** (Dec 2025; +182 YoY, incl. +351 from iKhokha). [OBS] ([Nedbank Group home](https://group.nedbank.co.za/home.html), [FY25 analyst presentation](https://group.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-analyst-presentation-nedbank.pdf))
- **Total assets:** ~R1.3–1.5tn (reported "R1.3tn" for FY2024 basis; R1.494tn at June 2025 per third party). [OBS]/[UNVERIFIED] ([AskCyborg](https://askcyborg.com/preview/nedbank), [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Nedbank))
- **Network (FY2025):** 541 branches and 4,014 ATMs. [OBS] ([BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/banking/876479/battle-of-the-banks-2026-capitec-vs-fnb-vs-standard-bank-vs-absa-vs-nedbank/))
- **Market position:** 4th of the Big Five by total income (R75.1bn), **4th by market cap (R140.4bn)**, and the **2nd-most-expensive** on P/E (15.53). [OBS] ([BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/banking/876479/battle-of-the-banks-2026-capitec-vs-fnb-vs-standard-bank-vs-absa-vs-nedbank/))
- `[OBS]` Nedbank brand value rose **20% to R20bn** (FY2025). ([FY25 highlights](https://personal.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-highlights-nedbank.pdf))

## 4. Ownership
- **Old Mutual Limited:** reduced from 52% → 19.9% (unbundling, Oct 2018) → ~7% (2021) → **~3.9%** (per Nedbank's own website, cited 2025). [OBS forbids first two; last cited by third party] ([Old Mutual plc](https://www.oldmutual.com/investor-relations/old-mutual-plc/), [CoreGroup](https://coregroup.media/SocialMediaPosts/NewsArticles/South%20African%20Giant.pdf)) — the 3.9% figure is `[UNVERIFIED]` against a primary Nedbank filing.
- **Public Investment Corporation (PIC):** ~16.95% (Dec 2024) — largest single institutional holder. [OBS] ([Investing.com ownership](https://www.investing.com/equities/nedbank-group-ltd-(j)-ownership))
- Nedbank's market cap (R140.4bn, Sep 2026) is **more than double** Old Mutual's (~R57bn). [OBS] ([CoreGroup](https://coregroup.media/SocialMediaPosts/NewsArticles/South%20African%20Giant.pdf))

## 5. Strategy positioning
- Emphasis on a "client-centred organisational design" (2025 reorganisation) and a **"strategic portfolio tilt"** toward home loans, vehicle finance, overdrafts and retail deposits. [OBS] ([Nedbank FY25 highlights](https://personal.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-highlights-nedbank.pdf))
- Geographic reset: focus on **SADC & East Africa**; exited ETI; targeting ~66% of **NCBA Group**. [OBS] ([Nedbank FY25 transcript](https://group.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-presentation-transcript-nedbank.pdf))
