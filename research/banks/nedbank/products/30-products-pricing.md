## 3. Loans & credit
- **Personal loans:** R2,000 – R400,000; terms 6–72 months; fixed or variable rate; direct deposit on approval. [OBS] ([Finanso](https://finanso.com/za/loans/nedbank/))
- **Home loans, vehicle & asset finance, overdrafts:** under "strategic portfolio tilt" — market-share gains recorded; new home-loan payouts +20% (FY25). [OBS] ([Nedbank FY25](https://personal.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-highlights-nedbank.pdf))

## 4. Rewards — Greenbacks
- **Greenbacks** = Nedbank's loyalty/money-management programme; earn based on how you manage money; benefits at higher levels. [OBS] ([Credit card fee schedule](https://personal.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/nedbank/pdfs/nedbank-credit-cards-pricing-guide-2025.pdf))
- Earn mechanics: linked Visa/Mastercard/Amex cards; **American Express cards earn at a higher rate**; Greenbacks **never expire**, no cap on Amex spend. [OBS] ([Greenbacks T&Cs](https://design.nedbank.co.za/content/nedbank/za/en/personal/legal/terms-and-conditions/greenbacks-rewards-terms-and-conditions.html))
- Partner cashback: up to **30% cashback** at Checkers/Shoprite/UNIQ/LittleMe/Petshop Science (from Greenbacks Level 3, in-store, Amex card). [OBS] ([Nedbank Private Bundle](https://private-clients.nedbank.co.za/accounts/private-bundle.html))
- Qualifying behaviours include spending R5,000+/month, swiping ≥3 times, or maintaining a loan. [OBS] ([Greenbacks brochure](https://business.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/nedbank/pdfs/greenbacks-rewards-programme-brochure.pdf))

## 5. Marketplace — Avo
- **Avo (Avo SuperShop)** — launched 2020; e-commerce marketplace integrated into the Money app; 1% back in AvoPoints. [OBS] ([Nedbank/Avo](https://www.nedbankfreshstart.com/content/private-wealth-sa/south-africa/en/info/avo-unbeatable-deals-on-Apple-products.html), [Old Mutual MFC](https://oldmutualmfc.bflye.com/nedbank-reports-strong-growth-for-its-avo-supershop-platform/))
