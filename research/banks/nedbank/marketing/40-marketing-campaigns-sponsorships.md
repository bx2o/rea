- `[OBS]` An Africa Sports industry report maps Nedbank's sport sponsorships across **Soccer, Golf, Running, Cycling** (Nedbank Cup, Nedbank Golf Challenge, Nedbank Running Club, Nedbank Tour de...). ([ASCI SinN Report](https://africansci.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ASCI-SinN-Report.pdf))

> **Correction to brief assumption:** the brief linked "Cape Epic" to Nedbank. Evidence shows **Cape Epic is an ABSA property** (Absa Cape Epic). Nedbank's flagship golf event is the **Nedbank Golf Challenge** (Sun City). Nedbank's football property is the **Nedbank Cup**.

## 2. Marketing activation
- **Nedbank Cup competition:** a client-facing "JustSave" competition runs during the tournament (Jan–May 2025), promoting savings products alongside the football sponsorship. [OBS] ([Nedbank Cup 2025 T&Cs](https://personal.nedbank.co.za/legal/terms-and-conditions/just-save-nedbank-cup-competition.html))
- **Avo SuperShop** is integrated into the Money app as a marketing/engagement channel: registered users 2.7m (+19% YoY), GMV +80%. [OBS] ([Old Mutual MFC](https://oldmutualmfc.bflye.com/nedbank-reports-strong-growth-for-its-avo-supershop-platform/))
- `[OBS]` Nedbank brand value up **20% to R20bn** (FY2025). ([Nedbank FY25 highlights](https://personal.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-highlights-nedbank.pdf))
