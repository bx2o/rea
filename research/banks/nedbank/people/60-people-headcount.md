## 2. Headcount
- **Permanent employees: 25,795** (Dec 2025); up **182** YoY (incl. **+351** from the iKhokha acquisition). [OBS] ([Nedbank Group home](https://group.nedbank.co.za/home.html), [FY25 analyst presentation](https://group.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-analyst-presentation-nedbank.pdf))
- BusinessTech reports the same **25,795 group employees** (SA split not disclosed in results). [OBS] ([BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/banking/876479/battle-of-the-banks-2026-capitec-vs-fnb-vs-standard-bank-vs-absa-vs-nedbank/))
- `[UNVERIFIED]` A third-party headcount tracker (Revelio) gives ~29,069 (2025) / 29,175 (2026), which likely includes contractors/total workforce (not just permanent). ([Revelio Labs](https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/nedbank-group/employees))

## 3. Restructuring & cost
- `[OBS]` "Over the past five years, **Nedbank has cut its workforce by over 5,000 employees**" (sector context; Absa and Standard Bank shed ~1,200 each). ([TITC](https://titc.io/fintech-jobs-already-being-cut/))
- `[OBS]` Cost-to-income rose to **57.8%** (FY2025) — the highest of the Big Four — as expense growth outstripped modest revenue growth. ([Nedbank FY25 booklet](https://group.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-booklet-nedbank.pdf))
- `[OBS]` Completed a "strategic reorganisation" into a client-centred design (2025). ([Nedbank interim 2025](https://group.nedbank.co.za/news-and-insights/press/2025/nedbank-group-interim-results-2025.html))

## 4. Employment equity / B-BBEE
- **Level 1 B-BBEE status maintained for the 8th consecutive year** (FY2025). [OBS] ([Nedbank FY25 transcript](https://group.nedbank.co.za/content/dam/group/pdf/20_financial-results/2025/annual-results/2025-annual-results-presentation-transcript-nedbank.pdf))
- `[OBS]` Improvements in African, Coloured and Indian employee representation; **+3%** African talent representation at senior/middle management. (same)
- `[OBS]` Provided **3,800+ YES (Youth Employment Service) opportunities** in 2025; cumulative **>17,000 since 2019**. (same)
