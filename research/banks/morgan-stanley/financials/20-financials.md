# Morgan Stanley — Financials

## 1. Firm-Level Results

| Metric | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Net revenues | $53.7B | $54.1B | **$61.8B** | $70.6B* |
| Net income (applicable to MS) | — | $9.1B | **$13.4B** | — |
| Diluted EPS | — | $5.18 | **$7.95** | — |

\* FY2025 figure from an aggregator ([puffling](https://puffling.co/companies/ms/)) — **[UNVERIFIED]**.

- [OBS] FY2024 net revenues were a **record $61.8B** vs **$54.1B** a year earlier; net income
  applicable to Morgan Stanley **$13.4B**, or **$7.95** per diluted share (vs $9.1B / $5.18 in 2023)
  ([4Q2024 earnings release](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/4q2024.pdf)).
- [OBS] Q3 2024: net revenues **$15.4B**, EPS **$1.88**, ROTCE **17.5%**
  ([Q3 2024 earnings release](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/3q2024.pdf)).
- [OBS] Revenue trajectory 2021–2025: $59.8B → $53.7B → $54.1B → $61.8B → $70.6B (2025 aggregator)
  ([puffling](https://puffling.co/companies/ms/)).

## 2. Segments (net revenues)

- [OBS] Three segments: **Institutional Securities**, **Wealth Management**, **Investment Management**
  ([4Q2024 earnings release](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/4q2024.pdf)).
- [OBS] Q2 2024 segment net revenues: Institutional Securities **$6,982M**; Wealth Management
  **$6,792M**; Investment Management **$1,433M** (approx.)
  ([2Q2024 financial supplement](https://www.morganstanley.com/about-us-ir/finsup2q2024/finsup2q2024.pdf)).
- [OBS] Wealth Management represented ~**58% of firm revenue** in 2023
  ([meritshot case study](https://www.meritshot.com/case-studies/morgan-stanley-case-study)) —
  secondary, [UNVERIFIED].
- [OBS] Investment banking (Q4 2024): **$1,791M** vs $1,590M (Q4 2023), +13%
  ([4Q2024 earnings release](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/4q2024.pdf)).

## 3. Assets & Scale

- [OBS] Total client assets across Wealth + Investment Management grew to **$7.9T** as of Q4 2024
  ([dcfmodeling VRIO](https://dcfmodeling.com/products/ms-vrio-analysis)).
- [OBS] Wealth Management AUM ~**$5.7T** (2023)
  ([meritshot](https://www.meritshot.com/case-studies/morgan-stanley-case-study)).

## 4. Expenses

- [OBS] Information processing & communications: $4,088M (2024) vs $3,775M (2023), +8%
  ([4Q2024 earnings release](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/4q2024.pdf)).
- [OBS] Distribution expenses rose with higher average AUM ([Q3 2024 release](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/3q2024.pdf)).
