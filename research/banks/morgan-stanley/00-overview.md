# Morgan Stanley — Overview

> Council C10 (Finance & IR) · Global reference institution (wealth-management leader).

## 1. Identity & Listing

- [OBS] **Founded 1935** — Morgan Stanley opened for business on **September 16, 1935**, at 2 Wall
  Street with six officers and a staff of **13**, created when **J.P. Morgan spun off its securities
  underwriting division** following the **Glass–Steagall Act** (1933)
  ([Our History — Morgan Stanley](https://www.morganstanley.com/about-us/history);
  [Morgan Stanley MUFG history](https://www.morganstanleymufg.com/en/about-us/history)).
- [OBS] Founded by **Henry S. Morgan and Harold Stanley**
  ([dcf-analysis history](https://dcf-analysis.com/blogs/history/ms)).
- [OBS] Obtained **NYSE membership in 1941**; **publicly listed on the NYSE in 1986** (ticker **MS**)
  ([Morgan Stanley MUFG history](https://www.morganstanleymufg.com/en/about-us/history)).
- [OBS] HQ: New York City; offices in **42 countries**
  ([4Q2024 earnings release](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/3q2024.pdf)).
- [OBS] On **September 21, 2008** the Federal Reserve approved Morgan Stanley's application to
  **become a bank holding company**
  ([Federal Reserve press release](https://federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20080921a.htm)).

## 2. Business Structure

- [OBS] Three reportable segments: **Institutional Securities**, **Wealth Management**,
  **Investment Management** ([4Q2024 earnings release](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/4q2024.pdf)).
- [INF] Under James Gorman the firm repositioned toward **wealth management** as the stabilising,
  fee-based engine — a strategic pivot that reshaped its revenue mix.

## 3. Key Anchors

- [OBS] FY2024 record net revenues **$61.8B**, net income **$13.4B**, diluted EPS **$7.95**
  ([4Q2024 earnings release](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/4q2024.pdf)).
- [OBS] Wealth Management scale ~**$5.7T** AUM (2023) and firm client assets ~**$7.9T** (Wealth +
  Investment Management, Q4 2024) ([meritshot](https://www.meritshot.com/case-studies/morgan-stanley-case-study);
  [dcfmodeling VRIO](https://dcfmodeling.com/products/ms-vrio-analysis)).
- [OBS] Employees ~**80,478** (Q4 2024) ([4Q2024 earnings release](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/4q2024.pdf)).

## 4. Acquisitions that Shaped the Firm

- [OBS] **E*TRADE** — announced **Feb 20, 2020**, ~**$13B** all-stock; closed **Oct 2, 2020**
  ([E*TRADE press release](https://cdn2.etrade.net/1/08142023/aempros/content/dam/etrade/about-us/en_US/documents/newsroom/press-releases/2020/4682042639390047.pdf);
  [Strategic Update](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/4q2022-strategic-update.pdf)).
- [OBS] **Eaton Vance** — closed **Mar 1, 2021** ([Strategic Update](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/4q2022-strategic-update.pdf)).

## 5. People

- [OBS] **Ted Pick** — CEO from **January 2024**, Chairman from **January 2025**.
  **James P. Gorman** — CEO 2010–2023, Chairman 2012–2023, Executive Chairman 2024, Chairman
  Emeritus from January 2025 ([Our History](https://ourhistory.morganstanley.com/timeline/era-5);
  [Gorman bio](https://www.morganstanley.com/about-us-governance/operating-committee/james-gorman)).
