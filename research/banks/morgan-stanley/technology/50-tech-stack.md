# Morgan Stanley — Technology Stack

## 1. AI-First Strategy

- [OBS] Morgan Stanley became **OpenAI's sole wealth-management strategic partner** in **March
  2023** — four months after ChatGPT's release
  ([Observer](https://observer.com/2024/06/morgan-stanley-wall-street-generative-ai/)).
- [OBS] Launched the **"AI @ Morgan Stanley Assistant"** (September 2023), used by **98% of the
  firm's financial-advisor teams** ([Observer](https://observer.com/2024/06/morgan-stanley-wall-street-generative-ai/)).
- [OBS] **Debrief** saves advisors ~**half an hour of grunt work per meeting**
  ([Observer](https://observer.com/2024/06/morgan-stanley-wall-street-generative-ai/)).
- [OBS] A dedicated **AI team** implements an aligned AI strategy and coordinates initiatives
  ([AI @ Morgan Stanley deck](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/im/staticContent/investment-ideas/ideas-paris-2025/pdf/jeff-mcmillan-ideas-paris-2025.pdf)).

## 2. Engineering & Developer Transformation

- [OBS] Morgan Stanley employs **~15,000–16,000 developers**
  ([Business Insider Africa](https://africa.businessinsider.com/news/how-banks-like-morgan-stanley-and-citi-are-upskilling-their-engineers-for-the-ai-era/eqk3lxe);
  [dcf-model](https://dcf-model.com/products/ms-porters-five-forces-analysis)).
- [OBS] **DevGen.AI** modernised **16 million lines of legacy code** by Feb 2026; the firm tested
  **550+ internally developed and patented innovations** in a sandbox
  ([dcf-model](https://dcf-model.com/products/ms-porters-five-forces-analysis)).
- [OBS] By June 2026, **98.0% of employees** had access to at least one generative-AI tool
  ([dcf-model](https://dcf-model.com/products/ms-porters-five-forces-analysis)).
- [INF] Aggregator-sourced figures (above two bullets) are third-party; treat as **partial**.

## 3. Adoption Model

  ([Business Insider Africa](https://africa.businessinsider.com/news/how-banks-like-morgan-stanley-and-citi-are-upskilling-their-engineers-for-the-ai-era/eqk3lxe)).
