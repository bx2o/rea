# Morgan Stanley — People & Headcount

## 1. Leadership & Succession

- [OBS] **Ted Pick** — CEO effective **January 1, 2024**; Chairman from **January 2025**.
  Previously Co-President, Co-Head of Firm Strategy and Head of the Institutional Securities Group
  ([Our History — era 5](https://ourhistory.morganstanley.com/timeline/era-5);
  [2024 Proxy Statement](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-2024ams/2024_Proxy_Statement.pdf)).
- [OBS] **James P. Gorman** — CEO **January 2010–December 2023**; Chairman **January 2012–December
  2023**; Executive Chairman Jan–Dec 2024; **Chairman Emeritus from January 2025**. Joined the
  firm February 2006 ([Gorman bio](https://www.morganstanley.com/about-us-governance/operating-committee/james-gorman)).
- [OBS] The Board ran an "intentional, multi-year, methodical succession process" resulting in
  Pick's appointment ([2024 Proxy Statement](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-2024ams/2024_Proxy_Statement.pdf)).
