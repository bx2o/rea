# Morgan Stanley — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

## 1. E*TRADE Brand Campaigns

- [OBS] **E*TRADE** (Morgan Stanley) runs **Super Bowl ("The Big Game")** spots featuring the
  iconic **E*TRADE Baby** — e.g. a pickleball-themed 30-second spot
  ([Morgan Stanley press release](https://www.morganstanley.com/press-releases/get-in-the-game-with-e-trade-from-morgan-stanley)).
- [OBS] E*TRADE ran a **walrus-themed ad during NCAA March Madness (March 2025)** explicitly
  promoting its connection to parent **Morgan Stanley**
  ([Ad Age](https://adage.com/article/marketing-news-strategy/e-trade-march-madness-ad-connection-morgan-stanley/2606526/)).
