# Morgan Stanley — Licence & Regulatory

## 1. Bank Holding Company Status

- [OBS] On **September 21, 2008** the **Federal Reserve Board** approved Morgan Stanley's
  application to **become a bank holding company** (pending a statutory five-day antitrust waiting
  period). This granted access to the Fed's discount window and deposit-taking capacity
  ([Federal Reserve press release](https://federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20080921a.htm);
  [Morgan Stanley history](https://ourhistory.morganstanley.com/timeline/era-5)).
- [OBS] Morgan Stanley subsequently operated as a **financial holding company** under Fed
  consolidated supervision.
  ([Morgan Stanley history](https://ourhistory.morganstanley.com/timeline/era-5))

## 2. G-SIB & Prudential Regime

- [OBS] Morgan Stanley is designated a **Global Systemically Important Bank (G-SIB)**, triggering
  enhanced capital surcharges, stricter liquidity standards, resolution-planning obligations and
  the Fed's annual supervisory stress test
  ([Federal Reserve explainer](https://www.financialregulationcourses.com/dictionary/f/federal-reserve)).
- [OBS] As of early 2026 the eight US G-SIBs are JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Citigroup,
  Goldman Sachs, **Morgan Stanley**, Wells Fargo, BNY Mellon and State Street
  ([Legal Clarity — G-SIB review](https://legalclarity.org/too-big-to-fail-summary-systemic-risk-and-reform/)).
- [INF] The G-SIB surcharge is recalibrated annually on size, interconnectedness, substitutability,
  complexity and cross-jurisdictional activity.

## 3. Securities Regulation
