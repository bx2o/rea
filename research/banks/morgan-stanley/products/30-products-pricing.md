# Morgan Stanley — Products & Services

## 1. Segment Product Map

| Segment | Core products |
|---|---|
| **Institutional Securities** | Investment banking (M&A advisory, equity/debt underwriting), Sales & Trading (equities, fixed income), Global Capital Markets, Research |
| **Wealth Management** | Full-service brokerage/advisory, **E*TRADE** self-directed, workplace/401(k) solutions, family-office services, lending |
| **Investment Management** | Active asset management, Eaton Vance, Parametric (custom indexing) |

([4Q2024 earnings release](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/about-us-ir/shareholder/4q2024.pdf);
[WM Pitchbook](https://advisor.morganstanley.com/the-cornerstone-group-milwaukee/documents/field/c/co/cornerstone-group-milwaukee/WM_Pitchbook_2_2026.pdf))

## 2. E*TRADE (self-directed brokerage)

- [OBS] E*TRADE — a pioneer in **online brokerage** — was acquired by Morgan Stanley in an
  all-stock deal valued at ~**$13B** (announced Feb 20, 2020; closed Oct 2, 2020)
  ([E*TRADE press release](https://cdn2.etrade.net/1/08142023/aempros/content/dam/etrade/about-us/en_US/documents/newsroom/press-releases/2020/4682042639390047.pdf)).
- [OBS] Clients gain access to E*TRADE's **self-directed platform** alongside Morgan Stanley advice
  ([WM Pitchbook](https://advisor.morganstanley.com/the-cornerstone-group-milwaukee/documents/field/c/co/cornerstone-group-milwaukee/WM_Pitchbook_2_2026.pdf)).
- [OBS] E*TRADE added ~**5.2M retail clients** to Morgan Stanley
  ([top10grid](https://top10grid.com/top-10-us-wealth-management-firms)) — secondary, [UNVERIFIED].

## 3. Wealth Management Scale

- [OBS] **~16,000 financial advisors** serving HNW/UHNW clients
  ([marginofinsight](https://app.marginofinsight.com/stocks/ms/financials)).
- [OBS] **$2.6T+** in investment advisory client assets; **$7.0T+** in client assets under
  management ([WM Pitchbook](https://advisor.morganstanley.com/the-cornerstone-group-milwaukee/documents/field/c/co/cornerstone-group-milwaukee/WM_Pitchbook_2_2026.pdf)).
- [OBS] **Workplace solutions**: employer-sponsored 401(k), equity-compensation administration
  (Solium platform); **family-office services** for multi-generational planning; **Asia private
  wealth** for UHNW ex-Japan ([marginofinsight](https://app.marginofinsight.com/stocks/ms/financials)).
