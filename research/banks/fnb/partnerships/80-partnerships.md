# FNB — Partnerships

> Commercial/technology/affinity partnerships evidenced publicly. Sponsorships are covered in [../marketing/40-marketing-campaigns-sponsorships.md](../marketing/40-marketing-campaigns-sponsorships.md).

## 1. Technology & product partnerships

- **VALR (crypto)** — FNB embedded crypto buying/selling/sending in its app for ~9 m customers, powered by **VALR** (FSCA licence 53308); 5 major assets from R10 (announced **6 Oct 2026**). `[OBS]` [Cryptonomist](https://ko.cryptonomist.ch/2026/10/07/fnb%EB%8A%94-%EB%82%A8%EC%95%84%ED%94%84%EB%A6%AC%EC%B9%B4%EA%B3%B5%ED%99%94%EA%B5%AD%EC%97%90%EC%84%9C-%EC%95%BD-900%EB%A7%8C-%EB%AA%85%EC%9D%98-%EA%B3%A0%EA%B0%9D%EC%97%90%EA%B2%8C-%EC%95%94/)
- **FNB Connect (MVNO)** — network partner **Cell C** (2015 launch) and **MTN** (added 2023). `[OBS]` [Touchline News](https://touchlinenews.com/absa-joins-telecom-race-as-final-big-four-bank-to-launch-mobile-network-service/)
- **eWallet on WhatsApp** — messaging-platform distribution partnership (March 2026). `[OBS]` [The Vibe ZA](https://thevibeza.com/fnb-ewallet-whatsapp-south-africa/)

## 2. Sports / brand partnerships

- **SA Rugby / Springboks** — principal partner of all national teams since March 2025. `[OBS]` [SA Rugby](https://www.springboks.rugby/news-features/articles/2025/03/26/sa-rugby-broadens-and-deepens-relationship-with-fnb/)
- **FNB Stadium** — naming-rights partner (Stadium Management South Africa / City of Johannesburg). `[OBS]` [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/FNB_Stadium)
- **Cricket Namibia** — associate sponsor of the national men's team. `[OBS]` [New Era](https://neweralive.na/cn-joins-forces-with-fnb/)

## 3. Distribution partnerships

- **FNB CashPlus / AgencyPlus agents** — 5,089 agents (FY2025, up 17%) providing deposit/withdrawal access, mainly in broader Africa. `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY25 booklet](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-analysis-of-financial-results-June-2025-single-page-printable-booklet.pdf)
- **Cash@Till / retailer cash access** — withdrawal at participating retailers (e.g. Pick n Pay). `[OBS]` [FNB eWallet](https://www.online.fnb.co.za/send-money/eWallet.html)

## 4. Merchant acquiring & card schemes

- FNB acts as a **card acquirer** (1,112 m acquiring transactions FY2025) and card issuer (1,300 m). `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY25 booklet](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-analysis-of-financial-results-June-2025-single-page-printable-booklet.pdf)
- `[INF]` Card issuance operates on global schemes (Visa/Mastercard) via FirstRand Bank's scheme memberships — scheme not explicitly restated per product in reviewed sources.
