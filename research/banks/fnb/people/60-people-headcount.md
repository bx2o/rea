# FNB — People, Leadership & Headcount

## 2. Leadership

- **FirstRand Group CEO: Mary Vilakazi** — CEO since **April 2024**; the **first woman and first Black woman** to lead the group. `[OBS]` [Grokipedia: Mary Vilakazi](https://grokipedia.com/page/mary_vilakazi)
- **FNB CEO: Harry Kellan** — set to step down at **end-2026**. `[OBS]` [Empire Magazine Africa](https://empiremagazineafrica.com/firstrand-restructures-banking-operations-appoints-new-fnb-ceo/)
- **Incoming FNB CEO: Lytania Johnson** — appointed effective **April 2026** (previously led the personal banking segment). `[UNVERIFIED]` (reported via Reuters/BusinessTech, single secondary source) [Empire Magazine Africa](https://empiremagazineafrica.com/firstrand-restructures-banking-operations-appoints-new-fnb-ceo/)
- **2026 restructuring:** FirstRand replaced its retail/commercial segment model with a **Retail and Business Banking (RBB)** division. `[OBS]` [Empire Magazine Africa](https://empiremagazineafrica.com/firstrand-restructures-banking-operations-appoints-new-fnb-ceo/)
- Historical founders: **Laurie Dippenaar, GT Ferreira, Paul Harris** (RCI, 1977). `[OBS]` [Daily Investor](https://dailyinvestor.com/banking/128042/three-men-turned-r10000-into-south-africas-most-valuable-bank/)

## 3. Transformation / B-BBEE

- **FirstRand holds B-BBEE Level 1** under the Financial Sector Code — achieved for **eight consecutive financial years** (as of the 2025 transformation report). `[OBS]` [FirstRand Transformation & EE Report 2025](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/policies-and-practice/pdf/FirstRand_transformation_and_employment_equity_report_2025.pdf)
- Scorecard achieved **score 124**; **Black Ownership 30.1%**, Black Female Ownership 13.96%. `[OBS]` [FNB presentation to DTIC/Standing Committee (Feb 2025)](https://www.thedtic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/FNB.pdf), [FirstRand B-BBEE certificate](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/governance/2021_B-BBEE_certificate.pdf)
- **Senior management 50% Black (23% Black women)**. `[OBS]` [FNB DTIC presentation](https://www.thedtic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/FNB.pdf)

## 4. Culture / employer brand

- FirstRand frames its culture as "uniquely empowering" and credits it with enabling founder success. `[OBS]` [RMB "Our story"](https://www.rmb.co.uk/page/our-story)
