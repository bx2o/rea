# FNB — Products & Pricing

> Fees are **VAT-inclusive** where the source states so; FNB pricing guides run **1 July – 30 June**. Values are as published in FNB/FirstRand documents unless a third-party aggregator is cited (marked). Always confirm at point of sale — fees change annually.

## 2. Family Banking

- FNB offers a **Family Banking** proposition: **zero monthly fees for children's youth accounts** linked to a parent profile, and **50% off the spouse/partner's** monthly account fee on qualifying accounts; family members are added via the FNB App. `[OBS]` [FNB Family Banking](https://www.online.fnbswaziland.co.sz/family-banking/), [FNB Aspire guide](https://www.fnb.co.za/downloads/pricing-guides/FNB-Aspire-Account.pdf)

## 3. Payments & money movement

- **eWallet** — send money to any SA cellphone number without a bank account; **flat R10** from Easy Zero/Easy PAYU; send up to **R15,000** via the App. `[OBS]` [FNB eWallet](https://www.online.fnb.co.za/send-money/eWallet.html)
- **eWallet on WhatsApp** launched **11 March 2026** — allow the unbanked to create and use an eWallet via WhatsApp. `[OBS]` [The Vibe ZA](https://thevibeza.com/fnb-ewallet-whatsapp-south-africa/)
- **PayShap** — instant A2A rail supported by FNB. `[OBS]` [Payment Brief — SA payments](https://paymentbrief.com/markets/south-africa/)
- **FNB Virtual Card** — dynamic CVV, supported in Apple Pay/Google Pay/Samsung Pay; usable for eCommerce and QR ("Scan to Pay"). `[OBS]` [FNB Virtual Card FAQs](https://www.fnb.co.za/downloads/ways-to-bank/FNBVirtualCardFAQs.pdf)
  - Virtual-card spend reached **R101 bn** in FY2026 (+47% p.a. per The Vibe). `[OBS]` [The Condia](https://thecondia.com/fnb-digital-banking-changing-how-south-africans-bank/), [The Vibe ZA](https://thevibeza.com/fnb-virtual-card-spending-growth/)

## 4. Loyalty — eBucks

- **eBucks** is FNB's rewards programme, integrated into the FNB App and core transactional base. `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY25 media release](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-results-media-release-june-2025.pdf)
- Earn mechanics: shop at **eBucks partners** (e.g. **Pick n Pay**, **Clicks**, **Engen**) and via qualifying FNB Insurance/Connect products; reward level determines rate (e.g. **up to 15% back at Clicks** with a qualifying virtual card). `[OBS]` [eBucks Clicks partner page](https://www.ebucks.com/web/eBucks/partners/clicks.jsp), [eBucks 2025 earn rules](https://www.ebucks.com/web/eBucks/earn/fnb-private-clients-2025.jsp)
- Business eBucks: up to **R6/litre** fuel back at Engen; 40% back on hotdesks at Workshop17. `[OBS]` [eBucks business guide](https://www.ebucks.com/web/ebucks/binaries/content/assets/ebucks/frg-earn-rules/2025-2026/earn-guides/business/ebucks_summary_guide_2025_business_private_healthcare.pdf)

## 5. Value-added services

- **FNB Connect** — SA's first bank MVNO (launched 2015 on Cell C; added MTN in 2023); **>1 m active subscribers (Nov 2025)**; unit serves **~3 m customers**, revenue **~R3 bn** (+14%). `[OBS]` [Touchline News](https://touchlinenews.com/absa-joins-telecom-race-as-final-big-four-bank-to-launch-mobile-network-service/), [HeadTopics](https://za.headtopics.com/news/how-mobile-services-are-gaining-traction-at-fnb-88286000)
- **FNB crypto trading with VALR** — 5 major crypto assets, from R10, inside the FNB app (announced Oct 2026). `[OBS]` [Cryptonomist](https://ko.cryptonomist.ch/2026/10/07/fnb%EB%8A%94-%EB%82%A8%EC%95%84%ED%94%84%EB%A6%AC%EC%B9%B4%EA%B3%B5%ED%99%94%EA%B5%AD%EC%97%90%EC%84%9C-%EC%95%BD-900%EB%A7%8C-%EB%AA%85%EC%9D%98-%EA%B3%A0%EA%B0%9D%EC%97%90%EA%B2%8C-%EC%95%94/)
