# FNB — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

> Public marketing/sponsorship footprint. Sponsorship values are frequently undisclosed; unconfirmed figures are flagged.

## 1. Brand positioning

- FNB's brand tagline/positioning around **"Help is at the heart of human greatness"**, framed on trust, innovation and doing good. `[OBS]` [Brand Africa 100 — FNB profile](https://brand.africa/documents/reports/BrandAfrica100-2025-South-Africa.pdf)
- FNB markets itself as an **award-winning digital bank**: its app has been named **South Africa's Best Consumer Digital Bank and Best Mobile Banking App** for multiple consecutive years (its own claim on the app store listing). `[OBS]` [Google Play — FNB Banking App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=za.co.fnb.connect.itt)

## 2. Major sponsorships

### 2.1 FNB Stadium (soccer)

- The stadium has been **officially named "FNB Stadium" since it opened in 1989** under a naming-rights deal with FNB; it became **"Soccer City"** only during FIFA World Cup 2010 (FIFA clean-stadium rules). `[OBS]` [Grokipedia — FNB Stadium](https://grokipedia.com/page/FNB_Stadium)
- A **renewed 5-year naming-rights deal (2017–2022)** was agreed at ~**R14 m/year**, making FNB "South Africa's longest continuous stadium naming sponsor". `[OBS]` [Grokipedia — FNB Stadium](https://grokipedia.com/page/FNB_Stadium)
- FNB also spent ~**US$30 m** on the 2010 World Cup as a FIFA partner. `[OBS]` [PanZimbabwe](https://www.pazimbabwe.com/sports-55423-a-tale-of-two-giant-stadiums.html)

### 2.2 SA Rugby / Springboks

- FNB has been an **SA Rugby partner since 2017**; in **March 2025** it became the **principal partner of all SA Rugby national teams**, with the FNB logo moving to the **front of the Springbok jersey** (and men's/women's sevens and age-group teams). `[OBS]` [SA Rugby](https://www.springboks.rugby/news-features/articles/2025/03/26/sa-rugby-broadens-and-deepens-relationship-with-fnb/), [Media Update](https://www.mediaupdate.co.za/marketing/157790/fnb-announces-principal-sponsorship-of-sa-rugby)
- Reported (not officially confirmed) value: **~R150 m/year**. `[UNVERIFIED]` [Planet Rugby](https://www.planetrugby.com/news/new-springboks-sponsors-unveiled-as-local-powerhouses-outbid-overseas-investors)

## 3. Note on cricket (Proteas)

- The task brief suggested FNB sponsors the Proteas. Research indicates the **Proteas' banking sponsor historically was Standard Bank** (1998–2011, 2016–2020), not FNB. `[OBS]` [ESPNcricinfo](https://www.espncricinfo.com/story/south-africa-lose-title-sponsor-as-freefall-continues-1209203)
- FNB does sponsor **Cricket Namibia** (associate sponsorship of the national men's team) at a regional level, and **FNB Wanderers** in domestic cricket. `[OBS]` [New Era](https://neweralive.na/cn-joins-forces-with-fnb/)
- Note: Senior Proteas shirt sponsors as of 2026 are Jonsson Workwear (Test) and others — **not FNB**. `[OBS]` [Cricket South Africa](https://cricket.co.za/news/)

## 4. Campaigns & channels

- **eBucks** is used as a retention/acquisition engine tied to FNB card and insurance usage. `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY25 media release](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-results-media-release-june-2025.pdf)
- **FNB Connect / eWallet** marketed as ecosystem "value-added services" driving non-interest revenue. `[OBS]` same source.
