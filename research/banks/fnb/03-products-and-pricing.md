## Pricing findings summary

1. FNB bundles banking + telco + rewards + insurance to maximise switching cost. SEEN/high
2. eBucks is priced as a merchant-funded discount engine, not a cash cost. SEEN/medium
3. Fee transparency remains a weak point across the tiered ladder. INFERRED/medium
