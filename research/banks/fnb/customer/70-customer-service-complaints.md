# FNB — Customer Service & Complaints

> Complaint data from the **National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFO) – Banking Division** (formerly Ombudsman for Banking Services), plus app-store sentiment.

## 1. Ombud complaint share (2025)

- Complaints are concentrated in SA's "big five" banks, which together accounted for **>80%** of formal banking complaints. `[OBS]` [Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/banking-complaints-surge-as-fraudsters-follow-customers-online/)
- **Bank share of formal complaints (2025):** Standard Bank **20.23%**, Absa 17.06%, Capitec 16.05%, Nedbank 15.47%, **FNB 14.88%**. `[OBS]` [Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/banking-complaints-surge-as-fraudsters-follow-customers-online/)
- In a prior reporting cycle, **FNB had 1,017 cases opened (17% of all cases)** — second only to Capitec (1,203 / 20%). `[OBS]` [Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/fraud-floods-complaints-as-banking-ombud-speeds-up-resolutions/)
- **Fraud dominates** the caseload: mobile + internet banking fraud = **39%** of all complaints; online banking fraud complaints rose **15%** YoY. `[OBS]` [African News Agency](https://africannewsagency.com/financial-ombuds-banking-division-returns-over-r60m-to-consumers-as-fraud-complaints-surge/)
- The banking division **returned >R60 m to consumers in 2025**. `[OBS]` same source.

## 2. App-store sentiment

- **FNB Banking App (Google Play): rating 4.7**, updated Aug 2026; publisher FirstRand Bank Limited. `[OBS]` [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=za.co.fnb.connect.itt)
- MyBroadband ranked **FNB among the best banking apps in SA** based on iOS/Android user reviews. `[OBS]` [MyBroadband](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/banking/531571-best-banking-apps-in-south-africa.html)
- Third-party sentiment trackers show **~95% positive** recent ratings for the FNB app. `[OBS]` [Appfigures](https://app.appfigures.com/reports/app-profile/8230631)

## 3. Themes

- `[INF]` Dominant complaint themes align with the industry pattern: **digital-banking fraud, phishing/impersonation, and card-not-present fraud**; the Ombud reports fraudsters "following customers online."
- `[INF]` High satisfaction on app UX (ratings ~4.7) coexists with fraud-driven complaints — a common incumbent-bank pattern.
