# FNB / FirstRand — Financials

> Figures on a **normalised** basis unless stated. FY ends **30 June**. FNB is reported within FirstRand; FNB-specific lines are flagged.

## 1. JSE listing & ownership

- **FirstRand Limited** is listed on the **Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE: FSR)** and the **Namibian Stock Exchange (NSX: FST)**. `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY2026 booklet](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-analysis-of-financial-results-booklet-june-2026.pdf)
- **Listed 1998** (28 May 1998 per Grokipedia; formed effective ~1 April 1998). `[OBS]` [Grokipedia: FirstRand](https://grokipedia.com/page/FirstRand)
- **Market capitalisation (2026):** c. **R490–530 bn** across sources — R498.4 bn (stockanalysis.com, 16 Oct 2026) and R530.3 bn (BusinessTech "Battle of the banks 2026"). `[OBS]` [Stockanalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/FSR/), [BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/banking/876479/battle-of-the-banks-2026-capitec-vs-fnb-vs-standard-bank-vs-absa-vs-nedbank/)
- FirstRand describes itself as "**the largest financial institution by market capitalisation in Africa**". `[OBS]` [Listcorp](https://www.listcorp.com/jse/fsr/firstrand-limited)

## 4. Balance sheet & scale (FY2025)

- Normalised **total assets: R2,586,538 m** (FY2024: R2,366,815 m). `[OBS]`
- **Advances (net): R1,748,639 m**; **deposits and debt funding: R2,181,874 m**. `[OBS]`
- **FNB deposit base exceeded R1 trillion** for the first time. `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY25 media release](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-results-media-release-june-2025.pdf)

## 5. FNB franchise segment

- FNB normalised PBT growth and contribution: FNB is FirstRand's largest franchise by earnings. `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY25 results presentation](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-results-presentation-June-2025.pdf)
- **FNB active customers: 12.37 m** (FY2025) → **12.77 m South Africa alone in FY2026**. `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY25 booklet](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-analysis-of-financial-results-June-2025-single-page-printable-booklet.pdf), [FirstRand FY2026 booklet](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-analysis-of-financial-results-booklet-june-2026.pdf)
- **Value-added services (FNB Connect, Send Money/eWallet, eBucks, nav):** FNB Connect produced volumes worth **R22 bn** with **3 m customers**; total retail revenue from these services grew **15% to >R2.9 bn** (FY2025). `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY25 media release](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-results-media-release-june-2025.pdf)
- FNB's **insurance income up 9%**, insurance PBT up 8% (FY2025). `[OBS]` same source.

## 6. FY2026 direction (year ended 30 June 2026)

- FirstRand published audited results for FY2026 (SENS, 10 Sep 2026). FNB SA active customers **12.77 m** in FY2026. `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY2026 booklet](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-analysis-of-financial-results-booklet-june-2026.pdf), [JSE SENS](https://trade.sharenet.co.za/v3/sens_display.php?scode=FSR&seq=25&tdate=20260910083000)
- FNB digitally-active customers **8.16 m**, app processed **782 m transactions** (+10%), virtual-card spend **R101 bn**, PayShap payments grew >8x (FY2026). `[OBS]` [The Condia summary of FY2026](https://thecondia.com/fnb-digital-banking-changing-how-south-africans-bank/)
