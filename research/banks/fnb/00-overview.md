# FNB (First National Bank) — Overview

> Entity: **FNB**, a division of **FirstRand Bank Limited**, the banking subsidiary of **FirstRand Limited** (JSE: FSR). Council C16 (Banking & Fintech). Public-information research only.

## 1. What FNB is

- FNB is the **retail and business banking franchise** of FirstRand Limited, one of Africa's largest financial-services groups. `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY2025 results](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-analysis-of-financial-results-June-2025-single-page-printable-booklet.pdf)
- FNB is legally **a division of FirstRand Bank Limited**, a registered bank supervised by the South African Reserve Bank (SARB), and an Authorised Financial Services and Credit Provider `[OBS]` (registration number 1929/001225/06; NCRCP20) — see [legal/10-licence-regulatory.md](./legal/10-licence-regulatory.md).
- FirstRand Limited is "the largest financial institution by market capitalisation in Africa" per its own listing profile `[UNVERIFIED]` (self-description; corroborated by market-cap comparisons) — see [financials/20-financials.md](./financials/20-financials.md).

## 3. Journey (founding → today)

- **1838** — Origins traced to the **Eastern Province Bank**, established 12 November 1838 in Grahamstown, Eastern Cape, by wool merchants to finance the local wool trade. `[OBS]` [FNB Zambia "About us"](https://www.fnbzambia.co.zm/about-us/), [Grokipedia: FNB](https://grokipedia.com/page/First_National_Bank_(South_Africa))
- **1977** — **Rand Consolidated Investments (RCI)** founded with R10,000 capital by Laurie Dippenaar, GT Ferreira and Pat Goss — the seed of the modern FirstRand group. `[OBS]` [Daily Investor](https://dailyinvestor.com/banking/128042/three-men-turned-r10000-into-south-africas-most-valuable-bank/)
- **1998** — **FirstRand Limited formed** (announced March 1998, effective ~1 April 1998; renamed from Momentum Life) by merging Rand Merchant Bank Holdings with Anglo American's financial-services interests (FNB, Southern Life, Momentum, WesBank). Listed on the JSE. `[OBS]` [RMB "Our story"](https://www.rmb.co.uk/page/our-story), [Grokipedia: RMB Holdings](https://grokipedia.com/page/RMB_Holdings)
- **2010s–2020s** — Digital-led growth: FNB describes itself via value-added services (FNB Connect MVNO, eWallet/Send Money, eBucks, nav) inside the core transactional base. `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY25 media release](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-results-media-release-june-2025.pdf)
- **2026** — FNB re-organised under a new **Retail and Business Banking (RBB)** division; **Lytania Johnson** appointed FNB CEO from April 2026, succeeding **Harry Kellan** (stepping down end-2026). `[UNVERIFIED]` (single secondary source) [Empire Magazine Africa](https://empiremagazineafrica.com/firstrand-restructures-banking-operations-appoints-new-fnb-ceo/)
