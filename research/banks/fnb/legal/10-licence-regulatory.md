# FNB — Licence & Regulatory Status

> Scope: bank licence class, regulator, FAIS/NCR registrations. Public sources only.

## 1. Legal entity & bank licence

- FNB is **not a separate legal entity**: "First National Bank — a division of **FirstRand Bank Limited**". `[OBS]` [FNB account confirmation letter](https://www.sabreproducts.com/assets/banking/20250430%20Sabre%20FNB%20Bank%20Acocunt%20details.pdf)
- **FirstRand Bank Limited** is a **registered bank** in South Africa, supervised by the **South African Reserve Bank (SARB)** through its **Prudential Authority**. `[OBS]` [FirstRand Bank appears on the SARB "Locally Controlled Banks" register](https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/publications/prudential-authority/sa-registered-financial-banks-and-rep-offices/2025/Locally%20Controlled%20Banks%20as%20at%2029%20Aug%202025.pdf); [Grokipedia: FirstRand — licensed by SARB](https://owiki.org/wiki/FirstRand)
- **Company registration number:** 1929/001225/06. `[OBS]` [FNB account confirmation letter](https://www.sabreproducts.com/assets/banking/20250430%20Sabre%20FNB%20Bank%20Acocunt%20details.pdf)
- Primary bank-supervision statute: the **Banks Act 94 of 1990**; conduct + market-conduct role for the **FSCA**; credit-provider registration with the **National Credit Regulator (NCR)**. `[INF]` (regulatory framework; specific statutes not restated in every FNB document)

## 2. FAIS (financial services) licensing

- FNB Commercial (a business unit of FNB, a division of FirstRand Bank Limited) is "licensed as a **Financial Services Provider in terms of section 8 of the Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act, 2002 (Act No. 37 of 2002)**". `[OBS]` [FNB Financial Services Provider licence](https://www.online.fnbswaziland.co.sz/downloads/legal/CommercialFNB.pdf)
- FNB is branded as "**An Authorised Financial Services and Credit Provider (NCRCP20)**". `[OBS]` [FNB account confirmation letter](https://www.sabreproducts.com/assets/banking/20250430%20Sabre%20FNB%20Bank%20Acocunt%20details.pdf)
- Specific FSCA **FSP number** for FirstRand Bank Limited / FNB was not retrieved from a primary source in this pass. `[UNK]`

## 3. Credit-provider registration

- **NCR credit-provider number: NCRCP20.** `[OBS]` [FNB account confirmation letter](https://www.sabreproducts.com/assets/banking/20250430%20Sabre%20FNB%20Bank%20Acocunt%20details.pdf)
- Insurance activities sit in group entities, e.g. **FirstRand Short Term Insurance Limited** (Reg. No. 2018/234369/06) — a licensed non-life insurer. `[OBS]` [FNB Business Law-on-call T&Cs](https://www.online.rmbprivatebank.com/downloads/insurance/business/FNB_Business-Law-on-call_Terms.pdf)

## 5. Compliance context

- FICA (Financial Intelligence Centre Act) KYC is applied via FNB's KYC forms. `[OBS]` [FNB KYC form](https://online.firstrand.co.za/downloads/fica/foreignCo.pdf)
- South Africa was **removed from the FATF grey list in 2025**, a market-wide compliance event affecting all SA banks including FNB. `[OBS]` [Standard Bank FY25 results](https://reporting.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/Documents/Financial-results/Annual-Results/SBG-2025-Annual-Results-Booklet-(spreads).pdf)
