# FNB — Banking Rails & Payment Infrastructure

> SA payment rails FNB participates in, and its own money-movement instruments.

## 1. National payment rails (SA)

| Rail | Operator | FNB role | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PayShap** (instant A2A, launched 13 Mar 2023) | BankservAfrica / PASA | **Participant** (both send + receive) | [Payment Brief](https://paymentbrief.com/markets/south-africa/), [FNB PayShap](https://www.online.fnbbotswana.co.bw/ways-to-bank/digital/fnb-pay.html) |
| **EFT / EFT credit** | BankservAfrica | Participant | [FNB payment request FAQs](https://www.online.fnbswaziland.co.sz/downloads/ways-to-bank/PayPayment.pdf) |
| **RTC / RTGS (SAMOS)** | SARB | Participant (high-value interbank) | `[INF]` (all registered banks are SAMOS participants) |
| **Card (Visa/Mastercard)** | Schemes | Issuer + acquirer | [FirstRand FY25 booklet](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-analysis-of-financial-results-June-2025-single-page-printable-booklet.pdf) |
| **QR — "Scan to Pay"** | BankservAfrica | Supported in FNB App / Virtual Card | [FNB Virtual Card FAQs](https://www.fnb.co.za/downloads/ways-to-bank/FNBVirtualCardFAQs.pdf) |

- **PayShap** operates **24/7/365 with sub-10-second settlement**; FNB is among the integrated majors. `[OBS]` [Payment Brief](https://paymentbrief.com/markets/south-africa/)
- FNB-launched **PayShap Request / Payment Request** allows a payer to be billed via the FNB app "View Request → Pay now". `[OBS]` [Ozow PayShap guide](https://ozow.com/blog/payshap-request-a-step-by-step-user-guide)
- FNB reports **PayShap payments grew more than 8× in one year** (FY2026). `[OBS]` [The Condia](https://thecondia.com/fnb-digital-banking-changing-how-south-africans-bank/)

## 2. FNB money-movement instruments

- **eWallet** — card-less, bank-account-less remittance to a cellphone; withdrawable at participating retailers/ATMs; flat R10 (Easy accounts); up to R15,000 via app. `[OBS]` [FNB eWallet](https://www.online.fnb.co.za/send-money/eWallet.html)
- **Send Money / eWallet on WhatsApp** (March 2026). `[OBS]` [The Vibe ZA](https://thevibeza.com/fnb-ewallet-whatsapp-south-africa/)
- **FNB Virtual Card** with dynamic CVV. `[OBS]` [FNB Virtual Card FAQs](https://www.fnb.co.za/downloads/ways-to-bank/FNBVirtualCardFAQs.pdf)

## 3. Access network

- **ATMs: 5,772** (SA 4,771 + broader Africa 1,001); **branches: 767** (SA 630); **CashPlus/AgencyPlus agents: 5,089** (FY2025). `[OBS]` [FirstRand FY25 booklet](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-analysis-of-financial-results-June-2025-single-page-printable-booklet.pdf)
- **ADTs** (Automated Deposit Terminals) and **Cash@Till** retailer deposits extend reach. `[OBS]` same.

## 4. Interchange & economics

- `[INF]` FNB earns **interchange** on card issuing and **merchant-service fees** on acquiring; both sit within FirstRand's **non-interest revenue** (R58.5 bn FY2025, of which fee & commission R40.3 bn). `[DER]` from [FirstRand FY25 booklet](https://www.firstrand.co.za/media/investors/reports/fsr-analysis-of-financial-results-June-2025-single-page-printable-booklet.pdf)
