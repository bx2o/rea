# J.P. Morgan Private Bank — Products & Services

## 1. Family Office Services

- [OBS] **Outsourced CIO** services covering asset allocation, investment policy and risk
  management; global markets insight, institutional trading solutions and strategic tax management
  ([Family Office Services](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/services/wealth-planning-and-advice/family-office-services)).
- [OBS] **Private markets**: direct-investment access and a broad alternatives platform.
- [OBS] Family-office solutions are generally viable for families with significant wealth
  (the site notes "generally at least a few hundred million in assets")
  ([Family Office Services](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/services/wealth-planning-and-advice/family-office-services)).

## 2. Wealth Management & Advisory

- [OBS] Three global business lines work together: investments, advisory and administrative
  ([Family Office Services](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/services/wealth-planning-and-advice/family-office-services)).
- [OBS] **Holistic wealth management** backed by research analysts in **25 countries**; disciplined
  wealth planning aligned to objectives, risk tolerance and time horizon
  ([J.P. Morgan Private Client](https://www.jpmorgan.com/private-client)).
- [OBS] "Assistance across critical areas of family life" — specialised services supporting
  ultra-high-net-worth families ([J.P. Morgan Private Client](https://www.jpmorgan.com/private-client)).

## 3. Banking & Digital Tools

- [OBS] Online tools: portfolio performance tracking, positions/historical data, index
  comparison, hedge-fund/private-investment views, equity research; bill pay, domestic/international
  wires with real-time FX, mobile check deposit, data export
  ([Private Bank online tools](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/services/banking/online-tools)).
- [OBS] Aggregation: clients can link accounts from thousands of other financial institutions
  ([Private Bank online tools](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/services/banking/online-tools)).
