# J.P. Morgan Private Bank — Overview

> Council C10 (Finance & IR) · Division of JPMorgan Chase & Co. · Reverse-engineering profile.
> Note: many granular figures come from secondary/aggregator sources and are tagged accordingly.

## 1. Identity

- [OBS] **J.P. Morgan Private Bank** is the private-banking/wealth-management division of
  **JPMorgan Chase & Co.**, serving ultra-high-net-worth (UHNW) individuals, families and
  institutions with wealth management, investment advisory and family-office services
  ([Private Bank — Family Office Services](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/services/wealth-planning-and-advice/family-office-services)).
- [OBS] It markets itself as the "World's Best Private Bank" and cites **over two centuries** of
  experience advising successful individuals and families
  ([Family Office Services](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/services/wealth-planning-and-advice/family-office-services)).
- [OBS] Entry tier: **J.P. Morgan Private Bank is designed for clients with more than $5 million
  in investable assets** ([Chase/Media release](https://media.chase.com/news/jpmorganchase-opens-flagship-financial-centers-for-affluent-clients)).
  - [UNK] A third-party wiki claims a higher UK threshold (~£10M+) — single-source, not confirmed
    ([Private Law Wiki](https://wiki.private.law/en/jpmorgan-private-bank-uk.md)).

## 2. Scale (secondary source)

- [OBS] Reported to oversee **more than $3.5 trillion in client assets** (as of Dec 31, 2025),
  operate from **75+ offices** and employ **3,500+ advisors**
  ([Grokipedia — J.P. Morgan Private Bank](https://grokipedia.com/page/JP_Morgan_Private_Bank)) —
  single-source, treat as **[UNVERIFIED]**.

## 3. Positioning

- [OBS] Three global business lines work together to serve families across investments,
  advisory and administrative needs
  ([Family Office Services](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/services/wealth-planning-and-advice/family-office-services)).
- [OBS] "Assistance across critical areas of family life" — specialised services supporting
  ultra-high-net-worth families (lifestyle/concierge, governance, next-gen education)
  ([J.P. Morgan Private Client](https://www.jpmorgan.com/private-client)).

## 4. Primary Research Asset

- [OBS] Publishes an annual **Global Family Office Report** — the 2024 edition surveyed single
  family offices across **48 geographies**, with an **average family net worth of $1.4B** and an
  average target portfolio return of ~11%
  ([2024 Global Family Office Report](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/services/wealth-planning-and-advice/family-office-services/2024-global-family-office-report)).
