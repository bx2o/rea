# J.P. Morgan Private Bank — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

## 1. Brand Positioning

- [OBS] Positions itself as the **"World's Best Private Bank"** with **200+ years** of heritage
  ([Family Office Services](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/services/wealth-planning-and-advice/family-office-services)).
- [OBS] Messaging centres on family (the "most important asset"), governance, next-generation
  support and global expertise ([J.P. Morgan Private Client](https://www.jpmorgan.com/private-client)).

## 2. Content / Thought-Leadership Marketing

- [OBS] Flagship owned-media asset: the annual **Global Family Office Report** — a primary research
  survey used for positioning and lead generation (48 geographies; $1.4B avg family net worth)
  ([2024 Global Family Office Report](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/services/wealth-planning-and-advice/family-office-services/2024-global-family-office-report)).
- [OBS] "Global expertise" marketing emphasises research analysts in **25 countries**
  ([J.P. Morgan Private Client](https://www.jpmorgan.com/private-client)).

## 3. Relationship / Referral Model

- [INF] Private-bank acquisition is primarily relationship- and referral-driven rather than mass
  advertising; eligibility tiers act as a natural qualifier.
