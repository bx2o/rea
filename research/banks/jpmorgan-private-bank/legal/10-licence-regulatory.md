# J.P. Morgan Private Bank — Licence & Regulatory

## 1. Regulatory Home

- [OBS] J.P. Morgan Private Bank is **not a standalone licensed entity** — it is a division/brand
  within **JPMorgan Chase & Co.** and its subsidiaries
  ([J.P. Morgan Private Bank services](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/nam/en/services/wealth-planning-and-advice/family-office-services)).
- [OBS] The parent's national bank subsidiaries (JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A.; Chase Bank USA, N.A.)
  are regulated by the **OCC**, **Federal Reserve** and **FDIC**; US broker-dealers are regulated
  by the **SEC/FINRA**; the group is a **bank holding company** under Fed consolidated supervision
  ([SEC filing 210081](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/node/210081/html);
  [SEC filing 525601](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/node/525601/html)).
- [INF] Investment advisory and brokerage services offered through the Private Bank are delivered
  by SEC-registered entities within the group; global private-banking activities are also subject
  to local regulators in each jurisdiction it operates.

## 2. Client Protection / Disclosures

- [OBS] Deposits held at group national banks are FDIC-insured; investment products are **not**
  FDIC-insured and carry market risk (standard disclosures).
