# African Bank — Products & Pricing

Sources: MyWORLD pricing guide ([PDF](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/sgqbbsoi/tb2309_001_mw_pricing-guide_update_p4.pdf)),
MyWORLD banking page, MyWORLD launch release ([PDF](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/xmydatlh/231-myworld-launch-release-21-may-2019.pdf)),
Audacious Rewards. All [OBS] unless noted.

## 1. Transactional banking — MyWORLD

| Feature | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Launched | 21 May 2019 (moved AB into transactional/digital retail banking) | [OBS] |
| Monthly account fee | **No monthly account fee** on the core transactional proposition | [OBS] |
| Pocket accounts | Up to **6 accounts** (shared banking with family) | [OBS] |
| Monthly bundle fee (up to 5 pockets) | R5.00 | [OBS] |
| Additional pocket fee | Free | [OBS] |
| Interest on positive balances | 4.5% (advertised "best rate in SA") | [OBS] |
| MyWORLD→MyWORLD transfers | Free | [OBS] |
| Overdraft monthly fee (Power Pocket, when used) | R69 | [OBS] |
| Balance enquiries (digital) | Free | [OBS] |

Sources: [MyWORLD pricing guide (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/sgqbbsoi/tb2309_001_mw_pricing-guide_update_p4.pdf),
[MyWORLD banking page](https://www.africanbank.co.za/en/home/banking/). [OBS]

> **Note**: marketing states "NO monthly fees"; the pricing guide shows a R5 bundle fee plus a R69 overdraft
> fee when an overdraft facility is used — a nuance to verify per product variant. [DER]

## 2. Credit & lending

- **Personal loans** and **credit cards** (incl. the **Black credit card**, Visa-branded, embossed, instant
  issue in-branch) ([Black Credit Card brochure (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/f4qipadj/hr_abc2302_006b_black-cc_digital-flyer.pdf)). [OBS]
- **Secured lending** — pilot home-loan offering; rental, instalment sale and leasing solutions in Business &
  Commercial ([FY25 SENS release (PDF)](https://www.sharenet.co.za/jsepdf/SENS_20251124_S513811.pdf)). [OBS]
- **Sasfin acquisition** added Commercial Property Finance and Capital Equipment Finance books
  ([African Insider](https://www.africaninsider.com/companies/african-bank-announces-acquisition-of-sasfin-banks-divisions/)). [OBS]

## 3. Savings & investments

- **Savings Pocket** within MyWORLD; positive-balance interest (advertised 4.5%). [OBS]
- Investment products via the bank ([MyWORLD banking](https://www.africanbank.co.za/en/home/banking/)). [OBS]

## 4. Insurance

- **Credit Life Assurance (CLA)** — extended to short-term lending products. [OBS]
- **Funeral cover** — embedded in affinity partnership transactional accounts. [OBS]
- Net insurance income R582m FY25 ([FY25 SENS release (PDF)](https://www.sharenet.co.za/jsepdf/SENS_20251124_S513811.pdf)). [OBS]

## 5. Business & Commercial banking

- Transactional banking, secured lending, digital services for SMEs; SMME digital lending platform in
  development with fintech partners ([FY25 SENS release (PDF)](https://www.sharenet.co.za/jsepdf/SENS_20251124_S513811.pdf)). [OBS]
- **Alliance Banking** (banking-as-a-service) for retail/fintech partners; FY26 1H key principle "MyWorld as a
  standalone proposition" ([1H26 presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/3soalx12/african-bank-interim-results-1h26-presentation-final-2506.pdf)). [OBS]

## 6. Loyalty — Audacious Rewards

| Feature | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Points-based loyalty for everyday banking | [OBS] |
| Earning | Double points on the **20th** of each month; debit ~0.6%, credit ~2% in points on qualifying spend | [OBS] |
| Redemption | Airtime, data, grocery, travel, entertainment; points-to-cash (R5 = 550 points) | [OBS] |
| Audacious Rewards Store | Online store via website/app | [OBS] |

Sources: [Audacious Rewards FAQ](https://www.audaciousrewards.co.za/faq),
[Audacious Rewards T&Cs v25 (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/vcklgkaf/audacious-rewards-terms-and-condition-version-25_2024_09_03-pdf-2-docx.pdf),
[Audacious Rewards store](https://www.audaciousrewards.co.za/). [OBS]

## 7. Mobile wallets

- Supports **Garmin Pay**, **Samsung Wallet** and **SwatchPAY**, plus **PayShap** ([Garmin Pay FAQs (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/sxhlcduv/garmin-pay-faqs.pdf)). [OBS]
