## Gaps Stremiium can exploit

1. **Rewards unlocked by activity, not health/ecosystem** — African Bank already does this; Stremiium can do it **with a transferable currency** African Bank lacks.
2. **No crypto/investment rails** — African Bank is credit+transactional only; Stremiium's wallet/token is additive.
3. **High credit-loss model** — Stremiium can position a **behaviour-verified lending** product (rewards tied to repayment, as African Bank does but with on-chain proof).
4. **Merchant/softPOS gap** — Tap2Glass is nascent; Stremiium can integrate softPOS **with** its rewards engine.
