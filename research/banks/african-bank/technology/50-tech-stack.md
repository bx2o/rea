# African Bank — Technology Stack

> **Confidence: low.** African Bank does not publish detailed technology architecture. Findings are drawn from
> strategy statements in results material. Vendor-level specifics are largely [UNK].

## 1. Stated strategy (FY25–FY26)

Strategic pillar **"IT Systems"**: *"Enhance the technology platform and cybersecurity practices, while
improving speed of delivery"* — with a "Unified platform, simplified stack, AI" ambition and a data &
insights goal of a "single view, real-time, data-driven decisions"
([1H26 presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/3soalx12/african-bank-interim-results-1h26-presentation-final-2506.pdf)). [OBS]

Other stated pillars: **Digitalisation** (build on digital/analytics to improve end-to-end journeys) and
**Digital Ways of Work** ([1H26 presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/3soalx12/african-bank-interim-results-1h26-presentation-final-2506.pdf)). [OBS]

## 2. Integration complexity (context)

- FY25/1H26 disclosures repeatedly cite **integration complexity from acquisitions** (Grindrod Bank, Ubank,
  Sasfin books) and "high-cost base compounded by complexity created from acquisitions"; a stated priority is
  to **"rationalise product, technology & operating complexity"**
  ([1H26 presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/3soalx12/african-bank-interim-results-1h26-presentation-final-2506.pdf)). [OBS]
- This implies a **multi-core / heterogeneous legacy estate** post-acquisitions [INF]. [INF]
