## 3. Culture & employer brand

- **Top Employer** certified since 2022; 2024 assessment scored **87%** (industry benchmark 85%)
  ([Interim 2025 results booklet (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/1bgjgwdx/ab-interim-booklet-2025-v2-24-05-25-15h22-final.pdf)). [OBS]
- Values: Sustainability, Creativity, Transparency, Collaboration, Empathy, Excellence
  ([1H26 presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/3soalx12/african-bank-interim-results-1h26-presentation-final-2506.pdf)). [OBS]

## 4. Transformation (B-BBEE)
