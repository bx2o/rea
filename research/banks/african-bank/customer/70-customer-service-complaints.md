Sources: [African Bank — Contact information](https://www.africanbank.co.za/en/home/talk-to-us/),
[African Bank FAQs](https://www.africanbank.co.za/en/home/frequently-asked-questions/). [OBS]

## 2. Escalation path

- Internal complaints → if unresolved → **National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFO)** at 0860 800 900
  ([African Bank FAQs](https://www.africanbank.co.za/en/home/frequently-asked-questions/)). [OBS]
- Credit-related matters → **National Credit Regulator (NCR)**, Call Centre 0860 627 627,
  complaints@ncr.org.za ([Complaints Policy (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/ijhojp0n/african-bank-customer-complaints-policy.pdf)). [OBS]

## 4. External complaint signals

- Public complaint boards show recurring themes: **app instability / downtime**, "**high charges**", and
  inability to complete immediate payments, e.g.
  ([Hellopeter — "Terrible app and high charges"](https://www.hellopeter.com/african-bank/reviews/terrible-app-and-high-charges-5176519),
  [Hellopeter — "Inactive online banking app!"](https://www.hellopeter.com/african-bank/reviews/inactive-online-banking-app-5144685)). [UNVERIFIED] (self-selected)
- App store ratings (see §5) reflect mixed sentiment despite the high self-reported CSAT. [DER]
