## 2. Income drivers (FY25)

- Interest income on advances ~R7.3bn (+4%), lifted by Business & Commercial growth. [OBS]
- **Non-interest income +39% to R2.1bn** (FY24: R1.5bn) — MyWORLD transactional usage, credit cards, value-add
  services and partnership fees. [OBS]
- Net insurance income **R582m** (FY24: R821m) — impacted by lower Personal Banking advances, partly offset by
  fewer retrenchment claims. [OBS]
- Operating expenses rose to **R4.9bn** (FY24: R4.4bn), driving cost-to-income up to 64%. [OBS]

## 4. Explanatory notes

- Losses attributed to **transformation/integration costs, higher impairments, challenging collections, NIM
  compression and a high cost base** from acquisitions
  ([African News Agency](https://africannewsagency.com/african-bank-reports-r624-million-loss-amid-transformation-costs-and-integration-challenges/)). [OBS]
- FY25 saw a **R2.7bn debt-capital-markets issuance** at improved pricing and R7.4bn cash reserves (excl.
  statutory requirements) ([FY25 SENS release](https://www.sharenet.co.za/jsepdf/SENS_20251124_S513811.pdf)). [OBS]
- Strategic pivot: from acquisitive diversification to **focused consolidation**, targeting a
  profitable, **IPO-ready** bank ([1H26 presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/3soalx12/african-bank-interim-results-1h26-presentation-final-2506.pdf)). [OBS]
