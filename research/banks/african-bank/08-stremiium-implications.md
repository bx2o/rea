# 08 — Stremiium Implications · African Bank

Each finding is classified **ADOPT / ADAPT / AVOID / BEAT** per Program Brief §7, with the specific Stremiium change.

## ADOPT

| # | Finding (African Bank) | Why | Stremiium implementation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Activity-based rewards that target risk-reducing behaviour** (points for saving, loan repayment, card swipes, VAS) | Aligns customer reward with lower credit risk & deeper deposits | Reward `$STM` specifically for **on-time repayment and savings** — loyalty as risk management |
| A2 | **Zero-fee MyWorld with pockets + shared banking** | Frictionless entry; household banking is a SA reality | Zero-fee tier, multi-pocket budgets, and **shared/household `$STM` rewards pooling** |
| A3 | **SoftPOS merchant acceptance (Tap2Glass)** | Cheap acquiring for informal merchants, drives acceptance | Ship Stremiium softPOS where **each acceptance earns merchant + customer `$STM`** |
| A4 | **Rewards avoid health/insurance dependency** | Broad addressable market, lighter POPIA burden | Behavioural rewards from banking activity alone; no health data required |
| A5 | **Business/SME transactional accounts** | High-value, sticky segment | SME accounts bundled with merchant acquiring + `$STM` treasury rewards |

## ADAPT

| # | Finding | Mismatch | Adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Unsecured-lending-led economics (5.3% credit loss)** | High loss model is fragile and reputationally risky | Keep lending **behaviour-gated**: rewards + evidence-based limits to compress loss ratio below African Bank's |
| B2 | **Closed-loop points store** | Not fungible; limited perceived value | Make rewards a **transferable `$STM` balance** with the voucher store as one redemption path among many |
| B3 | **Legacy + acquired-system integration drag** | One-off transformation costs hurt P&L | Stremiium: start cloud-native (Mambu/AWS) to avoid the integration debt African Bank is paying for |
| B4 | **Merchant fees/MDR opaque** | Merchants distrust hidden pricing | Publish transparent softPOS pricing in `$STM`-denominated terms |

## AVOID

| # | Finding | Trap | Stremiium rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Guessing the core system** | Stremiium cannot plan integrations until African Bank's CBS is confirmed | Do not assume a vendor; treat as UNKNOWN until REA/primary docs confirm |
| C2 | **Consolidating via acquisition without system convergence** | Integration cost drag and loss years | Prefer organic build on a single cloud core; if acquiring, converge systems first |
| C3 | **Over-reliance on high-margin unsecured credit** | Concentration risk in a weak labour market | Diversify revenue: interchange, merchant acquiring, VAS, Web3 fees |
| C4 | **Opaque rewards terms** | Points-crediting rules can feel unfair | Transparent, on-chain-verifiable reward accrual |

## BEAT

| # | African Bank weakness | Stremiium attack |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Closed-loop rewards** (points only redeemable in-store) | Transferable `$STM` rewards redeemable anywhere |
| D2 | **No crypto/investment rails** | Wallet + custody + tokenised assets in the same app |
| D3 | **SoftPOS is nascent and reward-blind** | SoftPOS where acceptance **mints rewards** for both sides |
| D4 | **High credit-loss model** | Behaviour-verified lending with on-chain repayment proof to win better risk |
| D5 | **Unresolved profitability/integration narrative** | Position Stremiium as the **clean-slate, cloud-native** alternative — no legacy integration debt |

## Net strategic read for Stremiium

African Bank supplies two genuinely valuable mechanics Stremiium should copy: **rewards that reward risk-reducing behaviour (A1)** and **softPOS merchant acceptance (A3)** — plus a mass-market, insurance-free rewards philosophy (A4). Stremiium should **adopt A1–A5**, **adapt B1–B4 to a transferable `$STM` model**, **avoid replicating its legacy-integration and unsecured-concentration traps (C1–C4)**, and **beat it decisively on openness and Web3 rails (D1–D5)**.
