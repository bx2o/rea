## 2. History & journey

- **1975** — African Bank opened its first branch in Ga-Rankuwa as a black-owned, black-managed bank
  serving black communities; founders include Dr Sam Motsuenyane and Dr Richard Maponya; **2025 marked
  50 years** ([African Bank Integrated Report 2025](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/jzffsxq0/african-bank-ir-2025-final-1205.pdf)). [OBS]
- **1998–1999** — Theta Group acquired the troubled bank's licence (reportedly ~R1.7bn) and renamed it
  **African Bank Investments Limited (ABIL)**; Leon Kirkinis led it
  ([MBA智库 case study](https://doc.mbalib.com/m/view/6057e08d2f361eaa027ea7f98bb79b1a.html)). [UNVERIFIED] (secondary)
- **10 August 2014** — SARB placed African Bank under **curatorship** at 16:00, after a projected **R7.6bn
  FY2014 loss** and an **R8.5bn capital shortfall**; JSE trading suspended
  ([Grokipedia — African Bank](https://grokipedia.com/page/African_Bank_Limited),
  [PMG — New African Bank key milestones (PDF)](https://pmg.org.za/files/160524keypoints.pdf),
  [SARB curation report index (PDF)](https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/publications/media-releases/2016/7288/1---Index.pdf)). [OBS]
- **Resolution** — a "good bank" was restructured and recapitalised with **R10bn equity** from **SARB (50%),
  PIC (25%) and a consortium of banks (25%)**; the "residual bank" became **African Phoenix**
  ([PIC — African Bank investment details (PDF)](https://www.pic.gov.za/DocPresentations/5.-African-Bank.pdf)). [OBS]
- **Relaunch** — the new **African Bank Limited** operates under **African Bank Holdings Limited**
  ([PMG key milestones (PDF)](https://pmg.org.za/files/160524keypoints.pdf)). [OBS]
- **2019** — launched **MyWORLD** transactional banking, moving from monoline lender to retail bank
  ([MyWORLD launch release, 21 May 2019 (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/xmydatlh/231-myworld-launch-release-21-may-2019.pdf)). [OBS]
- **2022–2025** — "Excelerate" strategy: acquisition-led diversification into Business & Commercial
  banking. **2025 declared a "fully fledged bank"** ([FY25 results presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/thxhry1a/african-bank-holdings-annual-financial-results-presentation-for-fy25.pdf)). [OBS]
- **2026** — strategy pivot from "acquisitive diversification" to **focused consolidation** and becoming
  **"IPO-ready"** ([1H26 interim presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/3soalx12/african-bank-interim-results-1h26-presentation-final-2506.pdf)). [OBS]

## 3. Key findings (for the NeoBank programme)

1. **Rescued/relaunched incumbent** — African Bank is a fully-licensed bank rebuilt after the 2014
   curatorship, now diversifying beyond unsecured lending. [OBS]
2. **Acquisition-led scale** — Grindrod Bank (R1.5bn), Ubank (R80m), Sasfin books (~R3.26bn) → full-service
   bank with Business & Commercial. [OBS]
3. **Financial stress** — FY25 net profit R274m (down from R517m); 1H26 net loss R624m; CLR 7.7%; the pivot
   to consolidation aims to fix cost-to-income and integration complexity. [OBS]
4. **Transactional-led proposition** — MyWORLD (no monthly account fee, up to 6 accounts, 4.5% interest).
   [OBS]
5. **Audacious Rewards** loyalty programme (points-based) is the core engagement hook. [OBS]
