# African Bank — Banking Rails & Payment Infrastructure

## 1. Domestic real-time rails

| Rail | Status | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PayShap** | Live | Instant A2A; send/receive via account number or **ShapID** (cellphone); 24/7/365 | [OBS] |
| **PayShap Request** | Live | Merchant/business payment requests; approve in-app, instant settlement | [OBS] |
| **Immediate payments / RTC** | Live | Instant transfers (referenced in customer complaints) | [OBS] |
| **Card acquiring** | Live | Card machines referenced in product catalogue / business banking | [OBS] |

Sources: [African Bank — PayShap](https://www.africanbank.co.za/en/home/payshap/),
[PayShap payment request FAQs (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/tfffievw/payshap-payment-request-faqs.pdf). [OBS]

## 2. PayShap context (national)

- Launched **13 March 2023** by BankservAfrica (now **PayInc**) under an SARB mandate; the initial cohort was
  Absa, FNB, Standard Bank and Nedbank, with more banks joining later
  ([SARB press release (PDF)](https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/publications/media-releases/2023/payshap-/Press%20release%20on%20the%20launch%20of%20Payshap%20-%20a%20digital%20payment%20service.pdf)). [OBS]
- By 2026: >R100bn processed, >136m transactions, >5m ShapIDs, 12 participating banks
  ([Stitch](https://stitch.money/blog/real-time-payments-in-south-africa-the-state-of-payshap-in-2026)). [OBS]

## 3. Cards & wallets

- Issues **Visa** debit/credit cards (incl. Black Credit Card), with **tap/dip/swipe** and PIN on contactless
  ([Black Credit Card brochure (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/f4qipadj/hr_abc2302_006b_black-cc_digital-flyer.pdf)). [OBS]
- Supports digital wallets **Garmin Pay, Samsung Wallet, SwatchPAY** via tokenisation
  ([Garmin Pay FAQs (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/sxhlcduv/garmin-pay-faqs.pdf)). [OBS]

## 4. Alliance Banking rails

- Provides **banking-as-a-service** rails to partners (Lesaka, MTN, Shoprite) — prepaid/transactional
  distribution ([FY25 results presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/thxhry1a/african-bank-holdings-annual-financial-results-presentation-for-fy25.pdf)). [OBS]

## 5. Distribution network

- Omnichannel: physical branches plus digital; "fit-for-purpose, mobile-first, tiered branches" principle.
  Customer base ~6.3m (FY25) ([1H26 presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/3soalx12/african-bank-interim-results-1h26-presentation-final-2506.pdf)). [OBS]
