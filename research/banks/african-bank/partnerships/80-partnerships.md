# African Bank — Partnerships, M&A & Alliances

## 1. Acquisitions (the "Excelerate" build-out)

| Target | Consideration | Timing | Purpose | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Grindrod Bank** (100%) | ~**R1.5bn** | announced May 2022; completed Nov 2022 | Re-enter Business Banking | [OBS] |
| **Ubank** (majority of assets/liabilities + staff) | **R80m** | Aug 2022 | Scale, retail base | [OBS] |
| **Sasfin** commercial property finance + capital equipment books | ~**R3.26bn** | 2024/25 | Business & Commercial diversification | [OBS] |

Sources: [Daily Maverick — Grindrod acquisition](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-05-26-pics-pending-african-bank-takes-a-leap-into-business-banking-with-r1-5bn-acquisition-of-grindrod-bank/),
[Retail Banker International — Ubank deal](https://www.retailbankerinternational.com/news/african-bank-ubank-assets-liabilities/),
[African Insider — Sasfin divisions](https://www.africaninsider.com/companies/african-bank-announces-acquisition-of-sasfin-banks-divisions/),
[FY25 results presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/thxhry1a/african-bank-holdings-annual-financial-results-presentation-for-fy25.pdf). [OBS]

- Grindrod Bank was part of Johann Rupert-linked Grindrod group; the price was slightly below Grindrod's
  R1.67bn NAV ([Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-05-26-pics-pending-african-bank-takes-a-leap-into-business-banking-with-r1-5bn-acquisition-of-grindrod-bank/)). [OBS]
- Ubank had been under curatorship since 2020; African Bank did not acquire the legal entity or residual
  assets. The deal was expected to add ~4.7m retail customers
  ([Retail Banker International](https://www.retailbankerinternational.com/news/african-bank-ubank-assets-liabilities/)). [OBS]

## 2. Alliance Banking (banking-as-a-service)

- African Bank's **Alliance Banking** pillar partners with fintech, non-bank financial and retail
  institutions. Named partners: **Lesaka, MTN, Shoprite**
  ([FY25 results presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/thxhry1a/african-bank-holdings-annual-financial-results-presentation-for-fy25.pdf)). [OBS]
- Model: strategic alliances for faster market entry; embedded funeral cover on affinity transactional
  accounts ([FY25 SENS release (PDF)](https://www.sharenet.co.za/jsepdf/SENS_20251124_S513811.pdf)). [OBS]
