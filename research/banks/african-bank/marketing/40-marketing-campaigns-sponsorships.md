# African Bank — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

## 1. Brand platform

- Brand theme/reporting theme: **"Of Audacity"** / **"Legacy in Motion: 50 Years of Evolution"** — African
  Bank positions itself as audacious, resilient and community-rooted
  ([Integrated Report 2025 (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/jzffsxq0/african-bank-ir-2025-final-1205.pdf)). [OBS]
- Purpose: **"Advancing lives through financial and related services"**; vision **"For the people, by the
  people, serving the people"** ([1H26 presentation (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/3soalx12/african-bank-interim-results-1h26-presentation-final-2506.pdf)). [OBS]

## 2. Loyalty as the central engagement engine — Audacious Rewards

- Points-based programme rewarding everyday banking; users redeem for airtime, data, groceries, travel and
  vouchers, and via **points-to-cash** ([Audacious Rewards FAQ](https://www.audaciousrewards.co.za/faq)). [OBS]
- Double-points **Audacious Rewards Day** on the 20th of each month
  ([T&Cs v25 (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/vcklgkaf/audacious-rewards-terms-and-condition-version-25_2024_09_03-pdf-2-docx.pdf)). [OBS]
- Independent brand site: [audaciousrewards.co.za](https://www.audaciousrewards.co.za/). [OBS]

## 5. Customer-satisfaction marketing signals (FY25)

- **CSAT 95%** and **NPS 70** reported for the Personal Banking franchise
  ([FY25 SENS release (PDF)](https://www.sharenet.co.za/jsepdf/SENS_20251124_S513811.pdf)). [OBS]
- Proportion of **new-to-bank customers rose from 28% to 35%**; MyWORLD account volumes +20%. [OBS]
