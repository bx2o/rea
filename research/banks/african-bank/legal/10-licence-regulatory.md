## 2. Regulatory history — the 2014 curatorship

- On **10 August 2014** the Minister of Finance (Nhlanhla Nene), on the Registrar of Banks' advice, placed
  African Bank Limited under **curatorship** ([PMG — key milestones (PDF)](https://pmg.org.za/files/160524keypoints.pdf)). [OBS]
- Triggered by projected **R7.6bn FY2014 loss** and **R8.5bn capital need** from non-performing unsecured
  loans ([Grokipedia — African Bank](https://grokipedia.com/page/African_Bank_Limited)). [OBS]
- **Resolution plan**: "good bank" recapitalised with **R10bn equity** (SARB 50%, PIC 25%, bank consortium
  25%); "residual bank" → African Phoenix ([PIC (PDF)](https://www.pic.gov.za/DocPresentations/5.-African-Bank.pdf)). [OBS]
- SENS/current disclosures note an **unqualified audit** for FY25, with a restatement of CODI-related cash
  flows in the FY24 statement ([FY25 SENS release (PDF)](https://www.sharenet.co.za/jsepdf/SENS_20251124_S513811.pdf)). [OBS]

## 4. Conduct regulation

- **National Credit Regulator (NCR)** — African Bank is a registered credit provider (NCRCP7638); NCR has a
  free complaints division ([African Bank Complaints Policy (PDF)](https://www.africanbank.co.za/media/ijhojp0n/african-bank-customer-complaints-policy.pdf)). [OBS]
- **National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFO)** — external dispute-resolution escalation; 0860 800 900
  ([African Bank FAQs](https://www.africanbank.co.za/en/home/frequently-asked-questions/)). [OBS]
- **Section 54 Banks Act** approval was secured in 2024 for the Sasfin asset acquisitions
  ([Grokipedia — African Bank](https://grokipedia.com/page/African_Bank_Limited)). [UNVERIFIED] (secondary)
