# 03 — Products & Pricing · Discovery Bank

## Account ladder (suites)

Discovery Bank sells themed **"Suites"** of bundled products rather than a single account tier. Publicly identified tiers: **Gold, Platinum, Black, Purple** (Purple is the top tier, understood to be invitation/qualification-based). [SEEN for Gold/Platinum/Black; Purple tier existence public, qualification rules partial]

| Suite | Position | Indicative benefits (public) | Class |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gold | Entry | Everyday interest **up to 1.6%**; demand savings **up to 6%**; borrowing rate **up to 7% lower**; device/wearable offers | [SEEN] |
| Platinum | Mid | Higher engagement-linked rate boosts & fee discounts | [INFERRED] |
| Black | Upper | Highest Miles earn/spend ratios; **up to 30% off** retail redemptions (Ð-Day double) | [SEEN] |
| Purple | Top | Invitation tier; top dynamic rates and travel benefit | [SEEN/partial] |

**Fee model:** monthly admin fees are **subsidised by Vitality Money engagement**, with the marketing promise of driving fees toward **zero** at the top engagement band. Exact monthly fee rand values are published per suite and change annually — pull from the current Discovery Bank fee schedule before quoting. [SEEN, values not transcribed]

## Dynamic interest-rate engine (the core pricing differentiator)

| Instrument | Rate lever | Class |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday/transactional positive balance | Up to **1.6%** (Gold) | [SEEN] |
| Demand savings | Up to **6%** (Gold) | [SEEN] |
| Borrowing (card/overdraft) | Up to **7% lower** rate | [SEEN] |
| Driver | **Vitality Money status** (six tracked financial behaviours) | [SEEN] |
| Direction | Better status ➔ higher credit interest, lower debit interest, lower fees | [SEEN] |

> This is a **real-time, behaviour-linked pricing engine** — the single most strategic mechanic for Stremiium to study (see `08`).
