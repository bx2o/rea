## Gaps Stremiium can exploit

1. **Non-affluent under-served.** Discovery's model rewards an existing health/Vitality relationship; Stremiium can offer behavioural rewards to clients with **no prior relationship** (open-loop, token-based).
2. **Closed-loop loyalty.** Discovery Miles cannot leave the ecosystem; a transferable `$STM` reward is a superior value proposition.
3. **No digital-asset rail.** Discovery has no crypto/custody offering; Stremiium's wallet+token is a clean differentiator.
4. **Health-data sensitivity.** A purely activity/engagement-based reward (no health-data dependency) is a lighter-touch POPIA posture.
