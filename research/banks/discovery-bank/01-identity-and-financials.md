## Strategy framing

* Positioning: **"world's first fully behavioural digital bank."** Shared-value model extends Discovery's Vitality economics (Health, Drive, Money) into banking.
* Stated ambition (Pillar III FY2025): **2 million clients and R3 billion profit by FY2029**. [SEEN]
