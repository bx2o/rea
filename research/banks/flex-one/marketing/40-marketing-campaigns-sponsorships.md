# Flex (Flex.one) — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

## 1. Brand Positioning

- [OBS] Positions itself as an **"AI-native private bank"** for **high-net-worth middle-market
  business owners** — a category-creation play rather than a generic SMB neobank
  ([Fintech Magazine](https://fintechmagazine.com/globenewswire/3199876)).
- [OBS] Taglines: "Free for Every Business Owner"; "One Card to Rule Them All"
  ([flex.one Pricing](https://www.flex.one/pricing); [flex.one Business Banking](https://www.flex.one/business-banking)).

## 2. Value-Proposition Messaging

- [OBS] **$0/month**, **1% cashback**, **up to 2.47% APY** ("42× the national average"),
  **Net-60**, **up to $3M FDIC** — contrast-led marketing against incumbent business banking
  ([banklist.co](https://banklist.co/flex); [flex.one home](https://www.flex.one/)).
- [OBS] The valuation narrative is tied to an **AI + stablecoin** brand story
  ([Woofun](https://www.woofun.ai/en/news/detail/101902)).

## 3. Owned Events / Community

- [OBS] Flex hosts its own events — **10,000+ attendees at Flex-hosted events in 2024**
  ([flex.one About](https://www.flex.one/pt/company)).
- [OBS] Founder **Zaid Rahman** is the primary public face (Thiel Fellow, Columbia dropout
  narrative) ([AllScale](https://www.allscale.io/posts/how-flex-turned-a-contractor-card-into-a-unicorn-neobank)).

## 4. PR / Fundraising Marketing

- [OBS] Funding announcements are used as marketing beats ($225M Mar 2025; $60M Dec 2025;
  $70M/$1.2B Jul 2026) ([Fintech Magazine](https://fintechmagazine.com/globenewswire/3199876)).
