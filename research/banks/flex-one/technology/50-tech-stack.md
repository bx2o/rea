# Flex (Flex.one) — Technology Stack

## 1. Positioning: "AI-native"

- [OBS] Flex is marketed as an **AI-native "private bank"** for business owners, with AI-powered
  tools (e.g. an "AI Inbox" to turn vendor documents/spend into actions)
  ([Fintech Magazine](https://fintechmagazine.com/globenewswire/3199876);
  [AllScale](https://www.allscale.io/posts/how-flex-turned-a-contractor-card-into-a-unicorn-neobank)).
- [OBS] Strategic pivot toward **AI + stablecoin infrastructure** underpins the 2026 valuation
  step-up ([Woofun](https://www.woofun.ai/en/news/detail/101902)).

## 2. Payments & Rails

- [OBS] Product set spans **ACH/wires, card issuance (via partner banks), multi-currency accounts
  (32 currencies) and stablecoin rails (USDC/USDT)**
  ([flex.one Global Banking](https://www.flex.one/global-banking)).
- [OBS] **Net-60** charge card and expense-management automation imply a ledger + underwriting
  engine integrated with partner banks
  ([flex.one Pricing](https://www.flex.one/pricing)).

## 3. Partner-Bank / BaaS Integration

- [OBS] Banking services, cards and lending delivered through **Lead Bank, Column N.A. and Thread
  Bank** ([neobankbeat](https://www.neobankbeat.com/n/flex/who-owns/)).
- [INF] Flex maintains orchestration/programme-management layers over these partner APIs plus its
  own AI/UX front-end.
