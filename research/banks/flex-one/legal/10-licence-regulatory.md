# Flex (Flex.one) — Licence & Regulatory

## 1. Not a Bank — Partner-Bank (BaaS) Model

- [OBS] **Flexbase Technologies, Inc. ("Flex") is a financial technology company, not a bank**;
  business banking services are provided by **Column N.A., Member FDIC**, and the Flex Business
  Reward Card is a Visa® charge card issued by Column N.A.
  ([flex.one Business Banking](https://www.flex.one/business-banking)).
- [OBS] Flex works with multiple partner banks — **Column, Lead Bank and Thread Bank** — which hold
  customer deposits and, for lending products, make loans
  ([neobankbeat](https://www.neobankbeat.com/n/flex/who-owns/);
  [Private Law Wiki](https://wiki.private.law/en/flex.md)).
- [OBS] **FDIC insurance available up to $3M** for funds on deposit **through Thread Bank, Member
  FDIC** (via sweep/networked deposits) ([flex.one home](https://www.flex.one/)).
- [OBS] Loans, lines of credit, banking services, loan disbursements and payment transmissions are
  provided by **Lead Bank or Column N.A.** ([Private Law Wiki](https://wiki.private.law/en/flex.md)).

## 2. Regulatory Structure

- [INF] This is the standard US construction: the **regulated entity is the partner bank**; the
  fintech acts as **programme manager and agent**
  ([Private Law Wiki](https://wiki.private.law/en/flex.md)).
- [OBS] Regulators therefore are the partner banks' prudential supervisors (OCC/Fed/FDIC as
  applicable to each bank), plus the **CFPB** and state licensing for the fintech's activities
  (consumer protection).
