# Flex (Flex.one) — Overview

> Council C10 (Finance & IR) · US fintech ("digital bank") profile.
> **Entity disambiguation is critical** — see §0 below. The task brief mentioned a "rent-and-credit
> model"; that describes a **different** company (see §0).

## 0. Entity Disambiguation (read first)

- [OBS] **Flex.one** = **Flexbase Technologies, Inc.** (trading as "Flex"), a US **business-banking /
  financial-management platform for business owners**, founded **2022**
  ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250306625979/en/);
  [Private Law Wiki](https://wiki.private.law/en/flex.md)).
- [OBS] The **rent-payment "Flex"** (getflex.com, operated by **Flexible Finance, Inc.**) is a
  **separate entity**: it pays a user's full rent on the due date and lets them repay in two
  installments, with optional rent reporting to credit bureaus
  ([getflex.com](https://getflex.com/); [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Flex_rent_payment_platform)).
- [INF] The task's "rent-and-credit model" description maps to the **getflex.com** entity, while
  "Flex.one" maps to the **business-banking** entity. This file documents **Flex.one / Flexbase**;
  the rent platform is noted for completeness. **[INF]** — flagged for the orchestrator.

## 1. Identity

- [OBS] Legally **Flexbase Technologies, Inc.**, brand "Flex"; a **financial technology company,
  not a bank** ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250306625979/en/);
  [flex.one Business Banking](https://www.flex.one/business-banking)).
- [OBS] Founded **2022** by **Zaid Rahman** (a **Thiel Fellow** and Columbia dropout who previously
  launched the learning startup Volley), with co-founder **Hadi Solh**
  ([Private Law Wiki](https://wiki.private.law/en/flex.md); [YesPress](https://yespress.io/zaid-rahman)).
- [OBS] First product (2022) served **construction contractors** with a working-capital/credit card
  ([Private Law Wiki](https://wiki.private.law/en/flex.md); [AllScale](https://www.allscale.io/posts/how-flex-turned-a-contractor-card-into-a-unicorn-neobank)).

## 2. Positioning

- [OBS] "All-in-one business and personal financial management platform for business owners"
