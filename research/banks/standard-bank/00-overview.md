# Standard Bank Group — Overview

> Entity: **Standard Bank Group Limited** (JSE: SBK), and its SA banking subsidiary **The Standard Bank of South Africa Limited** (SBSA). Council C16 (Banking & Fintech). Public-information research only.

## 1. What Standard Bank is

- **Standard Bank Group** is Africa's largest bank by assets, "purpose-driven, African focused, client led and digitally enabled," with on-the-ground presence in **21 African countries** plus offshore hubs. `[OBS]` [SBG FY2025 results](https://reporting.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/Documents/Financial-results/Annual-Results/SBG-2025-Annual-Results-Booklet-(spreads).pdf)
- The SA banking entity is **The Standard Bank of South Africa Limited** (Reg. No. 1962/000738/06) — see [legal/10-licence-regulatory.md](./legal/10-licence-regulatory.md).
- Behind FNB, Standard Bank is often cited as SA's **second-largest bank by market capitalisation**. `[OBS]` [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/JSE-SBK/)

## 3. Journey (founding → today)

- **1862** — **Standard Bank of British South Africa** established in **London**, to serve British colonies in southern Africa. `[OBS]` [FinanceCharts company profile](https://www.financecharts.com/stocks/SGBLY/profile)
- **1863** — First branch opened in **Port Elizabeth**, Cape Colony. `[OBS]` same source.
- Financed the region's **mining and agricultural industries** from its earliest days. `[OBS]` same source.
- **1970** — Listed on the **JSE** (IPO/first listing 9 Feb 1970); "listed on the JSE since 1970 … >163 years of operation." `[OBS]` [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/JSE-SBK/), [SBG FY25 booklet](https://reporting.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/Documents/Financial-results/Annual-Results/SBG-2025-Annual-Results-Booklet-(spreads).pdf)
- **2026** — Capital Markets Day (March 2026) set **2026–2028 targets**; continued AI/cloud-led transformation. `[OBS]` [SBG strategic update](https://www.careers.standardbank.com/sbg/standard-bank-group/newsroom/news-and-insights/standard-bank-group-strategic-update)
