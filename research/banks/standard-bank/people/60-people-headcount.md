# Standard Bank — People, Leadership & Headcount

> Group leadership and workforce. B-BBEE from the group's own reports.

## 1. Headcount

| Metric | FY2025 | FY2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Employees (FY25 financial results) | 50,714 | 50,316 |
| Employees (Annual Integrated Report 2025) | 54,244 | 54,115 |

`[OBS]` [SBG FY25 results booklet](https://reporting.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/Documents/Financial-results/Annual-Results/SBG-2025-Annual-Results-Booklet-(spreads).pdf), [SBG Integrated Report 2025](https://www.standardbank.com/static_file/StandardBankGroup/filedownloads/RTS/2025/SBG_AnnualIntegratedReport2025.pdf)

- `[INF]` The two figures use different measurement scopes (statutory-reported employees vs broader workforce). Both are cited; the difference (~3,500) is not reconciled in reviewed sources. `[UNVERIFIED]`
- The group states it "employs over 50,000 people across its geographies". `[OBS]` [SBG FY2025 Fact Sheet](https://reporting.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/Documents/Financial-results/Annual-Results/SBG-2025-Annual-Results-Factsheet.pdf)

## 2. Leadership

- **Group CEO: Simpiwe "Sim" Tshabalala** — joined Standard Corporate and Merchant Bank in **2000**; CEO of Standard Bank South Africa in **2008**; **Joint CEO 2013–2017**; **sole Group CEO since 2017**. `[OBS]` [SBG CMD 2026 — CEO strategy update](https://reporting.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/CMD_2026/downloadable%20files/SBG-CMD-CEO.pdf)
- **Group Chairman: Nonkululeko Nyembezi(-Heita)** — Independent Chairman **since 2022**. `[OBS]` [Investing.com company profile](https://www.investing.com/equities/standard-bank-group-ltd-(j)-company-profile)
- **Chief Finance & Value Management Officer: Arno Daehnke** — supervised FY25 results preparation. `[OBS]` [SBG FY25 results](https://reporting.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/Documents/Financial-results/Annual-Results/SBG-2025-Annual-Results-Booklet-(spreads).pdf)
- **Chief Operating Officer: Margaret Nienaber** — presented Technology, AI & Payments at CMD 2026. `[OBS]` [SBG CMD 2026](https://careers.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/CMD_2026/downloadable%20files/Technology_AI-&-payments.pdf)
- Group leadership forum is the **Group Leadership Council**. `[OBS]` [SBG CMD script](https://www.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/CMD_2026/downloadable%20files/Capital%20Markets%20Day%20Script%20consolidated.pdf)

## 3. Board changes (2026)

- **Nomgando Matyumza** and **Geraldine Fraser-Moleketi** retire from the board at the 2026 AGM; **Jacko Maree** also retires at the AGM. `[OBS]` [SBG Governance Report 2025](https://www.standardbank.com/static_file/StandardBankGroup/filedownloads/RTS/2025/SBG_GovernanceReport2025.pdf)

## 4. Transformation / B-BBEE

- **Standard Bank is B-BBEE Level 1 (South Africa)** — maintained from 2024 (Level 1 both years). `[OBS]` [SBG Integrated Report 2025](https://www.standardbank.com/static_file/StandardBankGroup/filedownloads/RTS/2025/SBG_AnnualIntegratedReport2025.pdf)

## 5. Culture

- CEO Sim Tshabalala emphasises "skilful, committed and honourable people at every level", citing the Group Leadership Council. `[OBS]` [SBG CMD script](https://www.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/CMD_2026/downloadable%20files/Capital%20Markets%20Day%20Script%20consolidated.pdf)
