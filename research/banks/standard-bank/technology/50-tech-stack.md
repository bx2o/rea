# Standard Bank — Technology Stack

> Standard Bank publicly discloses a substantial cloud/AI transformation narrative. Figures below are from its own Capital Markets Day and annual results materials.

## 1. Cloud & infrastructure

- **Cloud migration:** migratable servers moved to **public cloud rose 44% (2024) → 71% (2025)**. `[OBS]` [SBG CMD 2026 — Technology, AI & Payments (Margaret Nienaber, COO)](https://careers.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/CMD_2026/downloadable%20files/Technology_AI-&-payments.pdf)
- **Legacy decommissioning:** **-88%** legacy servers (moved to cloud or switched off since 2020). `[OBS]` same.
- **Physical data-centre footprint reduced -27%.** `[OBS]` same, [InfoTechLead](https://infotechlead.com/cio/standard-bank-steps-up-ai-led-digital-transformation-with-cloud-data-analytics-and-platform-innovation-94759)
- **Cloud spend up 11× since 2020; software spend up 2×**; **73%** of technology staff are "technical". `[OBS]` [SBG 2025 Annual Results presentation](https://www.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/Documents/Financial-results/Annual-Results/SBG_2025_Annual_Results_Presentation.pdf)
- `[OBS]` Cloud/AI partners reported as **AWS** and **Microsoft Azure** (AWS case-study/industry coverage; Azure named in summaries). `[OBS]` [Classace summary](https://www.classace.io/answers/standard-bank-has-partnered-with-major-cloud-service-providers-such-as-amazon-web-services-(aws)-and-microsoft-azure-to-leverage-cloud-computing-this-collaboration-aims-to-offer-faster-innovation-flex_cdb642e0c068471570d7b7f61e213e9c)

## 2. AI

- Standard Bank runs an **enterprise AI platform built on Amazon Bedrock** with a **multi-model strategy** (open + closed models) under common governance. `[OBS]` [Wansom.ai](https://www.wansom.ai/briefly/news/standard-bank-ai-strategy-south-africa-core-capability_47bcf027-dcd1-4604-920d-fb9e1f400721)
- Ranked **leading bank in Africa** in the first **Evident AI Index for Banks — Middle East and Africa** (June 2026). `[OBS]` [EdgeX summary](https://pro.edgex.exchange/ru-RU/news/article/standard-bank-digital-payments-africa)

## 3. Payments infrastructure (OneHub / modern rails)

- Standard Bank operates a **modern, scaled payments platform**: it moved **>R164 trillion in payments in 2025** across **~20 m clients** and correspondent-banking relationships — **>R300 m/minute**. `[OBS]` [SBG newsroom — Our modern, scaled payments infrastructure](https://careers.standardbank.com/sbg/standard-bank-group/newsroom/news-and-insights/our-modern-scaled-payments-infrastructure-is-driving-growth)
- `[INF]` "OneHub" is the bank's branding for consolidated client/tech platforms; the specific internal architecture is not fully restated in reviewed sources.

## 4. Digital channels & app

- **Standard Bank / Stanbic Bank app** (Android `com.sbg.mobile.phone`): Google Play rating **~4.68** from **~480k ratings**; "award-winning"; last updated Sep 2026. `[OBS]` [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/standard-bank-for-phone-(new)/com.sbg.mobile.phone), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sbg.mobile.phone)
- **Virtual Credit Card / prepaid virtual card** created in-app with unique number/CVV; Tap to Pay, Masterpass, Samsung Pay. `[OBS]` [Standard Bank virtual card](https://www.sims.sbgzr.com/southafrica/personal/learn/create-a-prepaid-virtual-card-and-shop-online-like-a-pro)
- SA digital engagement: **67%** of transactional clients transact digitally (2025); digital clients **+9%**. `[OBS]` [SBG FY25 results](https://reporting.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/Documents/Financial-results/Annual-Results/SBG-2025-Annual-Results-Booklet-(spreads).pdf)
