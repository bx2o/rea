# Standard Bank — Customer Service & Complaints

> Complaint data from the **National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFO) – Banking Division** (formerly Ombudsman for Banking Services), plus app-store sentiment.

## 1. Ombud complaints (2025)

- **Standard Bank accounted for the largest share of formal banking complaints in 2025: 20.23%**, ahead of Absa (17.06%), Capitec (16.05%), Nedbank (15.47%) and FNB (14.88%). `[OBS]` [Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/banking-complaints-surge-as-fraudsters-follow-customers-online/)
- Complaints are heavily fraud-driven: **mobile + internet banking fraud = 39%** of all complaints; online-banking fraud complaints **+15% YoY**; the banking division **returned >R60 m** to consumers in 2025. `[OBS]` [African News Agency](https://africannewsagency.com/financial-ombuds-banking-division-returns-over-r60m-to-consumers-as-fraud-complaints-surge/)
- `[INF]` Standard Bank's large formal-complaint share is consistent with its scale as Africa's largest bank by assets and its large digital client base.

## 2. App-store sentiment

- **Standard Bank / Stanbic Bank app (Google Play): rating ~4.68 from ~480k ratings**; "award-winning". `[OBS]` [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/standard-bank-for-phone-(new)/com.sbg.mobile.phone)
- `[OBS]` MyBroadband ranked Standard Bank among the best banking apps in SA (user-review based). `[OBS]` [MyBroadband](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/banking/531571-best-banking-apps-in-south-africa.html)

## 3. Digital-inclusion & service metrics

- **67%** of transactional clients transact digitally; digital clients **+9%**; digital volumes **+5%** (2025). `[OBS]` [SBG FY25 results](https://reporting.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/Documents/Financial-results/Annual-Results/SBG-2025-Annual-Results-Booklet-(spreads).pdf)
- **Active client base 19.6 m** (FY25), enabling service-scale comparison. `[OBS]` same.

## 4. Themes

- `[INF]` Dominant complaint themes: digital-banking fraud, phishing, and unauthorised-transaction disputes — the same industry-wide pattern the Ombud highlights.
- `[INF]` High app ratings (~4.7) reflect UX satisfaction, while fraud drives formal complaints — a typical incumbent pattern.
