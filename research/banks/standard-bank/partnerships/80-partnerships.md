# Standard Bank — Partnerships & Alliances

> Strategic partnerships across sport, technology/cloud, payments, and cross-border clearing.
> Scope note: verified public information only; commercial values are frequently undisclosed.

## 1. Sport — SAFA (national football)

- **2 March 2026:** SAFA and Standard Bank announced a **landmark sponsorship** making Africa's largest bank by assets an **official partner** supporting **Bafana Bafana, Banyana Banyana and grassroots national teams from 2026 and beyond**. `[OBS]` [SAFA](https://www.safa.net/general-news/safa-standard-bank-announce-partnership-to-power-south-african-football/), [Standard Bank Sponsorships](https://sponsorships.standardbank.com/sbgs/sponsorship/news/standard-bank-and-safa-announce-partnership-to-power-south-african-football)
- Announced ahead of Bafana Bafana's 2026 global tournament qualification. Reported deal length **4 years**; **financial value not officially disclosed**. Any wider rand figure circulating in media is **`[UNVERIFIED]`** and excluded here. `[OBS]`/`[UNK]` same sources.
- **Historic football:** Standard Bank previously sponsored **Kaizer Chiefs and Orlando Pirates** (both ended ~2011; the Kaizer Chiefs shirt sponsor is now Vodacom). `[INF]` derived from public sponsorship history. See `marketing/40-marketing-campaigns-sponsorships.md`.

## 2. Technology — hyperscalers & AI

- **AWS (Amazon Web Services):** In **March 2019** Standard Bank Group selected **AWS as preferred cloud provider**, migrating production workloads incl. customer-facing platforms and strategic core banking applications, subject to regulator approval. `[OBS]` [BizNews](https://www.biznews.com/briefs/2019/03/28/standard-bank-amazon-cloud)
- **Multi-cloud ("and" strategy):** Standard Bank runs **AWS + Microsoft Azure**, with **Huawei in select African markets**; AI platform built with hyperscalers (**AWS Bedrock, Azure Foundry**). `[OBS]` [CMD 2026 — Technology, AI & Payments (Margaret Nienaber)](https://careers.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/CMD_2026/downloadable%20files/Technology_AI-&-payments.pdf), [Most AI-mature bank](https://reporting.standardbank.com/sbg/standard-bank-group/newsroom/news-and-insights/we-have-been-ranked-South-Africas-most-ai%E2%80%93mature-bank)
- CIO and newly appointed **Chief AI Officer: Jörg Fischer**; open-architecture approach to both open and closed AI models. `[OBS]` [Most AI-mature bank](https://reporting.standardbank.com/sbg/standard-bank-group/newsroom/news-and-insights/we-have-been-ranked-South-Africas-most-ai%E2%80%93mature-bank)

## 3. Payments & cross-border corridors

- **CIPS (Cross-border Interbank Payment System):** Standard Bank is described as the **first African bank to plug clients directly into the Africa–Asia payment corridor via CIPS**, processing **R9.5 billion since launch** (late 2024/2025). `[UNVERIFIED]` (single corporate newsroom source) [Standard Bank newsroom](https://careers.standardbank.com/sbg/standard-bank-group/newsroom/news-and-insights/our-modern-scaled-payments-infrastructure-is-driving-growth)
- **Renminbi clearing bank:** reported that in **June 2026** the People's Bank of China authorised **Standard Bank and ICBC to jointly operate as the Renminbi Clearing Bank of Africa** (clearing RMB in 19 African countries). `[UNVERIFIED]` (single third-party source) [XTransfer](https://www.xtransfer.com/blog/south-africa-cross-border-payment-platforms)
- **Payments as platform:** RMB/Renminbi, tokenised deposits, stablecoins and digital-asset custody/investment listed as focus areas. `[OBS]` [CMD 2026](https://careers.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/CMD_2026/downloadable%20files/Technology_AI-&-payments.pdf)
- **Programmatic partnership inventory:** 35 partnership records logged (third-party tracker), latest involving **Nigeria's OPay**. `[UNVERIFIED]` [Usearch](https://www.usearch.com/company/standard-bank/overview)

## 4. Financial-services / group alliances

- **Stanbic bank sister entities:** Standard Bank Group operates as **Stanbic Bank** across much of Africa (e.g. Stanbic Bank Kenya, Uganda, Ghana, Nigeria) — an intra-group brand/operating model rather than an external JV. `[OBS]` group structure. See `legal/10-licence-regulatory.md`.
- **Insurance & asset management:** wholly-owned (Liberty, Stanlib) — see `financials/20-financials.md`.
