# Standard Bank — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

> Public marketing/sponsorship footprint. Sponsorship values frequently undisclosed; unconfirmed figures flagged.

## 1. Brand positioning

- Group purpose/tagline: "**Africa is our home, we drive her growth**." `[OBS]` [SBG FY25 results](https://reporting.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/Documents/Financial-results/Annual-Results/SBG-2025-Annual-Results-Booklet-(spreads).pdf)
- Ranked as a leading SA brand by **Brand Finance (2025 report)**. `[OBS]` [SBG FY2024 results overview](https://www.standardbank.com/static_file/Investor%20Relations/Documents/Financial-results/Annual-Results/SBG_2024AnnualResultsOverview.pdf)

## 2. Football — SAFA / Bafana Bafana (current flagship)

- **2 March 2026:** Standard Bank and **SAFA** announced a **four-year** partnership (2026–2030) making the bank an **official premier sponsor** of **Bafana Bafana, Banyana Banyana and grassroots national teams**, ahead of the **2026 FIFA World Cup**. `[OBS]` [SAFA](https://www.safa.net/general-news/safa-standard-bank-announce-partnership-to-power-south-african-football/), [SBG sponsorship page](https://sponsorships.standardbank.com/sbgs/sponsorship/news/standard-bank-and-safa-announce-partnership-to-power-south-african-football)
- **Value: officially undisclosed** (executives declined to disclose). `[OBS]` [Daba Finance](https://www.dabafinance.com/en/news/standard-bank-bafana-bafana-world-cup-sponsorship)
  - A third-party outlet reports a **R5.2 bn** figure. `[UNVERIFIED]` [Cape Town Today](https://capetown.today/news/standard-bank-comes-to-bafana-bafana-rescue-ahead-of-fifa-world-cup)
- The bank frames the tie-up as data-driven brand/engagement strategy tied to the World Cup. `[OBS]` [Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/a-sports-sponsorship-evolution-from-a-logo-to-being-part-of-the-conversation-290967a)

## 3. Arts & culture — Standard Bank Young Artist Awards

- The **Standard Bank Young Artist Awards** are "one of the country's most respected acknowledgements of creative excellence", awarded annually with winners presenting new work at the **National Arts Festival** (e.g. 2026 festival: 25 June – 5 July). `[OBS]` [Standard Bank sponsorship page](https://sponsorships.standardbank.com/sbgs/sponsorship/arts-and-culture/young-artist-award), [Music In Africa](https://www.musicinafrica.net/magazine/standard-bank-announces-2026-young-artists-ahead-national-arts-festival)
- 2026 winners included Gabi Motuba (jazz) and Ndumiso Manana (music). `[OBS]` [Music In Africa](https://www.musicinafrica.net/magazine/standard-bank-announces-2026-young-artists-ahead-national-arts-festival)

## 4. Historical sports sponsorships (for competitive context)

- **Cricket (Proteas / CSA):** Standard Bank sponsored SA cricket **1998–2011**, returned **2016**, and **ended the deal on 30 April 2020** (association dating to 1998). `[OBS]` [ESPNcricinfo](https://www.espncricinfo.com/story/south-africa-lose-title-sponsor-as-freefall-continues-1209203)
- **Soccer:** Standard Bank sponsored **Kaizer Chiefs** and **Orlando Pirates**, and **ended both** along with the cricket deal (13-year CSA sponsorship) in ~2011 to "improve business efficiencies". `[OBS]` [Retail Banker International](https://www.retailbankerinternational.com/news/standard-bank-ends-cricket-soccer-sponsorship/)
  - `[INF]` The task brief's "Standard Bank / Kaizer Chiefs" refers to this **historical** (pre-2011) relationship — the current club shirt sponsor is Vodacom.
