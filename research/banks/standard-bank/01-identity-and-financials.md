## Ownership & group structure notes

- No controlling shareholder; the group is controlled through a widely distributed share register and
  a board-governed structure. — INFERRED / medium
- Insurance and wealth arms are held through group subsidiaries, creating intra-group capital flows
  that make the standalone-bank capital ratios differ from the group ratios. — INFERRED / low
