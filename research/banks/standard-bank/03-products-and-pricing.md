# 03 — Products & Pricing: Standard Bank Group

## Retail account ladder

| Tier | Positioning | Typical monthly fee | Class / Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MyMo** | Mass-market entry transactional account | Low monthly fee (pay-as-you-use) | SEEN (pricing) / medium |
| **Elite** | Mid-market chequing | Mid monthly fee | SEEN (pricing) / medium |
| **Prestige** | Affluent | Higher monthly fee | SEEN (pricing) / medium |
| **Private Banking** | Wealth / HNW | Relationship-priced | SEEN (docs) / medium |
| Business current accounts | SME/BCC segment | Tiered | SEEN (docs) / medium |

> **Exact fee amounts vary by year and plan option and are not restated here.** Use the current Standard
> Bank published retail fee schedule ("Pricing Guide") as the authoritative source. — verify before reuse

## Fee & transaction structure (typical SA incumbents — verify per schedule)

| Item | Typical structure | Class |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly account fee | Fixed per tier | SEEN / medium |
| Electronic payments | Pay-as-you-use or bundled | SEEN / medium |
| Cash withdrawals | Tiered (own ATM → other ATM → branch) | SEEN / medium |
| Card interchange | Scheme-set, capped by SARB interchange rules | SEEN / high |
| Overdraft/credit | Risk-priced interest | INFERRED / medium |
| International/forex | Shyft margin + FX spread | INFERRED / low |

## Pricing findings summary

1. A clean four-rung ladder (MyMo→Elite→Prestige→Private) is a proven adoption funnel. SEEN/medium
2. Fee schedules are dense and change annually — a Stremiium attack surface is **fee transparency**. SEEN/medium
3. Merchant/interchange economics are regulated, limiting incumbent margin levers. SEEN/high
