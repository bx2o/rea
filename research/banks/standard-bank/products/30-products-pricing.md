# Standard Bank — Products & Pricing

> Fees are **VAT-inclusive (15%)** and effective **1 January 2026** unless stated. Confirm at point of sale — fees change annually.

## 2. Cards

- **Gold Credit Card** — create a **Virtual Credit Card** linked to it for safer online shopping; Tap to Pay, SnapScan, Masterpass. `[OBS]` [Standard Bank Gold Credit Card](https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products-and-services/bank-with-us/credit-cards/our-cards/gold-credit-card)
- **Prepaid Virtual Card** — standalone digital card with its own number/expiry/CVV; no link to your account details. `[OBS]` [Standard Bank virtual card](https://www.sims.sbgzr.com/southafrica/personal/learn/create-a-prepaid-virtual-card-and-shop-online-like-a-pro)

## 3. Loans

- **Personal Overdraft:** monthly service fee **R69**; once-off initiation fee up to **R1,207.50** (VAT incl.). `[OBS]` [Standard Bank Overdraft](https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/personal/products-and-services/borrow-for-your-needs/personal-loans/our-loans/overdraft)

## 5. Loyalty — UCount Rewards

- **UCount Rewards** is Standard Bank's rewards programme (personal + business variants). `[OBS]` [UCount programme rules](https://ucount.standardbank.co.za/personal/wp-content/uploads/0541/2018/02/UCount-Rewards-New-Programme-Rules.pdf)
- Card earning: **0.05% up to 1%** of qualifying spend; tiered by banking activity and products; **up to 40% back** at chosen category retailers (grocery/fashion/lifestyle); **up to 30%** at Rewards Retailers. `[OBS]` [UCount rules (eff. Apr 2026)](https://dlvjpsxz62zol.cloudfront.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/UCount-Rewards-Programme-Rules_Effective-April-2026.pdf), [Signature pricing](https://www.standardbank.co.za/static_file/South%20Africa/PDF/Personal%20Pricing/2026/Signature_Banking_Account_Pricing_Guide_2026.pdf)
- **Redemption cap: 200,000 points (R20,000)/month.** Tiering Points determine tier level (T1: 0–400; T2: 401–550; T3: 551–700+). `[OBS]` [UCount rules](https://ucount.standardbank.co.za/personal/wp-content/uploads/0541/2018/02/UCount-Rewards-New-Programme-Rules.pdf)
- Participation cost cited as **~R25/month** by a third-party comparison. `[UNVERIFIED]` [FinanSmart](https://finansmart.com.br/credit-card-with-rewards-south-africa/)
