# VALR — Entity Overview

## 1. Identity

| Field | Value | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Name | VALR (VALR Pty Ltd) | [OBS] |
| Founded | **2018** | [OBS] |
| Founders | **Farzam Ehsani** (CEO, ex-FirstRand/Barclays Africa; ex-Deloitte) and **Badi Sudhakaran** (Co-founder & Chief Product Officer) | [OBS] |
| Headquarters | Johannesburg (Sandton), South Africa | [OBS] |
| Ownership | Privately held, VC-backed | [OBS] |

| CMO | Ben Caselin | [OBS] |

VALR positions itself as "Africa's largest crypto exchange by trade volume" and the "**exchange of choice for more than 1.8 million users globally**". [OBS]

## 2. Regulatory standing

- **CASP Category I & II** licence from the FSCA — announced **15 April 2024**; among the first crypto platforms approved in both categories. [OBS]
- **Over-The-Counter Derivatives Provider (ODP)** licence + an additional **FSP** licence from the FSCA — **20 October 2025**. [OBS]

## 3. Product suite

Spot trading, Spot Margin (up to 10x), Perpetual Futures (up to 60x), Liquid Staking, Lending/Borrowing, OTC desk, **VALR Invest**, **VALR Pay** (free pay-anyone), institutional API and Business/white-label services. [OBS]

## 4. Funding

- Series B **US$50m** (announced 28 Feb 2022), led by **Pantera Capital**; total raised ~US$54.9m. Pre-money valuation ~US$190m (Crunchbase); another source cites ~US$240m post-money. [OBS]
- Investors: Pantera Capital, Coinbase Ventures, Alameda Research, Cadenza, CMT Digital, Distributed Global, GSR, Third Prime, Avon Ventures (Fidelity affiliate / F-Prime), Bittrex, 4Di Capital. [OBS]
