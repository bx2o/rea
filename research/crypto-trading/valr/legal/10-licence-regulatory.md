# VALR — Licences & Regulation

## 1. FSCA CASP licence (Category I & II)

- On **15 April 2024** VALR was granted both a **Category I** and **Category II** licence by the FSCA as a **Crypto Asset Service Provider (CASP)**, making it one of the first platforms approved in both categories in South Africa. [OBS]
- Category I covers basic exchange and advisory services; Category II permits discretionary portfolio management under certain mandates. [OBS]

## 2. ODP + FSP licence (Oct 2025)

- On **20 October 2025**, VALR was granted an **Over-The-Counter Derivatives Provider (ODP)** licence plus an **additional FSP licence** by the FSCA — reportedly one of the first in SA for crypto assets. [OBS]
- This enables VALR to offer **CFDs, quarterly and perpetual futures, options, forwards and swaps** with crypto underlyings, plus a limited set of traditional instruments (shares, bonds). [OBS]

## 3. AML / travel rule & exchange control

- As a CASP, VALR is an accountable institution under **FICA** and subject to **Directive 9** (travel rule) for crypto-asset transfers (effective **30 April 2025**). VALR uses **Sumsub** for travel-rule compliance and financial-crime prevention. [OBS]
- Crypto treated as **capital** for exchange-control purposes (draft Capital Flow Management Regulations). [OBS]

## 4. Corporate / group

- VALR is listed in the FSCA public CASP directory. [OBS]
- It also has **EU regulatory approval** and preliminary clearance from Dubai's **VARA** (expansion). [OBS]
