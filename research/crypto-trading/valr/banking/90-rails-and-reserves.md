## 2. Proof of reserves (PoR)

- VALR completes **independent Proof-of-Reserves audits by Hacken** (blockchain security firm). [OBS]
- **August 2025** audit and a further audit **conducted 19 August 2026** (report dated **1 September 2026**). [OBS]
- Findings: **1:1 collateralisation** for all in-scope digital assets, with **collateral ratios exceeding 100%** for BTC, ETH, SOL, USDT, USDC and others. [OBS]
- Four procedures covered: Proof of Liabilities, Proof of Ownership, Reserves Calculation, PoR Assessment. [OBS]

## 3. Caveats

- As with Luno, PoR is an **independent attestation**, not a statutory audit; it addresses **reserve sufficiency** (assets ≥ liabilities), not solvency or custody quality. [INF]
