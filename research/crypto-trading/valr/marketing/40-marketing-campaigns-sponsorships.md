# VALR — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

## 1. Brand positioning

- "**Pro Trading, For Everyone**" — bridging retail simplicity and institutional sophistication. [OBS]
- CMO **Ben Caselin** (based in Hong Kong, joined VALR ~2023) leads brand locally and globally. [OBS]

## 2. Flagship sponsorship — DHL Stormers

| Field | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Partner | **DHL Stormers** (rugby) | [OBS] |
| Role | Official crypto exchange partner | [OBS] |
| Term | 3 years, **1 Sep 2025 – 31 Aug 2028** | [OBS] |
| Scope | Stormers teams in URC, Champions Cup, Currie Cup, U19/U21 | [OBS] |
| Assets | VALR logo on jerseys/kits, DHL Stadium presence, **VIP suite** | [OBS] |

## 3. Campaigns

- **"Crypto For Everyone"** campaign: VALR flew a **helicopter with a banner** around the country to raise awareness. [OBS]
- 2025 retrospective communications emphasised the ODP licence, DHL Stormers deal and awareness push. [OBS]
