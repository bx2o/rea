# Luno — Customer Service & Complaints

## 1. Recurring complaint themes

| Theme | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Withdrawal delays** | Incidents where withdrawals stalled due to a **third-party banking partner** (e.g. 29 Apr 2026 SA withdrawal delays) | [OBS] |
| **Instant Buy outage (SA)** | "Instant Buy not available in South Africa" identified on Luno's status page (Aug 2026) | [OBS] |
| **Bank rail removals** | **Absa Instant Deposits discontinued** (change in a banking partner); Capitec customers must use Capitec Pay | [OBS] |
| **Market-exit friction** | Customers in exited regions given deadline to **sell all positions by 31 Aug 2026**; accounts closed 1 Sep; monthly fees on leftover fiat | [OBS] |
| **App-store complaints** | User reviews cite slow withdrawals (e.g. withdrawn 19 Dec, not received by 21 Dec) | [OBS] |

## 2. Service model

- Support via help centre + status page; no clear SLA published. [UNK]
- Exchange-adjacent issues often trace to **banking partners** (Standard Bank EFT, Stitch for instant deposits), not Luno's own rails. [INF]

## 3. Assessment

Luno's complaint profile is dominated by **payment-rail and availability incidents** rather than security breaches (CryptoSlate notes 2026 events were availability/footprint rather than security). The 2026 market-exit wind-down created a distinct, high-severity complaint cohort. [INF]
