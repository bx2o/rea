Maker fees range from **0% down to a rebate of ~-0.02%**; taker fees range from **0.60% down to ~0.05%** as volume rises (Luno says its negative maker fees are the lowest in SA). Exact tiers depend on pair and volume. [OBS]/[UNVERIFIED for exact tier values]
