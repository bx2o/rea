# Luno — Financials

## 1. Disclosure status

Luno is a **privately held subsidiary of Digital Currency Group (DCG)**; it does **not publish standalone audited financial statements**. Revenue, costs and profitability are therefore **not publicly disclosed** — [UNK]. [OBS]

## 3. Inference

Fees are Luno's primary disclosed revenue mechanism; the **pivot to B2B/white-label** and **stablecoin (ZARU)** initiatives [INF] suggest an attempt to diversify away from volatile retail trading revenue after a ~20% workforce cut. Precise financials cannot be verified from public sources. [INF]
