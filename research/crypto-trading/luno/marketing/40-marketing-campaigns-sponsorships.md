# Luno — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

## 1. Brand positioning

- Retail-education-led: "learn about crypto", safety, simplicity, regulated trust. [OBS]
- SA country manager **Christo de Wit** is the public spokesperson for local marketing. [OBS]

## 2. Agency

- Luno appointed **Ogilvy South Africa** after a three-way pitch to handle its brand; Ogilvy framed it as creating impact "for millions of South Africans in tomorrow's new money world". (Year [UNK].) [OBS]

## 3. Sponsorships

| Partner | Deal | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vodacom Bulls** (Pretoria rugby) | 3-year sponsorship | Bulls players receive **performance incentives in crypto** | [OBS] |
| Discovery Bank | Product partnership (co-marketing) | In-app crypto trading, Vitality points (see `partnerships/80-partnerships.md`) | [OBS] |

## 4. Campaigns

- **Luno Pay launch (7 Oct 2025)**: "spend crypto at 700,000 merchants in SA" positioned as crypto becoming mainstream; framed with rewards messaging. [OBS]

## 5. Assessment

Luno leans on **sport sponsorship + education + regulatory trust** rather than aggressive acquisition marketing. The Vodacom Bulls deal (performance incentives paid in crypto) is a distinctive, PR-friendly proof-of-concept. Budget figures are **[UNK]**. [INF]
