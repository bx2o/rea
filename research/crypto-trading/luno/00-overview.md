# Luno — Entity Overview

## 1. Identity

| Field | Value | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Legal/operating name | Luno (formerly **BitX**, renamed Luno in 2016) | [OBS] |
| Founded | 2013 | [OBS] |
| Founders | Marcus Swanepoel (ex-investment banker) and Timothy Stranex (ex-Google engineer); some sources also list Carel van Wyk and Pieter Heyns | [OBS] |
| Headquarters | London (registered/home), with regional hubs in Singapore and Cape Town | [OBS] |
| Ultimate owner | Digital Currency Group (**DCG**), which acquired Luno outright in **2020** | [OBS] |
| Employees | ~400 globally at peak (press kit); subsequently restructured | [OBS] |
| Customers | ~5 million (early), ~15–16 million users claimed by 2025 | [OBS] |
| SA country manager | Christo de Wit | [OBS] |
| CEO | James Lanigan (since March 2023); co-founder Marcus Swanepoel became Executive Chairman | [OBS] |

Luno launched in South Africa in 2013 as **BitX**, focused on Bitcoin education and wallet services, then expanded across sub-Saharan Africa and into Southeast Asia. Early backing came from Naspers/Prosus (USD 3m in 2015) and Digital Currency Group, which later acquired the company outright. [OBS]

## 2. Positioning

- **Retail-first on-ramp**: simple "instant buy/sell", ZAR wallet, education, monthly proof-of-reserves. [OBS]
- **Regulated**: claims the title of **South Africa's first licensed crypto asset service provider** (FSCA, April 2024) — see `legal/10-licence-regulatory.md`. [OBS]
- **Product suite**: Wallet, Instant Buy/Sell, Luno Exchange (advanced order book), Staking, Luno Pay (crypto spend at ~700,000 merchants), and the planned ZARU ZAR-pegged stablecoin. [OBS]

## 3. Notable 2023–2026 events

- **Mar 2023**: leadership transition — Swanepoel → Executive Chairman, COO James Lanigan → CEO; accompanied by restructuring. [OBS]
- **2023**: substantial global layoffs (reported ~35%). [UNVERIFIED]
- **2024**: became, per multiple secondary sources, SA's first FSCA-licensed crypto asset service provider (April 2024). [OBS]
- **2025–2026**: ~20% of global staff cut in an automation-driven restructuring; pivot to B2B (white-label crypto for banks/fintechs) and local stablecoins; **exit of the EU and other non-core markets** by 1 September 2026. [OBS]
- **Dec 2025**: partnership with **Discovery Bank** to offer in-app crypto trading (Vitality Money points on crypto balances) — Luno's marquee bank partnership. [OBS]

## 4. Programme relevance (NeoBank)

Luno is the reference for **how a licensed crypto on-ramp becomes a banking partner** (Discovery Bank white-label/in-app integration) and for **proof-of-reserves trust signalling**, both directly relevant to a SA NeoBank considering crypto rails. [INF]
