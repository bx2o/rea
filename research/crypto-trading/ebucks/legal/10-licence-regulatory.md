# eBucks — Licences & Regulation

## 1. Licensing status

- **eBucks is a loyalty/rewards programme, not a licensed financial product.** It is **not** separately FSCA-registered — it operates under FNB (FirstRand Bank), which holds the banking licence; the programme itself is governed by **T&Cs** rather than a standalone FSCA licence. [INF]
- Loyalty points are **not money** and generally **cannot be converted to cash** — they are a marketing/rewards mechanism. [OBS]

## 2. Applicable regulation (inferred)

| Regime | Relevance | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer Protection Act (CPA) | Marketing & T&Cs fairness | [INF] |
| POPIA | Processing of customer data for personalisation | [INF] |
| FSCA / FAIS | Only indirectly (via FNB); points not a financial service in their own right | [INF] |
| Banking regulation (SARB/PA) | Via FNB/FirstRand | [INF] |

## 3. Notes

- If a NeoBank issued **transferable/redeemable** points with monetary value, additional scrutiny (e-money, FIC) could apply — a design consideration, not an eBucks fact. [INF]
