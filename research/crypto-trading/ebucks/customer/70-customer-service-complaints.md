# eBucks — Customer Service & Complaints

## 1. Recurring complaint themes

| Theme | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Rule complexity** | Earn rules and reward-level criteria are widely seen as **complicated**; "free-and-easy" points threshold perception | [OBS] |
| **Devaluation / tier downgrades** | **July 2025** levelling criteria change: free-and-easy points dropped 10,000 → 8,500; most customers fell **Level 4 → Level 3** | [OBS] |
| **Net-cost pressure** | To offset losses, customers pushed to buy insurance with minimum covers (R4m Premier / R6m Private) and meet new spend criteria | [OBS] |
| **Social backlash** | Angered customers threatened to switch banks; "partner losses, tier downgrades and customer backlash" narrative | [OBS] |

## 2. Counter-evidence

- FNB positions growth as healthy: **R2.3bn** rewarded (+9% YoY), 8m customers, 87% active — i.e., complaints coexist with high engagement. [OBS]

## 3. Assessment

The traditional loyalty-programme risk is **devaluation backlash**: tightening earn criteria to control cost erodes the perceived value and trust. eBucks shows both the upside (mass engagement) and the downside (level downgrades → churn threats). Key NeoBank design lesson: **make earn rules simple and avoid retrospective devaluation**. [INF]
