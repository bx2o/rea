## 2. FNB × Pick n Pay milestones

- **R600 million** in customer rewards in first 12 months (Apr 2025–). [OBS]
- **31 banking kiosks** installed inside PnP stores; weekly pop-up banking across **200** locations. [OBS]
- 3.4 million "99c bread" vouchers since Sep 2024. [OBS]

## 3. Assessment

eBucks is essentially a **two-sided partner marketplace**: retailers co-fund earn rates for footfall/customer acquisition; FNB supplies the customer base and data. The **Pick n Pay** deal (replacing Checkers) is the single most material partnership shift. [INF]
