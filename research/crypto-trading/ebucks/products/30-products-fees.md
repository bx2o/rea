# eBucks — Products & Fees

> eBucks has **no purchase fee** (free to join for FNB/RMB customers). "Fees" here = the **earn/redemption mechanics**. Rules are published annually and **change**; values below are **2025/2026 earn rules** as at **2026-10-16**.

## 1. How it works (mechanics)

1. **Qualify** — hold qualifying FNB/RMB accounts and meet monthly criteria.
2. **Earn** — collect points monthly from banking behaviour (**Smart Spend**) and partner spend.
3. **Reward level** — monthly points determine a **reward level 1–5** (higher level = higher earn/redeem rates). [OBS]

- Currency: **R1 spend = eB10**; **eBucks never expire**. [OBS]

## 2. Earn mechanics

| Mechanism | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Smart Spend | Points on qualifying card spend | [OBS] |
| Partner spend | Additional eBucks from retail partners (stackable) | [OBS] |
| Product holdings | FNB Insurance, FNB Connect, etc. drive level | [OBS] |
| Virtual card | Many max-reward tiers require **virtual card** use (70+ exempt) | [OBS] |
| Fuel | Up to **R8/litre** back at Engen (private wealth tier) | [OBS] |

## 3. Illustrative earn rates (2025/26 guides)

| Partner | Headline earn | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Pick n Pay (asap!) | up to **30%** back | [OBS] |
| Pick n Pay (in-store) | up to **20%** back | [OBS] |
| Pick n Pay Clothing | up to **30%** back | [OBS] |
| Clicks (virtual card) | up to **15%** back | [OBS] |
| ARC stores | up to **30%** back | [OBS] |
| Checkers / SPAR | up to **15%** back (private wealth) | [OBS] |
| Woolworths | up to **25%** back (varies by level) | [OBS] |

> Max earn rates apply at the **highest reward level** and often require virtual-card payment and specific account tiers. [INF]

## 4. Redemption

- Redeem as **in-partner discounts**, **travel**, and partner **vouchers** (e.g. Makro, Checkers/Shoprite, Mr Price, Ster Kinekor, Nu Metro, KFC, Dis-Chem). [OBS]
