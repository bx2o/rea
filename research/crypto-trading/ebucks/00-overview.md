# eBucks — Entity Overview

> **Note:** eBucks is **not a crypto/trading platform**; it is **FNB's (FirstRand's) bank loyalty programme**, included here as the "loyalty/gamification" reference for the NeoBank programme (see `../00-ZA-CRYPTO-LANDSCAPE.md`).

## 3. Positioning

eBucks rewards **banking behaviour** (Smart Spend, product holdings, responsible banking) with a points currency redeemable at a **retail partner network** and on travel. It sits at the centre of FNB's customer-engagement and retention strategy. [OBS]

## 4. Programme relevance (NeoBank)

eBucks is the **gold-standard SA loyalty teardown**: behavioural nudge engine + partner-funded rewards + reward-level gamification. Directly relevant to a NeoBank loyalty/gamification play. [INF]
