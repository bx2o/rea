# eBucks — Banking Rails & Reserves

> eBucks is a **loyalty currency**, not a deposit-taking or custody product; "reserves" in the crypto-exchange sense **do not apply**. [INF]

## 1. Rails

| Function | Mechanism | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Issuance | Points credited to eBucks account on qualifying spend/behaviour | [OBS] |
| Card rails | FNB **Virtual Card** / physical FNB cards gate max earn | [OBS] |
| Redemption | In-app vouchers, partner discounts, **eBucks Travel** on the FNB App | [OBS] |
| Physical footprint | **31 banking kiosks** in Pick n Pay stores + 200 pop-up locations | [OBS] |
| Settlement | Points settle into the eBucks wallet; no external settlement rail | [OBS] |

## 2. "Reserve"/liability note

- Points are a **liability on FNB's balance sheet** (deferred reward cost) rather than a backed reserve; there is **no proof-of-reserves** concept. [INF]
- Value is **partner co-funded** (retailers contribute), reducing FNB's net cost. [INF]

## 3. Assessment

For a NeoBank, the eBucks "rails" lesson is **distribution**: put the loyalty inside the banking app **and** inside partner stores (kiosks/pop-ups) to drive both engagement and partner footfall. [INF]
