# eBucks — Technology & Tech Stack

## 1. Delivery surface

| Layer | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Primary channel | **FNB App** (eBucks earn/redeem, eBucks Travel, Partner Shop) | [OBS] |
| Partner integrations | eBucks Partner Shop (Hirsch's, iStore, FNB Connect); **The ENTERTAINER** 2-for-1 offers | [OBS] |
| Redemption tech | In-app vouchers, travel booking, streaming discounts | [OBS] |
| Card tech | **Virtual Card** gating for max earn tiers | [OBS] |
| Data engine | Behavioural "Smart Spend" scoring → monthly reward levels | [OBS] |

## 2. What is NOT public

- Underlying personalisation/recommendation engine, cloud stack, and data-science architecture are **[UNK]**. [UNK]

## 3. Assessment

eBucks' technology is **app-embedded and data-driven**: it converts banking behaviour into a scored reward level and merchandises partner offers inside the FNB App. The moat is **data + partner network**, not novel infrastructure. [INF]
