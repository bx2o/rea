# eBucks — Financials

> eBucks is a **cost centre / marketing programme** inside FNB; it is not separately reported. Figures below are **reward pay-out values** disclosed in press releases, not revenue. [INF]

## 1. Reward pay-outs (public)

| Metric | Value | Period | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Value rewarded | **R2.3 billion** (+9% YoY) | FY2025 / trailing 12 months | [OBS] |
| Value rewarded | **R2.2 billion** | trailing 12 months to Jun 2025 | [OBS] |
| Value paid out | **R2.1 billion** | past financial year (to Sep 2025) | [OBS] |
| Travel sales | **R1.4 billion** (+16% YoY) | past FY | [OBS] |
| Engen (fuel) eBucks paid | **R295 million** | trailing 12 months | [OBS] |
| Clicks eBucks paid | **R98 million** | trailing 12 months | [OBS] |
| Pick n Pay partnership rewards | **R600 million** in first 12 months (Apr 2025–) | trailing 12 months | [OBS] |
| Cumulative paid | **>R25 billion** | since 2000 | [OBS] |

## 2. Growth

- eBucks rewards grew **9% YoY** in 2025, "outpacing inflation"; YTD up **3%**, forecast to reach ~4% by FirstRand FY-end (30 June 2026). [OBS]

## 3. Assessment

eBucks is a **large, growing marketing spend** (~R2.3bn/yr in rewards) that FNB uses to drive **cross-sell and retention**, largely **partner-funded** (retailers co-fund earn rates for footfall). The true cost split (FNB vs partner) is **[UNK]**. [INF]
