# eBucks — Marketing, Campaigns & Sponsorships

## 1. Brand positioning

- **"Not transactional — transformational"**: eBucks markets itself as helping customers "do life confidently while saving and making smarter financial decisions". [OBS]
- Promise: "ease of use, real value, and helpful rewards". [OBS]

## 2. Awards

- **South African Loyalty Awards 2025** — top recognition for industry innovation. [OBS]

## 3. Campaigns & partner co-marketing

| Campaign | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Pick n Pay partnership** (Feb 2025) | Named PnP eBucks' **primary retail partner**; up to 30% back on asap!/Clothing, 20% in-store; replaced Checkers | [OBS] |
| **"99c bread offer"** | 3.4 million vouchers given since Sep 2024 | [OBS] |
| **Tech/device rewards** | up to 40% back on devices via **eBucks Partner Shop** (Hirsch's, iStore, FNB Connect) over 24 months | [OBS] |
| **Streaming** | up to 40% back on Netflix/Spotify via Virtual Card (family banking) | [OBS] |

## 4. Assessment

eBucks' marketing is **embedded in partner retail experiences** (Pick n Pay kiosks/pop-ups) rather than standalone ad campaigns — a **co-funded, distribution-led** approach. Ad-spend is **[UNK]**; the measurable lever is reward pay-out (~R2.3bn/yr). [INF]
