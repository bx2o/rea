> All facts carry per-entity tags in the child files; this document indexes and compares.

## 3. Notable cross-cutting 2025–2026 events

1. **Discovery Bank × Luno** (from **Dec 2025**): first SA mobile bank to offer in-app crypto trading; clients earn **Vitality Money Savings points** on crypto balances. [OBS]
2. **FNB × VALR "Crypto Investing"** (**6 Oct 2026**): crypto trading for ~**9 million** FNB retail customers. [OBS]
3. **VALR ODP licence** (**20 Oct 2025**): CFDs, perps, options, forwards, swaps on crypto. [OBS]
4. **Luno EU exit** (deadline **1 Sep 2026**) + ~20% global layoffs; pivot to **B2B/white-label** and **ZARU** ZAR-stablecoin. [OBS]
5. **EasyEquities** client assets reach **R94.93bn** (+41.2% YoY), 1.24m active clients. [OBS]
6. **eBucks** pays **R2.3bn** in rewards (2025, +9%); names **Pick n Pay** primary retail partner. [OBS]

## 4. Key patterns (derived)

- **Crypto is being absorbed into banks, not replacing them.** Both Discovery Bank and FNB now embed crypto via licensed exchanges (Luno, VALR) rather than building it. A NeoBank's fastest route is a **CASP partnership/white-label**. [DER]
- **Trust signalling is table stakes**: monthly **proof-of-reserves** (both Luno and VALR) + named custodians/auditors. [DER]
- **Fee convergence**: exchange maker/taker fees are compressing toward **0% maker**; retail "simple buy/sell" still charges **1.6–2%**. [DER]
- **Loyalty is a distribution weapon**: eBucks shows a co-funded partner marketplace inside the app is the engagement engine for a bank. [DER]

## 5. Corrections / caveats carried from research

- **[UNCORROBORATED]** The brief's "**VALR/Vitality**" pairing is not supported: research places the **Vitality Money** crypto-rewards integration with **Luno/Discovery Bank**, and the **FNB** crypto integration with **VALR**. Treat the two partnerships as distinct.
- **Licence numbers** for Luno conflict across secondary sources (FSP 51334 vs 53314 vs 53308) — verify against the **FSCA register**. [UNK]
- **Fees and limits change frequently**; all fee values are dated **2026-10-16**.
