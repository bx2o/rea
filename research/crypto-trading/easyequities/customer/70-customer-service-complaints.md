# EasyEquities — Customer Service & Complaints

## 1. Recurring complaint themes

| Theme | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Slow/absent support** | "The most repeated complaint"; no live chat, slow phone support | [OBS] |
| **Withdrawals** | Sold-investment funds require **5 business days** to settle before withdrawal; users report delays | [OBS] |
| **Verification / account locks** | Widely reported, frustrating | [OBS] |
| **Outages & bugs** | Platform outages, clunky tools, "confusing fees" | [OBS] |
| **Regulatory-scope confusion** | WikiFX risk alert (no forex/derivatives licence) can be mis-read as "unregulated" | [OBS] |

## 2. Service model

- Phased to be email/help-centre led; settlement-based withdrawal delay is by design (5 business days). [OBS]

## 3. Assessment

EasyEquities' complaint profile is a **classic scaling-CX problem** (support latency, verification, outages) rather than fraud or loss of funds. The 5-day settlement withdrawal delay is a documented policy, not a defect. [INF]
