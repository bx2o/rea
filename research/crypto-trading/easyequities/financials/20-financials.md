# EasyEquities / Purple Group — Financials

> EasyEquities financials are disclosed at **"Easy Group"** level within JSE-listed **Purple Group Limited** results.

## 1. FY25 (year ended 28 February 2025)

| Metric | Value | Change | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy Group revenue | **R450.2m** | +25.0% (from R360.2m) | [OBS] |
| Profit after tax | **R79.5m** | +76.6% (from R45.0m) | [OBS] |
| Client assets | **R80.7bn** | +38.6% | [OBS] |

## 2. H1 FY26 (six months ended 28 February 2026)

| Metric | Value | Change | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group revenue | **R258.49m** | +8.8% YoY | [OBS] |
| Profit | **R58.26m** | +34.8% | [OBS] |
| Active clients | **1,244,996** | +21.9% (from 1,021,620) | [OBS] |
| Client assets | **R94.93bn** | +41.2% (from R67.2bn) | [OBS] |

> **Note:** one report cites H1 revenue +25.8% to R238m (a different comparative period) — figures vary by period/report; treat period boundaries carefully. [INF]

## 3. Trajectory

- Client assets grew from ~R1bn to ~R100bn "in a decade". [OBS]
- **Sanlam** deepened its investment in Purple Group (strategic/balance-sheet backing; Sanlam reported R127bn in client flows). [OBS]

## 4. Assessment

EasyEquities demonstrates profitable, capital-light growth at scale: **revenue +25% (FY25), PAT +76.6%**, driven by client-asset growth and operating leverage. This is a strong benchmark for a NeoBank investing arm. [INF]
