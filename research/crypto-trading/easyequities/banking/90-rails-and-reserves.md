# EasyEquities — Banking Rails & Reserves

## 1. Payments & settlement rails

| Function | Mechanism | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| ZAR funding | Bank EFT/instant transfer into the EasyEquities ZAR account | [OBS] |
| FX funding | **EasyFX** in-platform conversion to USD/AUD/GBP/EUR | [OBS] |
| Withdrawal | Withdrawable after **5 business days** settlement post-sale | [OBS] |
| Offshore | USD/EUR/GBP/AUD accounts, subject to SARB allowances | [OBS] |
| Custody | Asset custody via market infrastructure (JSE/global custodians) — specifics [UNK] | [UNK] |

## 2. Reserves / safeguarding

- As a **securities brokerage** (not a crypto exchange), EasyEquities does **not** publish proof-of-reserves attestations; client assets are held via regulated **market/custody** arrangements rather than a self-custodied reserve pool. [INF]
- Investor protection: reviews note **no investor-protection scheme** of the kind European brokers offer; SA brokerage protection is limited. [OBS]

## 3. Assessment

EasyEquities' rails are **fiat (ZAR) + easyFX + regulated market settlement**, fundamentally different from Luno/VALR's **crypto reserve + bank-rail** model. For a NeoBank, this is the "**investing rail**" reference, not the "crypto custody" reference. [INF]
