## 2. Positioning

EasyEquities is a **low-cost retail investment platform** ("democratise all things investing") that launched targeting under-invested millennials, with a simple fee structure and fractional shares. It sits inside a JSE-listed group, giving it compliance scaffolding from day one. [OBS]

## 4. Programme relevance (NeoBank)

EasyEquities is the reference for **low-cost, fractional, mass-market investing** and for **how a bank/insurer-backed challenger scales client assets** — directly relevant to a NeoBank bundling investing. [INF]
