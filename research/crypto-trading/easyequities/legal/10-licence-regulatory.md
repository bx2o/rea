# EasyEquities — Licences & Regulation

## 1. FSCA licensing

| Entity | FSP number | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| First World Trader (Pty) Ltd t/a EasyEquities | **22588** | [OBS] |
| Purple Group Limited (JSE-listed) | **46315** | [OBS] |

- EasyEquities is an **authorised Financial Services Provider** regulated by the FSCA (FAIS). [OBS]
- **Important scope caveat:** WikiFX issued a risk alert noting EasyEquities holds **no authorised forex/derivatives trading licence** — i.e. it is a **securities/brokerage** platform, not a leveraged-FX/CFD broker. This is a scope limitation, not a licence failure for its core business. [OBS]

## 2. Tax-free & retirement wrappers

- **TFSA (Tax-Free Savings Account)**: JSE/National Treasury-regulated wrapper — annual contribution limit **R46,000** (raised; older sources cite R36,000) and **R500,000 lifetime**; returns tax-free; withdrawals do not reset the limit. [OBS]
- **RA (Retirement Annuity)** accounts available. [OBS]

## 3. Conduct / complaints framework

- EasyEquities is bound by the **FAIS** complaints regime; an account's Complaints Management Framework references the **FAIS Ombud** (case fees up to R1,000; Ombud determinations up to R800,000). [OBS]
- Note: that framework document appears to belong to a group entity (RISE) — apply with care. [INF]

## 4. Exchange control

- Offshore investing (USD/AUD/GBP/EUR accounts) is subject to **SARB** allowances (SDA/FIA) and the annual limits on foreign investment. [OBS]
