## 4. Bottom line

Morgan Stanley is the **wealth-economics benchmark**. Stremiium should **ADOPT** the **fee-based AUM revenue model, E*TRADE-style digital-advisor hybrid, the advisor funnel, and planning/risk tooling**; **ADAPT** cash-sweep and securities-backed lending into **transparent, NCA-compliant** SA products; **AVOID** opaque sweep pricing and blind copying of a limited crypto footprint; and **BEAT** Morgan Stanley on **cost, accessibility, loyalty (`$STM`), digital-first distribution and Web3-native capability**.
