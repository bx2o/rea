## 3. Pricing / economics mechanics

### 3.1 Fee-based advisory
- Charged as a **percentage of assets under management** (tiered by relationship size). Public uniform rate cards are **not** published → **UNKNOWN**.
- Fee-based assets: ~**$2.478tn** (per Q2-2025 supplement reference) → recurring, capital-light revenue.

### 3.2 Cash-sweep (the hidden NII engine)
```
Client idle cash ──► auto-sweep ──► deposit/sweep programme
        │                                   │
        │                       bank pays interest on deposits
        ▼                                   │
   client (lower yield) ◄──── spread = NII ─┘
```
- **INFERRED:** the firm earns a **net interest spread** between what it earns on swept deposits and what it credits clients.
- Regulatory scrutiny of sweep rates has increased industry-wide (context, not asserted as MS-specific enforcement).

### 3.3 Securities-based lending (SBL)
- Lend against securities portfolios → interest income (NII). Q4'25 NII rose on **lending growth + balance-sheet mix**.

## 4. Comparison framing for Stremiium

| Dimension | Morgan Stanley | Stremiium target |
|---|---|---|
| Primary revenue | **Fee-based AUM + NII** | Interchange + fees → target fee-based AUM |
| Distribution | Advisor-led + E*TRADE digital | Digital-first + optional advisors |
| Advice | Monte Carlo planning | Automated advice (`$STM`-integrated) |
| Banking | Sweep + SBL | Deposits + credit (NCA-compliant) |
| Segment | HNW/affluent + institutional | SA mass-market → affluent |

**Conclusion:** Morgan Stanley is a **revenue-architecture benchmark**. Stremiium should copy the **shift from transaction fees to fee-based/AUM + NII**, adapt it to a mass-market NeoBank, and **avoid** opaque cash-sweep practices that attract regulatory scrutiny.

## 5. What Stremiium should not copy

- **Opaque cash-sweep economics** — a known conduct-risk area; Stremiium should use **transparent** deposit rates.
- High minimums / advisor-gated access to the best products — Stremiium's differentiator is **democratised access**.
