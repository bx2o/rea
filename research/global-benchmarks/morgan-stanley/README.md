## Executive summary

Morgan Stanley (NYSE: **MS**) is a global wealth-and-investment-management powerhouse whose strategy — under CEO Ted Pick — is built on **fee-based wealth management** at scale. Its Wealth Management segment reported **FY2025 net revenues of $31.8bn with a 29% pre-tax margin**, **$356bn of net new assets** and **$160bn of fee-based flows**, backed by roughly **~16,000 financial advisors** and **~$7.4tn of client assets** (Q4-2025). The engine combines an **advisor-led model**, the **E*TRADE** self-directed digital platform (acquired Oct-2020 in a ~$13bn all-stock deal), **portfolio risk engines (Monte Carlo)**, **automated cash-sweep banking**, and **net interest income on securities-backed lending**. For Stremiium, Morgan Stanley is the **benchmark for fee-based wealth economics, hybrid digital+advisor distribution, automated advice, and cash-sweep/NII mechanics** — the revenue architecture a NeoBank aspires to as it matures beyond transactions.
