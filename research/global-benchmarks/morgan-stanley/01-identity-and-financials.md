## Key take-aways for Stremiium

1. Morgan Stanley proves the **fee-based wealth model outperforms transaction revenue** at scale — Stremiium's maturity path should build toward advisory/AUM fees, not just interchange.
2. **E*TRADE** shows how to bolt a **self-directed digital platform** onto an advisor-led business without cannibalising it.
3. **Cash-sweep + securities-backed lending = NII** is the hidden profit engine of wealth — a mechanic Stremiium can adapt once it has deposits.
