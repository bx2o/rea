## 5. Regulatory gaps / lessons for Stremiium

| Lesson | Stremiium action |
|---|---|
| **Cash-sweep opacity attracts scrutiny** | Use **transparent** deposit rates/practices |
| Fiduciary/advice conduct is heavily policed | If Stremiium offers advice, adopt clear suitability/fiduciary standards |
| SBL is powerful but risky | If offering credit against assets, apply conservative LTV + NCA compliance |
| Fee-based AUM is cleanest revenue | Build toward **fee-based** products with clear disclosure |
| Dense multi-regulator oversight | SA regime (FSCA/SARB/NCR) differs — map equivalents early |
