# SECTION 5: RULES, SOPs, & OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORKS

## 5.1 Marketing SOPs

### SOP 1: Annual Marketing Planning

**Trigger:** Q4 planning cycle

**Steps:**

1. **Business Review (Week 1-2)**
   - Review prior year performance
   - Analyze market trends
   - Assess competitive landscape
   - Gather stakeholder input

2. **Strategy Development (Week 3-4)**
   - Define marketing objectives
   - Identify target segments
   - Develop messaging themes
   - Plan campaign calendar

3. **Budget Planning (Week 5-6)**
   - Allocate spend by channel
   - Plan headcount needs
   - Identify technology investments
   - Build business case

4. **Approval & Communication (Week 7-8)**
   - Present to executive team
   - Incorporate feedback
   - Finalize plan
   - Communicate to team

**Success Criteria:** Approved plan with clear objectives, budget, and timeline
**Output:** Annual Marketing Plan document

### SOP 2: Campaign Execution

**Trigger:** Campaign launch date

**Steps:**

1. **Pre-Launch (2 weeks before)**
   - Finalize creative assets
   - Set up tracking and attribution
   - Prepare landing pages
   - Brief sales team

2. **Launch (Day 1)**
   - Activate all channels
   - Monitor performance hourly
   - Respond to engagement
   - Track early metrics

3. **Optimization (Week 1-4)**
   - Daily performance review
   - A/B test creative variants
   - Adjust spend allocation
   - Optimize conversion paths

4. **Reporting (Week 5)**
   - Compile performance report
   - Document learnings
   - Share results with stakeholders
   - Plan follow-up campaigns

**Success Criteria:** Meet or exceed campaign KPIs

### SOP 3: Content Publishing

**Trigger:** Content calendar schedule

**Steps:**

1. **Content Creation**
   - Draft and review content
   - SEO optimization
   - Legal/compliance review
   - Final approval

2. **Production**
   - Design supporting visuals
   - Format for channels
   - Schedule publication
   - Set up tracking

3. **Distribution**
   - Publish on primary channel
   - Share on social media
   - Include in email newsletters
   - Repurpose for other formats

4. **Promotion**
   - Paid amplification if applicable
   - Influencer/partner sharing
   - Community engagement
   - Monitor and respond

**Success Criteria:** Published on time, meets quality standards, achieves engagement targets
