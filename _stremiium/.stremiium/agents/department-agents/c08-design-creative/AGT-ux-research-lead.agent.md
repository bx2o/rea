# SECTION 4: KEY FAQS & KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY

## 4.1 Research Method FAQs

**Q1: When should you use qualitative vs. quantitative research?**
A: **Qualitative:** Small sample, deep understanding, "why" questions, exploratory, generative. Methods: Interviews, usability tests, observation. **Quantitative:** Large sample, statistical significance, "what" and "how many" questions, validation. Methods: Surveys, A/B tests, analytics. Best: Combine both—qualitative for depth, quantitative for breadth.

**Q2: How many users do you need for usability testing?**
A: **Qualitative testing:** 5-8 users per round reveals 85% of usability issues. Test, fix, retest. **Quantitative testing:** 20+ users for statistical confidence. **Card sorting:** 15-30 participants. **Surveys:** 100+ for significance. Rule: More users for numbers; fewer users for insights.

**Q3: How do you recruit research participants?**
A: Sources: (1) **Current users:** In-app recruitment, email lists. (2) **User panels:** Research panel services. (3) **Social:** LinkedIn, Twitter, relevant groups. (4) **Agencies:** Recruiting firms. (5) **Guerrilla:** Coffee shops, public spaces. (6) **Internal:** Employees (for some research). Best: Build ongoing participant database; offer appropriate incentives.

**Q4: How do you avoid bias in user research?**
A: Strategies: (1) **Neutral questions:** Don't lead participants. (2) **Diverse sample:** Different backgrounds, experiences. (3) **Multiple methods:** Triangulate findings. (4) **Blind analysis:** Analyze without knowing hypothesis. (5) **Peer review:** Colleagues check methodology. (6) **Awareness:** Know your own assumptions. (7) **Validation:** Test insights with follow-up research.

**Q5: How do you make research insights actionable?**
A: Framework: (1) **Clear findings:** What did we learn? (2) **So what:** Why does this matter? (3) **Now what:** What should we do? (4) **Prioritized:** Rank by impact/feasibility. (5) **Specific:** Concrete recommendations. (6) **Tracked:** Follow up on implementation. (7) **Collaborative:** Involve team in synthesis.

## 4.2 Usability FAQs

**Q6: What's the difference between usability and user experience?**
A: **Usability:** Ease of use. Can users complete tasks? Is it efficient? Learnable? Error-tolerant? **User Experience:** Broader—emotions, attitudes, perceptions about product. Includes usability + desirability + brand + value. Relationship: Usability is component of UX; great UX requires good usability.

**Q7: How do you measure usability?**
A: Metrics: (1) **Effectiveness:** Task success rate (% completing task). (2) **Efficiency:** Time on task, clicks to complete. (3) **Satisfaction:** System Usability Scale (SUS), satisfaction ratings. (4) **Error rate:** Errors per task. (5) **Learnability:** Improvement over time. (6) **Cognitive load:** Mental effort required. Combine quantitative metrics + qualitative observations.

# SECTION 5: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

## SOP 1: Usability Test

**Duration:** 1-2 weeks | **Frequency:** Per feature/design

1. **Plan:** Objectives, tasks, success criteria
2. **Recruit:** 5-8 target users
3. **Prepare:** Test script, prototype, environment
4. **Pilot:** Test with 1 user, refine
5. **Conduct:** Moderate sessions, observe
6. **Analyze:** Find patterns, issues
7. **Report:** Findings, recommendations, severity
8. **Present:** Share with team
9. **Track:** Follow implementation

## SOP 2: User Interview Study

**Duration:** 2-3 weeks | **Frequency:** Quarterly or per initiative

1. **Define questions:** What do we need to learn?
2. **Create guide:** Interview structure
3. **Recruit:** 8-15 participants
4. **Conduct:** 45-60 min interviews
5. **Transcribe:** Record and document
6. **Code:** Tag themes and patterns
7. **Synthesize:** Develop insights
8. **Create artifacts:** Personas, journey maps
9. **Share:** Present findings

## SOP 3: Research Repository Maintenance

**Duration:** Ongoing | **Frequency:** Continuous

1. **Collect:** Gather all research
2. **Catalog:** Tag by topic, method, date
3. **Store:** Central, searchable location
4. **Synthesize:** Cross-study insights
5. **Share:** Make accessible to organization
6. **Maintain:** Update, archive old studies
