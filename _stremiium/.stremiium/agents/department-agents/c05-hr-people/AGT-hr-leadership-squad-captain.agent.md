# SECTION 5: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

## SOP 1: Strategic Workforce Planning

**Duration:** 4-6 weeks | **Frequency:** Quarterly | **Owner:** HR Leadership

- Analyze business strategy and growth plans
- Identify skills gaps and talent needs
- Forecast headcount requirements by function
- Assess internal talent pipeline and readiness
- Develop hiring plan with priorities and timelines
- Present plan to executive leadership
- Monitor execution and adjust quarterly

## SOP 2: Executive Talent Review

**Duration:** Half-day | **Frequency:** Quarterly | **Owner:** HR Leadership + CEO

- Prepare talent data (performance, potential, readiness)
- Review leadership pipeline for critical roles
- Identify high-potential employees for development
- Discuss succession plans for key positions
- Address retention risks and action plans
- Calibrate performance ratings across teams
- Agree on development investments and priorities

## SOP 3: Culture Assessment & Action Planning

**Duration:** 6-8 weeks | **Frequency:** Annual + pulse | **Owner:** Culture Team

- Design comprehensive culture/engagement survey
- Administer survey with strong participation drive
- Analyze results by team, tenure, demographics
- Identify strengths to celebrate and leverage
- Prioritize improvement areas with highest impact
- Develop action plans with owners and timelines
- Communicate results and actions transparently
- Track progress and adjust initiatives

## SOP 4: Compensation Planning Cycle

**Duration:** 8-10 weeks | **Frequency:** Annual | **Owner:** HR Leadership + Finance

- Review market data and compensation benchmarks
- Establish budget parameters with Finance
- Develop salary increase guidelines by performance
- Prepare equity refresh recommendations
- Conduct manager training on compensation decisions
- Support managers in compensation conversations
- Review for equity and consistency
- Communicate decisions and timeline
- Process changes in payroll/HRIS

## SOP 5: Employee Investigation Protocol

**Duration:** 1-4 weeks | **Trigger:** Complaint received | **Owner:** HR Leadership

- Acknowledge receipt within 24 hours
- Assess severity and assign investigator
- Plan investigation approach and timeline
- Interview complainant, subject, witnesses
- Document findings with evidence
- Consult Legal on serious matters
- Determine appropriate resolution/actions
- Communicate outcome to parties
- Document and close case
- Follow up to ensure resolution effective

| -------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **HR as admin only** | Strategic irrelevance        | Business partnership, metrics            |
