## 0.3 Content Strategy Framework

**Editorial Pillars:**

1. **Educate:** Make Web3 accessible to mainstream audiences
2. **Entertain:** Content that delights and engages sports fans
3. **Empower:** Help users make informed decisions confidently
4. **Engage:** Build community through interactive and participatory content
5. **Elevate:** Establish thought leadership and industry authority

**Content Mix Strategy:**

- **60% Educational:** How-to guides, explainers, tutorials
- **20% Cultural/Sports:** Entertainment, community, lifestyle
- **15% Product:** Features, updates, use cases
- **5% Trust/Company:** Security, team, transparency

**Content Funnel:**

- **Awareness:** Broad reach educational content (blog, social, video)
- **Consideration:** Deeper product education (guides, webinars, case studies)
- **Conversion:** Action-oriented content (tutorials, setup guides, FAQs)
- **Retention:** Community and advanced content (Discord, Telegram, insider updates)
- **Advocacy:** User-generated content, referral programs, ambassador features

## 0.4 Web3 Content Challenges

**Education Complexity:**

- **Challenge:** Blockchain concepts are foreign to mainstream audiences
- **Solution:** Progressive disclosure, analogies to familiar concepts, visual explanations
- **Format:** Video explainers > infographics > written guides
- **Measurement:** Content completion rates, support ticket reduction

**Regulatory Constraints:**

- **Challenge:** FSCA prohibits promises of returns and financial advice
- **Solution:** Focus on education and utility, not speculation
- **Guidelines:** All investment content reviewed by legal/compliance
- **Risk Warnings:** Clear disclaimers on all crypto-related content

**Trust Deficit:**

- **Challenge:** Crypto scams have created public skepticism
- **Solution:** Radical transparency, security communication, team visibility
- **Proof Points:** Audit reports, licensing documentation, security measures
- **Crisis Playbook:** Rapid, honest communication for any incidents

**Cultural Localization:**

- **Challenge:** Global crypto content doesn't resonate locally
- **Solution:** South African sports references, local slang, cultural moments
- **Languages:** English primary, Zulu/Xhosa/Afrikaans for key campaigns
- **Stories:** Feature local users, partner clubs, South African success
