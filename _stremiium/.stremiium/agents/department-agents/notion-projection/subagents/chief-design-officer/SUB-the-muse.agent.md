# SECTION 4: FAQs (20+)

**Q1: What is creative direction vs. design?**
A: Direction = vision, concept, guidance. Design = execution. Director sets direction; designers execute.

**Q2: How do you maintain brand consistency?**
A: Guidelines, templates, asset libraries, approval process, training, regular audits, clear standards.

**Q3: What makes a strong creative concept?**
A: On strategy, distinctive, memorable, extendable, simple, emotional connection, culturally relevant.

**Q4: How do you present creative work?**
A: Context first (problem, insight), then concept, then execution. Connect to strategy. Tell story.

**Q5: What is a creative brief and why important?**
A: Document defining objectives, audience, message, deliverables, constraints. Alignment tool. Reduces revisions.

**Q6: How do you handle subjective feedback?**
A: Ground in objectives, ask "what problem are we solving?", educate on design principles, find middle ground.

**Q7: What is art direction in photography/film?**
A: Creative vision for visual execution. Mood, style, composition, casting, location, lighting guidance.

**Q8: How do you adapt creative for different channels?**
A: Core concept consistent, execution adapted. Format specs, audience context, platform norms, testing.

**Q9: What is the difference between brand and campaign creative?**
A: Brand = enduring identity. Campaign = temporary promotion. Brand is foundation; campaigns build on it.

**Q10: How do you manage creative burnout?**
A: Inspiration sources, variety in projects, breaks, professional development, team collaboration, recognition.

**Q11: What is visual hierarchy and why important?**
A: Guiding eye through importance. Size, color, contrast, position. Makes communication clear.

**Q12: How do you ensure accessibility in design?**
A: Color contrast, alt text, font size, screen reader compatibility, motion sensitivity, inclusive imagery.

**Q13: What is a design system?**
A: Standardized components, patterns, guidelines. Enables consistency, efficiency, scale. Foundation for design.

**Q14: How do you balance creativity and business goals?**
A: Strategy informs creativity. Measure impact. Beautiful work that doesn't perform isn't successful.

**Q15: What is responsive design?**
A: Adapts to screen size. Mobile, tablet, desktop. Content priority, flexible layouts, performance.

**Q16: How do you manage creative feedback rounds?**
A: Clear process, consolidated feedback, specific comments, decision maker identified, revision limits.

**Q17: What makes good typography?**
A: Readability, hierarchy, personality appropriate, pairing harmony, spacing, alignment.

**Q18: How do you stay current with design trends?**
A: Industry publications, Dribbble/Behance, conferences, competitor analysis, experimentation, networking.

**Q19: What is brand voice vs. visual identity?**
A: Voice = how you speak (words, tone). Visual = how you look (logo, colors, imagery). Both brand.

**Q20: How do you measure creative effectiveness?**
A: Brand recall, engagement rates, conversion, sentiment, brand tracking, A/B testing, sales attribution.
