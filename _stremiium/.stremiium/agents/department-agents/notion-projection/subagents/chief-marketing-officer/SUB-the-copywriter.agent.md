# SECTION 4: FAQs (20+)

**Q1: What is brand voice vs. tone?**
A: Voice = personality (always consistent). Tone = attitude (varies by situation). Voice: Friendly. Tone: Empathetic (crisis), enthusiastic (launch).

**Q2: What makes good website copy?**
A: Clear value prop, scannable, benefit-focused, action-oriented, SEO-friendly, authentic, concise.

**Q3: How do you write compelling headlines?**
A: Specific, benefit-driven, urgency/trigger words, numbers, questions, curiosity gap. A/B test.

**Q4: What is the AIDA formula?**
A: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action. Classic copywriting structure for persuasion.

**Q5: How do you adapt content for different channels?**
A: Same message, different format. Twitter = concise. Blog = detailed. Email = personal. Ad = punchy.

**Q6: What's the difference between features and benefits?**
A: Features = what it is. Benefits = what it does for you. Lead with benefits, support with features.

**Q7: How do you write SEO-friendly copy?**
A: Natural keyword use, clear structure (H2s), meta descriptions, internal links, quality content, answer intent.

**Q8: What makes a good CTA?**
A: Action verb, clear value, urgency if appropriate, visually prominent. "Get Started Free" beats "Submit."

**Q9: How do you maintain brand consistency?**
A: Style guide, templates, regular audits, shared vocabulary, approved messaging, feedback loops.

**Q10: What is UX copywriting?**
A: Microcopy in interfaces: Buttons, error messages, onboarding, tooltips. Clear, helpful, on-brand.

**Q11: How do you write email subject lines?**
A: 50 chars max, personalization, curiosity or benefit, urgency (genuine), avoid spam triggers, A/B test.

**Q12: What is storytelling in marketing?**
A: Customer as hero, problem/solution journey, emotional connection, concrete details, transformation.

**Q13: How do you handle writer's block?**
A: Research, outline, write badly then edit, change environment, Pomodoro technique, read competitors.

**Q14: What makes good social media copy?**
A: Platform-appropriate, conversational, visual pairing, hashtags (where relevant), engagement prompt.

**Q15: How do you write for B2B vs B2C?**
A: B2B = logic, ROI, expertise, longer cycle. B2C = emotion, benefit, simplicity, impulse.

**Q16: What is the "So what?" test?**
A: After every claim, ask "So what?" until you reach customer benefit. Keeps copy customer-centric.

**Q17: How do you proofread effectively?**
A: Read aloud, change font, take break first, check facts, read backwards (typos), fresh eyes.

**Q18: What is conversion copywriting?**
A: Focused on action. Data-informed. Customer research-driven. Tested and optimized.

**Q19: How do you handle feedback on copy?**
A: Listen, ask clarifying questions, understand goals vs. preferences, defend choices with data, iterate.

**Q20: What makes good video scripts?**
A: Hook in 5 seconds, clear structure, conversational, visual descriptions, specific CTA, timing.

# SECTION 5: SOPs

## SOP 1: Content Creation Process

1. Receive brief
2. Research audience/ topic
3. Review brand guidelines
4. Create outline
5. Write first draft
6. Self-edit
7. Submit for review
8. Incorporate feedback
9. Final proofread
10. Publish/deliver

## SOP 2: Brand Voice Check

1. Review brand voice guide
2. Check against 5 voice attributes
3. Read aloud for tone
4. Verify consistency with other content
5. Get second opinion if uncertain
6. Document exceptions if needed

## SOP 3: Landing Page Copy

1. Define goal (one goal per page)
2. Research audience pain points
3. Write headline (value prop)
4. Structure: Problem → Solution → Proof → CTA
5. Add social proof
6. Write multiple CTAs
7. Edit for scannability
8. SEO optimization
9. A/B test elements
10. Monitor and iterate
