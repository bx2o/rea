# SECTION 3: KEY FAQS

**Q1: What's a strong culture?**
A: Characteristics: (1) **Clear values:** Everyone understands shared values. (2) **Psychological safety:** Safe to speak up, make mistakes, ask questions. (3) **Belonging:** People feel welcomed, included, valued. (4) **Trust:** People trust leadership, each other. (5) **Purpose:** Connection between work and meaningful purpose. (6) **Growth:** Continuous learning, development opportunity. (7) **Recognition:** Good work celebrated. (8) **Sustainability:** Pace and expectations sustainable long-term.

**Q2: How do you measure culture/engagement?**
A: Methods: (1) **NPS-style survey:** "How likely to recommend as employer?" (2) **Culture survey:** Questions on values, belonging, safety, purpose, growth. (3) **Pulse surveys:** Quick monthly check-ins. (4) **Retention metrics:** Turnover rate, tenure. (5) **Focus groups:** Deeper conversations on culture. (6) **Exit interviews:** Why did people leave?

# SECTION 4: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

## SOP 1: Annual Culture Survey

**Duration:** 6-8 weeks | **Frequency:** Annual

1. **Survey design:** Create questions aligned to culture priorities
2. **Communication:** Announce survey to company
3. **Deployment:** Launch survey, track participation (target 85%+)
4. **Analysis:** Analyze results by team, tenure, demographics
5. **Reporting:** Share themes with leadership and company
6. **Action planning:** Create initiatives to address gaps
7. **Follow-up:** Track progress quarterly

## SOP 2: Culture Event Planning

**Duration:** 4-6 weeks | **Frequency:** Quarterly major events

1. **Event concept:** Align to culture goals and calendar
2. **Budget approval:** Secure funding from leadership
3. **Planning committee:** Recruit volunteers to help
4. **Logistics:** Venue, catering, activities, communications
5. **Promotion:** Build excitement and registration
6. **Execution:** Run event, capture moments
7. **Feedback:** Post-event survey and learnings

## SOP 3: Pulse Survey Program

**Duration:** Ongoing | **Frequency:** Monthly

1. **Question rotation:** 3-5 questions max, rotating themes
2. **Distribution:** Send via email/Slack
3. **Real-time dashboard:** Track responses
4. **Trend analysis:** Identify emerging issues
5. **Rapid response:** Address concerns within 1 week
6. **Close loop:** Share actions taken with employees

## SOP 4: Wellness Program Development

**Duration:** 8-12 weeks | **Frequency:** Annual program design

1. **Needs assessment:** Survey employees on wellness priorities
2. **Program design:** Physical, mental, financial, social wellness
3. **Vendor selection:** Choose EAP, fitness, financial wellness partners
4. **Launch communications:** Promote new offerings
5. **Engagement tracking:** Monitor participation rates
6. **ROI analysis:** Measure healthcare cost impact

# SECTION 5: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (DETAILED)

## 5.1 SOP 1: Culture Assessment Process

**Duration:** 4-6 weeks | **Frequency:** Annual | **Owner:** Culture Manager

1. **Diagnostic selection:** Choose assessment tools (surveys, interviews, observation)
2. **Stakeholder mapping:** Identify key culture influencers
3. **Data collection:** Deploy surveys, conduct focus groups
4. **Analysis framework:** Map current vs. desired culture
5. **Gap identification:** Pinpoint specific culture gaps
6. **Report development:** Create comprehensive culture report
7. **Presentation:** Share findings with leadership
8. **Action planning:** Develop culture roadmap

## 5.2 SOP 2: Employee Experience Journey Mapping

**Duration:** 6-8 weeks | **Frequency:** Annual review | **Owner:** Culture Manager

1. **Journey stages:** Map employee lifecycle (hire → exit)
2. **Touchpoint identification:** List all employee interactions
3. **Experience measurement:** Survey at each stage
4. **Pain point analysis:** Identify friction points
5. **Opportunity prioritization:** Focus on high-impact moments
6. **Experience design:** Redesign problematic touchpoints
7. **Implementation:** Roll out improvements
8. **Measurement:** Track experience scores

## 5.3 SOP 3: Psychological Safety Initiative

**Duration:** Ongoing | **Frequency:** Continuous | **Owner:** Culture Manager + Leadership

1. **Leadership training:** Train managers on psychological safety
2. **Team assessments:** Measure safety by team
3. **Intervention design:** Create team-specific improvement plans
4. **Behavior modeling:** Leaders demonstrate vulnerability
5. **Feedback mechanisms:** Anonymous reporting of safety concerns
6. **Recognition:** Celebrate teams with high safety scores
7. **Accountability:** Address leaders who create unsafe environments

# SECTION 11: PITFALLS & RISK MITIGATION

## 11.1 Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall                     | Risk                             | Mitigation                     |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Survey fatigue**          | Low participation                | Limit frequency, share actions |
| **Culture theater**         | Empty initiatives without impact | Tie to business outcomes       |
| **Ignoring feedback**       | Employee cynicism                | Close the loop always          |
| **One-size-fits-all**       | Programs don't fit all teams     | Customize by segment           |
| **Leadership misalignment** | Leaders model wrong behaviors    | Executive coaching             |

## 11.2 Risk Management

- **Engagement decline:** Early warning systems, rapid intervention
- **Cultural dilution:** M&A integration protocols
- **Remote isolation:** Intentional connection programs
- **Burnout epidemic:** Wellness and workload monitoring
