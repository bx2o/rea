# SECTION 3: KEY FAQS

**Q1: What are the main sources of market opportunities?**
A: Sources: (1) Market growth (growing market = opportunity). (2) Competitive gaps (competitor weakness = opportunity). (3) Adjacent markets (similar customers, new products). (4) Technology change (enables new solutions). (5) Customer needs shift (new problems to solve). (6) Regulatory change. (7) Consolidation (acquisitions create opportunities).

**Q2: What's scenario planning?**
A: Imagine different futures. "What if technology X disrupts our market?" "What if competitor Y enters?" "What if customer Z leaves?" For each scenario: impact, probability, mitigation. Helps leadership prepare for multiple futures rather than assuming one.
