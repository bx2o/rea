# SECTION 3: KEY FAQS

## 3.1 FAQs (Sample)

**Q1: What's the difference between strategy and execution?**
A: **Strategy:** Where are we going and why? (high-level direction, multi-year). **Execution:** How do we get there? (detailed plans, quarterly actions). Strategy informs execution. All strategy, no execution = ideas only. All execution, no strategy = working on wrong things.

**Q2: What should a 3-year strategy include?**
A: Contents: (1) Current state (where are we now?). (2) Vision (where do we want to be?). (3) Market opportunity (size, growth, competitive). (4) Strategy (how will we win?). (5) Capabilities needed (what do we need to build?). (6) Milestones (what's 1-year, 2-year, 3-year?). (7) Risks (what could go wrong?). (8) Financial projections (revenue, profit, growth).

**Q3: What are OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)?**
A: **Objective:** Qualitative goal (we want to become market leader in X). **Key Results:** Measurable metrics proving objective achieved (revenue $X, market share Y%, customer count Z). OKR structure: (1) Big ambitious goal, (2) 3-4 measurable outcomes. Used for: Annual planning, team alignment, progress tracking.
