## 5. 50+ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

### Getting Started in Sales Development (15+ FAQs)

**Q: How do I build a sales development function from scratch?**
A: Start with 2-3 junior SDRs, define ICP and target personas, establish activity expectations, create discovery/qualification process, implement tracking in CRM, and optimize weekly based on results.

**Q: What's the right SDR-to-AE ratio?**
A: Typical ratio is 1 SDR per 2-3 AEs, depending on deal complexity and AE experience. High-velocity deals may support 1:5 ratio, while complex enterprise sales might be 1:1.

**Q: How many dials should an SDR do per day?**
A: Target 40-50 dials per day, roughly 200+ per month. Quality matters more than pure volume - 3-5 meaningful conversations often trump 100 dial-and-hang-up calls.

**Q: How do I know if a prospect is qualified?**
A: Establish BANT criteria: Budget (has it), Authority (decision-maker), Need (business problem), Timeline (buying window). All 4 necessary for truly qualified opportunity.

**Q: What's a good conversation-to-opportunity conversion rate?**
A: Target 15-20% of meaningful conversations converting to opportunities. This varies by ICP and offer. Track and continuously improve through better qualification.

**Q: How do I recruit top SDR talent?**
A: Recruit for attitude and communication skills - the rest is teachable. Look for sales hunger, coachability, resilience, and smart people willing to learn. Network and referrals work best.

**Q: What should I teach new SDRs in onboarding?**
A: Month 1: Product knowledge, objection handling, basic discovery. Month 2: ICP deep dive, qualification framework, prospecting campaigns. Ongoing: Continuous coaching and optimization.

**Q: How long does it take an SDR to ramp?**
A: Expect 90-120 days to productivity. Month 1: learning curve (30-50% productivity). Month 2: ramping (60-80%). Month 3: full productivity (80-100%+).

**Q: What tools should my SDR team use?**
A: Dialer (RingCentral, Outreach), CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot), Email sequences (Outreach, Salesloft), Data (ZoomInfo, Apollo), LinkedIn automation (optional). Choose integrated stack avoiding duplication.

**Q: How do I manage SDR turnover?**
A: Turnover is natural (15-20% industry average). Focus on: clear career path, development opportunities, competitive compensation, team culture, and celebrating wins. Promote best SDRs to AE roles.

**Q: What's the right compensation structure for SDRs?**
A: Typical: $40-50K base + $10-20K variable based on meetings booked or pipeline created. Ensure attainability - SDRs should hit bonus 80%+ of time for motivation.

**Q: How do I handle an underperforming SDR?**
A: First 30 days: daily coaching and support. Weeks 2-4: performance improvement plan with specific targets. Month 2: clear consequences if improvement absent. Be realistic about fit.

**Q: What's the right lead handoff process?**
A: Qualified lead passes to assigned AE with clear context. AE has 24hr to contact prospect. Track SLA compliance. Create feedback loop - AE reports back if prospect quality or fit is off.

**Q: How do I establish prospecting campaigns?**
A: Select target vertical/persona. Define ICP criteria. Create 3-4 personalized messages. Schedule outreach (LinkedIn, cold email, phone). Track metrics. Iterate based on results.

**Q: What's a realistic pipeline generation target?**
A: Mature SDR should generate $500K-$1M monthly qualified pipeline depending on deal size and sales cycle. This becomes $2M+ for full team of 3-4 experienced SDRs.

### Challenges & Problem-Solving (15+ FAQs)

**Q: How do I handle poor lead quality from SDRs?**
A: Review actual calls together to assess qualification standards. Provide immediate coaching on disqualification criteria. Tighten BANT framework. Review specific opportunities rejected by AEs to calibrate quality.

**Q: What if my SDRs aren't hitting activity targets?**
A: Understand why: system issues? Unclear expectations? Insufficient training? Too many meetings reducing dial time? Unclear prospecting mission? Adjust coaching, remove obstacles, reset expectations.

**Q: How do I reduce SDR turnover?**
A: Invest in career development showing clear path to AE role. Create positive team culture. Celebrate wins publicly. Ensure competitive compensation. Remove disengaging managers. Build coaching relationships.

**Q: What if I'm not generating enough pipeline for sales team?**
A: Audit current activity levels and conversion rates. Identify gap (volume, quality, or both). Increase SDR team size if scalable. Improve prospecting campaigns and qualification. Extend target territories.

**Q: How do I manage remote SDR team effectiveness?**
A: Establish clear daily/weekly reporting. Use call recording for coaching. Schedule regular 1:1s. Create accountability peer teams. Invest in prospecting tools that track activity. Build team culture virtually.

**Q: What if AEs aren't following up on qualified leads?**
A: Review lead quality first - are they truly qualified? Establish clear SLAs for contact timelines. Track SLA compliance. Conduct AE coaching on lead follow-up discipline. Create incentives for pursuing warm leads.

**Q: How do I handle SDRs gaming activity metrics?**
A: Shift focus from pure volume to quality metrics (conversations, meetings booked). Review calls regularly. Emphasize qualification quality. Tie incentives to outcome (meetings/pipeline) not activity.

**Q: What if my ICE is wrong?**
A: Audit top customers - are they matching your ICP? Ask AEs which prospects they most enjoy selling to. Analyze won deals for customer profile patterns. Refine and retarget accordingly.

**Q: How do I improve conversation rates?**
A: Audit voicemail effectiveness - does it compel call back? Test messaging and value props. Practice with SDRs. Analyze best performers - what are they doing differently? Share techniques with team.

**Q: What if prospects aren't answering phones?**
A: Shift to multi-channel approach combining phone, email, LinkedIn. Time calls better - reach out when prospect is likely available. Use email to warm up before calls. Persist with multi-touch sequences.

**Q: How do I handle objections to cold outreach?**
A: Develop objection handling framework. Role play common concerns. Coach SDRs on responding to "it's not a good time" or "I get too many calls". Build authentic prospecting approach, not pushy.

**Q: What if deal sizes are smaller than expected?**
A: Review ICP - are we targeting the right level? Ensure SDRs are reaching decision-makers, not end users. Coach AEs on qualification. Adjust targeting to higher-level personas.

**Q: How do I handle SDR to AE transition fail**ures?\*\*
A: Some SDRs aren't wired for complex deal sales. Be honest - not everyone should be AE. Reposition to Senior SDR role or lateral opportunities. Don't force transition that will lead to failure.

**Q: What if we lack enough leads to keep SDRs busy?**
A: Expand target markets or geographies. Lower-qualify some targets to increase volume. Create inbound lead nurturing. Partner with marketing on account-based campaigns. Provide meaningful non-prospecting work.

**Q: How do I balance prospecting with follow-up?**
A: Establish discipline: 70% new prospecting, 30% follow-up. Automate follow-up sequences in CRM. Prevent SDRs from getting stuck on follow-up. Maintain fresh prospecting pipeline.

**Q: What if my sales cycles are too long for SDR sourcing?**
A: SDR model works for 3-18 month sales cycles. For longer cycles, may need Account-Based Marketing approach. Focus on early-stage pipeline qualification rather than short-term opportunities.

### Excellence & Advanced Practices (15+ FAQs)

**Q: What does a best-in-class sales development function look like?**
A: Consistent $2M+ monthly qualified pipeline. 15%+ lead-to-opp conversion. <10% SDR turnover. 40%+ of new revenue sourced by SDRs. Clear career path producing internal AEs. Strong AE/SDR collaboration.

**Q: How do I establish vertical specialization?**
A: Assign SDRs to specific vertical markets. Deep ICP knowledge within vertical. Tailored messaging and prospecting campaigns. SDRs become vertical experts. Better qualification and conversion rates.

**Q: How do I create an inside sales hybrid model?**
A: SDRs handle prospecting and qualification. Sales Specialists or ISRs handle $50-100K deals. Account Executives focus on enterprise deals. Create handoff between roles. Optimize deal routing.

**Q: How do I use LinkedIn for sales development?**
A: Personalized connection requests with value message. LinkedIn InMail for VPs/C-level. LinkedIn content visibility and engagement. Strategic targeting by role/company. LinkedIn outreach before cold calls.

**Q: How do I implement account-based sales development?**
A: Focus SDR prospecting on target account list. Coordinate multi-threading within accounts. Penetrate with multiple personas. Build relationship before sales conversation. Different from traditional prospecting volume model.

**Q: How do I measure campaign ROI?**
A: Track campaign pipeline generated ($). Divide by campaign cost (SDR time + tools). Calculate payback period. Typical campaigns deliver 10:1 to 20:1 ROI. Track by campaign cohort for comparisons.

**Q: What's the future of sales development?**
A: Likely trends: AI-assisted dialing and email. More account-based development. Virtual-first prospecting. SDR role becoming more strategic/consultative. Continued high demand for quality development.

**Q: How do I coach SDRs on tone and authenticity?**
A: Record calls (with permission). Review together focusing on authenticity vs. reading script. Role play objections and responses. Emphasize genuine interest in prospect. Share best call examples.

**Q: How do I transition SDR team to customer success?**
A: Some top SDRs excel at post-sale customer management. Development skills transfer well to customer relationship management. Create clear path: SDR → CSM roles. Ensures top talent retention.

**Q: How do I leverage predictive analytics in prospecting?**
A: Use company growth signals to identify expansion timing. Monitor technology stack changes indicating buying. Identify account engagement predicting buying. Prioritize outreach based on propensity models.

**Q: What's the remote vs. in-office SDR consideration?**
A: Pros of remote: larger talent pool, cost savings, flexibility. Cons: harder to build culture, coaching more difficult, isolation challenges. Hybrid model increasingly popular: in-office 2-3 days/week.

**Q: How do I build Sales Development Center of Excellence?**
A: Document best prospecting practices. Create standardized qualification process. Share top performer techniques. Implement continuous improvement cadence. Establish SDR mentorship program. Celebrate wins.

**Q: How do I plan SDR career path?**
A: Year 1: SDR role - foundational prospecting skills. Year 2: Senior SDR - leadership and mentoring development specialists. Year 3: Transition to Account Executive or Account-Based Development Manager. Clear progression.

**Q: How do I establish SDR peer coaching?**
A: Pair junior and senior SDRs. Senior SDR reviews junior SDR calls weekly. Share prospecting techniques and scripts. Create friendly competition/partnership. Leverage peer pressure for high performance.

## 7. STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (3+ Detailed SOPs)

### SOP 1: SDR Onboarding & Ramp Program (30-90 Days)

**Purpose:** Establish standardized onboarding for new SDRs to reach full productivity in 90 days

**Timeline:**

- **Weeks 1-2:** Company and product training
- **Weeks 3-4:** Prospecting skills and qualification training
- **Weeks 5-6:** ICP and campaign deep dive
- **Weeks 7-8:** Live prospecting with coaching
- **Weeks 9-12:** Independent prospecting with weekly coaching

**Day 1 Onboarding:**

- Welcome by Manager
- Company history, mission, and culture
- Sales development function overview
- HR paperwork and setup
- System access setup (CRM, dialer, email, etc.)
- Team introduction and lunch

**Week 1: Orientation**

- Day 2-3: Product training (features, benefits, use cases, differentiators)
- Day 4-5: Market and competitive positioning, customer profiles
- Daily: System navigation training (CRM, dialer, email sequences)
- Friday: Week 1 review and goals for Week 2

**Week 2: Prospecting Skills**

- Discovery calls training (framework, example calls)
- Objection handling playbook and role play
- Call recording review (best practices)
- Email writing standards and templates
- LinkedIn prospecting approach
- Mock calls with manager focused on coaching

**Week 3: ICP & Campaigns**

- Ideal Customer Profile deep dive
- Target personas and decision-makers
- Account selection criteria
- Vertical market deep dive
- Current prospecting campaigns overview
- Competitive landscape and positioning

**Week 4: Prospecting Preparation**

- Live call observation (listening to top SDRs)
- Call script familiarization and practice
- Email template customization
- LinkedIn profile setup and strategy
- CRM workflow review
- Call assignment and first prospects

**Week 5-6: Guided Prospecting**

- Daily prospecting with activity target (20-30 dials, not 50)
- Same-day call review and coaching
- Weekly 1:1 focused on improvement areas
- Gradual increase in activity targets
- Feedback on voicemail and email approach
- Measurement: conversation rate, tone, approach

**Week 7-8: On-Own Prospecting**

- Independent prospecting with daily tracking
- Activity target: 40-50 dials per day
- Weekly 1:1 with manager coaching
- Monthly meetings booked target: 5-10 starts
- Qualification focus: BANT verification
- Call recording review (manager selects recordings)

**Week 9-12: Full Productivity Ramping**

- Target activity: 200+ dials per month
- Meetings booked target: 10-15 per month
- Qualification consistency review
- Lead handoff to Account Executive observation
- Quarterly campaign effectiveness analysis
- 90-day performance review

**Success Criteria - 90 Days:**

- 200+ dials per month achieved
- 10-15 meetings booked per month
- > 15% conversation-to-meeting rate
- BANT qualification consistent
- Good feedback from AEs on lead quality
- Demonstrates coachability and sales hunger
- Ready for independent management

### SOP 2: Weekly Sales Development Team Coaching (Team & Individual)

**Purpose:** Establish consistent coaching cadence to maximize SDR performance and development

**Weekly Rhythms:**

**Daily (3:00 PM standup - 15 min):**

- Team gathers on video call

- Best practices share: top performer shares successful approach
- Campaign analysis: conversion rates by campaign
- New messaging or targeting adjustments
- Team recognition and contest standings
- Troubleshooting and Q&A

**Individual 1:1 Coaching (Weekly - 30 min with each SDR):**

**Structure:**

1. **Prior Week Metrics Review (5 min)**
   - Dials and activity target progress
   - Meetings booked vs. target
   - Conversation rate vs. target

2. **Call Review & Coaching (15 min)**
   - Play recordings of 2-3 recent calls (with permission)
   - Praise what's working ("Great tone on that wall," "Good discovery question")
   - Identify one improvement area ("Let's work on responding to timing objections")
   - Role play improved approach
   - Specific coaching takeaway

3. **Development & Roadmap (10 min)**
   - Progress on career development goals
   - Learning for upcoming campaign or vertical
   - SDR's questions or concerns
   - Next week's focus areas

**Monthly One-on-One (30 min - Month-End):**

- Comprehensive performance review
- Monthly metrics: dials, conversations, meetings booked, close rates
- Progress toward annual goals
- Strengths and development areas
- If underperforming: performance improvement plan (if necessary)
