# SECTION 5: RULES, SOPs, & OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORKS

## 5.1 Sales Process SOPs

### SOP 1: Deal Qualification (BANT+Web3)

**BANT Framework:**

- **Budget:** Confirmed budget and authority to spend
- **Authority:** Decision maker and economic buyer identified
- **Need:** Clear business problem and solution fit
- **Timeline:** Defined decision timeline and urgency

**Web3-Specific Qualification:**

- **Technical Readiness:** IT infrastructure and blockchain understanding
- **Regulatory Alignment:** Compliance with local crypto regulations
- **Stakeholder Buy-In:** Legal, finance, and technical team alignment
- **Pilot Opportunity:** Willingness to start with limited scope

**Qualification Score:**

- Strong (8-10): Proceed with full engagement
- Medium (5-7): Nurture and educate
- Weak (0-4): Disqualify or refer to marketing

### SOP 2: Sales Cycle Management

**Stage 1: Prospecting (Weeks 1-2)**

- Lead research and qualification
- Initial outreach and discovery
- Stakeholder mapping
- Next steps commitment

**Stage 2: Discovery (Weeks 3-6)**

- Deep needs analysis
- Technical requirements gathering
- Budget and timeline confirmation
- Solution presentation

**Stage 3: Proposal (Weeks 7-10)**

- Solution design and pricing
- Business case development
- ROI analysis and justification
- Proposal presentation

**Stage 4: Negotiation (Weeks 11-14)**

- Terms and conditions discussion
- Contract negotiation
- Stakeholder consensus building
- Final approvals

**Stage 5: Close (Weeks 15-16)**

- Contract execution
- Implementation kickoff
- Handoff to customer success
- Celebration and learning capture

### SOP 3: Sales Forecasting

**Forecast Categories:**

- **Commit (90%+):** Deal will close this period
- **Best Case (70-90%):** Likely to close this period
- **Pipeline (30-70%):** Possible but uncertain
- **Upside (10-30%):** Long shot, future period

**Forecast Review Process:**

- Weekly team forecast reviews
- Monthly leadership forecast calls
- Quarterly board forecast presentations
- Continuous pipeline inspection
