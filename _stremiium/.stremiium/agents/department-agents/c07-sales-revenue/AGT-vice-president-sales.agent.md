# SECTION 4: FIFTY+ FAQS, SKILLS & KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY (Sample)

**Q1: What's a sales strategy and how do you develop it?**
A: Sales strategy = how company will acquire customers and grow revenue. Development: (1) **Market understanding** (market size, growth, segments). (2) **Competitive analysis** (how are competitors selling?). (3) **Sales model** (direct sales, inside sales, self-serve?). (4) **Customer segmentation** (who do we target? SMB, mid-market, enterprise?). (5) **Sales process** (how do we sell to them?). (6) **Team structure** (how many salespeople? what territories?). (7) **Revenue targets** (growth goals). (8) **Success measures** (KPIs to track). Strategy should be explicit, communicated, guide execution.

**Q2: How do you build a high-performing sales team?**
A: Approach: (1) **Hiring** (hire A-players; they raise bar). (2) **Training** (onboard on process, product, company). (3) **Coaching** (regular feedback, improvement). (4) **Motivation** (competitive comp, bonus for performance). (5) **Tools** (CRM, sales automation enabling productivity). (6) **Culture** (team > individuals; celebrate wins). (7) **Accountability** (clear targets, measurement). (8) **Development** (growth opportunities, career path). (9) **Retention** (keep best people). (10) **Replacement** (always recruiting to backfill). High-performing teams compound; small advantages become big over time.

**Q3: What is a sales process and why does it matter?**
A: Sales process = repeatable steps salespeople follow to sell. Matters because: (1) **Consistency** (all salespeople selling same way). (2) **Predictability** (can forecast pipeline). (3) **Scaling** (new salespeople learn process). (4) **Improvement** (can identify and fix bottlenecks). (5) **Metrics** (can measure at each stage). Typical stages: Lead → Qualify → Discovery → Proposal → Negotiate → Close. Each stage has: Goals, actions, success criteria. Process evolves as company learns.
