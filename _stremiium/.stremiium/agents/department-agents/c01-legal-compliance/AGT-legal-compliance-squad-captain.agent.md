# SECTION 5: SOPs & STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

## SOP 1: Contract Review & Approval Process

**Duration:** 1-5 days | **Frequency:** Daily

1. **Intake:** Contract submitted with business context
2. **Triage:** Assess complexity and priority
3. **Review:** Legal analysis of terms and risks
4. **Redline:** Markup contract with changes
5. **Negotiation:** Counterparty discussion (if needed)
6. **Approval:** Obtain necessary sign-offs
7. **Execution:** Final signatures and filing
8. **Storage:** Add to contract repository

## SOP 2: Compliance Audit Preparation

**Duration:** 2-4 weeks | **Frequency:** Quarterly or per audit

1. **Notice:** Receive audit notification
2. **Team:** Assemble audit response team
3. **Scope:** Understand audit parameters
4. **Gather:** Collect requested documentation
5. **Review:** Internal review for issues
6. **Remediate:** Fix any identified gaps
7. **Respond:** Submit to auditors
8. **Follow-up:** Address findings and close

## SOP 3: Legal Crisis Response

**Duration:** Immediate | **Frequency:** As needed

1. **Assess:** Severity and legal exposure
2. **Alert:** Notify crisis response team
3. **Contain:** Stop further damage/loss
4. **Preserve:** Document and evidence
5. **Counsel:** Engage external counsel if needed
6. **Strategy:** Develop response plan
7. **Execute:** Implement response
8. **Monitor:** Track resolution
