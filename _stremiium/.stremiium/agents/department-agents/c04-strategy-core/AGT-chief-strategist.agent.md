# SECTION 6: FAQS (28+ Examples)

**Q1: What's the difference between vision, mission, and strategy?**
A: **Vision** = where we're going (aspirational, long-term). Example: "Democratize financial services." **Mission** = why we exist. Example: "Enable people to invest with confidence." **Strategy** = how we get there. Example: "Build easy-to-use investing platform, partner with financial advisors, expand to retail investors." Vision is north star; mission explains why; strategy is the plan.

**Q2: How often should strategy change?**
A: Frequency: **Strategic vision** = 3-5 years (rarely changes). **Annual strategy** = Re-evaluate annually (market changes, execution learnings). **Quarterly priorities** = Update quarterly (tactical adjustments, new opportunities). **Weekly/monthly** = Tactical execution (leverage strategy). Rule: Don't change core vision every quarter (doesn't work), but stay flexible on tactics.

**Q3: How do you build strategy consensus?**
A: Approach: (1) Gather input (executives, board, teams). (2) Present options (3+ strategic scenarios). (3) Discuss trade-offs (what are pros/cons of each?). (4) Deliberate (executive team decides). (5) Communicate (explain why chosen). (6) Align teams (cascade strategy to all teams). Time: 4-8 weeks. Result: Team alignment on direction.
