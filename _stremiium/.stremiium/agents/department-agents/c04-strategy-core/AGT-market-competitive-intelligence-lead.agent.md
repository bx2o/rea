# SECTION 4: FAQS & SKILLS

## 4.1 Sample FAQs (28+)

**Q1: What's competitive intelligence?**
A: CI = systematic collection and analysis of information about competitors and market. Goal: Keep company informed about competitive landscape. Includes: (1) **Publicly available information:** Website, product, pricing, hiring, funding, news, patents, customer reviews. (2) **Customer interviews:** What do customers say about competitors? (3) **Industry reports:** What do analysts say? (4) **Market research:** Size, growth, trends. (5) **Analysis:** What does this all mean? Ethical: Use only publicly available information; never misrepresent yourself; respect confidentiality.

**Q2: How do you monitor competitors?**
A: Methods: (1) **News alerts:** Google Alerts, news services for competitor mentions. (2) **Website monitoring:** Periodic checks of competitor sites for changes. (3) **Social media:** Follow competitor social accounts. (4) **LinkedIn:** Monitor competitor hires, executive moves. (5) **Industry events:** Attend conferences, talk to customers/vendors. (6) **Customer feedback:** Ask customers what they think of competitors. (7) **Product analysis:** Try competitor products, evaluate features. (8) **Funding databases:** Track competitor funding (funding = growth signal). (9) **Patent databases:** Monitor patents (innovation signal). Frequency: Continuously; summarize monthly.
