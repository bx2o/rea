# Market Expansion Decision Framework

**Purpose**: Evaluate expansion into new markets or geographies  
**Use When**: Geographic expansion, new customer segment, new product category  
**Owner**: The Rainmaker + The Strategist  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Pre-Expansion Assessment

### Market Analysis

- Market size and growth?
- Customer profile similar?
- Competitive landscape?
- Regulatory barriers?
- Economic indicators?

### Product-Market Fit

- Does product work there?
- Need localization?
- Pricing strategy?
- Distribution channels?

### Company Readiness

- Available resources?
- Team expertise/presence?
- Capital for expansion?
- Organizational capability?

### Risk Factors

- Political stability?
- Currency/forex risk?
- Talent availability?
- Vendor reliability?

---

## Decision Matrix

| Factor              | Entry     | Defer       | Skip        |
| ------------------- | --------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| **Market Size**     | $500M+    | $100M-500M  | <$100M      |
| **Growth Rate**     | >15%      | 5-15%       | <5%         |
| **Competition**     | Moderate  | High        | Intense     |
| **Resources**       | Available | Constrained | Unavailable |
| **Risk**            | Low       | Medium      | High        |
| **Time to Revenue** | <6 mo     | 6-12 mo     | >12 mo      |

---

## Entry Strategies

### Test & Learn (Low Risk)

- Partner with local company
- Small sales team
- Evaluate for 12 months
- Cost: $100k-$500k

### Full Entry (Medium Risk)

- Hire local team
- Regional marketing
- Full product support
- Cost: $500k-$2M

### Acquisition (High Risk)

- Buy existing player
- Integrate operations
- Immediate revenue
- Cost: $5M+

---

## Success Metrics (12 months)

- $X revenue achieved?
- Team size reached?
- Customer count milestone?
- Profitability timeline?
