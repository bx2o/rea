# The Capitalist - Finance & Investor Relations Workflows

**Agent**: The Capitalist 💼  
**Domain**: Finance, budgeting, investor relations, financial modeling  
**Purpose**: Financial planning, investor relations, financial forecasting, reporting

---

## Workflow 1: Financial Planning & Budgeting Workflow

### Trigger

- Annual budget cycle
- Quarterly planning
- New project budget needed
- Budget review cycle
- Financial forecasting required

### Process

#### Step 1: Gather Requirements

**Goal**: Understand financial needs  
**Actions**:

- Collect department requests
- Understand business plans
- Review last period's actuals
- Identify new initiatives
- Gather market data
- Document constraints

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ All requirements gathered
- ✅ Business plans understood
- ✅ Constraints clear

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 2: Develop Financial Model

**Goal**: Project financials  
**Actions**:

- Build budget spreadsheet
- Model revenue scenarios
- Project costs
- Include capital expenditures
- Model cash flow
- Create sensitivity analysis

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Model comprehensive
- ✅ Assumptions clear
- ✅ Scenarios realistic

**Duration**: 4-6 hours

---

#### Step 3: Analyze & Review

**Goal**: Ensure budget is sound  
**Actions**:

- Review budget vs. plan
- Identify variances
- Stress test scenarios
- Check reasonableness
- Gather feedback
- Make adjustments

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Budget realistic
- ✅ All concerns addressed
- ✅ Buy-in obtained

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 4: Present & Get Approval

**Goal**: Secure budget approval  
**Actions**:

- Create presentation
- Present to leadership
- Address questions/concerns
- Defend assumptions
- Get sign-off
- Document approvals

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Budget approved
- ✅ All stakeholders aligned
- ✅ Documented and filed

**Duration**: 1-2 hours presentation

---

#### Step 5: Manage & Monitor

**Goal**: Track financial performance  
**Actions**:

- Track actual spending
- Monitor vs. budget
- Report variances
- Investigate major variances
- Adjust forecast as needed
- Communicate status

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Spending under control
- ✅ Variances explained
- ✅ Forecast updated

**Duration**: Monthly + ongoing

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Budget comprehensive and realistic
- ✅ Approved by leadership
- ✅ Financial performance tracked
- ✅ Variances managed
- ✅ Financial health maintained

## Workflow 2: Financial Analysis & Investment Decision Workflow

### Trigger

- Investment decision needed
- Capital allocation decision
- Venture/acquisition opportunity
- Business case for project
- Cost/benefit analysis needed

### Process

#### Step 1: Frame the Analysis

**Goal**: Define what to analyze  
**Actions**:

- Clarify decision to be made
- Identify timeline
- Define success criteria
- Gather initial information
- Identify stakeholders
- Create analysis plan

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Analysis scope clear
- ✅ Success criteria defined
- ✅ Plan documented

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

#### Step 2: Gather Financial Data

**Goal**: Collect relevant information  
**Actions**:

- Research market/industry
- Gather company/project financials
- Interview stakeholders
- Collect comparable data
- Review assumptions
- Document sources

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Data comprehensive
- ✅ Sources reliable
- ✅ Assumptions documented

**Duration**: 3-5 hours

---

#### Step 3: Perform Analysis

**Goal**: Evaluate options  
**Actions**:

- Model financial scenarios
- Calculate NPV/IRR
- Create pro formas
- Analyze risks
- Compare alternatives
- Document findings

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Analysis rigorous
- ✅ Scenarios realistic
- ✅ Risks identified

**Duration**: 4-6 hours

---

#### Step 4: Prepare Recommendation

**Goal**: Make recommendation  
**Actions**:

- Analyze results
- Make recommendation
- Document rationale
- Address risks
- Outline next steps
- Prepare presentation

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Recommendation clear
- ✅ Analysis sound
- ✅ Presentation ready

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 5: Present & Decide

**Goal**: Get decision  
**Actions**:

- Present analysis
- Address questions
- Defend recommendations
- Facilitate decision
- Document decision
- Plan implementation

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Decision made
- ✅ Analysis understood
- ✅ Implementation plan clear

**Duration**: 1-2 hours presentation

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Financial analysis comprehensive
- ✅ Options compared
- ✅ Recommendation provided
- ✅ Decision made
- ✅ Implementation planned

## Workflow 4: Financial Forecasting & Scenario Planning Workflow

### Trigger

- Annual planning cycle
- Quarterly forecast review
- Financial uncertainty
- Market change impact assessment
- Strategic planning requires scenarios

### Process

#### Step 1: Collect Historical Data

**Goal**: Understand past performance  
**Actions**:

- Gather historical financials
- Analyze trends
- Understand drivers
- Note seasonality
- Document anomalies
- Create baseline

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Historical data complete
- ✅ Trends understood
- ✅ Baseline established

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 2: Develop Base Case Forecast

**Goal**: Project most likely future  
**Actions**:

- Project revenues
- Project expenses
- Project capital needs
- Project cash flow
- Build financial model
- Document assumptions

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Forecast realistic
- ✅ Assumptions clear
- ✅ Model robust

**Duration**: 4-6 hours

---

#### Step 3: Create Alternative Scenarios

**Goal**: Think about different futures  
**Actions**:

- Develop upside scenario
- Develop downside scenario
- Identify key variables
- Model sensitivity
- Understand breakeven points
- Document scenarios

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Scenarios reasonable
- ✅ Range clear
- ✅ Drivers identified

**Duration**: 3-4 hours

---

#### Step 4: Communicate Forecast

**Goal**: Share forecast with leadership  
**Actions**:

- Create forecast presentation
- Present scenarios
- Discuss assumptions
- Address concerns
- Address risks
- Plan for contingencies

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Forecast understood
- ✅ Leadership aligned
- ✅ Contingencies planned

**Duration**: 1-2 hours presentation

---

#### Step 5: Update & Monitor

**Goal**: Keep forecast current  
**Actions**:

- Track actuals monthly
- Update forecast
- Note variances
- Adjust assumptions
- Communicate updates
- Plan adjustments

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Forecast accurate
- ✅ Updates current
- ✅ Leadership informed

**Duration**: Monthly reviews

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Financial forecast comprehensive
- ✅ Scenarios developed
- ✅ Leadership aligned
- ✅ Contingencies planned
- ✅ Financial health transparent

### When to Delegate to Specialists

- [ ] Tax implications → Tax advisor
- [ ] Accounting standards → Accounting firm
- [ ] External audit → Auditors
- [ ] Investment advice → Investment advisor
