# The Guardian - Human Resources & Culture Workflows

**Agent**: The Guardian 👥  
**Domain**: Talent, culture, team development, performance  
**Purpose**: Recruitment, hiring, team development, conflict resolution, culture initiatives

---

## Workflow 1: Recruitment & Hiring Workflow

### Trigger

- New position approved
- Team expansion needed
- Key role vacancy
- Skill gap identified

### Process

#### Step 1: Define the Role

**Goal**: Clearly articulate position requirements  
**Actions**:

- Document role responsibilities
- Define required skills/experience
- Identify nice-to-have skills
- Determine compensation range
- Define reporting structure
- Outline growth opportunities

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Role description clear
- ✅ Requirements well-defined
- ✅ Compensation market-appropriate

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

#### Step 2: Source Candidates

**Goal**: Attract qualified applicants  
**Actions**:

- Post job description
- Reach out to network
- Engage recruiters
- Review applications
- Screen resumes
- Identify promising candidates

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Strong candidate pipeline
- ✅ Multiple qualified candidates
- ✅ Diversity of candidates

**Duration**: 1-2 weeks

---

#### Step 3: Screen & Interview

**Goal**: Evaluate candidate fit  
**Actions**:

- Phone screening
- Technical screening (if applicable)
- Prepare interview questions
- Conduct interviews
- Gather interviewer feedback
- Check references

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Candidates thoroughly evaluated
- ✅ Top candidates identified
- ✅ Cultural fit assessed

**Duration**: 2-3 weeks

---

#### Step 4: Make Offer

**Goal**: Extend offer to best candidate  
**Actions**:

- Get approval from hiring manager
- Prepare offer letter
- Extend offer
- Negotiate if needed
- Provide offer in writing
- Get signed acceptance

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Offer accepted
- ✅ Start date confirmed
- ✅ Reference checks complete

**Duration**: 2-3 days

---

#### Step 5: Onboard New Employee

**Goal**: Integrate employee into team  
**Actions**:

- Prepare workspace
- Create onboarding plan
- Assign mentor/buddy
- Conduct orientation
- Provide training
- Schedule 30/60/90 day check-ins

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Employee successfully onboarded
- ✅ Engaged with team
- ✅ Productive early

**Duration**: 1-3 months

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Qualified candidate hired
- ✅ Smooth onboarding completed
- ✅ Employee productive and engaged
- ✅ Team strengthened

## Workflow 2: Performance Management Workflow

### Trigger

- Annual performance review cycle
- Performance concern identified
- Promotion opportunity
- Career development discussion needed

### Process

#### Step 1: Set Expectations (Ongoing)

**Goal**: Clear goals and expectations  
**Actions**:

- Define performance expectations
- Set SMART goals
- Clarify success criteria
- Document in writing
- Get employee acknowledgment

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Expectations crystal clear
- ✅ Goals measurable
- ✅ Employee understands

**Duration**: 1 hour (annually + updates)

---

#### Step 2: Provide Feedback (Ongoing)

**Goal**: Regular coaching and feedback  
**Actions**:

- Provide regular feedback
- Address issues promptly
- Coach on improvement
- Recognize good performance
- Document significant events
- Maintain feedback log

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Feedback timely and constructive
- ✅ Employee engaged
- ✅ Issues tracked

**Ongoing throughout year**

---

#### Step 3: Conduct Review

**Goal**: Formal performance assessment  
**Actions**:

- Self-assessment (employee)
- Manager assessment
- Peer feedback (if applicable)
- Compile 360 feedback
- Document achievements
- Identify development areas
- Compare to goals

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Comprehensive assessment
- ✅ Fair and balanced
- ✅ Well-documented

**Duration**: 3-5 hours (over several days)

---

#### Step 4: Review Discussion

**Goal**: Discuss performance with employee  
**Actions**:

- Schedule meeting
- Share feedback openly
- Listen to employee perspective
- Discuss achievements
- Discuss areas for improvement
- Plan development
- Discuss compensation/promotion

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Constructive conversation
- ✅ Employee heard
- ✅ Development plan created

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

#### Step 5: Plan Development

**Goal**: Support growth  
**Actions**:

- Identify development goals
- Create learning plan
- Identify mentoring opportunities
- Plan skill development
- Allocate resources
- Schedule follow-up

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Clear development plan
- ✅ Resources allocated
- ✅ Employee motivated

**Duration**: 1 hour

---

#### Step 6: Monitor Progress

**Goal**: Track development  
**Actions**:

- Regular check-ins
- Monitor goal progress
- Provide coaching
- Remove obstacles
- Celebrate progress

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Development on track
- ✅ Employee growing
- ✅ Engagement maintained

**Ongoing**

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Fair performance review completed
- ✅ Development plan created
- ✅ Employee engaged and motivated
- ✅ Performance trajectory clear

## Workflow 3: Conflict Resolution Workflow

### Trigger

- Conflict between team members
- Interpersonal issue identified
- Performance problem related to conflict
- Team dynamics affected

### Process

#### Step 1: Understand the Situation

**Goal**: Gather information  
**Actions**:

- Meet with people involved
- Listen without judgment
- Understand each perspective
- Identify root causes
- Document issues
- Assess urgency/severity

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Multiple perspectives understood
- ✅ Root causes identified
- ✅ Severity assessed

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

#### Step 2: Meet with Parties Separately

**Goal**: Individual perspectives  
**Actions**:

- Private meetings with each
- Ask open-ended questions
- Listen actively
- Identify underlying needs
- Explore their proposed solutions
- Document key points

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Full picture understood
- ✅ Underlying needs clear
- ✅ Both sides heard

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

#### Step 3: Facilitate Resolution

**Goal**: Help resolve conflict  
**Actions**:

- Bring parties together (if appropriate)
- Use mediation techniques
- Guide to mutual understanding
- Brainstorm solutions together
- Get agreement on resolution
- Document agreement

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Resolution agreed
- ✅ Relationships repaired
- ✅ Agreement documented

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

#### Step 4: Implement Resolution

**Goal**: Execute agreed solution  
**Actions**:

- Monitor implementation
- Support both parties
- Remove obstacles
- Celebrate progress
- Address any new issues

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Agreement implemented
- ✅ Relationship improving
- ✅ Work continuing smoothly

**Duration**: 1-2 weeks

---

#### Step 5: Follow-Up

**Goal**: Ensure resolution sticks  
**Actions**:

- Check in with parties
- Verify satisfaction
- Monitor for regression
- Provide ongoing support
- Close case when resolved

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Resolution maintained
- ✅ Both parties satisfied
- ✅ Team dynamics healthy

**Duration**: Ongoing check-ins

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Conflict resolved
- ✅ Relationships repaired
- ✅ Team dynamics improved
- ✅ Productivity restored

---

## Workflow 4: Team Development & Culture Workflow

### Trigger

- Team engagement survey shows issues
- Culture improvement initiative
- Team capability gaps
- Team building needed
- Retention risk identified

### Process

#### Step 1: Assess Team

**Goal**: Understand team needs  
**Actions**:

- Conduct survey
- Interview team members
- Assess skills/capability gaps
- Evaluate team dynamics
- Understand career aspirations
- Identify cultural issues

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Team needs understood
- ✅ Gaps identified
- ✅ Cultural issues surfaced

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 2: Create Development Plan

**Goal**: Plan team improvement  
**Actions**:

- Identify development priorities
- Plan training/workshops
- Plan team building
- Plan career development
- Allocate budget
- Schedule activities

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Plan comprehensive
- ✅ Budget approved
- ✅ Activities scheduled

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 3: Implement Initiatives

**Goal**: Execute development activities  
**Actions**:

- Conduct training/workshops
- Facilitate team building
- Support individual development
- Create learning culture
- Recognize achievements
- Maintain momentum

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Activities delivered
- ✅ Participation strong
- ✅ Engagement high

**Duration**: Varies (multiple weeks)

---

#### Step 4: Measure Impact

**Goal**: Verify improvements  
**Actions**:

- Conduct follow-up survey
- Measure engagement
- Assess capabilities
- Gather feedback
- Measure retention
- Document results

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Impact measured
- ✅ Improvements validated
- ✅ ROI demonstrated

**Duration**: 1-2 months post-initiatives

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Team engagement improved
- ✅ Capabilities enhanced
- ✅ Culture strengthened
- ✅ Retention improved
- ✅ Team productivity increased
