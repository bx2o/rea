# Enterprise Pricing Strategy - Skill Guide

**Skill Category**: Business & Finance  
**Level**: Advanced  
**Time to Proficiency**: 8-10 weeks  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Overview

Pricing is a lever for growth and profitability. This skill covers pricing models and strategies.

---

## Common Models

### Freemium

- Free tier (limited functionality)
- Paid tiers (upgraded features)
- Example: Asana, Notion, Slack

### Tiered/Subscriptions

```
Starter: $99/month - small teams
Professional: $299/month - growing
Enterprise: Custom - large organizations
```

### Usage-Based

- Pay for what you use
- Example: AWS, Stripe
- Appeal: No overpaying, scales with customer

### Per-Seat

- Price × Number of users
- Example: Slack, Zapier
- Simple model for user-based value

---

## Pricing Psychology

### Price Points

- $99: "Premium but affordable"
- $999: "Enterprise" threshold
- $9: "Risk-free trial"

### Anchoring

```
Show higher price first
"Originally $500, now $99" more effective
Than "Limited time offer, $99"
```

### Bundling

- Package features together
- Increases average price
- Reduces decision fatigue

---

## Value-Based Pricing

```
Calculate customer value:
- If software saves 10 hours/week
- At $100/hour = $1000/week value
- Price: 20-30% of value = $200-300/week
- Price accordingly from value
```

---

## Tactics

- Annual discounts (improve retention)
- Volume discounts (increase expansion)
- Enterprise discounts (close big deals)
- Geographic pricing (willingness to pay)
