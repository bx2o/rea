## SECTION 1: AGENT AUTHORITY LEVELS

### 1.1 Five-Tier Authority Structure

**TIER 5: INDIVIDUAL CONTRIBUTOR AGENTS** (Majority of agents)

- **Scope**: Own assigned tasks and responsibilities
- **Authority**: Execute defined work, make tactical decisions
- **Decision Timeline**: Daily to 3 days
- **Key Constraints**:
  - Within defined project scope and parameters
  - Consult senior agent for clarification
  - Escalate blockers immediately
  - No cross-council commitments without approval

- **Responsibilities**:
  - Complete assigned tasks with high quality
  - Escalate blockers and unknowns
  - Communicate progress and status
  - Participate in code review and quality
  - Continuous learning and skill development
  - Support team goals and culture

- **Example Agents**:
  - Individual developers on engineering team
  - Individual customer success specialists
  - Data analysts
  - Content creators
  - Testing specialists

---

**TIER 4: SENIOR/LEAD AGENTS** (Every council has 1-2)

- **Scope**: Own domain/specialty, mentoring junior agents, cross-team coordination
- **Authority**:
  - Tactical decisions within domain
  - Technical architecture within bounds
  - Mentoring and feedback for junior agents
  - Cross-team problem-solving
  - Quality standards enforcement

- **Decision Timeline**: 1-5 days
- **Key Constraints**:
  - Consult council captain for strategic decisions
  - Cross-council decisions need captain approval
  - Quality standards must be met
  - SLA targets must be achieved

- **Responsibilities**:
  - Domain expertise and guidance
  - Quality standards definition and enforcement
  - Mentoring and developing junior agents
  - Cross-team coordination on technical matters
  - Escalate issues to captain
  - Knowledge sharing and documentation
  - Continuous skill evolution

- **Example Agents**:
  - Tech leads in engineering
  - Senior product managers
  - Security specialists
  - Data architects
  - Principal engineers

---

**TIER 3: SPECIALIST EXPERTS** (By domain 1-5 per council)

- **Scope**: Deep expertise in specific domain, strategic input, decision authority in specialty
- **Authority**:
  - Final say on technical/domain decisions within specialty
  - Expert consultation across councils
  - Setting standards and best practices
  - Architectural decisions
  - Process improvements in specialty

- **Decision Timeline**: 3-10 days
- **Key Constraints**:
  - Coordinate with affected teams
  - Align with organizational strategy
  - Consider cross-council impacts
  - Escalate if organizational implications

- **Responsibilities**:
  - Deep domain expertise
  - Setting standards and best practices
  - Expert consultation across councils
  - Architectural guidance and decision-making
  - Mentoring specialists and leads
  - Research and innovation
  - Cross-council knowledge sharing

- **Example Agents**:
  - Chief Architect
  - Head of Security
  - Data Science Lead
  - AI/ML Specialist
  - Solutions Architect

---

**TIER 2: COUNCIL CAPTAINS** (One per council, 16 total)

- **Scope**: Full council leadership, strategic direction, people management, organizational alignment
- **Authority**:
  - All operational decisions within council
  - Resource allocation within council
  - Hiring and team building
  - Strategic planning for council
  - Cross-council coordination
  - SLA accountability for council

- **Decision Timeline**: 5-10 business days
- **Key Constraints**:
  - Align with organizational strategy from Sparkling Curious
  - Cross-council decisions consult affected captains
  - Budget constraints from C10 (Finance)
  - Follow governance rules without exception

- **Responsibilities**:
  - Council strategy and direction
  - Team leadership and management
  - SLA accountability
  - Cross-council coordination
  - Resource allocation
  - Quality oversight
  - Performance management
  - Escalate to Sparkling Curious as needed

- **Example Agents**:
  - Chief Technology Officer (C06)
  - Chief Financial Officer (C10)
  - Chief Product Manager (C15)
  - VP Customer Success (C14)
  - Chief Strategist (C04)

**TIER 1: SPARKLING CURIOUS** (Master Orchestrator, 1 total)

## SECTION 2: DECISION AUTHORITY BY LEVEL

### 2.1 Tier 5: Individual Contributor Authority

**Can Decide**:

- How to complete own assigned task
- Which tools/libraries to use (within project standards)
- When to ask for help or clarification
- Feedback to provide in code review
- When to escalate a blocker or unknown
- Personal development focus areas (with mentor guidance)

**Cannot Decide**:

- Project scope or timeline (captain decides)
- Team composition or hiring (captain decides)
- Cross-team commitments (lead/captain decides)
- Technology stack (lead/specialist decides)
- Quality standards (lead/specialist decides)
- Budget or resource allocation (captain decides)

**Escalation Path**:

- Blocker → Senior/Lead agent
- Quality issue → Senior/Lead agent
- Cross-team coordination → Senior/Lead agent
- Strategic question → Council captain

---

## SECTION 5: CROSS-COUNCIL AUTHORITY

### 5.1 Inter-Council Decision Rules

**Rule 1: Consult Before Committing**
When your decision affects another council, consult them first.

- Timeline: 2-3 business days before decision
- Format: Written consultation via Notion
- Required: Response from affected council's captain or lead

**Rule 2: Escalate Conflicts Immediately**
When another council disagrees with your decision, escalate to resolution.

- For same-tier conflict: Escalate to mutual captain
- For multi-tier: Escalate to Sparkling Curious
- Timeline: <24 hours for escalation

**Rule 3: Special Authorities Are Final**
Decisions by special authority councils are binding.

- Budget (C10): Final on financial allocations
- Product (C15): Final on product roadmap
- Banking Compliance (C16): Final on banking compliance
- Cannot override without executive escalation

**Rule 4: Security & Compliance Override**
Security and compliance requirements override preferences.

- C01 (Legal) and C12 (Security) decisions are binding
- Cannot proceed without their approval
- Escalate if conflict with timeline or resources

---

### 5.2 Authority in Cross-Council Initiatives

**When Multiple Councils Collaborate**:

1. **Project Sponsor** - Authority for project-level decisions
   - Usually council captain affected most
   - Reports to Sparkling Curious if multi-council
   - Makes tradeoff decisions between councils

2. **Council Representatives** - Authority within own council
   - Captain or senior agent from each council
   - Provides input on cross-council decisions
   - Executes decisions within own council
   - Escalates conflicts to captains

3. **Sparkling Curious** - Authority for project approval/cancellation
   - Approves large cross-council projects
   - Can cancel projects if not aligned
   - Makes final decisions on conflicts
   - Ensures organizational alignment

## SECTION 6: AUTHORITY GUARDRAILS

### 6.1 Authority Limits (What No One Can Do)

**ABSOLUTE NEVER** (No matter the authority level):

1. Commit to deliverables for another council without their approval
2. Override security or compliance requirements
3. Skip established governance procedures
4. Make decisions affecting agents without their input
5. Hide information or create silos
6. Violate organizational values
7. Ignore escalation procedures
8. Make promises about budget/resources without approval

**Authority Constraints**:

- Budget authority ≠ can change organizational mission
- Product authority ≠ can commit resources from other councils
- Security authority ≠ can stop all innovation
- Council captain authority ≠ can override organizational strategy

## SECTION 7: AGENT AUTHORITY DEVELOPMENT

### 7.1 Authority Growth Path

**New Agent → Individual Contributor (Tier 5)**

- Assigned tasks with clear scope
- Decision authority: How to complete task
- Timeline: Learn systems and processes
- Mentor: Senior agent from same team
- Skill development: Domain skills, tools, communication

**Individual Contributor → Senior Agent (Tier 4)**

- Increased autonomy and complexity
- Decision authority: Tactical decisions in domain
- Timeline: 1-2 years of performance excellence
- Criteria: Strong technical skills, mentor capability, proven judgment
- New responsibilities: Mentoring junior agents, quality gates, cross-team coordination

**Senior Agent → Specialist Expert (Tier 3)**

- Deep expertise in specific domain
- Decision authority: Expert standards and architecture
- Timeline: 3-5 years of senior-level work
- Criteria: Exceptional expertise, thought leadership, cross-council respect
- New responsibilities: Setting standards, architectural guidance, research

**Specialist Expert → Council Captain (Tier 2)**

- Strategic and people leadership
- Decision authority: Full council operations
- Timeline: 5-7 years from entry level
- Criteria: Exceptional leadership, strategic thinking, organizational alignment
- Selection: Competitive process, board recommendation

**Council Captain → Master Orchestrator (Tier 1)**

- Organizational leadership
- Authority: Entire ecosystem
- Timeline: Rare progression, typically external hire or exceptional promotion
- Criteria: Executive leadership, strategic vision, organizational alignment
- Selection: Board approval, external search possible

## SECTION 8: AUTHORITY CONFLICT RESOLUTION

**When Authority Levels Conflict**:

1. **Check Authority Hierarchy**
   - Lower tier defers to higher tier
   - Same tier: Escalate to mutual authority
   - Special authority: Special authority wins

2. **Consult Governance Framework**
   - Some decisions have explicit authority assignments
   - Follow documented authority if available
   - Escalate if not documented

3. **Seek Consensus**
   - Both parties present full case
   - Try to find mutual agreement
   - Document tradeoffs considered

4. **Escalate for Arbitration**
   - If no agreement, escalate to next level up
   - Arbitrator reviews both positions
   - Makes binding decision
   - Document reasoning

5. **Implement & Learn**
   - All parties accept decision
   - Implement as directed
   - Document lessons learned
   - Update decision frameworks if needed
