# SaaS Metrics & Unit Economics - Skill Guide

**Skill Category**: Business & Finance  
**Level**: Intermediate  
**Time to Proficiency**: 6-8 weeks  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Overview

SaaS metrics measure business health and growth. This skill covers key financial metrics.

---

## Subscription Metrics

### MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)

- Predictable revenue per month
- Example: 100 customers × $100/mo = $10,000 MRR

### ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)

- MRR × 12
- Example: $10,000 × 12 = $120,000 ARR

### Churn Rate

- % of customers lost per month
- Target: < 5% monthly (healthy)
- Formula: Churned / Start of Month

---

## Unit Economics

### CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)

```
Sales & Marketing Spend / New Customers Acquired
Example: $100,000 / 1,000 = $100 CAC
```

### LTV (Lifetime Value)

```
Average Revenue per Account / Monthly Churn Rate
Example: $100 / 0.05 = $2,000 LTV
```

### CAC Payback

```
CAC / Monthly Revenue per Customer
Example: $100 / $100 = 1 month (excellent)
Target: < 12 months
```

---

## Viability Threshold

```
LTV:CAC Ratio > 3:1
Example: $2,000:$100 = 20:1 (great business)
```

---

## Growth Metrics

- MoM Growth Rate: (This Month - Last Month) / Last Month
- Delinquent MRR: Customers late on payment
- Logo Churn vs. Revenue Churn
