# Copywriting & Content Strategy - Skill Guide

**Skill Category**: Content  
**Level**: Intermediate  
**Time to Proficiency**: 6-8 weeks  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Overview

Copywriting is the art of persuasive writing that drives action. This skill covers messaging, positioning, and creating content that converts.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Know Your Audience

- Who are they?
- What do they want?
- What are their fears?
- How do they speak?

### 2. Have a Clear Purpose

- What do you want them to do?
- What's the desired outcome?
- Make it obvious

### 3. Lead With Benefit

```
❌ "We offer cloud storage"
✅ "Keep your files safe and accessible from anywhere"
```

### 4. Use Simple Language

```
❌ "Utilize our platform to optimize compliance"
✅ "Stay compliant with our simple tools"
```

---

## Copywriting Formulas

### AIDA

```
Attention: Get notice
Interest: Show relevance
Desire: Create want
Action: Tell them what to do
```

### PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve)

```
Problem: Name the problem
Agitate: Show why it matters
Solve: Present your solution
```

---

## Content Strategy

### Define Your Message

- What's your unique value?
- Why should anyone care?
- What's the core message?

### Create Content Pillars

```
Product Focus
  - Features
  - Updates
  - Case studies

Educational Focus
  - How-tos
  - Guides
  - Best practices

Community Focus
  - User stories
  - Events
  - Conversations
```
