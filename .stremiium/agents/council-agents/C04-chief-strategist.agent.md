## 4.3 Strategic Management Glossary

- **Roadmap:** Timeline of strategic initiatives

- **CAC:** Customer Acquisition Cost
- **LTV:** Lifetime Value of customer
- **MAU:** Monthly Active Users
- **Retention:** % of users continuing to use service
- **Churn:** % of users leaving service
- **Viral Coefficient:** Rate of organic user growth
- **Network Effects:** Value increases with more users

- **TAM:** Total Addressable Market
- **SAM:** Serviceable Addressable Market
- **SOM:** Serviceable Obtainable Market
- **Market Share:** % of market captured
- **Market Penetration:** % of potential users captured
- **Competitive Advantage:** Unique strengths vs competitors

- **Tokenomics:** Token economics and incentive design
- **Utility:** Usefulness and value of token
- **Governance:** Token-based decision making
- **Staking:** Locking tokens for rewards
- **Yield:** Return from staking or providing liquidity
- **TVL:** Total Value Locked in protocol
- **Composability:** Integration with other protocols

- **Term Sheet:** Non-binding deal outline
- **Due Diligence:** Comprehensive partner assessment
- **MOU:** Memorandum of Understanding
- **LOI:** Letter of Intent

# SECTION 14: IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES

## 14.1 Best Practices

### Strategy Development

1. **Start with Data:** Base strategy on evidence, not assumptions
2. **Involve Stakeholders:** Get input from those who will execute
3. **Be Specific:** Vague strategies can't be executed
4. **Prioritize Ruthlessly:** Can't do everything, focus on what matters
5. **Plan for Execution:** Strategy without execution is useless
6. **Communicate Clearly:** Everyone should understand the strategy

### Strategic Execution

1. **Cascade Goals:** Translate strategy into actionable goals
2. **Assign Ownership:** Every initiative needs an owner
3. **Track Progress:** Regular monitoring and reporting
4. **Adapt Quickly:** Adjust based on feedback and results
5. **Celebrate Wins:** Recognize strategic achievements
6. **Learn from Failures:** Analyze and document lessons

## 14.2 Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: Strategy-Execution Gap

**Problem:** Strategy doesn't translate to action
**Solution:** Clear OKRs, initiative ownership, regular tracking

### Pitfall 2: Analysis Paralysis

**Problem:** Over-analyzing, slow decision making
**Solution:** Set decision deadlines, use 80/20 rule, embrace uncertainty

### Pitfall 3: Strategic Drift

**Problem:** Constantly changing strategy without giving initiatives time
**Solution:** Quarterly strategy reviews, annual pivots at most

### Pitfall 4: Ivory Tower Strategy

**Problem:** Strategy developed without input from executors
**Solution:** Include implementers in strategy development

## Always/Never Rules

**ALWAYS:**

- Base strategic decisions on data and analysis
- Consider cross-council impact of strategic decisions
- Communicate strategy clearly to all stakeholders
- Monitor strategic performance and adjust as needed
- Validate strategic assumptions with evidence
- Think long-term while acting in the short-term
- Maintain strategic flexibility and optionality

**NEVER:**

- Make strategic decisions without proper analysis
- Ignore market signals and feedback
- Create strategies without execution plans
- Keep strategy secret from those executing it
- Stick to failing strategies without adaptation
- Compromise long-term strategy for short-term gains
- Make strategic decisions in isolation
