# SECTION 5: RULES, SOPs, & OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORKS

## 5.1 Coordination SOPs

### SOP 1: Feature Intake & Feasibility Assessment

**Trigger:** New feature request from Product

**Steps:**

1. Receive product requirements document (PRD)
2. Clarify product goals and success metrics
3. Conduct technical feasibility analysis
4. Map dependencies (internal and external)
5. Estimate engineering effort (story points/hours)
6. Identify risks and mitigation strategies
7. Assess resource availability
8. Provide feasibility recommendation (Go/No-go/Modify)
9. Align with roadmap priorities
10. Present to engineering leadership
11. Obtain engineering commitment
12. Schedule kickoff meeting

**Success Criteria:** Clear technical specs, realistic timeline, agreed scope
**Timeline:** 3-5 business days
**Escalation:** Complex architectural decisions → CTO

### SOP 2: Sprint Planning Coordination

**Trigger:** Bi-weekly sprint planning cycle

**Steps:**

1. Review product backlog priorities
2. Assess engineering capacity
3. Identify sprint goals
4. Break down features into stories
5. Estimate story points
6. Identify dependencies
7. Resolve capacity conflicts
8. Confirm sprint commitment
9. Document sprint plan
10. Communicate to stakeholders

**Success Criteria:** Committed sprint plan with 85%+ completion rate
**Timeline:** 2-3 hours per sprint

### SOP 3: Weekly Progress Synchronization

**Trigger:** Weekly (Tuesdays 10:00 AM)

**Steps:**

1. Collect status from engineering teams
2. Review burn-down charts
3. Identify blockers
4. Assess timeline risks
5. Update product on progress
6. Resolve cross-team issues
7. Adjust priorities if needed
8. Document decisions
9. Distribute status report

**Success Criteria:** All teams aligned, blockers cleared within 24 hours
