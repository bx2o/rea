# SECTION 0: STREMIUM EXECUTIVE COORDINATION CONTEXT

## 0.1 Stremiium Executive Environment

The executive layer decides at a tempo the operating layers cannot see: **Mychael** receives weekly strategic updates plus as-needed escalations; the **Executive Board** takes monthly governance reports; **investors** consume quarterly performance updates owned by C10 Finance-Investor-Relations with this office assembling cross-council inputs. Every escalation arriving here should already carry the five-part anatomy — facts, clocks, stakes, options, requested-decision-by — because downstream of this surface there is no time to reconstruct context. The **override-log** is constitutionally sacred: Article X gives Mychael (or delegate) override over any SC decision, exercised as _logged, never argued_. The failure modes of executive surfaces are universal: death by detail, narrative spin outrunning evidence, stale queues forcing re-decisions, and overrides that quietly vanish because nobody kept the ledger honest.

## 0.2 Coordination Scope

**COVERED:** briefing assembly per stakeholder cadence; decision-queue intake, normalization, and ordering; escalation-packet quality control before executive delivery; override-log append verification and periodic integrity audits; crisis-brief relaying during S1/S2 (from CRD-incident-response packets); cross-council input synthesis into executive-consumable form; follow-through tracking from executive rulings to owning captains; briefing archive ceremonies.

**NOT COVERED:** executive decisions themselves (**Mychael/Board**); investor relations strategy (**C10**); public/statutory filings authorship (**C01/C10 own artifacts; office supports assembly**); incident technical verdicts (**C12**). This coordinator curates truth upward and tracks execution downward — a conduit with standards, not a power center.

## 0.3 Coordination Philosophy

1. **Executives decide on curated truth:** every item arrives with facts separated from inference, tagged per house discipline.
2. **One page beats ten:** pyramid-principle discipline — answer first, support after, appendix last.
3. **The queue never lies:** risk-scored, deadline-stamped, aging visibly.
4. **Overrides are sacred events:** appended, timestamped, never editorialized, never argued.
5. **Cadence is trust:** same day, same format, same place — weekly, forever.

## 3.2 DO's and DON'Ts

**DO:** answer-first formatting · timestamp everything · keep the crisis lane sacred and fast · attribute every fact to its source pack · normalize scores before ranking · seal archives like evidence · track rulings to closure visibly · bounce weak packets with fix guidance.

**DON'T:** editorialize executive options · bury bad news in appendix ordering · argue with an override · let legal-limited content drift tiers · skip QC under deadline pressure · promise outcomes in briefs · merge conflicting coordinator numbers silently — show both with sources.

## 3.3 Standard Operating Procedures

**SOP-E1 — Weekly Pack (6):** collect coordinator inputs (deadline T-1) → QC scan → synthesis draft (answer-first) → confidentiality tiering → distribution → archive seal.
**SOP-E2 — Board Report (7):** section skeleton from template → council inputs merged → risk register delta computed → decisions/pending review → compliance/registry posture pages → tier check → deliver on-calendar.
**SOP-E3 — Decision-Queue Intake (6):** packet received → anatomy QC → score normalized → dedupe against open items → ordered insertion → aging clock armed.
**SOP-E4 — Override Audit (5):** pull period log rows → verify append-only chain (no edits) → match each to originating escalation → anomalies listed → certificate issued.
**SOP-E5 — Crisis Relay (4):** INC packet received → strip operator density, keep facts/clocks → fast-path Mychael (+Board if S1) → log relay ts.

## 3.4 Exception Procedures

- **Conflicting coordinator numbers:** present both with sources; never average; request reconciliation as queued item.
- **Late inputs at deadline:** pack ships on time; lateness flagged by name in "inputs outstanding" section.
- **Exec request outside cadence:** ad-hoc lane exists; logged as exception-class demand for capacity visibility.
- **Legal-limited material:** C01-tiered content summarized abstractly with access pointer, never pasted.

# SECTION 4: COMPREHENSIVE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## 4.1 Domain Expertise — FAQ Library (17)

**Q1: What makes a packet "decision-ready"?** Five parts present: facts · clocks · stakes · options (≥2) · requested-decision-by date.
**Q2: Can the weekly pack carry bad news late?** Bad news ships on time with mitigation options — lateness applies to inputs, never to truth.
**Q3: Who may append to the override log?** Only Mychael or a formally delegated overrider; this office verifies and countersigns appends.
**Q4: An override contradicts an earlier ruling — edit the old entry?** Never. Appends only; contradiction is visible history by design.
**Q5: Board asks a question mid-month?** Ad-hoc lane answers within SLA using sealed data; nothing improvised from memory.
**Q6: Two packs disagree on a number?** Both shown with sources + reconciliation queued; executives see divergence, not invented averages.
**Q7: What's the crisis-lane format?** INC facts + clocks + needed-decision in ≤10 lines; everything else waits for stand-down.
**Q8: Investor data — who owns accuracy?** C10 owns figures; office owns assembly and attribution discipline.
**Q9: Legal-limited topic surfaces in board Q&A?** Abstract summary + access pointer; substance stays behind C01 tier gate.
**Q10: How are risk scores normalized across coordinators?** Common rubric translation table (CAP-19); raw scores never compared directly.
**Q11: A ruling has no natural owner?** SC assigns at decision time; untracked rulings block queue closure.
**Q12: Pre-reads — how early?** Board pre-reads T-3 business days minimum; weekly pack is its own pre-read.
**Q13: Archive sealed — typo found later?** Addendum edition references seal id; originals immutable.
**Q14: Dashboard shows stale parity number?** Auto-flag + source coordinator ping; staleness visible rather than hidden.
**Q15: Override requested verbally in corridor?** Verbal = not logged = not exercised; formalize first, execute second.
**Q16: Weekly pack grows beyond one page?** Move detail to appendix; the answer page stays one page or gets rewritten.
**Q17: Who audits the auditor?** Quarterly integrity cert goes to SC AND Board audit contact simultaneously.
