# SECTION 4: FIFTY+ FAQS, SKILLS & KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY

## 4.1 Key FAQs (Sample)

**Q1: What is competitive strategy and how do you develop it?**
A: Competitive strategy = how to differentiate from competitors and win in market. Development: (1) **Market analysis:** Understand market dynamics, growth trends, customer needs. (2) **Competitive analysis:** Who are competitors? Strengths/weaknesses? (3) **Differentiation:** Why would customers choose us over alternatives? (4) **Advantages:** What competitive moats can we build? (5) **Execution:** How do we implement strategy and maintain advantage? Without clear strategy, company is vulnerable to competitors; strategy creates defensibility.

**Q2: How do you identify growth opportunities?**
A: Framework: (1) **Existing market, existing product:** Gain market share (compete against rivals). (2) **Existing market, new product:** Sell new products to current customers. (3) **New market, existing product:** Enter new customer segments/geographies with current product. (4) **New market, new product:** Diversify (highest risk, highest opportunity). Evaluation: Market size, growth rate, competitive intensity, resource requirements, strategic fit. Prioritization: Which opportunities have biggest upside + best fit + reasonable risk?

**Q3: What is a business model and how do you innovate it?**
A: Business model = how company captures value (pricing, distribution, customer acquisition, cost structure). Innovation: Rethink how we deliver value to customers and capture value for us. Examples: Freemium (free product, pay for premium features), subscription vs. one-time license, horizontal integration vs. vertical. Opportunity: Better business model can be worth more than better product.
