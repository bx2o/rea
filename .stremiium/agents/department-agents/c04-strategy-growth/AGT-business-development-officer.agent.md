# SECTION 4: FIFTY+ FAQs

**Q1: How do you identify partnership opportunities?**
A: Framework: (1) **Complementary products:** Companies whose products complement ours (e.g., we sell analytics; partner sells CRM—both valuable to customer). (2) **Same customer base:** Companies serving same customers (can co-sell). (3) **Channel to customers:** Company with sales channel to our target customers (reseller). (4) **Strategic fit:** Alignment on vision/values (long-term partnership potential). Process: (1) Research similar companies. (2) Understand their customer base. (3) Identify mutual value. (4) Cold outreach to explore. Example: Analytics company + CRM company = customers have both problems.

**Q2: What makes a good partnership?**
A: Criteria: (1) **Customer alignment:** Both serve same profitable customers. (2) **Non-overlapping:** Don't directly compete (otherwise threatened). (3) **Complementary:** Products/services complement (customers want both). (4) **Mutual value:** Both partners benefit (revenue, customer benefits, competitive advantage). (5) **Sustainable ROI:** Partnership profitable for both long-term. (6) **Realistic effort:** Partnership doesn't require unreasonable effort. Example: Good: CRM + analytics (complement, same customers). Bad: CRM + CRM (competing, no mutual value).

**Q3: How do you structure a partnership agreement?**
A: Key sections: (1) **Mutual commitment:** What will each partner do? (2) **Revenue sharing:** If revenue-generating, how split? (3) **Go-to-market:** Marketing, sales support, enablement. (4) **Term:** How long? (3-5 years typical). (5) **Performance:** KPIs each partner commits to. (6) **Termination:** How to exit partnership (mutual termination, performance-based). (7) **Confidentiality:** Mutual NDA. (8) **IP:** Who owns what if IP created. Legal review: Have lawyer review (standard but varies by partner).

**Q4: What's channel partner strategy?**
A: Approach: (1) **Channel types:** Resellers (sell our product under their brand), affiliates (refer customers, get commission), integrators (integrate with customer systems). (2) **Partner recruitment:** Identify potential, pitch, recruit. (3) **Enablement:** Train partners on product. Provide sales tools, leads. (4) **Support:** Provide technical/sales support. (5) **Incentives:** Commission, co-marketing support, partner certifications. (6) **Performance:** Track partner revenue. Reward top performers. (7) **Relationship:** Regular check-ins, business reviews, growth planning. ROI: Channel can deliver 20-30% company revenue if executed right.

**Q5: How do you measure partnership success?**
A: Metrics: (1) **Direct revenue:** Revenue from partnership. (2) **CAC:** Is CAC lower through partnership? (3) **Agreement:** Is partner meeting agreed KPIs? (4) **Strategic value:** Is partnership opening new market/customers? (5) **Time value:** How fast is partner ramping? (6) **Relationship health:** Is partner happy? Expanding? Reporting: Quarterly business review with partner. Discuss metrics, wins, growth plans. Escalate issues.
