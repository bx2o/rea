# SECTION 4: FIFTY+ FAQs

**Q1: What are OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)?**
A: Framework: (1) **Objectives:** Qualitative goals (what we want to achieve). Example: "Accelerate enterprise customer acquisition." (2) **Key Results:** Quantitative metrics (how we measure success). Example: "Close 10 enterprise customers." Structure: 3-5 OKRs per quarter. Each OKR has 3-4 Key Results. Ownership: Each OKR owned by 1 person. Timeline: Review every week; adjust monthly if needed. Example: Objective = "Improve product quality." Key Results = (1) Reduce crash rate to <1%, (2) Improve Nps to 50+, (3) Reduce support tickets 20%.

**Q2: How do you set growth OKRs?**
A: Process: (1) **Analyze last quarter:** What worked? What didn't? (2) **Identify bottlenecks:** Where is growth limited? (3) **Set ambition:** What growth rate are we targeting? (4) **Define OKRs:** Which levers will drive growth? (5) **Operationalize:** Break into team OKRs (sales, marketing, product, ops). (6) **Align:** All teams understand + agree. (7) **Commit:** Make OKRs public + accountable. Example: Growth target = 50% revenue growth. Sales OKR = "Close $5M revenue." Marketing OKR = "Generate 100 qualified leads." Product OKR = "Launch feature X (increases retention 10%)."

**Q3: What are growth metrics and which ones matter most?**
A: Framework: (1) **Acquisition:** How fast are we acquiring customers? (CAC, CAC payback, pipeline value). (2) **Retention:** How well do we keep customers? (Churn, NRR). (3) **Expansion:** How much do customers expand spending? (ACV growth, expansion revenue). (4) **Efficiency:** How efficient is our growth? (LTV:CAC ratio, CAC payback). (5) **Funnel:** Where are we losing customers? (Awareness → Consideration → Decision → Retention). Most important: 3-5 metrics that ladder up to revenue goal. Example: Revenue goal = $10M. Metrics = (1) Pipeline value $50M, (2) Win rate 20%, (3) CAC $2K, (4) LTV $20K.

**Q4: How do you run A/B experiments?**
A: Framework: (1) **Hypothesis:** "If we change X, we'll see Y improvement." (2) **Control vs. treatment:** Split sample—control gets old version, treatment gets new. (3) **Metric:** What will we measure? (4) **Duration:** How long to run (minimum 1 week, often 2-4 weeks). (5) **Sample size:** Big enough for statistical significance? (6) **Run:** Launch treatment. (7) **Analyze:** Did treatment beat control? (8) **Decide:** Scale winner or archive loser. Tools: Optimizely, Google Optimize, Statsig. Speed: Run 1-2 experiments per week on key metrics.

**Q5: How do you identify growth bottlenecks?**
A: Analysis: (1) **Map funnel:** Trace customer journey (awareness → consideration → decision → expansion). (2) **Measure dropoff:** Where do people drop off most? (3) **Prioritize:** Fix biggest drops first. (4) **Root cause:** Why are they dropping? (interview, data, feedback). (5) **Experiment:** Test fix. (6) **Scale:** If fix works, scale. Example: 10K people aware → 1K consider → 100 buy → 50 expand. Bottleneck = expand (95% churn). Root cause = product doesn't deliver enough value. Fix = improve product; measure if churn improves.
