# SECTION 4: COMPREHENSIVE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## 4.1 FAQs: Sales Development Category (15+)

**Q: What defines an "ideal customer profile" (ICP)?**
A: Firmographic data (industry, revenue, employee count), role distribution (decision-makers), buying signals (growth, funding, public initiatives), and past win analysis. Document with examples; refresh quarterly.

**Q: How should I structure an outbound prospecting sequence?**
A: Multi-touch omni-channel approach: Email Day 1 (introduction), Call Days 2-3 (follow-up), Email Day 5 (value angle), Call Day 7 (final touch), Move to nurture if no response. Track response at each step.

**Q: What does BANT qualification look like in practice?**
A: B (Budget): "What's approved for this initiative?" | A (Authority): "Who makes final decision?" | N (Need): "What specific challenge?" | T (Timeline): "When would you implement?" Document each answer.

**Q: How do I manage SDR/BDR team compensation?**
A: Salary (base security) + Quota-based commission (8-15% of salary if on-target). Qualify leads, not just dials. Track leading (dials, conversations) and lagging metrics (conversion, deals). Transparent scorecards.

**Q: What's the optimal number of touches before disqualifying a prospect?**
A: 5-7 touches across 21 days (email, call, email, call, email, social, final call). Document disqualification reason. Circle back to nurture list quarterly.

**Q: How do I handle objection "We're happy with our current vendor"?**
A: Acknowledge (valid perspective) → Reframe (specific improvement areas, competitive differentiation) → Discovery question (What would cause you to evaluate?) → Set next step.

**Q: What's the role of LinkedIn in SDR prospecting?**
A: Profile research (role, recent activity, connections), personalized outreach (mention specifics), warm introductions (2nd/3rd connections), content engagement (build relationship before direct ask).

**Q: How should I build territory assignments fairly?**
A: Analyze account potential (revenue, seat count, opportunity), balance workload, give high performers growth territories, provide emerging talent learning territories. Review quarterly.

**Q: What makes a sales engagement tool valuable for SDRs?**
A: Email sequencing (automated follow-up), call logging integration, CRM sync (data accuracy), analytics dashboard (visibility), automation workflows (reduce manual work).

**Q: How do I prevent SDR burnout given high-pressure environments?**
A: Reasonable activity expectations (not 200 dials/day), transparent path to promotion (AE transition), coaching/skill development (feel competent), healthy competition culture (no blame for rejections).

**Q: What's the handoff process from SDR to AE?**
A: Complete discovery notes in CRM (needs, timeline, budget, stakeholders), context setting call (AE + SDR + prospect), documented next steps, expected follow-up date assigned to AE.

**Q: How do I measure SDR/BDR productivity objectively?**
A: Leading: Dials, conversations/connects, email opens/responses | Lagging: Qualified leads created, conversion rate to opportunity, average sales cycle impact, revenue attributed.

**Q: What's a realistic first-year SDR ramp?**
A: Month 1-3: Training/ramp (25% quota), Month 4-6: Ramping (50% quota), Month 7-12: Full expectation (100% quota). Document skill development milestones.

**Q: How should I approach territory management with field expansion?**
A: Start with existing strong territories (maintain revenue), assign growth territories to high performers, add new geographic or vertical territories, rotate to prevent stagnation.

**Q: What frequency for prospecting message testing is optimal?**
A: Monthly email subject line testing (hold body consistent), Quarterly messaging audit (value prop refresh), Annual overall strategy review (market changes, competitive landscape).
