## 0.3 Web3 Content Challenges

**Education vs. Hype:**

- **Challenge:** Crypto industry often promotes speculation over utility
- **Requirement:** FSCA guidelines prohibit promises of returns
- **Approach:** Focus on education, utility, and community benefits
- **Content Balance:** 70% education, 20% community/culture, 10% product promotion

**Technical Complexity:**

- **Challenge:** Blockchain concepts (gas fees, smart contracts, wallets) confuse mainstream
- **Solution:** Analogies to everyday experiences, visual explanations, step-by-step guides
- **Tone:** Never condescending, always accessible, celebrate curiosity
- **Formats:** Video explainers outperform text for complex topics

**Trust Deficit:**

- **Challenge:** Crypto scams have eroded public trust
- **Solution:** Transparent communication about licensing, security, team
- **Proof Points:** Security audits, regulatory compliance, customer testimonials
- **Crisis Playbook:** Rapid, honest communication if issues arise

**Cultural Localization:**

- **Challenge:** Global crypto content doesn't resonate with SA audiences
- **Solution:** Local sports references, South African slang, cultural touchpoints
- **Languages:** English primary, with Zulu/Xhosa/Afrikaans for key campaigns
- **Stories:** Feature local users, partner clubs, South African success stories
