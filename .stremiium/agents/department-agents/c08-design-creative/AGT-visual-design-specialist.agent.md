# SECTION 4: KEY FAQS & KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY

## 4.1 Visual Design FAQs

**Q1: How do you create visual hierarchy?**
A: Techniques: (1) **Size:** Larger elements attract attention first. (2) **Color:** Contrast draws focus. (3) **Position:** Top and left get attention first. (4) **Typography:** Weight, style create order. (5) **Space:** Isolation emphasizes importance. (6) **Contrast:** Difference attracts eye. Combine techniques: Most important = largest, highest contrast, best position, most space.

**Q2: How do you choose a color palette?**
A: Process: (1) **Brand alignment:** Colors reflect brand personality. (2) **Purpose:** Consider emotional response needed. (3) **Contrast:** Ensure readability and accessibility. (4) **Harmony:** Colors work together. (5) **Application:** Test in context. (6) **Flexibility:** Enough range for various uses. Tools: Adobe Color, Coolors, Color Hunt.

**Q3: What's the difference between good and great typography?**
A: **Good:** Readable, appropriate, consistent. **Great:** Readable + expressive + harmonious + distinctive. Principles: (1) **Hierarchy:** Clear type scale. (2) **Pairing:** Complementary fonts. (3) **Spacing:** Proper kerning, tracking, leading. (4) **Context:** Right type for medium. (5) **Details:** Attention to every letterform.

**Q4: How do you maintain consistency across diverse assets?**
A: System: (1) **Style guide:** Documented standards. (2) **Templates:** Pre-built foundations. (3) **Components:** Reusable elements. (4) **Token system:** Colors, type as variables. (5) **Review process:** Quality checks. (6) **Library:** Central asset repository. Consistency comes from systems, not memory.

## 4.2 Illustration FAQs

**Q5: When should you use illustration vs. photography?**
A: **Illustration:** Abstract concepts, whimsy, flexibility, consistency, differentiation, complex explanations, fantasy/scenarios. **Photography:** Realism, authenticity, people/faces, products, trust-building, specific places. Hybrid: Illustrated elements on photos, photo-based illustration.

**Q6: How do you develop a distinctive illustration style?**
A: Process: (1) **Brand alignment:** Style reflects brand personality. (2) **Audience:** What resonates with them? (3) **Differentiation:** Not generic, ownable. (4) **Scalability:** Works at all sizes. (5) **Consistency:** Same style across all illustrations. (6) **Flexibility:** Adaptable to various subjects. (7) **Test:** Audience response validation.
