# SECTION 4: COMPREHENSIVE KNOWLEDGE BASE

## 4.1 Domain Expertise (50+ FAQs)

### Getting Started (10 FAQs)

**Q: What is a fan token exactly?**
A: A fan token is a blockchain-based digital asset that represents fan engagement and loyalty for a specific sports property. It enables fans to participate in governance decisions, earn rewards based on team performance, and engage in interactive experiences. Examples: Fans own voting rights on jersey designs, exclusive merchandise, and team decisions.

**Q: How do fan tokens differ from traditional sports merchandise?**
A: Fan tokens are digital (not physical), tradeable on secondary markets, provide utility through engagement mechanics, enable governance participation, and can appreciate in value. They create ongoing engagement rather than one-time purchases.

**Q: What sports properties can have fan tokens?**
A: Individual teams, leagues, esports organizations, and individual players can all have fan tokens. Current: Football (US/EU), Soccer, Basketball, Baseball, Cricket, esports. Potential: All professional and some amateur sports.

**Q: How are token mechanics determined?**
A: Mechanics are designed based on:

- Sports property's goals (fan engagement, revenue, loyalty)
- Fan community size and engagement level
- Available sports data streams
- Regulatory environment
- Partnership preferences

**Q: What drives fan token value?**
A: Fan token value is driven by:

- Utility (governance rights, rewards, exclusive access)
- Community size and engagement
- Team performance
- Scarcity (total supply)
- Secondary market demand
- Emotional attachment to sports property

**Q: How are fan rewards calculated?**
A: Rewards are calculated using:

- Real-time game performance
- Player statistics
- Team outcomes
- Fan prediction accuracy (if applicable)
- Engagement level (based on platform activity)
- Tiered reward systems (bronze/silver/gold)

**Q: What compliance requirements apply?**
A: Varies by jurisdiction but generally:

- Securities regulations (in some jurisdictions)
- Money transmission requirements
- Consumer protection laws
- Gambling/betting restrictions
- Tax implications
- KYC/AML requirements

**Q: How do fans purchase tokens?**
A: Multiple onramps:

- Fiat/crypto exchanges (regulated)
- In-platform purchasing with credit card
- Rewards earned through engagement
- Secondary peer-to-peer trading

**Q: What prevents token manipulation?**
A: Multiple safeguards:

- Market monitoring algorithms
- Suspicious trading pattern detection
- Volume limits during launch
- Insider trading prevention
- Regular smart contract audits
- Whitelisting of approved exchanges

**Q: How often should engagement mechanics be updated?**
A: Best practice:

- Weekly mechanic reviews (during season)
- Monthly engagement analysis
- Seasonal adjustments
- Post-season evaluation
- Annual comprehensive redesign based on learnings
