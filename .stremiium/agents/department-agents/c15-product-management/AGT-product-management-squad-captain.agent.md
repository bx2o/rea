## 5.1 Product Development SOPs

**Quarterly Strategic Planning:**

### SOP 2: Feature Development Lifecycle

**Phase 1: Problem Definition (Week 1)**

- Problem statement and user story
- Success criteria definition
- Stakeholder alignment
- Scope and constraints

**Phase 2: Solution Design (Weeks 2-3)**

- User research and validation
- Prototype and wireframe development
- Technical feasibility assessment
- Design review and approval

**Phase 3: Development (Weeks 4-7)**

- Sprint planning and execution
- Daily standups and progress tracking
- Testing and quality assurance
- Stakeholder demos

**Phase 4: Launch (Week 8)**

- Go-to-market preparation
- Launch checklist execution
- Monitoring and support
- Post-launch analysis

### SOP 3: Product Experimentation

**Hypothesis Framework:**

- **If we** [change], **then** [metric] will [impact] **because** [reasoning]
- Define success and failure criteria upfront
- Set experiment duration and sample size

**Experiment Types:**

- **A/B Tests:** Feature variations, UI changes
- **Multivariate:** Multiple variable testing
- **User Research:** Interviews, surveys, usability tests
- **Beta Programs:** Limited releases for feedback

**Analysis & Decision:**

- Statistical significance check
- User feedback synthesis
- Business impact assessment
- Rollout or rollback decision

## 5.2 Product Governance

**Prioritization Framework (RICE):**

- **Reach:** How many users will this impact?
- **Impact:** How much will this affect the metric?
- **Confidence:** How sure are we about the estimates?
- **Effort:** How much work is required?

**Roadmap Approval:**

- Monthly roadmap review meeting
- Stakeholder input and feedback
- Executive approval for major initiatives
- Communication and change management
