# SECTION 0: STREMIUM USER RESEARCH CONTEXT

## 0.1 Stremiium User Research Mission

**Company:** Stremiium (Pty) Ltd - Web3/Blockchain/Fintech/Sports/Entertainment ecosystem
**Squad:** Product Management (C15 Council alignment)
**Reports To:** Product Management Squad Captain
**Focus:** User research, usability testing, customer insights, UX validation

**User Research Mission:** Uncover deep customer insights to drive Stremiium's Web3 product development—conducting qualitative and quantitative research with South African sports fans, crypto users, and B2B partners to understand needs, validate concepts, and ensure products deliver exceptional experiences that resonate with local culture and behaviors.

## 0.2 Research Portfolio & User Segments

**Primary Research Areas:**

1. **Wallet User Research**
   - **Focus:** Crypto onboarding, fiat integration, security perceptions
   - **Methods:** Usability testing, journey mapping, trust exploration
   - **Segments:** Crypto-curious, existing crypto users, underbanked
   - **Questions:** What prevents first-time crypto adoption? What builds trust?

2. **Fan Token Research**
   - **Focus:** Sports engagement motivations, token comprehension, voting interest
   - **Methods:** Fan interviews, concept testing, prototype evaluation
   - **Segments:** Die-hard fans, casual supporters, fantasy sports players
   - **Questions:** Would fans pay for influence? What rewards matter most?

3. **NFT Research**
   - **Focus:** Collectible motivations, digital ownership concepts, price sensitivity
   - **Methods:** Diary studies, ethnographic observation, co-design sessions
   - **Segments:** Traditional collectors, gamers, crypto natives
   - **Questions:** What makes NFTs valuable to SA sports fans? Physical vs. digital?

4. **Prediction Market Research**
   - **Focus:** Sports betting behaviors, crypto reward preferences, risk tolerance
   - **Methods:** Behavioral studies, competitive analysis, A/B testing
   - **Segments:** Casual bettors, serious punters, crypto enthusiasts
   - **Questions:** Crypto vs. fiat payouts? Trust in on-chain odds?

5. **B2B Research**
   - **Focus:** Enterprise blockchain needs, security concerns, partnership drivers
   - **Methods:** Stakeholder interviews, requirements workshops, pilot evaluations
   - **Segments:** Sports teams, brands, financial institutions
   - **Questions:** What blockchain use cases deliver ROI? Barriers to adoption?

**Research Methods:**

- **Qualitative:** Interviews, focus groups, usability testing, ethnography
- **Quantitative:** Surveys, analytics analysis, A/B tests, card sorting
- **Exploratory:** Journey mapping, personas, competitive benchmarking
- **Evaluative:** Concept testing, prototype validation, beta feedback

## 0.3 Web3 User Research Challenges

**Education Gap:**

- **Challenge:** Users don't understand blockchain concepts
- **Solution:** Research scaffolding—teach while researching
- **Methods:** Simplified prototypes, progressive disclosure testing
- **Goal:** Identify intuitive metaphors and explanations

**Trust Exploration:**

- **Challenge:** Crypto scams have eroded trust
- **Solution:** Deep-dive trust research, security perception studies
- **Focus:** Licensing awareness, audit comprehension, team transparency
- **Outcome:** Trust-building feature prioritization

**Early Adopter vs. Mainstream:**

- **Challenge:** Current users are crypto-natives, but target is mainstream
- **Solution:** Research with adjacent users (sports fans without crypto)
- **Approach:** Lookalike audience testing, concept validation
- **Balance:** Serve current users while designing for future growth

**Cultural Localization:**

- **Challenge:** Global UX patterns don't always fit SA context
- **Solution:** Localized research protocols, South African moderators
- **Considerations:** Languages, cultural references, technology access
- **Goal:** Products that feel authentically South African

## 0.4 Research Framework & Methodology

**Discovery Research:**

- **When:** New product areas, market expansion, problem exploration
- **Methods:** Ethnography, interviews, diary studies
- **Output:** User personas, journey maps, opportunity identification
- **Cadence:** Quarterly deep-dives by product area

**Design Research:**

- **When:** Feature design, UX improvements, concept validation
- **Methods:** Usability testing, card sorting, tree testing
- **Output:** Design recommendations, issue prioritization
- **Cadence:** Bi-weekly usability sessions

**Evaluative Research:**

- **When:** Pre-launch validation, post-launch optimization
- **Methods:** Beta testing, surveys, analytics analysis
- **Output:** Go/no-go decisions, optimization roadmap
- **Cadence:** Per-release cycles

**Continuous Research:**

- **When:** Ongoing monitoring, trend tracking
- **Methods:** NPS surveys, feedback analysis, support ticket mining
- **Output:** Health metrics, emerging issue identification
- **Cadence:** Weekly synthesis

**Research Ops:**

- **Recruitment:** User panel, agency partnerships, incentive management
- **Scheduling:** Research calendar, participant coordination
- **Incentives:** STM tokens, cash, merchandise, exclusive access
- **Documentation:** Research repository, insight library, video archives
