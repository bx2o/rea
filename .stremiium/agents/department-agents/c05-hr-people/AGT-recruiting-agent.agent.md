# SECTION 5: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

## SOP 1: Intake & Job Definition

**Duration:** 1-2 hours | **Trigger:** New requisition opened | **Owner:** Recruiter + Hiring Manager

- Understand business need and role purpose
- Define key responsibilities and deliverables
- Identify must-have vs. nice-to-have qualifications
- Determine interview team and process
- Set timeline and expectations
- Document in ATS and create job posting
- Review compensation band with HR
- Launch sourcing strategy

## SOP 2: Sourcing & Pipeline Development

**Duration:** Ongoing | **Frequency:** Daily/Weekly | **Owner:** Recruiter + Sourcer

- Post to job boards (LinkedIn, Indeed, niche sites)
- Search internal ATS for previous candidates
- LinkedIn Recruiter outreach and boolean searches
- Employee referral program activation
- Talent community and pipeline nurturing
- Networking events and meetups
- University recruiting (if applicable)
- Diversity sourcing partnerships
- Track pipeline metrics by source

## SOP 3: Screening & Interview Process

**Duration:** 2-6 weeks per candidate | **Owner:** Recruiter + Interview Team

- **Phone Screen (30 min):** Basic fit, motivation, logistics
- **Technical/Skills Assessment:** Work sample, case study, or test
- **Hiring Manager Interview (45-60 min):** Role-specific depth
- **Team Interviews (2-3 sessions):** Collaboration, culture fit
- **Final/Executive Interview (30 min):** Values alignment, strategic fit
- **Reference Checks:** 2-3 professional references
- **Background Check:** Per company policy
- **Decision Meeting:** Interview team debrief and decision

## SOP 4: Offer & Negotiation

**Duration:** 3-7 days | **Owner:** Recruiter + Hiring Manager

- Prepare offer package (base, equity, benefits, start date)
- Present offer with enthusiasm and context
- Handle questions and concerns
- Negotiate if needed within approved parameters
- Escalate if outside parameters
- Get verbal acceptance
- Send written offer letter
- Set start date and begin onboarding prep

## SOP 5: Candidate Experience Management

**Duration:** Throughout process | **Owner:** Recruiter

- Acknowledge applications within 24-48 hours
- Provide timeline expectations upfront
- Communicate status weekly minimum
- Give specific feedback to finalists (if policy allows)
- Close candidates professionally (rejected applicants)
- Survey candidate experience (post-process)
- Analyze feedback and improve process
- Maintain talent community for future roles
