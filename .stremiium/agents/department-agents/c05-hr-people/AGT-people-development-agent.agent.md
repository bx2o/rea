# SECTION 5: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

## SOP 1: Development Needs Assessment

**Duration:** 3-4 weeks | **Frequency:** Annual + ongoing | **Owner:** People Development

- Review business strategy and skills requirements
- Analyze current capabilities vs. future needs
- Survey employees on development interests
- Interview managers on team development needs
- Assess leadership pipeline gaps
- Prioritize critical skill development areas
- Create assessment report with recommendations

## SOP 2: Leadership Development Program Design

**Duration:** 6-8 weeks | **Frequency:** Annual cycle | **Owner:** People Development

- Define target audience (high-potentials, new managers, executives)
- Identify competency framework for the level
- Design blended learning approach (workshops, projects, coaching)
- Select internal and external faculty
- Create program curriculum and materials
- Establish cohort selection criteria and process
- Set program metrics and success criteria
- Launch with executive sponsorship

## SOP 3: Individual Development Planning

**Duration:** 2-3 hours per employee | **Frequency:** Annual + quarterly check-ins | **Owner:** Manager + Employee

- Employee completes self-assessment of strengths/growth areas
- Manager provides feedback on performance and potential
- Discuss career aspirations and interests
- Identify 2-3 development priorities for the year
- Select development activities (training, stretch assignments, mentoring)
- Document plan with timeline and success metrics
- Review progress quarterly, adjust as needed
- Celebrate accomplishments and growth

## SOP 4: New Manager Onboarding

**Duration:** 90 days | **Trigger:** New manager assignment | **Owner:** People Development + Manager

- Pre-start: Welcome package, role expectations
- Week 1: HR fundamentals (policies, systems, compliance)
- Weeks 2-4: Management essentials (1:1s, feedback, goal-setting)
- Months 2-3: Advanced skills (coaching, difficult conversations, delegation)
- Assign experienced manager mentor
- Provide manager toolkit and resources
- Check-ins at 30, 60, 90 days
- Measure new manager confidence and effectiveness

## SOP 5: Training Program Evaluation

**Duration:** 2-4 weeks post-program | **Frequency:** Per program | **Owner:** People Development

- **Level 1 - Reaction:** Participant satisfaction surveys
- **Level 2 - Learning:** Knowledge/skill assessments pre/post
- **Level 3 - Behavior:** Manager observations of application (30-90 days post)
- **Level 4 - Results:** Business impact metrics tied to program goals
- Analyze data and identify improvement opportunities
- Report results to stakeholders and leadership
- Iterate program design based on findings
