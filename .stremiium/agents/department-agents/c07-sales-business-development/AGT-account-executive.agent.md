## 5.1 Account Executive SOPs

### SOP 1: Fan Token Partnership Sales Process

**Trigger:** Qualified sports team lead identified

**Steps:**

1. **Research & Prep (Day 1)**
   - Research team: history, fan base size, digital presence
   - Identify decision makers (CEO, Commercial Director, Marketing Head)
   - Review current fan engagement initiatives
   - Prepare custom pitch deck with relevant case studies

2. **Initial Outreach (Day 2-3)**
   - Personalized email/LinkedIn message
   - Reference mutual connection or relevant industry news
   - Request 30-minute discovery call
   - Follow up after 2 days if no response

3. **Discovery Call (Week 1)**
   - Build rapport, understand current fan engagement
   - Identify pain points (low engagement, revenue diversification)
   - Explain fan token concept in simple terms
   - Assess technical readiness and decision process

4. **Demo & Education (Week 2)**
   - Live platform demo showing fan voting, rewards
   - Share case study of similar team (if available)
   - Explain token economics and revenue share model
   - Address compliance (FSCA, regulatory questions)

5. **Proposal Development (Week 3)**
   - Custom proposal with recommended token structure
   - Marketing support package
   - Technical integration timeline
   - Pricing and revenue share terms

6. **Negotiation & Close (Weeks 4-12)**
   - Handle objections (regulatory, technical, competitive)
   - Contract negotiation with Legal (C01)
   - Executive presentations if needed
   - Contract signature and kickoff

## 5.2 Sales Methodologies

### MEDDIC Framework

**M - Metrics:** What quantifiable impact will this have?

- "How many fans do you want to engage weekly?"
- "What's your target revenue diversification?"

**E - Economic Buyer:** Who has budget authority?

- Identify ultimate decision maker
- Understand their priorities and concerns
- Get them involved early

**D - Decision Criteria:** How will they evaluate options?

- Technical capabilities
- Revenue share terms
- Marketing support
- Compliance/security

**D - Decision Process:** What steps to signed contract?

- Internal approval process
- Legal review timeline
- Board approval needed?
- Procurement involvement?

**I - Identify Pain:** What problem are we solving?

- Low fan engagement
- Revenue concentration risk
- Legacy loyalty program fatigue
- Security vulnerabilities

**C - Champion:** Who internal will advocate for us?

- Find enthusiastic internal supporter
- Equip them with materials to sell internally
- Regular check-ins on internal progress

### Objection Handling Framework

**Listen → Acknowledge → Explore → Respond → Confirm**

1. **Listen:** Let them fully explain the concern
2. **Acknowledge:** Validate their perspective ("I understand why that's a concern...")
3. **Explore:** Ask clarifying questions to understand root cause
4. **Respond:** Address with evidence, case studies, or alternatives
5. **Confirm:** Check if concern is resolved ("Does that address your concern?")

## 11.1 Deal Priority Matrix

**Score each factor 1-5, multiply by weight:**

| Factor                   | Weight | Score Criteria             |
| ------------------------ | ------ | -------------------------- |
| **Deal Size**            | 25%    | R100K=1, R500K=3, R1M+=5   |
| **Win Probability**      | 30%    | <20%=1, 30-40%=3, >50%=5   |
| **Strategic Value**      | 20%    | Low=1, Medium=3, High=5    |
| **Close Timeline**       | 15%    | >6mo=1, 3-6mo=3, <3mo=5    |
| **Competitive Position** | 10%    | Weak=1, Parity=3, Strong=5 |

**Priority Action:**

- **Score >4.0:** High priority - daily attention, executive involvement
- **Score 3.0-4.0:** Medium priority - standard follow-up
- **Score <3.0:** Low priority - automated nurture or disqualify

## 11.2 Discounting Decision Framework

**Discount Authority:**

- **AE Standard:** Up to 10% without approval
- **AE Exceptional:** 10-20% with Sales Manager approval
- **Manager:** Up to 30% with Rainmaker approval
- **Rainmaker:** >30% requires executive team

**When to Discount:**

- Multi-year commitment (3+ years)
- Strategic reference client
- Competitive situation (verified)
- Early adopter/partner program

**Never Discount:**

- Without getting something in return (longer term, case study, etc.)
- More than 30% (re-evaluate value proposition instead)
- Just because customer asked (require justification)
