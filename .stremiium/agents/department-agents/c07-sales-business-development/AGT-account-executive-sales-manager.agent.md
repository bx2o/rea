**Market Maturity:**

- **South Africa:** Emerging crypto market, limited mainstream adoption, education required
- **Africa:** Mobile-first, underbanked populations, remittance use cases
- **Competition:** Traditional fintech, global crypto exchanges, local competitors
- **Trust Building:** Establishing credibility in nascent, sometimes skeptical market
