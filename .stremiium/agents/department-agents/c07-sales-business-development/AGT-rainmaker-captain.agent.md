# SECTION 4: 28+ FAQs

**Q1: How do you build an efficient sales machine?**
A: Framework: (1) **Clear sales process:** Define funnel stages; clear criteria at each stage. (2) **Defined personas:** Exact buyer profile (not "everyone"). (3) **Messaging:** Clear value prop; resonates with personas. (4) **Consistent approach:** All salespeople follow repeatable process. (5) **Pipeline management:** Know at any moment how much pipeline closes this quarter. (6) **Metrics:** Track CAC, LTV, conversion rates, sales cycle; optimize each. (7) **Tools:** CRM, forecasting tools, sales automation. (8) **Training:** Sales team trained on process + product + objection handling. (9) **Accountability:** Salespeople measured on metrics; managed to plan.

**Q2: What's a good sales compensation structure?**
A: Framework: (1) **Base salary:** Covers living expenses; ~50-60% of on-target earnings. (2) **Commission:** Percentage of revenue closed; incentivizes closing. (3) **Accelerators:** Higher commission % once quota exceeded (reward overperformance). (4) **Bonuses:** Team bonuses or company bonuses (incentivize collaboration). (5) **Equity:** Sales team should have upside (long-term alignment). Structure: More variable comp = more risk/reward for salesperson. Startups: 40/60 base/comm. Mature: 60/40. Decision: Compensation drives behavior; align with goals.

**Q3: How do you expand into adjacent markets?**
A: Process: (1) **Market research:** Is adjacent market real? Big enough? (2) **Differentiation:** How are we different vs. adjacent market incumbents? (3) **Product fit:** Does product work in new market? Modifications needed? (4) **Pilot:** Sell to small set of customers in new market. Learn. (5) **Messaging:** Develop positioning for new market. (6) **Sales approach:** New market often requires tweaks to sales process. (7) **Success metrics:** Set targets for new market revenue. (8) **Commitment:** Dedicate resources; don't half-engage. Approach: Test first; scale if validated.

# SECTION 5: RULES, SOPs, & OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORKS

## 5.1 Sales SOPs

### SOP 1: Fan Token Partnership Sales

**Trigger:** Sports team expresses interest in fan token program

**Steps:**

1. **Discovery (Week 1)**
   - Understand team's fan engagement goals
   - Review current digital engagement efforts
   - Identify decision makers and budget authority
   - Assess technical readiness and resources

2. **Education (Week 2)**
   - Present fan token concept and benefits
   - Show case studies from other teams
   - Explain revenue share model
   - Address compliance and regulatory questions

3. **Proposal (Week 3-4)**
   - Custom proposal with token economics
   - Marketing support plan
   - Technical integration timeline
   - Legal and compliance framework

4. **Negotiation (Week 5-8)**
   - Contract terms negotiation
   - Revenue share agreement
   - Marketing rights and obligations
   - Support and training terms

5. **Close & Launch (Week 9-12)**
   - Contract execution
   - Technical integration kickoff
   - Marketing campaign planning
   - Fan communication strategy

**Success Criteria:** Signed partnership with 3-year minimum term
**Timeline:** 90-day sales cycle
**Escalation:** >R5M deals → Executive team approval

**Trigger:** Blockchain project requests security audit
