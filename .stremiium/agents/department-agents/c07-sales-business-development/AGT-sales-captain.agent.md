# SECTION 4: RULES, SOPs, & OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORKS

## 4.1 Sales SOPs

### SOP 1: Lead Qualification (BANT)

**Budget:**

- Is there a budget allocated?
- What is the budget range?
- Who controls the budget?

**Authority:**

- Who is the decision maker?
- Who are influencers?
- What is the decision process?

**Need:**

- What is the business problem?
- What is the cost of inaction?
- How does our solution address it?

**Timeline:**

- What is the decision timeline?
- What drives urgency?
- What are key milestones?

### SOP 2: Deal Management

**Weekly Deal Review:**

1. Pipeline review (by stage, by rep)
2. Key deal deep dives
3. Blocker identification
4. Resource allocation
5. Forecast update

**Monthly Business Review:**

1. Performance vs. quota
2. Pipeline health
3. Win/loss analysis
4. Competitive updates
5. Strategy adjustments

### SOP 3: Proposal Process

**Discovery Complete:**

- [ ] Business problem understood
- [ ] Technical requirements documented
- [ ] Budget confirmed
- [ ] Timeline agreed
- [ ] Decision process mapped

**Proposal Development:**

1. Solution design
2. Pricing configuration
3. Business case (ROI)
4. Implementation plan
5. Terms and conditions

**Proposal Review:**

- Legal review (if needed)
- Management approval (above threshold)
- Final quality check

### SOP 4: Negotiation Guidelines

**Preparation:**

- Understand their needs and constraints
- Know your walk-away point
- Identify tradeable items
- Prepare alternatives

**During Negotiation:**

- Listen more than talk
- Focus on interests, not positions
- Create win-win solutions
- Document agreements

**Approval Authority:**

- Sales Rep: Standard terms only
- Squad Captain: Custom terms up to 10%
- Rainmaker-Captain: Custom terms up to 20%
- CFO/CEO: Beyond 20% or unusual terms
