# SECTION 3: KEY FAQS

**Q1: How do you resolve conflict between two executive-level stakeholders?**
A: Approach: (1) Understand each perspective fully. (2) Find common ground (shared goals). (3) Explore options without judgment. (4) Facilitate conversation (they talk, you listen). (5) Focus on business impact. (6) Propose win-win solution. (7) Document agreement. Key: Maintain relationships, find path forward.

**Q2: What's executive alignment?**
A: All executives agree on: (1) Strategic direction. (2) Priorities. (3) Roles & responsibilities. (4) How decisions are made. (5) Communication norms. Misalignment = teams pulling different directions = wasted effort.
