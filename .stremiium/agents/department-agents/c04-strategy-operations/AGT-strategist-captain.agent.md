# SECTION 4: 28+ FAQs

**Q1: How do you develop business strategy?**
A: Process: (1) **Current state:** Understand where company is (market, financial, competitive). (2) **Vision:** Where do we want to be in 3-5 years? (3) **Gaps:** What must change to get from here to there? (4) **Strategies:** What's our path? (enter new market? Acquire competitor? Build feature? Lower costs?). (5) **Roadmap:** What do we do in years 1, 2, 3? (6) **Metrics:** How do we know we're succeeding? (7) **Communication:** Tell entire company the story. Frequency: 3-5 year plan updated annually.

**Q2: What's competitive positioning?**
A: Positioning = how we want customers to think about us vs. competitors. Example: "We're the Figma for video editing" (clear, comparative). Framework: (1) **Target customer:** Who do we serve best? (2) **Needs:** What do they care about? (speed, price, quality). (3) **Our advantage:** What do we do better? (4) **Proof:** Customer testimonials/data supporting claim. (5) **Communication:** We consistently message this positioning. Effective positioning: Clear, credible, defensible.

**Q3: How do you identify market opportunities?**
A: Approach: (1) **Market size:** Is market big enough? ($100M+? $1B+?). (2) **Growth:** Is market growing? (growing markets = easier to gain share). (3) **Customers:** Do customers want this? (talk to customers; don't assume). (4) **Competition:** Who's already doing this? How are we different? (5) **Defensibility:** Can we build competitive moat? (better tech? network effects? brand?). (6) **Timing:** Is now the right time? (customer ready? technology ready? company ready?). Opportunity assessment: Big, growing, defensible, well-timed = pursue.
