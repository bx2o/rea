# SECTION 4: KEY FAQS & KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY

## 4.1 Thirty+ Frequently Asked Questions

### Category 1: TALENT ACQUISITION (6 FAQs)

**Q1: How do you build an effective recruiting function?**
A: Effective recruiting: (1) **Strategy:** Clear employer value proposition. (2) **Sourcing:** Multi-channel pipeline. (3) **Process:** Efficient, candidate-friendly. (4) **Selection:** Valid, predictive assessments. (5) **Closing:** Compelling offers, fast process. (6) **Analytics:** Data-driven optimization. (7) **Experience:** Every candidate treated well. Metrics: Time to fill, quality of hire, candidate NPS.

**Q2: What makes a good interview process?**
A: Good interview process: (1) **Structured:** Same questions for all candidates. (2) **Behavioral:** Past behavior predicts future. (3) **Diverse Panel:** Multiple perspectives. (4) **Skills-based:** Test actual capabilities. (5) **Efficient:** Respect candidate time. (6) **Decision Framework:** Clear criteria. (7) **Feedback:** Timely communication. Avoid: Unconscious bias, "culture fit" ambiguity, decision fatigue.

**Q3: How do you assess culture add vs. culture fit?**
A: **Culture Fit (avoid):** Similarity to existing team, leads to homogeneity. **Culture Add (prefer):** Diversity of thought, background, skills that strengthen culture. Assess: Values alignment + unique perspective. Questions: "What would you add to our culture that's missing?" "Tell us about a time you challenged the status quo."

**Q4: What is employer branding?**
A: Employer brand is organization's reputation as employer: (1) **Value Proposition:** Why work here? (2) **Differentiation:** Unique from competitors. (3) **Authenticity:** True to reality. (4) **Visibility:** Active presence. Channels: Careers site, social media, Glassdoor, events, employee advocacy. Measure: Application volume, quality, acceptance rates.

**Q5: How do you reduce time to fill?**
A: Speed strategies: (1) **Pipeline:** Proactive sourcing, not reactive. (2) **Process:** Remove unnecessary steps. (3) **Scheduling:** Priority on interview calendars. (4) **Decision:** Same-day decisions when possible. (5) **Offer:** Competitive, ready to go. (6) **Communication:** Keep candidates warm. Balance: Speed vs. thoroughness—don't sacrifice quality.

**Q6: What makes effective onboarding?**
A: Effective onboarding: (1) **Pre-boarding:** Before day one engagement. (2) **Structure:** 30-60-90 day plan. (3) **Connection:** Relationships built. (4) **Clarity:** Role, expectations, tools. (5) **Culture:** Immersion in values. (6) **Feedback:** Check-ins and adjustment. (7) **Productivity:** Quick time to contribution. Duration: 6-12 months for full integration.

### Category 2: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT (6 FAQs)

**Q7: What is effective performance management?**
A: Effective performance management: (1) **Continuous:** Ongoing, not annual. (2) **Clear:** Known expectations. (3) **Developmental:** Growth focus, not judgment. (4) **Two-way:** Dialogue, not monologue. (5) **Fair:** Consistent, unbiased. (6) **Actionable:** Leads to action. Evolution: Annual reviews → Continuous feedback + periodic calibration.

**Q8: How do you give effective feedback?**
A: Feedback best practices: (1) **Timely:** Soon after observation. (2) **Specific:** Observable behavior. (3) **Impact:** Effect on others/results. (4) **Future:** What to continue/change. (5) **Dialogue:** Listen to their perspective. (6) **Support:** Offer help. Frameworks: SBI (Situation-Behavior-Impact), STAR (Situation-Task-Action-Result).

**Q9: What is the difference between performance and potential?**
A: **Performance:** Current results in current role. **Potential:** Ability to take on greater scope/complexity. 9-box model: Low/high performance × low/high potential. Usage: Performance = rewards, potential = development/ succession. Calibration: Separate conversations, don't assume high performance = high potential.

**Q10: How do you handle underperformance?**
A: Underperformance process: (1) **Clarify:** Ensure expectations clear. (2) **Understand:** Root cause (skill, will, hill). (3) **Support:** Resources, coaching, training. (4) **Document:** Record discussions. (5) **Timeline:** Reasonable improvement period. (6) **Decision:** Improve, move, or exit. (7) **Respect:** Dignity throughout. Early: Address quickly, don't let fester.

**Q11: What are effective 1:1s?**
A: Effective 1:1s: (1) **Regular:** Weekly or bi-weekly, protected time. (2) **Employee's Agenda:** They set topics. (3) **Holistic:** Work and wellbeing. (4) **Forward-looking:** Not just status updates. (5) **Action-oriented:** Clear next steps. (6) **Documented:** Notes for follow-up. (7) **Relationship-building:** Trust and rapport.

**Q12: How do you calibrate performance ratings?**
A: Calibration process: (1) **Pre-work:** Managers rate independently. (2) **Session:** Discuss across team/manager. (3) **Standards:** Apply consistently. (4) **Challenge:** Question outliers. (5) **Bias Check:** Watch for patterns. (6) **Documentation:** Rationale recorded. (7) **Communication:** Managers deliver. Goal: Fair, consistent, defensible ratings.

### Category 3: COMPENSATION & BENEFITS (6 FAQs)

**Q13: How do you design compensation strategy?**
A: Compensation strategy: (1) **Philosophy:** Lead, match, or lag market? (2) **Structure:** Base, bonus, equity mix. (3) **Levels:** Clear salary bands. (4) **Progression:** How to move through bands. (5) **Pay for Performance:** Differentiation approach. (6) **Equity:** Fairness across demographics. (7) **Communication:** Transparency level. Review: Annually, with market data.

**Q14: What is pay equity?**
A: Pay equity: Equal pay for substantially equal work regardless of gender, race, etc. Analysis: Statistical analysis controlling for legitimate factors (role, experience, performance). Action: Address unexplained gaps. Transparency: Pay ranges, criteria. Prevention: Structured compensation decisions, regular audits.

**Q15: How do you benchmark compensation?**
A: Benchmarking process: (1) **Data Sources:** Radford, Mercer, CompAnalyst, OptionImpact. (2) **Matches:** Accurate job matching. (3) **Cuts:** Geography, industry, size. (4) **Market Position:** Target percentile (50th, 75th). (5) **Currency:** Refresh regularly. (6) **Total Comp:** Include equity, benefits. (7) **Apply:** Adjust bands accordingly.

**Q16: What should be in a benefits package?**
A: Core benefits: (1) **Health:** Medical, dental, vision. (2) **Retirement:** 401(k), matching. (3) **Time Off:** PTO, holidays, sick leave. (4) **Parental:** Maternity, paternity, adoption. (5) **Insurance:** Life, disability. (6) **Perks:** Flexible work, learning, wellness. Differentiation: Tailor to workforce demographics and values.

**Q17: How do you communicate compensation?**
A: Communication principles: (1) **Transparency:** Explain how decisions made. (2) **Total Value:** Show full package value. (3) **Context:** Market position, company performance. (4) **Growth:** Path to increase. (5) **Early:** Offer stage clarity. (6) **Regular:** Annual total rewards statements. (7) **Accessibility:** Easy to understand.

**Q18: What is the role of equity compensation?**
A: Equity purpose: (1) **Alignment:** Employee and company success linked. (2) **Retention:** Vesting keeps people. (3) **Ownership:** Psychological ownership. (4) **Startup:** Essential when cash limited. Types: Stock options, RSUs, ESPP. Education: Help employees understand value, tax implications, vesting.

### Category 4: CULTURE & ENGAGEMENT (6 FAQs)

**Q19: How do you define and build culture?**
A: Culture building: (1) **Define:** Values and behaviors (real, not aspirational). (2) **Model:** Leaders demonstrate. (3) **Hire:** Select for values alignment. (4) **Reinforce:** Recognition and consequences. (5) **Ritual:** Repeated practices. (6) **Measure:** Engagement and culture surveys. (7) **Evolve:** Culture adapts as company grows.

**Q20: What is employee engagement?**
A: Engagement: Emotional commitment to organization and goals. Drivers: (1) **Purpose:** Meaningful work. (2) **Development:** Growth opportunities. (3) **Management:** Effective leadership. (4) **Recognition:** Appreciation. (5) **Wellbeing:** Work-life balance. (6) **Voice:** Input matters. Measurement: Engagement surveys (eQ12, Gallup). Action: Priority issues addressed.

**Q21: How do you measure and improve engagement?**
A: Engagement process: (1) **Survey:** Regular, validated instrument. (2) **Analyze:** Segment results. (3) **Share:** Transparent reporting. (4) **Action:** Team-level action planning. (5) **Follow-up:** Progress on commitments. (6) **Trend:** Track over time. Cadence: Annual comprehensive + quarterly pulse.

**Q22: What is psychological safety?**
A: Psychological safety: Belief that team is safe for interpersonal risk-taking (speaking up, admitting mistakes, asking questions). Importance: Foundation of high-performing teams. Building: (1) **Model:** Leader vulnerability. (2) **Invite:** Input and questions. (3) **Respond:** Productively to mistakes. (4) **Frame:** Work as learning. Measure: Team assessments, observation.

**Q23: How do you manage remote/hybrid culture?**
A: Remote culture: (1) **Intentional:** Design for remote-first. (2) **Connection:** Create social moments. (3) **Communication:** Async and documented. (4) **Equity:** Same experience regardless of location. (5) **Trust:** Focus on results, not hours. (6) **Boundaries:** Respect work-life separation. (7) **Inclusion:** Ensure remote voices heard.

**Q24: What are effective employee recognition programs?**
A: Recognition principles: (1) **Timely:** Close to the action. (2) **Specific:** What exactly was great. (3) **Values-based:** Tied to company values. (4) **Peer-to-peer:** Not just top-down. (5) **Variety:** Monetary and non-monetary. (6) **Public:** When appropriate. Programs: Kudos, spot bonuses, anniversaries, awards.

### Category 5: DEI & COMPLIANCE (6 FAQs)

**Q25: How do you build a DEI strategy?**
A: DEI strategy components: (1) **Commitment:** Executive sponsorship. (2) **Data:** Representation, hiring, promotion, pay. (3) **Recruiting:** Diverse sourcing, blind resume review. (4) **Culture:** Inclusive behaviors. (5) **Development:** Advancement opportunities. (6) **Employee Resource Groups:** Community support. (7) **Accountability:** Metrics and goals. (8) **Continuous:** Long-term commitment.

**Q26: What is inclusive leadership?**
A: Inclusive leadership behaviors: (1) **Self-awareness:** Know your biases. (2) **Curiosity:** Different perspectives. (3) **Courage:** Challenge status quo. (4) **Cultural Intelligence:** Adapt across differences. (5) **Collaboration:** Empower diverse teams. (6) **Commitment:** Prioritize inclusion. Training: Bias interruption, microaggressions, allyship.

**Q27: How do you ensure pay equity?**
A: Pay equity process: (1) **Audit:** Statistical analysis by gender, race, ethnicity. (2) **Adjust:** Address unexplained gaps. (3) **Prevent:** Structured decision-making. (4) **Transparency:** Pay bands, criteria. (5) **Monitor:** Regular analysis. (6) **Communication:** Share progress. Legal: Equal Pay Act, state laws.

**Q28: What HR compliance is essential?**
A: Key compliance areas: (1) **Hiring:** Non-discrimination, proper documentation. (2) **Pay:** Minimum wage, overtime (FLSA), equal pay. (3) **Leave:** FMLA, state leave laws. (4) **Benefits:** ACA, COBRA, ERISA. (5) **Safety:** OSHA. (6) **Privacy:** Data protection. (7) **Harassment:** Prevention and response. (8) **Documentation:** Records retention.

**Q29: How do you handle workplace investigations?**
A: Investigation process: (1) **Take seriously:** All reports. (2) **Prompt:** Start quickly. (3) **Neutral:** Objective investigator. (4) **Thorough:** Gather all evidence. (5) **Confidential:** Limited disclosure. (6) **No retaliation:** Protect reporter. (7) **Document:** Detailed records. (8) **Decide:** Based on evidence. (9) **Act:** Appropriate consequence. (10) **Follow-up:** Monitor for recurrence.

**Q30: What is an employee handbook?**
A: Employee handbook contents: (1) **Welcome:** Mission, values. (2) **Employment:** At-will, classifications. (3) **Conduct:** Expected behavior. (4) **Compensation:** Pay practices. (5) **Benefits:** Programs overview. (6) **Leave:** Types and procedures. (7) **Safety:** Workplace safety. (8) **Technology:** Acceptable use. (9) **Grievance:** Complaint procedures. (10) **Acknowledgment:** Receipt confirmation. Update: Regular legal review.

# SECTION 5: SOPs & STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

## SOP 1: Full-Cycle Recruiting

**Duration:** 30-60 days | **Frequency:** Per open req | **Owner:** Recruiter + Hiring Manager

1. **Intake:** Hiring manager kickoff, define requirements
2. **Sourcing:** Active sourcing, post job, referrals
3. **Screening:** Resume review, phone screens
4. **Interviewing:** Structured interviews, assessments
5. **Debrief:** Calibration meeting, decision
6. **Offer:** Compensation approval, extend offer
7. **Negotiation:** Address concerns, finalize terms
8. **Background:** Reference and background checks
9. **Close:** Signed offer, start date set
10. **Pre-boarding:** Welcome communications, paperwork

**Checkpoints:**

- Week 1: Sourcing plan confirmed
- Week 2: Pipeline of qualified candidates
- Week 3: Finalists identified
- Week 4: Offer extended

## SOP 2: Performance Review Cycle

**Duration:** 4-6 weeks | **Frequency:** Annual/Semi-annual | **Owner:** HR + Managers

1. **Launch:** Communication and timeline
2. **Self-Review:** Employee input on accomplishments
3. **Manager Review:** Manager assessment and rating
4. **Calibration:** Cross-manager calibration sessions
5. **Approval:** Leadership review and approval
6. **Delivery:** Manager shares feedback with employee
7. **Planning:** Goals and development plan for next period
8. **Documentation:** Records in HRIS
9. **Compensation:** Pay decisions aligned with ratings
10. **Follow-up:** Check-ins on goals and development

**Templates:**

- Self-assessment form
- Manager evaluation form
- Goal setting template
- Development plan template

## SOP 3: Employee Relations Issue

**Duration:** 1-7 days | **Frequency:** As needed | **Owner:** HR Business Partner

1. **Report:** Issue reported or identified
2. **Intake:** Document initial information
3. **Assess:** Severity and appropriate response
4. **Investigate:** Gather facts, interview parties
5. **Analyze:** Determine findings
6. **Decide:** Appropriate action
7. **Communicate:** Inform involved parties
8. **Document:** Maintain detailed records
9. **Follow-up:** Monitor situation
10. **Close:** Case resolution

**Severity Levels:**

- Low: Informal coaching
- Medium: Formal documentation
- High: Disciplinary action
- Critical: Legal/executive involvement
