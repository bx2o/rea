# SECTION 4: KEY FAQS & KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY

## 4.1 Thirty+ Frequently Asked Questions

### Category 1: SALES STRATEGY (6 FAQs)

**Q1: What is a go-to-market strategy?**
A: GTM strategy defines how to reach customers: (1) **Target:** Who is the customer (ICP)? (2) **Offering:** Product/service value prop. (3) **Channel:** How to reach them (sales-led, product-led, partner). (4) **Pricing:** Revenue model. (5) **Messaging:** Why buy. (6) **Metrics:** Success measures. Iteration: Test, measure, refine.

**Q2: How do you define an Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)?**
A: ICP identifies best-fit customers: Firmographics (size, industry, geography), Technographics (tech stack), Behavioral (use case, buying process), Outcomes (value achieved). Process: Analyze best current customers, find patterns, test hypothesis, refine. Usage: Focus resources, disqualify poor fits, tailor messaging.

**Q3: What sales methodology should we use?**
A: Popular methodologies: **MEDDPICC:** Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion, Competition. **Challenger:** Teach, tailor, take control. **SPIN:** Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff. **Sandler:** Pain, budget, decision. **Inbound:** Help, don't sell. Selection: Match to product, buyer, culture. Often: Hybrid approach.

**Q4: How do you design territories?**
A: Territory design: (1) **Data:** Account potential, historical performance. (2) **Balance:** Equal opportunity across territories. (3) **Coverage:** Geographic, vertical, named accounts. (4) **Specialization:** By segment, product, stage. (5) **Overlap:** Minimize conflicts. (6) **Flexibility:** Adjust quarterly. Tools: Territory optimization software, CRM data.

**Q5: What is account-based selling?**
A: Account-based approach: (1) **Targeting:** Focus on high-value accounts. (2) **Research:** Deep account intelligence. (3) **Personalization:** Tailored outreach. (4) **Multi-threading:** Multiple stakeholders. (5) **Orchestration:** Coordinated touchpoints. (6) **Measurement:** Account-level metrics. Use when: Enterprise deals, concentrated market, complex sales.

**Q6: How do you build a sales playbook?**
A: Sales playbook components: (1) **Company:** Story, value prop. (2) **Product:** Features, benefits, differentiation. (3) **Buyer:** Personas, pain points. (4) **Process:** Stages, activities, exit criteria. (5) **Competition:** Battlecards. (6) **Objections:** Responses. (7) **Tools:** Templates, scripts. (8) **Resources:** Content, tools. Format: Wiki, deck, interactive. Update: Regularly based on wins/losses.

### Category 2: PIPELINE & FORECASTING (6 FAQs)

**Q7: What are the stages of a sales pipeline?**
A: Typical B2B stages: (1) **Prospecting:** Initial outreach. (2) **Qualification:** Fit and interest confirmed. (3) **Discovery:** Needs identified. (4) **Solution:** Proposal developed. (5) **Validation:** Stakeholder buy-in. (6) **Negotiation:** Terms discussed. (7) **Closed:** Won or lost. Customize: To your sales process. Key: Clear exit criteria per stage.

**Q8: How do you qualify opportunities?**
A: Qualification frameworks: **BANT:** Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline. **MEDDPICC:** More comprehensive. **GPCT:** Goals, Plans, Challenges, Timeline. Process: Early and ongoing qualification. Action: Disqualify poor fits fast. Red flags: No budget, no decision maker, no timeline, no pain.

**Q9: What makes a good forecast?**
A: Forecast quality: (1) **Data-driven:** Based on pipeline, not hope. (2) **Consistent:** Same methodology monthly. (3) **Granular:** By rep, product, segment. (4) **Probability-weighted:** Stage-based likelihood. (5) **Verified:** Manager inspection. (6) **Adjustable:** Scenario planning. Cadence: Weekly trends, monthly commits, quarterly planning.

**Q10: What is pipeline coverage?**
A: Pipeline coverage = Pipeline value / Quota. Target: 3-4x for qualified pipeline. Calculation: Different stages have different probabilities. Action: If coverage low, generate more pipeline. If high but conversion low, improve qualification or sales skills. Quality > quantity.

**Q11: How do you improve forecast accuracy?**
A: Accuracy improvement: (1) **Discipline:** Consistent process. (2) **Inspection:** Regular deal reviews. (3) **Data:** CRM hygiene. (4) **Methodology:** Stage-weighted or forecast categories. (5) **History:** Learn from past variance. (6) **Rigorous:** Hold reps accountable. Measure: Forecast vs. actual, by rep, by period.

**Q12: What are forecast categories?**
A: Salesforce categories: **Commit:** Will close (best case). **Best Case:** Might close if all goes well. **Pipeline:** Early stage. **Oversight:** At risk. **Closed:** Won or lost. Usage: Weighted forecast, rep accountability. Coaching: Discuss what moves deals between categories.

### Category 3: SALES OPERATIONS (6 FAQs)

**Q13: What is sales operations?**
A: Sales ops functions: (1) **Strategy:** Territory, compensation, planning. (2) **Enablement:** Training, content, tools. (3) **Technology:** CRM, sales stack. (4) **Analytics:** Reporting, forecasting. (5) **Process:** Optimization, efficiency. Value: Scale sales, improve productivity, enable data-driven decisions. Team: 1:10-20 ratio with sales.

**Q14: How do you design sales compensation?**
A: Compensation design: (1) **Pay Mix:** Base/variable split (60/40, 50/50 typical). (2) **Quota:** Realistic but challenging. (3) **Accelerator:** Higher rate above 100%. (4) **Components:** New logo, expansion, retention. (5) **Cliff:** Minimum threshold. (6) **Timing:** Monthly, quarterly. (7) **Simplicity:** Easy to understand. (8) **ROI:** CAC efficient.

**Q15: What sales technology do we need?**
A: Sales tech stack: **CRM:** Salesforce, HubSpot (core). **Engagement:** Outreach, SalesLoft (sequences). **Data:** ZoomInfo, LinkedIn (prospecting). **Enablement:** Highspot, Seismic (content). **Analytics:** Tableau, InsightSquared (reporting). **Conversational:** Gong, Chorus (coaching). Integration: Connected data flow.

**Q16: How do you improve CRM adoption?**
A: CRM adoption strategies: (1) **Value:** Show what's in it for them. (2) **Easy:** Minimize clicks, auto-capture. (3) **Required:** Pipeline, forecast, commissions depend on it. (4) **Mobile:** Access anywhere. (5) **Training:** Skill building. (6) **Leadership:** Managers use and inspect. (7) **Data:** Clean, accurate. Consequence: No CRM, no credit.

**Q17: What is sales enablement?**
A: Sales enablement: Equipping sales to sell effectively. Components: (1) **Content:** Case studies, decks, battlecards. (2) **Training:** Skills, product, process. (3) **Tools:** CRM, engagement, data. (4) **Coaching:** Manager 1:1s. (5) **Onboarding:** New rep success. Success: Shorter ramp, higher win rates, bigger deals.

**Q18: How do you reduce sales cycle length?**
A: Cycle reduction: (1) **Qualification:** Focus on buyers with urgency. (2) **Multi-threading:** Multiple stakeholders early. (3) **Value:** Quantify ROI. (4) **Process:** Know customer's buying process. (5) **Content:** Address objections proactively. (6) **Next Steps:** Always have scheduled. (7) **Champion:** Build internal advocate. (8) **Executive:** Engage when stalled.

### Category 4: TEAM DEVELOPMENT (6 FAQs)

**Q19: How do you hire great salespeople?**
A: Sales hiring: (1) **Profile:** Define ideal candidate. (2) **Sourcing:** Referrals, LinkedIn, networks. (3) **Assessment:** Skills testing, role plays. (4) **Interview:** Structured, behavioral. (5) **References:** Past performance. (6) **Culture:** Values fit. Red flags: Job hopping, can't explain wins, negative attitude. Time: Quality over speed.

**Q20: How do you onboard sales reps?**
A: Sales onboarding: (1) **Pre-boarding:** Before day one. (2) **Week 1:** Culture, tools, product. (3) **Month 1:** Process, pitch, shadow. (4) **Month 2-3:** Practice, early calls, feedback. (5) **Month 4-6:** Full quota, independence. (6) **Ongoing:** Continuous learning. Success: Shorter time to first deal, faster ramp to quota.

**Q21: What is effective sales coaching?**
A: Sales coaching: (1) **1:1s:** Regular, structured. (2) **Observed:** Listen to calls. (3) **Specific:** Focus on one skill. (4) **Data:** Use metrics. (5) **Practice:** Role play. (6) **Goals:** Clear action items. (7) **Follow-up:** Check progress. Different from: Managing (telling) vs. coaching (developing).

**Q22: How do you handle underperforming reps?**
A: Underperformance management: (1) **Diagnose:** Skill or will issue? (2) **Plan:** Performance improvement plan. (3) **Support:** Training, coaching, resources. (4) **Timeline:** Reasonable improvement period. (5) **Check-ins:** Regular review. (6) **Decision:** Improve, transfer, or exit. Early: Address quickly, don't wait for quarter end.

**Q23: How do you motivate a sales team?**
A: Motivation strategies: (1) **Recognition:** Celebrate wins. (2) **Competition:** Leaderboards, SPIFs. (3) **Career:** Growth paths. (4) **Autonomy:** Trust and freedom. (5) **Mastery:** Skill development. (6) **Purpose:** Connect to mission. (7) **Fun:** Culture and camaraderie. Individual: Know what drives each person.

**Q24: What is a sales cadence?**
A: Sales cadence: Sequence of touchpoints: (1) **Channels:** Email, phone, LinkedIn, video. (2) **Timing:** Spaced appropriately. (3) **Content:** Value-added messaging. (4) **Personalization:** Tailored to prospect. (5) **Persistence:** Multiple attempts. (6) **Exit:** Know when to stop. Tools: Outreach, SalesLoft automate. Test: A/B test messaging and timing.

### Category 5: REVENUE EXPANSION (6 FAQs)

**Q25: What is net revenue retention (NRR)?**
A: NRR = Starting ARR + Expansion - Contraction - Churn / Starting ARR. Healthy: >100% (land and expand). Great: >110%. Best: >120%. Drivers: Upsell (more of same), cross-sell (new products), price increases, retention. Focus: NRR more important than new logos for SaaS health.

**Q26: How do you drive account expansion?**
A: Expansion strategies: (1) **Usage:** Drive adoption of current features. (2) **Users:** Add seats/licenses. (3) **Features:** Upsell to higher tiers. (4) **Products:** Cross-sell new offerings. (5) **Services:** Add professional services. (6) **Timing:** Trigger-based outreach. Team: CSMs lead, AEs support, specialists for products.

**Q27: What is customer success and how does it relate to sales?**
A: Customer Success: Ensure customers achieve desired outcomes. Handoff: Sales to CS at close. Collaboration: CS identifies expansion, brings AE. CS owns: Adoption, retention, satisfaction. Sales owns: New, expansion revenue. Alignment: Shared NRR goals, coordinated account plans.

**Q28: How do you prevent churn?**
A: Churn prevention: (1) **Onboarding:** Strong start. (2) **Adoption:** Drive usage. (3) **Health Scoring:** Identify at-risk early. (4) **QBRs:** Quarterly business reviews. (5) **Advocacy:** Build champions. (6) **Expansion:** More products = stickier. (7) **Feedback:** Listen and act. Save plays: For at-risk accounts.

**Q29: What is pricing optimization?**
A: Pricing optimization: (1) **Value:** Price based on customer value. (2) **Segmentation:** Different prices for different segments. (3) **Packaging:** Good/better/best tiers. (4) **Trials:** Reduce friction. (5) **Discounting:** Controlled process. (6) **Annual:** Push for upfront. Test: A/B pricing, willingness-to-pay research.

**Q30: How do you build a partner/channel strategy?**
A: Channel strategy: (1) **Type:** Referral, reseller, integration, co-sell. (2) **Recruit:** Right partners with customer access. (3) **Enable:** Train and support. (4) **Comp:** Motivating economics. (5) **Manage:** Regular engagement. (6) **Measure:** Partner-sourced ARR. Caution: Channel can distract if not ready for direct sales success.

# SECTION 5: SOPs & STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

## SOP 1: Opportunity Management

**Duration:** Ongoing | **Frequency:** Per deal | **Owner:** Account Executive

1. **Qualify:** Use MEDDPICC to assess fit
2. **Discovery:** Understand pain, power, process
3. **Champion:** Build internal advocate

6. **Negotiate:** Terms and pricing
7. **Close:** Final signatures
8. **Handoff:** To customer success
9. **Post-mortem:** Win/loss analysis

**Checkpoints:**

- Discovery complete before proposal
- Economic buyer engaged before negotiation
- Technical validation before close

## SOP 2: Pipeline Review

**Duration:** 1-2 hours | **Frequency:** Weekly | **Owner:** Sales Manager

1. **Pre-work:** Reps update CRM
2. **Review:** Each opportunity in commit/best case
3. **Challenges:** What's blocking progress?
4. **Next Steps:** Specific actions scheduled
5. **Resources:** What help is needed?
6. **Risk:** What could go wrong?
7. **Forecast:** Commit/best case assessment
8. **Coaching:** Skill development
9. **Actions:** Clear follow-up

**Focus:**

- Next quarter deals
- Stalled opportunities
- Large deals
- At-risk forecast

## SOP 3: Forecast Call

**Duration:** 30-60 min | **Frequency:** Monthly/Quarterly | **Owner:** CRO

1. **Review:** Last period actuals vs. forecast
2. **Pipeline:** Current coverage analysis
3. **By Segment:** Trends by product/territory
4. **Large Deals:** Major opportunities status
5. **Risks:** What could miss
6. **Upside:** Best case scenarios
7. **Commit:** Rep and manager commits
8. **Actions:** Mitigation plans
9. **Communication:** Board/executive reporting

**Output:**

- Forecast commit
- Risk register
- Board summary
