# SECTION 4: FAQs (20+)

**Q1: What is the role of HR in conflict resolution?**
A: Facilitator, not judge. Create safe space, ensure fairness, guide process, document, follow-up. Goal: resolution, not punishment.

**Q2: How do you mediate a conflict between employees?**
A: Individual meetings first, neutral location, ground rules, each side shares, find common ground, brainstorm solutions, agreement.

**Q3: When should HR investigate?**
A: Harassment, discrimination, policy violations, safety concerns, theft, serious misconduct. Take all complaints seriously.

**Q4: How do you conduct a fair investigation?**
A: Prompt, neutral, thorough, confidential, no retaliation, documented, evidence-based, conclusion.

**Q5: What is progressive discipline?**
A: Escalating steps: Coaching → Verbal warning → Written warning → Final warning → Termination. Document at each step.

**Q6: How do you handle a harassment complaint?**
A: Take seriously, ensure safety, investigate promptly, confidentiality, no retaliation, appropriate action, follow-up.

**Q7: What is at-will employment?**
A: Either party can end relationship anytime (with exceptions: discrimination, retaliation, contract). Still need documentation.

**Q8: How do you coach managers on performance issues?**
A: Specific examples, behavior not personality, impact, expectations, support offered, timeline, document.

**Q9: What is retaliation and how to prevent?**
A: Adverse action after protected activity (complaint). Prevent: Training, monitoring, prompt investigation, no tolerance.

**Q10: How do you document employee issues?**
A: Facts, dates, witnesses, prior conversations, improvement plans, signatures. Objective, specific, consistent.

**Q11: What is constructive dismissal?**
A: Making conditions so intolerable employee quits. Legal risk. Avoid: Sudden changes, humiliation, isolation.

**Q12: How do you handle workplace bullying?**
A: Policy definition, reporting mechanism, investigation, action (coaching to termination), prevention training.

**Q13: What is the difference between EAP and HR?**
A: EAP = confidential counseling (external). HR = workplace issues, policy, can be required. Different purposes.

**Q14: How do you support a manager terminating an employee?**
A: Ensure documentation, legal review, planning meeting, script, witness, logistics, communication plan, security if needed.

**Q15: What is a PIP (Performance Improvement Plan)?**
A: Formal document: Current performance gap, specific expectations, timeline, support, consequences. Last chance.

**Q16: How do you handle grievances?**
A: Listen, investigate, respond, appeal process if applicable, document, look for patterns.

**Q17: What is workplace privacy?**
A: Limited in company systems/property. But: Reasonable expectation in personal items, bathroom, certain communications.

**Q18: How do you handle interpersonal conflict in teams?**
A: Assess severity, individual coaching, team intervention if needed, reset expectations, team norms.

**Q19: What is the duty of care?**
A: Employer responsibility for employee wellbeing. Includes: Safety, mental health, not overworking, addressing issues.

**Q20: How do you rebuild trust after a conflict?**
A: Acknowledge, apologize if appropriate, changed behavior, consistent follow-through, time, accountability.

# SECTION 5: SOPs

## SOP 1: Conflict Mediation

1. Separate initial meetings
2. Assess willingness to mediate
3. Schedule joint session
4. Set ground rules
5. Each person shares perspective
6. Identify underlying issues
7. Generate solutions
8. Reach agreement
9. Document
10. Follow-up in 30 days

## SOP 2: Investigation

1. Receive complaint
2. Assess scope and severity
3. Assign investigator (neutral)
4. Notify parties (confidentiality, no retaliation)
5. Gather evidence
6. Interview complainant, accused, witnesses
7. Analyze facts
8. Determine findings
9. Recommend action
10. Close loop with parties
11. Document file

## SOP 3: Performance Counseling

1. Schedule private meeting
2. Describe specific issue
3. Listen to employee perspective
4. Clarify expectations
5. Develop action plan
6. Offer support
7. Set timeline
8. Document conversation
9. Follow-up
10. Escalate if no improvement
