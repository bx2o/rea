# SECTION 4: FAQs (20+)

**Q1: What is FP&A?**
A: Financial Planning & Analysis. Bridge between finance and operations. Planning, forecasting, analysis, decision support.

**Q2: What makes a good financial model?**
A: Flexible, accurate, transparent, well-documented, error-checked, scenario-capable, driver-based.

**Q3: What's the difference between budget and forecast?**
A: Budget = annual plan, fixed. Forecast = updated view, dynamic. Budget is the target; forecast is the current expectation.

**Q4: What are driver-based models?**
A: Based on operational drivers (units, headcount, conversion) vs. historical percentages. More accurate, actionable.

**Q5: How do you handle budget season?**
A: Clear timeline, templates, training, iterative review, consolidation, executive alignment, board approval.

**Q6: What is variance analysis?**
A: Comparing actual to plan/forecast. Understand what happened (volume, price, mix) and why. Action-oriented.

**Q7: How do you forecast revenue?**
A: Bottom-up: Pipeline, conversion rates, ACV. Top-down: Market, growth rate. Blend both. Segment by product/geo.

**Q8: What are SaaS metrics?**
A: ARR, MRR, churn, expansion, NRR, CAC, LTV, payback, gross margin, burn rate, runway, magic number.

**Q9: How do you build scenario models?**
A: Base, upside, downside cases. Key variables (growth, churn, hiring, pricing). Probability-weighted if needed.

**Q10: What is rolling forecast?**
A: Always 12+ months forward. Updated regularly. More agile than annual budget. Common in fast-growing companies.

**Q11: How do you partner with business units?**
A: Understand their business, attend meetings, provide insights, not just reports, be proactive, build relationships.

**Q12: What is zero-based budgeting?**
A: Start from zero, justify every expense. Vs. incremental (last year + %). More rigorous but time-intensive.

**Q13: How do you present to the board?**
A: Clear narrative, focus on key metrics, variances, trends, risks/opportunities, forward view. Practice Q&A.

**Q14: What is working capital?**
A: Current assets - current liabilities. Cash conversion cycle. AR, inventory, AP management.

**Q15: How do you ensure data quality?**
A: Source systems, reconciliation, validation rules, regular audits, clear definitions, single source of truth.

**Q16: What is sensitivity analysis?**
A: How output changes with input changes. Tornado charts, data tables. Shows key drivers and risks.

**Q17: How do you handle mid-year changes?**
A: Formal reforecast process, business case for material changes, approval workflow, communication plan.

**Q18: What is the difference between accrual and cash?**
A: Accrual: Revenue when earned, expenses when incurred. Cash: When money moves. Accrual better for operations.

**Q19: How do you build a headcount plan?**
A: Current + open roles + planned hires by dept. Timing, cost, productivity assumptions. Linked to revenue model.

**Q20: What is financial storytelling?**
A: Not just numbers. Narrative around what happened, why it matters, what's coming. Context, not just data.
