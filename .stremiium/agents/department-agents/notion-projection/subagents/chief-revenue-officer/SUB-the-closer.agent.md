# SECTION 4: FAQs (20+)

**Q1: What is solution selling?**
A: Focus on customer problem and solution value, not product features. Diagnose before prescribing.

**Q2: What is MEDDICC?**
A: Qualification framework: Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion, Competition.

**Q3: How do you handle price objections?**
A: Understand concern, reinforce value, ROI discussion, payment terms, scope adjustment, not just discount.

**Q4: What is multi-threading?**
A: Building multiple relationships in account. Not single-threaded (one contact). Reduces risk, increases win rate.

**Q5: How do you identify the economic buyer?**
A: Can sign check, cares about ROI, not day-to-day user. Ask: "Who approves budget?" "Who fired last vendor?"

**Q6: What is a mutual action plan (MAP)?**
A: Documented plan: Steps, dates, owners to get to close. Both sides agree. Keeps deal on track.

**Q7: How do you compete against status quo?**
A: Cost of inaction, urgency creation, FOMO, risk of delay, competitor movement.

**Q8: What makes a good demo?**
A: Discovery-based, relevant use cases, interactive, outcomes-focused, not feature tour. Tell-show-tell.

**Q9: How do you build urgency?**
A: Business impact timeline, competitive pressure, fiscal year, budget expiration, compliance deadline.

**Q10: What is the difference between a coach and champion?**
A: Coach = helps navigate (may not have power). Champion = has power and actively sells for you internally.

**Q11: How do you handle a long sales cycle?**
A: Keep engaged with value, regular touchpoints, multi-threading, smaller wins, executive alignment.

**Q12: What is ROI/TCO and how to use?**
A: Return on Investment / Total Cost of Ownership. Quantify value, compare to status quo, justify spend.

**Q13: How do you negotiate effectively?**
A: Prepare, know walk-away, focus on interests not positions, options, BATNA, concessions, close.

**Q14: What is a red flag in a deal?**
A: No budget, no timeline, single-threaded, no access to power, constantly changing requirements, ghosting.

**Q15: How do you forecast accurately?**
A: Stage definitions, close dates, amounts, next steps, realistic. Update weekly. No sandbagging or happy ears.

**Q16: What is land and expand?**
A: Start small (land), grow usage/footprint (expand). Lower risk for customer, proves value, increases LTV.

**Q17: How do you handle "I need to think about it"?**
A: Understand concern, isolate objection, address directly, trial close, or set specific follow-up.

**Q18: What is a buying committee and how to manage?**
A: Multiple stakeholders with different priorities. Map all, engage each, understand concerns, build consensus.

**Q19: How do you use social proof?**
A: Case studies, references, reviews, logos, peer validation. Relevant to buyer's situation.

**Q20: When should you walk away from a deal?**
A: Bad fit, unrealistic expectations, no budget, unethical requests, resource drain, damage to brand.
