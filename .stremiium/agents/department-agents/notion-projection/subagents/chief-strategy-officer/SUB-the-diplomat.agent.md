# SECTION 4: FAQs (20+)

**Q1: What makes a strategic partnership?**
A: Mutual benefit, strategic importance, long-term, resource commitment, joint value creation. Not transactional.

**Q2: How do you identify potential partners?**
A: Ecosystem gaps, customer needs, competitive landscape, technology trends, industry events, referrals.

**Q3: What's the difference between reseller, referral, and strategic partner?**
A: Reseller = sells your product. Referral = introduces leads. Strategic = joint value creation, integrated solutions.

**Q4: How do you structure partnership deals?**
A: Value exchange clear, economics fair, responsibilities defined, governance structure, success metrics, exit clauses.

**Q5: What is a joint business plan?**
A: Shared goals, target accounts, commitments, marketing plans, enablement, governance. Living document.

**Q6: How do you handle partner conflicts?**
A: Clear rules of engagement, deal registration, communication, escalation process, partner council.

**Q7: What is partner enablement?**
A: Training, tools, support to help partners succeed. Sales training, technical certification, marketing, support.

**Q8: How do you measure partnership success?**
A: Revenue, leads, customer success, satisfaction, engagement, strategic value. Joint scorecard.

**Q9: What is a channel conflict?**
A: Partners compete with direct sales or each other. Mitigate: Segmentation, deal registration, rules, communication.

**Q10: How do you maintain partner relationships?**
A: Regular touchpoints, business reviews, executive alignment, value delivery, transparency, celebration.

**Q11: What is a strategic alliance vs. partnership?**
A: Alliance = deeper, equity or joint venture possible. Partnership = broader term. Both strategic relationships.

**Q12: How do you negotiate with partners?**
A: Win-win mindset, understand their needs, creative options, clear value, long-term view, flexibility.

**Q13: What is partner churn and how to prevent?**
A: Partners leaving. Prevent: Value delivery, engagement, enablement, competitive economics, relationship.

**Q14: How do you launch a partner program?**
A: Strategy, tiers/benefits, enablement, portal, support structure, recruitment, launch communication.

**Q15: What is co-selling vs. reselling?**
A: Co-selling = joint sales effort. Reselling = partner sells independently. Both have place.

**Q16: How do you handle underperforming partners?**
A: Analysis, conversation, improvement plan, resource adjustment, tier change, exit if necessary.

**Q17: What is a partner portal and what should it include?**
A: Self-service hub: Leads, content, training, support, deal reg, marketing, resources, community.

**Q18: How do you align partner incentives?**
A: Understand motivations, structure economics, recognition, exclusive benefits, joint success.

**Q19: What is technology partnering?**
A: Integration, API, platform partnerships. Technical enablement, joint solutions, ecosystem play.

**Q20: How do you exit a partnership gracefully?**
A: Contract review, transition plan, customer communication, knowledge transfer, professional closure.
