# SECTION 4: FAQs (20+)

**Q1: What is competitive intelligence vs. corporate espionage?**
A: CI = legal, ethical gathering of public information. Espionage = illegal. CI: Public sources, analysis, not deception.

**Q2: What are primary vs. secondary research?**
A: Primary = new data (surveys, interviews). Secondary = existing data (reports, articles). Both needed.

**Q3: How do you build a competitive battlecard?**
A: Overview, products, pricing, positioning, strengths, weaknesses, objections handling, win themes, latest intel.

**Q4: What is market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)?**
A: TAM = total demand. SAM = serviceable (your market). SOM = obtainable (realistic share). Top-down and bottom-up.

**Q5: How do you monitor competitors?**
A: Website tracking, job postings, press releases, social media, product updates, pricing changes, patent filings, events.

**Q6: What is a SWOT analysis?**
A: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats. Internal (S/W) and external (O/T). Strategic planning tool.

**Q7: How do you conduct win/loss analysis?**
A: Interview recent wins/losses, structured questions, identify patterns, product, price, process, sales factors.

**Q8: What is Porter's Five Forces?**
A: Industry analysis: Competitive rivalry, supplier power, buyer power, threat of substitution, threat of new entry.

**Q9: How do you identify market trends?**
A: Leading indicators, industry reports, customer feedback, patent analysis, startup activity, expert interviews.

**Q10: What is the difference between qualitative and quantitative research?**
A: Qualitative = depth, insights, "why" (interviews). Quantitative = scale, statistics, "what" (surveys). Both valuable.

**Q11: How do you ensure research objectivity?**
A: Multiple sources, triangulation, awareness of bias, external validation, peer review, methodology transparency.

**Q12: What is scenario planning?**
A: Multiple future scenarios, not single forecast. Plausible futures, implications, strategic options. Prepares for uncertainty.

**Q13: How do you present research to executives?**
A: So what first, 3 key insights, clear recommendations, visual, concise, backup data available.

**Q14: What is a buyer persona?**
A: Research-based representation of ideal customer. Goals, challenges, behaviors, preferences. Not demographic-only.

**Q15: How do you track technology trends?**
A: Patent filings, VC investments, GitHub activity, industry publications, conferences, expert networks.

**Q16: What is the Kano model?**
A: Customer satisfaction model: Basic needs (must have), performance needs (linear), delighters (excitement).

**Q17: How do you do pricing research?**
A: Van Westendorp (price sensitivity), conjoint analysis (trade-offs), competitor benchmarking, value-based testing.

**Q18: What is jobs-to-be-done (JTBD)?**
A: Framework: What "job" is customer hiring product to do? Functional, emotional, social. Innovation framework.

**Q19: How do you handle information gaps?**
A: Acknowledge, estimate with assumptions, triangulate, primary research, sensitivity analysis, ongoing monitoring.

**Q20: What is ethical intelligence gathering?**
A: Public sources only, no deception, respect confidentiality, legal compliance, transparency with sources.
