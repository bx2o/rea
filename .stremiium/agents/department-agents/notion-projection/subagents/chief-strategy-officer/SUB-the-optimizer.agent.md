# SECTION 4: FAQs (20+)

**Q1: What is Lean methodology?**
A: Eliminate waste, maximize value. 8 wastes: Defects, Overproduction, Waiting, Non-utilized talent, Transportation, Inventory, Motion, Extra-processing.

**Q2: What is Six Sigma?**
A: Reduce variation, improve quality. DMAIC: Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control. Statistical approach.

**Q3: What is value stream mapping?**
A: Visualize flow of value from request to delivery. Current state vs. future state. Identify waste, bottlenecks.

**Q4: How do you identify automation opportunities?**
A: High volume, repetitive, rule-based, stable, digital inputs. ROI threshold. Start with pain points.

**Q5: What is the difference between efficiency and effectiveness?**
A: Efficiency = doing things right (fast, cheap). Effectiveness = doing right things (right outcomes). Both matter.

**Q6: How do you handle resistance to change?**
A: Involve early, show value, address concerns, training, quick wins, champions, communication, persistence.

**Q7: What is kaizen?**
A: Continuous improvement. Small, ongoing changes. Everyone involved. Culture of improvement.

**Q8: How do you measure process performance?**
A: Cycle time, touch time, error rate, cost per unit, throughput, quality, customer satisfaction.

**Q9: What is a process bottleneck?**
A: Constraint limiting throughput. Identify, elevate, subordinate. Often has work piling up before it.

**Q10: What is standard work?**
A: Documented best practice. Current best way, not permanent. Foundation for improvement and training.

**Q11: How do you calculate ROI for process improvement?**
A: (Benefits - Costs) / Costs. Benefits: Time saved, errors reduced, headcount avoided, satisfaction.

**Q12: What is the 5S methodology?**
A: Sort, Set in order, Shine, Standardize, Sustain. Workplace organization foundation.

**Q13: How do you prioritize improvement projects?**
A: Impact vs. effort matrix. Strategic alignment, pain points, quick wins, resources, dependencies.

**Q14: What is RPA and when to use?**
A: Robotic Process Automation. UI-based bots for repetitive tasks. When: Stable, high volume, rule-based.

**Q15: What is cycle time vs. lead time?**
A: Cycle time = time to complete one unit. Lead time = total time from request to delivery. Both important.

**Q16: How do you sustain improvements?**
A: Control plans, metrics, ownership, audits, standard work, training, management review, continuous monitoring.

**Q17: What is a Gemba walk?**
A: Go to where work happens. Observe, ask questions, understand actual vs. documented. Respectful inquiry.

**Q18: How do you involve employees in improvement?**
A: Suggestion systems, kaizen events, training, empowerment, recognition, time allocation, listening.

**Q19: What is PDCA?**
A: Plan-Do-Check-Act. Iterative improvement cycle. Small experiments, learn, adapt, scale.

**Q20: How do you balance standardization and flexibility?**
A: Standardize core processes (80%), allow flexibility for variation (20%). Tiered standardization.
