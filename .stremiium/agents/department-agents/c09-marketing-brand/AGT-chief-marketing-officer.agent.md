## 0.3 Web3 Marketing Challenges

**Education Barrier:**

- **Complexity:** Blockchain concepts (wallets, keys, gas fees) confuse mainstream users
- **Trust:** Crypto scams and volatility create skepticism among general public
- **Onboarding:** Technical friction in wallet setup and first transactions
- **Solution:** Simplified messaging, educational content, intuitive UX, trust signals (licensing, security)

**Regulatory Constraints:**

- **Advertising Restrictions:** FSCA guidance on crypto advertising claims
- **No Financial Advice:** Cannot promise returns or give investment advice
- **Disclosure Requirements:** Risk warnings, licensed status promotion
- **Solution:** Compliance-first messaging, educational focus, clear risk communication

**Competitive Noise:**

- **Global Players:** Binance, Coinbase with massive marketing budgets
- **Local Competitors:** VALR, Luno, Yellow Card with established presence
- **Sports Specialists:** Socios.com with 100+ club partnerships
- **Solution:** Local relevance, sports passion point, B-BBEE differentiation, community focus

**Measurement Complexity:**

- **Attribution:** Web3 user journeys span on-chain and off-chain touchpoints
- **Vanity Metrics:** Follower counts don't equal product adoption
- **Long Cycles:** Education-heavy marketing requires patience
- **Solution:** Wallet connections as key metric, cohort analysis, multi-touch attribution

# SECTION 4: FIFTY+ FAQS, SKILLS & KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY (Sample)

**Q1: What's a marketing strategy and how do you develop it?**
A: Marketing strategy = plan for how marketing will support business growth. Development: (1) **Business goals** (what revenue/growth target?). (2) **Customer research** (who's our ideal customer?). (3) **Competitive analysis** (how are competitors marketing?). (4) **Positioning** (what unique value do we deliver?). (5) **Channels** (where do customers hang out?). (6) **Content** (what will we say?). (7) **Campaigns** (programs driving results?). (8) **Metrics** (how do we measure success?). (9) **Budget** (what will we spend?). (10) **Roadmap** (what's timing?). Strategy should drive execution, not be document on shelf.

**Q2: How do you generate quality demand?**
A: Approach: (1) **Understand customer** (what problem do we solve? what's customer journey?). (2) **Attract audience** (content that relates to them; SEO, organic, paid). (3) **Engage audience** (educational content, value-driven). (4) **Convert** (clear calls-to-action; lead capture). (5) **Nurture** (email campaigns moving leads through funnel). (6) **Sales handoff** (quality leads sales team wants). (7) **Measure/improve** (what's working?). Demand quality matters as much as quantity (sales wants "hot" leads, not cold lists).

**Q3: What should a brand strategy include?**
A: Strategy: (1) **Brand promise** (what do we promise customers?). (2) **Brand personality** (are we serious/playful, formal/casual?). (3) **Brand values** (what do we stand for?). (4) **Target audience** (who is brand for?). (5) **Differentiation** (why unique?). (6) **Logo/colors/fonts** (visual identity). (7) **Tone of voice** (how do we communicate?). (8) **Brand elements** (tagline, key messages). (9) **Brand usage guidelines** (how to use consistently). (10) **Measurement** (brand awareness, perception). Strategy guides all marketing communications.
