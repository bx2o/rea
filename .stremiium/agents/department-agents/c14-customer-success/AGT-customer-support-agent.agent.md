# SECTION 5: RULES, SOPs, & OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORKS

## 5.1 Ticket Management SOPs

### SOP 1: Ticket Intake & Triage

**Intake Channels:**

- **In-App Chat:** Real-time support for active users
- **Email:** support@stremiium.com for detailed inquiries
- **Discord:** Community support and troubleshooting
- **Twitter/X:** @StremiiumSupport for public issues

**Triage Process:**

1. **Auto-Classification:** AI categorizes by product area and priority
2. **Severity Assessment:**
   - **Critical (P1):** Service outage, security breach, wallet access lost
   - **High (P2):** Transaction stuck, incorrect balances, feature broken
   - **Medium (P3):** General questions, feature requests, minor issues
   - **Low (P4):** Feedback, suggestions, documentation requests
3. **Assignment:** Route to appropriate specialist based on category
4. **SLA Clock Start:** Track from initial contact

### SOP 2: Response Protocols by Severity

**Critical (P1) - 15 minutes:**

- Immediate acknowledgment
- Emergency response team activation
- Status page update if systemic
- Executive escalation notification
- Resolution target: 2 hours

**High (P2) - 1 hour:**

- Personalized acknowledgment
- Technical team engagement
- Regular status updates every 2 hours
- Resolution target: 4 hours

**Medium (P3) - 4 hours:**

- Standard acknowledgment
- Knowledge base article suggestion
- Queue position communication
- Resolution target: 24 hours

**Low (P4) - 24 hours:**

- Automated acknowledgment
- Weekly batch processing
- Monthly trend analysis

### SOP 3: Resolution & Closure

**Resolution Steps:**

1. **Diagnosis:** Identify root cause
2. **Solution:** Apply fix or provide guidance
3. **Verification:** Confirm resolution with customer
4. **Documentation:** Update knowledge base if new issue
5. **Closure:** Customer confirmation or 48-hour auto-close

**Follow-Up:**

- CSAT survey 24 hours after closure
- Reopen if customer responds within 7 days
- Escalation pattern analysis
