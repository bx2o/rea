# SECTION 5: RULES, SOPs, & OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORKS

## 5.1 CSM Team Lead SOPs

### SOP 1: Strategic Account Management

**Trigger:** New strategic account assigned to portfolio

**Steps:**

1. **Discovery (Week 1)**
   - Schedule executive kickoff meeting
   - Document business objectives and success criteria
   - Map organizational structure and stakeholders
   - Review contract terms and implementation history
   - Identify expansion opportunities

2. **Success Plan Development (Week 2-3)**
   - Create customized success plan
   - Define key milestones and metrics
   - Establish regular meeting cadence
   - Document technical requirements and constraints
   - Set mutual expectations for engagement

3. **Ongoing Management (Monthly)**
   - Executive business reviews (EBRs)
   - Usage and adoption monitoring
   - Expansion opportunity identification
   - Risk assessment and mitigation
   - Relationship health check

4. **Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)**
   - Usage trends and ROI analysis
   - Product roadmap alignment
   - Expansion planning
   - Feedback collection
   - Strategic planning

**Success Criteria:** Customer achieving objectives, expansion revenue, executive satisfaction
**Check-in Frequency:** Weekly operational, Monthly strategic, Quarterly EBRs
**Escalation:** Critical risks → VP Customer Success within 24 hours

### SOP 2: Team Coaching & Development

**Trigger:** Regular team development cycle

**Steps:**

1. **Weekly 1:1s (60 minutes per CSM)**
   - Review account health scores
   - Discuss challenging situations
   - Provide specific feedback
   - Set weekly priorities
   - Address questions and concerns

2. **Monthly Team Meetings (2 hours)**
   - Review team KPIs and trends
   - Share customer wins and learnings
   - Process improvement discussions
   - Product updates and training
   - Peer learning and case studies

3. **Quarterly Performance Reviews**
   - Comprehensive performance assessment
   - Goal setting for next quarter
   - Career development planning
   - Compensation and promotion discussions
   - Training needs identification

4. **Ad-Hoc Coaching**
   - Real-time call shadowing
   - Deal strategy sessions
   - Difficult conversation preparation
   - Escalation support
   - Best practice sharing

**Success Criteria:** CSM competency development, team retention, performance improvement
**Cadence:** Weekly 1:1s, Monthly team meetings, Quarterly reviews
**Tools:** CRM data, call recordings, performance dashboards

### SOP 3: Health Score Management

**Trigger:** Monthly health score review cycle

**Steps:**

1. **Data Collection (Day 1-3)**
   - Pull usage metrics from all platforms
   - Review support ticket volume and resolution
   - Check engagement frequency and quality
   - Analyze sentiment (NPS, CSAT, surveys)
   - Review business outcome achievement

2. **Score Calculation (Day 4-5)**
   - Apply weighted scoring model:
     - Product Adoption: 30%
     - Engagement: 25%
     - Support: 20%
     - Business Outcomes: 25%
   - Assign color rating (Green/Yellow/Red)
   - Identify trend direction

3. **Action Planning (Day 6-10)**
   - Green accounts: Expansion planning
   - Yellow accounts: Intervention strategy
   - Red accounts: Recovery plan with VP
   - Assign ownership and timelines
   - Schedule follow-up actions

4. **Review & Reporting (Day 11-15)**
   - Team health review meeting
   - Report to VP Customer Success
   - Update account plans
   - Execute interventions

**Success Criteria:** Accurate health scoring, proactive risk identification, appropriate interventions
**Review Frequency:** Monthly comprehensive, Weekly quick pulse
**Escalation:** Red accounts → VP within 48 hours
