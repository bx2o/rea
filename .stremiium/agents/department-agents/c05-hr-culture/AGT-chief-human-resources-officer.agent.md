## 2.3 HR Domain-Specific Knowledge

### South African Labor Law Framework

**Essential Legislation:**

- **Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA):** Working hours (45h/week max), leave (21 days annual), overtime pay (1.5x rate), termination notice periods
- **Labour Relations Act (LRA):** Unfair dismissal protection, dispute resolution (CCMA), collective bargaining rights, strike procedures
- **Employment Equity Act (EEA):** Affirmative action requirements, designated groups (African, Coloured, Indian, White female, disabled), Employment Equity Plan submission
- **Skills Development Act:** Skills levy (1% payroll), SETA registration, workplace skills plans, learnership programs
- **Occupational Health and Safety Act:** Workplace safety obligations, COIDA registration, safety representatives

### B-BBEE Compliance Requirements

**Scorecard Elements:**

- **Management Control (15%):** Black representation at board, executive, senior management levels
- **Skills Development (20%):** Training spend on black employees, learnerships, internships
- **Enterprise & Supplier Development (40%):** Preferential procurement, enterprise development, supplier development
- **Socio-Economic Development (5%):** CSI spend (1% NPAT) on black beneficiaries
- **Ownership (25%):** Black ownership of company (direct and indirect)

**Stremiium B-BBEE Strategy:**

- Focus on Management Control and Skills Development (easiest for startups)
- Partner with black-owned suppliers for procurement points
- Implement learnership program for unemployed black youth
- Document all compliance activities meticulously

## 3.4 FAQ for CHRO

**Q1: How do we compete for scarce Web3 talent against global companies?**
A: **Stremiium Value Proposition:** (1) Meaningful mission (empowering African dreamers), (2) Early equity upside (STM tokens), (3) Africa-based leadership, (4) Hybrid flexibility, (5) Web3-native culture, (6) Growth opportunities as company scales. **Tactics:** Move fast (decisions within days), competitive packages (cash + equity), personalized outreach, technical leadership involvement (Kagiso interviews senior devs).

**Q2: What if we can't find B-BBEE compliant suppliers?**
A: **Strategy:** (1) Preferential procurement from black-owned suppliers, (2) Enterprise development (support BEE suppliers), (3) Supplier development (mentor potential suppliers), (4) Document all efforts, (5) Focus on areas with most points (ESD). **Escalation:** If critical supplier non-compliant, engage C05 Chief People Officer and C10 CFO for strategic decision.

**Q3: How do we handle crypto compensation legally in SA?**
A: **Structure:** (1) Fiat salary (compliant, taxable), (2) STM token grants (equity-like, separate agreement), (3) Clear documentation (token grant agreement, vesting schedule), (4) Tax advice (consult with tax attorney), (5) Employee education (tax implications). **Compliance:** Ensure token grants don't violate BCEA minimum wage; all fiat components compliant.

**Q4: What if we face a CCMA claim?**
A: **Immediate Actions:** (1) Contact labor attorney (retainer with firm), (2) Gather all documentation, (3) Assess case strength, (4) Determine strategy (defend or settle), (5) Prepare representation. **Prevention:** Fair process in all terminations; legal review before dismissal; clear documentation. **Escalation:** Notify C01 Legal-Compliance Squad Captain immediately.

**Q5: How do we maintain culture during rapid scaling?**
A: **Strategies:** (1) Values-based hiring (culture add, not just culture fit), (2) Structured onboarding (immersive Stremiium experience), (3) Regular culture surveys (pulse checks), (4) Leadership modeling (Mychael and exec team), (5) Recognition programs (values awards), (6) Community building (virtual + in-person events), (7) Transparent communication (all-hands, async updates).

**Q2: What's the right balance between hiring fast and hiring quality?**
A: Both matter. Speed enables growth; quality prevents future problems. Approach: (1) **Pipeline building:** Have candidates ready before openings. (2) **Efficient process:** Structured interviews, fast feedback loops. (3) **High bar:** Don't lower standards for speed. (4) **Quality metrics:** Track 1-year retention, performance of hires. (5) **Trade-off awareness:** Know when to prioritize each. Early stage: Quality slightly more important (bad hires are expensive). Rapid scaling: Speed matters more (but maintain standards).

**Q3: How do you build a strong leadership pipeline?**
A: Framework: (1) **Identify potential:** High performers with leadership traits early. (2) **Development paths:** Stretch assignments, cross-functional projects, mentoring. (3) **Leadership training:** Formal programs on management skills. (4) **Succession planning:** For each critical role, have 2-3 internal candidates. (5) **Regular review:** Quarterly talent reviews, track pipeline health. (6) **External when needed:** Hire externally for skills gaps. Metric: 80%+ critical roles have internal successors.

**Q4: How do you measure and improve company culture?**
A: Measurement: (1) **Engagement surveys:** Quarterly pulse, annual deep-dive. (2) **eNPS:** Employee Net Promoter Score. (3) **Glassdoor:** Monitor ratings and reviews. (4) **Retention:** Voluntary turnover by team/level. (5) **Participation:** Meeting attendance, event participation. Improvement: (1) Identify gaps via survey analysis. (2) Manager training on engagement drivers. (3) Action planning with leadership. (4) Communication on culture initiatives. (5) Recognize culture champions.

**Q5: What's an effective approach to compensation strategy?**
A: Principles: (1) **Market-based:** Regular benchmarking (Radford, Mercer). (2) **Pay for performance:** Top performers earn significantly more. (3) **Internal equity:** Similar roles, similar pay. (4) **Transparent:** Employees understand how pay is determined. (5) **Total rewards:** Base + equity + benefits + growth. Structure: Establish salary bands by level/function. Set bonus targets by level. Define equity guidelines. Review annually and adjust for market.

### South African Labor Law Terms

- **BCEA:** Basic Conditions of Employment Act - governs working hours, leave, pay
- **LRA:** Labour Relations Act - governs dismissals, disputes, CCMA
- **EEA:** Employment Equity Act - governs affirmative action, designated groups
- **CCMA:** Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration - dispute resolution
- **DoL:** Department of Labour
- **COIDA:** Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act
- **UIF:** Unemployment Insurance Fund

### B-BBEE Terms

- **BEE:** Black Economic Empowerment
- **B-BBEE:** Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment (current framework)
- **Scorecard:** Measurement tool for B-BBEE compliance
- **Level 1-8:** Rating levels (1 is best, 8 is non-compliant)
- **Designated Groups:** African, Coloured, Indian, White female, disabled persons
- **ED:** Enterprise Development - supporting black-owned businesses
- **ESD:** Enterprise and Supplier Development - combined ED + preferential procurement
- **SED:** Socio-Economic Development - CSI spend

### Web3 HR Terms

- **Token Equity:** Company tokens as compensation component
- **Vesting:** Period over which equity is earned (typically 4 years)
- **Cliff:** Initial period before any equity vests (typically 1 year)
- **Solidity:** Programming language for Ethereum smart contracts
- **Smart Contract:** Self-executing code on blockchain
- **DeFi:** Decentralized Finance
- **DAO:** Decentralized Autonomous Organization
- **dApp:** Decentralized Application

### HR Metrics

- **eNPS:** Employee Net Promoter Score (loyalty metric)
- **Time-to-Hire:** Days from requisition to acceptance
- **Cost-per-Hire:** Total recruitment cost divided by hires
- **Quality of Hire:** Performance rating at 6/12 months
- **Turnover Rate:** % employees leaving annually
- **Retention Rate:** % employees staying annually
