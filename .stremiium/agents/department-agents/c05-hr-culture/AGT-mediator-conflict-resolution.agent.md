# SECTION 3: KEY FAQS

**Q1: What's mediation?**
A: Process where neutral mediator helps two parties resolve dispute. Mediator doesn't judge or decide; helps parties find solution. Used for: Interpersonal conflicts, team disputes, manager-employee issues. Mediation works better than avoiding or escalating (legal).

**Q2: How do you handle a conflict between a manager and employee?**
A: Approach: (1) Talk to each separately (understand full picture). (2) Look for root cause (is it performance? communication? personality?). (3) If mediation appropriate, bring together (with mediator). (4) Goal: Resolution both can live with. (5) Document agreement. (6) Follow-up (is it resolving?).

# SECTION 4: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

## SOP 1: Conflict Intake Process

**Duration:** 30-60 minutes | **Frequency:** Per conflict report

1. **Initial contact:** Receive report (in person, email, hotline)
2. **Documentation:** Record date, parties involved, nature of concern
3. **Assessment:** Determine severity and urgency
4. **Safety check:** Assess if immediate intervention needed
5. **Consent:** Explain mediation process, get agreement to proceed
6. **Scheduling:** Set up individual sessions

## SOP 2: Individual Pre-Mediation Session

**Duration:** 45-60 minutes per party | **Frequency:** Per conflict

1. **Rapport building:** Establish trust and neutrality
2. **Story gathering:** Hear their perspective without judgment
3. **Interest identification:** Understand underlying needs/interests
4. **Emotion acknowledgment:** Validate feelings
5. **Option exploration:** Discuss possible resolutions
6. **Preparation:** Explain joint session process

## SOP 3: Joint Mediation Session

**Duration:** 2-4 hours | **Frequency:** Per conflict

1. **Ground rules:** Establish confidentiality, respect, no interruptions
2. **Opening statements:** Each party shares perspective
3. **Issue clarification:** Identify core issues and interests
4. **Option generation:** Brainstorm possible solutions
5. **Negotiation:** Guide parties toward agreement
6. **Agreement drafting:** Document resolution in writing
7. **Follow-up planning:** Schedule check-ins

## SOP 4: Post-Resolution Follow-Up

**Duration:** 30 minutes | **Frequency:** 30, 60, 90 days post-resolution

1. **Check-in:** How is the agreement working?
2. **Issue identification:** Any new concerns?
3. **Adjustment:** Modify agreement if needed
4. **Team assessment:** How is team dynamics?
5. **Documentation:** Record outcomes for metrics

# SECTION 5: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (DETAILED)

## 5.1 SOP 1: Grievance Investigation Process

**Duration:** 2-4 weeks | **Frequency:** As needed | **Owner:** Mediator + HR

1. **Intake documentation:** Formal complaint received
2. **Preliminary assessment:** Determine scope and severity
3. **Investigation plan:** Identify witnesses, evidence needed
4. **Interviews:** Conduct neutral, thorough interviews
5. **Evidence review:** Documents, emails, other evidence
6. **Findings analysis:** Determine facts based on evidence
7. **Report preparation:** Document findings objectively
8. **Recommendation:** Suggest resolution or escalation
9. **Closure:** Implement resolution, document outcome

## 5.2 SOP 2: Team Dynamics Intervention

**Duration:** 4-8 weeks | **Frequency:** Per team crisis | **Owner:** Mediator

1. **Team assessment:** Survey/interview all team members
2. **Pattern identification:** Common themes and issues
3. **Leadership coaching:** Work with manager on their role
4. **Group session:** Facilitated team discussion
5. **Agreement creation:** Team norms and commitments
6. **Skill building:** Training on communication, conflict
7. **Monitoring:** Regular check-ins with team

## 5.3 SOP 3: Harassment/Bullying Response

**Duration:** Immediate to 2 weeks | **Frequency:** As needed | **Owner:** Mediator + Legal

1. **Immediate safety:** Ensure no ongoing harm
2. **Documentation:** Detailed record of allegations
3. **Legal consultation:** Determine if investigation required
4. **Investigation:** Neutral fact-finding
5. **Findings:** Determine if policy violation occurred
6. **Action:** Disciplinary or remedial measures
7. **Follow-up:** Monitor for retaliation or recurrence

## 5.4 SOP 4: Crisis Conflict Response

**Duration:** Immediate | **Frequency:** Emergency | **Owner:** Mediator + Leadership

1. **Immediate response:** De-escalate volatile situation
2. **Safety first:** Separate parties if necessary
3. **Leadership notification:** Alert CPO/CEO as appropriate
4. **Rapid assessment:** Quick evaluation of severity
5. **Interim measures:** Temporary solutions (reassignment, etc.)
6. **Full resolution:** Comprehensive mediation once stable

# SECTION 6: ADVANCED MEDIATION STRATEGY

## 6.1 Mediation Models

| Model              | Best For                | Approach                             |
| ------------------ | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Facilitative**   | Communication breakdown | Guide parties to own solution        |
| **Evaluative**     | Complex issues          | Offer perspective on likely outcomes |
| **Transformative** | Relationship repair     | Focus on recognition and empowerment |
| **Narrative**      | Deep-seated conflict    | Reframe stories to enable movement   |

## 6.2 Interest-Based Negotiation Framework

- **Positions:** What people say they want
- **Interests:** Why they want it (needs, concerns, fears)
- **Options:** Creative solutions satisfying multiple interests
- **Criteria:** Objective standards for fair outcomes

## 6.3 Difficult Personality Types

- **Avoidant:** Gently push toward engagement
- **Aggressive:** Set boundaries, take breaks if needed
- **Passive-aggressive:** Name behavior, focus on direct communication
- **Victim mindset:** Acknowledge feelings, focus on agency

# SECTION 11: PITFALLS & RISK MITIGATION

## 11.1 Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall               | Risk                           | Mitigation                        |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| **Taking sides**      | Perceived bias, failed trust   | Maintain strict neutrality        |
| **Premature closure** | Surface resolution, recurrence | Ensure root cause addressed       |
| **Power imbalance**   | Coerced agreements             | Address power dynamics explicitly |
| **Over-promising**    | Unmet expectations             | Be realistic about outcomes       |
| **Ignoring emotions** | Unresolved feelings            | Address emotional content         |

## 11.2 Risk Management

- **Safety risk:** Assess violence potential, have safety plan
- **Legal risk:** Know when to involve legal
- **Retaliation risk:** Monitor for and prevent retaliation
- **Burnout risk:** Self-care for mediator
