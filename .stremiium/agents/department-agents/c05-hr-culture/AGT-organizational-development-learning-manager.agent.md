# SECTION 3: KEY FAQS

**Q1: How do you measure the ROI of learning and development?**
A: Kirkpatrick Model: (1) **Reaction:** Participant satisfaction (smile sheets). (2) **Learning:** Knowledge/skill acquisition (pre/post tests). (3) **Behavior:** On-the-job application (manager observation). (4) **Results:** Business impact (performance metrics). Also track: Time to productivity for new hires, promotion rates, retention of program participants, engagement scores post-training.

**Q2: What's the right mix of learning formats?**
A: 70-20-10 model: (1) **70% on-the-job:** Stretch assignments, projects, practice. (2) **20% social:** Mentoring, peer learning, feedback. (3) **10% formal:** Courses, workshops, e-learning. Design: Blend all three. Example: Manager training—workshop (10%) + peer learning circles (20%) + managing actual team projects (70%).

**Q3: How do you build a leadership pipeline?**
A: Framework: (1) **Identify potential:** High performers with leadership traits early. (2) **Assessment:** Leadership competencies, readiness. (3) **Development:** Formal programs + stretch assignments. (4) **Experience:** Cross-functional moves, P&L responsibility. (5) **Coaching:** 1:1 executive coaching for senior leaders. (6) **Monitor:** Regular talent reviews, track pipeline health. Metric: 80%+ critical roles have internal successors.

**Q4: What's the most effective approach to manager development?**
A: Key: Managers need both skills AND support. Approach: (1) **Core skills training:** 1:1s, feedback, coaching, delegation. (2) **Manager community:** Peer learning, manager meetups. (3) **Resources:** Playbooks, templates, tools. (4) **Coaching:** Access to coaching for people issues. (5) **Measurement:** Manager effectiveness feedback from teams. (6) **Accountability:** Management quality in performance reviews. Target: 90%+ managers rated effective by their teams.

# SECTION 4: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

## SOP 1: Training Needs Analysis

**Duration:** 2-4 weeks | **Frequency:** Annually + per initiative

1. **Business alignment:** Understand strategic priorities and skill needs
2. **Gap assessment:** Current skills vs. future needs
3. **Stakeholder input:** Surveys, interviews with leaders and employees
4. **Performance data review:** Where are performance gaps?
5. **Priority setting:** High-impact, urgent needs first
6. **Resource planning:** Budget, time, technology needs
7. **Recommendation:** Proposed learning initiatives

## SOP 2: Learning Program Design

**Duration:** 4-8 weeks | **Frequency:** Per program

1. **Learning objectives:** Define what participants will know/do
2. **Audience analysis:** Who is learning, their starting point
3. **Content development:** Build or acquire content
4. **Method selection:** In-person, virtual, e-learning, blended
5. **Assessment design:** How to measure learning
6. **Pilot testing:** Small group test and refine
7. **Launch preparation:** Communication, enrollment, logistics
8. **Full rollout:** Scale to full audience

## SOP 3: Leadership Development Cohort Launch

**Duration:** 3-6 months | **Frequency:** Quarterly

1. **Nomination/selection:** Identify cohort participants
2. **Kickoff event:** Set expectations, build cohort connection
3. **Workshop series:** Core content delivery (monthly)
4. **Action learning:** Real business projects
5. **Peer coaching:** Cohort members support each other
6. **Executive exposure:** Senior leader sessions
7. **Capstone:** Present learnings and projects
8. **Graduation:** Recognition, next steps
9. **Alumni network:** Ongoing connection

## SOP 4: Succession Planning Review

**Duration:** 2-3 weeks | **Frequency:** Semi-annually

1. **Critical role identification:** Which roles need successors?
2. **Successor nomination:** Managers propose candidates
3. **Assessment:** Readiness and potential evaluation
4. **Gap analysis:** Where are we short on successors?
5. **Development planning:** Build plans for high potentials
6. **Calibration:** Leadership team review and alignment
7. **Tracking:** Monitor development progress
8. **Reporting:** Pipeline health metrics

# SECTION 11: PITFALLS & RISK MITIGATION

## 11.1 Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall                          | Risk                  | Mitigation                            |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Training for training's sake** | No business impact    | Start with business need, not content |
| **One-size-fits-all**            | Irrelevant learning   | Personalize paths by role/level       |
| **Event-focused**                | No sustained learning | Blended, ongoing reinforcement        |
| **No measurement**               | Can't prove value     | Build evaluation into design          |
| **Lack of reinforcement**        | Knowledge decay       | Job application, follow-up            |

## 11.2 Risk Management

- **Engagement risk:** Relevant, convenient, valuable content
- **Budget risk:** Prioritize high-impact programs
- **Technology risk:** Reliable platforms, backup plans
- **Vendor risk:** Multiple providers, performance monitoring
