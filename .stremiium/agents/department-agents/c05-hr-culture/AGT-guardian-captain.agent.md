# SECTION 4: 28+ FAQs

**Q1: How do you attract top talent in competitive market?**
A: Strategy: (1) **Brand:** Build reputation as great place to work (through glassdoor, referrals, inbound). (2) **Sourcing:** Find talent before they job hunting (direct outreach, passive candidates). (3) **Compensation:** Competitive (75th percentile of market). (4) **Opportunity:** Growth opportunity (top talent want to grow). (5) **Culture:** Mission-driven, healthy culture. (6) **Interview experience:** Great candidates expect great interview experience. (7) **Referrals:** Your team is best source (incentivize referrals). Avoid: Job description that could apply to anyone.

**Q2: How do you structure compensation fairly?**
A: Framework: (1) **Market rate:** Benchmark position against market. (2) **Internal equity:** Similar roles should have similar pay (avoid huge ranges). (3) **Transparent:** Consider publishing pay bands (reduces arguing). (4) **Progression:** Clear path to higher pay through experience/impact. (5) **Equity:** All team should have upside (not just executives). (6) **Review:** Adjust annually for inflation/merit. (7) **Equity vs. cash:** Startups: more equity, less cash. Growth: balanced. Mature: mostly cash. Decision: Budget 50-60% of revenue on people; most goes to compensation.

**Q3: What's your approach to performance management?**
A: Modern approach: (1) **Frequent feedback:** Weekly 1-on-1s (not just annual reviews). (2) **Clear expectations:** Employee knows what success looks like. (3) **Development:** Focus on growth (feedback helps people improve). (4) **Merit:** Pay/promotion based on performance. (5) **Continuous improvement:** Manager and employee continuous learning. (6) **Alignment:** Employee goals aligned with company goals. (7) **Conversation:** Manager-employee dialogue; not top-down judgment. Avoid: Forced curves (not all people can be top performers; healthy organization has distribution).
