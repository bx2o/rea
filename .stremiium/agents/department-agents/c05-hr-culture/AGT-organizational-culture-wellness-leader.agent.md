# SECTION 4: FIFTY+ FAQS, SKILLS & KNOWLEDGE LIBRARY

## 4.1 Fifty+ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### **Category 1: CULTURE STRATEGY (14 FAQs)**

**Q1: What's company culture and why does it matter?**
A: Culture: The "way we do things here"—values, behaviors, norms, how people interact. Why: (1) **Retention:** People stay if they fit culture and feel valued. (2) **Performance:** Culture aligned with strategy = better execution. (3) **Recruitment:** Strong culture attracts talent. (4) **Innovation:** Open, inclusive culture encourages ideas. (5) **Decision-making:** Shared values guide decisions (don't need rulebook). (6) **Brand:** Culture shapes how company seen externally. Strong culture = competitive advantage. Weak culture = turnover, dysfunction, poor execution.

**Q2: How do you define company values?**
A: Process: (1) **Assess current:** What are we already doing? What do we care about? (2) **Leadership input:** CEO + leadership define what matters. (3) **Employee input:** Ask employees what values should guide company. (4) **External:** Industry norms, customer needs, market. (5) **Test:** Do values resonate? Are they actionable? (6) **Communicate:** Share values; reinforce constantly. (7) **Live them:** Leadership models values; hire for alignment. Effective values: 3-5 values (not 10), memorable, actionable, authentic. Examples: "Ownership," "Customer obsession," "Continuous learning," "Diversity," "Transparency."

**Q3: What's eNPS (employee Net Promoter Score) and why matters?**
A: eNPS: Survey question (single): "How likely would you recommend company as place to work? (0-10 scale)." Calculation: % promoters (9-10) minus % detractors (0-6). Scoring: 50+ = excellent, 30-50 = good, 0-30 = improve. Why: (1) **Predictive:** eNPS predicts turnover (low eNPS = high turnover risk). (2) **Simple:** One question = high response rate. (3) **Action:** Detailed survey asks why; use to improve. (4) **Benchmark:** Compare to industry standards. Track quarterly. If declining = culture issue developing; address early.

**Q4: How do you build inclusive culture?**
A: Strategy: (1) **Intentional hiring:** Recruit diverse candidates. (2) **Inclusive language:** Use inclusive language (pronouns, names, examples). (3) **Representation:** Ensure leadership reflects diversity. (4) **Belonging:** Everyone feels they belong (not just tolerated). (5) **Voice:** Create forums where diverse voices heard. (6) **Accountability:** Track diversity metrics; hold leaders accountable. (7) **Education:** Training on bias, inclusion. (8) **Listening:** Act on feedback from underrepresented groups. (9) **Continuous:** Culture takes time; ongoing not one-time effort. Result: Diverse AND inclusive (diversity alone = not enough if not inclusive).

**Q5: How do you address toxic behavior or bad cultural fit?**
A: Approach: (1) **Early:** Address immediately (silence = acceptance). (2) **Clear:** Person should understand behavior unacceptable. (3) **Consequences:** Progressive discipline (warning, improvement plan, termination). (4) **Support:** Help person change if possible. (5) **Protect:** Protect victim; don't blame victim. (6) **Transparency:** Rest of team understands action taken (builds trust). (7) **Documentation:** Document everything (legal protection). (8) **Cultural message:** Other employees see behavior not tolerated. Note: Bad actors damage culture more than good people improve it. Move fast on termination if person won't change.

**Q6: How do you measure and improve engagement?**
A: Approach: (1) **Survey:** Annual engagement survey (trust, communication, growth, belonging, purpose). (2) **Pulse:** Quarterly mini-surveys (track trends). (3) **Analysis:** What's driving low engagement? Low scores on which dimensions? (4) **Focus groups:** Dive deeper on low-scoring areas. (5) **Action planning:** Leadership team decides: What will we do to improve? (6) **Communication:** Transparent about findings + planned improvements. (7) **Follow-up:** Execute plans. Re-survey to see improvement. (8) **Owners:** Assign owner for each improvement initiative (accountability). Key: Survey only valuable if you act on results (if not, erodes trust).

**Q7: What's a great wellness program?**
A: Program covers: (1) **Physical health:** Gym membership, health screenings, nutrition, standing desks. (2) **Mental health:** Counseling (EAP), meditation, mental health days. (3) **Financial wellness:** Retirement planning, financial literacy, debt management. (4) **Work-life balance:** Flexible schedule, generous time-off, clear boundaries. (5) **Prevention:** Preventative care (vaccines, screenings) cheaper than treatment. (6) **Accessibility:** Easy to access (no stigma for mental health). (7) **Communication:** Tell people about programs (many don't know). (8) **Personalization:** Different people need different support. Cost: 0.5-2% of payroll typical. ROI: High (reduces healthcare costs, sick days, turnover).

**Q8: How do you support remote teams and build connection?**
A: Approach: (1) **Intentional:** Don't assume remote = disconnected; be intentional. (2) **Sync + async:** Meetings (sync) but also async collaboration (Slack, docs, etc.). (3) **Regular connections:** Team meetings, one-on-ones, informal chats. (4) **Virtual events:** Online team building, social events, celebrations. (5) **In-person:** Annual or quarterly in-person gatherings for connection. (6) **Informal:** Encourage coffee chats, interest groups, mentoring across time zones. (7) **Clear processes:** Asynchronous-first processes (include people across time zones). (8) **Inclusion:** Include remote employees in decisions and social events. Challenge: Can take more intentionality than co-located teams. Advantage: Can build geographically distributed teams.

**Q9: How do you handle departing employees well?**
A: Process: (1) **Exit interview:** Ask: Why leaving? What could we improve? (2) **Offboarding:** Clear process for knowledge transfer. (3) **Stay bonus:** For certain people, offer incentive to stay (may prevent good resignations). (4) **Transition:** Help person transition; don't burn bridges. (5) **Reference:** Build relationship for future references. (6) **Alumni network:** Stay in touch; they might come back or refer people. (7) **Learning:** Aggregate departing employee feedback; share with leadership (themes?). (8) **Message:** How company handles departures signals culture (treat people well = positive word-of-mouth). Note: How you treat people leaving is as important as hiring.

**Q10: How do you build belonging for underrepresented groups?**
A: Approach: (1) **Listen:** Ask underrepresented employees: What would help you feel like you belong? (2) **Sponsorship:** Senior leaders sponsor high-potential people from underrepresented groups. (3) **Mentoring:** Formal mentoring programs pairing with senior mentors. (4) **Networks:** Employee resource groups (ERGs) for community and support. (5) **Representation:** Ensure leadership/hiring reflects diversity. (6) **Explicit:** Make clear we value diversity; discrimination not tolerated. (7) **Education:** Train on bias, inclusion. (8) **Accountability:** Track belonging metrics; hold leaders accountable. (9) **Continuous:** Ongoing not one-time effort. Result: Underrepresented employees feel they belong (not "diversity hire" but valued member).

**Q11: What's the role of celebration and recognition in culture?**
A: Importance: (1) **Motivation:** Recognition motivates people to do good work. (2) **Culture:** What you celebrate = what you value. (3) **Connection:** Celebrating together builds community. (4) **Morale:** Celebrations lift mood and energy. (5) **Retention:** Recognized people more likely to stay. Recognition approaches: (1) **Peer recognition:** Colleagues recognize colleagues (frequent, informal). (2) **Team recognition:** Public at team meetings (more formal). (3) **Company recognition:** All-company celebrations (big wins, milestones). (4) **Informal:** Casual appreciation (thank you, shout-outs). (5) **Formal:** Bonuses, promotions, awards. Range: From simple (thank you) to elaborate (celebrations, bonuses). Key: Authentic and frequent better than rare and forced.

**Q12: How do you handle conflict and maintain culture?**
A: Approach: (1) **Nip early:** Address conflict early before toxic. (2) **Listen:** Understand both sides; don't assume. (3) **Mediation:** For interpersonal conflicts, facilitate resolution. (4) **Clear expectations:** People should know behavior expectations. (5) **Consequences:** Enforce consequences fairly and consistently. (6) **Repair:** After conflict, help people repair relationship. (7) **Learning:** What can we learn to prevent future conflict? (8) **Privacy:** Handle conflicts privately (protect dignity). Note: Unaddressed conflict = toxic culture. Fast, fair resolution = trusting culture. Manager role critical; support managers in handling conflicts.

**Q13: How do you sustain culture as company scales?**
A: Challenge: Culture works easily at 10 people; harder at 100+. Approach: (1) **Intentional:** Don't assume culture scales automatically; be intentional. (2) **Communication:** Over-communicate values (repetition needed). (3) **Hiring:** Culture fit in hiring = critical as you scale. (4) **Leadership:** Leaders model and reinforce values. (5) **Systems:** Processes that reinforce values (recognition, hiring, discipline). (6) **Diversity of backgrounds:** Intentionally bring in people who think differently (growth) while maintaining core values (stability). (7) **Evolution:** Culture evolves; that's okay (adapt to company stage). (8) **Monitoring:** Regular surveys to track culture health. Key: Culture doesn't happen by accident; it's cultivated.

**Q14: What's the connection between culture and business performance?**
A: Research shows: (1) **Engagement → Productivity:** Engaged employees ~ 20% more productive. (2) **Culture → Retention:** Good culture = lower turnover. (3) **Retention → Experience:** Long-tenure employees more effective. (4) **Trust → Operations:** Trust culture = faster decisions, better execution. (5) **Innovation:** Open culture encourages experimentation and ideas. (6) **Recruitment:** Strong culture = attracts talent, reduces recruiting cost. (7) **Brand:** Culture impacts external brand; employees are brand ambassadors. (8) **Bottom line:** Good culture ~15-20% revenue uplift (through many mechanisms). Invest in culture = invest in business performance.
