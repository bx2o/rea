# SECTION 3: KEY FAQS

**Q1: What's a good time-to-hire benchmark?**
A: Target: <30 days (industry average 40-50 days). Time to hire includes: Job posting to first phone screen (1-2 weeks), screening to interviews (1 week), interviews to offer (1-2 weeks), offer to start (2 weeks). To reduce: (1) Speed each step. (2) Build candidate pipeline (have candidates ready). (3) Fast decision-making. (4) Parallel interviews.

**Q2: How do you build a strong recruitment pipeline?**
A: Approach: (1) **Proactive sourcing:** Don't wait for openings; source continuously. (2) **Relationship building:** Keep candidate relationships warm (even if not hiring now). (3) **Employee referrals:** Incentivize employee referrals (usually cheaper, faster). (4) **University recruiting:** Build relationships with schools. (5) **Network:** Build your own network (alumni networks, industry connections). (6) **Outreach:** Regular outreach to candidates about opportunities.

**Q3: How do you assess candidate quality?**
A: Multi-dimensional: (1) **Skills:** Technical/work sample tests. (2) **Experience:** Past performance, trajectory. (3) **Cultural fit:** Values alignment, behavioral interview. (4) **Motivation:** Why they want this role, company. (5) **Growth potential:** Ability to scale with company. (6) **References:** 360-degree input from past colleagues. (7) **Interview performance:** How they present, communicate, problem-solve. Avoid: Over-reliance on one factor.

**Q4: How do you compete for top talent against big tech?**
A: Play to your strengths: (1) **Mission:** Impact, purpose, meaningful work. (2) **Growth:** Faster career progression, more responsibility. (3) **Equity:** Meaningful ownership upside. (4) **Culture:** Collaborative, not bureaucratic. (5) **Flexibility:** Remote work, flexible hours. (6) **Transparency:** Open communication, access to leadership. (7) **People:** Quality of team they'll work with. Sell: "Join us to do the best work of your career with people you love working with."

# SECTION 4: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

## SOP 1: Job Requisition Intake

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes | **Frequency:** Per new role

1. **Intake meeting:** Meet with hiring manager to understand role
2. **Requirements gathering:** Skills, experience, competencies needed
3. **Success profile:** Define what success looks like in role
4. **Approval process:** Get headcount and compensation approval
5. **Job description:** Draft and finalize JD with hiring manager
6. **ATS setup:** Create requisition in recruiting system
7. **Sourcing strategy:** Plan where to find candidates

## SOP 2: Candidate Sourcing

**Duration:** Ongoing | **Frequency:** Daily/Weekly per role

1. **Channel selection:** Choose sourcing channels (LinkedIn, job boards, referrals)
2. **Boolean search:** Create effective search strings
3. **Outbound outreach:** Send personalized messages to candidates
4. **Inbound management:** Review applications, screen resumes
5. **Pipeline building:** Keep warm candidates for future roles
6. **Diversity sourcing:** Target underrepresented candidate pools
7. **Tracking:** Log all candidate interactions in ATS

## SOP 3: Interview Process Management

**Duration:** 2-4 weeks per candidate | **Frequency:** Per candidate

1. **Phone screen:** 30-minute initial screening call
2. **Skills assessment:** Technical or work sample evaluation
3. **Interview panel:** Coordinate team interviews
4. **Debrief sessions:** Gather feedback from interviewers
5. **Reference checks:** Contact 2-3 professional references
6. **Decision meeting:** Final evaluation with hiring manager
7. **Offer preparation:** Draft offer letter with compensation details

## SOP 4: Offer Negotiation & Closing

**Duration:** 1-7 days | **Frequency:** Per selected candidate

1. **Offer presentation:** Present verbal offer with enthusiasm
2. **Q&A session:** Address candidate questions and concerns
3. **Negotiation:** Handle counter-offers within guidelines
4. **Written offer:** Send official offer letter
5. **Follow-up:** Check in during candidate decision period
6. **Acceptance:** Celebrate and begin onboarding coordination
7. **Decline analysis:** Learn from declined offers
