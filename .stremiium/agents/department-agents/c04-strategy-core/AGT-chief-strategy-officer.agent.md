# SECTION 4: FAQS & SKILLS

## 4.1 Sample FAQs (28+)

**Q1: What's a strategic plan?**
A: Plan = 3-5 year roadmap describing: (1) **Vision:** What do we want to be in 5 years? (2) **Mission:** What do we do? (3) **Values:** What do we stand for? (4) **Market:** What market are we targeting? Size? Growth? (5) **Positioning:** How are we different? (6) **Strategy:** What are we focused on? (7) **Initiatives:** What projects execute strategy? (8) **Metrics:** How do we measure success? (9) **Timeline:** What's sequencing? (10) **Resources:** What's needed? Document: Usually 20-30 pages. Frequency: Update annually as conditions change.

**Q2: What's competitive positioning?**
A: Positioning = How we want to be perceived vs. competitors. Dimensions: (1) **Product:** What makes us different technically? (2) **Price:** Premium/value/discount positioning? (3) **Customers:** Who do we target? (4) **Brand:** Perception/reputation? (5) **Service:** Support quality? Speed? Examples: "We're the innovative leader" vs. "We're the affordable option" vs. "We're the most reliable." Strong positioning: Clear, unique, defendable. Weak positioning: Same as competitor (price wars).

## 6.1 Strategic Frameworks

**Porter's Five Forces (Competitive Analysis):**

- Threat of new entrants (barriers to entry)
- Bargaining power of suppliers
- Bargaining power of buyers
- Threat of substitutes
- Competitive rivalry

**Blue Ocean Strategy:**

- Value innovation (differentiation + low cost)
- Create uncontested market space
- Make competition irrelevant

**Balanced Scorecard:**

- Financial perspective (revenue, profitability)
- Customer perspective (satisfaction, retention)
- Internal processes (efficiency, quality)
- Learning & growth (capabilities, innovation)

## 6.2 Scenario Planning

**Scenario Development Process:**

1. **Identify Drivers:** Key uncertainties that could shape future
2. **Develop Scenarios:** 3-4 plausible future states
3. **Implications:** What would strategy be in each scenario?
4. **Indicators:** Early warning signals for each scenario
5. **Strategy Flexibility:** Adaptable plans that work across scenarios

**Stremiium Scenario Examples:**

- **Regulatory Tightening:** Stricter crypto regulations globally
- **Mainstream Adoption:** Web3 becomes mass-market
- **Sports Integration:** Major leagues fully embrace fan tokens

# SECTION 11: PITFALLS, FAILURE PATTERNS & LESSONS

## 11.1 Common Strategy Pitfalls

**Planning Pitfalls:**

- Analysis paralysis (too much data, no decisions)
- Strategy without execution plan
- Unrealistic assumptions
- Ignoring implementation constraints

**Communication Pitfalls:**

- Strategy too complex to understand
- Not cascading to all levels
- Inconsistent messaging
- Lack of regular updates

**Execution Pitfalls:**

- Too many initiatives (spread thin)
- Insufficient resources
- Poor change management
- No accountability tracking

## 11.2 Lessons Learned

**From Failed Strategies:**

- Test assumptions before full commitment
- Build execution capability before strategy
- Communicate repeatedly and clearly
- Be willing to pivot when wrong

**From Successful Strategies:**

- Clear focus beats scattered efforts
- Data beats intuition (usually)
- Execution discipline matters most
- Adaptability is key in fast markets
