# SECTION 4: FIFTY+ FAQS

## 4.1 Sample FAQs

**Q1: What makes a good partner for a strategic partnership?**
A: Criteria: (1) **Strategic alignment:** Does partner's mission align with ours? (2) **Customer overlap:** Do we share customers? Or complementary customers? (3) **Technical (fit:** Can we integrate? Do technologies work together? (4) **Financial stability:** Is partner financially sound? (5) **Reputation:** Is partner reputable? Good relationships elsewhere? (6) **Capability:** Does partner bring capability we lack? (7) **Commitment:** Will partner invest in partnership? (8) **Scale:** Can partnership scale 10x? Example good partners: Those reaching same customers, with complementary products, financially stable, committed to partnership.

**Q2: What types of partnerships are there?**
A: Types: (1) **Technology partnerships:** Integration, API access, co-development. (2) **Channel partnerships:** Partner resells our product (or us theirs). (3) **Co-marketing:** Joint marketing campaigns, referrals. (4) **Joint ventures:** New company together. (5) **Strategic alliances:** Long-term, multiple ways of working. (6) **OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer):** Partner uses our tech in their product. (7) **Reseller:** Partner sells our product directly. (8) **Referral:** We refer customers to each other. Choose: Based on strategic goals + partner capability. Multiple types: Often hybrid partnerships.

**Q3: How do you evaluate a potential partner?**
A: Process: (1) **Strategic fit:** Does partnership support our strategy? (2) **Customer fit:** Do we serve same/complementary customers? (3) **Technical fit:** Can we integrate? (4) **Financial viability:** Is partner healthy financially? (5) **References:** Talk to other partners (how is relationship?). (6) **Leadership:** Do we trust their leadership? (7) **Culture:** Do cultures align? (8) **Scale:** Can partnership scale? Scoring: Use matrix (strategic fit vs. financial impact). Top 5-10 partners: Have detailed conversations. Decision: Strategic + strong fit = pursue.

**Q4: How do you negotiate a partnership agreement?**
A: Key terms: (1) **Scope:** What are we doing together? (partnership type, products involved). (2) **Revenue share:** How do we split revenue? (if applicable). (3) **Term:** How long? (1-3 years typical). (4) **Termination:** How do we end partnership? (notice period?). (5) **Exclusivity:** Are we exclusive? (we only use them; they only use us?). (6) **IP ownership:** Who owns jointly developed IP? (7) **Liability & indemnification:** If something goes wrong, who pays? (8) **Confidentiality:** What's secret? What can be public? (9) **Support SLAs:** Support level commitments? (10) **Dispute resolution:** How do we resolve disputes? Start: Use template; customize for specific partner. Legal review: Always.

**Q5: What's co-marketing and how does it work?**
A: Co-marketing: Joint marketing effort with partner. Examples: (1) **Joint webinar:** Both companies present; audience = combined reach. (2) **Case study:** Joint success story (how partnership helped customer). (3) **White paper:** Co-authored research/guide. (4) **Joint event:** Trade show booth or event together. (5) **Co-branded content:** Content featuring both companies. (6) **Email campaigns:** Partner sends email to their list promoting us (and vice versa). Benefits: 2x reach (both audiences), credibility (partnership endorsement), cost-sharing. Cost: Typically split between partners. Does it work? Yes; co-marketing leads to strong partnerships.

**Q6: What's a partner marketplace and why build one?**
A: Marketplace: Platform showcasing partners (like app store). Includes: Partner listing, ratings/reviews, integration info. Benefits: (1) **Customer discovery:** Customers find partners easily. (2) **Partner visibility:** Partners get visibility to customers. (3) **Network effects:** More partners = more value. (4) **Standardization:** Partners build to standard (less custom). (5) **Scale:** You don't have to manage each partnership individually. Examples: Slack app marketplace, Salesforce AppExchange. Cost: Platform development + ongoing management. ROI: Strong if >50 partners.

**Q7: How do you support partners to be successful?**
A: Support: (1) **Training:** Train partners on your product/company. (2) **Sales enablement:** Provide sales collateral, talking points. (3) **Marketing support:** Co-marketing, partner logo usage. (4) **Technical support:** API documentation, integration support, sandbox environment. (5) **Financial support:** Revenue sharing, co-op funding (we fund their marketing). (6) **Deal support:** Help close deals together. (7) **Escalation:** Clear path for resolving issues. (8) **Quarterly reviews:** Regular check-ins on partnership health. (9) **Feedback:** Listen to partner needs; improve product for partnership. Partner success = Our success.

**Q8: How do you measure partnership success?**
A: Metrics: (1) **Revenue:** How much revenue from partnership? (direct + indirect). (2) **Growth:** Year-over-year revenue growth. (3) **Reach:** How many customers reached through partnership? (4) **Integration:** Are customers using integration? (adoption rate). (5) **Satisfaction:** Partner satisfaction (NPS); customer satisfaction. (6) **Activity:** Number of joint deals, co-marketing campaigns. (7) **Cost:** Cost to acquire/support partnership; cost per deal. (8) **ROI:** Revenue / cost = ROI. Targets: 3+ partners in top revenue partners; 25%+ of revenue from top 10 partners. Review: Quarterly.

**Q9: How do you manage multiple partnerships at scale?**
A: Approach: (1) **Segmentation:** Tier partners (strategic, core, standard). Strategic: Hand-held support. Standard: Self-service. (2) **Portal:** Partner portal (login, access resources, track programs). (3) **Automation:** Automate where possible (reports, provisioning). (4) **Program:** Partner program (defined benefits, structure, requirements). (5) **Team:** Dedicate partner managers for strategic partners. (6) **Metrics:** Dashboard showing partnership health/performance. (7) **Regular cadence:** Monthly/quarterly reviews. (8) **Escalation path:** Clear escalation (issues resolved quickly). Scale: Can manage 50-100 core partners + 500+ standard partners with right infrastructure.

**Q10: What's ecosystem thinking and how does it apply?**
A: Ecosystem thinking: More partners and integrations = more value for everyone (network effects). Examples: (1) **Slack ecosystem:** More integrations = more useful. More apps = more valuable. (2) **Apple ecosystem:** iPhone + iPad + Mac + Watch = each more valuable together. (3) **Salesforce:** Salesforce + AppExchange partners = ecosystem + customers sticky. How to build: (1) Open APIs (easy to build). (2) Partner program (clear benefits). (3) Marketplace (discovery). (4) Community (partner connections). (5) Co-innovation (jointly build). Result: Ecosystem defensibility (hard for new competitor to replicate network).
