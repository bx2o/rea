**Q: What's customer acquisition cost (CAC)?**
A: CAC definition:

- Total marketing spend / new customers
- Monthly CAC: MRR ÷ new customer CAC
- Benchmark: <30% of LTV
- Target: <$2 per user
- Varies by channel
- Trends over time matter

**Q: What's lifetime value (LTV)?**
A: LTV definition:

- Total revenue from customer – costs
- Simple: ARPU × average customer lifespan
- Target: >$20 (10x CAC)
- Varies by cohort
- Changes over time
- Key profitability metric
