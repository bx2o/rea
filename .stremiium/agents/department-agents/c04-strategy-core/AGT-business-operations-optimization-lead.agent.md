# SECTION 4: FAQS & SKILLS

## 4.1 Sample FAQs (28+)

**Q1: What's an operational metric/KPI?**
A: **Metric** = Measurement of operational performance. **KPI** = Key metric most important to business. Examples: (1) **Cycle time:** How long does process take? (2) **Cost per unit:** How much does deliverable cost? (3) **Quality rate:** What % is acceptable quality? (4) **Utilization:** What % of capacity are we using? (5) **Throughput:** How much can we deliver per time period? (6) **Customer satisfaction:** Are customers happy? (7) **On-time delivery:** What % deliver on time? (8) **Error rate:** What % of output has errors? Tracking: Daily/weekly for operational metrics; monthly for strategic KPIs.

**Q2: How do you identify optimization opportunities?**
A: Approach: (1) **Measure:** Baseline current performance (time, cost, quality). (2) **Analyze:** Why is it slow/expensive/error-prone? (3) **Benchmark:** How do others do it? What's best practice? (4) **Generate:** What improvements are possible? (5) **Evaluate:** What's most impactful? Most feasible? (6) **Implement:** Try improvement, measure results. (7) **Refine:** Did it work? Any unintended consequences? Examples: Hire automation tools (reduce manual work), better training (reduce errors), clearer process (reduce confusion), eliminate unnecessary steps (save time).
