# SECTION 3: ADVISORY OPERATIONS & PROCEDURES

## 3.1 Advisory Request Process

**Standard Advisory Request:**

1. Captain/Leader identifies decision requiring advisory input
2. Submits formal advisory request with context and timeline
3. Route to appropriate advisor(s)
4. Advisor conducts research and analysis (48-72 hours typical)
5. Advisor provides written brief with recommendations
6. Advisor available for follow-up discussion if needed
7. Leader incorporates into decision-making
8. Advisory Council tracks outcomes for learning

**Urgent Advisory Requests:**

- Submit "URGENT" flag with 24-hour response target
- Advisor provides preliminary analysis within 24 hours
- Deeper analysis as time permits
- Typically leverages existing research and frameworks

**Standing Advisory Processes:**

- Monthly organizational health briefing
- Quarterly competitive landscape briefing
- Quarterly financial performance review
- Quarterly risk and compliance assessment

## 3.2 Advisory Working Groups

**Topic-Based Working Groups:**

- Emerging advisors form working groups on major topics
- Conduct in-depth research and generate recommendations
- Present findings to captains for decision-making
- Ongoing research groups update regularly

**Example Working Groups:**

- Strategic Technology Investment Priorities
- Organizational Capability Gap Analysis
- Market Expansion Feasibility Study
- Risk and Compliance Framework Review
- Organizational Culture and Engagement Enhancement

## 3.3 Advisory Council Meetings

**Frequency:** Bi-weekly coordination + Monthly strategic planning

**Bi-Weekly Coordination Meetings:**

- All 12 advisors + Advisor Coordinator
- Share recent analysis and findings
- Identify cross-advisor dependencies
- Coordinate on current advisory requests
- Duration: 1-2 hours

**Monthly Strategic Planning:**

- All advisors + Agents Council Captain
- Discuss emerging trends and themes
- Plan standing analyses and research
- Coordinate with captains on major decisions
- Duration: 2-3 hours

# SECTION 4: ADVISORY GOVERNANCE & STANDARDS

## 4.1 Advisory Integrity Standards

**Core Principles:**

- **Independence:** Advisors give honest advice regardless of consequences
- **Expertise:** All recommendations grounded in specialized knowledge
- **Research-Based:** Recommendations based on data and analysis
- **Balanced:** Present multiple perspectives and trade-offs
- **Confidential:** Maintain confidentiality of advisory discussions
- **Professional:** Follow professional ethics and standards

**Advisory Boundaries:**

- Advisors recommend, not decide
- Advisors never have veto authority
- Advisors defer to council captains on final decisions
- Advisors maintain independence from council politics
- Advisors available to all captains equally

## 4.2 Advisory Quality Standards

**Analysis Quality Requirements:**

- All major analyses peer-reviewed by 2nd advisor
- Recommendations grounded in credible research
- Alternative options presented explicitly
- Trade-offs and risks clearly identified
- Sources cited and verifiable
- Recommendations explained with clear rationale

**Timeliness Standards:**

- Standard requests: 48-72 hours
- Urgent requests: 24 hours preliminary analysis
- Standing reports: Per-established schedule
- Follow-up: Within 24 hours if needed

**Communication Standards:**

- All advice in writing with executive summary
- Complex analysis accompanied by brief verbal summary
- Visual aids used for clarity when appropriate
- Jargon minimized, technical terms explained
- Clear action-oriented recommendations
