## SECTION 1: GOVERNANCE FUNDAMENTALS

### 1.1 Governance Principles

**Core Principles** (Always Apply):

1. **Clear Authority** - Every decision has a clear authority owner
2. **Transparent Process** - All decisions documented and justified
3. **Hierarchical Resolution** - Escalate conflicts up the chain
4. **Consensus-First** - Seek agreement before deciding
5. **Data-Driven** - Decisions backed by evidence and metrics
6. **Speed-Quality Balance** - Fast decisions when appropriate, careful when needed
7. **Continuous Learning** - Document decisions and improve processes
8. **Agent Empowerment** - Enable individual agent autonomy within boundaries

**Governance Constraints** (Never Violate):

- Never make unilateral decisions affecting other councils without consultation
- Never skip escalation procedures for critical issues
- Never override documented governance rules without explicit authority
- Never hide information or create information silos
- Never make decisions conflicting with organizational values
- Never prioritize speed over security or compliance
- Never let individual preferences override organizational needs
- Never fail to communicate decisions to affected parties

### 1.2 Authority Hierarchy

**4-Level Authority Structure**:

**Level 1: EXECUTIVE (Sparkling Curious)**

- Authority: Organizational strategy, multi-council decisions, final arbitration
- Scope: All 16 councils, organizational-level decisions
- Decision Timeline: 15-20 business days (urgent: 48 hours)
- Escalation: Executive board (external)
- Responsibilities:
  - Strategic direction and alignment
  - Multi-council conflict resolution
  - Authority delegations
  - Budget allocation oversight
  - Crisis management

**Level 2: COMMAND (Council Captains)**

- Authority: Council-level strategy, team management, SLA accountability
- Scope: Within own council and direct reports
- Decision Timeline: 5-10 business days
- Escalation: Sparkling Curious
- Responsibilities:
  - Council operations and strategy
  - Agent assignment and management
  - Cross-council coordination
  - Performance accountability
  - Quality oversight

**Level 3: OPERATIONAL (Senior Agents/Specialists)**

- Authority: Tactical decisions, problem-solving, expert judgment
- Scope: Own domain/specialty
- Decision Timeline: 1-3 business days
- Escalation: Council captain
- Responsibilities:
  - Technical implementation
  - Expert guidance
  - Quality delivery
  - Issue resolution
  - Knowledge sharing

**Level 4: INDIVIDUAL (All Agents)**

- Authority: Daily tasks, personal accountability, feedback
- Scope: Assigned work within clear parameters
- Decision Timeline: Daily
- Escalation: Immediate supervisor
- Responsibilities:
  - Task completion
  - Quality work
  - Communication
  - Continuous learning
  - Problem escalation

## SECTION 2: COUNCIL DECISION FRAMEWORK

### 2.1 Decision Categories

**Strategic Decisions** (Executive Level)

- Definition: Affects multiple councils or organizational direction
- Authority: Sparkling Curious (with council input)
- Process: Multi-council review, consensus-seeking
- Timeline: 15-20 business days
- Documentation: Decision log with justification
- Example: Entering new market, major feature strategy, org restructure

**Operational Decisions** (Command Level)

- Definition: Affects specific council or team
- Authority: Council captain (own council)
- Process: Consultation with affected parties
- Timeline: 5-10 business days
- Documentation: Decision log with alternatives considered
- Example: Hiring, resource allocation, project prioritization

**Tactical Decisions** (Operational Level)

- Definition: Implementation-level, within known parameters
- Authority: Senior agents/specialists
- Process: Expert judgment, team input
- Timeline: 1-3 business days
- Documentation: Brief rationale in task notes
- Example: Coding approach, testing strategy, technical implementation

**Emergency Decisions** (Fast-track)

- Definition: Time-sensitive, potential business impact
- Authority: Lowest qualified authority that can act immediately
- Process: Act first, review later (within 24 hours)
- Timeline: Real-time to 2 hours
- Documentation: Comprehensive post-decision review
- Example: Security incident, system outage, critical customer issue

### 2.2 Decision-Making Process

**Standard Decision Process** (applicable to all levels):

```
1. IDENTIFY DECISION REQUIRED
   └─ What decision needs to be made?
   └─ What authority level is this?
   └─ Who are stakeholders?

2. INFORMATION GATHERING
   └─ Collect relevant data
   └─ Consult experts/stakeholders
   └─ Review historical context
   └─ Identify alternatives

3. ANALYSIS & EVALUATION
   └─ Analyze each alternative
   └─ Consider pros/cons
   └─ Evaluate risks/tradeoffs
   └─ Align with organizational goals

4. CONSENSUS-SEEKING
   └─ Propose to stakeholders
   └─ Gather feedback
   └─ Negotiate if conflicts
   └─ Seek agreement if possible

5. DECISION MAKING
   └─ Authority makes final call
   └─ Document justification
   └─ Communicate clearly
   └─ Assign implementation

6. IMPLEMENTATION
   └─ Assign clear owners
   └─ Set timelines
   └─ Monitor progress
   └─ Support execution

7. REVIEW & LEARNING
   └─ Assess outcomes
   └─ Compare to expectations
   └─ Document lessons learned
   └─ Update processes if needed
```

### 2.3 Conflict Resolution

**Conflict Escalation Procedure** (7-step):

```
STEP 1: IDENTIFY CONFLICT
├─ Two or more parties/councils with differing positions
├─ Decision cannot be made without resolution
└─ Document: parties, positions, stakes

STEP 2: DIRECT NEGOTIATION
├─ Parties meet directly
├─ Each presents full position
├─ Seek to find common ground
├─ Timeline: 2-3 business days
└─ If resolved: DONE. Document agreement.

STEP 3: MEDIATION (if Step 2 fails)
├─ Neutral party from Sparkling Curious team mediates
├─ Each party presents case
├─ Mediator proposes potential resolutions
├─ Timeline: 2-3 business days
└─ If resolved: Document and implement

STEP 4: ARBITRATION BY RELEVANT AUTHORITY
├─ If parties involved are same tier: escalate 1 level up
├─ Authority reviews all positions
├─ Authority makes binding decision
├─ Timeline: 3-5 business days
└─ Document decision with justification

STEP 5: SPARKLING CURIOUS ARBITRATION (if needed)
├─ For multi-council or organizational conflicts
├─ Sparkling Curious reviews all evidence
├─ Makes final binding decision
├─ Timeline: 5-10 business days
└─ Decision is final unless overridden by executive board

STEP 6: IMPLEMENT DECISION
├─ All parties accept decision
├─ Implementation timeline set
├─ Progress tracked
└─ Compliance monitored

STEP 7: LEARNING & PREVENTION
├─ Post-mortem on conflict
├─ Identify root cause
├─ Update processes to prevent
├─ Share learnings across councils
└─ Update decision frameworks
```

## SECTION 3: COUNCIL OPERATIONAL STANDARDS

### 3.1 Communication Standards

**All Councils Must**:

- Communicate decisions to affected parties within 48 hours
- Use Notion as single source of truth
- Document all major decisions
- Escalate critical issues within 2 hours
- Participate in weekly cross-council sync
- Provide monthly status to Sparkling Curious
- Respond to queries from other councils within 24 hours
- Share knowledge and lessons learned
- Be transparent about constraints and blockers

**Communication Formats**:

- Strategic: Notion page with full context
- Operational: Notion update with action tracking
- Tactical: Inline comments in task system
- Emergency: Direct Slack + follow-up Notion update
- Cross-Council: Weekly async updates + monthly sync

### 3.2 SLA Standards (Defined per Council)

**Response Time SLAs**:

- Critical Issues: 2-hour response
- High Priority: 4-24 hour response
- Medium Priority: 1-5 day response
- Low Priority: 1-2 week response

**Completion SLAs**:

- Target: 95%+ of tasks completed within SLA
- Escalation: Issues missing SLA escalated automatically
- Review: Monthly SLA performance review
- Adjustment: SLAs adjusted quarterly based on actual data

### 3.3 Quality Standards

**All Deliverables Must Meet**:

- Code: 80%+ test coverage, security review passed, performance validated
- Documentation: Complete, accurate, peer-reviewed
- Decisions: Documented with justification and alternatives considered
- Communications: Clear, timely, action-oriented
- Escalations: Appropriate severity level, complete context

### 3.4 Performance Expectations

**Individual Agents**:

- Task completion: 95%+ of assigned tasks completed on time
- Quality: 85%+ quality score across deliverables
- Communication: Respond to queries within 24 hours
- Learning: Continuous skill development and capability growth
- Collaboration: Effective cross-council coordination

**Council Level**:

- SLA adherence: 98%+ of tasks meet SLA
- Quality: Average quality score 85%+ across all deliverables
- Escalation: Critical issues escalated within 2 hours
- Collaboration: Active participation in cross-council initiatives
- Innovation: Continuous process improvement and capability enhancement

**Organizational Level**:

- Task completion: 95%+ of all organizational tasks completed
- Quality: 85%+ average quality across all domains
- SLA: 98%+ SLA adherence organization-wide
- Satisfaction: Agent satisfaction 8/10 or higher
- Health: System health score 90%+ (uptime, performance, security)

## SECTION 7: EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS

### 7.1 Crisis Response Authority

**Crisis Level 1: CRITICAL** (Business impact, security, regulatory)

- Response Authority: Sparkling Curious (immediate)
- Response Timeline: Real-time action
- Notification: All affected councils within 30 minutes
- Communication: Real-time Slack + formal follow-up
- Escalation: Executive board if needed
- Post-mortem: Within 48 hours

**Crisis Level 2: HIGH** (Major degradation, customer impact)

- Response Authority: Scout or Sparkling Curious
- Response Timeline: Within 2 hours
- Notification: Within 1 hour
- Communication: Slack + Notion update
- Root cause analysis: Within 24 hours
- Post-mortem: Within 72 hours

**Crisis Level 3: MEDIUM** (Process breakdown, inefficiency)

- Response Authority: Council captain + Scout
- Response Timeline: Within 4 hours
- Notification: Within 2 hours
- Communication: Notion update
- Root cause analysis: Within 2 days

**Crisis Level 4: LOW** (Maintenance, optimization)

- Response Authority: Council captain or agent
- Response Timeline: Within 1-2 days
- Communication: Status update

### 7.2 Emergency Decision Procedure

```
1. IMMEDIATE ACTION
   └─ Take action to contain crisis
   └─ Alert affected parties
   └─ Escalate if needed

2. COMMUNICATION
   └─ Notify Sparkling Curious (30 mins)
   └─ Notify affected councils (30 mins)
   └─ Public communication if customer impact

3. INVESTIGATION
   └─ Gather facts
   └─ Understand root cause
   └─ Document timeline

4. DECISION & RESPONSE
   └─ Lowest qualified authority decides
   └─ Execute response
   └─ Monitor effectiveness

5. ESCALATION
   └─ If decision outside authority: escalate
   └─ Document reasoning

6. POST-MORTEM
   └─ Within 24-72 hours depending on severity
   └─ Full root cause analysis
   └─ Identify prevention measures
   └─ Update processes

7. LEARNING & PREVENTION
   └─ Share learnings across councils
   └─ Update decision frameworks
   └─ Implement preventive measures
```
