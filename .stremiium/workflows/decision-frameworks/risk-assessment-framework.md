# Risk Assessment Framework

**Purpose**: Systematically evaluate and prioritize risks  
**Use When**: Planning projects, making decisions, entering new markets  
**Owner**: Multiple Agents (context-dependent)  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Risk Identification

### Categories

**Technical Risks**:

- Architecture complexity
- Performance unknowns
- Scalability concerns
- Integration challenges

**Business Risks**:

- Market response
- Competitive threats
- Customer adoption
- Revenue impact

**Operational Risks**:

- Team capability
- Process maturity
- Vendor reliability
- Regulatory compliance

**Financial Risks**:

- Budget overrun
- Cost of failure
- Opportunity cost
- Cash flow impact

---

## Risk Score Matrix

```
Risk = Probability × Impact

Probability: 1-5 (unlikely to certain)
Impact: 1-5 (negligible to catastrophic)

Score 1-5: Low priority
Score 6-15: Medium priority, monitor
Score 16-25: High priority, mitigate
```

---

## Mitigation Strategies

### By Risk Type

**Technical**:

- Spike/prototype high-risk areas
- Load testing
- Security audit
- Documentation

**Business**:

- Customer interviews
- Market research
- Competitive analysis
- MVP validation

**Operational**:

- Training programs
- Process documentation
- Vendor vetting
- Compliance audit

**Financial**:

- Phased budget allocation
- ROI models
- Cost control measures
- Contingency reserve
