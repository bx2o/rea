# Product Pivot Decision Framework

**Purpose**: Evaluate changing product direction or strategy  
**Use When**: Considering major direction change, market response negative, opportunity identified  
**Owner**: The Strategist + The Rainmaker  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Pivot Types

### Pivot 1: Feature Pivot

- Focus on different core feature
- Example: Pivot from communication to analytics

### Pivot 2: Target Market Pivot

- Same product, different customer
- Example: B2C to B2B, or SMB to Enterprise

### Pivot 3: Channel Pivot

- Different sales approach
- Example: Direct sales to freemium

### Pivot 4: Architecture Pivot

- Rebuild on different platform/tech
- Example: Monolith to microservices

### Pivot 5: Complete Pivot

- Entirely new product
- Example: Photo app becomes messaging (Instagram → Instagram DM focus)

---

## Pivot Signal Detection

**Signs You Should Pivot**:

- ✓ Customer validation for alternative direction
- ✓ Metrics not improving despite efforts
- ✓ Market gap identified
- ✓ Better opportunity found
- ✓ Competitive threat identified

**Reasons NOT to Pivot**:

- ✗ Impatience (too early)
- ✗ Lack of focus ("Shiny object syndrome")
- ✗ Single customer request
- ✗ Founder boredom
- ✗ Without strong data

---

## Pivot Decision Process

```
Week 1: Hypothesis
- What would we pivot to?
- Why would this work?
- Market evidence?

Week 2: Testing
- Customer interviews
- Demand signals
- Competitive analysis

Week 3: Planning
- What must change?
- What resources needed?
- Timeline?

Week 4: Decision
- GO: Revise roadmap, communicate
- NO-GO: Refocus efforts
```

## Risk Assessment

| Factor                 | Low        | Medium       | High        |
| ---------------------- | ---------- | ------------ | ----------- |
| **Market uncertainty** | Validated  | Some signals | Unknown     |
| **Resource impact**    | 20% effort | 50% effort   | 100% effort |
| **Financial impact**   | Small      | Medium       | Existential |
| **Timeline**           | <3 months  | 3-6 months   | 6-12 months |

---

## Recommendation

**Pivot if**:

- Strong market validation exists
- Resources available
- Clear competitive opportunity
- Not just running from failure

**Stay course if**:

- Early in adoption cycle
- Metrics trending positive
- No better opportunity exists
- Unclear data
