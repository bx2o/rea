# Hiring Decision Framework

**Purpose**: Evaluate whether to hire for a role  
**Use When**: Team requests new hire, department growth, replacement hire  
**Owner**: The Guardian + Hiring Manager  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Pre-Hire Assessment

### Need Validation

- [ ] Is this role truly needed?
- [ ] Existing team member could grow into it?
- [ ] Contractor/consultant alternative?
- [ ] Process/tools could reduce need?

### Financial Justification

- Total cost (salary + benefits + taxes): $X/year
- Revenue this role generates: $Y/year
- Ratio: Should be 3:1 or better
- Payback period: < 1 year

### Timing

- Budget cycle approved?
- Resources to onboard?
- Team capacity to mentor?
- Business need urgent?

---

## Hire vs. Alternatives

| Alternative        | Cost        | Time       | Pros                   | Cons            |
| ------------------ | ----------- | ---------- | ---------------------- | --------------- |
| **Hire FTE**       | $100k+      | 2-3 months | Dedicated, culture fit | Expensive, long |
| **Contractor**     | $150-250/hr | 1-2 weeks  | Flexible, quick        | Less committed  |
| **Process change** | $10-50k     | 1-2 weeks  | Automation             | Requires time   |
| **Promote/Train**  | $5-20k      | 1-3 months | Retain talent          | Time to ramp    |

## Hiring Plan

### Role Definition

- Title and level
- 3-5 key responsibilities
- Required skills
- Nice-to-have skills
- Compensation range

### Timeline

- Week 1-2: Recruiting, applications
- Week 3-4: Initial screening
- Week 5-6: Interviews
- Week 7: Final candidates
- Week 8: Offer negotiation
- Week 9: Onboarding

### Success Criteria (3, 6, 12 months)

- Productivity metrics
- Team impact
- Cultural fit

---

## Rejection Criteria

**Don't hire if**:

- Can't articulate clear need
- Can't secure budget approval
- Team not ready to onboard
- Unclear role requirements
- Desperation driving decision
