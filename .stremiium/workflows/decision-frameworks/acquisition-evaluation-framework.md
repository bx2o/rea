# Acquisition Target Evaluation Framework

**Purpose**: Evaluate potential acquisition targets  
**Use When**: Considering acquiring another company or technology  
**Owner**: CEO + The Strategist + The Capitalist  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Pre-Evaluation Screening

### Must-Haves

- [ ] Aligns with strategy?
- [ ] Solves real problem?
- [ ] Technology differentiated?
- [ ] Team has relevant skills?

### Red Flags

- ❌ Overlapping team only (talent acquisition disguised)
- ❌ Negative cash flow
- ❌ Poor customer retention
- ❌ Hostile founders to integration
- ❌ Significant debt or lawsuits

---

## Evaluation Criteria

### Business Metrics

- Revenue: $X annual
- Growth: % YoY
- Profitability: Margin %
- Customer base: Count + churn
- Market size: TAM

### Technology Assessment

- Code quality: Assess
- Architecture: Scalable?
- Technical debt: Manageable?
- Patents/IP: Valuable?
- Security: Adequate?

### Team Assessment

- Founders: Staying?
- Engineers: Retention plan?
- Key roles: Backup available?
- Culture fit: Compatible?

### Financial Analysis

- Valuation: Fair price?
- Revenue multiples: Reasonable?
- DCF analysis: NPV positive?
- Integration costs: Estimated?

## Integration Planning

**Key considerations**:

- Founder retention terms?
- Board seat changes?
- Rebranding or consolidation?
- Engineering integration timeline?
- Customer communication strategy?
- Staff integration/cross-training?

---

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Overpaying (paying for best case scenario)
- ❌ Underestimating integration costs
- ❌ Losing key people post-close
- ❌ Cultural misalignment not addressed
- ❌ Ignoring technical debt issues
