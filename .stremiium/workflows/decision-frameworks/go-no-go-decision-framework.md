# Go/No-Go Decision Framework

**Purpose**: Make binary decision to proceed with major initiative  
**Use When**: Starting significant project, pivot decision, or market expansion  
**Owner**: The Strategist + Executive Team  
**Last Updated**: February 18, 2026

---

## Pre-Decision Checklist

### Strategy Alignment

- [ ] Aligns with company strategy?
- [ ] Clear business objective?
- [ ] Competitive advantage?
- [ ] Fits existing roadmap?

### Resource Readiness

- [ ] Team available/trained?
- [ ] Budget approved?
- [ ] Timeline realistic?
- [ ] Risks identified?

### Market Validation

- [ ] Customer need confirmed?
- [ ] Market size validated?
- [ ] Competitive landscape understood?
- [ ] Revenue model clear?

### Execution Plan

- [ ] Detailed requirements?
- [ ] Milestone defined?
- [ ] Success metrics clear?
- [ ] Escalation path obvious?

---

## Decision Criteria

### Must Haves

- Positive ROI expected?
- Aligns with strategy?
- Customer requested/validated?
- No blockers?

### Nice to Haves

- Enables future opportunities?
- Builds capabilities?
- Strengthens team?

---

## GO Decision requires:

- All "Must Haves" met ✓
- > 50% "Nice to Haves" met ✓
- Team confidence high ✓
- Resource manager approval ✓

---

## NO-GO Indicators

```
❌ Unclear business value
❌ Team is overloaded
❌ Budget not approved
❌ No customer validation
❌ Misaligned with strategy
❌ Better alternatives exist
❌ Execution risk too high
```

---

## Decision Outcomes

### GO

- Allocate resources
- Communicate decision
- Establish checkpoints
- Plan review cycle

### NO-GO

- Document rationale
- Revisit in 6 months?
- Explore alternatives
- Notify stakeholders

### GO / WITH CONDITIONS

- Proceed with constraints
- Address risks
- Require approval for next phase
- Set review triggers

---

## Review Cycle

- Week 2: Checkpoint on progress
- Week 4: Major milestone review
- Week 8: Pivot/continue decision
- Quarterly: Overall reassessment
