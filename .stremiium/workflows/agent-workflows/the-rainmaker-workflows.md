# The Rainmaker - Sales & Business Development Workflows

**Agent**: The Rainmaker 💰  
**Domain**: Sales strategy, revenue, partnerships, client management  
**Purpose**: Sales processes, deal pipeline, business development, client acquisition

---

## Workflow 1: Sales Pipeline Management Workflow

### Trigger

- Sales pipeline needs organization
- Leads identified and need follow-up
- Deal closure process needs optimization
- Sales forecast needed

### Process

#### Step 1: Organize Sales Pipeline

**Goal**: Structure and categorize all opportunities  
**Actions**:

- Inventory all active opportunities
- Classify by stage (prospect, conversation, proposal, negotiation, closing)
- Assign ownership
- Estimate deal size and timeline
- Identify risk factors
- Calculate potential revenue

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ All opportunities captured
- ✅ Stages defined clearly
- ✅ Revenue potential calculated
- ✅ Realistic assessment

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 2: Activate Outreach

**Goal**: Engage with prospects  
**Actions**:

- Map contact strategies for each prospect
- Schedule outreach activities
- Prepare messaging/materials
- Execute initial outreach
- Track responses
- Qualify opportunities

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Prospects engaged
- ✅ Conversations initiated
- ✅ Response rate tracked

**Duration**: 1-2 weeks

---

#### Step 3: Advance Deals

**Goal**: Move opportunities forward  
**Actions**:

- Conduct discovery calls
- Qualify requirements
- Present solutions
- Address objections
- Move qualified prospects to proposal stage
- Track deal progress

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Deals advancing
- ✅ Good qualification happening
- ✅ Pipeline moving

**Duration**: Ongoing

---

#### Step 4: Close Deals

**Goal**: Convert opportunities to customers  
**Actions**:

- Prepare proposals
- Present to decision makers
- Negotiate terms
- Close agreements
- Set implementation date
- Celebrate wins

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Deals closed
- ✅ Terms favorable
- ✅ Smooth transition to delivery

**Duration**: 1-4 weeks per deal

---

#### Step 5: Monitor & Forecast

**Goal**: Track pipeline and revenue  
**Actions**:

- Weekly pipeline reviews
- Track velocity through stages
- Monitor close rates
- Forecast monthly revenue
- Identify bottlenecks
- Adjust strategy as needed

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Pipeline tracked
- ✅ Forecast accurate
- ✅ Issues identified early

**Duration**: Weekly + ongoing

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Sales pipeline organized
- ✅ Prospects engaged
- ✅ Deals advancing
- ✅ Revenue forecasted
- ✅ Success metrics tracked

## Workflow 3: Partnership Development Workflow

### Trigger

- Co-selling arrangement proposed

- Channel partner recruitment

#### Step 1: Identify Partnership Opportunit

**Goal**: Find right partners  
**Actions**:

- Map market ecosystem
- Identify potential partners
- Analyze fit and value
- Research partner capability
- Understand their business model
- Assess mutual benefit

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Potential partners identified
- ✅ Fit assessed
- ✅ Value proposition clear

**Duration**: 2-4 hours

---

#### Step 2: Develop Proposal

**Goal**: Create partnership plan  
**Actions**:

- Define partnership scope
- Outline benefits for each party
- Identify operational details
- Plan go-to-market
- Define success metrics
- Create business case

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Proposal compelling
- ✅ Win-win for both sides
- ✅ Clear expectations

**Duration**: 3-4 hours

---

#### Step 3: Negotiate & Sign

**Goal**: Formalize partnership  
**Actions**:

- Present proposal
- Negotiate terms
- Address concerns
- Prepare agreement

- Sign partnership

- ✅ Terms fair to both sides

**Success Criteria**:

#### Step 5: Manage & Grow Partnership

**Goal**: Maximize partnership value  
**Actions**:

- Identify expansion opportunities
- Provide support/resources
- Celebrate successes
- Plan enhancements

**Success Criteria**:

## Workflow 4: Sales Strategy & Target Setting Workflow

### Trigger

- Annual sales planning
- Market shift requires strategy change
- Growth target challenging
- Sales process needs optimization

### Process

#### Step 1: Analyze Market & Competition

**Goal**: Understand market landscape  
**Actions**:

- Market size and trends
- Customer segments
- Competitive positioning
- Customer buying patterns
- Industry dynamics
- Market gaps

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Market clearly understood
- ✅ Opportunity identified
- ✅ Competitive position clear

**Duration**: 3-4 hours

---

#### Step 2: Set Revenue Targets

**Goal**: Define what to achieve  
**Actions**:

- Analyze historical performance
- Set ambitious but realistic targets
- Break down by segment/product
- Allocate to sales team
- Identify what they need
- Communicate targets

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Targets clear
- ✅ Team engaged
- ✅ Resources understood

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 3: Design Sales Strategy

**Goal**: Plan how to reach targets  
**Actions**:

- Identify target customer profiles
- Design value propositions
- Plan sales process
- Identify channels
- Allocate resources
- Plan marketing support

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Strategy clear
- ✅ Resources allocated
- ✅ Team aligned

**Duration**: 3-4 hours

---

#### Step 4: Execute & Monitor

**Goal**: Drive sales activities  
**Actions**:

- Activate sales execution
- Track activities (calls, meetings, proposals)
- Monitor against pipeline plan
- Weekly performance reviews
- Identify early issues
- Adjust tactics

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Activity strong
- ✅ Pipeline on track
- ✅ Issues addressed early

**Duration**: Ongoing throughout period

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Clear sales strategy
- ✅ Ambitious targets set
- ✅ Sales team activated
- ✅ Performance tracked
- ✅ Revenue goals in reach
