# The Amplifier - Marketing & Communications Workflows

**Agent**: The Amplifier 📢  
**Domain**: Marketing, brand, PR, public communication  
**Purpose**: Marketing strategy, PR, brand communication, trend analysis

---

## Workflow 1: Marketing Campaign Workflow

### Trigger

- Product launch
- New service introduction
- Seasonal campaign
- Lead generation campaign needed
- Brand awareness campaign

### Process

#### Step 1: Define Campaign Strategy

**Goal**: Plan the campaign  
**Actions**:

- Define campaign objective
- Identify target audience
- Set success metrics
- Define messaging
- Plan channels
- Create campaign brief

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Objective clear
- ✅ Audience identified
- ✅ Metrics measurable
- ✅ Messaging compelling

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 2: Develop Creative Assets

**Goal**: Create campaign materials  
**Actions**:

- Brainstorm creative concepts
- Design visuals
- Write copy
- Create videos (if needed)
- Develop landing pages
- Prepare assets for launch

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Creative compelling
- ✅ On-brand
- ✅ Diverse formats
- ✅ High quality

**Duration**: 4-8 hours

---

#### Step 3: Plan Distribution

**Goal**: Plan how to reach audience  
**Actions**:

- Select channels (social, email, web, paid ads, etc.)
- Plan timing/cadence
- Prepare channel-specific content
- Plan budget allocation
- Set up tracking/analytics
- Create distribution calendar

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Multi-channel approach
- ✅ Timing optimized
- ✅ Budget allocated
- ✅ Tracking ready

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 4: Launch Campaign

**Goal**: Execute the campaign  
**Actions**:

- Launch across channels
- Monitor initial response
- Adjust messaging if needed
- Engage with audience
- Answer questions
- Track metrics

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Campaign live
- ✅ Audience engaging
- ✅ Performance tracking

**Duration**: Day 1

---

#### Step 5: Optimize & Measure

**Goal**: Improve performance  
**Actions**:

- Monitor daily metrics
- Identify top-performing content
- Optimize underperforming elements
- Test variations
- Gather audience feedback
- Measure ROI

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Performance metrics tracked
- ✅ Optimizations working
- ✅ ROI positive
- ✅ Insights documented

**Duration**: 2-4 weeks

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Campaign successfully executed
- ✅ Audience engaged
- ✅ Goals achieved
- ✅ ROI measured
- ✅ Insights captured

**Goal**: Identify newsworthy story  
**Actions**:

- Determine news value

- Identify journalist contacts
- Gather background info
- Create story positioning

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Story compelling
- ✅ News-worthy
- ✅ Media targets identified
- ✅ Contacts ready

**Duration**: 1-2 hours

---

**Goal**: Prepare for media outreach

- Gather supporting materials
- Quality review

- ✅ Messaging on-brand

- Follow up appropriately

- Provide additional materials
- Track responses

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Journalists engaged
- ✅ Interest generated

- Measure impact

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Impact measured

## Workflow 4: Trend Analysis & Forecasting Workflow

### Trigger

- Annual planning cycle
- Strategic planning input needed
- Market shift observed
- Competitive threat identified
- Innovation initiative

### Process

#### Step 1: Gather Market Intelligence

**Goal**: Understand market trends  
**Actions**:

- Research industry trends
- Track competitor activity
- Monitor social conversations
- Survey customers
- Review market research
- Document insights

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Trends identified
- ✅ Competitive landscape understood
- ✅ Customer insights gathered

**Duration**: 4-6 hours

---

#### Step 2: Analyze Implications

**Goal**: Understand what trends mean  
**Actions**:

- Analyze impact on business
- Identify opportunities
- Identify threats
- Consider timeframes
- Assess probability
- Create scenarios

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Implications clear
- ✅ Opportunities identified
- ✅ Threats understood
- ✅ Scenarios developed

**Duration**: 3-4 hours

---

#### Step 3: Prepare Forecast

**Goal**: Predict likely future  
**Actions**:

- Create trend forecast
- Project trajectory
- Estimate impact
- Prepare recommendations
- Document confidence level
- Prepare presentation

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Forecast reasonable
- ✅ Recommendations actionable
- ✅ Presentation clear

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

---

#### Step 4: Share Insights

**Goal**: Inform decision-making  
**Actions**:

- Present forecast to leadership
- Discuss implications
- Support strategic planning
- Answer questions
- Monitor forecast accuracy over time
- Update forecasts

**Success Criteria**:

- ✅ Insights understood
- ✅ Strategy informed
- ✅ Decisions improved

**Duration**: 1-2 hours presentation + ongoing

---

### Outcomes

- ✅ Market trends understood
- ✅ Future forecast created
- ✅ Strategic planning informed
- ✅ Opportunities identified
- ✅ Risks mitigated
